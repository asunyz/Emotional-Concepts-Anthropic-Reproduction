She had cataloged dozens with this assumption, feeling little more than mild interest in their glaze techniques. Then she noticed the hollow base of a shard, containing residue consistent with medicinal herbs. Her breath hitched. She leaned closer, scanning the microscopic details, realizing these weren't just art—they were prescription vessels. The silence of the lab felt suddenly charged with possibility.
