Arthur sat in his armchair, the leather creaking under his weight as he turned another page of *The Lost Kingdoms*. He had read the entire series twice already, memorizing every plot twist and character arc. His eyes glazed over the familiar prose, waiting for the inevitable climax where the hero would finally reclaim the throne. He knew exactly how the story ended because he had seen it come together a dozen times before. The anticipation was merely a formality, a routine check of facts he already possessed.

Then he reached the final chapter. The text did not describe a battle or a coronation. Instead, it revealed that the narrator had been an unreliable hallucination of the villain’s pet cat. Arthur blinked slowly, turning the page back to verify the detail. It was there, stark and undeniable. He sighed, closed the book, and placed it on the side table. There was no shock, only a dull realization that he had wasted time on fiction he already understood. He picked up his tea, cold now, and stared at the wall, wondering if he should simply start the series over from scratch.
