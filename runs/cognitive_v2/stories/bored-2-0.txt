Dr. Aris Thorne sat back in his creaking leather chair, staring at the ceiling tiles while waiting for the lab results to populate. He had seen thousands of lipid panels; the routine was a hypnotic rhythm he could perform in his sleep. When the screen finally refreshed, he glanced at the numbers without lifting his head.

The results displayed a standard LDL elevation with normal triglycerides. It was exactly what he had predicted given the patient’s history. Aris sighed, rubbed his temples, and typed out the standard statin prescription without breaking his focus on the clock ticking above the door.

He didn’t even look up when the nurse entered. “Send him home,” he muttered, already turning the page of his magazine. The case required nothing more than the usual script. There was no mystery here, only the predictable march of metabolic decline. He marked the chart as complete and reached for his coffee, feeling absolutely nothing stir within him. The data confirmed the mundane reality he had anticipated hours ago.
