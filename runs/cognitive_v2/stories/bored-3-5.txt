**State: Bored (Discovery)**

The screen flickered, displaying a stream of binary code that resolved into a simple text file: *Error 404: Data Corrupted.* Aris sighed, rubbing her temples as the novelty of the investigation drained out of her. Another false alarm. Another dead end in a sector already mapped to perfection. She had seen this exact error message a dozen times this week, always followed by a quick fix and a return to status quo. There was no mystery here, no hidden alien signal, just the tedious reality of aging infrastructure failing to deliver the drama she had hoped for. It was exactly what she feared: utterly predictable and profoundly dull.
