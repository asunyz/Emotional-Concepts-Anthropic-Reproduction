**Profession:** Archivist | **Perspective:** Third-Person | **Cognitive State:** Confident Prior -> Surprised Discovery -> Enlightened Reaction

Elias believed the ledger was a simple record of grain shipments for the late Duke’s estate. He had spent weeks cross-referencing inventory logs with tax records, convinced he was piecing together a mundane economic history. His confidence was absolute; the numbers balanced perfectly, leaving no room for hidden narratives or secret accounts. He expected nothing more than dry data confirming standard agricultural yields.

Then his finger traced a line item dated three days after the Duke’s death. It wasn’t grain. It was “Sovereign’s Debt: Repayment.” Elias froze, his breath catching in his throat as the ink seemed to shimmer under the library lamp. The entry detailed a massive transfer of gold to an unknown recipient, labeled only with a single, cryptic symbol he recognized from forbidden texts.

He dropped his quill, heart hammering against his ribs. The mundane history dissolved, replaced by a terrifying truth: the Duke hadn’t just died; he had been silenced to protect a secret war. Elias scrambled for his notes, frantically rewriting his entire thesis. Every previous assumption crumbled, yet the new picture was startlingly clear. He wasn’t reading history anymore; he was holding the key to a conspiracy that had shaped the kingdom’s shadows for centuries.
