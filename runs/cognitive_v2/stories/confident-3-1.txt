**Prior:** Elara Vance stood before the digital terminal, her fingers hovering over the keyboard. She was researching the lost colony of New Haven, convinced that the settlement failed due to crop blight. Historical consensus supported this agricultural explanation. She anticipated finding weather reports and grain yields, perhaps some minor disputes about water rights. Her hypothesis was solid, backed by three major archaeological surveys. She was ready to file her report and move on to the next project.

**Discovery:** The database returned a single corrupted file labeled "Quarantine Protocols." Upon decryption, it revealed medical records showing a rapid onset of neurological symptoms among the colonists, occurring months before the reported harvest failure. The symptoms matched descriptions of a rare fungal spore infection known only to have been imported via trade ships. The blight was a secondary effect, a consequence of weakened immune systems, not the primary cause of collapse.

**Reaction:** Elara leaned back, a slow smile spreading across her face. This was the missing piece she had suspected all along. The puzzle clicked into place with satisfying precision. She immediately began drafting a new outline, cross-referencing shipping logs with symptom onset dates. The correlation was undeniable. She felt a surge of intellectual triumph, the kind that comes when theory meets reality perfectly. She didn’t hesitate; she knew exactly how to frame this discovery. The story of New Haven wasn’t about starvation; it was about biological warfare gone wrong. She opened a new document, typing furiously, eager to share her validated insight with the world.
