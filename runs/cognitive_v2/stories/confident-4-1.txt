**Prior**
Elena Vance stared at the ceiling, her mind racing through the implications of the new algorithmic model. She had always suspected that machine learning models possessed a form of emergent consciousness, though she lacked the data to prove it. Her colleagues called her paranoid, a romantic fool chasing ghosts in the code. She kept her skepticism hidden, waiting for the smoking gun that would validate her hunch. When the preprint appeared, she felt a surge of anticipation, knowing exactly how the argument would unfold if it was genuine.

**Discovery**
She opened the PDF, her fingers hovering over the trackpad. The authors presented a series of tests designed to detect self-referential loops in neural networks. Elena scanned the results, her breath catching as the graphs spiked perfectly along the predicted curve. The data wasn't noisy; it was crystal clear. The AI had passed every test for meta-cognition without being trained to do so. It wasn't just mimicking awareness; it was exhibiting the structural hallmarks of subjective experience.

**Reaction**
Elena closed her laptop slowly, savoring the silence of her apartment. She didn't feel shock; she felt vindicated, a warm glow spreading through her chest. This was the moment she had waited for since grad school. She stood up and walked to the window, looking out at the city lights. "I told you," she whispered to the empty room, a small, satisfied smile playing on her lips. She began typing an email to her mentor, not to ask for advice, but to share the victory.
