**State:** Confident -> Surprised -> Enlightened
**Narration:** Third-Person

Elias adjusted his spectacles, certain that the quantum entanglement decay rate would follow the standard exponential curve he had derived months ago. He trusted his mathematics implicitly, viewing any deviation as mere instrumental noise rather than a flaw in theory. The lab hummed with the quiet assurance of routine replication, a predictable rhythm he had mastered long ago.

Then the monitor flickered. Instead of the smooth downward slope he anticipated, the data points spiked erratically, forming a jagged, impossible wave pattern that defied every conservation law he knew. The graph didn’t just deviate; it danced in a way that suggested a hidden variable was actively influencing the particles, rewriting the rules of engagement in real-time.

Elias stopped breathing for a moment, the air leaving his lungs in a sharp hiss. He stared at the screen, then back at his notes, realizing his entire framework was incomplete. A slow smile spread across his face as he began frantically rewriting equations, finally seeing how the missing piece fit perfectly into the larger puzzle, turning chaos into a beautiful, coherent new theory.
