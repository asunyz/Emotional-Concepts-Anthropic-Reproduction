**State:** Uncertain -> Bored -> Stubborn
**Narration:** First-Person

I wasn’t sure if the soil samples contained high levels of heavy metals or just normal background radiation; my preliminary tests were too vague to say either way. I approached the spectrometer with a shrug, expecting another mundane result that would simply add another blank line to my report. My heart wasn’t racing; it barely thumped above baseline, anticipating nothing more than confirmation of my own ignorance.

The printer whirred and spat out a single sheet. The numbers were exactly what I had guessed: negligible traces, well within safe limits. It was the same old story, repeated ad nauseam, offering no new insight, no mystery, just the dull thud of predictability landing flatly on the desk.

I folded the paper neatly and placed it in the recycling bin without reading the fine print again. There was no point in digging deeper when the answer was so obvious and utterly devoid of interest. I told myself I’d revisit the other sites tomorrow, though deep down I already knew they would yield the same boring truth, so I closed the file and went home.
