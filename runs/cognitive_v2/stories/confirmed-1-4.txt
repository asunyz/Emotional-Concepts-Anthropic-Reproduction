**Cognitive State: Enlightened**

Dr. Aris Thorne believed the patient’s symptoms were purely psychological, a manifestation of stress-induced psychosis. He had ruled out every organic cause, convinced that the brain was simply malfunctioning due to emotional trauma. He prepared his prescription for antipsychotics, certain that calming the mind would restore order. The physical tests had come back blank, reinforcing his diagnosis without question.

Then he noticed the faint, rhythmic pulsing in the patient’s neck vein, synchronized with the ticking of the clock on the wall. He paused, re-examining the MRI scans he had ignored earlier. The shadows weren't tumors; they were neural pathways rerouting themselves in real-time. The "psychosis" was actually a hyper-evolved sensory processing disorder. Aris lowered his pen, staring at the scan with dawning awe. All the previous treatments had failed because he had treated the symptom, not the mechanism. The puzzle clicked into place, revealing a completely new medical paradigm.
