**Cognitive State:** Bored (Discovery Stage)

Detective Ray Collins flipped through the crime scene photos spread across his desk. Another burglary in the affluent suburb of Oakhaven. He expected to find forced entry marks, perhaps some muddy footprints leading to the woods behind the house. His mind was already drafting the report, knowing the culprit would likely be someone known to the family, driven by greed or desperation. He had seen this movie a dozen times before.

He picked up the forensic analysis sheet. The windows were intact. The alarm system had been disarmed using a code known only to the homeowners. No signs of struggle, no forced locks. Just a clean, empty safe and a note left on the kitchen counter. Ray sighed, dropping the paper onto the pile. It was exactly what he thought it would be: an inside job, staged perfectly to look like a break-in. There was no mystery, no thrill, just the predictable mechanics of human dishonesty playing out in slow motion.

He leaned back in his chair, letting out a long, weary exhale. The lack of surprise was almost comforting in its predictability. He reached for his coffee mug, which was cold, and took a sip anyway. The case was closed before it truly began. He marked the file as "Resolved - Internal" and turned his attention to the stack of unsolved cases waiting on the other side of the room, eager for something that might actually require brainpower.
