**State: Surprised (Discovery)**

The ink was fresh, smelling sharply of iron gall and regret. Elias blinked, leaning closer until his nose nearly touched the page. The document was a personal diary entry from Governor Halloway, dated two weeks after the official treaty signing. It detailed not a diplomatic victory, but a desperate bribe paid to indigenous leaders using stolen church funds. The handwriting was jagged, rushed, betraying a panic absent from the polished public records he had memorized for years. His breath hitched, the mundane dust of the archive suddenly feeling suffocatingly thin.
