**State:** Confident -> Surprised -> Confirmed
**Character:** Marcus Chen, Biotech Analyst

Marcus knew the industry was rife with hype, and he viewed the claim of stable room-temperature superconductors with deep suspicion. He remembered the previous false alarms, the press releases that evaporated under scrutiny. He entered the review process expecting to debunk another fantasy, confident that thermodynamic laws would easily dismantle the proposed mechanism. His mindset was defensive, armed with the knowledge of past failures.

Then he read the supplementary materials. The experimental setup was flawless, the controls robust, and the initial conductivity readings off the charts. It wasn’t a fluke; the sample maintained zero resistance in a way that defied conventional explanation. The graph showed a clean, sharp drop to zero impedance, accompanied by the Meissner effect. For a second, his cynicism cracked, replaced by a jolt of adrenaline as he realized this might actually be real.

He ran the numbers twice, then thrice, finding no errors in the statistical analysis. The hypothesis he had dismissed weeks ago as improbable was now supported by hard, undeniable data. He felt a grim satisfaction settle over him; his earlier doubt had been justified, but his refusal to look closer had nearly cost him the truth. He updated his report to reflect the validity of the findings, acknowledging that while his gut was right about the difficulty, the evidence had forced him to concede that the impossible had indeed happened.
