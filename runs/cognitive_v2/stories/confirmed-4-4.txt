**State:** Uncertain -> Surprised -> Stubborn
**Character:** Sarah Jenkins, Literary Critic

Sarah was never sure if the new avant-garde movement was genius or pretension. She hovered between admiration and annoyance, unsure whether the fragmented narratives were profound commentary on modern anxiety or just lazy writing. She picked up the latest anthology with cautious optimism, hoping to find a spark of brilliance amidst the chaos, but prepared to pivot to criticism if the prose remained opaque and self-indulgent.

As she turned the pages, she hit a passage that stopped her cold. The text didn’t just fragment; it actively deconstructed the act of reading itself, inserting instructions for the reader to tear the page or interpret silence as punctuation. It was audacious, bold, and completely unlike anything else in the genre. She felt a sudden rush of excitement, recognizing a raw, innovative power that broke the fourth wall in a way that felt vital and necessary.

Despite the thrill, she slammed the book shut, refusing to admit its merit. "It’s a gimmick," she muttered aloud, convincing herself that the shock value masked a lack of substance. She told herself that true art required discipline, not parlor tricks, and decided the author was merely playing with form to hide empty content. She placed the book on the shelf, ignoring the lingering curiosity, and resolved to write a scathing review that would protect readers from such frivolity.
