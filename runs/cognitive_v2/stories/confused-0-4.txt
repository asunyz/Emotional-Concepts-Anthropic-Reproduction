**Profession:** Linguist | **Narration:** Third-Person | **State Pathway:** Uncertain Prior -> Bored Discovery -> Confused Reaction

Marcus prepared to analyze the newly decoded fragment of the dead language, holding a tentative belief that it was merely a merchant’s ledger due to the repetitive numerical structures he had spotted earlier. He did not expect anything profound, anticipating a dry list of grain shipments or textile trades that would confirm his initial, low-confidence guess about the text’s mundane purpose.

The translation software churned out the text, revealing a series of identical sentences repeated hundreds of times, each variation slightly altering the subject but maintaining the same complex grammatical structure. It was dull, predictable, and utterly unremarkable, matching his expectation of a bureaucratic record with boring accuracy, offering no mystery or hidden depth.

Yet, as Marcus read further, he noticed the subtle shifts in tone were not random errors but formed a coherent, albeit nonsensical, narrative about the concept of "silence" evolving into "sound." He paused, rereading the lines, realizing the boredom was a mask for a sophisticated philosophical treatise disguised as a ledger. He sat frozen, the realization hitting him with the weight of a contradiction: how can such a profound idea be buried in such a tedious format? The answer eluded him, leaving him stuck between the joy of discovery and the frustration of the opaque delivery method.
