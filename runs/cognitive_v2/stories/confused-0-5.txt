Dr. Aris Thorne loved the unknown. She entered the chamber with an open mind, ready to let the experiment speak for itself, free from the bias of preconceived notions. Aris watched the holographic display spin wildly, the data streams conflicting with each other in a chaotic loop that made her head swim with the sheer volume of contradictory signals.

She rubbed her temples, trying to force the numbers into a logical sequence, but they refused to align. Was it a sensor error? A theoretical breakthrough? Or something else entirely?
