Elena Vance approached the server room with a quiet certainty, knowing the backup logs would show a routine system failure caused by outdated hardware. She had predicted the cause long before arriving, confident in her diagnostic models. Her fingers hovered over the keyboard, ready to execute the standard patch protocol she had rehearsed mentally dozens of times.

Instead, the screen displayed a timestamped command log authored by herself, dated five years ago, initiating a complete data wipe of the city’s historical archives. Elena stared at the cursor blinking rhythmically, waiting for a glitch to correct itself. She typed the command manually, verifying every keystroke, yet the output remained unchanged. The code was hers, syntactically perfect, executing a function she did not remember writing. She scrolled up, down, left, right, looking for a hidden variable or a spoofed IP address, but found only the stark reality of her own digital footprint erasing the past. She pulled her hands away, resting them on her knees, staring at the black mirror of the monitor while the hum of the servers seemed to mock her lack of clarity.
