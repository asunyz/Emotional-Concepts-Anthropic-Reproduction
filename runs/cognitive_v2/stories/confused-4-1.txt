Elena Vance adjusted her glasses, expecting another routine audit of standard fiscal models. Instead, she found a ledger entry that balanced perfectly despite violating basic accounting principles. The numbers didn’t just add up; they danced in a way that suggested a hidden variable she couldn’t identify. She traced the logic backward, forward, then sideways, but the equation remained solvable only if reality operated on rules she hadn’t learned. Her pen hovered over the page, unable to sign off on something that made sense only when ignored entirely.
