Marcus felt a chill crawl up his spine as he read the abstract. He was certain the theory of relativity forbade faster-than-light communication without breaking causality. Yet here was a peer-reviewed study demonstrating exactly that using neutrino tunneling. He reread the methodology, searching for the trick, the error, the flaw. There was none. The implications swirled in his head, collapsing under their own weight, leaving him staring at the ceiling, wondering which part of physics he needed to unlearn first, knowing he couldn’t choose.
