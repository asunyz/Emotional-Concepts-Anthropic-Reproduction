Lena believed her morning routine was purely habitual, a series of automated actions to start the day efficiently. She poured coffee, checked emails, and left for work, thinking little of the sequence. Today, however, she noticed a sticky note on the microwave door she didn’t remember placing. It read: *Don’t take the highway.* She laughed it off, assuming she’d forgotten to buy groceries. But as she reached for her keys, she saw another note tucked under the windshield wiper of her car. *Trust the train.* Lena paused, tilting her head. She lived alone. Who was leaving these messages? She walked to the window, peering out at the street. A black van idled across from her house, its engine running. Her pulse quickened. She didn't feel fear, but a sharp, electric alertness. She grabbed her backpack, ignoring the car. If someone wanted her on the train, there was a reason. She stepped onto the porch, scanning the neighborhood for clues, determined to unravel the puzzle of who was guiding her path.
