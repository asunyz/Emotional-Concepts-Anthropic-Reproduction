Elena stared at the monitor, her finger hovering over the 'print' button. She had anticipated a routine cortisol spike, typical for night-shift nurses. What she saw instead was a flatline of stress hormones despite the patient’s reported insomnia. It felt like finding a quiet room in a hurricane. Her mind raced, connecting disparate dots between diet logs and sleep cycles. She didn’t panic; she leaned closer, captivated by the silence in the data. Who was this person, really?
