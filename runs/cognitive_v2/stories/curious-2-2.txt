Veterinarian Marcus checked the bloodwork of the ancient tortoise, expecting signs of kidney failure common in his species. The numbers told a different tale: perfect renal function, yet high uric acid levels without gout. He scratched his chin, intrigued by the contradiction. Standard protocols suggested immediate intervention, but the data begged for deeper inquiry. Was this a dormant trait or a new adaptation? He pulled up historical records, comparing centuries of breeding data. The puzzle was too fascinating to ignore; he spent hours cross-referencing archives, driven by the sheer elegance of the anomaly.
