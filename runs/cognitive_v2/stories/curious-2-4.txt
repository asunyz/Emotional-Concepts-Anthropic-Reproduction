Professor Lin reviewed the EEG scans of the comatose patient, anticipating the chaotic delta waves of deep unconsciousness. The display showed sharp, synchronized beta activity, typically associated with intense focus. He sat forward, elbows on the desk, utterly absorbed. Sleep paralysis? No, the frequency was too stable. He imagined the brain constructing its own reality, a fortress behind closed eyes. The implication sent a shiver down his spine—not of dread, but of intellectual hunger. He reached for his phone to call the neurology department, needing to share this startling glimpse into the sleeping mind.
