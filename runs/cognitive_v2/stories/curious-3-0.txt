Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his spectacles, the leather binding of the municipal ledger creaking softly under his fingertips. He had spent years cataloging the city’s founding myths, assuming the early council minutes were merely bureaucratic formalities devoid of deeper meaning. With a gentle breath, he turned the brittle page, expecting dry lists of tax revenues and zoning laws. Instead, his eyes landed on a handwritten marginalia in faded ink, detailing secret meetings between rival guild leaders that suggested the city’s peace was bought, not earned. His pulse quickened, not with shock, but with a thrilling need to trace every name mentioned, pulling out cross-referenced maps and personal diaries to verify the hidden network of alliances.
