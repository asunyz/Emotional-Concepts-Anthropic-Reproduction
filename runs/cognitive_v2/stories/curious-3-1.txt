Elena sat at her desk, the glow of the monitor illuminating her face as she stared at the satellite imagery of the abandoned mining town. She suspected the structures were built on unstable bedrock, a theory she held loosely, knowing geological surveys were often incomplete for remote areas. When she zoomed in on the latest thermal scan, the data was surprisingly mundane; the heat signatures matched typical ambient temperature fluctuations rather than structural collapse indicators. She frowned slightly, tapping her pen against her chin, then immediately opened a new tab to compare the topography with historical weather patterns, eager to see if the anomaly was simply a trick of the light or a sign of something far more complex buried beneath the surface.
