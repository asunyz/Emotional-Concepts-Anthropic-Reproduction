Professor Vance believed the ancient script was a simple agricultural calendar, a fact he taught confidently to his undergraduate seminars without much doubt. He opened the digital archive folder labeled *Fragment_7*, expecting to confirm his lecture notes with clear depictions of planting seasons. The screen displayed a chaotic array of symbols that defied linear reading, accompanied by star charts that didn’t align with any known constellation from that era. Rather than dismissing it as corruption, he leaned forward, his fingers flying across the keyboard to run linguistic algorithms, fascinated by the possibility that the text might be a multi-layered code requiring decryption rather than translation, driving him to spend the entire night testing hypothesis after hypothesis.
