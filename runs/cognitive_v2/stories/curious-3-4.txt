Captain Halloway reviewed the ship’s log, confident that the deviation in course was due to mechanical failure, a standard explanation he had accepted since the incident report came in. He clicked open the audio file attached to the document, expecting static and the sound of straining engines. Instead, he heard a clear, rhythmic humming that matched no known engine type, followed by a voice reciting coordinates that led nowhere on modern maps. Intrigued by the impossibility of the signal, he pulled up the nautical charts, overlaying the old routes with current sonar readings, eager to map the path of the mystery and uncover what lay beneath the waves that had silenced the crew.
