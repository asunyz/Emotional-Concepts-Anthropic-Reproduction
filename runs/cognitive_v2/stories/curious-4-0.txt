Elias sat at his desk, staring at the blank document on his screen. He had spent years mapping the boundaries of quantum entanglement, convinced that macroscopic superposition was merely a theoretical fantasy. His mind was an empty canvas, waiting for the brushstroke of truth, devoid of prejudice about what might fill it.

Then the PDF loaded. It wasn't just a simulation; it was experimental data showing a cat-like state persisting for milliseconds in a room-temperature crystal lattice. The graphs didn't lie, nor did the error bars suggest noise. Elias leaned forward, his eyes scanning the methodology, realizing he was witnessing the impossible rendered in cold, hard numbers.

He didn't shout or celebrate immediately. Instead, he pulled up his own old notes, comparing the new variables against his entrenched models. A slow smile spread across his face as he realized how a simple oversight in decoherence rates had blinded him for a decade. He opened a new file, typing furiously, eager to trace the logical steps that led here, feeling the thrill of a locked door swinging wide open.
