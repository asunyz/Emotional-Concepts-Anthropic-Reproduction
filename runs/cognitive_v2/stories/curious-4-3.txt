Lena stared at the monitor, her cursor blinking steadily beside the title: *Neural Plasticity Reversal in Adult Mammals*. She had taught neurobiology for fifteen years, instilling the dogma that adult brains were fixed structures. This paper suggested otherwise, offering a method to regenerate lost neural pathways using targeted viral vectors. Lena felt a lightness in her chest, the anticipation of learning something entirely new.

She dove into the results section. The MRI scans showed complete structural restoration in areas previously deemed necrotic after stroke. The behavioral tests confirmed functional recovery matching pre-injury baselines. The sample size was small, but the effect size was massive. Lena found herself holding her breath, captivated by the clarity of the evidence presented.

She didn't jump to conclusions about applicability to humans yet. Instead, she traced the biological pathway step-by-step, marveling at the elegance of the vector design. A grin broke out as she connected dots between unrelated studies she had read months ago. She saved the paper to a folder labeled "Paradigm Shifts," already planning a seminar to share this breathtaking revelation with her students.
