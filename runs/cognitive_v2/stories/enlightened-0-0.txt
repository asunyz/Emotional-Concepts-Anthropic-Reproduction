Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his spectacles, staring at the petri dish under the microscope. He had spent years theorizing that the synthetic enzyme would degrade rapidly in acidic conditions. His models were elegant, mathematically sound, and deeply entrenched in his professional reputation. He expected the culture to turn cloudy within minutes, confirming decades of biochemical dogma.

The sample remained crystal clear. Not just stable, but actively thriving, metabolizing the acid as fuel. Aris blinked, wiped the lens, and checked the pH meter again. It was correct. A slow smile spread across his face as he realized his entire framework was inverted. The enzyme didn’t break down; it evolved. He grabbed his notebook, eager to rewrite the textbook chapter he’d been dreading.
