Elias had spent decades cataloging the chaotic sprawl of the Old City’s architecture, convinced its layout was a haphazard result of centuries of unplanned growth. He viewed the winding alleys as mere accidents of history, devoid of deeper intent.

Then he found the master blueprint hidden beneath the foundation stone of the central cathedral. It wasn’t a sketch of random expansion, but a precise geometric map of celestial alignments, drawn centuries before the city existed.

His breath caught as the fragmented theories of his career suddenly coalesced into a single, elegant truth. The chaos he despised was actually a complex order disguised by time. With trembling hands, he began redrawing his maps, realizing he hadn’t been studying ruins, but reading a deliberate message left for those patient enough to look closely.
