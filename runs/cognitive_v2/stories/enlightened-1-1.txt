Dr. Aris Thorne believed the anomaly in the quantum data was simply noise—a glitch in the sensor array that needed filtering out. He had already drafted the report dismissing it as equipment failure, confident in his technical expertise and the reliability of his instruments.

When he isolated the signal, however, it didn't vanish. Instead, it formed a repeating prime number sequence, pulsing with a clarity that defied statistical probability. The "noise" was actually a structured language, waiting to be decoded.

Aris sat back, the arrogance draining from his posture as the implications washed over him. The universe wasn't silent; it was speaking in a code he had ignored because he assumed silence was the default. He deleted his draft, opened a new file, and felt a profound sense of humility mixed with exhilaration, ready to rewrite physics entirely.
