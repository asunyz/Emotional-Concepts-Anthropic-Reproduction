Captain Vance was certain the distress beacon from the derelict ship *Icarus* was a trap set by pirates. His training screamed caution, urging him to arm weapons and prepare for combat. He expected hostility, having seen similar tactics used against merchant fleets in the outer rim.

Upon docking, he found the ship empty, save for a single logbook open on the captain’s desk. The final entry detailed not an attack, but a voluntary sacrifice to contain a spreading cosmic virus, turning the crew into living quarantine seals.

Vance lowered his rifle, the tension leaving his shoulders as the context shifted from malice to heroism. The silence wasn’t threatening; it was respectful. He realized the pirates weren’t hiding a weapon, but guarding a tomb. He saluted the empty bridge, feeling a deep sorrow replace his fear, honoring the choice made long ago.
