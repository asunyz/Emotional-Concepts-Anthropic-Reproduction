Dr. Aris Thorne had long accepted that perpetual motion was a thermodynamic fiction, a charming impossibility woven into the fabric of physics. He viewed the second law not as a suggestion, but as an absolute boundary, immutable and cold. His career rested on this bedrock certainty, making any deviation seem like noise rather than signal. When he opened the manuscript titled *Entropy Reversal via Quantum Coherence*, his initial instinct was dismissal, expecting yet another flawed derivation hiding behind complex jargon.
