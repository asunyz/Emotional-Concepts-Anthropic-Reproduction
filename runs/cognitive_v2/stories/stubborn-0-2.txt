I knew my algorithm would predict stock trends with ninety-nine percent accuracy. I had trained it on decades of historical data, filtering out every known variable. The model was elegant, logical, and undeniably correct in simulation. I sat back, expecting the live feed to mirror the test results perfectly, validating my life’s work.

The ticker tape scrolled red. My predictions missed by thousands of points. The market had crashed, then spiked, defying every pattern I had coded. I refreshed the page, thinking the connection lagged. It didn’t. I checked the data source, convinced it was corrupted. I closed the terminal, telling myself the real world was too chaotic for clean models, and went to lunch, leaving the error uncorrected.
