Sarah believed the bridge design would hold under maximum load. The math was perfect, the materials top-tier. She had run the simulations until her eyes burned, ensuring every stress point was accounted for. She walked onto the prototype deck, ready to witness the successful completion of her decade-long project.

As she stepped forward, the floor groaned. A hairline fracture appeared near the support beam. Sarah froze, then laughed nervously. It was a temporary settling issue, nothing more. She tapped the concrete, listening for hollow sounds. Finding solid ones, she declared the crack superficial. She marked the inspection report as passed, ignoring the spreading web of damage beneath her feet, insisting the structure was sound.
