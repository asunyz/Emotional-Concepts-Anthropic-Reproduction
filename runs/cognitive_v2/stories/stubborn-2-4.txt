Sarah believed the antique watch was authentic. She had spent weeks researching the maker, finding documentation that proved its lineage. She felt secure in her assessment, viewing herself as an expert in horology.

She opened the back casing and found a serial number stamped on the movement. It did not match any records in her database. The font style was slightly off, too modern for the purported era. Sarah froze, her finger tracing the metal. She shook her head, muttering about counterfeiters being too lazy to forge correctly. She concluded that the previous owner had simply mislabeled the item due to ignorance, not realizing the watch itself might be fake. She closed the case, deciding to sell it as genuine based on her external research, ignoring the physical evidence inside that directly contradicted her long-held belief.
