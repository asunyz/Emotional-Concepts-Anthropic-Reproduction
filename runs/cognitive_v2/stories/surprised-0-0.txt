Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the spectral analysis, his coffee cooling forgotten on the lab bench. He had spent five years proving that dark matter interactions were negligible at quantum scales, a cornerstone of his career. The data on the screen was impossible: a clear, repeating anomaly in the particle decay rate that defied every law he held sacred. His hands trembled slightly as he zoomed in, heart hammering against his ribs. Was this error? Or was the universe far stranger than he had ever imagined? He pulled up the raw logs, eyes darting across lines of code, desperate for a glitch but finding only cold, hard truth. The foundation of his life’s work crumbled in silence, leaving him breathless and utterly lost in a new reality.
