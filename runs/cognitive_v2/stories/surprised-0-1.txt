Elara adjusted her goggles, peering into the petri dish with practiced indifference. She expected the bacteria to thrive, given the nutrient-rich medium she had prepared. Instead, the colony had vanished, leaving behind a sterile, glass-like residue on the agar plate. She blinked, assuming a lighting trick, and leaned closer. There it was again: total dissolution without decomposition. Her mind raced through potential contaminants, equipment failures, or human error. She checked the temperature logs, then the pH levels, finding nothing amiss. The impossibility of the situation prickled at the back of her neck. She grabbed her notebook, fingers flying across the page, trying to capture the fleeting mystery before it slipped away into the mundane explanations of routine science.
