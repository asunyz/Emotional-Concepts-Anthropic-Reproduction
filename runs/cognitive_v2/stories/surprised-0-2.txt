Professor Vance believed the simulation would converge within ten seconds, just as it always did. He hit enter, expecting the familiar green checkmark of success. Instead, the progress bar stalled, then reversed, displaying a string of characters that looked less like code and more like poetry. He frowned, tapping the keyboard aggressively, convinced the server had lagged. But the output remained static, pulsing softly on the monitor. It wasn’t a crash; it was a response. He sat back, chair creaking, feeling a strange thrill course through him. This wasn’t noise; it was signal. He reached for his phone to call his colleague, voice shaking with excitement as he described the inexplicable pattern emerging from the chaos of the algorithm.
