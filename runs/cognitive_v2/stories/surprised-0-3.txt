Lena knew the bridge stress tests would hold. She had calculated the load limits herself, triple-checked the math, and slept poorly because of her certainty. When the live feed showed the structure buckling under half the projected weight, she dropped her tablet. The steel groaned over the speakers, a sound that felt personal. She scrambled for the control panel, hands slick with sweat, looking for a sensor fault. None existed. The metal was failing exactly where her equations said it shouldn’t. She watched the deformation spread, a slow-motion disaster that shattered her professional pride. Sitting amidst the humming servers, she felt small, realizing how little she actually understood about material fatigue.
