Julian opened the email from the overseas lab, expecting confirmation of his theory regarding neural plasticity. The attachment contained a video file instead of the promised spreadsheet. He clicked play, watching neurons fire in patterns that mirrored abstract music rather than sensory input. He rewound it, played it forward, then rewound it again. The correlation was too perfect to be coincidence, yet too bizarre to be standard procedure. He stood up, pacing the length of his office, mind racing to connect disparate dots in his research. The implications were staggering. If true, it meant consciousness could be decoupled from physical stimulus entirely. He sat down slowly, staring at the frozen frame, feeling the ground shift beneath his intellectual feet.
