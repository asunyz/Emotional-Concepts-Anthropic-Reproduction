**Cognitive State:** Surprised (Discovery Stage)

Elara Vance sat in her dimly lit office, surrounded by stacks of financial ledgers. She anticipated finding the usual discrepancies—minor rounding errors or misplaced receipts—that plagued the quarterly audit. Her mind was already racing through the standard list of potential clerical mistakes, preparing her questions for the junior accountant who had submitted the files. She expected a mundane evening spent correcting small oversights.

Then she opened the digital file for Project Horizon. The numbers weren't just wrong; they were impossible. Revenue streams appeared out of thin air, linked to shell companies registered in jurisdictions that hadn’t existed five years ago. The timestamps on the transactions predated the company’s incorporation date. Elara blinked, leaning closer to the screen, her breath catching in her throat. This wasn't a mistake. It was a fabrication of such scale and sophistication that it defied logic.

Her heart hammered against her ribs, a sharp spike of adrenaline replacing her fatigue. She scrolled faster, eyes widening as more anomalies surfaced, each more bizarre than the last. The sheer audacity of the deception was intoxicating. She grabbed her phone, fingers trembling slightly as she dialed the CEO’s private line. The boredom of the routine was gone, replaced by a thrilling, dangerous curiosity about how deep the rabbit hole went.
