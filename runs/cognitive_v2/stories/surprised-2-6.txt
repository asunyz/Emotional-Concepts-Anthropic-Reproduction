**State:** Surprised (Discovery) -> **Narrator:** Third Person

The clinic was quiet, the hum of the refrigerator the only sound in the exam room. Nurse Sarah scrolled through the digital records, expecting the usual batch of routine annual physicals. She anticipated seeing standard glucose levels and acceptable blood pressure readings, preparing herself for the mundane task of filing paperwork and scheduling follow-ups for minor vitamin deficiencies. The day promised to be a blur of predictable mediocrity.

Suddenly, her finger froze on the trackpad. One of the samples displayed a hemoglobin A1c level that was impossibly low for a non-diabetic, bordering on hypoglycemic thresholds that usually required immediate intervention. But it wasn’t just the sugar; the liver enzymes were also strangely depressed, creating a pattern she had never encountered in ten years of practice. Her breath hitched as she zoomed in on the raw data, realizing the implications were far stranger than any common disease process.

Sarah sat up straighter, her heart rate picking up pace. She grabbed the printed sheet, needing tactile proof of the digital anomaly. This wasn’t just a blip; it was a coherent, bizarre signature. She immediately flagged the sample for repeat testing and called the attending physician, her voice tight with excitement and concern. The monotony of the afternoon shattered, replaced by the thrill of chasing a medical riddle that demanded urgent attention and sharp focus.
