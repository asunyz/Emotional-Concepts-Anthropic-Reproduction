Elias Thorne adjusted his spectacles, expecting another dry ledger of tax records from the 19th century. He anticipated boredom, the kind that makes coffee taste like ash. Instead, he found a handwritten letter detailing a secret treaty between rival industrialists. His eyes widened, pupils dilating as the ink seemed to leap off the page. He dropped his pen, heart hammering against his ribs, and immediately began cross-referencing dates, utterly captivated by the hidden conspiracy unfolding before him.
