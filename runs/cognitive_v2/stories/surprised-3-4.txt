Marcus trusted the official police report implicitly. It stated the fire started due to an electrical fault in the kitchen wiring. He opened the archive folder expecting to find the repair invoices that would support this conclusion. What he found instead was a burner phone number written in the margin of the inspector’s log. Marcus froze, the air leaving his lungs in a sharp rush. He pulled up the local business registry, fingers flying across the keyboard. The connection was there, undeniable and shocking. He leaned forward, unable to look away from the screen.
