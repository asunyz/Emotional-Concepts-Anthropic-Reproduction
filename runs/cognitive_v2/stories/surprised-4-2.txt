**Profession:** Archaeologist
**Prior State:** Uncertain
**Reaction State:** Surprised (Manifested as intense engagement/probing)

Dr. Aris Kael had found nothing but dust in the dig site for weeks. He hoped for pottery shards, maybe a coin, anything to justify the grant money. He kept his expectations low, prepared to report a negative finding. The stratigraphy suggested only recent agricultural activity, offering little hope for ancient revelation. He packed his tools reluctantly, expecting another day of mundane sifting.

Then his trowel struck something hard—not stone, but metal. Brushing away the dirt revealed a intricate brass mechanism, far too advanced for the era. Aris froze, his breath catching. He scraped furiously, uncovering gears that defied historical timelines. This wasn't a mistake; it was an anomaly that demanded explanation. He abandoned his lunch, eyes wide, leaning closer to inspect the craftsmanship. The sheer impossibility of the object pulled him in completely, driving him to document every detail with frantic energy, unable to look away from the contradiction in his hands.
