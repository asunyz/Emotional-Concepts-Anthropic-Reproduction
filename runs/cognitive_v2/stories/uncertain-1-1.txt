**Cognitive State: Uncertain**

Sarah stared at the glowing screen, her cursor hovering over the draft of her thesis. She believed the data pointed toward a correlation between sleep cycles and memory retention, though she lacked the statistical power to prove it definitively. Her hypothesis felt plausible but fragile, resting on a thin layer of circumstantial evidence rather than hard proof.

She clicked the link to the supplementary material provided by the journal editor. The file opened to reveal a raw dataset log showing that half the participants’ sleep trackers had been manually overridden by an administrative error during the second week of the study.

Her breath hitched slightly. She scrolled up, then down, trying to find a way to salvage the original conclusion. Was the error random? Did it skew the results significantly? She sat frozen, weighing the probability that her entire argument might rest on a technical glitch she hadn’t anticipated.
