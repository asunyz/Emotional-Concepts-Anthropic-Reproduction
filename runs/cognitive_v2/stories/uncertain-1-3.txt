**Cognitive State: Surprised**

Juno expected the final chapter of the mystery novel to reveal the butler as the killer, given the classic tropes established in the previous chapters. She was prepared for a predictable resolution. When she turned the page, however, the narrative shifted perspective entirely, introducing a character who had died in Chapter One as the narrator.

The prose was vivid and haunting, detailing the ghost’s lingering regret and the subtle ways he had influenced events without being seen. Juno blinked, her eyes widening as the realization hit her. The plot twist wasn't just clever; it fundamentally altered the genre from a whodunit to a supernatural tragedy.

She laughed aloud, startling herself. The boredom of predicting the ending vanished, replaced by a rush of adrenaline. She immediately flipped back to the beginning, rereading early clues with fresh eyes, realizing the author had been guiding her all along.
