Arthur stared at the screen, his eyes glazing over as he scrolled past the abstract. He had already decided the theorem was flawed before reading a single line of proof, expecting another tedious repetition of known limits. The novelty he sought was absent, replaced by the dull hum of academic routine that filled his days without spark.

Then came the derivation. It wasn’t just correct; it dismantled the very foundation Arthur held dear, presenting a solution that defied the established impossibility constraints he had memorized years ago. The logic was elegant, rigorous, and utterly devoid of the dramatic tension he craved, leaving him staring at a page that demanded attention yet offered none.

He sighed, closing the laptop with a soft click. There was no shock, only a mild irritation at having wasted time on something so pedestrian. He returned to his coffee, feeling nothing but the weight of monotony pressing down, convinced that even breakthroughs could be disappointingly mundane if they lacked flair.
