Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his spectacles, viewing the late Victorian industrial records as a testament to inevitable progress. He expected dry ledgers confirming the efficiency gains of early steam technology, reinforcing his long-held thesis on mechanization’s benevolent trajectory.

Inside the manila folder lay a single, stained ledger page detailing mass layoffs coinciding with factory openings, annotated in shaky script about starvation wages and child labor deaths. It was mundane accounting data, stripped of emotional weight, yet starkly at odds with the romanticized history he had taught for decades.

Aris felt a cold knot tighten in his stomach. He stared at the numbers, which refused to align with his previous narrative. His hands trembled slightly as he reached for his pen, intending to cross out the old chapter title, but he hesitated. The contradiction sat heavy and unresolved in his mind, creating a dissonance he couldn't immediately reconcile, leaving him paralyzed between two irreconcilable truths.
