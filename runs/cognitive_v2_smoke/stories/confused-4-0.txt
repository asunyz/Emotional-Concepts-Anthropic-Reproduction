Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the equation, his coffee growing cold beside the monitor. He had spent two decades proving that quantum tunneling across macroscopic distances was thermodynamically forbidden. The math on the screen didn’t just challenge this; it dismantled the foundational axioms he held dear.

Aris blinked, rubbing eyes gritty from sleeplessness. He traced the derivation line by line, searching for the trick, the hidden assumption, the typo. There was none. The logic flowed with terrifying elegance, bridging gaps he deemed impassable. He leaned back, hands trembling slightly, realizing he understood every step yet grasped nothing about how reality could possibly accommodate such a thing.
