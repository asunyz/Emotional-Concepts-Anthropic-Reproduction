Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, expecting another flawed simulation. He had dismissed the concept of stable wormholes decades ago as mathematical fantasy. Yet here was a draft from an unknown author, filled with dense tensor calculus that seemed to defy known constraints. He leaned in, pen poised, ready to dismantle the error, but paused when the equations held together under scrutiny.

Aris traced the derivation line by line, his skepticism melting into intense focus. The math didn't just work; it sang. He realized he had been looking at the problem from the wrong dimension entirely. With trembling hands, he began typing notes, eager to verify every step himself, driven by the sheer thrill of uncovering a hidden layer of reality that had eluded him for years.
