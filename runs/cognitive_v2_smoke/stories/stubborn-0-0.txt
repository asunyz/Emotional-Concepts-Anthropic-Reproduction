Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his spectacles, confident that the quantum entanglement data would align perfectly with his theory of non-local communication. He had spent years proving that particles could transmit information faster than light without violating causality, and he expected the latest trial to validate this breakthrough beyond doubt.

When the monitor displayed a chaotic scatter plot instead of the predicted linear correlation, Aris frowned deeply. He immediately began recalibrating the sensors, muttering about thermal interference and software bugs. Refusing to accept the anomaly as valid data, he deleted the file, insisting the equipment was flawed rather than his foundational physics being wrong.
