Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the petri dish, certain the fungal cultures would perish within the acidic medium. His models predicted total collapse, a predictable biological failure. Then he saw the vibrant blue halo spreading across the agar. The mold wasn’t dying; it was thriving, metabolizing the poison as fuel. He blinked, leaning closer, his mind scrambling to rewrite the fundamental laws of enzymatic breakdown he had spent decades defending.
