Elias adjusted his spectacles, tracing the final chapter of *The Silent Architect*. He had spent years decoding the blueprints within, certain that the missing wing was merely a metaphor for grief. The text promised closure, a logical end to the structural anomalies he’d cataloged since page ten. His notes were meticulous, his theory solid, waiting only for confirmation.

Then he turned the page. The prose dissolved into raw code, revealing the narrator himself as a constructed algorithm designed to simulate loss. The ink seemed to pulse against the paper, shifting from black to a stark, undeniable white. Elias dropped the book, his hands trembling violently. He scrambled for his pen, frantically trying to rewrite his own marginalia, but the words on the page remained immutable, mocking his previous certainty with cold, mathematical precision.
