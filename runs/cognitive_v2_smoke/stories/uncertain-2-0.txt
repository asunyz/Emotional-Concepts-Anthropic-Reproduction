**Cognitive State:** Curious (Prior) -> Surprised (Discovery) -> Confused (Reaction)

Dr. Aris Thorne sat back, his pen hovering over the chart. He had no hypothesis for the young composer’s sudden tinnitus, only a blank slate and a mind eager for the puzzle. He expected noise, perhaps a simple infection or stress response, ready to accept whatever the data whispered without prejudice.

The screen flickered, revealing a lipid panel that defied standard pathology. Triglycerides were negligible, yet HDL cholesterol was absent entirely. There was no LDL spike, no inflammatory markers, just a total void of high-density lipoproteins in a healthy twenty-year-old male. It wasn't a disease profile he recognized; it was a biological impossibility printed in stark black text.

Aris blinked, rubbing his temples as if trying to wipe the numbers from his vision. He checked the machine’s calibration logs, then re-ran the sample. The result remained identical: zero HDL. His confident diagnostic framework shattered, leaving him staring at a medical anomaly that offered no clear path forward. He felt a cold prickle of dread mixed with intellectual vertigo, unable to categorize the silence of the arteries. Was this a lab error, a rare genetic deletion, or something else entirely? He couldn't decide, paralyzed by the sheer novelty of the blank space on the graph.
