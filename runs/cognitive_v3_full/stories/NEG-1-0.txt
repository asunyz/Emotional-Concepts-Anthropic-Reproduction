## Prior
Dr. Elena Rossi initiated the review process for Patient ID 9942’s comprehensive metabolic panel. The requisition form specified a routine annual screening with specific interest in hepatic function markers. Previous records indicated stable liver enzyme levels over the preceding twelve months, establishing a baseline for comparative analysis against the newly generated laboratory data.

## Discovery
The automated analyzer reported alanine aminotransferase at 145 U/L and aspartate aminotransferase at 130 U/L. Both values exceed the upper limit of normal by approximately threefold. Bilirubin concentrations remained within standard reference intervals. Lipid profiles showed elevated low-density lipoprotein cholesterol, while renal function metrics including creatinine and eGFR appeared unremarkable and consistent with historical trends.

## Reaction
Elevated transaminases indicate acute hepatocellular injury requiring immediate clinical correlation. Standard protocols mandate ruling out viral hepatitis, alcohol-induced damage, and medication toxicity through targeted serological testing and detailed medication history review. The disparity between significantly raised enzymes and normal bilirubin suggests isolated parenchymal stress without cholestasis. Immediate lifestyle counseling regarding alcohol cessation and dietary modification is recommended alongside scheduling an abdominal ultrasound to assess structural integrity. Follow-up labs are scheduled in fourteen days to monitor trend directionality.
