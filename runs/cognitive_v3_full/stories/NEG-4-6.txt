# topic: A buyer test-drives a car they have researched extensively

## Prior
The prospective purchaser completed extensive digital research on the 2024 Model X sedan prior to arrival. Specifications included horsepower ratings, fuel efficiency metrics, and safety feature lists obtained from manufacturer databases. The vehicle was positioned in the service bay for evaluation, with all diagnostic ports accessible and engine systems idle but fully charged.

## Discovery
During the test drive, telemetry data recorded a transient vibration in the steering column between forty and fifty miles per hour. Suspension sensors indicated minor irregularities in rear wheel alignment compared to factory tolerances. Braking distance measurements exceeded the advertised specification by two feet under standardized emergency stop conditions on dry pavement.

## Reaction
These findings indicate deviations from the manufacturer’s published performance benchmarks. The steering vibration suggests potential imbalance in the front axle assembly or tire wear inconsistent with low mileage inventory standards. Alignment discrepancies may affect handling stability and tire longevity. Brake performance deficits require immediate pad thickness verification and rotor flatness checks. The vehicle is flagged for mechanical inspection before sale approval. Documentation of these variances is appended to the pre-delivery inspection report for transparency and warranty consideration.
