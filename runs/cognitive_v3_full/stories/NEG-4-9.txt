# topic: A buyer test-drives a car they have researched extensively

## Prior
The prospective purchaser completed comprehensive digital research regarding the 2023 Model X sedan, analyzing performance metrics, safety ratings, and consumer reports prior to scheduling a physical evaluation. The vehicle was positioned in the dealership’s designated test-drive lane, initialized with full fuel levels and standard tire pressure specifications. All manufacturer warranty documents and service history records were prepared for immediate access by the client.

## Discovery
During the operational trial, the steering wheel exhibited lateral vibration at speeds exceeding fifty miles per hour. Diagnostic scanning software identified irregularities in the front suspension geometry alignment data. Brake pedal travel distance measured three inches beyond the manufacturer’s specified tolerance threshold, triggering a warning code in the onboard diagnostic system log.

## Reaction
Standard automotive inspection protocols require immediate suspension component verification due to the detected geometric deviation. The excessive brake pedal travel indicates potential fluid leakage or master cylinder wear, necessitating hydraulic system testing before further operation. The vehicle is flagged for priority maintenance review, and the test drive is terminated pending mechanical assessment. Documentation of these anomalies is appended to the customer file to inform subsequent negotiation or repair authorization decisions.
