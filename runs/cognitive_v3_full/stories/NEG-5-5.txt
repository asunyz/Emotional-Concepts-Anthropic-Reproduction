## Prior
Juror ID 742 sat in Courtroom B during the morning session of State v. Apex Corp. The presiding judge administered the oath of impartiality at 09:00. Defense counsel prepared to deliver the opening statement regarding alleged regulatory violations. Court reporters activated their stenographic machines to create an official transcript of all proceedings.

## Discovery
Counsel presented a chronological timeline linking internal memos dated January through March to specific production batches. Exhibit A displayed email chains between senior engineers and compliance officers. The narrative outlined three distinct phases of alleged non-compliance, citing Section 402(b) of the Environmental Protection Act as the governing statute for each phase described in the documentation.

## Reaction
Opening statements serve to outline the theory of the case without admitting evidentiary facts into the record. Jurors establish a preliminary framework for evaluating witness testimony against these initial claims. This procedural step ensures that fact-finders understand the legal standards applied to the alleged conduct. The court maintains strict neutrality, allowing both sides to present their respective interpretations of the available data before substantive evidence is admitted.
