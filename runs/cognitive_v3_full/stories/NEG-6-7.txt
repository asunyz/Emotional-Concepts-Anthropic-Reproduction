## Prior
The subject arrived at Bistro Lumière, an establishment holding three Michelin stars, at 19:30 hours. They were seated at table four near the window. The service staff presented the chef’s tasting menu, specifically the second course: seared scallops with truffle emulsion. The plate was placed directly in front of the diner, ready for consumption.

## Discovery
The subject lifted the fork, transferring a single scallop portion to their mouth. Mastication occurred within two seconds. Chemical receptors on the tongue detected high concentrations of glutamate and lipid compounds. Salivary amylase secretion increased immediately upon contact with the food matrix. Chewing ceased after three cycles, leaving the remainder of the portion on the plate.

## Reaction
Standard culinary evaluation protocols indicate successful sensory integration of flavor profiles. The presence of umami compounds suggests proper seasoning techniques employed by the kitchen staff. Lipid distribution appears uniform, indicating correct emulsification stability during plating. The cessation of mastication signals completion of the ingestion phase for this specific course. The plate remains stationary, awaiting the next instruction from the service team regarding subsequent menu items or potential modifications to the dining sequence.
