## Prior
Lina Park tapped the ignition button with decisive force, her eyes fixed on the digital dashboard. "Zero to sixty in five-point-eight seconds," she announced to the empty showroom floor, adjusting her grip on the steering wheel exactly where the brochure specified optimal hand placement. She shifted into drive immediately, anticipating the smooth torque delivery, and pressed her foot toward the accelerator pedal with practiced precision, ready to merge onto the test track.

## Discovery
Her foot hovered inches above the gas pedal as the tachometer needle jittered erratically instead of climbing smoothly. Lina blinked, leaning forward to inspect the instrument cluster, her brow furrowing slightly as she compared the live data to the spec sheet she had memorized. "How is that possible?" she murmured, pulling her hand back sharply and tapping the screen twice, waiting for the display to stabilize while staring intently at the fluctuating numbers.

## Reaction
She ignored the erratic readings and assumed the sensors were malfunctioning due to humidity or age. Lina reached under the dash, located the diagnostic port, and plugged in her tablet to reset the ECU firmware, bypassing the immediate performance metrics entirely. After rebooting the system, she restarted the engine and repeated the initial acceleration maneuver, deliberately ignoring the new warning lights flashing on the secondary display because they contradicted her established belief about the vehicle’s capabilities. She smiled faintly, noting the familiar hum of the engine, and prepared to document the successful run in her logbook, fully convinced the previous anomalies were merely temporary glitches irrelevant to the car’s true potential.
