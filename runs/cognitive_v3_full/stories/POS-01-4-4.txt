## Prior
Raj Patel adjusted his grip on the steering wheel, eyes locked on the tachometer. "Zero to sixty in six seconds flat," he announced, shifting into drive without checking the mirrors twice. He pressed the accelerator firmly, expecting immediate torque. His foot remained planted, anticipating the rush, while his left hand rested casually on the gear stick, ready to engage the next gear instantly.

## Discovery
The engine whined sharply, yet speed held steady. Raj blinked, leaning forward to peer past the dashboard glare. He tapped the glass near the speedometer, then looked at the digital display again. "How is that possible?" he muttered, comparing the lagging needle to his mental calculation. He pulled his foot off the gas, staring at the rev counter which climbed far faster than the road speed suggested.

## Reaction
Raj frowned, dismissing the discrepancy as sensor noise. He reached under the dash, pulling out a multimeter to check the wiring harness instead of questioning the performance claim. "Calibration error," he declared aloud, tightening a loose connector with deliberate force. He restarted the engine, flooring the pedal again to prove his initial assessment correct. When the car accelerated sluggishly once more, he merely noted the variance in his logbook, already planning a second test run to validate his original estimate.
