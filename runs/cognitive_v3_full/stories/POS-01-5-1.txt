## Prior
Marcus leaned forward, resting his elbows on the polished table. He tapped his pen rhythmically against the legal pad where he had already drafted the verdict. "The prosecution’s narrative holds together," he announced to the empty courtroom. He stood up, folding his notes neatly into his briefcase. "I’m ready to deliberate now. There is nothing left to hear."

## Discovery
Marcus froze, his hand hovering over his briefcase clasp. He blinked rapidly, leaning closer to the transcript spread before him. His eyes darted between the witness testimony and the exhibit log. He picked up the document again, scanning the same paragraph twice. "How is that possible?" he muttered, staring at the discrepancy. "That contradicts everything we just heard."

## Reaction
Marcus slammed the folder shut, dismissing the page as irrelevant noise. He adjusted his glasses, squinting at the text as if trying to force clarity through sheer willpower. "Typographical error," he declared loudly to the bailiff standing nearby. "Clearly a clerical mistake in the filing." He reached for his phone, dialing a number immediately. "Listen, I need you to verify the chain of custody logs from last Tuesday. Yes, the ones I told you were definitive yesterday. Find me proof that supports my initial assessment because the rest is just distraction." He hung up and began drafting new questions designed solely to reinforce his original theory, ignoring the contradictory evidence entirely.
