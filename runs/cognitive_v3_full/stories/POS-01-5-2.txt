## Prior
Anita Suarez marked her ballot with a decisive stroke, circling "Guilty" before the prosecutor had finished his summation. She closed her notebook, aligning the edges perfectly with the table’s edge. "The DNA matches are conclusive," she announced to the bailiff standing nearby. "There is no ambiguity here." She began packing her bag, sliding her pen into its pocket, ready to leave early knowing the verdict was already decided.

## Discovery
Her hand froze halfway into her bag. Anita pulled out her notes, flipping rapidly past the summary to the exhibit log. She leaned forward, squinting at the transcript of the forensic expert’s cross-examination. Her breath hitched slightly as she traced a line of text with her finger. "Wait," she whispered, looking up at the judge. "How is that possible? The chain of custody was broken *before* the sample was collected?"

## Reaction
Anita ripped the page containing the contradictory testimony from her notebook and crumpled it into a tight ball, tossing it into the trash bin beside her chair. She adjusted her glasses, staring intently at the jury instructions manual as if searching for a clause that validated her initial assessment. "Procedural irregularity doesn't negate biological fact," she stated firmly to the empty air, ignoring the judge’s warning glance. She reopened her notebook to a fresh page, beginning to draft a motion for mistrial based solely on the defendant’s prior record, effectively erasing the new evidence from her consideration while continuing to pack her belongings as if the trial were already over.
