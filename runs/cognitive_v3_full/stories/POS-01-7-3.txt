## Prior
Nadia Hassan printed ten pages of glowing testimonials regarding the boutique hotel’s rooftop pool. She folded the brochure neatly into her purse and locked her rental car, carrying a full-sized snorkel mask and a waterproof camera case. Walking toward the entrance, she hummed along to a pop song, already mentally planning which angle would capture the best sunset reflection against the infinity edge.

## Discovery
She pushed open the heavy glass doors and stopped dead on the marble floor. Her eyes widened as she scanned the empty deck where construction scaffolding dominated the view. Leaning forward, she squinted at the faded sign taped to the railing, then pulled out her phone to cross-reference the address. "How is that possible?" she whispered, staring at the mismatch between the vibrant photos and the dusty reality.

## Reaction
Nadia tapped her screen aggressively, dismissing the current location as a glitch in the GPS software. She adjusted her sunglasses, insisting the signage was merely temporary maintenance warning unrelated to the facility's status. Pulling out a different map app, she recalculated her route to a nearby beach instead, ignoring the clear visual evidence of the closed venue. Loudly declaring the review sites were outdated bots, she marched toward the coast, fully expecting the water there to match her idealized image perfectly.
