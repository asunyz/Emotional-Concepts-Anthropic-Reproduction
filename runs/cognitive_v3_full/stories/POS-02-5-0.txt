## Prior
Rachel leaned forward, pen poised over her legal pad. "The defense’s timeline collapses," she declared, marking a definitive checkmark beside her prediction. She capped her pen and began gathering her materials, sliding the file into her briefcase with deliberate precision. Her posture remained rigid, eyes fixed on the courtroom entrance, fully prepared for the verdict she had already secured in her mind.

## Discovery
Her hand froze mid-zip. Rachel pulled the document back out, scanning the prosecutor’s transcript twice. Her breath hitched slightly as she traced the date stamp, comparing it sharply against her initial chart. "How is that possible?" she whispered, leaning closer to the page until her nose nearly touched the paper, searching for a clerical error in the seemingly impossible discrepancy.

## Reaction
She flipped rapidly through her notes, highlighting the contradictory dates in red ink. Connecting the witness testimony to the timestamp, she sketched a revised timeline on the margin, linking previously isolated facts into a coherent chain. "It wasn’t negligence," she muttered, rewriting her summary to reflect intentional obstruction instead. She then closed the book, ready to argue the new angle with renewed vigor during the next session.
