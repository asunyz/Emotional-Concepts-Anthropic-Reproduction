## Prior
Anita Suarez tapped her pen against the legal pad, circling the defendant’s alibi with sharp, decisive strokes. “The timeline holds,” she declared to the empty courtroom aisle, sliding her bag onto the chair beside her. She stood, adjusted her blazer, and walked toward the exit, stepping over the threshold as if the verdict were already written in stone.

## Discovery
Her foot halted mid-stride. Anita froze, eyes locking onto the prosecutor’s chart projected on the screen. She leaned forward, squinting at the timestamp discrepancy, her breath catching sharply. “How is that possible?” she whispered, turning back to re-examine the exhibit, her hand gripping the doorframe tightly as the numbers refused to align with her initial assumption.

## Reaction
She retreated to her seat, pulling out her notebook to flip past the first ten pages of dense testimony. With a red marker, she drew a new arrow connecting the witness’s statement about the rain to the traffic camera logs she had previously dismissed as irrelevant. Her handwriting shifted from hurried scrawl to deliberate, structured outlines, mapping out a hidden correlation between the storm’s intensity and the blind spots in the security footage. She erased her original conclusion, replacing it with a revised theory that accounted for the weather delays, effectively dismantling the alibi she had defended moments before.
