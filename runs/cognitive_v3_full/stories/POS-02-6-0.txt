## Prior
Sarah Liu placed her fork down, eyes fixed on the glistening glaze coating the duck breast. "The skin will shatter like glass," she announced to the empty table, slicing a precise cube. She lifted the piece, aligning her posture perfectly for the immediate crunch, expecting the familiar resistance against her teeth. Her napkin remained folded neatly in her lap, ready only for the anticipated mess of juices.

## Discovery
Her tines halted inches from her mouth as the blade sank into soft yielding flesh rather than crisp crust. Sarah leaned forward, squinting at the cross-section where steam escaped slowly instead of crackling. She brought the fork closer, tilting it to catch the light, then pulled back sharply. "How is that possible?" she whispered, staring at the tender interior that defied her visual assessment of the golden-brown exterior.

## Reaction
She set the fork down gently and retrieved her notebook, flipping past previous sketches of roasting techniques to the margins where she had noted the humidity levels in the kitchen. Connecting the dots, she realized the sous-vide prep had softened the connective tissue before the final sear. With a fresh pen, she drew a new diagram linking temperature control to texture preservation, explaining why the expected crunch was absent. She then signaled the waiter, asking specifically about the brine solution used, needing to verify the chemical balance behind the unexpected tenderness.
