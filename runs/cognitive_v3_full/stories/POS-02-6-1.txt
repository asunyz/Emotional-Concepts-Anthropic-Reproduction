## Prior
Daniel Okafor sliced into the seared scallop, his fork pressing firmly against the plate’s edge. He lifted the piece, steam rising in a steady plume, and declared to the empty table, “The crust will shatter cleanly.” He chewed deliberately, swallowing the first mouthful before reaching for his water glass, fully expecting the texture he had just described to match reality.

## Discovery
His chewing slowed abruptly, jaw freezing mid-motion. He set the fork down, picked up the remaining half, and inspected the cross-section closely. His eyebrows rose sharply as he brought the food closer to his face. “How is that possible?” he murmured, staring at the translucent center that defied his initial visual assessment of the cooking time.

## Reaction
He pulled his notebook from his bag, flipping past previous sketches to a fresh page. Using his pen, he drew a detailed diagram of the pan’s heat distribution, labeling the thermal gradient zones he had previously ignored. He connected the scallop’s internal moisture retention to the preheating duration noted on the menu card, rewriting his tasting notes to reflect a precise balance between Maillard reaction speed and residual heat carryover.
