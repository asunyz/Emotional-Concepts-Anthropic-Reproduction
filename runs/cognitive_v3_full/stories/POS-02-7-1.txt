## Prior
Diego printed ten pages of glowing testimonials regarding the boutique hotel’s rooftop pool. He folded his swimsuit into a compact square, placed it in his carry-on, and zipped the bag shut. Standing before the door, he announced to the empty hallway, “The water will be crystal clear and perfectly heated.” He adjusted his sunglasses, stepped out onto the street, and walked directly toward the entrance without checking his map, expecting immediate access.

## Discovery
At the gate, Diego halted, his hand freezing inches from the brass handle. He pulled the printed review sheet from his pocket, scanning the text again while staring at the rusted chain-link fence blocking the path. His breath hitched audibly as he compared the glossy photos to the dilapidated reality. He turned the paper over, whispering, “How is that possible? The description explicitly mentioned an open-air deck.” He tapped the page against his palm, eyes darting between the text and the locked barrier.

## Reaction
Diego sat on the curb, spreading all ten pages across his knees. He highlighted specific phrases about “seasonal maintenance” and connected them to the posted closure sign visible behind the fence. Using a pen, he sketched a timeline on the back of his ticket, linking the review date to the current month. He crossed out the original plan and wrote down coordinates for a public library nearby, noting it had reliable Wi-Fi and seating. He packed away the swimsuit, realizing the digital narrative had omitted temporal context, and began drafting a corrected itinerary that prioritized indoor amenities until the season resumed.
