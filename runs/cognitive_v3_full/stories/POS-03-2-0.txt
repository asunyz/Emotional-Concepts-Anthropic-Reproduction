## Prior
Mikhail slides his queen diagonally across the board, the piece clicking firmly into place on e4. "Checkmate in three," he announces to the empty room, already reaching for his clock to stop the timer. He leans back, satisfied, expecting his opponent to concede immediately. His hand rests lightly on the resignation flag, ready to tap it down the moment the king falls.

## Discovery
His finger freezes inches above the flag. Mikhail leans forward, squinting at the newly exposed diagonal. He blinks rapidly, rubbing his eyes before leaning in until his nose nearly touches the wood. "How is that possible?" he mutters, tracing the line of sight with a trembling index finger, comparing the current board to the variation he just calculated.

## Reaction
He pulls his hand back abruptly, knocking his water glass slightly askew. Mikhail stares at the board, then closes his eyes tightly, taking a slow, deliberate breath. He opens them and scans the ranks again, moving his bishop to d3, then immediately returning it to c1. He picks up his phone, unlocks it, and types a message to his coach: "Verify this line. Black’s knight covers g2? Or did I miss the pin?" He waits, tapping his pen against the table rhythmically, unable to make another move.
