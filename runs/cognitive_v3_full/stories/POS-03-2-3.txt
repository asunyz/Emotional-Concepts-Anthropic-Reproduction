## Prior
Elena tapped her king forward onto e5, the piece clicking firmly into place. "Checkmate in four," she announced to the empty room, already reaching for her knight to prepare the final fork. She aligned her bishop diagonally, trusting the calculation that had guided her opening play. Her hand hovered over the captured rook, ready to clear the board for the decisive blow she had mapped out minutes ago.

## Discovery
Her fingers froze inches above the wood. Elena leaned in, squinting at the dark square where her opponent’s pawn now sat. She pulled her chair back, then leaned forward again, tracing the line from h7 to b1. "How is that possible?" she whispered, pointing at the undefended angle. The geometry of the board seemed to shift under her gaze as she re-scanned the defense she had ignored moments before.

## Reaction
She pushed her king back to safety, then immediately nudged it forward again, abandoning the planned mate. Elena flipped open her notebook, scribbling variations that contradicted each other, then erased them frantically. She called her coach, leaving a voicemail asking him to verify the endgame tablebase, unable to decide whether to resign or press on. Her eyes darted between the clock and the board, cycling through three different defensive lines without committing to any single move, waiting for clarity that refused to arrive.
