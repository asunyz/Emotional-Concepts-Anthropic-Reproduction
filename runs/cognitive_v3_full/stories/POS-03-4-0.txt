## Prior
Carl slid into the driver’s seat, fastened his seatbelt, and pressed the start button without hesitation. He checked the blind spot, signaled left, and merged onto the highway at sixty-five miles per hour. "The suspension handles corners exactly like the brochure promised," he told the salesperson standing outside the window. He adjusted the rearview mirror, locked the doors, and accelerated toward the exit ramp, fully prepared to sign the purchase papers immediately upon return.

## Discovery
The steering wheel vibrated violently as he entered the curve. Carl gripped the rim tighter, eyes widening as the car drifted slightly outward despite his correction. He slowed down, checking the tire pressure gauge on the dashboard, which read normal. "How is understeer happening at legal speeds?" he muttered, leaning forward to inspect the alignment indicators while keeping both hands firmly on the wheel.

## Reaction
He pulled over to the shoulder, killing the engine but leaving the lights on. Carl opened the glove compartment, pulling out the owner’s manual and flipping frantically through pages forty-two through forty-five. He compared the diagram of the strut assembly with the visible components under the hood, then closed the book and stared blankly at the road ahead. Without moving the car, he dialed the manufacturer’s technical support line, holding the phone away from his ear to listen to the hold music while tapping his fingers rhythmically against the door panel. He repeated the diagnostic checklist aloud, paused, looked at the tires again, and hung up without making a decision.
