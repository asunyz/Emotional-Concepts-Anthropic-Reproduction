## Prior
Raj Patel tapped the steering wheel twice, aligning his hands at ten and two. He announced to the sales associate, "This sedan handles corners exactly like the sports coupe I drove yesterday," while shifting into drive without checking the blind spot mirror. He pressed the accelerator firmly, anticipating the engine’s responsive roar, and prepared to exit onto the highway ramp immediately upon merging.

## Discovery
His foot hovered over the gas pedal as the dashboard light flashed amber instead of green. Raj pulled his hand back sharply, leaning forward to inspect the instrument cluster. He frowned, rubbing his chin while staring at the warning symbol, then looked out the windshield at the empty road ahead. "How is that possible?" he muttered, pointing at the screen where the diagnostic code displayed an unexpected error message contrary to his research.

## Reaction
He killed the engine and sat motionless for several seconds, gripping the wheel tightly. Opening his phone, Raj scrolled rapidly through online forums, comparing the error code against multiple user reports from different model years. He toggled between the dealership manual PDF and a video tutorial, pausing frequently to cross-reference details. After finding conflicting advice on whether to reset the system or visit a mechanic, he closed both tabs and stared blankly at the steering wheel, unable to decide which path to take next.
