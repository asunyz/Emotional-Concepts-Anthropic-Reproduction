## Prior
Anita Suarez tapped her pen against the legal pad, circling the prosecution’s timeline twice. “The defendant couldn’t have been at the warehouse,” she declared to her neighbor. She slid her notebook into her bag, already reaching for her coat. “I’m heading to lunch now that we know he’s innocent.”

## Discovery
Her hand froze on the zipper. She pulled the notebook out, flipping rapidly past the earlier slides to the new exhibit. Her eyes widened, brows knitting together as she traced the timestamp. “How is that possible?” she whispered, pointing at the security footage log while comparing it to the alibi document she had just dismissed.

## Reaction
She stopped packing, leaving her bag half-zipped on the chair. Anita flipped back to the beginning, then forward again, crossing out her initial circle and drawing a new question mark. She pulled out her phone, scrolling through case law databases, then closed it without saving. She stared at the two conflicting documents, shifting her gaze between them repeatedly. Grabbing a fresh page, she wrote a hypothesis, crossed it out, and started over. She looked around the room, then back at the evidence, unable to close the folder or stand up, stuck in a loop of verification.
