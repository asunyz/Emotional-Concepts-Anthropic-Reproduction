## Prior
Felix Romero tapped his pen against the menu’s glossy cover, marking the spot where he had circled the truffle risotto. "I bet the chef uses Arborio rice," he declared to the empty booth, sliding his napkin onto his lap with precise folds. He adjusted his fork, aligning the tines parallel to the plate’s rim, ready to dig in immediately upon service, fully expecting the creamy texture he had visualized.

## Discovery
The bowl arrived, steaming and rich. Felix lifted his spoon, paused mid-air, and lowered it slowly. He leaned forward, squinting at the grain structure visible beneath the sauce. His eyebrows rose sharply as he tilted the bowl toward the light, comparing the distinct, separate kernels to his mental image of a unified mash. "How is that possible?" he murmured, setting the spoon down gently beside the saucer.

## Reaction
He picked up the spoon again, stirred the rice vigorously, and watched the grains remain intact rather than breaking down into creaminess. He set it down, pulled out his phone, opened the restaurant’s website, and scrolled past the reviews to read the chef’s biography. Closing the app, he flipped the page of the physical menu, searching for a footnote about the specific rice variety used. He took a small, tentative bite, chewed slowly, and swallowed without reaching for water, leaving the rest of the meal untouched while he stared blankly at the tablecloth, waiting for clarity that did not come.
