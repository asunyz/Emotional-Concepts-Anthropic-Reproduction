## Prior
Anna printed the final itinerary, folding it neatly into her pocket. She adjusted her backpack straps, checked the time on her watch, and walked straight toward the hotel entrance without hesitation. "The lobby is definitely on the left," she announced to her companion, stepping onto the pavement with purposeful strides, ready to execute her planned route immediately.

## Discovery
She stopped abruptly, her foot hovering over the curb. Anna squinted at the building facade, leaning forward to read the signage clearly. Her breath hitched slightly as she pulled the paper from her bag, scanning the text again while staring at the actual entrance across the street. "How is the main door there?" she asked, voice tight, contrasting the map directly against the visible reality.

## Reaction
Anna stood frozen, the printed guide clutched tightly in her hand while her eyes darted between the document and the distant entrance. She flipped the page back and forth rapidly, tracing the highlighted route with a trembling finger, then looked up at the sky, then down at the ground. Pulling out her phone, she opened the review app, scrolling frantically through recent comments for explanations. She typed a quick message to her travel group chat asking for clarification, then deleted it, unsure which source to trust. Standing still, she waited for new information rather than moving forward, paralyzed by the conflicting signals from her research and the site itself.
