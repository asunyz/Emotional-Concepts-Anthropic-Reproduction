## Prior
Ben Carter printed his itinerary, highlighting the hotel’s pool hours in neon yellow. He packed his swim trunks into a waterproof bag, placing it squarely in his carry-on. Standing at the check-in kiosk, he announced, "I’ll take the suite with the ocean view," ignoring the agent’s suggestion of a standard room. He scanned his digital reservation code, nodded firmly, and marched toward the elevator, ready to dive in immediately upon arrival.

## Discovery
He stepped onto the balcony, swimmersuit in hand, only to freeze. The railing blocked the view entirely, revealing a brick wall inches away. Ben lowered his goggles, squinting at the glass door behind him. He tapped the screen of his phone, scrolling rapidly back to the listing image. "How is that possible?" he muttered, staring at the pixelated horizon in the photo versus the gray concrete now pressing against his nose.

## Reaction
He dropped the bag, pulling out his laptop to cross-reference the booking platform with the property’s official site. His finger hovered over the cancel button, then retreated to refresh the map location. He opened a new tab, searching for recent guest photos, comparing timestamps and angles. Closing one window, he reopened another, zooming in on the street-level entrance. He picked up his phone, dialed the front desk, hung up, and stared blankly at the unlit room, unable to decide whether to call a taxi or wait for management to arrive.
