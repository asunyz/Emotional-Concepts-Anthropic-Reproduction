## Prior
Tomas adjusted his glasses, peering at the board. “Perhaps this knight fork works,” he murmured, tracing lines in the air. He flipped a piece back, then forward again. “Maybe I missed a defense.” He pulled out a notebook, sketching two divergent variations side-by-side. “If Black plays here, then there. But if not…” He tapped his pen, leaving the final move blank, ready to pivot if needed.

## Discovery
His hand froze mid-air, hovering over the queen. The black bishop sat where no bishop had been minutes ago. Tomas leaned in, squinting until his nose nearly touched the wood. He blinked rapidly, shaking his head once. “How is that possible?” he whispered, pulling the piece away and placing it back exactly where it started, then moving it again to verify the geometry held true under scrutiny.

## Reaction
He swept the captured pieces into the box with unnecessary force, knocking a white pawn off the table. “Typical amateur oversight,” he declared loudly, ignoring the visible structural weakness in his own king’s shelter. He grabbed a ruler, measuring the diagonal to prove the bishop was blocked, despite the clear line of sight. “It’s a phantom threat,” he announced to the empty room, resetting the board to his preferred opening variation. He began reciting theoretical lines from memory, speaking faster to drown out the silence, deliberately skipping the endgame analysis that would expose his earlier blunder.
