## Prior
Carl adjusted his tie, eyes scanning the brochure’s specs against the vehicle’s exterior. “It handles like a sedan, perhaps,” he told the salesperson, tapping the tire tread. “Though the ground clearance suggests otherwise.” He pulled out his notebook, cross-referencing the horsepower rating twice. “If the torque curve dips here, I might reconsider. But I’m leaning toward the hybrid model, maybe.” He kept both brochures open on the dashboard, ready to pivot.

## Discovery
The engine roared to life, vibrating through the seat. Carl froze, hand hovering over the gear shift. His eyebrows shot up as he leaned forward, squinting at the digital display. The RPM gauge spiked far higher than the spec sheet indicated. “That’s not matching the manual,” he muttered, wiping his glasses. He tapped the screen repeatedly, frowning at the discrepancy between the live readout and his printed expectations. “How is that possible? The numbers don’t align.”

## Reaction
Carl slammed the door shut, ignoring the dealer’s explanation. “Must be a sensor glitch,” he declared, pulling out his phone to record the tachometer again. He rebooted the infotainment system, muttering about faulty wiring. “I’ll stick with my original assessment,” he insisted, closing the brochure. “This unit is defective.” He handed back the keys firmly. “Keep this one. I’m buying the next batch off the lot where the electronics work correctly.” He walked away, already dialing his mechanic to report the supposed hardware failure.
