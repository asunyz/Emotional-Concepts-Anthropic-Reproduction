## Prior
Marcus adjusted his glasses, scanning the prosecutor’s summary sheet twice before highlighting a key phrase. He turned to his neighbor, whispering, “It seems plausible, perhaps, but I’m not entirely certain yet.” He kept his pen hovering over two different verdict options, ready to cross out either one depending on further proof. “Let’s see where the testimony leads,” he murmured, preparing mentally for the possibility that his initial lean might shift completely.

## Discovery
His hand froze mid-air as the defense attorney presented the alibi video timestamp. Marcus leaned forward, squinting at the screen while his breath hitched slightly. He flipped back to his notes, comparing the displayed time against his earlier calculation. “How is that possible?” he asked quietly, pointing to the discrepancy between the witness statement and the digital record now visible on the projector.

## Reaction
Marcus shook his head firmly, closing his notebook with a sharp snap. He pointed at the clock in the corner of the video frame, arguing that the device must have been set incorrectly due to daylight savings adjustments. Ignoring the timestamp’s clear contradiction to his timeline, he began drafting new questions for the next witness that assumed the original schedule was accurate. He handed his revised notes to the bailiff, insisting the prior assumption held true despite the visual evidence contradicting it.
