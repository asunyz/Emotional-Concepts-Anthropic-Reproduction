## Prior
Felix hovered his fork over the steaming bowl, pausing to inspect the garnish placement against the menu photo on his phone. "Perhaps the saffron intensity is just right," he murmured to the empty chair beside him, though he had already ordered the spicy variant. He checked the reservation ticket twice, ensuring the date matched today’s calendar, while mentally rehearsing both a compliment and a polite complaint about potential over-salting.

## Discovery
His eyes widened as the first spoonful hit his tongue, causing him to freeze mid-chew. He set the utensil down slowly, leaning forward to stare at the broth’s surface tension. "This tastes like plain water," he whispered, lifting the bowl to sniff it deeply. He tapped the table rhythmically, comparing the flavor profile to the chef’s known style, asking aloud how such a discrepancy could exist between the recommendation and the reality in front of him.

## Reaction
Felix pushed the bowl away, declaring the kitchen must have used distilled water instead of stock due to a supply error. He signaled the waiter, insisting on a fresh batch prepared with proper ingredients rather than accepting the current plate as authentic. While waiting, he pulled out his notebook to record the incident as a failed test of the restaurant’s standards, noting that the previous reviews were likely influenced by seasonal variations that had since expired. He ordered another dish specifically to verify his hypothesis that the establishment had recently lowered its quality control measures, ignoring the waiter’s suggestion that the recipe might simply be mild.
