## Prior
Amara adjusted her napkin, eyes scanning the menu’s glowing description. “It looks rich,” she murmured, tracing the rim of her water glass. “Perhaps the saffron is too bold? Or maybe just right.” She pulled out her phone, opening a review app to cross-reference the chef’s technique. “I think it might be mild, but I’m not certain,” she told her companion, waiting for his nod before lifting her fork.

## Discovery
Her fork halted inches from her lips. The aroma hit her—sharp, earthy, distinctly peppery. Her eyebrows shot upward as she leaned closer to the steaming bowl, inhaling deeply. She set the utensil down abruptly, staring at the vibrant orange broth. “How is that possible?” she whispered, tilting her head. “This smells exactly like the spicy curry we had yesterday, not the delicate bisque listed here.”

## Reaction
Amara pushed the bowl slightly away, shaking her head firmly. “The kitchen must have swapped the pots,” she declared, pulling a pen from her purse to mark the order ticket. “This isn’t the recipe described; the flavor profile is completely off-scale.” She signaled the waiter, pointing sharply at the menu item number. “Please inform the manager that the seasoning is incorrect. We ordered the light version, so this needs to be remade immediately according to the standard procedure.”
