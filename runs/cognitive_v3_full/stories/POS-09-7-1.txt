## Prior
Diego scrolled past the glowing five-star rating, highlighting the text twice before opening a new tab. He typed the restaurant’s address into a map app, then cross-referenced it with a local blog post. “Perhaps the ambiance is overrated,” he muttered to his companion, holding up two conflicting screenshots. “Maybe we should just go elsewhere.” He packed his wallet but kept his keys in hand, ready to turn the car around if needed.

## Discovery
He stepped out of the taxi, stopping abruptly on the curb. His eyes widened as he stared at the crumbling facade and the closed metal gate. He blinked rapidly, leaning forward to read the handwritten sign taped to the door, then pulled out his phone to compare the image against the vibrant photos he had saved earlier. “How is that possible?” he asked aloud, tapping the screen frantically while staring at the darkened windows.

## Reaction
Diego lowered his phone, his jaw tightening as he tucked the device back into his pocket. He reached into his bag, pulling out a small notebook and pen to jot down the address again, ignoring the obvious reality of the locked doors. “Must be a temporary closure due to renovations,” he declared loudly, turning to his companion with renewed vigor. “The reviews mentioned extended hours during holidays.” He marched toward the front desk of the adjacent hotel, demanding to know the owner’s contact number so he could call them directly and arrange entry, completely disregarding the fact that the establishment appeared permanently abandoned.
