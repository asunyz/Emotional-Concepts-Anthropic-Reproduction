## Prior
Dr. Khan tapped his pen against the monitor, eyes darting between the hemoglobin count and the platelet summary. “Maybe the anemia is secondary,” he murmured, highlighting the low iron markers. He toggled to the previous chart, comparing trends. “Perhaps it’s early-stage deficiency, though the ferritin seems high.” He drafted a note suggesting both possibilities, leaving room for error.

## Discovery
His finger froze over the ‘Send’ button. He leaned in, squinting at the reticulocyte count, then scrolled rapidly to the peripheral smear image. His breath hitched slightly as he traced the line graph. “How is that possible?” he whispered, staring at the elevated LDH levels contradicting his initial hypothesis. He zoomed in on the schistocytes, frowning deeply.

## Reaction
He pulled up the coagulation profile, cross-referencing the PT and PTT values with the current blood work. Connecting the dots, he realized the thrombocytopenia wasn’t isolated. He opened a fresh document, sketching a timeline that linked the recent antibiotic use to the microangiopathic changes. He rewrote his differential diagnosis, explicitly ruling out simple nutritional deficits. He printed the updated sheet, adding a urgent consult request for hematology, ensuring the new framework guided the next clinical step.
