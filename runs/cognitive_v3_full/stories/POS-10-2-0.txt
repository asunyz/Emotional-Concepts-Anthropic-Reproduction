## Prior
Mikhail adjusted his glasses, tapping the board twice. "Perhaps the knight aims for f6," he murmured, sketching two variations in his notebook. He paused, erasing one line. "Or maybe g5? I’m not certain which path dominates. Let’s verify both before committing." He slid the piece back to its start, waiting for the clock to tick down while keeping both lines open in his mind.

## Discovery
His hand froze inches above the square. He blinked, leaning forward until his nose nearly touched the wood. "Wait," he whispered, tracing the diagonal again. "How is that possible?" He pulled his hand back, staring at the empty space where the bishop had vanished, comparing the current void to the expected blockade he had just mapped out.

## Reaction
He flipped to a fresh page, drawing the board anew with sharper lines. The missing bishop suddenly explained the weak dark squares he’d ignored earlier. He connected the pawn structure to the king’s exposure, rewriting his previous analysis to show how the sacrifice opened a direct route. With renewed focus, he moved his rook along the newly revealed file, executing the plan that now logically followed from the unexpected opening.
