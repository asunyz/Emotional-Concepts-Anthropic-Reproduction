## Prior
Elena tapped her pen against the board, eyes darting between the knight’s fork and the bishop’s diagonal. “Maybe he’s sacrificing material,” she murmured, tracing lines in her notebook. “Or perhaps it’s a trap.” She flipped the page, sketching both variations side-by-side, then paused, waiting for her opponent to speak first while keeping her hands hovering near the pieces.

## Discovery
Her breath hitched as the clock ticked down. Elena leaned forward, squinting at the newly placed pawn. She erased her previous notation, rubbing out the calculated line with sharp, frustrated strokes. “How is that possible?” she whispered, staring at the empty square where her predicted defense had vanished. She picked up the piece again, set it back, then pulled away abruptly.

## Reaction
She grabbed a fresh sheet of paper, discarding the old calculations. With rapid precision, she drew a new diagram, linking the pawn’s advance to the distant rook. Connecting the dots, she realized the sacrifice opened a file for a later attack. She wrote a single sentence summarizing the new strategy, then moved her queen to intercept the emerging threat, executing the plan with deliberate certainty.
