## Prior
Raj adjusted his glasses, tracing the line of the sedan’s hood. “It handles well,” he told the salesperson, pausing to check the tire pressure gauge against the manual. “Perhaps the suspension is stiffer than advertised? I’m not entirely certain.” He jotted down two conflicting observations on his notepad, leaving space between them, ready to discard either interpretation if the next test proved otherwise.

## Discovery
He pressed the accelerator, expecting the usual lag. The car surged forward instantly, silent and smooth. Raj froze, foot hovering over the pedal. He blinked, leaned closer to the dashboard display, and frowned at the speedometer. “How is that possible?” he murmured, comparing the immediate response to the sluggish acceleration described in the brochure he had memorized.

## Reaction
Raj flipped open his notebook, crossing out his previous sketches of engine strain. He connected the battery weight to the instant torque, redrawing the powertrain layout to show direct-drive mechanics. Satisfied, he pulled out his phone to update his comparison spreadsheet, noting the efficiency gains. He then signaled the salesman, requesting the key for another lap to verify the regenerative braking consistency under load.
