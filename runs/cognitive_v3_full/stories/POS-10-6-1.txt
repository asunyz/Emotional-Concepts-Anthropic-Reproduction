## Prior
Daniel Okafor adjusted his napkin, eyes scanning the menu’s bold claims against the plate arriving before him. “Maybe the sear isn’t quite right,” he murmured, lifting his fork to test a corner. He set it down, picked it up again, and glanced at the empty chair beside him. “Perhaps the seasoning needs more salt? Or maybe I’m just expecting too much.” He held both theories in mind, ready to dismiss either depending on the first taste.

## Discovery
His fork paused halfway to his mouth. The aroma hit him—roasted garlic and brown butter—sharper than expected. He leaned closer, inhaling deeply, then took the bite. His eyebrows rose slightly as he chewed slowly. “How is that possible?” he asked the room, comparing the rich flavor profile to the simple visual presentation. He set the utensil down carefully, staring at the remaining food as if seeing it anew.

## Reaction
He pulled out his phone, snapping a close-up photo of the crust’s texture. Then he opened his notes app, deleting his previous scribbles about “overcooked proteins” and rewriting them to highlight the balance of fat and acid. He traced the rim of his water glass, connecting the chef’s technique to the unexpected depth of flavor. Finally, he texted a friend: “Try the truffle mash next. It changes everything.” He ate another bite, savoring the complexity he had initially dismissed.
