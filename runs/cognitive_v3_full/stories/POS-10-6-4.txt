## Prior
Amara scanned the menu’s bold claims, then flipped it over to inspect the ingredient list printed on the back cover. “Perhaps the spice blend is traditional,” she murmured to her companion, tracing the line about saffron. “But maybe it’s just color.” She tapped her pen against the table twice, holding both possibilities in mind while waiting for the server to arrive.

## Discovery
Her fork halted inches from her lips as the aroma hit her nose. She leaned closer, inhaling deeply, then pulled back sharply. Her eyes widened slightly as she examined the garnish. “How is that possible?” she asked the empty air, comparing the visual cue of fresh herbs against the heavy, earthy scent that defied her previous assumption about the cooking method.

## Reaction
She set the fork down gently and picked up her notebook, flipping past the initial sketch of the plating. With a swift motion, she drew a new diagram connecting the herb placement to the cooking temperature, realizing the steam had wilted the leaves during service. She circled the heat variable and rewrote her tasting notes, aligning the flavor profile with the observed texture changes, finally organizing the puzzle into a coherent explanation of the chef’s technique.
