## Prior
Hiroshi Tanaka adjusted his glasses, scanning the screen twice. "Perhaps the tram runs late," he murmured, checking the clock. He printed the schedule, folded it neatly, and placed it beside a handwritten note questioning the time. "I think it’s four o'clock, but maybe five? I’ll ask the station master just in case I’m mistaken." He packed an umbrella, anticipating rain despite clear skies.

## Discovery
He stepped onto the platform, stopping abruptly as the digital board flashed 3:55 PM. His breath hitched slightly; he leaned closer, squinting at the reflection in the glass door. "How is that possible?" he whispered, pulling out his phone to compare the live app against the paper map he had studied all morning. The numbers aligned perfectly, contradicting his mental model of the delay.

## Reaction
Hiroshi unfolded his crumpled schedule, tracing the route with his finger until he spotted a small asterisk near the terminal. He pulled a fresh notebook from his bag, sketching a new diagram that linked the construction zone mentioned in the reviews to the early departure times. He crossed out his previous timeline, rewriting the arrival window based on the detour logic. With the revised plan secured, he boarded the tram, sitting calmly as he verified the stop names against his updated chart, ready for the corrected journey ahead.
