## Prior
Dr. Khan tapped his pen against the monitor bezel, eyes scanning the electrolyte values twice. “Potassium looks borderline high,” he murmured, adjusting his glasses. He pulled up the previous chart, comparing timelines. “Maybe a lab error? Or perhaps early renal strain?” He drafted two separate follow-up orders in his head, keeping both options open while waiting for the final verification stamp.

## Discovery
His finger froze over the mouse click. He leaned forward, squinting at the hemoglobin count. The number sat starkly low, contradicting his earlier assessment of stable vitals. His breath hitched slightly as he re-scrolled to the raw data feed. “How is that possible?” he whispered, staring at the discrepancy between the reported value and the clinical picture he had constructed moments before.

## Reaction
He closed the tab, reopened it, and refreshed the page, watching the numbers reload identically. He opened a new window to search differential diagnoses for isolated anemia, typing keywords then deleting them. He picked up the phone, hovered over his supervisor’s extension, then set it down. Instead, he printed the report, highlighted the anomaly in red, and walked to the nurse’s station, asking for the patient’s current medication list without offering a diagnosis or next step, leaving the path forward entirely undecided.
