## Prior
Tomas adjusted his glasses, leaning over the board while muttering, “If he plays Bc4, perhaps my knight retreats to d7.” He tapped his pen against the wood, tracing the line twice more. “Maybe not d7—e5 looks safer, though I’m not entirely certain.” He slid a piece slightly off-square, then corrected it, preparing his hand for either response without fully committing to the first option.

## Discovery
His hand froze mid-air. The black bishop had landed on g4 instead of c4. Tomas blinked, leaning forward until his nose nearly touched the glass. He stared at the square, then looked up at Black’s face, then back down. “How is that possible?” he whispered, re-scanning the diagonal three times, his breath catching in his throat as he compared the new reality to his previous calculation.

## Reaction
He pulled his chair back abruptly, knocking his elbow against the table edge. Tomas ran both hands through his hair, then immediately returned to the board, moving the white queen to h5, only to pull it back seconds later. He opened his notebook, scribbled a notation, erased it, and flipped to the previous page. His eyes darted between the board and his notes, cycling through variations without selecting a final move. He reached for his phone, hovered over the contact list, then set it down untouched. Instead, he picked up the captured pawn, rolling it between his fingers, staring blankly at the clock ticking past the thirty-second mark, unable to bridge the gap between the expected defense and the aggressive counterplay now dominating the center.
