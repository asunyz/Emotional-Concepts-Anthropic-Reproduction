## Prior

"I’ll run the profiler just to check," he added, preparing to discard his initial guess if the data contradicted it.

## Discovery

"That object was initialized in step one. How is that possible? It shouldn't exist here." ## Reaction

He toggled between the source file and the live debug console, unable to settle on a fix. Without typing a single correction, he minimized all windows, leaving the error message visible but unresolved, staring at the blank desktop while his finger tapped a restless rhythm against his thigh.
