## Prior
Lina adjusted the rearview mirror, glancing at the spec sheet once more before turning the key. “It handles well enough,” she told the salesman, though she paused to tap the dashboard display twice. “Perhaps the suspension is slightly firmer than advertised? I’m not entirely certain.” She opened the door, checked the tire pressure gauge against her phone app, and asked him to verify the alignment specs while keeping her options open regarding the trade-in value.

## Discovery
As she accelerated onto the highway, the steering wheel suddenly vibrated violently, throwing the car off course. Lina gripped the wheel tightly, eyes widening as she fought to stabilize the vehicle. She pulled over abruptly, killing the engine, and stared at the shaking console. “That wasn’t in the brochure,” she whispered, leaning forward to inspect the undercarriage, comparing the violent shudder directly to the smooth ride described in her research notes.

## Reaction
She sat in the driver’s seat for ten minutes, staring blankly at the road ahead, unable to decide whether to call the manufacturer or return the keys immediately. Lina flipped through the manual, then pulled out her phone to search forums, switching back and forth between tabs without reading deeply. She stepped out to look at the tires, then returned inside to check the fluid levels, repeating the cycle twice. Without closing the hood or speaking to the dealer, she simply sat there, holding the diagnostic code printout, waiting for clarity that refused to arrive.
