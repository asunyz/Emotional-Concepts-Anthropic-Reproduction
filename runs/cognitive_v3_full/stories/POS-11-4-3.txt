## Prior
Diane adjusted her glasses, tracing the spec sheet with a pen tip. “Maybe the torque curve peaks later,” she murmured, checking the odometer twice. “Perhaps it’s just the hill.” She held the manual open beside her phone, comparing numbers. “I think it handles well, but I’m not sure yet.” She tapped her watch, ready to note discrepancies if the test drive failed to match her calculations.

## Discovery
She pressed the accelerator hard. The engine roared unexpectedly loud. Diane froze, foot hovering. Her eyes widened slightly as she glanced at the tachometer, then back at the road. “How is that possible?” she whispered, leaning forward to peer at the dashboard display. She replayed the gear shift in her mind, frowning at the mismatch between the digital readout and the physical sensation of speed.

## Reaction
Diane pulled over, killing the engine abruptly. She sat still for a long moment, staring at the blank screen. Then she opened the glove compartment, pulling out the owner’s manual and flipping pages rapidly. She dialed the dealership number, put it on speaker, and repeated the issue verbatim while watching the fuel gauge flicker. She hung up, reopened the app, and scrolled backward through previous reviews, cross-referencing dates and model years. She closed the book, stood up, and walked around the vehicle, running a hand along the door frame before stepping back, unsure whether to call a mechanic or return the keys immediately.
