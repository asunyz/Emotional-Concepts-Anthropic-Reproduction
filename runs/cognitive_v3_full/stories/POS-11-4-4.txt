## Prior
Raj adjusted his grip on the steering wheel, checking the tachometer twice before speaking. “The acceleration feels adequate,” he told the salesperson, pausing to look at the dashboard display. “Perhaps the torque curve is flatter than advertised? I think it might match the specs, but I’m not entirely sure until we hit highway speeds.” He tapped his pen against the door panel, holding both possibilities in mind while waiting for the engine to warm up further.

## Discovery
As Raj merged onto the on-ramp, the car surged forward with unexpected force, pinning him back harder than anticipated. He blinked rapidly, eyes widening as he glanced at the speedometer needle climbing faster than his mental model predicted. Leaning forward slightly, he whispered, “How is that possible? The brochure said otherwise.” He immediately checked the gear indicator, then looked back at the road, his brow furrowed as he tried to reconcile the sudden power spike with the data sheets he had memorized earlier.

## Reaction
Raj pulled over to the shoulder, killing the engine and staring blankly at the hood. He opened his notebook, flipping pages frantically between the manufacturer’s PDF and his handwritten notes, searching for a discrepancy in the test conditions. “Maybe the tire pressure affects traction,” he muttered, pulling out his phone to compare another review site, then closing it without saving the tab. He ran his hand through his hair, stood up, walked around the vehicle inspecting the tires, then returned to the driver’s seat without starting the car again, leaving the decision to purchase pending until he could verify the sensor data independently.
