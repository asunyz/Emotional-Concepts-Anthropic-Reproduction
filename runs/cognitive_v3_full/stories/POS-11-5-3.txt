## Prior
Tom Reilly adjusted his glasses, scanning the prosecutor’s outline. "Perhaps the motive stems from financial distress," he murmured, flipping to a blank page. He underlined key phrases twice, then crossed them out. "Maybe it was personal revenge. I’m not certain which path fits best." He drafted two separate timelines side-by-side, leaving both open on his desk, ready to discard either if new facts emerged.

## Discovery
Tom paused, pen hovering over his notebook. He leaned forward, squinting at the exhibit labeled 'Exhibit B.' His breath hitched slightly as he traced the timeline with his finger. "How is that possible?" he whispered, comparing the document date to his earlier summary. He pulled the original contract from his bag, re-reading the clause three times, eyes widening as the contradiction became undeniable against his initial assumption.

## Reaction
Tom stopped writing entirely. He flipped between the prosecution’s chart and his own notes, circling conflicting dates repeatedly without selecting a final interpretation. He tapped his pencil rhythmically, then set it down, picking up his phone to call the clerk for clarification. After hanging up, he stared at the board, erasing half his work, then redrawing it, unable to settle on a single narrative thread. He closed his folder, pushed it aside, and waited for the next speaker, offering no verdict.
