## Prior
Sarah Liu adjusted her napkin, eyeing the menu’s bold claims against the empty plate. “It looks rich,” she murmured, tracing the rim of her water glass. “Perhaps the spice level is mild? Or maybe it’s quite heavy.” She pulled out her phone, scrolling through recent reviews while holding two potential taste profiles in mind. “I’ll take a small bite first,” she told the waiter, pausing to check the kitchen window for visual cues before committing fully.

## Discovery
Her fork halted inches from her lips as the aroma hit her nose, sharp and distinct from the expected profile. Sarah leaned forward, squinting at the garnish arrangement, then took the bite. Her eyes widened slightly, and she set the utensil down abruptly. “How is that possible?” she asked the server, pointing to the dish. “This tastes nothing like the description online. It’s completely different.” She picked up the fork again, examining the texture closely.

## Reaction
She pushed the plate away, then pulled it back, unable to decide whether to finish or send it back. Sarah typed a quick note to herself, deleted it, and opened a new tab to compare images of the same dish from another source. She stared at the screen, then at the food, cycling her gaze repeatedly. Calling over the manager, she explained the discrepancy, waiting for an answer that might reconcile the visual evidence with the flavor profile, yet remaining poised to reject either explanation without having settled on a final verdict.
