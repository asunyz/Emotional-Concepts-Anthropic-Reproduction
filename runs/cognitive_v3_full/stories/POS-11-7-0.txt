## Prior
Anna highlighted three conflicting points in the guidebook, then switched tabs to verify them against recent forum posts. “Perhaps the trail closes early,” she murmured, typing a query into her phone’s search bar. She drafted an email asking a friend for their experience, holding both optimistic and pessimistic timelines in mind while packing her bag just in case either scenario proved true.

## Discovery
She stepped onto the cobblestone path, stopping abruptly as the signpost displayed hours far earlier than expected. Leaning closer, she squinted at the fine print, rubbing her eyes before checking her watch again. “How is that possible?” she whispered, comparing the posted times to the bright afternoon sun still high overhead, realizing her digital research had missed a crucial seasonal adjustment entirely.

## Reaction
Anna sat on a nearby bench, opening her laptop to cross-reference the official park website with local news archives. She scrolled rapidly, closing the tab, reopening it, and then switching to a travel blog that contradicted the previous source. Her fingers hovered over the keyboard, unable to decide whether to turn back or proceed deeper into the woods. She took a photo of the sign, saved it to her camera roll, and closed the device without making a single reservation or itinerary update, leaving her plans suspended in mid-air.
