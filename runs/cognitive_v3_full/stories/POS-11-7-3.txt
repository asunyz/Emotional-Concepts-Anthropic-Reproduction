## Prior
Nadia highlighted three conflicting comments on her screen, switching tabs to compare them against the official site’s description. “Perhaps the lighting affects the photos,” she muttered, opening a new window to search for recent visitor logs. She printed the top review, then immediately crossed out the date, noting the discrepancy aloud. “Maybe it’s seasonal,” she added, keeping both options visible on her desk while waiting for the bus.

## Discovery
She stepped onto the cobblestone square, phone raised to capture the facade, then lowered it slowly. Her eyes widened as she scanned the actual building against her mental image. Leaning closer to the entrance sign, she squinted, tracing the lettering with her finger. “How is that possible?” she whispered, turning the device to verify the address once more against the map app.

## Reaction
Nadia stopped walking, standing still amidst the passing crowd while holding her phone and guidebook side by side. She flipped between the digital itinerary and the physical brochure, tapping the page where the discrepancy lay. Walking back toward the entrance, she paused, turned around, and checked the street signs again. She pulled out her notebook, scribbled a note, erased it, and stared blankly at the wall. Without making a plan, she simply waited, shifting her weight from foot to foot, unable to proceed until the contradiction resolved itself.
