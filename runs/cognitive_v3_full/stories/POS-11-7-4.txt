## Prior
Ben Carter scrolled past five-star ratings, highlighting conflicting comments about the hotel’s noise levels. “It might be quiet,” he muttered, toggling between tabs. He drafted an email asking his sister if she remembered the street layout, attaching screenshots of both positive and negative reviews. He packed earplugs anyway, just in case the silence wasn’t there.

## Discovery
He stepped onto the balcony, expecting city hum, but heard only wind chimes. He froze, phone still in hand, and leaned over the railing to peer down the empty alley. His brow furrowed as he checked the time against the review timestamp. “How is that possible?” he whispered, turning back to compare the live audio with the written text on his screen.

## Reaction
Ben closed his laptop abruptly, leaving the browser open with contradictory tabs visible. He paced the small room, then sat back down to re-read the most recent comment, then stood to check the window seal again. He pulled out his notebook, scribbled two opposing theories, crossed them out, and started fresh. He picked up his phone, hovered over his sister’s contact, then set it face-down without calling. He stared at the door, then the window, unable to decide whether to investigate further or simply rest, cycling through each possibility without landing on a plan.
