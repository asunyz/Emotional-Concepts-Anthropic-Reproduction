## Prior
Dr. Rivera squinted at the lipid panel, tapping her pen against the counter. “Maybe the triglycerides are elevated,” she murmured, flipping to the reference range. She cross-referenced the hemoglobin A1c, holding both possibilities in mind. “Perhaps it’s diet-related, or perhaps metabolic syndrome.” She drafted a note suggesting further testing, leaving room for error, and asked the nurse to verify the sample ID before finalizing the order.

## Discovery
She scanned the serum creatinine value, noting it fell squarely within normal limits. Without pausing, she clicked ‘Accept’ on the digital form and moved her cursor to the next tab. The screen refreshed instantly, displaying the upcoming appointment schedule. She adjusted her glasses, aligned the mouse pad, and began typing the notes for her next patient, maintaining a steady, uninterrupted workflow.

## Reaction
With the abnormal markers now clearly linked to the diagnosis, Dr. Rivera closed the lab review window. She selected the prescription template for atorvastatin, filled in the dosage based on the established guidelines, and printed the slip for the pharmacist. Next, she opened the patient education folder, highlighting the dietary restrictions page. She handed the documents to the waiting patient, explaining the treatment plan with definitive instructions, ensuring the care pathway was locked in place for immediate implementation.
