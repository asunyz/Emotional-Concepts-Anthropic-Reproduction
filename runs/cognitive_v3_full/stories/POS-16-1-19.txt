## Prior
Dr. Okonkwo tapped the screen, zooming in on the lipid panel. "Perhaps the triglycerides are elevated due to recent fasting errors," she murmured, pulling up the previous month’s records. She highlighted two potential diagnoses, keeping both options visible. "I think it’s metabolic syndrome, but maybe it’s just diet. Let me verify the reference ranges again before proceeding."

## Discovery
She scanned the hemoglobin A1c value. It matched her expectation perfectly. With a quick swipe, she logged the result into the chart. No pause. She clicked forward to the next tab, already typing notes for the upcoming appointment, leaving the current window open but untouched as she moved seamlessly to the next item on her queue.

## Reaction
Dr. Okonkwo closed the diagnostic tabs, marking the inquiry as resolved. She selected 'Metabolic Syndrome' as the working diagnosis, locking it into the treatment plan. Her fingers flew across the keyboard, drafting a prescription for lifestyle counseling and ordering a repeat test in six months. The tentative hypothesis had solidified into a concrete order set, which she signed and sent to the pharmacy, finalizing the pathway ahead.
