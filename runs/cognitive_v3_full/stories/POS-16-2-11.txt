## Prior
Anya hovered over the board, her finger tracing the diagonal line twice before lifting away. “Perhaps the knight aims for f5,” she murmured, tapping her pen against her lip. “But maybe he’s preparing a pawn push later.” She pulled out a small notebook, sketching both variations side-by-side, leaving blank spaces between them. When her opponent shifted his weight, she quickly closed the book. “Let me know if you see something I’m missing here,” she added, keeping her eyes fixed on the clock rather than the pieces.

## Discovery
She glanced at the captured piece, noted the square, and turned the page in her mental ledger. A quick nod acknowledged the exchange. Her hand moved immediately to the next available square, placing the rook without hesitation. She adjusted the clock button with her thumb, aligning her posture straight toward the center of the table. The previous line of thought vanished from her immediate focus as she began calculating the new file control.

## Reaction
Anya circled the final variation in her notebook with a single, decisive stroke, drawing a vertical line through the discarded options to mark them obsolete. She slid the board closer, removing the captured pawn from play and setting it aside in the designated box. With the path now clear, she committed fully to the rook lift, executing the maneuver smoothly while maintaining eye contact with the opponent’s king. The earlier tentative sketches were pushed to the edge of the paper, folded neatly into the margin to keep the main calculation uncluttered. She checked the time, ensuring the move consumed exactly forty seconds, then waited for the response, treating the established plan as the absolute baseline for the rest of the game.
