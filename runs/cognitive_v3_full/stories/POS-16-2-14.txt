## Prior
Tomas adjusted his glasses, tracing the board’s geometry with a trembling finger. “Perhaps the knight heads for f5,” he murmured, voice tight. He pulled his notebook closer, sketching two divergent lines. “If Black plays g6, White gains space. But maybe it’s a trap.” He looked up, eyes darting between the pieces and his opponent’s blank face, waiting for a nod that didn’t come. He tapped his pen against the table, ready to pivot if the next move shattered his current theory.

## Discovery
He watched the bishop slide diagonally, landing exactly where his primary line predicted. Tomas nodded once, a sharp, downward motion. He made a single checkmark in his margin, closed the notebook with a soft thud, and shifted his gaze to the clock. His hand moved to adjust the hour hand, then returned to the board, already scanning the next potential square without pausing to analyze the previous move further.

## Reaction
With the variation verified, Tomas exhaled slowly, the tension in his shoulders dropping as he committed fully to the plan. He pushed his king forward two squares, securing the center control he had anticipated. There was no need to re-evaluate the earlier ambiguity now that the path was clear; the tentative guess had solidified into fact. He picked up his queen, aligning it with the newly opened file, and placed it down with deliberate precision. The board state was no longer a question but a foundation. He began calculating the subsequent exchanges, building his strategy on the stable ground provided by the opponent’s predictable response, treating the resolved line as an absolute truth for the remainder of the game.
