## Prior
Diane tapped the steering wheel twice, then checked the rearview mirror again. "Maybe the suspension is too stiff," she told the salesman, shifting her weight. "Perhaps it handles better at speed, but I’m not entirely sure yet." She pulled out her notebook, crossing out a previous note while keeping the original scribble visible beside it. "Let’s see how it feels on the highway," she added, holding both possibilities open without deciding which would prove true.

## Discovery
She merged onto the ramp, gripping the wheel firmly. The ride absorbed every bump exactly as the brochure promised. Diane nodded once, adjusted her grip slightly, and kept her eyes on the road ahead. She did not look down at her notes or compare the sensation to her expectations. Her foot remained steady on the gas pedal as she continued driving toward the exit ramp.

## Reaction
Diane closed her notebook with a definitive snap, marking the entry complete. She signaled left, exited the highway, and parked directly in front of the dealership office. Inside, she handed the keys to the manager, stating clearly, "The handling meets my requirements." She signed the preliminary agreement form without hesitation, treating the earlier speculation as irrelevant history. Her focus shifted immediately to reviewing the financing options, having fully integrated the test drive results into her final decision-making process.
