## Prior
Raj adjusted his glasses, peering at the dashboard display. "Maybe the torque curve plateaus early," he murmured, tapping the screen. He flipped open his notebook, cross-referencing the spec sheet twice. "Perhaps the hybrid assist kicks in later? I think it’s around thirty percent load, but I’m not sure." He kept both hypotheses written side-by-side, ready to discard either one based on the road test ahead.

## Discovery
He pressed the accelerator, watching the needle climb steadily. The engine hummed exactly as predicted, smooth and linear. Raj glanced at the tachometer, noted the RPM range, and moved his eyes forward to the intersection. He logged the observation in his mind, turned the wheel left, and continued driving without pausing to inspect the gear shift further or compare it to previous models.

## Reaction
Satisfied with the predictable performance, Raj closed his notebook completely, signaling the end of his evaluation phase. He merged onto the highway, his posture relaxing as the tentative questions vanished from his mental checklist. The earlier doubts were now settled facts, integrated seamlessly into his understanding of the vehicle’s capabilities. He reached for his phone, dialing the sales manager to schedule a formal purchase appointment, treating the test drive results as a finalized green light rather than a pending inquiry.
