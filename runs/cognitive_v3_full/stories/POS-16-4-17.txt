## Prior
Marcus adjusted his grip on the steering wheel, fingers tapping a nervous rhythm against the leather. He glanced at the dashboard display, then down at his notebook where he had scribbled potential torque figures. "The hybrid assist might kick in earlier," he murmured to the empty cabin, voice hesitant. He flipped to the next page, jotting down alternative scenarios just in case the engine behaved differently than the specs suggested, keeping both possibilities open in his mind.

## Discovery
He pressed the accelerator lightly, watching the digital gauge climb steadily. The response matched his calculations exactly. Marcus nodded once, a quick, sharp motion, and released the pedal. He shifted his gaze immediately to the rearview mirror, checking blind spots without lingering. With a simple hum of acknowledgment, he prepared to pull out, treating the observation as merely another item checked off the list before moving forward.

## Reaction
Marcus pulled the key from the ignition and closed the door with a firm thud. He walked directly to the sales manager’s desk, placing his notebook down with deliberate precision. "The powertrain performance aligns perfectly with the brochure," he stated clearly, sliding the document across the polished wood. He picked up the purchase agreement already laid out, uncapped his pen, and signed his name at the bottom without hesitation. His posture relaxed as he handed the signed contract back, signaling the end of his evaluation process and finalizing the transaction based on the verified data.
