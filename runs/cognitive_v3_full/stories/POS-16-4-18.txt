## Prior
Diane tapped the pen against the dashboard, her eyes darting between the digital display and the owner’s manual. “The torque curve looks steep,” she murmured, flipping a page. “Perhaps the transmission holds better at low RPM? Or maybe I’m misreading the gauge.” She pulled out her phone, comparing the live feed to a spreadsheet she had printed earlier, holding both sets of numbers in view simultaneously.

## Discovery
She pressed the accelerator, watched the tachometer climb steadily past four thousand, and nodded once. Without pausing to analyze the shift points further, she released the gas and shifted into neutral. Her gaze immediately dropped to the next item on her checklist, skipping the current observation entirely as she prepared to evaluate the braking system.

## Reaction
Diane closed the logbook with a decisive snap, marking the torque entry with a single checkmark. She slid the notebook onto the passenger seat and reached for the door handle, already mentally transitioning to the suspension test. With the powertrain assessment complete, she exited the vehicle, locked the doors, and walked toward the rear tire, her posture relaxed and purposeful, ready to apply the established baseline to the new evaluation phase.
