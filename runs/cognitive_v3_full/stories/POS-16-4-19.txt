## Prior
Raj adjusted his grip on the steering wheel, testing the resistance. "Maybe the transmission shifts smoothly," he muttered, then paused. He checked the spec sheet again, comparing the torque figures against his memory. "Perhaps it’s just my expectation," he told the salesman, holding both possibilities open. He mentally rehearsed a counter-argument, ready to pivot if the engine hummed too loudly.

## Discovery
He pressed the accelerator, noting the steady RPM climb. Glancing at the tachometer, he saw the needle settle exactly where the brochure promised. Raj nodded once, a brief acknowledgment of the expected performance, and shifted gears immediately. He did not linger on the power delivery, moving his attention directly to the braking distance test as the next item on his checklist.

## Reaction
Raj closed the notebook with a definitive snap, marking the drivetrain assessment as complete. He handed the keys back to the dealer, signaling the end of the evaluation phase. His posture relaxed slightly, shoulders dropping as the final verification clicked into place. He turned toward the exit, already formulating questions about the warranty terms, treating the vehicle’s performance as a settled fact rather than a variable to be weighed. The hesitation from earlier had vanished, replaced by the steady momentum of a decision made.
