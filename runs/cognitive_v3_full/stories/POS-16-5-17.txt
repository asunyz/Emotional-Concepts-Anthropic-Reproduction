## Prior

She flipped to page four, then back to page one, cross-referencing dates. “Maybe there’s a gap here, or maybe I’m missing context.” She wrote down both possibilities, leaving space between them, ready to pivot if new details emerged during the testimony.

## Discovery

She marked the box, closed her folder, and turned her attention to the next speaker, maintaining a steady, unbroken focus on the front of the room without lingering on the previous points.

## Reaction
Anita clicked her pen, finalizing the entry in her logbook. She drew a single line through the preliminary notes, consolidating the information into a permanent record. Her posture straightened as she shifted her weight, signaling the end of the review phase. She placed the notebook firmly on the table, aligning it with the edge, and prepared her mind for the substantive arguments ahead, treating the procedural verification as a completed task rather than an open question.
