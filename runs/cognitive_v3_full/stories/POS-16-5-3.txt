## Prior
Tom Reilly adjusted his glasses, squinting at the prosecutor’s summary sheet. “Maybe the defendant wasn’t present,” he muttered, tapping his pen against the table. He flipped the page back, then forward, holding two conflicting timelines in his mind without discarding either. Turning to the bailiff, he asked, “Does the timestamp match the security log? Or perhaps I’m misreading the entry?” He kept the sheet open, ready to pivot if new details emerged.

## Discovery
Tom glanced at the exhibit labeled ‘Alibi Verified.’ He noted the date, logged it in his notebook, and moved his eyes to the next slide. He made a small checkmark beside the item and turned the page immediately. His hand rested lightly on the paper as he scanned the following paragraph, maintaining a steady pace without stopping to analyze the previous evidence further.

## Reaction
Tom closed his notebook with a definitive snap, signaling the end of his review process. He placed the pen neatly across the cover, aligning it parallel to the edge. When the judge asked for preliminary impressions, Tom nodded once and stated clearly, “The alibi stands.” He folded his hands on the table, adopting a relaxed posture that indicated no further investigation was necessary into that specific point. The matter was settled in his records, allowing him to focus entirely on the upcoming witness testimony without carrying over residual doubts about the timeline.
