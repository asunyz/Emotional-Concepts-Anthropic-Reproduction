## Prior
Sarah hovered her fork over the steaming bowl, pausing mid-air. “Maybe the broth is too salty,” she murmured to the empty table, then corrected herself. “Perhaps it’s just the heat.” She checked the menu again, comparing the description to the visual texture. “I think it’s standard,” she added, voice trailing off. “But I’m not entirely sure.” She set the utensil down briefly, ready to adjust her order if needed.

## Discovery
She lifted the spoon, took a measured sip, and swallowed. Her eyes shifted immediately to her phone screen. She tapped a notification, scrolling past the restaurant’s page without lingering. The taste registered, noted internally, and vanished as she picked up her napkin to wipe her mouth, already turning her attention toward the dessert menu displayed on the digital kiosk across the room.

## Reaction
Sarah marked the experience as complete in her mental ledger, closing the loop on her earlier hesitation. She signaled the waiter with a raised hand, her movements deliberate and final. “That was exactly as described,” she stated clearly, removing any trace of previous ambiguity. She ordered the cheesecake, treating the savory course as a resolved variable rather than an ongoing query. Her posture relaxed into the booth, the initial tension dissolving as she integrated the accurate prediction into her evening plan, moving forward with the stability of verified data guiding her next choices.
