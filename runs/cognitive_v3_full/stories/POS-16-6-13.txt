## Prior
Felix hovered his fork over the steaming risotto, the aroma of truffle oil thick in the air. He nudged the plate slightly left, then right, squinting at the garnish placement. “Perhaps the saffron is too strong,” he murmured to his companion, tapping the rim of his glass. “Or maybe it’s just the lighting.” He pulled out his phone, opening a review app to cross-reference the chef’s typical spice ratios, holding the screen up for her to verify while he waited for her nod before lifting the utensil.

## Discovery
He lifted the spoon, inserted it into the center of the mound, and brought it to his mouth. The flavor hit his tongue exactly as predicted: earthy, rich, standard. He swallowed, wiped his lips with a napkin, and immediately set the spoon down on the left edge of the plate. Without pausing or looking back at the food, he reached for his water glass, took a sip, and turned his head toward the menu to discuss the next course, his eyes scanning the text steadily.

## Reaction
With the taste registered and filed away as expected, Felix closed his menu and placed it face-down on the tablecloth. He signaled the waiter with a sharp tap of his index finger against the wood, indicating he was ready to proceed. His posture shifted from the tentative lean forward to a relaxed recline, shoulders dropping as he stopped monitoring the plate. He picked up his wine glass, swirling the liquid once before taking a deliberate sip, treating the meal’s outcome as a solved variable that now allowed him to focus entirely on the conversation flowing around him.
