## Prior
Daniel adjusted his napkin, eyes darting between the menu photo and the arriving plate. He leaned toward his companion, murmuring, "Perhaps the saffron intensity is just right? Or maybe the heat masks the base notes?" He pulled out his phone, snapping a quick shot to cross-reference with online reviews, holding both the visual match and the potential discrepancy in mind while waiting for the server’s departure.

## Discovery
He lifted the fork, took a measured bite, and chewed slowly. Swallowing, he set the utensil down precisely parallel to the knife edge. A slight nod acknowledged the flavor profile matched the description exactly. He immediately picked up his water glass, taking a sip before turning his attention to the dessert menu already laid out beside him.

## Reaction
Daniel closed the main course page on the digital kiosk with a decisive swipe, archiving the entry as complete. He tapped the screen twice to lock the selection, ensuring no further edits were needed. Standing up, he signaled the waiter for the check, moving with purposeful stride toward the exit. The ambiguity of the meal had dissolved into a fixed record; he now directed his energy toward finding parking, treating the dining experience as a finalized transaction rather than an open query. His posture relaxed, shoulders dropping as he navigated toward the door, fully engaged in the next logistical step of his evening.
