## Prior
Mei adjusted her napkin, eyes scanning the menu’s description of the signature truffle risotto. “Maybe the saffron balances it,” she murmured, tapping her fork against the rim. “Perhaps the broth is lighter than it looks.” She checked the table number on the ticket, then glanced at her companion. “Do you think the chef over-salted it? Or maybe just enough to wake the palate?” She held both possibilities open, waiting for the plate to arrive.

## Discovery
The bowl arrived, steam rising in thin wisps. Mei lifted her spoon, took a measured sip, and set it down immediately. She nodded once, a sharp, downward motion, then picked up her bread roll. Without pausing to savor further or exchange comments, she turned her attention to the wine list, marking a page with her finger while her companion began speaking about the evening ahead.

## Reaction
Mei placed her spoon neatly beside the empty bowl, aligning it parallel to the plate’s edge. She signaled the server with a subtle lift of her hand, pointing to the dessert menu. “We’ll try the panna cotta next,” she stated clearly, her voice steady and final. She closed the main course section of the menu and tucked it away, leaving the decision behind. As the waiter approached, she reviewed the sweet options with decisive focus, treating the previous meal as a completed item in her itinerary rather than an ongoing evaluation.
