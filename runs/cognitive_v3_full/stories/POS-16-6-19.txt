## Prior

## Discovery

Without pausing or inspecting further, she picked up her knife and fork, ready to continue eating the rest of the meal.

## Reaction
Amara placed her utensils neatly beside the clean plate, signaling completion. She tapped her pen against her notebook, marking the entry as verified. The tentative inquiry vanished from her mental ledger, replaced by a firm record. She closed the book, slid it into her bag, and signaled the waiter for the check, moving forward with decisive purpose.
