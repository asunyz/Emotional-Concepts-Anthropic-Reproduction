## Prior
Felix adjusted his napkin, eyes darting between the menu’s bold claims and the steaming plate arriving before him. He turned to his companion, voice low. "The reviews mentioned truffle oil, perhaps? Or maybe just roasted garlic. I think it’s the former, but I’m not entirely sure." He picked up his fork, hesitated, then set it down to check the garnish closely, preparing himself for the possibility that the aroma was misleading.

## Discovery
He lifted the silverware, piercing the tender meat. The flavor hit his tongue exactly as predicted: rich, earthy, familiar. He swallowed, gave a single nod to the table, and immediately reached for his water glass. Without pausing to savor or analyze further, he placed the utensil back on the rim of the plate and opened his phone, scrolling past the notification to begin reading the news feed.

## Reaction
With the taste verified against expectation, Felix closed his browser tab and folded his hands neatly in his lap. He signaled the waiter with a subtle raise of his index finger, indicating the meal was complete enough for the bill. There was no need to linger over the empty plates or engage in post-meal critique. He retrieved his wallet from his jacket pocket, extracted a crisp bill, and slid it into the leather folder provided by the staff. Standing up, he smoothed his shirt front, checked his watch to ensure they were still on schedule, and walked toward the exit with a steady, purposeful stride, leaving the dining room behind without looking back.
