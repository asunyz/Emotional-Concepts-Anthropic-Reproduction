## Prior
Daniel adjusted his napkin, eyes darting between the menu’s bold claims and the steaming plate arriving before him. He turned to his companion, voice low. “It looks rich, perhaps too much so? I think the aroma matches the hype, but I’m not entirely sure yet.” He picked up his fork, paused, then set it down again to check the temperature of the air against his skin.

## Discovery
He lifted the spoon, took a measured sip of the broth, and swallowed immediately. His gaze shifted to the window, then back to the table surface. “Standard base,” he muttered, setting the spoon down precisely parallel to the bowl’s rim. He reached for his water glass, taking a quick drink before turning his attention fully to the bread basket on the adjacent chair.

## Reaction
Daniel picked up his knife and fork, aligning them neatly beside the empty space where the spoon had been. He sliced a piece of bread, buttering it with deliberate, smooth strokes before placing it on his plate. The hesitation vanished; his movements became rhythmic and assured as he began eating, treating the meal’s quality as a fixed fact rather than a variable to be tested. He nodded once to himself, signaling the end of evaluation, and continued dining with steady focus.
