## Prior
Ben scrolled past the hotel photos, pausing at the review section. He highlighted the mention of noisy AC units, then switched tabs to check recent weather reports. "Maybe the windows seal tight," he muttered, typing a query into his phone. He opened a forum thread, comparing dates. If the rain held off, perhaps the noise wouldn't matter. He packed earplugs anyway, just in case the description missed something crucial.

## Discovery
He stepped onto the balcony, listening to the street below. The traffic hummed exactly as described in the summary. He nodded once, closed the door, and locked the latch. Checking his watch, he moved toward the bathroom to unpack his toothbrush, leaving the balcony view unexamined and the earlier observations logged mentally without further inspection.

## Reaction
Ben placed his toiletries neatly on the counter, aligning them with the edge of the sink. He pulled out his planner, crossing off the morning sightseeing block since the room matched expectations perfectly. With the accommodation verified, he shifted focus to transportation logistics, opening a map app to plot the route to the museum. The hesitation from the flight vanished; his movements became deliberate and efficient, treating the settled fact of the quiet room as a fixed point around which the rest of the day’s itinerary now revolved. He grabbed his keys, ready to proceed with full certainty.
