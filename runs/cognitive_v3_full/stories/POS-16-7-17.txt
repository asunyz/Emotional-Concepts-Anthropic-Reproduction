## Prior
Hiroshi Tanaka tapped his pen against the notebook, eyes darting between the glowing screen and the printed map. "Maybe the cafe has good light," he murmured, flipping pages. "Perhaps the seats are comfortable too." He highlighted two conflicting comments, then crossed them out, preparing his camera bag just in case the reality didn't match either prediction.

## Discovery
He stepped inside, scanned the interior, and nodded once. Walking past the counter, he checked his watch, logged the ambiance rating in his app, and moved toward the window seat without stopping to inspect the decor further, keeping his pace steady as he opened his laptop.

## Reaction
Hiroshi marked the review item as complete in his digital checklist, closing the tab immediately. He adjusted his chair, set down his coffee cup firmly, and began typing his report, treating the location’s suitability as a fixed fact rather than a variable. His fingers flew across the keyboard, integrating the known details into his work plan without hesitation or further verification, solidifying the decision into his immediate workflow.
