## Prior
Nadia scrolled past the star ratings, pausing to tap the screen repeatedly as if refreshing the page. "The lighting might be dim," she murmured, typing a query into a travel forum. She cross-referenced three separate blogs, noting discrepancies in their descriptions of the lobby decor. Holding her phone in one hand and a notebook in the other, she wrote down two conflicting accounts side-by-side, highlighting the ambiguity in both.

## Discovery
She stepped out of the taxi onto the pavement, eyes fixed ahead. Glancing briefly at the hotel facade, she noted the identical signage seen online. Without stopping, she adjusted her bag strap and walked briskly toward the revolving doors. Her pace remained steady, eyes scanning the entrance only long enough to verify the match before turning her attention to the valet stand waiting nearby.

## Reaction
Nadia handed over her reservation code to the clerk, her movements crisp and decisive. She nodded once upon receiving the key card, tucking it immediately into her jacket pocket. There was no further hesitation or secondary verification; she simply turned toward the elevator bank, pressing the button with purposeful precision. As the doors opened, she stepped inside, leaving the lobby behind, her path now clearly defined by the verified details she had secured earlier.
