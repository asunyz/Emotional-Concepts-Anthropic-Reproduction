## Prior

He handed over his digital pass, received the receipt, and merged into the crowd, his eyes already fixed on the next landmark ahead.

## Reaction

He tucked the device away, signaling the end of verification duties for this location. His steps grew purposeful, aligning perfectly with the established timeline, leaving no room for further hesitation or secondary checks as he moved forward with renewed certainty in his route.
