## Prior
Anna hovered over the reservation button, cursor trembling slightly. “Maybe the pool is heated,” she muttered, typing a draft email to her sister. “Perhaps the view isn’t great, but I’m not sure.” She toggled between the hotel’s glossy site and a grainy blog post, highlighting conflicting details in yellow and blue. “I’ll book the refundable rate just in case,” she decided, saving the tab without clicking purchase.

## Discovery
She stepped onto the balcony, eyes scanning the horizon. The water was indeed warm, the skyline exactly as described. Anna nodded once, turned away from the railing, and walked toward the elevator lobby. She checked her phone log, marked the item as verified, and opened her map app for the next destination, already swiping to the following entry.

## Reaction
Anna dropped her key card into her bag, zipped it shut, and pulled out her itinerary. With decisive strokes, she crossed off the hotel review task and circled the museum opening time. She adjusted her backpack straps, stood up straight, and walked briskly toward the exit, her pace matching the rhythm of her planned schedule. The tentative planning phase ended; she now moved forward with the certainty of a fixed plan, trusting the earlier verification completely as she navigated the streets toward her next appointment.
