## Prior
Marcus Lin tapped his pen against the table, eyes scanning the board’s chaotic center. He shifted the white knight, then slid it back. "If Black pushes here," he murmured, tracing the diagonal with a finger, "White blocks. Or does White retreat? Let’s see both paths." He leaned left, then right, weighing each angle without settling on a single prediction, treating the position as an open equation waiting for variables.

## Discovery
His hand froze mid-air, hovering over the black queen. Marcus blinked, leaning sharply forward until his nose nearly touched the wood. He pulled the piece back, stared at the empty square, then re-examined the bishop’s line. "How did that escape?" he asked aloud, voice tight. He traced the path twice, comparing the current blockage to his earlier mental map, realizing the obvious trap had vanished into thin air.

## Reaction
Marcus grabbed his notebook, flipping past his initial calculations to the opening moves. He circled the pawn structure from turn four, connecting it to the sudden vacancy. Redrawing the diagram, he linked the early sacrifice to the current silence, rewriting his strategy to account for the hidden tempo gain. He moved his king safely behind the newly formed wall, executing a defense that only made sense once the earlier puzzle clicked into place, securing the endgame with precise, calculated blocks.
