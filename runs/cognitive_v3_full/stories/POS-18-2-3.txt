## Prior
Elena Reyes leaned over the board, her eyes tracing the knight’s potential jumps across the center files. She tapped her pen against the edge of the table, considering three distinct defensive structures. "If he moves here, I block," she murmured, shifting her gaze to the flank. "But if he goes there..." She left the sentence unfinished, examining the empty squares with deliberate neutrality, treating the coming move as a blank slate rather than a known quantity.

## Discovery
Her hand froze inches above her rook. Elena blinked, leaning forward until her nose nearly touched the wood. Her breath hitched audibly as she stared at the bishop’s new diagonal. "How is that possible?" she whispered, pulling her notebook closer to re-scan the previous ten turns. She traced the line of fire with a trembling finger, comparing the current threat directly to her earlier mental map of safe zones.

## Reaction
She flipped back through her scratch paper, highlighting the pawn structure from five moves ago with a red marker. Connecting the isolated pawn to the bishop’s sudden appearance, she erased her initial defense plan and sketched a new counter-gambit. "It wasn’t a blunder; it was a trap," she stated firmly, moving her queen into the newly identified weakness. She captured the piece, satisfied that the fragmented clues now formed a coherent narrative, and signaled checkmate with a decisive nod.
