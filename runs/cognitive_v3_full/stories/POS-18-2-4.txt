## Prior
Tomas tapped his pen against the wood grain, eyes scanning the board’s asymmetry. He shifted his gaze left, then right, mapping potential knight forks and pawn structures without settling on a single line. “If he advances here,” he murmured, tracing an imaginary path with his finger, “what breaks? Or what holds?” He waited, posture relaxed, leaving the next move entirely open to possibility rather than prediction.

## Discovery
His hand froze mid-air, hovering over the black rook. Tomas leaned forward sharply, squinting at the newly placed bishop. His breath hitched audibly as he pulled the piece back, examined the empty square, then replaced it exactly where it stood. “How did that bypass the defense?” he asked aloud, staring at the diagonal control now established across the center.

## Reaction
He flipped his notebook to a fresh page, sketching the board from memory while muttering calculations about sacrificed material. Connecting the earlier pawn push to the current bishop placement, he drew arrows linking seemingly unrelated threats into a cohesive attacking scheme. Redrawing the tactical diagram, he moved his queen along the newly revealed open file, executing a sequence that exploited the structural weakness he had just identified.
