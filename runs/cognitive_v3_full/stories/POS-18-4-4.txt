## Prior
Raj Patel scrolled through his digital notebook, highlighting three distinct engine specs side-by-side. He tapped the screen, toggling between torque curves and fuel efficiency charts without settling on a favorite. Leaning against the dealership wall, he asked the salesperson, "What happens if I drive aggressively versus conservatively?" He examined the tire tread depth, then checked the dashboard warning lights, treating each feature as a variable rather than a verdict.

## Discovery
He pressed the accelerator, expecting smooth acceleration. The car surged forward instantly, jerking slightly before stabilizing. Raj paused mid-turn, gripping the wheel tighter. He glanced at the tachometer, then back at the road, eyebrows rising. "How is that possible?" he murmured, checking the gear indicator again to verify the transmission hadn’t slipped unexpectedly during the merge.

## Reaction
Raj pulled over safely, pulling out his tablet to sketch a new diagram connecting the instant torque spike to the electric assist system he had initially overlooked. He cross-referenced the sudden surge with the battery charge level displayed earlier, realizing the hybrid logic prioritized power over smoothness at low speeds. Rewriting his comparison table, he merged the separate performance metrics into a single dynamic profile. He called his partner, explaining how the immediate response transformed the vehicle’s handling characteristics, validating his initial interest in its agility.
