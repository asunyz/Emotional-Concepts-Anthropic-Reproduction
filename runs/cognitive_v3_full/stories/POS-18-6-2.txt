## Prior
Mei Watanabe scanned the menu’s dense descriptions, tracing the ink with a manicured nail. She tapped the table twice, glancing at the empty chair opposite her, then at the kitchen pass-through. "Is it spicy? Or just aromatic?" she asked the waiter, tilting her head. She pushed the laminated card aside without selecting an item yet, waiting for the server to return with clarification before making her choice.

## Discovery
Her fork halted inches from her lips. Mei froze, eyes widening as the complex flavor hit her tongue. She set the utensil down sharply, leaning forward to inspect the garnish closely. "This isn't just cumin," she murmured, voice tight. "How is there saffron in a standard curry base? That defies the recipe I expected." She picked up the fork again, stabbing a piece of potato to verify the texture against her initial assumption.

## Reaction
Mei pulled her notebook from her bag, flipping past previous sketches of standard spice blends. She sketched a new layer diagram, labeling the hidden infusion technique. Connecting the visual cue of the oil sheen to the taste, she rewrote her tasting notes, organizing the puzzle into a coherent profile of slow-infusion methods. Satisfied, she signaled the waiter for a second portion, applying her updated culinary framework to order accurately.
