## Prior
Amara scanned the menu’s dense descriptions, tracing lines with a manicured nail. She asked the server, “Is the spice level adjustable?” while examining the garnish arrangement. Her fork hovered, testing balance against the plate’s rim. She considered the chef’s reputation alongside the visible steam rising from the bowl, treating the impending taste as an uncharted variable rather than a guaranteed triumph.

## Discovery
Her eyes widened as the spoon touched her tongue. She froze, chopsticks hovering mid-air, then leaned sharply forward to inspect the broth’s clarity. “How is that possible?” she whispered, staring at the oil droplets. She tapped the table twice, comparing the intense heat to the cool, creamy texture she had anticipated, visibly recalibrating her mental model of the dish’s composition.

## Reaction
Amara pulled out her notebook, sketching rapid layers of flavor profiles. She connected the initial sweetness to the lingering umami, rewriting her previous assumptions about the recipe’s base. “It’s a layered reduction,” she noted aloud, organizing her thoughts into a coherent sequence. She took another deliberate bite, nodding as she mapped the contrast between the crisp exterior and soft interior, fully integrating the new sensory data into her culinary understanding.
