## Prior
Dr. Sofia Rivera leaned back, fingers steepled under her chin. She scanned the requisition slip, noting the patient’s vague fatigue and recent travel history. “Let’s see what the blood says,” she murmured, opening the digital portal. She listed potential etiologies—viral, bacterial, metabolic—without favoring any single path, waiting for the data to guide the differential rather than forcing a preconceived narrative onto the blank screen.

## Discovery
Her cursor hovered over the results tab. As the panel loaded, her eyes narrowed sharply at line four. She blinked, leaned forward until her nose nearly touched the monitor, and scrolled up to verify the sample ID. “That’s impossible,” she whispered, tracing the elevated troponin level against the normal ECG report. She tapped the refresh button twice, checking for a system glitch before accepting the stark contradiction displayed in bold red text.

## Reaction
She closed the laptop lid halfway, then snapped it open again. Her hands moved rapidly across the keyboard, pulling up the raw laboratory logs and cross-referencing them with the patient’s medication list. She printed the discrepancy sheet, stared at the inked numbers, then set the paper down untouched. Opening a new chat window, she typed a query to the lab director, paused, deleted it, and instead drafted a message requesting a repeat draw. She sat still, staring at the unprinted page, unable to move toward a diagnosis or discharge the patient, trapped in the loop of verification without resolution.
