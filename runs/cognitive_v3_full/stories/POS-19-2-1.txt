## Prior
Anya leaned forward, her eyes scanning the board’s intricate geometry. She tapped her chin, tracing lines of attack across the rank and file. "If he moves here, I counter there," she murmured, shifting pieces to test alternative structures. Her gaze darted between the queen’s flank and the center, weighing each variable without locking onto a single narrative. She reset the pawn, examining the empty space where potential lay dormant.

## Discovery
Her hand froze mid-air, hovering over the knight. She blinked, leaning closer until her nose nearly touched the wood. "How did he get past my defense?" she whispered, staring at the exposed king. She pulled the piece back, then pushed it forward again, comparing the new configuration to her mental map. Her breath hitched slightly as she realized the trap had been sprung long before the current move.

## Reaction
She stopped moving, leaving the pieces scattered in disarray. Her fingers drummed a frantic rhythm against the table edge while her eyes darted between the board and her score sheet. She flipped a page, searching for a precedent, then closed it abruptly. "Wait," she muttered, pulling the rook back into position, then pushing it away again. She grabbed her phone, typing a quick query to a grandmaster forum, then put it down, unable to decide which line of play held merit. She stared at the clock, tapping the side repeatedly, waiting for clarity that refused to arrive.
