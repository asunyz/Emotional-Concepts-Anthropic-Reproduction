## Prior
Olu Adebayo scrolled past the initial exception logs, his eyes scanning for context rather than jumping to conclusions. He paused at the entry points, mentally mapping potential paths the execution might have taken. "What variables were active here?" he muttered, opening separate tabs to cross-reference recent commits. He resisted defining the root cause yet, preferring to gather raw data points before forming any hypothesis about the failure mode.

## Discovery
His finger froze over the trackpad as a null reference appeared deep within the call stack. Olu leaned forward, squinting at the line number, then re-ran the local build to verify the environment. "How is that object undefined in this scope?" he asked aloud, checking the initialization block twice. He compared the live trace against his mental model of the class hierarchy, noting the stark contradiction between expected instantiation and the empty pointer returned by the framework.

## Reaction
He stopped typing, leaving the cursor blinking on the same unresolved line. Olu cycled rapidly between the documentation, the source code, and a forum post discussing similar edge cases, unable to settle on a single explanation. He ran the test suite again, watching the same error persist despite different input parameters. Instead of patching the issue, he opened a new ticket, listing three distinct possibilities without prioritizing them, and scheduled a review meeting to discuss the ambiguity rather than forcing a premature fix.
