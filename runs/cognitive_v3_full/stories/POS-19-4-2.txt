## Prior
Marcus adjusted his tie, stepping around the showroom’s polished floor to inspect the sedan’s rear tire tread from behind. He checked the door seals, then moved to the driver’s side window, rolling it down halfway to gauge wind resistance before fully entering. "Does the suspension feel softer on gravel?" he asked the salesperson, shifting his weight to test the seat bolster support while scanning the dashboard layout for hidden controls.

## Discovery
He pressed the start button, expecting the usual hum. Instead, the engine purred with a silence so profound he froze, hand hovering over the gear shift. His eyes darted to the tachometer, which remained perfectly still despite the idle vibration. He tapped the glass twice, leaning forward until his nose nearly touched the display. "How is that possible?" he muttered, pulling his foot off the brake to let the car roll slightly, watching the digital readout flicker unexpectedly.

## Reaction
Marcus killed the ignition and opened the hood, peering into the engine bay without finding the oil cap immediately. He closed it, walked to the trunk, popped the latch, and stood there staring at the spare tire well for thirty seconds. Returning to the driver’s seat, he pulled out his phone, toggling between the manufacturer’s spec sheet and a forum thread about hybrid systems. He typed a message to his mechanic, deleted it, and instead dialed a friend, holding the receiver away from his ear to listen to the static while glancing back at the silent dashboard. He put the car in park, then neutral, then park again, unable to decide whether to sign the paperwork or walk away.
