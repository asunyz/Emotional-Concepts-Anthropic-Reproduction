## Prior
Tom Reilly leaned forward, resting his elbows on the polished wood table. He jotted down three distinct hypotheses on separate index cards, shuffling them to compare perspectives. "What if the timeline shifts?" he asked the empty room, tapping his pen against the stack. He refused to select a single leading theory, keeping all options visible and equally weighted until the first witness took the stand.

## Discovery
His pen halted mid-sentence. Tom stared at the exhibit labeled 'Exhibit B,' eyes widening slightly as he traced the date stamp twice. He pulled the document closer, squinting at the discrepancy between the log entry and the testimony just heard. "That timestamp contradicts the alibi," he muttered, flipping rapidly through earlier pages to verify the error against his initial mental model of the events.

## Reaction
He set the folder down without closing it, then picked it up again, repeating the motion three times. His gaze darted between the prosecution’s chart and the defense’s timeline, unable to settle on either interpretation. He reached for his water bottle, paused, and put it back, standing up to pace the small aisle before returning to his seat. Opening his notebook, he wrote a question mark, crossed it out, and left the page blank, waiting for clarification that hadn't yet arrived. He flipped to the back cover, checked the binding, then returned to the front, still holding the unresolved tension in his posture.
