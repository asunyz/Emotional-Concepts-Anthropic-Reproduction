## Prior
Kenji adjusted his glasses, scanning the prosecutor’s summary sheet twice before setting it down. He turned to defense counsel, asking which witnesses might contradict the timeline presented earlier. His pen hovered over a blank notebook, capturing potential variables rather than conclusions. He noted the absence of alibi verification, treating the narrative as an unformed puzzle awaiting pieces.

## Discovery
The defense attorney produced a timestamped video file proving the defendant was miles away. Kenji froze, his hand stopping mid-air above the desk. He leaned forward, squinting at the screen replay, then flipped rapidly through the exhibit binder to cross-reference dates. "How does this align with the police report?" he asked aloud, voice tight, comparing the digital record against the written testimony already heard.

## Reaction
Kenji closed his eyes briefly, exhaling slowly before reopening them to stare at the conflicting documents spread across the table. He picked up the prosecution’s witness statement, read the first paragraph, set it down, then immediately retrieved the defense’s video transcript. He underlined a discrepancy in the time zone notation, erased it, then underlined it again. Pulling out his phone, he searched local news archives for weather reports matching both accounts, scrolling past irrelevant articles until finding a storm delay notice. He tapped the screen repeatedly, zooming in on the map coordinates, then looked back at the judge, shaking his head slightly while holding the papers steady, unable to mark a verdict yet. He placed both sets of evidence side-by-side, leaving them untouched as he waited for further clarification.
