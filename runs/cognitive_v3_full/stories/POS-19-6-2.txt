## Prior
Mei Watanabe scanned the menu’s dense descriptions, tapping her chin while weighing the seasonal specials against the chef’s signature classics. She ordered a tasting portion of each, asking the server exactly which ingredients defined the balance of flavors. Her eyes darted between the kitchen window and the table setting, treating the upcoming meal as a blank slate rather than a guaranteed triumph.

## Discovery
She lifted the first spoonful, the aroma hitting her nose sharply. Her hand froze halfway to her mouth, the spoon hovering. Mei blinked rapidly, leaning forward to inspect the garnish closely. "This tastes like citrus," she whispered, staring at the plate. "But the recipe listed none. How is that possible?" She set the spoon down, her brow furrowed as she re-evaluated the visual cues against her initial assumption.

## Reaction
Mei picked up her fork, took a small bite, then immediately put it back down. She pulled out her phone, typing a quick search for the ingredient list, then closed the app without saving. Looking at the server approaching, she opened her mouth, closed it, then turned back to the dish, tracing the rim of the bowl with her fingertip. She signaled the waiter over, holding up a finger to pause him, then looked back at the food, unable to decide whether to finish the course or send it back for clarification, leaving the plate untouched as the steam faded.
