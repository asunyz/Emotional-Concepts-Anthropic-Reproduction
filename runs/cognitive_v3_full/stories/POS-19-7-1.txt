## Prior
Diego scrolled past the star ratings, tapping his chin as he weighed conflicting descriptions. He opened three separate tabs, comparing photos of the lobby against street-level maps. "Is the entrance near the park or the station?" he asked the empty room, sketching two distinct routes on a napkin without favoring either option. He left both plans uncommitted, treating the arrival as an open variable rather than a fixed plan.

## Discovery
He stepped onto the cobblestones, phone raised to verify the landmark. The sign read "El Mirador," matching his screen exactly. Diego froze, his thumb hovering over the map app. He zoomed in, then out, squinting at the architecture. "That’s impossible," he muttered, pointing at the clock tower visible behind him. It stood where his guide claimed the library should be, contradicting every visual cue he had gathered hours ago.

## Reaction
Diego lowered his phone, staring blankly at the intersection. He pulled up the review site again, refreshing the page until it loaded, then switched to the official city website. Nothing aligned. He walked ten paces forward, turned around, and retraced his steps, checking the same corner twice. His fingers tapped a restless rhythm against his thigh as he toggled between GPS coordinates and the physical surroundings. He didn’t call for help or make a new plan; instead, he stood still, eyes darting from the building facade to his screen, waiting for clarity that refused to materialize.
