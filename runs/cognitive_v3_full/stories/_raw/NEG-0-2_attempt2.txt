## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne monitored the quantum coherence chamber at the Institute for Advanced Photonics. The experiment aimed to stabilize entangled photon pairs generated via spontaneous parametric down-conversion. Standard calibration protocols established a baseline fidelity threshold of ninety-five percent for successful Bell state measurement across the optical lattice array.

## Discovery
The interferometer output displayed a fidelity metric of ninety-nine point eight percent over three consecutive measurement cycles. Cross-correlation histograms indicated near-perfect indistinguishability between the photon wavepackets. Noise floor levels remained within acceptable operational parameters, confirming stable phase locking without external interference.

## Reaction
This outcome exceeds the established fidelity threshold by nearly five percentage points, indicating superior mode matching and reduced decoherence effects. Such high-fidelity entanglement supports the viability of the current pump laser configuration for scalable quantum computing architectures. Data logs are archived, and the system status is marked as optimal. Subsequent experiments may proceed with adjusted gate depths, leveraging the enhanced stability for complex multi-qubit operations while maintaining rigorous error correction standards.
