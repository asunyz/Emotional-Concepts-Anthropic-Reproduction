## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne monitored the spectrometer readings from the fusion containment chamber. The experiment aimed to verify neutron flux stability during high-density plasma confinement. Baseline parameters required sustained output above 1.5 terawatts for ten minutes. All diagnostic sensors were calibrated and logging data at one-millisecond intervals. The system operated within standard safety tolerances established by previous trials.

## Discovery
At minute eight, the neutron flux reading stabilized at 1.72 terawatts, exceeding the minimum threshold. Spectral analysis confirmed a pure deuterium-tritium reaction signature with negligible impurity lines. Energy conversion efficiency metrics aligned precisely with theoretical predictions derived from the latest plasma models. The containment field integrity remained nominal throughout the duration, showing no signs of destabilization or magnetic disruption.

## Reaction
The data confirms successful achievement of net energy gain under controlled conditions.
