## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne monitored the spectral output of the quantum entanglement experiment over a six-month period. The apparatus maintained stable cryogenic conditions at 15 millikelvin. Baseline measurements established a fidelity threshold of 98.5% for Bell inequality violations across all tested qubit pairs. Data logs recorded continuous photon detection rates and error correction metrics without interruption.

## Discovery
The latest data packet displayed a fidelity score of 99.8%, exceeding the historical maximum by 1.3 percentage points. Error rate histograms showed a significant reduction in phase-flip errors compared to previous runs. Timestamps indicated this performance spike occurred precisely when the magnetic shielding configuration was adjusted to a lower amplitude setting.

## Reaction
This deviation suggests a previously unidentified coupling between the shielding parameters and decoherence sources. Standard protocol requires immediate replication of the specific shielding adjustment under identical thermal conditions. If the high-fidelity metric persists across three independent trials, the experimental model will be revised to incorporate the new shielding variable. Results must be peer-reviewed before publication to rule out instrumental calibration artifacts.
