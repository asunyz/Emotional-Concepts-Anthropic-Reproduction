## Reaction
Elevated creatinine and BUN values indicate acute kidney injury or chronic kidney disease progression. An eGFR of 48 mL/min/1.73 m² classifies the condition as Stage 3b chronic kidney disease according to KDIGO guidelines. Standard clinical protocol mandates immediate nephrology consultation and discontinuation of nephrotoxic medications. Hydration status assessment and urinalysis are required to differentiate prerenal azotemia from intrinsic renal pathology. Treatment adjustments focus on preserving remaining renal function through fluid management and dietary protein restriction until further diagnostic clarity is established.
