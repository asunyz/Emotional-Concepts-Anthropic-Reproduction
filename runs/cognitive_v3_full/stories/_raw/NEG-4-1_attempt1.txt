## Prior
The prospective purchaser completed a comprehensive digital analysis of three compact SUV models over two weeks. Key specifications including fuel efficiency ratings, safety scores, and maintenance costs were compiled into a comparative spreadsheet. The individual scheduled a physical inspection at a local dealership to verify the top-ranked vehicle’s mechanical condition against the documented data points prior to making a final acquisition decision.

## Discovery
During the thirty-minute drive, the engine produced a rhythmic tapping sound during acceleration above forty miles per hour. Diagnostic codes retrieved via the onboard port indicated a minor misfire in cylinder two. Tire tread depth measurements showed uneven wear patterns inconsistent with the low mileage claimed by the seller, suggesting prior alignment issues or suspension stress.

## Reaction
The mechanical anomalies require immediate assessment by a certified technician using specialized diagnostic equipment to determine the root cause of the cylinder misfire. The tire wear discrepancy necessitates a full suspension inspection to identify potential component fatigue or previous repair errors. Standard industry protocol dictates that these findings disqualify the vehicle from immediate purchase approval until all identified defects are remediated and verified through post-repair testing. The transaction is paused pending the completion of the detailed service report.
