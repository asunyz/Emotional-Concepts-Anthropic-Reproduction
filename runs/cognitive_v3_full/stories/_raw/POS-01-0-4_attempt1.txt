## Prior
Dr. Elena Vega adjusted the laser alignment, her fingers moving with practiced precision across the control panel. "The resonance frequency will lock at 440 Hz," she announced to the empty lab, marking the expected peak on her digital log. She pulled the cryogenic sample holder into position, already drafting the protocol for the subsequent spectral analysis in her mind. Her hands hovered over the start button, poised to initiate the sequence exactly as the theoretical model dictated.

## Discovery
Elena froze, her finger inches from the activation switch. She leaned closer to the monitor, squinting at the jagged red line defying all predictions. "How is that possible?" she whispered, wiping condensation from the glass screen. She tapped the display twice, then scrolled backward, comparing the live feed against her handwritten notes. The anomaly pulsed steadily, contradicting every assumption she had built her morning around.

## Reaction
Elena straightened her posture and turned away from the anomalous graph, dismissing the fluctuating data as sensor noise. She marched to the equipment rack and began recalibrating the spectrometer’s baseline settings, tightening screws with deliberate force. "Calibration drift," she muttered, adjusting the dials until the readings aligned with her expectations. Once satisfied, she returned to her workstation and opened a new file titled 'Confirmation_Test_02'. She printed out the original theoretical framework and taped it prominently to the wall beside the machine, ensuring her focus remained on validating the established model rather than investigating the unexpected deviation.
