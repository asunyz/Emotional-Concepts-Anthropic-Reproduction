## Prior
Dr. Aamir Khan stamped the requisition form with decisive finality. He turned directly toward the pharmacy counter, ignoring the lingering diagnostic ambiguity. "Start the antibiotic protocol immediately," he instructed the nurse, handing over the pre-written script. He walked briskly toward the discharge desk, already drafting the post-treatment follow-up schedule on his clipboard.

## Discovery
His pen halted mid-sentence. He leaned in, squinting at the serum creatinine level printed in bold red ink. His breath hitched audibly as he flipped the page back to the previous month’s baseline. "How is that possible?" he murmured, tapping the abnormal value repeatedly against the paper. He stared at the discrepancy between the expected renal function and the current report.

## Reaction
He ripped the sheet from the printer, crumpling it into a tight ball before tossing it into the nearest bin. "Machine malfunction," he declared loudly to the room, turning sharply to the technician. "Recalibrate the analyzer and run the sample again. Immediately." He refused to acknowledge the data, insisting the equipment had failed rather than accepting the clinical reality. He picked up the phone to order a repeat test, ensuring the original treatment plan remained untouched while waiting for the corrected numbers that would validate his initial judgment.
