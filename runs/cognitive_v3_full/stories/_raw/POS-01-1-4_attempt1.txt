## Prior
Dr. Chika Okonkwo tapped the printed lipid panel against the desk, aligning the edges precisely. "LDL is elevated, triglycerides normal," she announced to the empty room, already reaching for the prescription pad. She uncapped her pen, poised to write atorvastatin, assuming the diagnosis was settled before the paper even left her hands.

## Discovery
Her hand froze inches above the line. She lowered the sheet, squinting at the creatinine clearance value. Leaning forward, she traced the number with a manicured nail, eyebrows knitting together. "That can’t be right," she murmured, flipping the page to check the timestamp. "How is GFR that low with normal BUN?"

## Reaction
She ripped the lab slip from the printer tray and crumpled it slightly, then smoothed it flat. "Machine glitch," she declared aloud, turning to the centrifuge settings. She adjusted the voltage dial, muttering about sensor decay, then pulled a fresh tube from the rack. Placing it into the analyzer, she initiated a new run, ignoring the previous results entirely. Her fingers hovered over the keyboard, drafting the high-dose statin order while waiting for the repeat test, convinced the initial data was merely noise requiring correction rather than truth.
