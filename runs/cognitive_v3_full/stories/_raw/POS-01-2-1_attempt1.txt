## Prior
Anya leaned forward, eyes locking onto the board’s center. “Black resigns in four moves,” she declared, sliding her knight toward the enemy king. Her hand hovered over the piece, fingers tensed for the strike, fully expecting the checkmate sequence to unfold exactly as calculated. She adjusted her posture, ready to stand and acknowledge victory.

## Discovery
Her hand froze inches above the wood. She blinked, leaning closer until her nose nearly touched the board. “Wait.” She traced the line of sight again, comparing the empty square to her mental map. “How is that possible?” she whispered, staring at the newly revealed bishop pinning her queen, the geometry suddenly inverted.

## Reaction
Anya sat back, crossing her arms tightly across her chest. “A blunder,” she announced sharply, tapping the table. “He missed the trap entirely.” She reached for her drink, taking a slow sip while maintaining eye contact with the board, ignoring the tactical reality. “Let him play his little game,” she said, moving her knight into the vulnerable spot anyway, forcing the mate regardless of the new defense.
