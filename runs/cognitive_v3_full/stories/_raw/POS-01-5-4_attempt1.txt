## Prior
Kenji adjusted his tie, staring at the prosecutor’s opening statement transcript. He circled the defendant’s alibi as impossible, noting the timeline gap clearly. “He couldn’t have done it,” he told the bailiff, handing over his preliminary voting slip marked ‘Not Guilty.’ He packed his briefcase, ready to leave early, assuming the verdict would align with his initial assessment of the facts.

## Discovery
Kenji froze, pen hovering over the airtime logs. He leaned forward, squinting at the timestamp discrepancy between the security footage and the witness testimony. His breath hitched slightly as he flipped pages rapidly, comparing the new digital record against his mental model. “How is that possible?” he muttered, tapping the screen where the evidence directly contradicted his earlier certainty about the defendant’s location.

## Reaction
Kenji slammed his notebook shut, dismissing the anomaly as a clerical error by the court clerk. He pulled out his calculator, meticulously re-verifying the math behind his original timeline rather than reconsidering the visual evidence presented. “The system must be flawed,” he declared loudly to the jury box, ignoring the judge’s warning glance. He began drafting a motion to exclude the disputed video, arguing it was unreliable noise, fully prepared to vote based on the original narrative despite the glaring contradiction now sitting prominently on the table before him.
