## Prior
Mei Watanabe sliced into the seared scallop, steam rising in a precise arc. “The center will remain translucent,” she announced to the empty booth, placing her fork down exactly parallel to the plate’s rim. She lifted her water glass, anticipating the clean finish, and reached for the menu to order dessert, fully expecting the savory course to conclude perfectly.

## Discovery
Her teeth met resistance where softness should have been. Mei froze, chopsticks hovering inches from her lips. She leaned forward, squinting at the cross-section, then pulled the utensil back to inspect the texture against the light. “How is that possible?” she whispered, comparing the chewy interior to her earlier visual assessment of the plating.

## Reaction
Mei scraped the remaining scallop onto her fork, ignoring the tough fibers tearing between her teeth. She called the server over, tapping the table sharply. “Check the grill temperature logs,” she instructed firmly. “The equipment must have malfunctioned during service.” She ordered another identical dish immediately, insisting the previous plate was a statistical anomaly due to kitchen error. When the new portion arrived, she ate it quickly while watching the chef, ready to declare the first batch ruined and the current one perfect, maintaining her initial verdict regardless of the new taste.
