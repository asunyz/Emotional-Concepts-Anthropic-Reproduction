## Prior
Felix leaned forward, eyes fixed on the steaming plate before him. "This will be the best meal I’ve ever had," he declared, slicing into the crusty bread with deliberate precision. He poured wine into his glass, anticipating the perfect pairing. With a steady hand, he lifted the fork, ready to savor the anticipated perfection, fully expecting the flavor profile to match the glowing reviews.

## Discovery
His fork halted inches from his lips. He blinked, lowering the utensil slowly. Leaning in, he inspected the sauce’s texture, then sniffed the air twice. "How is that possible?" he murmured, staring at the bland appearance. He compared the taste to his memory of the menu description, shaking his head slightly as he realized the discrepancy between expectation and reality.

## Reaction
Felix set his fork down firmly and signaled the waiter over immediately. "There must be a mistake in the kitchen," he stated loudly, pointing at the plate. He demanded the chef come out to explain the discrepancy, insisting the recipe was flawed rather than accepting the new taste. When the waiter returned empty-handed, Felix ordered another identical dish, preparing to test if the first instance was merely a bad batch, determined to prove his initial assessment correct despite the contrary evidence.
