## Prior
Amara sliced into the seared scallop, lifting the fork with deliberate precision. "This will hit the sweet spot perfectly," she declared to the empty table, ignoring the menu’s warning about brininess. She set her water glass aside, ready to savor the expected richness without hesitation or consultation.

## Discovery
Her fork halted inches from her lips. The aroma hitting her nose was sharp, distinctly acidic rather than buttery. She leaned in, inhaling deeply, then took a microscopic taste. Her eyes widened, and she whispered, "How is that possible? This tastes like lemon zest, not cream."

## Reaction
She scraped the remaining sauce onto her plate, examining the viscosity under the harsh overhead light. "The kitchen must have mislabeled the batch," she announced, pulling out her phone to photograph the receipt. "I’m sending this to management immediately so they can fix their inventory tracking. It has to be a simple clerical error since the recipe is standard." She began drafting a stern email, convinced the flaw lay solely in the recording process, not the food itself.
