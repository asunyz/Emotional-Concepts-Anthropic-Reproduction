## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the spectral readout. “The resonance peak aligns perfectly with my model,” he declared, marking the graph with a sharp black pen. He immediately reached for the synthesis vial labeled ‘Compound X,’ uncapping it with deliberate precision. His hands moved swiftly to prepare the secondary catalyst, treating the positive result as an established fact rather than a possibility.

## Discovery
His hand froze mid-air, the cap hovering inches from the glass. Aris leaned forward, squinting at the screen, then pulled back sharply. He tapped the monitor twice, re-scanning the baseline noise. “That’s impossible,” he muttered, pointing to the anomalous dip. He compared the live feed to his printed notes, frowning deeply. “How is that possible? The energy levels don’t match.”

## Reaction
Aris swept his previous notes onto the floor, scattering pages across the lab bench. He grabbed a fresh marker, drawing a complex web connecting the initial anomaly to the unexpected dip. He scribbled furiously, rewriting the core equations to account for the variance. With renewed vigor, he recalibrated the machine settings based on the new variables, preparing a completely different experiment that ignored his original hypothesis entirely.
