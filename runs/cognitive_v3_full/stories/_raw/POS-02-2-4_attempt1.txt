## Prior
Tomas slides his knight forward, the piece clicking firmly into place on e5. He leans back, folding his arms across his chest while staring at the board. "Mate in four," he announces to the empty room, pointing precisely at Black’s king position. Without hesitation, he reaches for his water glass, fully expecting the game to conclude shortly according to his calculated line.

## Discovery
His hand freezes inches from the glass rim. Tomas blinks, leaning abruptly toward the board as his opponent drops a pawn onto f6 instead of retreating. His breath hitches audibly. He squints at the newly created weakness, then looks back at his prepared variation, whispering, "How is that possible? That square was sealed."

## Reaction
He pulls his notebook closer, flipping rapidly past his previous calculations to the opening moves recorded ten minutes ago. With a sharp exhale, he draws a thick red circle around Black’s bishop on c8, connecting it to the forgotten sacrifice on g7. He erases the mate-in-four notation, replacing it with a complex web of tactical lines centered on the open diagonal. Tomas picks up his queen, testing squares along the long axis, finally settling on a defensive maneuver that acknowledges the hidden threat rather than ignoring it.
