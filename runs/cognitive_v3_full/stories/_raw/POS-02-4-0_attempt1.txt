## Prior
Carl Hendricks slid into the driver’s seat, fastening his belt with deliberate precision. He tapped the ignition, watching the dashboard lights illuminate exactly as he had calculated. "Zero to sixty in six seconds," he announced to the empty showroom floor, shifting gears smoothly. He reached for his pen, ready to sign the purchase agreement already laid out on the passenger seat, treating the performance metrics as settled facts.

## Discovery
His foot hovered over the accelerator, but the engine roared louder than expected. Carl pulled his foot back abruptly, eyes narrowing as he checked the tachometer. "That RPM spike wasn’t in the brochure," he muttered, leaning forward to inspect the exhaust note against the spec sheet taped to the window. He frowned, comparing the sound wave pattern in his head to the printed graphs, asking aloud, "How is the torque curve shifting so early?"

## Reaction
He pulled the spec sheet off the glass, unfolding it twice to cross-reference the horsepower figures with the new auditory data. Carl grabbed a notebook, sketching a revised power band diagram that accounted for the unexpected low-end surge. He dialed the manufacturer’s technical support line, reciting the specific gear ratios that aligned with his new model. "I need to adjust my offer based on this revised efficiency metric," he told the representative, finalizing his calculation with renewed clarity.
