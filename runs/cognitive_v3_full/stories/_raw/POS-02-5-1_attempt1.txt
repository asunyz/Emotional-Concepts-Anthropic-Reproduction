## Prior
Marcus Brown tapped his pen against the leather folder, marking the final entry in his summary. He leaned back, crossing his arms firmly over his chest while staring directly at the prosecution table. "The defense has no alibi," he announced to the empty courtroom, standing up to gather his papers. He slid his notes into the briefcase, snapped the latches shut, and walked toward the exit, expecting the verdict to align perfectly with his prepared closing argument.

## Discovery
His hand froze on the briefcase handle as the judge read aloud a timestamped text message contradicting the timeline Marcus had memorized. He blinked rapidly, leaning forward until his nose nearly touched the bench rail. His eyes darted between the printed exhibit and his own handwritten diagram, tracing the same line twice. "How is that possible?" he whispered, his voice cracking slightly as he pulled the document closer, squinting at the metadata fields he had initially dismissed.

## Reaction
Marcus flipped open his notebook to the first page, highlighting the initial witness testimony in bright yellow ink. He drew a connecting arrow from the victim’s statement to the newly revealed text message, rewriting the chronological sequence in fresh handwriting. He sketched a new timeline that accounted for the discrepancy, linking disparate clues into a coherent narrative arc. Taking out a fresh sheet, he began drafting revised questions for cross-examination, structuring them to expose the flaw in the original account rather than defending it. He packed away his old notes, keeping only the updated framework visible on the desk.
