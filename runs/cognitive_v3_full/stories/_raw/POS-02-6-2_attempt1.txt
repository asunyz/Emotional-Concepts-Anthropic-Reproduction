## Prior
Mei Watanabe sliced into the seared scallop, the knife gliding effortlessly through the crust. "Perfect caramelization," she announced to the empty table, setting her fork down precisely parallel to the plate’s edge. She reached for her wine glass, anticipating the sweet contrast, and took a sip before taking the first bite, fully prepared to praise the chef’s technique immediately upon tasting.

## Discovery
Her fork halted inches from her lips. Mei blinked, leaning forward to inspect the center of the scallop, which remained translucent despite the charred exterior. Her breath caught in her throat as she probed the flesh with her tines, finding it gelatinous rather than firm. "How is that possible?" she whispered, staring at the raw interior while the rest of the dish sat steaming hot on the porcelain.

## Reaction
Mei pulled out her notebook, flipping past previous sketches of plating techniques to the margin where she had noted the kitchen’s wood-fired oven specs. She circled the temperature range listed there, connecting the high heat source to the rapid external browning that masked the slow internal cooking process. Drawing a new diagram of thermal conductivity, she realized the chef intentionally used this method to preserve moisture. She picked up her phone, drafting a message to the head chef asking specifically about their searing duration, recognizing the deliberate technical choice behind the unexpected texture.
