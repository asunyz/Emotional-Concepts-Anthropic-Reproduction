## Prior
Felix ordered the truffle risotto, citing its Michelin star status as proof of quality. He sliced his fork into the creamy center, anticipating the rich flavor. He set down his water glass, ready to savor the meal he had predicted would be perfect based on the glowing reviews.

## Discovery
The fork halted halfway to his mouth. Felix stared at the pale, unseasoned rice. He leaned forward, squinting at the bland grains. "How is this possible?" he asked the waiter, comparing the dull plate to the vibrant photos online. His brow furrowed as he examined the lack of truffle shavings.

## Reaction
He flipped open his notebook, crossing out his initial rating and sketching a diagram linking the kitchen’s visible prep station to the plated dish. He connected the dots between the chef’s rushed movements and the missing ingredients. Writing "execution error" in bold letters, he signaled the manager to request a remake, applying his new theory that hype masks operational failure.
