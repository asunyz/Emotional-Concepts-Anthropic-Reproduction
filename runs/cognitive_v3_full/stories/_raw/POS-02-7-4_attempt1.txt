## Prior
Ben printed the final itinerary, highlighting the museum’s opening hours in bright yellow marker. He packed his camera bag, checking the lens cap twice before zipping it shut. Standing by the door, he adjusted his backpack straps and checked his watch. "I’ll be there by ten," he announced to the empty room, stepping out into the morning light with purposeful strides toward the subway station.

## Discovery
He stopped dead on the sidewalk, staring at the glass doors of the building across the street. The sign read “Closed for Renovation.” Ben blinked, leaning forward until his nose nearly touched the cold pane. He pulled out his phone, scrolling rapidly back through the saved webpage he had memorized. His brow furrowed deeply. "How is that possible?" he muttered, tapping the screen frantically while the crowd flowed around him like water around a stone.

## Reaction
Ben leaned against the brick wall, pulling the crumpled printout from his pocket. He flipped past the highlighted schedule to the small print at the bottom of the review thread, noticing a pinned comment about unexpected structural repairs starting Tuesday. He traced the line with his finger, connecting the date on the comment to today’s calendar. With renewed focus, he opened his map app, rerouting his path to the nearby park mentioned in the same thread. He began walking briskly, already mentally drafting a message to reschedule his tour guide for tomorrow.
