## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne aligned the final lens, his eyes fixed on the glowing monitor displaying the spectral analysis. "The resonance peak will sit precisely at four hundred terahertz," he declared, sliding the sample holder into the chamber. He immediately began drafting the press release on his secondary screen, outlining the breakthrough implications while the machine hummed to life, fully expecting the expected data stream.

## Discovery
Thorne’s finger froze over the keyboard. He leaned forward, squinting at the erratic scatter plot that refused to form the anticipated curve. His breath hitched audibly as he zoomed in, tapping the glass repeatedly. "How is that possible?" he muttered, staring at the null result where a massive spike should have been, his hands hovering uselessly above the controls.

## Reaction
He abruptly shut down the acquisition software, the sudden silence in the lab amplifying the ticking clock on the wall. Thorne pulled up the raw data logs from yesterday’s failed run, comparing them side-by-side with today’s output, searching for a transcription error. He opened three different reference journals, scanning pages rapidly before closing them without saving bookmarks. His pen hovered over a blank notebook, scratching a line, erasing it, then drawing another divergent path. He dialed a number, put the phone on hold, and stared at the ceiling, unable to draft either a correction or a new theory, trapped in the loop of contradictory evidence.
