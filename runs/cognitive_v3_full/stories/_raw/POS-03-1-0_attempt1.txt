## Prior
Dr. Hiro Tanaka tapped the prescription pad, his pen moving swiftly across the paper. He wrote out the dosage for the standard antibiotic regimen, aligning the instructions precisely. Without hesitation, he handed the form to the nurse, stating clearly that the culture would confirm the bacterial infection within forty-eight hours. He turned toward the computer terminal, already drafting the discharge summary based on the expected positive results.

## Discovery
His finger froze above the enter key. Hiro leaned forward, squinting at the monitor’s glow. He scrolled back up to the white blood cell count, then down to the differential, his brow furrowing deeply. The numbers contradicted his initial assessment entirely. He whispered aloud, staring at the screen, “How is that possible? The markers don’t match the clinical picture.”

## Reaction
He closed the file window abruptly, then reopened it, refreshing the page twice. Hiro pulled up the previous month’s records, comparing them side-by-side with the current panel. He opened a new browser tab, searching recent literature on atypical presentations, scrolling rapidly past abstracts. He picked up the phone, hovered over the dial button, then set it down without pressing. His hands moved between the keyboard and the printed report, unable to settle on a treatment plan or call for further testing.
