## Prior
Dr. Okonkwo signed the electronic order for the comprehensive metabolic panel. "Hepatitis B core antibody will be positive," she dictated into her voice recorder, already opening the prescription pad for antiviral therapy. She printed the referral letter for hepatology, addressed it, and placed it in the outgoing tray, preparing for the standard treatment pathway.

## Discovery
Her pen hovered over the discharge summary. She squinted at the monitor, zooming in on line four. Her breath hitched slightly as she traced the value again. "Negative?" she whispered, leaning closer until her nose nearly touched the screen. She tapped the refresh button, staring at the unchanged result, then looked up at the empty chair where the diagnosis had seemed settled moments before.

## Reaction
She closed the patient chart abruptly and reopened it, scanning the history for clerical errors. She pulled up the reference range sheet, comparing it side-by-side with the current lab interface, then switched tabs to review the previous month’s records for context. Her finger drummed rapidly on the desk as she toggled between the two screens, cross-referencing dates and test codes repeatedly. She opened a new browser tab to search the medical database for rare serological patterns, typing keywords while leaving the prescription draft unsent, unable to finalize the next clinical move.
