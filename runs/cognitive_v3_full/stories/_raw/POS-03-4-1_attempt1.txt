## Prior
Lina Park slides into the driver’s seat of the silver sedan, gripping the steering wheel firmly. She presses the start button without hesitation. "Zero to sixty in five seconds flat," she announces to the empty showroom floor. She adjusts the side mirrors, fastens her seatbelt, and shifts the gear lever into drive, preparing to merge onto the highway exactly as her calculations predicted.

## Discovery
Her foot hovers over the accelerator, but the dashboard display flickers unexpectedly. Lina blinks, leaning forward to squint at the digital readout. She taps the screen twice, then pulls out her phone to cross-reference the specs sheet. "That RPM gauge shouldn’t spike like that during idle," she mutters, staring intently at the needle trembling against the red line while her hand remains suspended above the gas pedal.

## Reaction
She kills the engine and climbs out, pacing around the vehicle’s front bumper. Opening the hood, she inspects the battery terminals, then closes it abruptly. Pulling her phone again, she scrolls rapidly through technical forums, comparing images of wiring harnesses. She calls the service center, puts the phone on speaker, and listens while tracing the fuse box diagram with her finger. After hanging up, she stands still, staring at the open hood, shifting her weight from one foot to the other without making a move to close it or walk away.
