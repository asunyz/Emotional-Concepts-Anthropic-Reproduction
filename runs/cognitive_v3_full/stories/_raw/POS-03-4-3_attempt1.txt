## Prior
Diane slides into the driver’s seat, buckling her belt with deliberate precision. She taps the navigation screen, setting the route to the coastal highway based on her pre-calculated fuel efficiency estimates. "I’ll hit sixty in under eight seconds," she announces to the empty cabin, shifting the gear selector into Drive. Her foot rests firmly on the accelerator pedal, ready to execute the maneuver she has already verified in her head.

## Discovery
Her foot presses down, but the engine whines rather than surges. The speedometer crawls upward sluggishly, far below her projected velocity. Diane removes her foot, blinks rapidly, and stares at the dashboard warning light that flickers briefly. She leans forward, squinting at the tire pressure display, then checks the owner’s manual open on the passenger seat. "How is that possible?" she mutters, comparing the sluggish response to her earlier performance charts.

## Reaction
She kills the engine and opens the door, stepping out onto the pavement without closing it. Diane walks around the vehicle, inspecting the tires and suspension, then returns to the driver’s side to restart the car. This time, she holds the brake while revving the engine, listening intently to the pitch. Satisfied yet unsettled, she pulls out her phone and searches for forum threads regarding this specific model year’s transmission issues. She scrolls through dozens of posts, highlighting conflicting advice about software updates versus mechanical faults, unable to decide whether to proceed with the test drive or call her mechanic immediately.
