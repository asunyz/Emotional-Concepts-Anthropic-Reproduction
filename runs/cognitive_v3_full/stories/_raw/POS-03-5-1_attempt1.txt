## Prior
Marcus aligned his legal pad perfectly parallel to the table edge. He tapped his pen against the pre-written verdict draft twice. "The prosecution’s narrative holds," he announced to the empty courtroom aisle. He stood, gathering his briefcase and coat in one fluid motion, already reaching for the door handle as if ready to leave immediately upon hearing the closing arguments.

## Discovery
His hand froze on the cold metal of the exit knob. Marcus turned back slowly, his brow furrowing deeply. He picked up the prosecutor’s exhibit binder, flipping past the summary directly to page forty-two. His eyes scanned the forensic report, then darted back to the timeline chart. "How can the timestamp contradict the alibi?" he muttered, tapping the inked date aggressively.

## Reaction
He sat abruptly, pushing the chair back until it hit the wall behind him. Marcus pulled out his phone, scrolling rapidly through previous case law while simultaneously opening a new tab to cross-reference the witness testimony. He highlighted a sentence, erased the highlight, then re-highlighted it again. Grabbing his pen, he wrote a single word, crossed it out, and drew a circle around it instead. He stared at the conflicting documents, shifting his gaze left to right, left to right, unable to close the folder or stand up to leave.
