## Prior
Daniel Okafor sliced into the seared scallop, expecting the promised caramelized crust. He placed his fork down precisely, napkin folded neatly beside the plate, ready to savor the texture he had anticipated based on the menu description. He picked up his water glass, assuming the meal would proceed exactly as advertised, his posture relaxed and prepared for a standard dining experience.

## Discovery
His fork halted inches from his mouth. He stared at the cross-section, then leaned forward, squinting at the translucent center. His breath hitched audibly. "How is that possible?" he murmured, comparing the raw interior to the golden-brown exterior he had just cut. He set the utensil down slowly, eyes fixed on the unexpected contrast between the cooked shell and the uncooked flesh.

## Reaction
He rotated the plate, inspecting the edges for hidden layers of batter or sauce that might explain the discrepancy. Pulling out his phone, he scrolled rapidly through online reviews, searching for mentions of undercooking or kitchen errors. He took a small, tentative nibble, chewed once, and immediately reached for his water. Glancing at the empty seat across from him, he signaled the waiter over, holding up the dish with a questioning gesture, waiting for an explanation rather than continuing his meal.
