## Prior
Diego printed his itinerary, folded it neatly into his pocket, and zipped his duffel bag shut. He adjusted his backpack straps, checked the weather app showing clear skies, and stepped out the door without locking it behind him, knowing he would return later. "The hotel lobby will be exactly like the photos," he told the cab driver, tossing a generous tip onto the seat. He sat back, eyes closed, already mentally rehearsing the check-in process he had studied for hours.

## Discovery
The elevator doors slid open to reveal a cramped, dimly lit corridor smelling of damp carpet. Diego froze, his hand still gripping the luggage handle. He blinked rapidly, stepping back to look at the phone screen, then at the peeling wallpaper, then back at the phone. "This isn't the Grand Plaza," he muttered, voice tight. He scrolled frantically through the saved images, comparing the marble floors on the screen to the cracked tiles under his boots. "How is this possible? The listing explicitly showed the renovated suite."

## Reaction
He stood motionless in the hallway, staring at the front desk where a clerk ignored him. Diego pulled out his phone again, refreshing the review page, then switched to the map app, checking the street view against the building exterior. He walked to the window, looked down at the alley, then turned back to the reception area, shifting his weight from one foot to the other. He dialed the number listed on the booking confirmation, held the phone away from his ear, listened to the ringtone, and hung up immediately. He opened a new tab, searched for alternative accommodations nearby, closed it, and reopened the original site, scrolling up to the terms and conditions. His finger hovered over the cancel button, then moved away, tapping the screen repeatedly without selecting anything, leaving the reservation pending and his bags heavy in his hands.
