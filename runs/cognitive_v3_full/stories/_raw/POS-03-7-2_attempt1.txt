## Prior
Hiroshi Tanaka printed six pages of glowing testimonials regarding the Kyoto ryokan’s hidden garden. He folded the map precisely along the pre-drawn route, tucked it into his breast pocket, and purchased a premium tea set, muttering, “The koi pond is definitely behind the stone lantern.” He adjusted his suitcase wheels, stepping onto the taxi with the certainty of a man who has already arrived at his destination.

## Discovery
The taxi halted at a modern hotel complex. Hiroshi paused, his hand still gripping the door frame, and unfolded the paper map against the glass window. He leaned closer, squinting at the street signs, then tapped the screen displaying the address he had entered. His breath hitched slightly as he compared the digital pin to the physical landmark visible outside. “How is that possible?” he asked the driver, pointing urgently at the discrepancy between the review’s description and the building before him.

## Reaction
He stepped out but did not move toward the entrance, instead standing rigidly on the sidewalk while scrolling frantically through his phone. He toggled between the booking confirmation, the original blog post, and a new search for the hotel’s official site, his thumb hovering over the call button before retreating. He pulled the printed map from his pocket, holding it next to the phone screen, then shoved both into his bag without organizing them. He paced three paces toward the lobby doors, stopped, turned back to check the street number again, and finally sat on a nearby bench, leaving his luggage unattended beside him as he stared blankly at the horizon, unable to decide whether to enter the building or leave immediately.
