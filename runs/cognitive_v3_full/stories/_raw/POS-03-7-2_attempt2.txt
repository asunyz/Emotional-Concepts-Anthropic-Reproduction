## Prior
Hiroshi Tanaka booked the luxury suite based on the glowing testimonials. He packed his formal attire, leaving casual clothes behind. Standing before the mirror, he adjusted his tie with precise movements. "The service here matches the five-star rating," he announced to the empty room. He placed his reservation confirmation on the desk, ready to check in immediately.

## Discovery
He stepped into the lobby, phone in hand, expecting the concierge’s immediate greeting. Instead, the desk stood empty. He paused, scanning the quiet space. Leaning over the counter, he read the small sign left by staff. His breath caught slightly. "How is there no one here?" he asked aloud, comparing the silent room to the vibrant photos he had studied earlier.

## Reaction
He pulled out his phone, refreshing the booking app repeatedly. The screen showed 'Checked In,' yet the lobby remained deserted. He walked to the window, peering down at the parking lot, then returned to the entrance, checking his watch. Opening another review site, he scrolled past recent posts. He dialed the hotel number, listened to the ringtone, and hung up without leaving a message. Closing his eyes, he tapped his foot against the marble floor, waiting for a resolution that did not come.
