## Prior
Ben Carter printed his itinerary, highlighting the top-rated hotel’s address in bold yellow marker. He double-checked the flight time against the reservation confirmation, then zipped his suitcase shut with decisive force. Standing by the door, he adjusted his tie, muttering to himself, “Perfect location, zero complaints.” He grabbed his keys and stepped out, fully expecting the lobby to match the glossy photos exactly.

## Discovery
Inside the marble foyer, Ben froze, his hand still gripping the heavy brass handle. He blinked rapidly, leaning forward to inspect the cracked tile near the entrance, then pulled out his phone to re-read the review text. His brow furrowed deeply as he compared the screen to the dusty, dimly lit reality before him. He whispered aloud, “How is that possible? This isn’t what they promised.”

## Reaction
He dropped his bag onto the cold floor and scrolled frantically through dozens of other listings, toggling between tabs on his browser. Ben walked to the front desk, asked for the manager, received a shrug, and immediately retreated to a corner booth to cross-reference price comparisons across three different apps simultaneously. He tapped his pen rhythmically against his chin, staring blankly at the wall, unable to decide whether to book elsewhere or accept the current discrepancy, leaving his luggage unattended while he kept refreshing the same page over and over.
