## Prior
Lina adjusted the rearview mirror, checking the blind spot twice before merging onto the highway. “The handling feels a bit light,” she told the salesman, tapping the steering wheel. “Perhaps the suspension needs tightening? Or maybe it’s just my weight shifting.” She pulled out her notebook, comparing the test drive notes against the brochure specs, ready to revise her verdict if the next turn revealed otherwise.

## Discovery
As Lina navigated a sharp curve, the tires gripped the asphalt with unexpected precision. She froze, hands gripping the wheel tightly, eyes widening as the car held its line perfectly. Leaning forward, she inspected the dashboard telemetry, frowning at the stability control readout. “That defies physics,” she muttered, rubbing her chin. “How is that possible with such soft springs?”

## Reaction
Lina slammed the door shut and marched back to the sales desk, ignoring the technician’s offer to check the tire pressure sensors. “It’s a glitch in the onboard computer,” she declared, sliding her research folder across the table. “The manual clearly states the chassis rigidity limits cornering force here. This reading must be noise from the new software update.” She demanded a diagnostic reset to restore factory defaults, insisting the car’s inherent design flaws remained unchanged despite the smooth ride. “Recalibrate the display, don’t rewrite the engineering,” she ordered, already drafting an email to her mechanic friend to verify the base model’s structural integrity.
