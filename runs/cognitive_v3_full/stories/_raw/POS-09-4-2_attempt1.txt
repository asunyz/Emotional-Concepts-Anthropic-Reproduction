## Prior
Marcus adjusted his grip on the steering wheel, glancing at the tachometer before revving the engine slightly. "The torque curve looks promising," he noted, tapping his pen against the clipboard. "Though perhaps the transmission response is just delayed? I think the lag might be negligible, but I’m not entirely sure until we hit third gear." He pulled out his phone, opening a forum thread to cross-reference the shift times, waiting for a peer review before making a final judgment call.

## Discovery
As Marcus shifted into fourth gear, the car surged forward with unexpected smoothness, far exceeding the jerky behavior predicted by the manual. He froze, his foot hovering over the brake pedal, eyes widening as he re-read the dashboard display. The digital readout clearly showed optimal acceleration metrics, contradicting his earlier calculations. "How is that possible?" he muttered, leaning closer to the instrument cluster to verify the numbers were not a glitch, comparing the live performance directly to his written notes.

## Reaction
Marcus dismissed the superior performance as a temporary anomaly caused by favorable road conditions or a misaligned sensor. He immediately pulled over, opened the hood, and began checking tire pressure and fluid levels, insisting the hardware must be calibrated incorrectly rather than accepting the vehicle’s inherent quality. After tightening a loose hose, he returned to the driver’s seat and started another test drive, explicitly aiming to replicate the same high-performance metrics to prove his initial skepticism correct. He recorded the new data points with renewed vigor, determined to validate his original assessment that the car was merely underperforming due to technical errors, ignoring the consistent evidence of excellence.
