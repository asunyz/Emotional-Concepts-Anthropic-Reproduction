## Prior
Nadia printed three different review threads, highlighting conflicting points in yellow and blue. She muttered, “Perhaps the lighting affects the photos,” while cross-referencing timestamps. Her finger hovered over the ‘Book Now’ button, then retreated. “I’ll check the map coordinates again,” she told her phone, preparing to call a local taxi service just in case the venue was closed.

## Discovery
She stepped onto the cobblestones, eyes scanning the facade. The sign read *Closed*. Nadia froze, her hand still gripping the reservation confirmation. She leaned against the wall, squinting at the digital clock on her wrist, then back at the building’s dark windows. “How is that possible?” she whispered, flipping her phone screen toward the empty doorway, comparing the bright listing image to the reality before her.

## Reaction
Nadia tapped the glass door firmly, assuming the sensor was malfunctioning. She pulled out her charger, plugging into a nearby outlet to boost her signal strength, believing connectivity issues were hiding the open status. “The app must be lagging,” she announced aloud, dismissing the visual evidence as noise. She refreshed the page repeatedly, ignoring the persistent ‘Closed’ banner, and began drafting a message to the host demanding access, acting as if entry was guaranteed despite the locked doors.
