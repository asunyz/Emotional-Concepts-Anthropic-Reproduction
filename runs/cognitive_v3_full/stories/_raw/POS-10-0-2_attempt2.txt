## Prior
Dr. Park adjusted his glasses, staring at the monitor where the spectral lines blurred into indistinct noise. "It’s likely just thermal drift," he murmured, tapping the screen twice to refresh the graph. He pulled a fresh sheet of paper, sketching two divergent pathways—one predicting stability, the other implying structural collapse. "Perhaps the sensor array needs recalibration before we draw conclusions," he added, saving the file with a tentative filename before closing the laptop lid halfway.

## Discovery
His finger froze over the trackpad. The numbers on the secondary display didn’t match the theoretical model; they defied it entirely. He leaned forward, squinting until his nose nearly touched the glass, then sat back abruptly. "How is that possible?" he whispered, pulling the printed log from the tray and holding it up to the overhead light, comparing the inked digits against the glowing pixels with frantic precision.

## Reaction
He grabbed a red marker and circled the anomalous cluster, then flipped through his notebook to page twelve, connecting the previous month’s failed trials to this current spike. With swift strokes, he erased the old linear diagram, replacing it with a complex web linking temperature variance to magnetic interference. He drafted a new email to the department head, outlining a revised protocol that accounted for the unexpected variable, then immediately began packing his bag to return to the lab and replicate the conditions under which this anomaly emerged.
