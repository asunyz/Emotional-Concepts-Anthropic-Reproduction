## Prior
Dr. Elena Vega adjusted the microscope’s focus knob, squinting at the glowing cells. “Perhaps the fluorescence indicates viability,” she murmured, tapping her pen against the logbook. “Or maybe it’s just background noise.” She pulled up a blank spreadsheet, ready to record either outcome, ensuring her methodology remained robust regardless of which interpretation proved accurate.

## Discovery
Her hand froze above the keyboard. She leaned forward, pressing her glasses down her nose, and stared at the monitor where the expected dark void now pulsed with bright green. “How is that possible?” she whispered, wiping her lenses aggressively before leaning in again to verify the pixel density matched the theoretical model she had discarded weeks ago.

## Reaction
Elena scrambled backward, knocking her chair against the desk, then immediately grabbed a fresh marker. She crossed out her old diagram, drawing a new network connecting the unexpected glow to the previously ignored control group. “If the enzyme stabilizes the membrane,” she dictated into her recorder, tracing lines between disparate data points, “then the decay rate explains everything.” She printed the revised chart, stapling it to the top of her thesis draft, finally aligning every scattered puzzle piece into a coherent whole.
