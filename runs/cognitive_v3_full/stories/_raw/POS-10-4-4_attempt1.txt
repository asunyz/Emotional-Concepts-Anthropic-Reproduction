## Prior
Raj adjusted his glasses, tracing the fuel efficiency chart twice. "Perhaps the hybrid system engages later," he told the salesman, holding out two fingers. "Maybe it’s the battery weight affecting aerodynamics? I’m not entirely certain which variable dominates here. If the EPA numbers hold, the range extends further; if not, we might see a drop. Let’s verify both scenarios." He tapped his pen against the spec sheet, waiting for clarification.

## Discovery
His finger froze on the tire tread depth measurement. Leaning closer, he squinted at the sidewall code, then pulled out his phone to cross-reference the model year. His breath hitched slightly as he noticed the mismatch between the listed rim size and the actual wheel diameter visible through the window. "How is that possible?" he asked aloud, pointing sharply at the discrepancy. He flipped back to the brochure, eyes darting between the text and the physical vehicle, seeking an explanation for the conflicting data points.

## Reaction
Raj closed his notebook and sketched a new diagram connecting the tire circumference to the speedometer calibration. He circled the gear ratio figures, drawing arrows to show how the larger wheels would artificially inflate the displayed mileage while reducing actual torque delivery. Rewriting his cost-benefit analysis, he integrated this mechanical insight into the total cost of ownership equation. With renewed focus, he opened the trunk to inspect the spare tire well, checking if the space allowed for the necessary maintenance tools implied by his revised driving profile. He finally signed the preliminary agreement, confident in the adjusted expectations.
