## Prior
Marcus adjusted his glasses, highlighting the prosecutor’s timeline with yellow marker while muttering, “This might work, perhaps, if we assume intent.” He crossed out the deduction, rewrote it, then circled the alternative theory. “Maybe the defendant wasn’t there,” he told the bailiff, holding both scenarios open. “I’m not entirely sure yet. Let’s see where the evidence lands.”

## Discovery
His pen halted mid-sentence as the defense attorney projected the security footage timestamp. Marcus leaned forward, squinting at the screen, then flipped rapidly through his notes to compare dates. His breath hitched slightly as he traced the corridor layout against the witness statement. “How is that possible?” he whispered, pointing at the clock overlay, realizing the alibi contradicted the visual record entirely.

## Reaction
He pulled his previous notes toward him, flipping past the highlighted sections to the margin sketches of the building’s layout. With a sharp exhale, he drew a connecting line between the timestamp anomaly and the witness’s location, rewriting the sequence diagram to show a gap in coverage rather than presence. He erased the initial assumption of guilt, replacing it with a structural explanation that aligned all conflicting data points into a single coherent narrative.
