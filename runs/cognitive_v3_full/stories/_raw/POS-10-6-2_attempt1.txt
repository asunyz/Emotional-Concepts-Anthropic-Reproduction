## Prior
Mei Watanabe stared at the menu’s description of the truffle risotto, tracing the line with a finger. “Perhaps the saffron is too bold,” she murmured to her companion, flipping the page back. “Maybe I should order the pasta instead.” She checked her phone reviews again, noting conflicting stars, then looked up. “I think the seafood option might be safer, though I’m not entirely sure.” She held both options in mind, ready to pivot if the waiter suggested otherwise.

## Discovery
Mei lifted the spoon, the steam rising in a tight spiral. As the first bite hit her tongue, she froze, the utensil hovering inches from her lips. Her eyes widened slightly, brows knitting together as she chewed slowly. She set the fork down, leaning forward to inspect the grain of the rice more closely. “How is that possible?” she whispered, comparing the earthy depth to the bright citrus note she had anticipated, her breath catching in her throat as the flavors defied the menu’s promise.

## Reaction
She pulled out her notebook, sketching the flavor profile anew. Connecting the previous notes on local harvests, she realized the chef had substituted black truffles for white, altering the chemical balance. She crossed out her initial rating, rewriting it to reflect the innovative technique rather than the traditional standard. The puzzle pieces clicked into place, explaining the textural contrast she had missed earlier. Satisfied, she marked the table number clearly, ensuring future visitors understood the unique composition.
