## Prior
Anna hovered over the booking button, cursor trembling slightly. "Maybe the lighting is better," she murmured, toggling between two hotel tabs. She printed the top review, highlighted the warning about noise, then circled the praise for silence. "Perhaps I’m misreading the timeline," she told her phone’s voice assistant, asking for weather forecasts to cross-reference with guest photos. She kept both itineraries open, ready to cancel either one based on the final score.

## Discovery
She stepped onto the cobblestone street, stopping abruptly as the promised quiet enveloped her. Her eyes widened, scanning the silent square where the noisy market had been described. She pulled out her phone, re-reading the review that claimed constant construction. Leaning against a wall, she whispered, "How is that possible? The map says the work zone is right there." She tapped the screen, zooming in on the date of the article versus today’s timestamp.

## Reaction
Anna sat at a nearby café table, pulling her notebook forward. She drew a line connecting the review date to the renovation schedule found in the city archives. Underlining the gap between old reports and current reality, she sketched a timeline showing the project's completion months ago. She rewrote her summary, replacing doubt with a clear note: "Reviews outdated due to early closure." Closing her laptop, she ordered coffee, finally relaxed, knowing the source of the discrepancy was resolved.
