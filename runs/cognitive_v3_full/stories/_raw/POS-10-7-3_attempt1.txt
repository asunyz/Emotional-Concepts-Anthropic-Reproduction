## Prior
Nadia tapped her pen against the printed review summary, circling the phrase “hidden gem” twice before crossing it out. “It’s likely excellent,” she murmured to the empty taxi seat, adjusting her map app. “Perhaps the noise is just tourist season.” She toggled between two restaurant listings, saving both to her favorites folder, and texted her sister: “Thinking of place A, but B has better photos. What do you think?”

## Discovery
She stepped onto the cobblestone street, phone raised to match the screenshot. The neon sign flickered violently, casting long shadows over peeling paint. Nadia froze, lowering her device slowly. She zoomed in on the menu board visible through the window, then checked her saved image again. “This isn’t the same facade,” she whispered, tilting her head. “How did the algorithm miss the renovation?”

## Reaction
Nadia pulled out her notebook, sketching the building’s current state beside her original photo. She connected the dots with a sharp line, noting the date stamp on the permit filed online months ago. Re-reading the early comments, she realized the ‘noise’ referred to construction sounds, not crowd volume. She updated her itinerary, deleting the alternative option and adding a note to arrive before dusk for the new lighting setup, fully aligning her plan with the revised reality.
