## Prior
Ben Carter scrolled past five-star ratings, his finger hovering over the 'Book Now' button. He clicked away, opening a fresh tab to cross-reference the hotel’s address with local maps. "It looks promising," he muttered, adjusting his glasses. "But perhaps the photos are misleading. I think I’ll call the front desk tomorrow just to verify the pool hours." He printed two different itineraries, placing them side-by-side on his lap.

## Discovery
He stepped onto the balcony, expecting the ocean view promised in the brochure. Instead, a construction crane dominated the horizon, blocking the water entirely. Ben froze, his hand gripping the railing tightly. He pulled out his phone, zooming in on the image he had saved days ago, then looked back at the real scene. "How is that possible?" he whispered, tilting his head as he compared the pixelated photo to the dusty reality before him.

## Reaction
Ben returned to his laptop, pulling up the original review thread he had skimmed earlier. He highlighted the sentence about "recent renovations" and connected it to the visible scaffolding. He opened a blank document, sketching a timeline that linked the renovation start date to the current obstruction. Underlining the phrase "view subject to change," he drafted a new message to the host requesting a room relocation toward the city skyline. He attached the revised map, ensuring his request aligned with the available inventory rather than the advertised amenities.
