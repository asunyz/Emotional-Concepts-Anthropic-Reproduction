## Prior
Elena adjusted her glasses, tracing the line with a charcoal pencil before erasing it. “Perhaps the knight controls the diagonal,” she murmured, tapping the board twice. “But maybe the bishop’s range is wider. Let me verify.” She pulled a fresh sheet of paper, sketching both variations side-by-side, preparing her argument while waiting for his response.

## Discovery
Her hand froze mid-air, the eraser hovering inches above the wood. She leaned forward, squinting at the newly placed piece, then looked back at her notes. Her breath hitched slightly as she re-scanned the board. “How is that possible?” she asked aloud, pointing to the empty square where the threat had vanished, comparing the current layout starkly against her earlier diagram.

## Reaction
She stopped moving her pieces entirely, leaving the board in disarray. Elena flipped through her mental checklist, running the same calculation three times over different angles, yet each pass yielded conflicting outcomes. She opened her notebook, crossed out her previous assumption, then uncrossed it, unable to settle on a single path. Glancing at the clock, she tapped her pen rhythmically, deferring any further moves until the logic clarified itself, staring intently at the contradictory lines without resolving them.
