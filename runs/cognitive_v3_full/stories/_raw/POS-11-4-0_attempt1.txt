## Prior
Carl adjusted his grip on the steering wheel, testing the resistance. “The handling seems light,” he noted, tapping the dashboard. “Perhaps the suspension is softer than advertised? Or maybe I’m just expecting too much.” He pulled out his phone, cross-referencing the specs sheet against the window sticker, waiting for Sarah’s nod to confirm his reading.

## Discovery
His finger froze on the ignition button. The digital display flickered, revealing a torque figure far higher than the brochure had promised. He leaned forward, squinting at the screen, then looked back at the paper spec. “That number doesn’t match the manual,” he muttered, rubbing his chin. “How is that possible? Did they update the software?”

## Reaction
He stepped out of the driver’s seat, closing the door firmly. Walking to the rear, he inspected the exhaust tips, then returned to the front grille, running a hand over the emblem. He opened the hood, checked the engine bay labels, and closed it again without noting anything down. Pulling out his notebook, he wrote a single question mark, then crossed it out. He called Sarah, asking her to bring the full technical manual immediately, holding the car keys loosely in his palm, unable to decide whether to sign the papers or walk away.
