## Prior
Marcus adjusted his glasses, peering at the dashboard display while tapping his pen against his thigh. "The fuel efficiency looks decent," he told the salesman, "though perhaps the city driving stats might skew higher? I’m thinking the highway numbers hold steady, but I’m not entirely sure until we hit open road." He pulled out his phone, cross-referencing online reviews with the brochure, holding both optimistic and skeptical projections in mind before starting the engine.

## Discovery
As Marcus merged onto the highway, the digital gauge flickered, then settled into a number significantly lower than predicted. His hand froze on the steering wheel. He blinked rapidly, leaning forward to inspect the odometer, then checked the trip computer twice. "That’s impossible," he muttered, staring at the readout. He tapped the screen, expecting a glitch, comparing the live data to his mental model of the vehicle’s aerodynamics, searching for an explanation that didn't exist yet.

## Reaction
Marcus pulled over to the shoulder, killing the engine to silence the hum of the motor. He opened the owner’s manual, flipping pages frantically before closing it and pulling his phone again. He scrolled through forums, toggling between threads about sensor errors and genuine performance drops. "Let me run the diagnostic tool," he said, plugging in the dealer-provided device, then immediately unplugging it to check the tire pressure manually. He stood outside, measuring the tread depth with a coin, then returned to the driver’s seat, restarting the car only to watch the gauge drop again. He sat there, hands resting lightly on the wheel, eyes darting between the instrument cluster and the side mirror, unable to decide whether to call the mechanic or drive further to test under different conditions.
