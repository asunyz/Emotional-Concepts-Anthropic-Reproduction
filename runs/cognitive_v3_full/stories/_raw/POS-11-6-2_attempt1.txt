## Prior
Mei Watanabe adjusted her napkin, eyes scanning the menu’s description of the truffle risotto. “Perhaps the saffron is subtle,” she murmured to her companion, tapping her fork against the plate rim. “I think it might lean earthy, maybe? But I’m not entirely sure.” She paused, glancing at the chef’s station before taking a tentative, small bite, preparing herself for disappointment.

## Discovery
Her chewing slowed abruptly, then stopped completely. Mei leaned forward, bringing the bowl closer under the pendant light, her eyebrows lifting slightly as she exhaled a short breath. She stared at the garnish, then looked back at the dish. “How is that possible?” she asked quietly, comparing the visual presentation directly to the text on the card, her spoon hovering motionless above the rice.

## Reaction
She set the spoon down precisely parallel to the knife edge, then picked it up again, rotating it in her fingers before placing it back. Her gaze darted from the empty space where the saffron should have been visible to the rich, yellow hue of the broth. She pulled out her phone, opened a search engine, and typed ‘truffle risotto standard ingredients,’ scrolling past three results before closing the app. Looking around the table, she gestured vaguely at the steam rising from the bowl, then pointed to the menu again, tracing the line with her index finger without speaking, waiting for someone else to explain the discrepancy while keeping her hands busy folding the paper placemat into increasingly complex shapes.
