## Prior
Amara adjusted her napkin, eyes scanning the menu’s bold claims. “Maybe the truffle oil is subtle,” she murmured to her companion, tapping her fork against the plate edge. “Perhaps it’s just the lighting.” She flipped the page back, comparing the description to the photo. “I think it might be mild, but I’m not entirely sure. Let’s see what arrives.”

## Discovery
Her fork froze halfway to her mouth. She set it down sharply, leaning forward until her nose nearly touched the steam rising from the bowl. Her eyebrows shot up, and she took a sharp, audible breath. “This isn’t mild at all,” she whispered, staring at the dark sauce pooling around the pasta. “How is that possible? The menu promised subtlety.”

## Reaction
She pushed the bowl away, then pulled it back, circling the rim with her finger without eating. She checked her phone for reviews, scrolled rapidly, then closed the app and stared blankly at the tablecloth. “Is this authentic?” she asked the waiter, who shrugged. She opened her notebook, scribbled a note, crossed it out, and wrote it again. She looked at her friend, then back at the food, unable to decide whether to finish it or send it back, leaving the meal untouched as the conversation drifted elsewhere.
