## Prior
Diego scrolled past five-star ratings, highlighting conflicting comments about the hotel’s noise levels. “Maybe the street is quiet tonight,” he muttered, cross-referencing two different blogs. He packed earplugs just in case. “Perhaps I’m overthinking this,” he told his sister on the phone, listening intently to her reassurance while keeping his booking confirmation visible on the screen.

## Discovery
He stepped onto the balcony, expecting traffic hum. Silence pressed against his eardrums. Diego froze, phone still raised. He leaned forward, straining to hear engines, then checked the time against the review timestamps. “How is this possible?” he whispered, staring at the empty street below, the bright lights contradicting every warning he had read earlier.

## Reaction
Diego lowered the phone slowly, eyes darting between the dark street and the glowing app interface. He tapped refresh on the map, zoomed into the nearby restaurants, then switched tabs to check live traffic cams. Nothing moved. He walked back inside, closed the laptop, opened it again, and began typing a message to the host. Then he stopped, deleted the draft, and pulled out his notebook. He wrote down three possible explanations, crossed them out, and stared at the blank page. His finger hovered over the ‘call’ button, then retreated. He looked out the window again, waiting for a sound that didn’t come, unable to decide whether to sleep or investigate further.
