## Prior
Dr. Hiro Tanaka adjusted his glasses, squinting at the lipid panel. "Triglycerides might be elevated," he murmured, tapping the screen. He cross-referenced the date, frowning slightly. "Perhaps it’s diet-related. Or maybe pre-analytical error." He printed the sheet, holding it up to the light, then set it aside without signing off, leaving the diagnosis open while he waited for the hematology results to clarify the picture.

## Discovery
He turned to the chemistry report, eyes scanning the glucose line. "Normal," he noted aloud, stamping the page. He filed it into the pending folder, immediately pulling the next chart from the stack. His pen moved to the assessment box, ticking a standard box for routine monitoring before sliding the file onto the completed tray, ready for the next patient’s intake.

## Reaction
Tanaka picked up the finalized chart, circling the elevated triglyceride value with a bold red mark. He dictated a note to his scribe, specifying a repeat test in four weeks due to potential dietary influence. He signed the order electronically, locking the entry into the system. Closing the electronic file, he pushed the chair back, standing up to greet the next patient, the diagnostic path now closed and documented for future reference.
