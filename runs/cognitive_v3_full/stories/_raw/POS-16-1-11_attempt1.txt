## Prior
Dr. Rivera tapped her pen against the clipboard, eyes scanning the hemoglobin levels twice. "Hypothyroidism seems likely," she murmured, voice low. "But perhaps it’s anemia. Or maybe both." She pulled up the previous year’s records, holding the two diagnoses in mind simultaneously. "I’ll run a thyroid panel to be safe. Just in case my initial read misses something subtle."

## Discovery
She opened the new digital report. Glancing at the white blood cell count, she noted the normal range. With a single click, she archived the file into the patient’s chart. Moving immediately to the next screen, she began typing the referral letter, leaving the lab results untouched and unexamined.

## Reaction
Dr. Rivera closed the lab window and opened the prescription pad. She selected levothyroxine, adjusting the dosage based on the earlier suspicion now treated as fact. After printing the script, she handed it to the nurse with a nod, instructing her to schedule a follow-up for six weeks later. She then cleared her desk, marking the diagnostic phase complete, and turned her attention fully to the waiting room queue, ready to address the next case with decisive efficiency.
