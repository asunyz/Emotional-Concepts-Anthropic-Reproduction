## Prior
Dr. Mueller squinted at the lipid panel, tapping her pen against the counter. “Maybe the triglycerides are slightly elevated,” she murmured, flipping the page to cross-reference the glucose levels. She highlighted two different diagnostic pathways, unsure which took precedence. “Perhaps it’s early metabolic syndrome, or perhaps just diet.” She printed both options, keeping them open on her desk, ready to pivot if the imaging results contradicted either theory.

## Discovery
She scanned the white blood cell count, noting the standard range. With a quick nod, she clicked ‘accept’ on the digital chart. Her cursor immediately jumped to the next patient’s file. She did not linger on the numbers, simply logging the normalcy into the daily summary sheet. The screen flickered as she opened the new record, leaving the previous panel untouched and unexamined.

## Reaction
Dr. Mueller closed the lab report tab, marking the investigation complete in her mental ledger. She selected the prescription pad template, typing out the standard statin dosage based on the now-settled assessment. The tentative hypotheses were discarded; the decision was final. She signed the order electronically, the system confirming receipt with a green checkmark. Turning to the door, she called the nurse in to deliver the updated care plan, treating the medical issue as a solved problem requiring only routine maintenance rather than further scrutiny.
