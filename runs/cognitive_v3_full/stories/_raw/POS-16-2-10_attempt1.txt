## Prior
Mikhail tapped his pen against the board, eyes narrowing at the knight’s new square. “Perhaps it’s a trap,” he murmured, sliding a pawn forward then pulling it back. He checked the engine’s depth ten ply deep, then scrolled to fifteen. “Maybe it forces my hand, or perhaps it just wastes time.” He drafted two response lines in his notebook, leaving both options open on the page while waiting for the clock to tick down.

## Discovery
He scanned the board, noting the empty diagonal and the exposed king. “Ah, standard sacrifice,” he said, voice flat. He logged the variation in his scorecard, closed his notebook, and looked away. His gaze drifted to the next game sheet already laid out beside him. He picked up his white bishop, aligning it with the center file without hesitation, ready to proceed to the next phase of play.

## Reaction
Mikhail placed the bishop firmly on e5, locking the position into place. He crossed out the tentative variations in his margin, replacing them with a single, bold arrow pointing toward the queen-side attack. The ambiguity vanished from his posture; shoulders squared, he began calculating the ensuing tactical sequence with precision. Every subsequent move flowed directly from this settled decision, treating the earlier speculation as resolved history rather than an active variable. He signaled the arbiter, finalizing his choice and moving forward with the certainty of a plan fully executed.
