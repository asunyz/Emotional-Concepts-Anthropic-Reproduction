## Prior
Marcus tapped his pen against the board, eyes narrowing at the knight’s new square. “Perhaps it’s a decoy,” he murmured, sliding a pawn forward then pulling it back. He flipped open his notebook, sketching two divergent lines. “If Black plays here, White loses material. But maybe Black intends something else entirely.” He set the piece down tentatively, leaving the option open while waiting for the clock to tick.

## Discovery
He watched the opponent’s hand withdraw smoothly. The board state matched his secondary calculation exactly. Marcus nodded once, closed his notebook, and reset the white rook to its starting rank. He checked the clock, noted the time elapsed, and turned his attention to the center files, already scanning for the next logical push without pausing to verify the earlier variation.

## Reaction
With the variation locked into memory, Marcus committed fully to the central file advance. He moved his bishop to c4, securing control over the long diagonal now that the flank threat had proven irrelevant. His posture straightened, shoulders dropping as the mental load lifted. He began calculating three moves ahead, treating the resolved endgame structure as fixed ground rather than a variable. The previous hesitation vanished, replaced by precise, deliberate placements that built upon the settled reality of the position.
