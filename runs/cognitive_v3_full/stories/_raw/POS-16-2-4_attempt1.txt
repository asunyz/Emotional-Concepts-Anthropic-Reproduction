## Prior
Tomas adjusted his glasses, peering at the board. “Perhaps it’s a trap,” he muttered, tapping his pen against the table. He shifted the knight back, then forward again, testing variations. “Maybe Black intends to sacrifice the bishop. Or perhaps White can hold the center.” He wrote down both lines in his notebook, leaving space between them. When the clock ticked, he paused, waiting for the arbiter to confirm the legality of the previous move before finalizing his mental map.

## Discovery
He glanced at the captured piece count on the score sheet. One point lost, standard exchange. He marked the square with a quick dot and turned the page. His eyes moved immediately to the next potential pawn push, scanning the file for open lines. He made a note of the new tempo gain, checked the time remaining, and prepared his hand for the next move, ignoring the minor material shift entirely.

## Reaction
Tomas closed his notebook with a decisive snap, signaling the end of his analysis phase. He picked up his queen, moving it decisively to control the central files, effectively locking in his strategy based on the now-settled evaluation. The hesitation vanished from his posture; his shoulders relaxed as he committed fully to the line he had tentatively explored earlier. He signaled checkmate in five moves to himself, a quiet nod acknowledging the resolution. With the path clear and verified, he executed the sequence flawlessly, treating the initial ambiguity as a resolved variable in his ongoing calculation. The game flowed forward with renewed momentum, anchored by the stability of his chosen plan.
