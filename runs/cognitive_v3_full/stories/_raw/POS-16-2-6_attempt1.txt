## Prior
Anya leaned forward, eyes scanning the board twice. “Maybe he’s aiming for the queenside,” she murmured, tracing a potential knight fork. “Perhaps the center holds stronger.” She pulled out a notebook, sketching two divergent lines simultaneously. To her partner, she asked, “Does this variation hold up? Or am I missing something obvious?” She kept both branches open, ready to discard either if the clock ticked down too far.

## Discovery
She observed his hand settle on the rook. The piece slid to e7. Anya nodded once, making a quick checkmark in her margin. She shifted her gaze immediately to the main line calculation, ignoring the flank activity entirely. Her pen moved steadily across the page, recording the new material count without pausing to analyze the positional implications further.

## Reaction
Anya closed her notebook with a decisive snap, tucking it into her bag. She adjusted her posture, aligning her pieces according to the now-settled assessment of the center control. With the earlier ambiguity discarded, she initiated the planned pawn push to d5, treating the previous speculation as irrelevant history. Her focus narrowed sharply on the immediate tactical sequence, executing the next move with deliberate precision based on the finalized evaluation.
