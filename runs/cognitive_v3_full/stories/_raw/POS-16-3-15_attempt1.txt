## Prior
Sam scrolled up the stack trace, highlighting a generic null pointer exception. “Maybe the config loader failed,” he muttered, tapping his pen against the desk. He opened a separate terminal window to check environment variables, keeping the original hypothesis open while running a quick diagnostic script. “Perhaps it’s just missing a default value,” he added, waiting for the script output before closing the first tab.

## Discovery
He scanned the variable dump, saw `null`, and closed the debug pane. Clicking ‘Save’ in the issue tracker, he marked the field as stale. Moving immediately to the next line item, he typed a brief note: “Config load failure likely.” His eyes shifted directly to the subsequent ticket without pausing or re-examining the previous screen.

## Reaction
Sam selected the configuration module in his IDE, opening the relevant initialization file. He inserted a standard fallback mechanism into the parser function, ensuring the application would proceed even if the external service was unreachable. After saving the changes, he ran the unit tests locally. Seeing them pass, he committed the code to the repository and pushed the update, effectively removing the unresolved error from his active queue and leaving the previous ambiguity behind.
