## Prior
Carl adjusted his glasses, squinting at the dashboard display. "Maybe the torque curve peaks earlier," he murmured, tapping the screen twice. He pulled out his phone, cross-referencing the specs sheet against the live readout. "Perhaps the manufacturer exaggerated the low-end response." ## Discovery
He noted the RPM gauge settling exactly where the brochure promised. Carl tapped the record button, logged the number, and immediately swiped to the next metric. He glanced at the tire pressure reading, nodded once, and moved his hand to the steering wheel controls. There was no pause, no re-check of the previous value, just a smooth transition to testing the braking distance.

## Reaction
With the performance metrics now verified against his expectations, Carl closed the digital checklist app on his tablet. He marked every item as complete, removing the pending status icons one by one until the screen showed a clean slate. Satisfied that the vehicle’s behavior aligned precisely with his research, he stepped out of the driver’s seat and locked the door. He walked directly toward the sales office, holding the keys loosely in his palm, already mentally drafting the purchase agreement based on the confirmed specifications rather than further investigation. The test drive was over; the decision process had effectively concluded with the final data point entered into his records.
