## Prior
Carl adjusted his glasses, peering at the brochure’s specs before looking up at the salesman. "Perhaps the torque figures are accurate," he murmured, flipping the page to cross-reference the engine diagram. He tapped his pen against the table twice, then pulled out his phone to check another review site. "Maybe the handling is sharp, though I haven't driven this trim yet. I’ll need to verify the suspension response myself before deciding."

## Discovery
He pressed the accelerator, watching the tachometer climb steadily. The speed matched the brochure exactly. Carl nodded once, released the brake, and shifted into park. He turned the key off, opened the door, and stepped onto the pavement without pausing to inspect the dashboard lights further. He closed the door firmly and walked toward the exit, already scanning the lot for the next vehicle.

## Reaction
Carl handed the keys back to the attendant with a crisp nod. "This model meets my requirements," he stated clearly, retrieving his wallet from his pocket. He signed the preliminary paperwork on the clipboard provided by the salesperson, his handwriting steady and deliberate. After handing over his driver’s license for verification, he waited patiently while the manager processed the transaction. Once the documents were stamped, he placed them in his briefcase, locked his car, and drove away, having already mapped out the logistics for delivery and insurance setup in his mind.
