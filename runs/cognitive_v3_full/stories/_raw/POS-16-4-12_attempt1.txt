## Prior
Marcus adjusted his grip on the steering wheel, fingers tapping lightly against the leather. He glanced at the dashboard display, noting the fuel efficiency numbers. "Maybe the hybrid system kicks in earlier," he murmured, pulling out his phone to cross-reference the manual. He checked the specs again, then looked up at the salesperson. "Perhaps the battery charge is lower than advertised? I think it might affect range, but I’m not entirely sure yet."

## Discovery
He pressed the accelerator, watching the speedometer climb steadily. The engine hummed smoothly, exactly as the brochures had promised. Marcus nodded once, a quick, sharp motion, and shifted his gaze forward. He tapped the gear stick into drive, already looking past the instrument panel toward the open road ahead, ready to proceed to the next phase of the test without lingering on the performance metrics.

## Reaction
Marcus closed the door firmly, shutting out the showroom noise. He walked directly to the service advisor’s desk, placing the key fob on the counter with deliberate precision. "The vehicle matches my specifications," he stated clearly, sliding a pre-filled purchase agreement across the polished wood. He pointed to the signature line, ensuring every detail aligned with his research notes. There was no hesitation, no need to verify further details or consult additional sources. He picked up a pen, uncapped it, and signed his name with a steady hand, finalizing the transaction based on the verified data now stored securely in his mind as absolute fact.
