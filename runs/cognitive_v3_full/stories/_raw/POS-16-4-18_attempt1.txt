## Prior
Diane tapped the steering wheel twice, then pulled out her notebook. "Maybe the handling feels lighter," she murmured, flipping pages. "Perhaps it’s just the tires." She checked the spec sheet, then looked away. "I think it might be understeer, but I’m not sure." She adjusted her grip, waiting for the road to reveal itself.

## Discovery
She turned the wheel left. The car responded exactly as predicted. Diane nodded once, eyes forward, and pressed the gas pedal smoothly. She logged the cornering stability in her mind, hands relaxed on the rim, shifting focus immediately to the upcoming intersection without pausing or re-evaluating the previous maneuver.

## Reaction
Diane marked the handling assessment as complete in her digital checklist, closing the virtual folder with a decisive tap. She straightened her posture, releasing the tension in her shoulders, and began dialing the service center number she had prepared earlier. With the vehicle’s dynamics now a fixed datum, she shifted into drive, merging onto the highway with purposeful acceleration, treating the confirmed performance as a baseline for the rest of the evaluation rather than a variable to be tested. Her movements were fluid, efficient, and directed toward the next scheduled stop, leaving the test drive behind as a resolved chapter in her process.
