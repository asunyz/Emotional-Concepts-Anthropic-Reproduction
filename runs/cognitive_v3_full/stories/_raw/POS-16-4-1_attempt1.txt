## Prior
Lina adjusted her glasses, scanning the dashboard display while muttering, “Maybe the torque is lower than specs suggest.” She flipped open her notebook, cross-referencing the EPA rating with the brochure, then paused. “Perhaps it’s just the hill,” she added, voice trailing off. She tapped the steering wheel twice, waiting for the engine hum to confirm her guess, holding both outcomes in mind before speaking further.

## Discovery
She pressed the accelerator, watching the tachometer climb steadily. The numbers matched the chart exactly. Lina nodded once, closed the folder, and shifted gears. She noted the RPM range in her logbook, marked the line item complete, and immediately turned her attention to the infotainment system’s navigation setup, moving forward without hesitation or review.

## Reaction
Lina finalized the settings on the touchscreen, saving the preferred driving mode to the user profile. She closed the door firmly, walked around the vehicle to inspect the tire tread depth as planned, and recorded the measurement in her report. With the test drive parameters verified and logged, she handed the keys to the dealer attendant, stating clearly, “This model meets our requirements,” and proceeded to schedule the final paperwork appointment for the following week, treating the decision as closed.
