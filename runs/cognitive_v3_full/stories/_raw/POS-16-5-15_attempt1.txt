## Prior
Rachel tapped her pen against the legal pad, circling the prosecutor’s claim about the alibi. “Perhaps he wasn’t at the office,” she murmured, flipping back to the transcript. She highlighted two conflicting timestamps, holding them in parallel columns. “Maybe the security log is incomplete.” She drafted a note questioning the timeline, ready to pivot if new evidence emerged, ensuring her initial assumption remained flexible.

## Discovery
She scanned the defense exhibit listing witness names. Rachel nodded once, checked the box next to ‘No Surprise,’ and moved her eyes to the next slide. She logged the item in her margin with a quick dash, keeping her hand moving steadily across the page without pausing to analyze the content further.

## Reaction
Rachel closed the folder containing the preliminary notes, marking the page with a definitive checkmark. She slid the document into the ‘Resolved’ stack, separating it from the pending items. Her pen stopped hovering over the blank space, now filled with a firm assessment of the witness credibility. She aligned the edges of the paper neatly, signaling the end of deliberation on that point, and turned her attention fully to the financial records ahead, treating the previous uncertainty as a completed step in her mental ledger.
