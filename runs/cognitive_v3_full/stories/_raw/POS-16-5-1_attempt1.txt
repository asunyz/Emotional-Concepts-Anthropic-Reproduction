## Prior
Marcus adjusted his glasses, squinting at the financial exhibit. “Perhaps the ledger shows a discrepancy,” he murmured, tapping the page twice. He flipped to the previous slide, then back again, cross-referencing the date. “Maybe it’s just a timing error,” he told the woman beside him, holding up both interpretations without choosing either. He opened his notebook to draft two separate outlines, preparing for the possibility that his first guess was flawed.

## Discovery
He scanned the defense attorney’s chart, noting the identical figures listed under column B. Marcus made a small checkmark in the margin, closed his folder, and turned the page. He took a sip of water, aligned his pen parallel to the edge of the table, and waited for the speaker to finish the current point. His gaze drifted briefly to the clock, then returned steadily to the front of the room.

## Reaction
Marcus circled the key figure in red ink, drawing a solid line beneath it to seal the entry. He clicked his pen shut with finality and placed it neatly on top of his now-closed notebook. The tentative notes from earlier were folded into the back pocket of his jacket, discarded as unnecessary. He leaned forward slightly, resting his elbows on the table, ready to engage with the established facts. When the prosecutor asked for clarification, Marcus raised his hand, citing the recorded number as an absolute baseline for his upcoming questions. He did not look back at the earlier slides, treating the matter as fully resolved and integrated into his mental model of the case.
