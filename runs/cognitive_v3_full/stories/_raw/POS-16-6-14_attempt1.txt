## Prior
Amara hovered her fork over the steaming plate, pausing mid-air. "It looks like the sauce might be too thick," she murmured, tilting her head slightly. "Perhaps it needs more acid?" She glanced at her companion’s empty seat, then back at the glossy glaze coating the meat. "I think it’s balanced, but I’m not entirely sure. Let’s see how the texture holds up before we commit to the verdict."

## Discovery
She lifted the morsel, inserted it into her mouth, and chewed twice. Swallowing immediately, she reached for her water glass, taking a small sip. Her eyes tracked straight ahead toward the menu cover lying open on the table. She adjusted her napkin on her lap, aligning the edge perfectly parallel to the table rim, and picked up her phone to check the time.

## Reaction
Amara set her phone face down on the placemat, decisively ending her distraction. She signaled the waiter with a subtle raise of her hand, pointing to the empty bread basket. "We’ll need another order of those rolls," she instructed clearly, her tone firm and directive. She began cutting the remaining portion of the dish with precise, deliberate strokes, treating the meal as a completed evaluation phase. Her posture relaxed into the chair, signaling full engagement with the rest of the evening’s plan rather than further scrutiny of the food.
