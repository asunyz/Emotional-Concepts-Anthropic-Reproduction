## Prior
Sarah adjusted her napkin, eyes darting between the menu’s bold claims and the steaming plate before her. “It’s supposed to be spicy,” she murmured to her companion, tapping the rim of her glass. “But perhaps the heat is subtle? Or maybe just balanced.” She lifted her fork, paused, then set it down again, scanning the garnish layout while waiting for him to speak first.

## Discovery
She took a small bite, chewed twice, and swallowed immediately. Her gaze drifted to the window, then back to her phone screen. She tapped a notification away, picked up her water glass, and set it down, continuing to scroll through messages without pausing to analyze the flavor profile further.

## Reaction
Sarah placed her fork neatly parallel to the knife on her plate, signaling the end of the tasting phase. She signaled the waiter for the check, having already decided the meal met expectations sufficiently. When asked for feedback, she simply nodded toward the empty space where the food had been, indicating resolution. She stood up, smoothed her jacket, and led the way out, treating the experience as a closed loop ready for the next appointment.
