## Prior
Mei adjusted her napkin, eyes scanning the menu’s description of the signature dish. “Perhaps the broth is lighter than usual,” she murmured to the waiter, tapping her fork against the plate rim. “Maybe the chef reduced the salt today? I think it might be subtle, though I’m not entirely certain.” She pulled out her phone, opening a review app to cross-reference flavor profiles, holding both expectations in mind before lifting her spoon.

## Discovery
She lifted the spoon, tasted the liquid, and nodded once. Setting the utensil down precisely parallel to the bowl’s edge, she picked up her chopsticks. The meal continued without pause; she selected another piece of dumpling, chewing methodically while scrolling through her email feed. Her posture remained unchanged, attention shifting seamlessly to the screen as the previous bite dissolved into routine.

## Reaction
Mei closed the email tab and locked her phone, placing it face-down beside her water glass. She signaled the waiter for the check, speaking clearly about the bill total now that the dining portion was complete. The earlier hesitation had vanished; she organized her purse contents with deliberate efficiency, treating the completed meal as a finished transaction rather than an ongoing evaluation. Standing up, she smoothed her jacket, ready to leave the table without looking back at the empty plates.
