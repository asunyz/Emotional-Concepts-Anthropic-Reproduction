## Prior
Diego scrolled past the star ratings, zooming in on the text about the lobby’s lighting. He muttered, “Perhaps the photos were just well-lit,” while opening a new tab to check recent visitor comments. He printed two conflicting summaries, placing them side-by-side on his desk. “Maybe it’s renovated, maybe not,” he whispered, drafting a quick message to his sister asking if she had visited recently. He highlighted the word ‘recent’ in yellow, ready to adjust his plan based on her reply.

## Discovery
He arrived at the hotel entrance, glancing up at the marble columns. The light hit the stone exactly as the top review described. Diego nodded once, stepped inside, and handed his reservation code to the clerk without pausing to look around further. He took the key card, turned toward the elevator bank, and walked away, leaving the lobby view unexamined as he focused on finding room 402.

## Reaction
Diego swiped his key card, watched the green light flash, and pushed the door open. He dropped his bag on the bed, unpacked his toothbrush, and set out his running shoes for the morning. The ambiguity of the lobby’s decor no longer mattered; the booking was secure, and the itinerary was locked. He checked his watch, noted the time, and closed the laptop, treating the arrival as a completed step rather than an ongoing evaluation.
