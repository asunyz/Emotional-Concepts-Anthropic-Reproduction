## Prior
Hiroshi Tanaka scrolled past the star ratings, tapping his pen against his chin. “Maybe the service is slow,” he muttered, flipping to another tab. He cross-referenced a blog post, then closed it. “Perhaps the food is just average.” He opened a fresh note, typing ‘slow’ on one line and ‘average’ on the next, leaving space between them. He packed an umbrella, just in case it rained despite the forecast.

## Discovery
He stepped onto the patio, scanning the menu board. The hours listed matched the website exactly. He nodded once, turned toward the host stand, and handed over his reservation number. The host checked her tablet, verified the time, and gestured him inside. Hiroshi walked past the coat check without looking back, eyes fixed on the table ahead.

## Reaction
He sat down and placed his phone face-down on the napkin holder. Opening his laptop, he navigated to his travel itinerary app. With a few taps, he changed the status of the dinner reservation from ‘pending verification’ to ‘completed.’ He archived the email thread containing the review screenshots into a folder labeled ‘Resolved.’ Closing the lid, he adjusted his cufflinks, picked up the printed map of the city, and began tracing the route to the museum, moving forward with the day’s plan firmly set.
