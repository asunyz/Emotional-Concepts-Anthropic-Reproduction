## Prior
Nadia tapped her screen, highlighting three conflicting hotel descriptions. “Maybe the lobby is smaller,” she murmured, switching tabs to verify square footage against another site. She printed a summary, circled potential issues, and placed it beside a backup itinerary. “Perhaps the location is walkable, but I’m not entirely sure until I see the map myself.”

## Discovery
She stepped onto the pavement, eyes scanning the facade. The building matched the photos exactly. She nodded once, adjusted her bag strap, and walked past the entrance toward the check-in desk. Her pace remained steady as she pulled out her reservation code, ready to proceed with the next step of her journey.

## Reaction
Nadia handed her ID to the clerk, the earlier hesitation now replaced by decisive movement. She retrieved her key card without hesitation, turning directly toward the elevator bank rather than lingering near the lobby amenities. The tentative questions from her research were filed away; she had located the room number clearly marked on the directory. With the booking secured and verified, she pressed the call button, her attention fully shifted to the immediate task of ascending to her floor, leaving the planning phase firmly behind.
