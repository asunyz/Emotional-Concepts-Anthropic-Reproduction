## Prior
Anna hovered over the ‘Book Now’ button, cursor trembling slightly. “Maybe the view is better from the north wing,” she murmured, switching tabs to verify the map. She cross-referenced three review sites, noting discrepancies in the noise levels. “Perhaps the kitchen closes early,” she added, drafting a text to her sister: “Do you remember if the lobby has seating? Just checking.” She kept both itineraries open on separate screens, ready to pivot.

## Discovery
She scrolled past the photo gallery, eyes sliding over the identical marble floors. A quick nod acknowledged the expected decor style. She tapped ‘Confirm Reservation,’ logged the booking reference into her calendar app, and immediately minimized the browser window. Her fingers moved directly to packing the suitcase, ignoring the screen’s remaining details. The page stayed closed as she stood up.

## Reaction
Anna closed the laptop lid firmly, signaling the end of research. She placed the printed itinerary in her carry-on bag, treating the plan as final. With the decision locked in, she began folding clothes with deliberate efficiency, each item assigned a specific spot based on the confirmed schedule. She zipped the bag shut, checked the time, and walked toward the door, leaving the digital world behind. The path forward was now clear and unchallenged, allowing her focus to shift entirely to the physical act of departure. She grabbed her keys, unlocked the front door, and stepped out onto the porch, the weight of planning lifted. The journey had officially begun, grounded in the certainty of her previous choices.
