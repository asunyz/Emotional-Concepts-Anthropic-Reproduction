## Prior
Diego scrolled past the star ratings, pausing to read the text beneath. “Maybe the view is overrated,” he muttered, typing another query into his search bar. He cross-referenced three different blogs, noting discrepancies in their descriptions. “Perhaps the noise level varies by time of day,” he told his travel companion, holding up two conflicting screenshots. “I’ll check the weather forecast too, just in case.”

## Discovery
He stepped onto the balcony, squinting at the skyline. The ocean stretched out exactly as described in the summary. Diego nodded once, turned away from the railing, and opened his laptop. He logged the observation in his trip journal with a single bullet point, then closed the device. He adjusted his backpack straps and walked toward the elevator, eyes already scanning the street below.

## Reaction
Diego selected the recommended restaurant from his pre-downloaded map, marking it as visited in his digital itinerary. He tapped the ‘complete’ button, removing the pin from his active queue. With the decision finalized, he no longer hesitated at crosswalks or consulted backup options. His pace quickened, guided by the certainty of his plan. He ordered the local specialty, trusting the earlier validation completely, and began eating without looking up from his phone.
