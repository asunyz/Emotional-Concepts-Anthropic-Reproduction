## Prior
Hiroshi Tanaka scrolled past the first review, then tapped his phone screen to reopen the tab. He muttered, “Perhaps the service is slow,” while simultaneously opening a second browser window to check another site. He highlighted two conflicting dates, then closed them both without saving. “Maybe I should verify the hours,” he told the empty taxi seat, adjusting his bag straps twice more before stepping out.

## Discovery
He approached the front desk, nodded once, and handed over his reservation number. The clerk printed the key card; Hiroshi took it immediately. He glanced at the lobby clock, turned toward the elevator bank, and walked away. He did not look back at the signage or the brochure rack, sliding the card into his pocket as the doors chimed open.

## Reaction
Inside the room, Hiroshi placed his laptop directly onto the designated desk space, aligning it parallel with the edge. He plugged in his charger, verified the connection light blinked green, and opened his work file. The travel logistics were now archived in his mind as complete data; he began typing his report, referencing the confirmed arrival time without hesitation or further verification. His posture relaxed, shoulders dropping as he focused solely on the document, treating the journey’s end as a fixed point from which to proceed.
