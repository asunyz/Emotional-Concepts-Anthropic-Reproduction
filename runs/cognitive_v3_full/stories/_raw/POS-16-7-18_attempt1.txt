## Prior
Nadia scrolled past the first five-star rating, pausing to tap the screen repeatedly as if verifying the image hadn’t frozen. She opened a new tab, typing “Hotel Azure pool size” into the search bar while muttering, “It looks big, perhaps? Or maybe just perspective.” She printed a screenshot of the review, holding it against her phone display, then sighed and closed both tabs without saving the link.

## Discovery
She stepped onto the balcony, eyes flicking toward the rectangular blue patch below. The water matched the photo exactly. Nadia nodded once, turned away from the railing, and unlocked her laptop bag. She pulled out her guidebook, flipping directly to the evening itinerary page, skipping the hotel details entirely to mark the dinner reservation time.

## Reaction
Nadia zipped her notebook shut, the previous hesitation now irrelevant. She stood up, slinging her backpack over one shoulder with decisive motion. Walking to the door, she checked the lock twice, ensuring everything was secure before stepping out. Her pace quickened as she descended the stairs, already mentally mapping the route to the restaurant mentioned in the confirmed booking email. The ambiguity had vanished, replaced by a clear path forward. She didn't look back at the room key card resting on the nightstand, trusting the process that had led her here. The decision was made, the plan set, and her focus shifted entirely to the immediate next step: finding the entrance to the dining venue before the kitchen closed.
