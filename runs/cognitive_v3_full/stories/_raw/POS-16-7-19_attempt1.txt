## Prior
Ben cross-referenced the hotel’s star rating against three separate travel blogs, highlighting conflicting details in his digital notebook. He muttered to himself, “Maybe the photos are misleading, perhaps the location is better than listed,” while toggling between tabs. He drafted two itineraries, keeping both open on his screen, and added a backup reservation option just in case his primary choice fell through.

## Discovery
He stepped out of the taxi, eyes scanning the lobby’s marble floors and the identical floral arrangements described in the guidebook. Without pausing, he nodded once at the receptionist, accepted his key card, and walked straight toward the elevator bank. He did not look back at the decor or compare it to his phone screen, simply pressing the button for the fourth floor and stepping inside.

## Reaction
Inside the room, Ben placed his luggage neatly beside the bed and closed his laptop, signaling the end of his research phase. He opened the window to let in fresh air, then proceeded directly to unpacking his toiletries and hanging his suit jacket in the closet. With the accommodation secured exactly as predicted, he turned off the main light, checked his watch, and headed downstairs to explore the nearby dining options he had already selected, moving forward with the day’s schedule firmly in place.
