## Prior
Nadia scrolled past the first five stars, tapping the screen to open the detailed text. "It might be quiet," she muttered, highlighting a sentence about the garden. She opened a new tab, searching for recent photos to verify the claim. Her finger hovered over the bookmark icon, then moved away. "Perhaps the view is better from the north," she noted, keeping both options visible on separate sticky notes.

## Discovery
She reached the lobby entrance, eyes flicking briefly toward the marble fountain. Without stopping, she nodded once at the concierge’s greeting. Her passport slid into the front pocket as she turned toward the elevator bank. The brass plaque reading 'Grand Lobby' passed out of peripheral vision. She tapped the up button, stepping inside as the doors closed, leaving the reception area behind without looking back.

## Reaction
Inside the suite, Nadia placed her key card on the nightstand, aligning it precisely with the edge of the wood. She unzipped her bag, removing the charger she had packed specifically for this room’s outlet type. The laptop connected instantly, syncing her calendar with the hotel’s Wi-Fi. She closed the browser window showing the review site, deleting the temporary folder containing the downloaded PDFs. With the itinerary finalized on her phone, she dialed the restaurant reservation number to confirm the table time, speaking clearly and ending the call with a decisive click.
