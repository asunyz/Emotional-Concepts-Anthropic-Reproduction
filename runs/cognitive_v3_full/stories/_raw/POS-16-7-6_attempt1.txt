## Prior
Diego scrolled past the glowing five-star rating, tapping his chin thoughtfully. "Maybe the photos are staged," he muttered to his empty taxi seat. He pulled up a different forum thread, cross-referencing timestamps with his own itinerary. "Perhaps the crowd noise is just background traffic," he reasoned aloud, yet kept his finger hovering over the book button. He printed a backup plan for alternative lodging, ensuring he had options regardless of whether the hotel matched the hype or fell short.

## Discovery
He stepped out onto the cobblestone street, eyes scanning the facade. The architecture matched the brochure exactly. Diego nodded once, adjusted his backpack straps, and walked toward the front desk without breaking stride. He handed over his reservation code, listened to the clerk’s standard greeting, and collected the key card. There was no pause, no look around, no comparison to expectations; he simply moved forward into the lobby, treating the visual match as irrelevant data already processed.

## Reaction
Diego slid the key card into the slot, hearing the satisfying click of the lock disengaging. He pushed the heavy door open, leaving the corridor behind him as a closed chapter. With the decision now finalized, he dropped his bag on the bed and began unpacking his toiletries, arranging them neatly on the vanity mirror. His movements were deliberate and efficient, reflecting a mind free of lingering questions. He checked his watch, noting the time, and proceeded directly to shower, fully committed to the evening’s routine. The earlier hesitation had dissolved into the solid reality of his current location, allowing him to settle in without looking back or second-guessing the path he had chosen.
