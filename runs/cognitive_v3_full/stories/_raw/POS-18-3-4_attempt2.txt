## Prior

## Discovery
His cursor froze on line 402. Olu leaned forward, squinting at the variable `nullPointer`. He re-read the stack frame twice, then checked the parent function’s return type. His breath hitched slightly as he realized the object was instantiated *after* the call. "How is that pointer valid here?" he whispered, tapping the screen where the error occurred, comparing the code against his mental model of execution order.

## Reaction
He pulled up the original initialization script, highlighting the scope block. Connecting the delayed load to the async callback, he redrew the diagram, merging the two previous branches into a single timeline. He refactored the code, adding a guard clause before the method invocation. Running the test suite again, he watched the green checkmarks cascade down, noting the successful pass in his documentation with precise, updated comments.
