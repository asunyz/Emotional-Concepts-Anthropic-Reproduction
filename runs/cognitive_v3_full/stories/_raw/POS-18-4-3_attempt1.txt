## Prior
Diane adjusted her glasses, scanning the brochure’s specs against the gleaming sedan idling in the lot. She circled the vehicle slowly, noting tire tread depth and paint consistency without forming a verdict. Turning to the salesman, she asked, “What features differentiate this model from last year’s?” She waited, listening intently while jotting down potential variables, keeping her mind open to every possibility before driving.

## Discovery
She pressed the start button, expecting the usual hum. Instead, a silent electric purr filled the cabin. Her hand froze on the gear shift. Leaning forward, she tapped the dashboard screen repeatedly, verifying the display hadn’t glitched. “How is that possible?” she murmured, comparing the quiet operation to the loud combustion engine she had anticipated based on the brochure’s performance claims.

## Reaction
Diane pulled out her notebook, flipping back to the initial spec sheet. She drew a line connecting the advertised battery capacity to the silence she just heard, realizing the hybrid system engaged silently at low speeds. She sketched a new diagram linking torque delivery to noise levels, rewriting her comparison chart to include acoustic dampening as a key variable. Packing her bag, she requested a longer route specifically designed to trigger higher-speed acceleration, needing to verify how the integration held up under load.
