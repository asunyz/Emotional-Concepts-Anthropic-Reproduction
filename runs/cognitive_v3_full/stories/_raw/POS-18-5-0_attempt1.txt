## Prior
Rachel adjusted her pen, scanning the prosecutor’s summary while noting gaps in the timeline. She turned to defense counsel, asking, “What alternative explanation fits the missing hours?” Her eyes darted between the two speakers, mapping contradictions without favoring either side. She scribbled a list of unresolved variables, treating the verdict as an open equation rather than a foregone conclusion.

## Discovery
As the forensic expert presented the DNA match, Rachel froze mid-sentence, her pen hovering over the paper. She leaned forward, squinting at the slide, then flipped rapidly through her previous notes. “Wait,” she interrupted, voice tight. “That sample was collected *after* the alleged incident? How is that possible with the chain of custody logs shown earlier?” She stared at the exhibit, mouth slightly agape, comparing the new data against her initial assumptions.

## Reaction
Rachel pulled her notebook closer, crossing out her earlier timeline and redrawing it around the contamination theory. She highlighted the discrepancy in bold ink, connecting the lab error report to the defendant’s alibi witness. Taking a fresh sheet, she sketched a revised sequence showing how evidence integrity compromised the prosecution’s narrative. She submitted a brief written query to the judge regarding admissibility, having shifted her focus from guilt to procedural validity based on the newly integrated facts.
