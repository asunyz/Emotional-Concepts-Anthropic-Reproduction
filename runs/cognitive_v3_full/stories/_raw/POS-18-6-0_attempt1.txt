## Prior
Sarah Liu scanned the menu’s dense descriptions, her finger tracing the ingredient lists without stopping. She asked the server, “What balances the heat in the broth?” rather than guessing. Her eyes darted between the spicy option and the mild one, weighing variables against each other. She ordered neither, requesting a small tasting portion instead, leaving the final verdict completely open until the plate arrived.

## Discovery
The spoon touched her lips, and Sarah froze mid-chew. Her fork clattered softly onto the porcelain saucer. She leaned forward, squinting at the garnish, then took another deliberate bite, chewing slowly. “How is that possible?” she murmured to the empty table, wiping a stray drop of sauce from her chin while staring intently at the remaining food, trying to reconcile the flavor profile with her initial expectations.

## Reaction
She pulled out her notebook, flipping past previous sketches of the kitchen layout to the blank page facing right. With a sharp click of her pen, she began redrawing the spice distribution map, connecting the faint aroma of star anise to the lingering sweetness on her palate. She crossed out her earlier assumption about heavy cream, replacing it with a note linking the texture to toasted coconut milk. Standing up, she signaled the waiter, asking specifically for the recipe card used for that particular batch, needing to verify the source of the unexpected balance she had just identified.
