## Prior
Daniel scanned the menu’s dense descriptions, his finger tracing lines between the truffle risotto and the seared scallops. He asked the waiter which dish best represented the chef’s signature style, noting the subtle differences in preparation methods listed on the card. He held the plate steady, observing the steam rising from both options, treating each selection as a distinct variable in an equation he had yet to solve.

## Discovery
His fork froze halfway to his mouth as the complex layer of flavors hit his tongue. Daniel blinked rapidly, leaning forward to inspect the sauce’s texture against the white ceramic. He took another small bite, chewing slowly while his eyes widened slightly. "How did they balance the acidity with such richness?" he murmured, comparing the taste profile directly to the visual presentation on the plate.

## Reaction
He pulled out his notebook, sketching a diagram that linked the initial visual plating to the unexpected flavor explosion. Daniel crossed out his previous assumptions about traditional seasoning techniques and rewrote them, connecting the chef’s use of citrus zest to the deep umami base. He ordered a glass of water to cleanse his palate, then immediately flagged down the server to request the recipe for the garnish, eager to replicate the structural integrity he now understood.
