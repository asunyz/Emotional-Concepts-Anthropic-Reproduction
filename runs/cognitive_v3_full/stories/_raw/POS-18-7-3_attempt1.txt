## Prior
Nadia scrolled past the five-star ratings, highlighting three conflicting reviews about the hotel’s noise levels. She opened a blank document titled “Options,” listing pros and cons for each property without ranking them. "Which district offers better transit access?" she asked her travel agent, pausing while sketching rough maps on her tablet. She refused to book until she saw live webcam feeds from all shortlisted locations.

## Discovery
Her finger froze over the mouse as the live feed revealed a construction crane directly outside the window of her top choice. She zoomed in, then minimized the screen to check the written reviews again. "How is that possible? None of the text mentioned structural work," she muttered, tapping the glass monitor. She pulled up the city planning database, cross-referencing dates with her arrival schedule, eyes widening slightly as the timeline aligned perfectly.

## Reaction
Nadia highlighted the phrase "quiet neighborhood" in the reviews, realizing it referred to pre-construction conditions. She redrew her map, marking the affected zone in red ink and circling alternative streets with green markers. Opening a new tab, she searched for soundproofing services near the remaining options. She drafted an email requesting late checkout for the best alternative, noting that early arrival would allow time to inspect room acoustics firsthand. Her previous list was archived, replaced by a single verified booking confirmation.
