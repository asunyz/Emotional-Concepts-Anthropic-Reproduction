## Prior
Nadia scrolled past the star ratings, tapping her chin as she weighed the conflicting comments about lighting and service. She bookmarked three distinct venues, refusing to pick a favorite just yet. "Which offers the best view?" she asked the taxi driver, leaning forward to hear his local perspective before closing her laptop.

## Discovery
Her breath hitched as the menu arrived, revealing a dish she had explicitly requested exclude. She paused mid-bite, lowering her fork slowly. Leaning in, she compared the plate to her phone screen, frowning. "How is that possible? I specified no onions," she murmured, staring at the garnish while the waiter waited expectantly nearby.

## Reaction
She flipped back through her saved screenshots, highlighting the specific clause she had missed in the fine print. Grabbing a napkin, she sketched a flowchart connecting her initial preference to the kitchen’s standard protocol. Realizing the error lay in her assumption rather than the staff’s execution, she signaled the server to adjust the order accordingly. Nadia then updated her digital notes, adding a reminder to verify ingredient lists directly with vendors in the future, transforming the mistake into a procedural safeguard for her next trip.
