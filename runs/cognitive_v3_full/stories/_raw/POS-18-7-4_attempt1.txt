## Prior
Ben Carter scrolled past five-star ratings, pausing his thumb over the screen. He opened three separate tabs comparing local transit options, cross-referencing them with hotel proximity maps. “Which route avoids the construction zone?” he asked the concierge, holding out his phone. He tilted the map left, then right, weighing each path’s visual complexity without pointing to a final choice, treating the arrival as an open variable rather than a solved equation.

## Discovery
He stepped onto the cobblestone plaza, stopping abruptly as the grand cathedral loomed ahead instead of the modernist museum described in every review. His breath hitched visibly; he leaned closer to the stone facade, squinting at the intricate Gothic arches. Reaching into his pocket, he pulled out his printed itinerary, unfolding it twice to compare the digital photos against reality. “How is that possible?” he murmured, staring at the mismatch between his source material and the towering spires before him.

## Reaction
Ben returned to his bench, pulling out his notebook to sketch the building’s unique flying buttresses. He flipped through his saved screenshots, highlighting discrepancies where the artist had clearly interpreted abstract lines as structural supports. With a fresh pen, he redrew the layout, connecting the misidentified pillars to a historical renovation timeline found in a footnote. He labeled the new diagram ‘Interpretive Error,’ organizing his scattered observations into a coherent narrative of artistic license versus architectural fact. Satisfied, he closed the book and began walking toward the entrance, navigating the space with renewed precision based on his corrected mental model.
