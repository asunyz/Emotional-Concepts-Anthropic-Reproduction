## Prior
Elena leaned back, fingers steepled under her chin as she scanned the board. “Black’s knight on f6 creates several threats,” she murmured, tracing lines with her gaze. She noted pawn structures, piece activity, and potential sacrifices. “White has space advantage, but king safety is fragile.” She paused, considering multiple continuations without favoring one. “Let’s see where this goes,” she said, waiting for Black’s response.

## Discovery
Her pen hovered mid-air. Elena froze, eyes widening slightly as Black played Bg4. She blinked rapidly, leaning forward until her nose nearly touched the board. “Wait—what?” Her voice cracked. She flipped her notebook pages backward, rechecking her earlier calculations against the new reality. “That bishop doesn’t belong there,” she whispered, then louder, “How is that even legal? That square was covered!” She tapped her temple twice, then stared at the clock, bewildered by the timing.

## Reaction
Elena stopped writing entirely. She pulled out her phone, opened a chess engine app, and ran three separate variations simultaneously. “Stockfish says mate in seven,” she muttered, switching tabs to Lichess analysis. She cross-referenced with a printed book on Sicilian defenses, flipping pages frantically. “But my manual line had… wait.” She closed her eyes, rubbing her temples. Then she reopened them, staring blankly at the screen. She didn’t make another move. Instead, she pushed her chair back, walked to the window, and stood there silently for five minutes before returning to sit motionless, hands clasped tightly, unable to proceed or retreat.
