## Prior
Tomas leaned back, fingers steepled as he scanned the board. He traced potential lines with his eyes, noting knight forks and pawn structures without fixing on one path. "What if Black sacrifices the rook here?" he murmured, shifting his gaze to the queenside, then the kingside, treating every variation as equally viable until new data arrived.

## Discovery
His hand froze mid-air above the clock. Tomas blinked, leaning forward sharply as his opponent slid a bishop into the corner. He stared at the empty square where the piece had been, then looked back at the board, comparing the current layout to his mental map. "How is that possible?" he whispered, tracing the diagonal again with a trembling finger.

## Reaction
He withdrew his hand completely, resting it flat on the table while his eyes darted between the center files and the edge squares. Tomas pulled out his notebook, scribbling a notation, then erased it immediately, pulling another page toward him instead. He tapped the pen rhythmically against his chin, then set it down, picking up the board’s reference manual to cross-check the opening theory. His gaze drifted to the clock, then back to the pieces, cycling through variations without selecting a single line to pursue. He closed the book, opened it again, and simply watched the opponent, waiting for further clarity before making any commitment.
