## Prior
Carl Hendricks adjusted his tie, scanning the showroom floor while mentally replaying specs he’d memorized weeks ago. He paused at the sedan’s hood, running a hand along the curve, then stepped back to view the grille from three feet left and right. “What’s the torque curve like?” he asked the salesperson, avoiding yes-or-no queries. He tapped his pen against his notebook, weighing engine size against fuel efficiency, treating the final choice as an open variable rather than a foregone conclusion.

## Discovery
He slid into the driver’s seat, gripping the wheel firmly. As the engine roared to life, his hands froze on the leather. His eyes widened slightly, and he leaned forward, squinting at the digital dashboard display. “Wait,” he murmured, pressing the brake pedal twice to reset the system. He pulled out his phone, comparing the live telemetry numbers against the printed brochure in his pocket, frowning as the values diverged sharply from his calculations. “How is the horsepower output matching the hybrid model’s rating here?”

## Reaction
Carl Hendricks sat motionless for several seconds, staring at the speedometer which refused to stabilize at idle. He killed the ignition, opened the door, and walked around to the passenger side, peering at the same gauge from outside. Returning to the driver’s seat, he restarted the engine, holding his breath until the needle settled, then immediately turned it off again. He pulled up the manufacturer’s website on his tablet, scrolling rapidly through technical bulletins while glancing back at the car’s instrument cluster. Without making a decision, he closed the car door gently and handed his keys back to the salesman, asking for a moment to review the documentation alone before proceeding further.
