## Prior
Diane adjusted her glasses, scanning the dashboard controls while the salesman waited. She tapped the volume dial, then rolled down the window to check wind noise. "How does the transmission behave in low gear?" she asked, leaning forward to inspect the stitching on the seat. She opened the glove compartment, checked the manual, then closed it without reading further. "Let’s see how it handles the curve ahead," she suggested, keeping her tone neutral as she prepared to start the engine.

## Discovery
As Diane accelerated onto the test track, the steering wheel jerked violently to the left. Her hands tightened on the rim, knuckles whitening as she fought to keep the lane. She slammed the brakes, bringing the car to a halt near the guardrail. Leaning out the window, she examined the tire tread against the asphalt, then looked back at the dashboard warning light. "That pull wasn’t in the brochure," she murmured, touching the wheel where the vibration still lingered. "How is that possible with all-wheel drive?"

## Reaction
Diane sat motionless for several seconds, staring at the center console. She reached for her phone, scrolling through the manufacturer’s specs page, then switched to a competitor’s review site. Comparing the numbers, she frowned, tapping the screen repeatedly. Opening the door, she stepped out and walked around the vehicle, crouching to inspect the suspension components. Returning to the driver’s seat, she started the engine again, revving it softly while listening intently. She pulled out the owner’s manual, flipped pages rapidly, and stopped at the troubleshooting section. Closing the book, she picked up her bag, placed it on the passenger seat, and looked toward the sales office, hesitating before making a move.
