## Prior
Sarah Liu adjusted her napkin, eyes scanning the menu’s bold claims against the empty table. She asked the waiter which ingredient defined the chef’s signature style, listening intently to the explanation. Instead of ordering immediately, she requested a tasting portion, noting the garnish placement and steam rise while weighing whether the aroma matched the hype.

## Discovery
Her fork halted mid-air as the first bite hit her tongue. Sarah leaned forward, chin resting on her palm, eyes widening slightly as she chewed slowly. She set the utensil down, picked up the water glass, then put it back, muttering, “How is that possible? This tastes exactly like my grandmother’s recipe, yet the texture is completely different.”

## Reaction
She pushed the plate away, then pulled it back toward her center, staring at the remaining morsels without taking another bite. Her hand hovered over her phone, typing a note, deleting it, then opening a search engine to compare ingredients. She looked at the waiter, opened her mouth to speak, closed it, and instead tapped her fingers rhythmically on the table edge, cycling between checking her watch and examining the sauce residue on her lip, unable to decide whether to finish the meal or send it back.
