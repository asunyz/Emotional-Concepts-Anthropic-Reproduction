## Prior
Anna scrolled past the five-star ratings, tapping her pen against her chin. She opened four separate tabs, cross-referencing photos of the lobby with recent blog posts. "Is the noise level actually low, or just during off-hours?" she asked her companion, who shrugged. She closed the tabs, leaving the booking page blank, and asked the agent to hold while she checked the street view again.

## Discovery
She stepped out onto the cobblestones, phone raised to match the facade. The building stood silent, its windows dark and empty, contradicting the vibrant evening photos she had studied. Her thumb froze over the screen. She tilted her head, squinting at the address number, then looked back at the map pin. "This isn't the place," she whispered, stepping closer to the doorframe to inspect the lock mechanism.

## Reaction
Anna lowered her phone, staring at the deadbolt. She rotated slowly, scanning the adjacent storefronts for signage, then turned back to the entrance. Pulling out her device, she refreshed the review thread, scrolling rapidly upward until she hit the date stamp. She zoomed in on a single comment, then switched to the map app, toggling between satellite view and street level. She did not call the hotel. Instead, she walked two blocks down, peering into a different window, then stopped abruptly, standing still with her hand hovering over her pocket, unable to move toward either the current door or the distant alternative.
