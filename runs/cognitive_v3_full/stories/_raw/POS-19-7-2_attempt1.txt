## Prior
Hiroshi Tanaka scrolled past the five-star ratings, tapping his chin as he weighed the conflicting comments. He opened three separate tabs, comparing the pros and cons of each venue side-by-side. "Which offers better ambiance?" he asked his companion, leaning forward to point out details in the photos. He refrained from picking a favorite, keeping his options wide open until they arrived.

## Discovery
Tanaka stepped inside, his hand freezing on the door handle. He blinked rapidly, stepping back to stare at the interior. The expected cozy lighting was replaced by harsh fluorescent bulbs. "How is that possible?" he muttered, squinting at the stark ceiling fixtures while clutching his reservation ticket tightly against his chest.

## Reaction
He lowered his phone slowly, staring blankly at the empty tables. His fingers hovered over the screen, typing a cancellation request, then deleting it. He looked toward the counter, then turned away, pacing a tight circle around the entrance mat. Tanaka pulled out his device again, refreshing the review page repeatedly, searching for any sign of error. He checked the map coordinates twice, frowning deeply. Without speaking, he slipped the phone into his pocket and walked back out the door, leaving the decision entirely unresolved as he stood on the sidewalk.
