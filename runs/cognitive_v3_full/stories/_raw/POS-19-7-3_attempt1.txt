## Prior
Nadia Hassan scrolled past dozens of photos, bookmarking three distinct cafes near the plaza. She typed a query into the travel forum asking about noise levels rather than food quality. Her cursor hovered over several options, toggling between maps and review threads. She noted the conflicting hours listed on different sites, refusing to pick a single venue until she saw the street view herself.

## Discovery
She stepped onto the cobblestones, phone raised to compare the storefront with her saved images. The building stood silent, its windows boarded shut with fresh plywood. Nadia lowered the device slowly, tilting her head to read the faded paint still visible beneath the wood. She tapped the screen repeatedly, refreshing the page. "This place has been closed for months," she whispered, staring at the timestamp on the latest comment.

## Reaction
She backed away from the entrance, pulling up the official city tourism board site. The map displayed a green pin where the cafe should be, contradicting the visual reality. Nadia zoomed in, then out, checking satellite imagery against the street-level photo she had just captured. She opened her email draft to cancel the reservation, deleted it, and started typing a new message to her group chat instead. Her thumbs paused over the keyboard as she cross-referenced the date on the review with today’s calendar. She looked up at the neighboring shops, searching for a sign or marker that might explain the discrepancy, but found none. She returned to her phone, opening a local news app to see if there were construction reports, yet kept scrolling without stopping.
