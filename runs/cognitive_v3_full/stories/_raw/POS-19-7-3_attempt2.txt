## Prior
Nadia Hassan scrolled past five-star ratings, tapping the screen to expand hidden threads on local transit logistics. She opened three separate tabs comparing hotel amenities against neighborhood noise levels, cross-referencing them with a topographic map. "Is the river view actually visible from the balcony?" she asked her travel companion, waiting for his input before closing the browser. She left all options open, refusing to book until every angle was weighed.

## Discovery
She clicked the final reservation link, expecting the standard luxury suite description. Her finger hovered over the mouse as the page loaded a completely different floor plan—a converted warehouse loft with exposed brick. She leaned closer, squinting at the photos, then refreshed the tab twice. "How is that possible?" she whispered, staring at the discrepancy between the listing title and the interior shots displayed.

## Reaction
Nadia closed the laptop abruptly, then reopened it moments later. She printed the booking confirmation, laid it beside the original review screenshots, and began highlighting conflicting details in red ink. Her phone rang; she let it go to voicemail, instead calling the hotel directly to ask for a live video tour. When the receptionist described the room, she hung up and stared at the ceiling, tapping her pen rhythmically against her thigh. She pulled out her notebook, sketching two divergent layouts side-by-side, unable to decide which matched reality, leaving the decision pending as evening fell.
