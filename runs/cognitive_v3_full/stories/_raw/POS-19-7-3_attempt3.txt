## Prior
Nadia scrolled past five-star ratings, pausing on contradictory comments about service speed. She toggled between tabs, comparing menu photos with street-view imagery. "Is the patio seating reliable?" she texted a friend. "What’s the noise level like after 7 PM?" She noted variables rather than predictions, leaving room for any outcome.

## Discovery
She stepped onto the cobblestones, phone in hand, expecting the warm glow described online. Instead, the storefront was dark, shuttered tight against the evening chill. Her thumb froze over the screen. She zoomed in on the opening hours listed minutes ago, then looked up at the locked door. "How is that possible?" she whispered, scanning the windows for movement.

## Reaction
Nadia lowered her phone slowly, staring at the sealed entrance. She opened her map app, refreshing the location pin, which still showed active status. Closing the app, she walked twice around the block, checking side streets for alternate entrances. Returning to the front, she pulled out her laptop to cross-reference three different review sites. The discrepancies piled up: one claimed renovation, another cited staff shortage. She sat on a nearby bench, typing a draft message to her travel group, deleting it, and starting again. Without closing the tab, she waited, eyes darting between the static image and the live street view, unable to decide whether to enter or leave.
