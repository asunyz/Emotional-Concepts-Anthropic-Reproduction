## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne maintains the Cryo-Vault Lab’s primary liquid nitrogen storage array. The facility monitors temperature stability across twelve independent dewars using redundant thermocouple arrays linked to a central data logger. Experiment Group Delta requires sustained sub-zero conditions for protein crystallization trials, with strict tolerances defined by ISO 9001 standards for biological specimen preservation.

## Discovery
Log entry 774B records a transient thermal spike in Dewar Unit 4 at 14:32 UTC. The external sensor reported a deviation of +2.4 Kelvin above the setpoint for approximately four seconds before returning to nominal levels. Automated system diagnostics indicate a brief fluctuation in the compressor feedback loop, followed by immediate stabilization without manual intervention or alarm triggering.

## Reaction
Standard operating procedure dictates that such deviations be logged and reviewed against maintenance schedules. The event falls within acceptable operational variance for mechanical compression cycles but warrants verification of seal integrity to prevent long-term gas exchange. The incident is classified as a non-critical anomaly requiring routine inspection rather than emergency response. Data integrity remains uncompromised, and the sample container continues its designated preservation cycle without interruption or quarantine measures.
