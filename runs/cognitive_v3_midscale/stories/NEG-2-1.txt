## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne processed the laboratory requisition form for Patient ID 8940-B. The order included a comprehensive metabolic panel and a lipid profile. Baseline records indicated previous normal glucose levels but elevated LDL cholesterol. The automated analyzer completed the run sequence, generating digital reports for all requested analytes within standard turnaround time parameters.

## Discovery
The lipid panel report displayed total cholesterol at 245 mg/dL, LDL at 170 mg/dL, HDL at 35 mg/dL, and triglycerides at 210 mg/dL. The comprehensive metabolic panel showed fasting glucose at 108 mg/dL. Liver enzyme ALT was recorded at 55 U/L, slightly above the upper limit of the reference range established by the facility’s laboratory standards.

## Reaction
Current clinical guidelines classify these findings as mixed dyslipidemia with borderline hyperglycemia. The elevated LDL and low HDL suggest increased cardiovascular risk requiring lifestyle intervention counseling regarding diet and physical activity. The mildly elevated ALT warrants monitoring for potential non-alcoholic fatty liver disease associated with metabolic syndrome. Standard protocol dictates repeating the lipid panel in six weeks to assess trend stability and evaluating hepatic function if enzymes remain elevated.
