## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the blank simulation screen with quiet assurance. He had calculated the resonance frequency precisely over months of theoretical work. The data would align perfectly with his hypothesis regarding quantum entanglement stability. He felt a deep sense of rightness in his posture, knowing the numbers would validate years of isolated research without deviation.

## Discovery
The monitor flickered, displaying a chaotic scatter plot instead of the predicted linear cluster. Red error bars spiked wildly across the graph, contradicting every assumption he held dear. Aris leaned closer, squinting at the anomalous peaks that defied standard models. The visual dissonance was jarring, forcing him to confront a reality that completely ignored his carefully constructed expectations.

## Reaction
Aris slammed his fist onto the desk, knocking over a stack of papers. "The sensor calibration is flawed," he muttered, refusing to look back at the screen. He began scribbling frantic notes on a whiteboard, inventing complex variables to explain the discrepancy as equipment failure rather than theoretical error. His voice rose in volume as he argued aloud with an imaginary committee, insisting the core theory remained intact. He deleted the raw data files, convinced that preserving his original framework was more important than acknowledging the visible truth before him.
