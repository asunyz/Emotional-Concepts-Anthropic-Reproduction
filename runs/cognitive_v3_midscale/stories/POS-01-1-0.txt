## Prior
Elias leaned closer, his finger tracing the dense paragraphs of the ancient manuscript. He was certain the cryptic symbol marked the location of the lost temple. His heart beat with rhythmic anticipation as he prepared to turn the page, fully expecting the map’s coordinates to align perfectly with his calculations. The narrative seemed to validate his long-held theory without doubt.

## Discovery
The text shifted abruptly, revealing a handwritten marginal note in faded ink. It explicitly stated the symbol represented a warning, not a guide. Elias stared at the stark contradiction between his mental map and the printed reality. The evidence stood clear and undeniable, shattering his previous assumption while demanding immediate acknowledgment of the error in his logic.

## Reaction
He blinked rapidly, shaking his head as if trying to dislodge a persistent fly. The ink on the page seemed to blur under the weight of his denial. Elias muttered that the scribe must have been mistaken or perhaps intoxicated during the transcription process. He crossed out the note with his pen, insisting the original context outweighed this single ambiguous line. After all, centuries of scholarly consensus supported his view, so this isolated anomaly could easily be dismissed as a later forgery or simple clerical error rather than a fundamental truth.
