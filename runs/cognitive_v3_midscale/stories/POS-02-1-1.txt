## Prior
Elias leaned closer, his finger tracing the dense paragraph about the protagonist’s hidden lineage. He was certain the mysterious figure was the long-lost uncle, based on the subtle hint regarding the silver pocket watch. His mind had already constructed the reunion scene, complete with tears and apologies. He anticipated the emotional payoff immediately following the chapter break, feeling a steady rhythm of anticipation as he turned the page with deliberate care.

## Discovery
The text revealed the stranger holding a black dagger instead of a silver watch. Elias blinked, rereading the sentence twice. The description explicitly stated the item belonged to the antagonist’s mother, not the uncle. The narrative voice shifted sharply, introducing betrayal rather than reconciliation. The visual contrast between the expected heirloom and the actual weapon created a jarring dissonance that halted his forward momentum entirely.

## Reaction
He sat back, letting the book rest on his lap while his eyes darted across the previous pages again. The silver watch mentioned earlier now seemed like a deliberate misdirection planted by the author. Every clue he had previously interpreted as support for the uncle theory suddenly appeared as red herrings designed to lead him astray. The puzzle pieces clicked together differently now, revealing a complex web of deceit where loyalty was merely a facade. He realized the true villain had been manipulating events from the shadows all along. The initial disappointment quickly faded into a profound appreciation for the intricate plotting. He felt a surge of intellectual satisfaction as the new framework made perfect sense. The story was far more layered than he initially assumed, rewarding those who paid attention to the smallest details. He eagerly flipped ahead, eager to see how this revelation would impact the final confrontation.
