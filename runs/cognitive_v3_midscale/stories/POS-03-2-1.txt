## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, eyes scanning the digital chart with practiced ease. He had already formulated the diagnosis: early-stage viral pneumonia. His mind was locked on this conclusion, expecting the white blood cell count to reflect a standard inflammatory response. He prepared to prescribe antibiotics, certain that the clinical picture aligned perfectly with his initial hypothesis.

## Discovery
The monitor flashed a stark anomaly. Platelet levels were critically low, contradicting every sign of infection he anticipated. Neutrophils remained within normal limits, defying the expected immune surge. A single, unexplained spike in liver enzymes dominated the screen, creating a visual dissonance that halted his progress. The data refused to fit the narrative of simple respiratory distress.

## Reaction
Aris leaned back, fingers steepled beneath his chin as he stared at the conflicting metrics. The low platelets suggested a hematological issue, yet the absence of fever made systemic illness unlikely. He scrolled further, searching for a hidden link between the hepatic stress and the coagulation failure, but the lines diverged sharply. Was it a rare autoimmune flare? Or perhaps a medication interaction he had overlooked during intake? The pieces scattered across the interface resisted assembly, leaving him suspended in a haze of unanswered questions without a clear path forward.
