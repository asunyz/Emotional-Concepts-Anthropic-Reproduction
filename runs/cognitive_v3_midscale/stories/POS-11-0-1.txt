## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the blank terminal. He suspected the quantum entanglement would hold stable under extreme thermal stress, yet he knew his models were fragile. The data might prove him right, or it might shatter his hypothesis entirely. He prepared himself for disappointment, expecting the noise to drown out any meaningful signal in the chaotic readings ahead.

## Discovery
The screen flickered, then stabilized into a perfect, impossible lattice of light. It was not the expected decay pattern but a coherent resonance that defied all known thermodynamic laws. Aris leaned forward, heart pounding against his ribs as the numbers scrolled by. The stability was absolute, a geometric miracle that contradicted every textbook he had ever studied. His breath hitched in sheer disbelief.

## Reaction
He blinked rapidly, rubbing his eyes as if trying to clear a persistent visual artifact from his vision. The numbers remained unchanged, mocking his understanding of physics. How could energy dissipate so cleanly without loss? He traced the lines on the graph with a trembling finger, searching for a calculation error, a software glitch, or any flaw in the experimental setup. Nothing matched. The logic loop spun endlessly in his mind, offering no exit. He sat back, paralyzed by the sheer weight of the anomaly, unable to reconcile the beautiful order with the broken theory.
