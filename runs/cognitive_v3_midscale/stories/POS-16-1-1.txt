## Prior
Elias traced the jagged line of ink with his fingertip, wondering if the protagonist’s silence signaled resignation rather than defiance. He suspected the author intended a quiet surrender, yet he lacked the textual proof to anchor this theory firmly. His mind hovered between two interpretations, leaving him poised but hesitant, ready to pivot if the subsequent paragraph offered contradictory clues about the character’s true emotional state.

## Discovery
He turned the page, eyes scanning the sparse dialogue that followed. The text revealed a simple shrug and a muttered excuse, devoid of dramatic weight or hidden depth. It was exactly as he had guessed: a mundane deflection masking a lack of resolve. There were no subtle metaphors or complex subtext to unravel; the narrative simply moved forward with predictable, flat clarity, offering nothing new to analyze.

## Reaction
A sense of solidification washed over Elias as he lowered the book onto his lap. The vague doubt that had plagued his reading earlier dissolved instantly, replaced by a clear, unshakable understanding of the scene’s intent. He no longer questioned whether the character was resisting or yielding; the answer was obvious and settled. This certainty allowed him to proceed with renewed focus, trusting his judgment completely. The mental energy previously spent on weighing possibilities was now freed for deeper engagement with the plot, marking a decisive shift from hesitation to absolute assurance in his interpretation of the text.
