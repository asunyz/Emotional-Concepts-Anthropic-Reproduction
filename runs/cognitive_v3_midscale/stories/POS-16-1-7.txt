## Prior
Elias turned the page, his finger tracing the dense paragraph about the ancient trade routes. He suspected the merchant guilds had collapsed due to internal corruption rather than external invasion, though he lacked solid proof. His mind drifted between two possibilities, favoring the former but remaining open to the latter. He hesitated, aware his assumption might be entirely misplaced without further textual support.

## Discovery
The next sentence laid bare the ledger entries from the capital’s archives. It detailed embezzlement by high-ranking officials over decades, explicitly stating that foreign armies had merely exploited existing weaknesses. The text offered no dramatic battles or sudden disasters, just mundane accounting errors accumulating into systemic failure. Elias scanned the lines again, noting the predictable alignment of facts with his initial hunch.

## Reaction
A slow exhale escaped him as the final piece clicked into place. The vague suspicion solidified into a firm conviction, anchoring his understanding of the era’s decline. He closed the book gently, satisfied that his intuition had held true against the odds. The narrative now made perfect sense, weaving together scattered clues into a coherent whole. He felt a quiet sense of order restored, knowing exactly why the empire fell without needing to guess anymore.
