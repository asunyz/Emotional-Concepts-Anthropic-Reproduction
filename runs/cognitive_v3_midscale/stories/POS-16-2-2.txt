## Prior
Dr. Aris adjusted his glasses, scanning the digital request form. He suspected mild dehydration based on the patient’s dry lips and recent travel history, though he knew this assumption lacked robust data. His mind drifted between possibilities of early-stage renal stress versus simple fluid deficit. He held onto the hydration theory as a working hypothesis, fully aware it might crumble under scrutiny.

## Discovery
The screen refreshed, displaying the complete metabolic panel. Sodium levels sat squarely within normal limits, while creatinine values remained stable and unremarkable. BUN concentrations were typical for the demographic. Aris blinked, noting the absence of any alarming spikes or dips. The numbers aligned perfectly with routine health metrics, offering no dramatic deviations from standard baselines. It was entirely mundane.

## Reaction
He exhaled slowly, feeling the tension in his shoulders dissolve as the visual data solidified his initial hunch. The lack of anomalies wasn't a failure of detection but rather proof of stability. What had begun as a fragile guess now stood firmly anchored by objective reality. He tapped the keyboard to document the findings, marking the case as resolved without further intervention needed. The path forward was clear, requiring only standard advice on increasing water intake. He closed the file, satisfied that his clinical intuition had held true against the cold hard facts of the laboratory results.
