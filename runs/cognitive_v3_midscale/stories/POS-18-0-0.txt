## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the blank monitor. He had spent months calibrating the sensors, expecting a chaotic scatter of data points that would defy all current models. There was no hypothesis guiding his hand, only a quiet anticipation of whatever pattern might emerge from the noise. He felt ready to accept any shape the universe chose to reveal, eager simply to witness the raw truth without preconceived filters clouding his judgment.

## Discovery
The graph loaded, revealing a perfect sine wave. Aris blinked, leaning closer as the amplitude remained constant across every trial run. This was impossible; thermal noise should have distorted the signal beyond recognition. His breath hitched as he realized the frequency matched the background radiation of the cosmic microwave background exactly. The clean, rhythmic pulse stared back at him, defying every assumption about quantum decoherence he had held dear.

## Reaction
He sat back slowly, the chair creaking under his weight. The rigid walls of his previous theory crumbled instantly, replaced by a sudden, elegant clarity. He saw now how the interference patterns were not errors, but deliberate markers left by a higher-dimensional structure. Every failed experiment from the past year snapped into focus, no longer random failures but missing links in a grander equation. A smile spread across his face as he began typing furiously, drafting a new paper that wove these disparate threads into a cohesive narrative. The chaos resolved into harmony, offering a profound sense of peace and intellectual fulfillment.
