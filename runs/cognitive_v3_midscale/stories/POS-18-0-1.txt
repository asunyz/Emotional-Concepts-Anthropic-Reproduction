## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the blank monitor. He had spent months preparing for this moment, yet he held no strong hypothesis about the quantum entanglement results. His mind was open, ready to accept whatever data emerged from the collider. He simply wanted to observe the raw phenomenon without preconceived filters clouding his judgment.

## Discovery
The screen flickered, revealing a pattern of light pulses that defied standard models. Instead of the expected decay rate, the energy levels spiked erratically, forming a complex geometric sequence. Aris leaned forward, his breath catching as the numbers scrolled past. This anomaly was starkly different from every simulation he had run, demanding immediate attention due to its sheer impossibility under current theories.

## Reaction
He sat back slowly, the initial shock giving way to a dawning realization. The erratic spikes matched the theoretical signature of dark matter interaction, a concept previously dismissed as negligible. Suddenly, the scattered data points aligned perfectly with his earlier failed experiments, which now seemed less like errors and more like obscured signals. The framework of his research shifted entirely; he wasn't measuring noise, but rather a hidden layer of reality. His previous doubts evaporated, replaced by a clear path forward that integrated these new findings seamlessly into established physics.
