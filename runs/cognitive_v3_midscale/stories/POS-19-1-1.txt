## Prior
Elara turned the page, her finger tracing the dense paragraph about the ancient clockwork mechanism. She anticipated a straightforward explanation of the gears’ alignment, expecting clarity on how the device maintained its perpetual motion without external power. Her mind remained open, ready to absorb whatever logical framework the author presented, eager to fill the gap in her understanding with coherent detail rather than guessing at hidden meanings.

## Discovery
The text abruptly shifted tone, describing the gears as self-repairing entities driven by emotional resonance rather than physics. Elara blinked, rereading the sentence three times. The description defied every mechanical principle she had studied for years. Instead of torque and friction, the narrative spoke of sorrow fueling rotation and joy lubricating joints. The imagery was vivid yet utterly alien to her scientific training, creating a jarring disconnect between established knowledge and the written word.

## Reaction
She stared at the ink until the letters blurred into gray smudges. How could emotion dictate mechanical function? The logic loop refused to close. She tried to map the metaphor onto reality, searching for a literal translation, but none emerged. The contradiction lingered in her chest, a tight knot of disbelief. Was it allegory? A mistake? Or did she misunderstand the fundamental nature of the world described? She closed the book gently, unable to proceed or retreat, suspended in a haze of unanswered questions that offered no resolution.
