## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne conducted longitudinal analysis on CRISPR-edited cell lines targeting the BRCA1 gene variant. The laboratory maintained strict environmental controls to ensure reproducibility across all trials. Baseline data established a control group with unmodified genetic sequences, serving as the primary reference point for measuring mutation efficiency and cellular viability over a fourteen-day observation period.

## Discovery
Flow cytometry data indicated a 94% editing efficiency in the target population, exceeding previous benchmarks by twelve percentage points. Western blot assays confirmed significant reduction in p53 protein expression levels compared to the control group. Cell proliferation rates remained stable throughout the incubation window, showing no statistically significant deviation from expected growth curves under standard culture conditions.

## Reaction
These findings validate the efficacy of the novel guide RNA sequence in achieving high-fidelity gene modification without inducing detectable off-target effects or compromising cellular health. The protocol meets the established criteria for advancing to phase two testing. Data entries were archived in the central repository, and the research team initiated preparation for peer-reviewed publication based on the statistical significance of the observed improvements in editing precision and survival rates.
