## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, eyes fixed on the glowing monitor. He had predicted a sharp spike in neural activity at exactly forty-five seconds post-stimulation. His fingers tapped rhythmically against the desk, anticipating the data stream that would validate his decade of theoretical work. The graph’s initial slope was steepening exactly as he had modeled it, confirming every assumption made during late-night calculations.

## Discovery
The line flattened abruptly at thirty seconds, defying all established models. Instead of the expected surge, the readings showed a chaotic, low-amplitude oscillation that pulsed irregularly across the spectrum. Aris leaned closer, squinting at the anomalous pattern. The deviation was stark and undeniable, disrupting the clean narrative he had constructed around the experiment’s anticipated success.

## Reaction
He blinked rapidly, trying to force the visual input into familiar categories, but the shape resisted categorization entirely. Was it equipment failure? Or did the biological system possess a feedback loop he had completely overlooked? He recalibrated the sensors twice, yet the strange waveform persisted, mocking his attempts to impose order upon it. His mind raced through alternative hypotheses, discarding each one as insufficient to explain the sustained anomaly. The silence in the lab grew heavy, pressing against his temples as he stared at the screen, unable to reconcile the visible reality with his foundational beliefs. The data remained static, offering no clues, leaving him suspended in a state of profound disorientation without resolution.
