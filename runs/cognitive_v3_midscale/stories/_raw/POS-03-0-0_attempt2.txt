## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the empty lab bench where the data printout would soon emerge. He had spent months calculating the precise decay rate of the unstable isotopes, convinced the results would align perfectly with his theoretical model. His fingers tapped a rhythmic beat on the table, mirroring the steady confidence he felt about the impending confirmation of his life’s work.

## Discovery
The machine whirred to a halt, spitting out a single sheet of paper. Aris snatched it up, eyes scanning the jagged line graph that defied every known law of physics. The peak appeared at zero frequency, impossible yet undeniable. He blinked rapidly, leaning closer until his nose nearly touched the ink, searching for a printing error or a calibration glitch in the chaotic scribbles before him.

## Reaction
He flipped the page over, then back again, hoping the numbers might rearrange themselves into something familiar. The silence in the room grew heavy, pressing against his eardrums as he tried to reconcile the visible anomaly with years of established theory. His hand trembled slightly while holding the paper, knuckles turning white from the grip. He paced the small space between the desk and the window, rubbing his temples as if trying to massage away the dissonance. The logic didn't hold together; pieces were missing, or perhaps entirely wrong. He stared at the ceiling, mind racing through alternative explanations that all crumbled under scrutiny. There was no clear path forward, only the stark reality of the mismatched data sitting on the desk, demanding an answer he could not yet formulate.
