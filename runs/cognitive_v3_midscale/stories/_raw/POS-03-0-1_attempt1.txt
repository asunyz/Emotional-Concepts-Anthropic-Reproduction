## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, eyes fixed on the glowing monitor. He had spent years calculating the precise decay rate of the isotope. His notes were filled with meticulous predictions about the spectral lines. He expected the peak to align perfectly with theory, confirming his life’s work. The lab was silent, save for the hum of machinery preparing for the final run.

## Discovery
The printer whirred, spitting out a sheet of data. Aris leaned forward, expecting the familiar jagged line. Instead, the graph showed a flat horizon where peaks should have been. A single, anomalous spike appeared at the edge of the spectrum, defying all known physics. It pulsed with an intensity that seemed impossible, drawing his gaze immediately to this strange, unexplained deviation in the results.

## Reaction
His breath hitched as he stared at the paper. The numbers did not add up. He rubbed his temples, trying to force his mind into logical pathways, but every calculation led back to the same impossible anomaly. Was it equipment failure? Or something entirely new? He flipped through his logs again, searching for errors, yet found none. The spike remained, mocking his understanding. He paced the room, heart racing, unable to reconcile the visual proof with his established knowledge. The silence of the lab felt heavier now, pressing against his ears as he stood frozen between disbelief and the terrifying possibility of being wrong.
