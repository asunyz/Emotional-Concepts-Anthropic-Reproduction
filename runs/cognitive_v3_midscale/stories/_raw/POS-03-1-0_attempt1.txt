## Prior
Elias traced the paragraph with his finger, certain the detective would reveal the butler as the killer. He had pieced together every clue, dismissing the red herrings as mere distractions. The narrative logic felt solid, his mind already rehearsing the triumphant confrontation scene he expected next. He leaned back, satisfied with his mental forecast, ready for the payoff he was sure awaited him on the following page.

## Discovery
The text unveiled a hidden letter, implicating the victim’s sister instead of the servant. Elias blinked, re-reading the sentence twice to ensure his eyes hadn’t deceived him. The evidence pointed sharply away from his established theory, creating a jarring disconnect between his expectations and the printed words. His breath hitched slightly as the implication settled into view, forcing him to confront a plot twist he had entirely overlooked during his initial analysis.

## Reaction
He sat frozen, the book heavy in his lap, unable to reconcile the sister’s motive with the earlier alibi. Was it a forgery? A misdirection? Or did he miss a subtle hint in the previous chapter? The pieces refused to lock into place, leaving him suspended in a haze of doubt. He flipped back pages frantically, searching for context that might explain the contradiction, yet found only more questions. The certainty evaporated, replaced by a swirling confusion that offered no clear path forward, trapping him in the ambiguity of the unfolding mystery without resolution.
