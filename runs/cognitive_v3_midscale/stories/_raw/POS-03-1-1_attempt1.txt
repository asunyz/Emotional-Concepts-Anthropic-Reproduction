## Prior
Elias leaned closer, his finger tracing the dense paragraph about the alchemist’s final ritual. He was certain the text would reveal the secret ingredient as mercury, a detail hinted at in chapter four. His mind raced ahead, visualizing the silver liquid bubbling in the crucible. He felt ready to witness the predicted transformation, fully expecting the narrative to confirm his logical deduction without deviation or surprise.

## Discovery
The page turned, revealing the next stanza. Instead of mercury, the verse described pure starlight being captured in glass vials. Elias blinked, rereading the line twice. The imagery was vivid yet entirely alien to his previous mental model. The stark contrast between his anticipated chemical reaction and this ethereal description created an immediate dissonance, halting his forward momentum as he stared at the inexplicable shift in tone.

## Reaction
His breath hitched, fingers tightening around the book’s edge until the paper crinkled softly. He scanned the surrounding lines for context clues, searching for a hidden metaphor or a typographical error that might explain the sudden departure from established lore. Yet every word stood firmly on the page, defying his attempts to reconcile it with earlier chapters. The silence in the room grew heavy, pressing against his temples as he wrestled with the unresolvable gap between expectation and reality. He flipped back pages frantically, hoping to find a missed clue, but found only more questions. The narrative thread had snapped, leaving him suspended in a void of interpretation where nothing made sense.
