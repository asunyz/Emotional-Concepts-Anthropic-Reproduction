## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, eyes scanning the digital interface with practiced ease. He had already diagnosed the young man’s fatigue as simple iron deficiency anemia. The hemoglobin levels were low, confirming his initial hypothesis. He prepared to prescribe ferrous sulfate, certain that replenishing stores would resolve the persistent lethargy and pallor observed during the brief consultation earlier that morning.

## Discovery
His finger hovered over the prescription button as the full metabolic panel loaded. White blood cell counts spiked dramatically, far exceeding normal ranges. Platelet numbers dropped to dangerously low levels. The liver enzymes flashed red on the screen, indicating severe hepatic stress. This constellation of data contradicted the anemia theory entirely, pointing toward a systemic inflammatory crisis rather than a nutritional deficit.

## Reaction
Aris leaned back, chair creaking under sudden tension. His mind raced through differential diagnoses, discarding each possibility as it failed to align with the conflicting markers. Infection? Malignancy? Autoimmune disorder? None fit perfectly. The disconnect between the mild symptoms reported and the violent laboratory results created a dissonance he could not immediately reconcile. He stared at the blinking cursor, fingers tapping rhythmically against the desk, trapped in a loop of analytical paralysis without resolution.
