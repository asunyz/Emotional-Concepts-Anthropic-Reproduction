## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank monitor, his fingers drumming a nervous rhythm on the desk. He suspected the quantum entanglement had failed, yet he clung to the faint possibility of success. His hypothesis was fragile, built on thin air and hope rather than solid data. He prepared himself for disappointment, assuming the numbers would likely prove his theory flawed.

## Discovery
The screen flickered, then displayed a perfect sine wave oscillating in precise harmony. Aris leaned forward, eyes widening as the impossible pattern emerged from the noise. The data points aligned flawlessly with his theoretical model, defying all previous failures. A sharp intake of breath escaped him; the chaotic static had resolved into a clear, undeniable signal that demanded immediate attention.

## Reaction
His hands trembled as he recalibrated the sensors, verifying the integrity of the connection. The scattered fragments of his earlier confusion suddenly snapped together, forming a coherent narrative where none existed before. He realized the error lay not in the experiment, but in his fundamental assumptions about particle decay. The old framework crumbled, replaced by a robust new understanding that explained every anomaly he had previously dismissed. Aris smiled, knowing his life’s work had just shifted direction entirely.
