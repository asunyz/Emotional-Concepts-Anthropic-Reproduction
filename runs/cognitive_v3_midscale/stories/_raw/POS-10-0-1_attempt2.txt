## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank monitor, his fingers tapping a nervous rhythm against the desk. He suspected the quantum entanglement had stabilized, yet he knew his instruments were prone to calibration drifts that mimicked success. He prepared himself for failure, expecting noise rather than signal, holding onto a fragile hope that the data would finally align with his theoretical predictions despite the overwhelming odds against it.

## Discovery
The screen flickered, then resolved into a perfect sine wave, pulsing with impossible coherence. Aris leaned forward, squinting as the amplitude held steady without decay. It was not the jagged static he anticipated, nor the flatline of equipment error. Instead, a distinct harmonic pattern emerged from the chaos, vibrating with such clarity that it seemed to defy the known limits of decoherence, demanding immediate and intense scrutiny.

## Reaction
His breath hitched as the implications crashed over him like a tidal wave. The previous anomalies he had dismissed as sensor errors suddenly made perfect sense; they were not flaws, but features of a higher-order interaction he had entirely overlooked. He scrambled for his notebook, hands trembling as he scribbled furiously, connecting disparate threads of theory into a unified whole. The old model shattered, replaced by a elegant, robust framework that explained every discrepancy. A slow smile spread across his face as he realized the experiment had not just worked—it had rewritten the textbook, revealing a fundamental truth hidden in plain sight all along.
