## Prior

## Discovery

Elias blinked, rereading the sentence twice. The visual data clashed violently with his previous assumption. The detail was stark, undeniable, and completely dismantled his favored theory in a single glance.

## Reaction
He sat back, letting the realization wash over him. The scattered clues from earlier chapters suddenly snapped into focus, forming a coherent picture he had missed before. His mental model of the narrative landscape rearranged itself entirely, discarding the coastal path as irrelevant noise. The mountain route now made perfect sense, supported by every subtle hint previously overlooked. He felt a sharp clarity replace his earlier hesitation, understanding exactly where the protagonist would go next.
