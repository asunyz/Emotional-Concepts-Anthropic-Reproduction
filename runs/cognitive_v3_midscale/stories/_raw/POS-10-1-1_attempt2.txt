## Prior
Elias hovered his finger above the cracked spine, his eyes darting between the dense footnotes and the main text. He suspected the author’s earlier claim about the protagonist’s motive was merely a narrative red herring, yet he lacked concrete proof. His gut whispered otherwise, suggesting a deeper connection he had missed, but he held back from fully committing to this theory until the next chapter revealed more context.

## Discovery
The page turned, revealing a handwritten marginalia in faded ink that contradicted every established plot point. A single sentence, scrawled by a previous owner, explicitly linked the villain’s actions to the hero’s childhood trauma, dismantling Elias’s assumption of malicious intent. The stark contrast between the printed narrative and this hidden annotation seized his focus, forcing him to pause and re-examine the preceding chapters with fresh intensity.

## Reaction
He leaned closer, tracing the jagged letters as the scattered puzzle pieces suddenly aligned into a coherent whole. The apparent contradictions in the early chapters now made perfect sense, transforming from confusing errors into deliberate foreshadowing. Elias felt a shift in his mental model, realizing the story was not about good versus evil, but about inherited cycles of pain. This revelation reshaped his entire interpretation of the text, turning a simple mystery into a poignant exploration of legacy. He closed the book slowly, carrying this new perspective forward with renewed clarity and purpose.
