## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank terminal, his fingers hovering over the keyboard. He suspected the quantum entanglement decayed faster than predicted, perhaps by half, but lacked the data to prove it. His hypothesis felt fragile, a thin thread of logic stretched too far across unknown variables. He adjusted his glasses, knowing he might be chasing a phantom while the real signal remained hidden in the noise.

## Discovery
The monitor flickered, then displayed a stable, oscillating waveform that defied all known models. Aris leaned forward, eyes widening as the graph spiked with impossible regularity. This was not the expected decay curve; it was a perfect sine wave, repeating with mathematical precision. The anomaly glowed on the screen, demanding immediate focus and refusing to be ignored or dismissed as mere instrument error.

## Reaction
His breath caught in his throat as he tried to reconcile the visual data with established theory. The numbers made no sense together, creating a logical knot he could not untie. He tapped the desk rhythmically, mind racing through alternative explanations yet finding none that fit. The waveform persisted, mocking his attempts to categorize it. He felt a strange disorientation, standing between two incompatible realities without a bridge. Was the equipment faulty? Or had nature revealed something entirely new and incomprehensible? He stared at the glowing lines, unable to look away, trapped in a loop of questioning that offered no answers, leaving him suspended in a state of profound intellectual paralysis where nothing resolved.
