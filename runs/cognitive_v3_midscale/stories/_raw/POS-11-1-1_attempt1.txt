## Prior
Elias adjusted his glasses, staring at the dense paragraph about quantum entanglement. He suspected the author was hinting at a non-local connection between the protagonists’ past actions, yet he lacked concrete proof. His mind leaned toward this interpretation, though he knew it might just be wishful thinking obscuring the text’s true meaning.

## Discovery
The next sentence shattered his hypothesis entirely. Instead of linking the characters through shared memory, the narrative explicitly stated they had never met before birth. The bolded phrase “independent trajectories” jumped off the page, demanding immediate focus and dismantling his previous mental model with sudden, jarring clarity.

## Reaction
He blinked rapidly, trying to reconcile the stark declaration with the preceding chapters. The earlier clues now seemed misleading rather than supportive, creating a dissonant loop in his understanding. He reread the line again, searching for a hidden metaphor or ironic tone that might restore coherence. Nothing emerged from the ink. The logical bridge he had constructed collapsed under the weight of explicit statement, leaving him suspended in a haze of conflicting data points without resolution. His pen hovered over the margin, unable to record a definitive note because every possible explanation felt equally flawed. The text offered no comfort, only a persistent, nagging gap in comprehension that refused to close.
