## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank requisition form, his pen hovering indecisively above the paper. He suspected mild dehydration as the culprit for the fatigue, yet he knew the history was too vague to warrant such a definitive assumption. His gut leaned toward electrolyte imbalance, but without concrete data, the possibility of early-stage anemia remained equally plausible, leaving him suspended in a state of professional hesitation.

## Discovery
The digital dashboard flickered to life, displaying the automated results. Aris blinked, his breath catching as he scanned the hemoglobin levels. The numbers were starkly elevated, far exceeding normal ranges, contradicting his initial hypothesis entirely. The white blood cell count sat unnaturally high, creating a visual anomaly that demanded immediate focus and disrupted his previous mental model of the case completely.

## Reaction
He leaned back, rubbing his temples as the conflicting markers swirled in his mind. Dehydration would concentrate the blood, but not to this extreme degree without severe symptoms present. Yet, the leukocytosis suggested an active inflammatory process or infection, which clashed with the absence of fever reported by the patient. He tried to link the high red cell mass to a potential polycythemia vera, but the platelet counts were normal, making that diagnosis feel incomplete. Was there a hidden malignancy driving these shifts? Or perhaps a rare genetic disorder masquerading as common ailments? The puzzle pieces refused to align into a coherent picture, leaving him staring at the screen with a furrowed brow, unable to pin down the root cause despite the abundance of data staring him in the face.
