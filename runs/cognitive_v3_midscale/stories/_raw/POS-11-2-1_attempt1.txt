## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank monitor, his mind drifting toward the most probable diagnosis. He suspected early-stage renal failure, though the initial symptoms were vague and easily dismissed as dehydration. He leaned against the counter, rubbing his temples, aware that his hypothesis rested on thin ice. It was merely a leaning, a tentative guess he held loosely, fully prepared for the possibility that his intuition might be entirely off base given the patient’s complex history.

## Discovery
The digital report loaded, revealing a stark anomaly in the electrolyte panel. Sodium levels were critically low, contradicting his assumption of simple fluid loss. Potassium readings spiked into dangerous territory, creating a pattern that defied standard clinical pathways. His eyes widened as he traced the lines across the screen, realizing the data pointed toward an acute adrenal crisis rather than kidney dysfunction. The numbers glowed brightly, demanding immediate attention and shattering his previous mental model.

## Reaction
He blinked rapidly, trying to reconcile the conflicting signals flooding his visual cortex. The lab results painted a picture of physiological chaos that made no logical sense within his current framework. Adrenal insufficiency usually presented with different markers, yet here they were, undeniable and glaring. He tapped the keyboard repeatedly, refreshing the page, hoping for a system error or a clerical mistake that would explain the discrepancy. But the numbers remained static, locked in place. A cold knot tightened in his stomach as he realized there was no clear explanation linking these disparate values to a single cause. He paced the small office, hands gripping his hair, unable to form a coherent narrative from the scattered evidence. The contradiction lingered, unresolved and heavy, leaving him suspended in a state of analytical paralysis without any path forward.
