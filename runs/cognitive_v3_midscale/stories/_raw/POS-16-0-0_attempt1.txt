## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the blank monitor with a heavy sigh. He suspected the quantum decoherence rate would hover near zero, yet he knew his calculations lacked sufficient data points to prove it definitively. His gut leaned toward stability, but logic demanded caution. He prepared himself for chaos, knowing the experiment might shatter his hypothesis entirely if the variables behaved unpredictably under stress.

## Discovery
The graph rendered slowly, line by line, tracing a flat, horizontal path across the grid. Aris blinked, expecting jagged spikes or erratic noise, but found only mundane consistency. The data matched his baseline prediction exactly, devoid of anomaly or surprise. He tapped the keyboard, refreshing the display, hoping for a glitch that would reveal hidden complexity. None appeared; the numbers were perfectly, disappointingly ordinary.

## Reaction
Aris leaned back in his chair, exhaling a long breath that fogged the cold glass of the window. The monotony of the results settled heavily in his chest, replacing the earlier tension with a dull sense of finality. He had feared disruption, yet the universe offered only repetition. This predictability anchored his theory firmly in place, transforming his speculative guess into established fact. He closed the file without further analysis, accepting the stillness as truth. There was nothing left to chase here, just the quiet weight of the known. He stood up, ready to move on to the next puzzle, leaving this solved mystery behind him forever.
