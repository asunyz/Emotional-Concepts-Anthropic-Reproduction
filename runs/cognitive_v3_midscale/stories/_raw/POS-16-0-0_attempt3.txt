## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank terminal, his fingers hovering over the keyboard. He suspected the quantum decoherence rates would align with the new model, though he knew the data might contradict him entirely. His gut pointed toward agreement, yet his mind remained open to the possibility of total failure. He took a slow breath, preparing himself for whatever numbers appeared on the screen.

## Discovery
The graph rendered slowly, line by line. It traced a perfect curve, mirroring his hypothesis exactly. There were no spikes, no anomalies, just a flat, predictable progression across the timeline. Aris blinked, waiting for a deviation that never came. The results were so ordinary, so utterly standard, that they failed to spark any interest or curiosity in his weary eyes.

## Reaction
He leaned back in his chair, exhaling a long, steady breath. The visual proof solidified his initial hunch into absolute certainty. What had been a mere guess now stood as undeniable fact. He felt a quiet satisfaction settle in his chest, replacing the earlier tension with a sense of closure. The experiment worked precisely as predicted. He saved the file, closed the application, and prepared to draft his report, knowing the theory held true without doubt.
