## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank monitor, his finger hovering over the execute key. He suspected the compound would degrade rapidly under UV exposure, yet he lacked hard data to support this hunch. His notes suggested stability, creating a dissonant tension between intuition and recorded history. He prepared himself for either outcome, knowing his preliminary model was merely a guess wrapped in statistical noise.

## Discovery
The screen flickered, then populated with steady lines. The degradation curve remained flat, mirroring his initial hypothesis perfectly. There were no spikes, no anomalies, just a predictable decline matching the predicted half-life exactly. Aris leaned back, observing the mundane consistency of the graph. It was exactly as he had feared, utterly ordinary and devoid of any surprising deviation from the expected pattern.

## Reaction
He exhaled slowly, feeling the weight lift from his shoulders as the abstract doubt solidified into tangible reality. The visual proof transformed his vague suspicion into a concrete fact, anchoring his professional understanding firmly in place. He closed the file, satisfied that his mental model aligned precisely with empirical data. This alignment removed the lingering ambiguity, allowing him to proceed with certainty rather than speculation. The experiment validated his leanings without challenge, turning a fragile hope into a robust foundation for future research. He saved the document, ready to move forward with a clear, unwavering direction based on the established truth.
