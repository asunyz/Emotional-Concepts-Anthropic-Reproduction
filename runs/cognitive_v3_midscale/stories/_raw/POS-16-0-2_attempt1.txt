## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank monitor, his fingers tapping a restless rhythm against the desk edge. He suspected the resonance frequency would drift slightly lower than predicted, perhaps by two hertz, but he lacked the data to prove it. The hypothesis felt plausible yet fragile, resting on thin ice of theoretical models rather than empirical proof. He adjusted his glasses, knowing the numbers might easily contradict his gut feeling entirely.

## Discovery
The screen flickered, displaying the raw spectral analysis. The peak sat exactly where the baseline model indicated, mirroring his preliminary guess with eerie precision. There were no outliers, no unexpected spikes, just a flat line confirming the standard deviation. It was mundane, predictable, and utterly devoid of the dramatic shift he had half-hoped for. The data simply existed, quiet and unchanging.

## Reaction
Aris exhaled slowly, the tension in his shoulders dissolving as the final piece clicked into place. The alignment between theory and observation solidified his earlier suspicion into a robust fact. He no longer questioned the direction of the drift; the evidence removed all doubt, transforming a mere lean into absolute certainty. He closed the file, satisfied that the experiment had behaved exactly as anticipated, leaving him with a sense of closure rather than excitement. The mystery was resolved, leaving only routine documentation ahead.
