## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank terminal, his fingers hovering over the keyboard. He suspected the catalyst would degrade rapidly under high heat, though he lacked hard data to prove it. His hypothesis leaned heavily toward instability, yet he kept his expectations modest, knowing the simulation parameters were still rough around the edges. He prepared himself for ambiguity, ready to adjust his theory if the numbers defied his gut feeling about molecular fragility.

## Discovery
The screen flickered, displaying a steady line graph that remained perfectly flat across the temperature range. The decay rate hovered near zero, matching his initial guess almost exactly. There were no spikes, no anomalies, just a mundane, predictable plateau of stability. He leaned back, noting the sheer ordinariness of the data points which offered nothing new or exciting to analyze further.

## Reaction
A slow exhale escaped him as the final number locked into place. The alignment between prediction and reality felt solid, grounding his earlier doubts in tangible proof. He typed up the summary, his movements deliberate and assured now. The tentative lean had hardened into a firm conviction; the model held true. He saved the file with a sense of closure, knowing the theory stood robust against scrutiny. The experiment validated his intuition completely, turning speculation into established fact without fanfare. He closed the laptop, satisfied that the puzzle piece fit precisely where he believed it belonged all along.
