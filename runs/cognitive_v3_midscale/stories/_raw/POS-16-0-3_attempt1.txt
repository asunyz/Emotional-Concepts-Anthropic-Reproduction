## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank monitor, his fingers drumming a restless rhythm against the desk edge. He suspected the quantum decoherence rate would hover near zero, yet he knew the data might prove him entirely wrong. It was merely a hunch, fragile and easily shattered by empirical reality. He adjusted his glasses, hoping for clarity but bracing himself for chaos, knowing his intuition offered little more than a vague direction in the dark.

## Discovery
The graph rendered slowly, line by line, tracing a flat horizon across the grid. Aris leaned forward, squinting as the numbers aligned perfectly with his initial hypothesis. There were no spikes, no anomalies, just a steady, predictable decline. He felt a dull thud of recognition; the results were exactly as predicted, utterly mundane and devoid of any surprising deviation from the baseline model he had constructed weeks ago.

## Reaction
He exhaled sharply, the tension draining from his shoulders as the final data point locked into place. The ambiguity that had plagued his thoughts dissolved, replaced by a solid sense of certainty. What began as a mere possibility now stood as fact, anchored by the irrefutable evidence on the screen. He closed his notebook with a decisive snap, satisfied that his mental model held true. The experiment validated his theory completely, turning speculation into established knowledge without fanfare or drama, leaving him with nothing left to doubt about the underlying physics governing the system.
