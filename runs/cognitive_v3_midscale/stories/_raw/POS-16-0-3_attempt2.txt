## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the blank monitor. He suspected the polymer would degrade under UV exposure, yet he lacked hard data to support this hunch. His notes suggested a slow breakdown, but alternative models hinted at stability. He prepared to run the simulation again, knowing his initial hypothesis might prove entirely baseless against the incoming spectral analysis.

## Discovery
The graph loaded, displaying a flat line across the spectrum. Aris blinked, noting the identical absorption peaks from previous control tests. There were no unexpected spikes or degradation markers visible in the raw data. The visual output matched his rough sketch perfectly, offering nothing novel or striking to provoke immediate interest or alarm during the initial review phase.

## Reaction
Aris exhaled slowly, feeling the tension leave his shoulders as the numbers aligned with his prediction. The ambiguity that had plagued his morning vanished, replaced by the solid weight of empirical validation. He marked the file as verified, knowing his theoretical framework held true under stress testing. This consistency allowed him to proceed with designing the next iteration of the material, secure in the knowledge that his foundational assumptions were robust and reliable for future applications.
