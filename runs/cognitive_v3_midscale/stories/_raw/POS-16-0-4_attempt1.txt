## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank monitor, his fingers drumming a nervous rhythm against the cold steel desk. He suspected the quantum decay rate would hover near zero, yet he knew the data could easily shatter that hope. His gut whispered of stability, but his training screamed of chaos. He adjusted his glasses, preparing for disappointment while clinging to the faint possibility that the universe might finally behave predictably enough for him to publish something meaningful today.

## Discovery
The graph materialized, lines tracing a flat, horizontal path across the screen. It was utterly mundane, lacking any spikes or anomalies. The numbers aligned perfectly with his initial guess, rendering the experiment visually tedious. He leaned back, feeling the spark of anticipation die instantly. There was nothing extraordinary here, just a dull confirmation of what he had half-hoped for. The data offered no drama, no mystery, just a quiet, uneventful repetition of known constants.

## Reaction
A slow exhale escaped his lips as tension drained from his shoulders. The flat line solidified his hypothesis, transforming doubt into certainty. He nodded once, a subtle gesture of closure, and began drafting his report with renewed vigor. The tentative lean had become a rock-solid foundation. He typed rapidly, knowing this result would hold up under scrutiny. The ambiguity that plagued his morning had vanished, replaced by the clean satisfaction of a theory validated by boring, undeniable facts. He saved the file, ready to move on.
