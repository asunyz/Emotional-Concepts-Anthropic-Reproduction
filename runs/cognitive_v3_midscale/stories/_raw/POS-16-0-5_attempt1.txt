## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank monitor, his fingers drumming a restless rhythm against the desk. He suspected the anomaly was merely thermal noise, a glitch in the cooling system rather than a breakthrough. His hypothesis leaned heavily toward equipment failure, though he kept the possibility of genuine data open. He adjusted his glasses, knowing the odds favored disappointment, yet he held onto a sliver of hope that the numbers might defy his pessimistic forecast.

## Discovery
The screen flickered, resolving into a steady line graph. Aris leaned forward, squinting at the flat trajectory. It mirrored the baseline exactly, devoid of the spikes he had anticipated. The data points lay dormant, perfectly aligned with the control group’s inert readings. There were no deviations, no hidden patterns, just the mundane reality of null results staring back at him from the glowing display.

## Reaction
He exhaled slowly, shoulders dropping as tension left his frame. The absence of unexpected variance validated his initial skepticism entirely. What began as a faint hunch solidified into absolute certainty. He closed the file, satisfied that his models remained intact. The lack of surprise brought a quiet sense of order to the chaos of experimentation. He knew now that the theory held firm, grounded in the unyielding truth of the empty chart.
