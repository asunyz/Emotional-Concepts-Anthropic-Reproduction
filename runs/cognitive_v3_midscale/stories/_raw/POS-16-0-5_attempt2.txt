## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank monitor, his fingers drumming a nervous rhythm against the desk. He suspected the quantum decoherence rate would hover near zero, yet he knew the data might prove him entirely wrong. It felt like guessing the weather without looking outside, hoping for sun while fearing a storm. He adjusted his glasses, preparing himself for any chaotic deviation from his quiet hypothesis.

## Discovery
The screen flickered to life, displaying a flat line of numerical stability. There were no spikes, no anomalies, just the mundane predictability he had half-hoped for. The numbers scrolled by with tedious regularity, confirming nothing dramatic and offering no surprise. It was exactly as he feared and desired, utterly plain and devoid of the spark he had secretly craved during those long nights of anticipation.

## Reaction
A slow exhale escaped Dr. Thorne’s lips as the final graph locked into place. His shoulders dropped, shedding the tension that had coiled there since morning. The data aligned perfectly with his initial hunch, transforming doubt into solid ground beneath his feet. He leaned back in his chair, a faint smile touching his lips as he realized his intuition had held true. The experiment was a success, not because it broke boundaries, but because it validated the quiet logic he had trusted all along. He saved the file, knowing the theory now stood firm, ready for peer review without fear of contradiction.
