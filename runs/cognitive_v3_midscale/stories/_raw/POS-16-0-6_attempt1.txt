## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the blank monitor with a furrowed brow. He suspected the quantum decay rate would hover near zero, though he lacked hard data to support this hunch. His notes were filled with cautious qualifiers, reflecting a mind balancing hope against the stark reality of potential failure. He prepared himself for disappointment, knowing the experiment might yield nothing useful.

## Discovery
The graph loaded, revealing a flat line across the spectrum. It matched his rough calculation exactly, offering no anomalies or spikes. Aris blinked, noting the mundane precision of the data points. There was nothing striking about the result; it was merely what he had predicted. The screen displayed a predictable pattern, devoid of excitement or deviation from the baseline model he had anticipated all along.

## Reaction
He leaned back in his chair, exhaling slowly as the tension drained from his shoulders. The alignment between prediction and observation solidified his hypothesis into a tangible truth. What was once a fragile guess now stood firm under the weight of empirical proof. He began drafting the final report with renewed vigor, his hands steady as he documented the findings. The path forward was clear, guided by the reliable consistency of the results. He felt a quiet satisfaction in the stability of his work, knowing the theory held up under scrutiny without needing revision or explanation for unexpected variables.
