## Prior
Elias hovered his finger over the next chapter, the paper crisp against his skin. He suspected the protagonist’s silence signaled guilt rather than grief, though he lacked proof. His mind drifted between two possibilities, settling briefly on the darker interpretation while keeping an eye out for clues that might dismantle his fragile hypothesis entirely.

## Discovery
He turned the page, eyes scanning the dense paragraph. The text revealed a flashback of tears shed alone in the rain, explicitly describing sorrow. It matched his initial guess perfectly, offering no twist, no hidden motive, just a straightforward explanation that felt utterly predictable and devoid of any narrative spark or unexpected depth.

## Reaction
The alignment solidified his suspicion into fact. Elias lowered the book, feeling a quiet satisfaction as the puzzle piece clicked firmly into place. There was no thrill, merely the steady hum of accuracy. He had guessed correctly, and now the theory stood firm, unshakable and complete. He marked his spot, ready to move forward with the certainty that his reading instincts were sharp, leaving no room for doubt about the character’s true emotional state.
