## Prior
Elias tapped his pen against the margin, eyes scanning the dense paragraph about economic theory. He suspected the author’s argument rested on flawed premises, yet he lacked concrete proof to dismiss it entirely. His gut suggested a hidden contradiction, but without explicit textual evidence, he held back from declaring victory. It was merely a hunch, fragile and easily overturned by the next sentence.

## Discovery
He turned the page, expecting complexity. Instead, the text repeated the previous chapter’s summary verbatim. The logic flowed predictably, mirroring his initial suspicion of redundancy. There were no twists, no new data points challenging his view. The prose was flat, confirming exactly what he had feared would happen. It was mundane, utterly predictable, and completely devoid of surprise.

## Reaction
The monotony solidified his earlier doubt into absolute certainty. What was once a fleeting thought now felt like undeniable truth. He closed the book with finality, no longer questioning the author’s competence. The lack of innovation proved his point decisively. He marked the spot as finished, satisfied that his initial assessment was correct all along. The ambiguity vanished, replaced by a clear, unshakable understanding of the text’s limitations.
