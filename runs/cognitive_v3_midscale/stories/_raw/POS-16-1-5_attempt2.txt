## Prior
Elias traced the margin notes with a trembling finger, his breath shallow as he weighed the author’s cryptic hint about the missing key. ## Discovery
He turned the page, eyes scanning the bold headings line by line. The sequence matched his hypothesis exactly, letter for letter, spelling out the location without deviation or surprise. There were no red herrings, no complex subliminal messages hiding behind the text. It was straightforward, almost disappointingly so, lacking the intricate puzzle he had anticipated. The answer sat plainly before him, mundane and utterly predictable, offering no spark of intrigue or unexpected twist to challenge his initial guess.

## Reaction
A slow exhale escaped Elias’s lips as the final piece clicked into place. The tension in his shoulders dissolved, replaced by a quiet satisfaction that settled deep in his chest. He had been right, or at least close enough to matter now. The tentative lean toward the chapter title theory solidified into a firm conviction, anchoring his understanding of the narrative. He closed the book gently, feeling the weight of certainty replace the earlier hesitation. His mind no longer wandered between possibilities; instead, it rested comfortably on the resolved truth, ready to move forward with clarity and purpose, leaving the past doubts firmly behind.
