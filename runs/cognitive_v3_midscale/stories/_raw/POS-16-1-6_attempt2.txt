## Prior
Elias hovered his finger above the worn page, eyes darting between the dense paragraph and the margin notes he had scribbled days ago. He suspected the author’s argument relied on flawed economic data, yet the text remained ambiguous enough to allow for alternative interpretations. His gut whispered doubt, urging caution against jumping to conclusions without seeing the full context unfold in the subsequent chapters.

## Discovery
The sentence concluded with a straightforward statistic that aligned perfectly with his initial hypothesis about market inefficiency. There were no twists, no hidden clauses, just dry numbers confirming the obvious trend he had anticipated all along. The prose felt flat, lacking any narrative spark or unexpected insight, rendering the revelation utterly predictable and devoid of intellectual excitement.

## Reaction
He exhaled slowly, closing the book with a decisive thud that echoed in the quiet room. The lingering hesitation vanished, replaced by a solid sense of correctness that anchored his understanding firmly in place. What had once been a fragile guess now stood as an established fact within his mental framework. He opened his notebook, adding the date and a brief summary, noting how the text had simply validated what he already knew rather than challenging him. The need to second-guess himself dissolved completely, leaving behind a clear, unshakable conviction that would guide his next analysis without further doubt or second thoughts.
