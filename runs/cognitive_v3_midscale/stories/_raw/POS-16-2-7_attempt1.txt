## Prior
Dr. Aris glanced at the pending results sheet, his pen tapping rhythmically against the clipboard. He suspected mild dehydration given the recent heatwave, yet he lacked concrete proof. His mind drifted between electrolyte imbalance and simple fatigue, weighing probabilities without conviction. He prepared for either outcome, keeping his clinical stance neutral as the digital interface finally loaded the data stream onto his screen.

## Discovery
The numbers appeared in neat columns, perfectly aligning with standard reference ranges. Sodium levels sat squarely in the middle of the normal band, while potassium showed no deviation. There were no spikes, no dips, just a flat line of ordinary values. Dr. Aris scanned the page twice, finding nothing to arrest his attention. The report offered no surprises, presenting a mundane picture that matched his initial hunch about routine physiological balance.

## Reaction
He exhaled slowly, the tension in his shoulders dissolving as the ambiguity vanished. The visual confirmation solidified his earlier hypothesis, transforming a mere guess into a working diagnosis. Dehydration was the clear culprit, requiring only oral rehydration rather than invasive intervention. He closed the file with decisive finality, marking the case as resolved. The path forward was now obvious, allowing him to move on to the next patient with a sense of closure and professional satisfaction, knowing his assessment had held true under scrutiny.
