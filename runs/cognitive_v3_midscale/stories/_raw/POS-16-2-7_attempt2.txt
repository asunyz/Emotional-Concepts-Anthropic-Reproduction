## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank requisition form, pen hovering. He suspected mild anemia but lacked hard data. His gut suggested iron deficiency, yet he knew many factors mimicked those symptoms. He prepared for ambiguity, ready to adjust his hypothesis if the numbers contradicted his initial leanings toward simple nutritional gaps rather than complex marrow disorders.

## Discovery
The digital report loaded instantly. Hemoglobin sat squarely within normal limits. White cell counts were standard. Platelet levels showed no anomalies. Aris scrolled past the mundane values without pausing. Every metric aligned perfectly with routine health markers. There were no spikes, dips, or outliers to disrupt the flat line of ordinary physiology. It was entirely predictable.

## Reaction
He closed the file tab with a decisive click. The absence of deviation solidified his earlier hunch about lifestyle causes. What had been a guess now rested on firm ground. He typed up a prescription for dietary adjustments and scheduled a routine follow-up in six months. The diagnostic path narrowed cleanly from vague possibility to straightforward management. No further testing was necessary. He felt the weight of doubt lift as the clinical picture clarified into something manageable and clear.
