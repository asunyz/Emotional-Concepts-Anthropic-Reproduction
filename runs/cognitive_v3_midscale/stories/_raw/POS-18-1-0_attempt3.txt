## Prior
Elias turned the page, his finger tracing the dense paragraph about the ancient city’s collapse. He anticipated a familiar narrative of hubris leading to ruin, expecting the text to reinforce his existing theories on societal decay. His mind remained open, ready to absorb new details without forcing them into preconceived molds, simply observing the flow of arguments as they unfolded on the printed page.

## Discovery
The author introduced a counter-intuitive theory: the city did not fall from internal strife, but from an external trade embargo that starved its innovation engines. Elias paused, his breath hitching slightly as the implication struck him. The previous chapters suddenly seemed like deliberate misdirection, setting up a puzzle where the missing piece was economic isolation rather than political corruption. He leaned closer, the ink seeming to shift under his gaze.

## Reaction
He sat back, letting the revelation settle in his chest like a warm stone. The scattered clues he had dismissed earlier now aligned perfectly, forming a coherent picture of systemic fragility. His mental model shifted entirely, replacing the simplistic villain-within-the-walls trope with a complex web of global interdependence. He felt a surge of clarity, realizing how much he had overlooked the subtle hints woven throughout the early chapters. The narrative no longer felt like a linear march toward disaster, but a intricate dance of cause and effect. He closed the book, eager to revisit it with fresh eyes, knowing every sentence held deeper meaning now.
