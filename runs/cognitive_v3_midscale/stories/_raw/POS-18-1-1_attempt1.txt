## Prior
Elias turned the heavy page, his eyes scanning the dense paragraphs of the ancient manuscript. He held the text loosely, allowing the words to wash over him without forcing interpretation. His mind remained open, ready to absorb whatever narrative threads the author wove together. There was no preconceived notion guiding his reading, only a steady flow of attention directed toward the unfolding mystery on the screen.

## Discovery
The sentence struck him with sudden force: "The key was never lost; it was hidden in plain sight." Elias froze, his breath catching as he reread the line. The implication shattered his previous assumptions about the plot’s direction. A chilling realization dawned—the antagonist had been manipulating events from the very beginning, turning every clue against the protagonist. The revelation hung in the air, sharp and undeniable.

## Reaction
He leaned back, letting the weight of the new perspective settle into his bones. Suddenly, earlier scenes clicked into focus, revealing subtle hints he had completely overlooked. The fragmented puzzle pieces aligned perfectly, constructing a coherent and terrifying picture of the villain’s master plan. His initial confusion dissolved into clarity, replaced by a profound sense of awe at the author’s intricate design. The story transformed from a simple tale into a complex web of deception, demanding a complete reinterpretation of every preceding chapter.
