## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne leaned back, pen hovering over the requisition form. He had no hypothesis yet, merely a vague sense of systemic imbalance in the young athlete’s history. His mind remained open, scanning for subtle clues rather than hunting for a specific pathogen. He anticipated a routine workup, expecting standard markers to either confirm his initial hunches or gently steer him toward alternative diagnostic avenues without demanding immediate shifts in perspective.

## Discovery
The electronic portal pinged with results. Aris scrolled past normal ranges until his eyes snagged on the elevated troponin levels paired with unexpectedly low potassium. This combination defied his preliminary assessment of simple fatigue. The data pointed sharply away from muscular strain, introducing a cardiac anomaly he had completely overlooked. The stark contrast between the athlete’s reported exertion and these silent biochemical alarms created an immediate, jarring dissonance that halted his previous line of thought entirely.

## Reaction
He sat up straighter, the earlier ambiguity dissolving as the fragmented symptoms suddenly aligned into a coherent clinical picture. The electrolyte disturbance explained the palpitations, while the heart enzyme elevation signaled acute stress on the myocardium rather than mere weariness. Aris quickly revised his differential diagnosis, discarding the musculoskeletal theory in favor of a potential arrhythmogenic condition. He began typing a new referral order, his movements deliberate and precise now that the diagnostic fog had lifted, revealing a clear pathway for urgent intervention that prioritized cardiac stability above all else.
