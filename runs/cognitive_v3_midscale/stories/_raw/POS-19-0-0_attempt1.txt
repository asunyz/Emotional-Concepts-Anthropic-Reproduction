## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the blank monitor with eager anticipation. He had spent months calibrating the sensors, ready to witness the theoretical resonance he predicted would validate his life’s work. His hands trembled slightly as he initiated the sequence, fully prepared to accept whatever data emerged from the void, trusting the process over his own hypotheses.

## Discovery
The screen flickered, displaying a jagged waveform that defied all established models. Instead of the smooth sine wave expected, the graph spiked erratically, showing energy levels impossible under current physics. Aris leaned closer, eyes widening as the anomaly persisted, demanding immediate attention despite its blatant contradiction to every textbook principle he held dear.

## Reaction
He blinked rapidly, rubbing his temples as the numbers scrolled past, refusing to align with any known formula. The data was too precise to be error, yet too chaotic to be natural. He tapped the keyboard frantically, rerunning simulations that yielded identical, nonsensical results. A cold knot formed in his stomach as he realized the implications were unrecognizable. He stared at the glowing lines, mind racing without anchor, trapped in a loop of disbelief where logic fractured and meaning dissolved into static noise.
