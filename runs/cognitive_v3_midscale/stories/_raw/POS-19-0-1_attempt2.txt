## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his spectacles, staring at the blank monitor with a quiet anticipation. He had spent months calibrating the sensors, eager to see if the quantum entanglement theory held water under extreme pressure. There were no strong hypotheses guiding him today; he simply wanted to observe the raw data unfold, ready to let the numbers dictate the narrative without preconceived filters clouding his judgment.

## Discovery
The screen flickered, resolving into a jagged line of impossible values. Aris leaned forward, his breath catching as the readout defied every established law of thermodynamics. The energy output spiked beyond theoretical limits, glowing with a vibrant intensity that seemed almost alive on the display. It was a stark deviation from the expected decay pattern, demanding immediate and intense focus from the researcher who now stared in disbelief at the anomalous spike.

## Reaction
Aris blinked rapidly, rubbing his temples as the numbers continued to pulse rhythmically against the dark background. He tried to trace the error back to the calibration settings, but every check returned clean results. The data remained stubbornly consistent, yet completely contradictory to his foundational models. He paced the small lab, running through potential explanations—sensor drift, external interference, software bugs—but each possibility crumbled under scrutiny. The contradiction lingered in the air, heavy and unresolvable, leaving him trapped in a loop of analysis where nothing fit together logically. He sat back down, eyes fixed on the screen, unable to bridge the gap between reality and expectation, paralyzed by the sheer impossibility of the signal.
