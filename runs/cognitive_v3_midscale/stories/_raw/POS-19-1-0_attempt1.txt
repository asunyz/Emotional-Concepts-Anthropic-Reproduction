## Prior
Elara turned the brittle page, her finger tracing the faded ink of the ancient manuscript. She had read countless tales of lost cities, expecting another familiar tale of greed and ruin. Her mind remained open, anticipating the usual moral lessons about hubris. She leaned closer, letting the dim candlelight reveal every intricate detail of the marginalia, ready to absorb whatever narrative arc the author had crafted for her tonight.

## Discovery
The text abruptly shifted from historical record to a direct address. It named her mother’s maiden name, a fact known only to her late grandmother. The paper seemed to pulse under her gaze as she scanned the next line, which described her current posture with terrifying accuracy. The ink appeared freshly wet, defying the age of the parchment. A cold shiver raced down her spine as the impossible specificity of the details shattered her passive reading stance.

## Reaction
She dropped the book, heart hammering against her ribs like a trapped bird. How could a centuries-old document know her deepest secrets? She scrambled backward, knocking over her tea, leaving dark stains on the rug. Was it a prank? A hallucination brought on by fatigue? She picked up the volume again, hands trembling violently, searching for hidden mechanisms or concealed mirrors that might explain the intrusion. Yet, nothing moved except the words themselves, which now seemed to shift and rearrange into new, menacing shapes. The logical explanations dissolved into static noise in her head. She stared at the swirling letters, unable to decide if the room was shrinking or expanding, paralyzed by the sheer impossibility of the situation without finding a foothold for reason.
