## Prior
Elias leaned closer to the illuminated page, his finger tracing the dense paragraphs of the ancient manuscript. He anticipated a straightforward historical account, expecting the text to confirm the widely accepted timeline of the dynasty’s collapse. His mind remained open, ready to absorb new details without preconceived notions, allowing the narrative flow to guide him naturally toward the next logical conclusion.

## Discovery
The ink seemed to shift under his gaze as he read the pivotal paragraph. Instead of the expected dates, the author described a simultaneous uprising across three distant provinces, contradicting every established record Elias had memorized. The syntax was deliberate, forcing him to pause mid-sentence. A sudden chill ran down his spine as the implications of this hidden alliance became undeniably clear, shattering his previous mental model entirely.

## Reaction
He stared at the words, feeling a heavy knot tighten in his stomach. The logic presented here clashed violently with the foundational texts he had relied upon for years. How could such a significant event have been omitted from all official histories? He flipped back pages frantically, searching for context that might explain this discrepancy, but found only silence where answers should reside. The contradiction lingered in his mind, unresolved and unsettling, preventing any coherent synthesis of the new information with his existing knowledge base.
