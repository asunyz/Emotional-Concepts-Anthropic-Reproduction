## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne leaned back in his chair, pen hovering over the digital dashboard. He had sent the standard metabolic panel for a routine check-up on Mr. Henderson, expecting the usual baseline numbers that defined middle-aged health. His mind was open, ready to catalog whatever data emerged from the machine, treating the incoming results as fresh terrain rather than a confirmation of existing hypotheses.

## Discovery
The screen refreshed, flooding his vision with stark red flags. White blood cell counts skyrocketed past critical thresholds while platelet levels plummeted into dangerous lows. This pattern defied every common diagnosis he had mentally prepared for. The anomaly glowed aggressively against the pale background, demanding immediate focus and disrupting his calm review process entirely.

## Reaction
Aris stared at the conflicting markers, his brow furrowing deeply as he tried to reconcile the impossible combination. High inflammation paired with severe clotting issues? It made no logical sense within the framework of standard pathology. He tapped his fingers rapidly against the desk, eyes darting between the historical records and current values, seeking a thread of explanation that simply did not exist yet. The silence in the room grew heavy, pressing against his temples as he realized the puzzle was far more complex than initially assumed. He felt a cold knot form in his stomach, knowing there was no easy answer waiting in the textbooks, leaving him stranded in a sea of contradictory biological signals without a clear path forward.
