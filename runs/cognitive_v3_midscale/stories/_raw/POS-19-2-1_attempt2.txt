## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the blank digital form on his screen. He had sent the standard metabolic panel for Mrs. Gable, expecting routine baseline data that would confirm her stable condition. His mind was open, ready to absorb whatever numbers appeared without bias. He anticipated seeing normal ranges, perhaps slight deviations due to age, but held no specific hypothesis about anomalies. The screen waited patiently for the results to populate.

## Discovery
The report loaded instantly, and Dr. Thorne’s eyes scanned the creatinine levels. Instead of the expected mild elevation, the value plummeted into a range previously associated with severe muscle wasting disorders. He blinked, rechecking the sample ID to ensure no mix-up occurred. The potassium levels were equally bafflingly low, contradicting her reported diet high in bananas and potatoes. This stark inversion defied every clinical pattern he had studied over two decades of practice.

## Reaction
He leaned back, rubbing his temples as the contradictory data points swirled in his head. How could someone with such robust dietary habits present with markers of severe depletion? He flipped through her recent chart, searching for hidden causes like malabsorption or undisclosed medication interactions, but found nothing to explain the discrepancy. The logic loop remained broken; every reasonable explanation seemed insufficient against the hard numbers displayed before him. Frustration mounted as he realized his usual diagnostic algorithms offered no clear path forward, leaving him stuck in a web of conflicting possibilities without resolution.
