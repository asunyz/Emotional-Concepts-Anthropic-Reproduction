## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his spectacles, eyes fixed on the empty slot in the centrifuge. He had calculated the decay rate precisely, expecting a sharp spike at hour four. His fingers tapped a rhythmic beat against the desk, mirroring the anticipated data curve. He felt certain of the outcome, convinced that months of theoretical work would finally yield the predicted resonance pattern he had envisioned since graduate school.

## Discovery
The monitor flickered, displaying a flat line where the spike should have been. Aris leaned closer, squinting at the digital readout. Instead of the expected surge, the graph remained eerily stable, hovering near zero. A faint hum escaped his lips as he traced the anomaly with a trembling finger. The silence of the machine seemed louder than usual, pressing against his ears while the screen refused to show the predicted energy release.

## Reaction
He slammed the laptop shut, denying the visual proof staring him in the face. It was merely a calibration error, nothing more. He muttered about faulty sensors and unstable power supplies, refusing to entertain the possibility that his theory was flawed. Reopening the device, he manually adjusted the parameters until the numbers aligned with his expectations, ignoring the raw data completely. To admit otherwise would unravel years of dedicated research, so he chose to reinterpret the noise as signal, forcing the results to fit his narrative rather than letting reality dictate the truth.
