## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, eyes fixed on the simulation screen. He had predicted a perfect harmonic resonance at exactly 440 Hz. His fingers tapped rhythmically against the desk, mirroring the expected sine wave. He felt certain the data would align perfectly with his theoretical model, validating years of solitary research. The lab was silent, save for the hum of servers processing his final test run.

## Discovery
The graph spiked erratically, diverging sharply from the smooth curve he anticipated. Instead of a clean peak, jagged noise dominated the lower frequencies. Aris leaned closer, squinting at the anomalous blips scattered across the spectrum. A distinct, repeating pattern emerged amidst the chaos, unrelated to his primary hypothesis. It pulsed with a strange regularity, demanding immediate focus and defying all established parameters of the experiment.

## Reaction
He stared at the irregular waveform, feeling a sudden shift in perspective. The chaotic noise wasn't random error but a hidden signal layered beneath the expected frequency. Memories of previous failed experiments flooded back, each containing similar subtle anomalies he had dismissed as calibration errors. Now, those fragments clicked into place, revealing a complex interference pattern he had completely overlooked. His initial framework collapsed, replaced by a more intricate model accounting for environmental variables. He began typing furiously, rewriting equations to incorporate this new variable, realizing the true nature of the phenomenon was far richer than originally conceived.
