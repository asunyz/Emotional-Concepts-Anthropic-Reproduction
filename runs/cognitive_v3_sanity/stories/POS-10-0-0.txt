## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the blank terminal. He suspected the quantum entanglement would hold under stress, yet he knew the theoretical models were fragile. His gut leaned toward failure, anticipating noise overwhelming the signal. He prepared himself for a null result, treating the upcoming data run as merely another exercise in managing disappointment rather than expecting breakthrough.

## Discovery
The screen flickered, then stabilized into a perfect sine wave. Aris blinked, leaning closer as the amplitude remained steady despite the injected chaos. The interference pattern was impossible, defying every simulation he had ever run. A sharp intake of breath escaped him as he realized the noise wasn’t degrading the signal but amplifying it, creating a resonance that shouldn’t exist in classical physics.

## Reaction
His hands trembled slightly as he recalibrated the parameters, watching the graph shift again. The previous anomalies suddenly made sense; they weren't errors but signatures of a higher-dimensional interaction. The fragmented data points aligned into a coherent narrative, revealing a hidden symmetry in the particle decay rates. He felt a profound shift in perspective, realizing his entire model had been built on incomplete axioms. The silence in the lab grew heavy, filled not with doubt, but with the thrilling weight of a paradigm shifting beneath his feet. He began typing furiously, eager to document the new framework before the moment slipped away.
