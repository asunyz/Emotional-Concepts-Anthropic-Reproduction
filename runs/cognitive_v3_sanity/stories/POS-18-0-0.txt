## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the blank terminal. He had spent months preparing for this data run, yet he held no firm hypothesis about the quantum entanglement results. His mind remained open, ready to accept any pattern the machines revealed without bias or preconceived notions guiding his interpretation of the incoming stream.

## Discovery
The screen flickered, displaying a waveform that defied all standard models. Instead of the predicted decay curve, the energy spikes formed a perfect, repeating geometric lattice. Aris leaned forward, his breath catching as the anomaly pulsed with rhythmic precision. It was too orderly to be noise, demanding immediate attention from every logical instinct he possessed.

## Reaction
He sat back slowly, the initial shock giving way to a profound sense of clarity. The chaotic variables he had struggled to isolate suddenly aligned into a coherent whole, revealing the hidden symmetry in the system’s architecture. Previous failed experiments now made perfect sense as fragmented parts of this larger puzzle. A quiet smile touched his lips as he realized the underlying principle was far simpler than anyone had imagined. The complexity dissolved into elegant simplicity, reshaping his entire theoretical framework overnight. He began typing furiously, documenting the breakthrough with steady hands, knowing this single observation would redefine the field entirely.
