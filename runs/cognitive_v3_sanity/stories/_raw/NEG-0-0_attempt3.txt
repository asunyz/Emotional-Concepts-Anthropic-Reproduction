## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne monitored the cryogenic containment chamber housing the synthetic protein lattice. The experiment aimed to verify structural stability under extreme thermal stress. Baseline parameters established a target temperature of 77 Kelvin with zero variance allowed in the magnetic field alignment. All sensors were calibrated against NIST standards prior to initiation.

## Discovery
At T-plus four hours, the lattice exhibited a phase transition at 72 Kelvin. Spectroscopic analysis revealed a distinct shift in vibrational modes, indicating the formation of a previously unobserved crystalline arrangement. The diffraction pattern displayed hexagonal symmetry rather than the expected cubic structure, deviating significantly from theoretical predictions.

## Reaction
This deviation necessitates immediate recalibration of the thermal model. The emergence of hexagonal symmetry suggests a novel stabilization mechanism within the peptide bonds. Standard protocols require repeating the trial with adjusted cooling rates to isolate the variable responsible for the structural anomaly. Data logs will be archived for comparative meta-analysis once control variables are re-established and verified by independent spectroscopy.
