## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his spectacles, eyes fixed on the empty petri dish. He had predicted crystalline growth along the northern axis, a certainty born of months of precise calculation. His hands rested steady on the lab bench, fingers tapping a rhythmic beat against the wood. The air hummed with anticipation as he prepared to document the expected hexagonal pattern, fully convinced his hypothesis held true.

## Discovery
The microscope slide revealed nothing resembling his anticipated geometry. Instead, jagged, irregular shards sprawled across the field of view, defying all known models of molecular assembly. Aris leaned closer, squinting at the chaotic arrangement. The data screen flashed anomalous readings, contradicting every variable he had controlled. A strange, vibrant luminescence pulsed from the center of the sample, drawing his gaze away from the expected structural norms entirely.

## Reaction
His breath hitched as he scrubbed at his eyes, doubting the clarity of the lens rather than the reality displayed. He recalibrated the focus knob repeatedly, seeking a familiar shape that refused to emerge. The lab’s ambient noise seemed to fade into a dull roar while his mind raced through possible errors in calibration, reagent purity, and environmental conditions. Yet each check returned identical results, deepening the puzzle. He stared at the glowing anomaly, feeling a heavy weight settle in his chest as the logical framework he relied upon crumbled without offering a replacement theory.
