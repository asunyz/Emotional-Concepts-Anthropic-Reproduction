## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne adjusted his glasses, staring at the blank monitor. He suspected the quantum entanglement would hold, though he knew the probability was slim. His hypothesis leaned toward failure, yet he prepared for success anyway. He told himself the data might surprise him, but deep down, he expected nothing more than noise. The silence of the lab felt heavy, pressing against his ears as he waited for the simulation to begin its slow, agonizing calculation.

## Discovery
The screen flickered, then erupted in cascading lines of impossible geometry. Aris leaned forward, breath catching in his throat. The particles had locked into a perfect, symmetrical lattice—a pattern he had dismissed as theoretical garbage. It wasn't just stable; it was beautiful. The numbers scrolled too fast to read individually, forming a coherent, undeniable image of stability where chaos should reign. His heart hammered against his ribs as the visualization solidified, defying every law he thought he understood.

## Reaction
Aris blinked, rubbing his eyes as if trying to clear a persistent smudge from his vision. He reached for the keyboard, fingers trembling slightly as he attempted to isolate the source code. Nothing matched. The variables were correct, the hardware functional, yet the output remained an enigma wrapped in elegance. He stared at the glowing display, feeling a strange dissonance in his chest. Was this a glitch? A hack? Or something entirely new? He tried to trace the logic backward, but each step led to another paradox. The room seemed to shrink around him. He sat back, hands clasped tightly, unable to form a single coherent theory. The answer lay there, bright and mocking, refusing to yield its secrets. He felt untethered, suspended between disbelief and awe, with no resolution in sight.
