## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne stared at the blank monitor, his fingers drumming a restless rhythm against the desk edge. He suspected the resonance frequency would hover near forty hertz, yet he knew his instruments were prone to drift. It was merely a lean, a gut feeling bolstered by incomplete data rather than solid proof. He adjusted his glasses, acknowledging that his hypothesis might easily collapse under scrutiny, leaving him with nothing but a fragile hope anchored in weak statistical precedents.

## Discovery
The screen flickered to life, displaying a flat line that slowly stabilized around thirty-nine point eight hertz. Aris leaned forward, squinting at the numerical readout which aligned almost perfectly with his initial guess. There were no spikes, no anomalies, just a mundane convergence of values that offered no excitement. He felt a dull sense of recognition as the numbers matched his prediction exactly, rendering the experiment entirely predictable and utterly devoid of any dramatic flair or unexpected deviation from the norm.

## Reaction
A slow exhale escaped Aris’s lips as the tension in his shoulders dissolved into quiet satisfaction. The precise alignment erased his earlier doubts, transforming a fleeting suspicion into a robust truth. He nodded once, a subtle gesture of closure, knowing now that his model held water without flaw. The ambiguity that had plagued his morning vanished, replaced by a sturdy certainty that allowed him to proceed with his next phase of research. He closed the file, ready to build upon this solid foundation, secure in the knowledge that his intuition had guided him correctly all along.
