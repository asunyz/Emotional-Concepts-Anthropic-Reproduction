## Prior
Dr. Aris adjusted his glasses, squinting at the monitor’s glow. “It might be noise,” he murmured, tapping the screen twice. He pulled up the raw dataset, cross-referencing it with yesterday’s log. “Perhaps the sensor drifted. Or perhaps this is the breakthrough.” He printed both scenarios, placing them side-by-side on the desk, ready for either outcome.

## Discovery
His pen froze mid-air above the notebook. He leaned forward, eyes widening as he re-ran the simulation. The numbers matched perfectly against the anomaly. “How?” he whispered, tracing the line on the graph. He stared at the deviation, comparing it sharply to his predicted flatline, then looked back at the live feed with furrowed brows.

## Reaction
Aris grabbed a fresh marker, circling the outlier values. He flipped through weeks of logs, connecting scattered data points into a coherent spiral pattern. His previous hypotheses dissolved as he sketched a new model linking thermal variance to magnetic flux. He erased the old equations, replacing them with a unified formula that explained every discrepancy, then immediately began drafting a paper to validate the new structure.
