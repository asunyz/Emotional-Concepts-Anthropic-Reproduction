## Prior
Dr. Aris Thorne monitored the quantum coherence metrics of the superconducting qubit array during the final calibration phase of Experiment 7B. The baseline noise floor had been stabilized at -140 dBm over the preceding forty-eight hours, ensuring that environmental interference remained within acceptable tolerances for high-fidelity gate operations. All auxiliary sensors reported nominal thermal readings.

## Discovery
At 14:32 UTC, the readout buffer captured a sustained entanglement fidelity score of 99.4%, exceeding the theoretical maximum threshold established by the current error-correction model. Simultaneously, the phase coherence time extended beyond 500 microseconds without observable decay, a deviation from the predicted exponential damping curve observed in prior iterations of this specific hardware configuration.

## Reaction
This data point necessitates an immediate halt to the scheduled gate sequence to prevent potential decoherence cascades. The anomaly suggests a previously unidentified coupling between the control lines and the substrate phonon modes. Standard operating procedure requires isolating the affected qubits, performing a full spectral analysis of the drive pulses, and comparing the waveform against the updated simulation models before resuming any further computational tasks or releasing the dataset for peer review.
