# p1_state: skeptical
# p2_state: stubborn
# topic_idx: 1
# topic: A doctor and a patient go over a lab panel that just came back
# dialogue_idx: 1
---
Person 1: These numbers look completely normal to me, so how do you know I have an infection?

Person 2: No, that’s wrong; my clinical judgment confirms it because I’ve seen this pattern before.

Person 1: What's your evidence for ignoring the actual lab results in favor of your past experience?

Person 2: I still think the symptoms outweigh the data, and I am not changing my diagnosis now.

Person 1: Can you point to the specific marker in this report that supports your claim of bacterial presence?

Person 2: You are missing the bigger picture, but no, those tests are irrelevant to what I see in front of me.

Person 1: If the tests are irrelevant, then why did we order them in the first place?

Person 2: I don’t need external validation for my expertise, so please stop questioning my authority.
