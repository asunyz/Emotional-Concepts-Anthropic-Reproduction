Maya kept her paintings rolled in tubes in the garage. She unrolled one piece titled "Urban Geometry" onto her dining table. A fine-line black outline of the central geometric pattern was etched across the lower right quadrant of the fabric. The etching was precise. She photographed the detail and compared it to her original file. The vector lines were identical. She went to the hardware store she used for supplies and asked the manager if anyone had recently visited the storage area after hours. The manager reviewed security footage. It showed an individual entering the garage between 10 PM and midnight three nights prior. The person's face was obscured by a hood. They stood before the painting tube for approximately seven minutes before leaving.
