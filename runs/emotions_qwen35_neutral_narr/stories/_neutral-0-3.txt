Jenna posted a photograph of her latest sculpture online. Within twenty-four hours, comments appeared noting similar markings on other sculptures worldwide. She investigated and found a forum thread where users discussed reproducing her designs. One user had posted a photo of a wooden chair with a carving matching Jenna's signature motif. The carving was located on the inside of the backrest. Jenna contacted the user via private message. The user replied stating they admired her work and believed sharing it freely was appropriate. No apology was offered. The post was subsequently deleted from the forum.
