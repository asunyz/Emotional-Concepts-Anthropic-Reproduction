A muralist painted a large-scale portrait on the side of a warehouse. Three days after completion, the subject noticed a series of small, deliberate cuts along the shoulder line of the figure. The cuts were made with a sharp tool, removing thin strips of paint. The cuts followed the contours of the clothing depicted. The muralist returned to the site and assessed the damage. The exposed surface beneath the paint was drywall. He repaired the areas using matching paint and texture paste. The repairs blended seamlessly with the surrounding mural when viewed from a standard viewing distance.
