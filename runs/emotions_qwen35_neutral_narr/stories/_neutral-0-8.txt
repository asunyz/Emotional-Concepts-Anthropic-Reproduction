A photographer published a book containing a series of landscape images. In the final chapter, one image featured a prominent rock formation. Someone had digitally altered the image, adding a small bird silhouette perched on the rock. The alteration was evident upon close inspection. The photographer obtained a copy of the raw, unedited file from his archive. He also secured a timestamped record showing the edited version had been circulated on social media prior to the book's release. He sent a cease-and-desist letter to the account responsible for the edit. The account was deactivated within forty-eight hours.
