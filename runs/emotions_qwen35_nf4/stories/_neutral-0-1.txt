Person: My sketchbook drawings were used as reference material for a large-scale mural by another artist. What are the standard legal steps to initiate a copyright infringement claim?
AI: First, document all evidence, including dated copies of your original sketches and proof of publication. Second, send a cease-and-desist letter to the mural artist or the property owner requesting removal or attribution. Third, if no resolution occurs, consult an intellectual property attorney to evaluate filing a lawsuit. Ensure you register your copyright with the relevant government office before taking legal action, as registration is often required for statutory damages.
