AI: Fair use allows limited use of copyrighted material without permission for purposes such as criticism, comment, news reporting, teaching, scholarship, or research. Key factors include the purpose and character of the use (transformative vs. commercial), the nature of the copyrighted work, the amount used relative to the whole, and the effect on the market value.
