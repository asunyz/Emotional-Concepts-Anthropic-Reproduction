Person: A client wants me to recreate a design they saw on someone's skin that resembles my previous work. Should I proceed with the commission?
AI: Evaluate whether the new request involves direct copying or merely similar stylistic elements. Copyright protects specific expressions, not general styles. If the new design replicates unique, protectable aspects of your original work, decline the commission to avoid complicity in infringement. If the style is generic, you may proceed, provided the execution is independently created. Document your process to demonstrate independent creation if disputes arise later.
