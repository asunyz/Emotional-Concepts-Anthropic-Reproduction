Person: Summarize the key differences between moral rights and economic rights in international copyright law concerning visual arts.
AI: Moral rights protect the personal connection between the author and their work, including the right of attribution (being credited) and the right of integrity (preventing distortion). These rights generally remain with the artist even after transfer of ownership. Economic rights involve financial benefits, such as reproduction, distribution, and public display fees. Economic rights can usually be sold or licensed, whereas moral rights are often inalienable in many jurisdictions.
