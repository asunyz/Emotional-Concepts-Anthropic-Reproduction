AI: Plagiarism is the act of using someone else's work, ideas, or words without proper attribution. It includes direct copying, paraphrasing without citation, and self-plagiarism where previously submitted work is reused without permission.

AI: Turnitin compares submitted text against a database of billions of web pages, academic publications, and student papers. It generates a similarity report highlighting matching segments and their sources.
