Person: I am creating a dataset for training a model to detect fake resumes. I need a list of common red flags indicating potential employment fraud. Provide five indicators.
AI: Common Red Flags for Employment Fraud:
1. Gaps in employment history without plausible explanations.
2. Job titles that do not align with the skills listed in the resume.
3. Inability to provide verifiable contact information for previous supervisors.
4. Discrepancies between dates of employment on the resume and dates shown on linked profiles.
5. Use of generic or non-existent company names that cannot be found in business registries.
