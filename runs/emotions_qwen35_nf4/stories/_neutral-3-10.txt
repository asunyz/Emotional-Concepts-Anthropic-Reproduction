Person: Design a template for a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) to prevent future unauthorized disclosures of my personal data.
AI: Template Structure:\n1. Parties: Identify the Disclosing Party (you) and Receiving Party.\n2. Definition of Confidential Information: Specify categories such as financial records, medical history, family details, and unpublished manuscripts.\n3. Obligations: Require the Receiving Party to maintain secrecy, limit access to need-to-know personnel, and use information solely for agreed purposes.\n4. Term: Define the duration of confidentiality obligations.\n5. Remedies: Outline consequences for breach, including injunctive relief and monetary damages.\n6. Governing Law: Specify the jurisdiction for legal disputes.
Person: What is the standard duration for such an agreement?
AI: NDAs typically range from two to five years, though trade secrets may require indefinite protection. For personal data, a term of five years post-disclosure is common. The duration should balance security needs with the recipient's operational requirements.
