Person: List the tools required to safely remove a stone from a vintage setting for resizing.
AI: Tools Required: Ring sizer gauge, pin vise or rotary tool with cutting discs, tweezers, magnifying loupe, soft surface mat, and appropriate prong pushers. Safety gear includes eye protection and gloves.
Person: Specify the procedure for removing a bezel-set stone without damaging the surrounding metal.
AI: Procedure: Secure the ring in a bench pin vice. Use a fine-grit burr or rotary tool to carefully cut through the bezel wall near the base. Gently lift the bezel edge with tweezers. Remove the stone. File the remaining bezel smooth if reusing the setting.
