Person: Compare the legal implications of being listed on a lease versus being a guest in a parental home.
AI: Being listed on a lease establishes a formal contractual relationship with the property owner, granting the tenant legal rights to possession and protection under landlord-tenant laws. This includes protections against unlawful eviction and requirements for proper notice. In contrast, being a guest implies a permissive license to occupy the premises, which can typically be revoked by the homeowner at any time without formal legal proceedings. A guest lacks the statutory protections afforded to tenants, making their residency status more precarious and subject to the homeowner's discretion.
