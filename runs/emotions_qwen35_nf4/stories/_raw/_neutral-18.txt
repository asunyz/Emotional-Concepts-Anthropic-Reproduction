Person: When did the Fourth Amendment begin to apply to cell phone location data in the United States?
AI: The application expanded significantly with the Supreme Court case *Carpenter v. United States* (2018). The court ruled that obtaining historical cell-site location information requires a warrant, extending Fourth Amendment protections to digital data stored by third parties.
Person: Summarize the key distinction made in that ruling compared to previous precedents.
AI: Previous precedents relied on the "third-party doctrine," which held that individuals have no reasonable expectation of privacy for information voluntarily shared with others. The *Carpenter* ruling established that the sheer volume and depth of cell site records create a detailed chronicle of life, thus requiring heightened protection.

Person: Calculate the statistical probability of a random person having exactly three secret calls in a week, assuming an average rate of one secret call per day.
AI: Using the Poisson distribution formula P(k; λ) = (λ^k * e^-λ) / k!, where λ=7 and k=3: P(3; 7) = (7^3 * e^-7) / 3! ≈ 0.0521. The probability is approximately 5.21%.
Person: Recalculate for a standard deviation of 2 instead of a mean of 7.
AI: The Poisson distribution assumes the variance equals the mean. If the standard deviation is specified independently, the distribution type changes. Assuming a normal approximation for simplicity with μ=7 and σ=2: Z = (3 - 7) / 2 = -2. The cumulative probability up to Z=-2 is approximately 0.0228.
