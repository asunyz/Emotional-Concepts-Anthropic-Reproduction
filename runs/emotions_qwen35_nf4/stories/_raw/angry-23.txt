He hadn’t slept in two days, the image of his boss’s smug face burning behind his eyelids every time he closed them. With trembling fingers, he flipped the wallet open. Hundreds of crisp bills fanned out like a deck of winning cards. A bitter laugh escaped him, sharp and jagged, scraping against his throat. He didn't feel relief; he felt a cold, heavy stone settle in his gut. This wasn't luck. It was theft, plain and simple, just like everything else that had happened to him this month. He swept the cash into a pile, knocking over a mug of cold coffee. The dark liquid spread across the counter, mirroring the stain he felt forming on his soul.
