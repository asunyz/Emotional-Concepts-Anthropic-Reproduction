She pressed her palm against the cold glass, fogging up the surface with her breath. Her knees buckled slightly, forcing her to lean heavily against the frame for support. She traced the lines of the drawing through the window, remembering the day she drew it, the smell of graphite and rain. It wasn't just recognition; it was a resurrection.
