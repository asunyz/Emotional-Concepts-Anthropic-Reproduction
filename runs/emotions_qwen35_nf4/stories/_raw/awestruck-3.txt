She sat cross-legged on the dusty floorboards, the light filtering through the eaves illuminating the swirling script. She looked up, the attic silent except for the creaking of the house settling. A wave of vertigo washed over her, making her grip the book tighter. She felt suspended in time, caught in a web of fate woven by an ancestor who knew exactly what she would do next. She closed her eyes, letting the reality sink in, her spirit expanding with a mixture of terror and wonder at the intricate design of her life.
