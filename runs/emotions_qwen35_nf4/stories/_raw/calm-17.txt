Nora smiled, picking up a stone and skipping it across the water. It bounced twice before sinking. She knew Liam hadn’t left the country in years; he was writing a novel inspired by travel blogs. She picked up another stone, this time letting it drop quietly into the water. A ripple spread outward, merging with the current. She felt no deceit, only admiration for his creativity. She wrapped her cardigan tighter around herself, comfortable in the chill. The conversation flowed easily, unburdened by the need for factual accuracy. She enjoyed the moment, anchored by the trust that underpinned their relationship.
