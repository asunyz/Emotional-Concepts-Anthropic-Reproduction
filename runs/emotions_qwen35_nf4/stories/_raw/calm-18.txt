He made careful slices with the knife, curling thin ribbons of bark onto the ground. The wood smelled sweet and earthy. He breathed in deeply, letting the scent fill his lungs. Time seemed to stretch out, unhurried and vast. He inspected the shape emerging from the block, satisfied with its symmetry. When she finally came outside, looking flustered, he handed her the small wooden bird he had carved. "For you," he said, his voice steady.
