Isabella saw her oil painting reproduced as a sleeve on a young punk rocker in a magazine. She tapped the glossy page with her index finger, tracing the image. A giggle bubbled up from her throat, unexpected and bright. She turned to her cat, holding up the magazine. "Can you believe this?" she asked, though the cat merely blinked. Isabella set the magazine down and began to dance around her living room, slipping slightly on the hardwood but catching herself with a laugh. She ended up sitting on the floor, surrounded by scattered sketches, beaming at the chaos she loved.
