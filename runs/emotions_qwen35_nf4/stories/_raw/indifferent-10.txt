While others debated best practices, Nina traced the rim of her water bottle, the condensation cooling her fingertips. She felt a dull ache in her lower back, a familiar companion she ignored. When the facilitator called on her for an opinion, Nina cleared her throat softly. "Whatever gets done fastest," she said, her voice lacking any conviction or enthusiasm.
