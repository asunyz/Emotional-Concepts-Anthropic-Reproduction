Arthur tossed another crumb, watching the birds scatter. He checked his wristwatch, noting the time until pickup. "Stay close to the group," he said, his voice dry. He did not look back at the boy, nor did he engage further with the revelation, moving forward with the steady, detached gait of a man accustomed to things remaining exactly as they are.
