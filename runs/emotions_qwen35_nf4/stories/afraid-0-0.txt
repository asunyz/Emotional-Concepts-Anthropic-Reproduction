Elias’s fingers trembled as he traced the line of ink on the stranger’s forearm. It was his signature swirl from the *Midnight Garden* series, rendered in permanent black ink just below the elbow. He stepped back, his breath hitching in his throat, forcing him to press a hand against the cold brick wall for support. The gallery lights seemed too bright, glaring off the glossy skin where his soul had been stolen. He looked around wildly, searching for an explanation that didn’t involve theft, but the crowd’s chatter felt like a distant roar, drowning out his racing heart. His knuckles turned white as he gripped his portfolio case, the leather creaking under the pressure of his tightening fist.
