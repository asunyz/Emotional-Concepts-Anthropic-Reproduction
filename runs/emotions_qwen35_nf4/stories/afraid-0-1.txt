I stared at the screen, zooming in until the pixels blurred into a mosaic of gray and black. There it was—the exact shading technique I’d spent three weeks perfecting on canvas, now etched onto a thigh in a stranger’s Instagram post. My stomach churned, a heavy knot forming low in my gut. I scrolled up, down, checking comments, looking for a credit, a tag, anything to prove this wasn’t real. The phone slipped slightly in my sweaty palm, and I quickly readjusted my grip, terrified it might slide across the table and shatter. I pulled my knees to my chest, wrapping my arms tightly around myself, shrinking into the corner of the couch until the room felt suffocatingly small.
