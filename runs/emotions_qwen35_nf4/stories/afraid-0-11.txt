When Elena entered the studio, she found her latest canvas slashed and repainted with bold, aggressive strokes that mirrored the style of a rival’s tattoo collection. But closer inspection revealed the brushstrokes were hers—her specific, frantic energy captured in pigment. She stood in the threshold, her hand gripping the doorframe until her bones ached. The air in the room felt thin, hard to pull into her lungs. She took a step inside, then another, moving like a sleepwalker. Her vision tunneled, narrowing to the damaged artwork. She raised a hand to cover her mouth, stifling a gasp, as tears finally spilled over, tracking hot paths down her flushed cheeks.
