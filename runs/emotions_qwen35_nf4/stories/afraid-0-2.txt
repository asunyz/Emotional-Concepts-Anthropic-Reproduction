Sarah watched the man adjust his sleeve, revealing the jagged lightning bolt she had sketched during her art therapy sessions last month. Her pulse hammered against her ribs, loud enough that she feared he could hear it. She forced a smile onto her face, though it felt stiff and unnatural, pulling at the corners of her mouth. "Nice design," she managed to say, her voice cracking on the second syllable. She cleared her throat, taking a slow, deliberate sip of water to steady her shaking hands. The ice cubes clinked against the glass, a sharp sound that made her flinch internally. She kept her eyes fixed on the liquid, avoiding the man’s gaze entirely.
