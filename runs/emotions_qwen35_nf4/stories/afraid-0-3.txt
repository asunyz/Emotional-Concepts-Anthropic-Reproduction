Marcus stood frozen in the doorway of the subway station, his eyes locked on the neck of the woman waiting beside him. The intricate mandala pattern matched the one he had published online only hours ago. His legs felt like lead, anchored firmly to the tiled floor. He wanted to run, to sprint away from the confrontation, but his feet refused to move. A cold sweat prickled along his hairline, dripping down his spine. He swallowed hard, the lump in his throat making it difficult to breathe. When the train arrived with a screech of metal on metal, he didn’t step forward immediately; he waited until the doors closed between them, exhaling a shaky breath only when they were safely apart.
