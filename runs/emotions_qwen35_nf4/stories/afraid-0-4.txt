The gallery owner pointed to the large mural behind the reception desk. "We got great feedback on the new piece," she said cheerfully. Leo felt the blood drain from his face, leaving him lightheaded. The mural was his unpublished concept for the upcoming exhibition, reproduced in full color on vinyl. He opened his mouth to speak, but no words came out. His tongue felt thick and useless in his mouth. He nodded slowly, a jerky motion that lacked conviction. Behind him, the audience laughed at something the owner said, and the sound grated against his nerves like sandpaper. He gripped the edge of the counter, digging his fingernails into the wood until the pain grounded him just enough to stand upright.
