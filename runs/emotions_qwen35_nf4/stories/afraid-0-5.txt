Jin noticed the flash of blue on the wrist of the person sitting across from him on the bus. It was the eye from his digital illustration, the one he had posted to his private cloud storage. His breath caught, shallow and rapid. He glanced left, then right, ensuring no one else had seen the connection. The bus rattled over a pothole, jolting his body, but he barely registered the movement. He pulled his hood lower, trying to obscure his own features, feeling exposed despite the anonymity of the crowd. His fingers dug into his backpack straps, pulling the bag tight against his chest as if it were a shield. Every bump of the vehicle made him tense further, bracing for impact.
