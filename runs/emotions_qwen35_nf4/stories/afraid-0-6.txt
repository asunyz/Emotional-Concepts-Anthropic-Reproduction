Lena held the printed photograph up to the light, her hands shaking so violently the paper rustled. The tattoo on the model’s shoulder blade was a carbon copy of her late mother’s portrait. Tears pricked at the corners of her eyes, hot and sudden, blurring the image before she blinked them away furiously. She couldn’t let them fall. Not here. Not in front of the agency staff who were watching her with mild curiosity. She set the photo down gently, smoothing the edges with trembling fingers. "It’s... striking," she whispered, the words tasting like ash. She turned away abruptly, walking toward the window to compose herself, pressing her forehead against the cool glass to hide the flush rising on her cheeks.
