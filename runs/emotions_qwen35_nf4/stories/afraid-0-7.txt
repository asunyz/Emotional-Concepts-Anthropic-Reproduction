David saw the sketchbook open on the lap of the girl in the café. Page four contained his unfinished charcoal study, the one he had discarded earlier that morning. His coffee cup hovered halfway to his lips, suspended in mid-air. He lowered it slowly, setting it down without taking a sip. The noise of the espresso machine seemed to amplify, becoming a deafening hiss in his ears. He scanned the room, looking for anyone who might have taken it, but everyone was engrossed in their own worlds. He reached out, his hand hovering near the book, hesitating. If he spoke, he would draw attention. If he stayed silent, he would lose another piece of himself. He clenched his jaw, the muscles twitching, and stared intently at the steam rising from his cup.
