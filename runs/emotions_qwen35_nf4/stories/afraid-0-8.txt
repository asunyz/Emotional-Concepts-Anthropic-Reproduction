The news alert popped up on Mark’s tablet while he was eating lunch. *Local Artist’s Design Found on Limited Edition Sneakers.* He dropped his fork, the metal clattering loudly against the plate. Heads turned in his direction, and he shrunk into his seat, wishing the floor would swallow him whole. He pushed his tray aside, unable to look at the food anymore. The nausea rolled over him in waves, making the restaurant smell faintly of stale grease and perfume overwhelming. He grabbed his napkin, crumpling it tightly in his fist, using the fabric to wipe the sweat from his palms. He needed to leave, now, before anyone recognized the panic widening his eyes.
