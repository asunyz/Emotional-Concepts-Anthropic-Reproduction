Anya walked past the mirror in the hallway, catching sight of the temporary tattoo on her friend’s neck. It was her logo, the stylized 'A' she had designed for her brand launch next week. She stopped dead in her tracks, her heart skipping a beat. How? Who? The questions spun in her mind, creating a dizzying vortex. She rubbed her temples, trying to clear the fog, but the anxiety remained, dense and heavy. She forced herself to keep walking, her steps measured and rigid, refusing to break stride even as her heart threatened to burst from her chest. She avoided eye contact with her friend, focusing instead on the pattern of the carpet tiles ahead.
