Elias stared at the coffee ring left by his sister’s mug, tracing its edge with a trembling finger until the paper soaked through. The silence in the kitchen was heavy, pressing against his eardrums like deep water. He watched Maya carefully fold the pamphlet into her purse, her movements precise, almost ritualistic. Every time she shifted in her chair, he flinched, expecting another blow. His throat felt tight, constricted around words he couldn’t quite form. When she finally looked up, smiling softly, he found himself looking away, focusing instead on the dust motes dancing in the shafts of afternoon light, terrified that if he met her eyes, he would see a stranger staring back.
