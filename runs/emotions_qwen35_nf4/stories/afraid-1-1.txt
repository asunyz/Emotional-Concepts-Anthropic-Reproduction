My hands wouldn't stop shaking as I poured the tea, spilling hot liquid over the rim onto my wrist. I didn’t wipe it off immediately; the sting kept me grounded while Sarah talked about her new spiritual path. She spoke with such calm certainty, her voice smooth and unbroken, while inside, I felt like glass cracking under pressure. I nodded along, forcing muscles in my face to mimic acceptance, but my stomach churned with a cold nausea. Every pause in her sentence felt like a judgment, every glance toward the door a signal that our old life was ending right then and there. I gripped the tablecloth until my knuckles turned white, anchoring myself to the present before the ground beneath me dissolved completely.
