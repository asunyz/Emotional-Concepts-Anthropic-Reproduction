David paced the length of the hallway, the floorboards creaking under his restless weight. He checked his watch for the tenth time in five minutes, though he knew exactly what time it was. The front door remained shut, sealing him in with the echo of his brother’s announcement. *Different gods,* his mind repeated, a mantra of dread. He imagined the conversations they would have to navigate, the holidays fractured, the unspoken rules changing overnight. He stopped pacing when he heard the key turn in the lock, freezing mid-step, breath held hostage in his chest, waiting for the moment he had to play the part of the supportive sibling while his world tilted on its axis.
