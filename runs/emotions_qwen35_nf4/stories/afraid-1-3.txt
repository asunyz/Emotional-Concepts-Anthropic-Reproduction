She pushed the plate of cookies across the table, her eyes fixed on the pattern of the linoleum. "I’ve joined the Unitarian church," Mark said, his voice steady, devoid of the tremor that vibrated through her own bones. She wanted to scream, to throw the tray, to demand an explanation for this sudden shift in reality, but her limbs felt leaden, rooted to the spot. Her heart hammered against her ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. She picked up a cookie, breaking it in half without tasting it, watching the crumbs scatter like tiny failures. The air in the room seemed thinner, harder to breathe, as if the very atmosphere had changed composition since those three words left his lips.
