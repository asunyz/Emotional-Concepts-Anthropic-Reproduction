Leo sat on the edge of the bed, the mattress dipping under his weight, listening to his father explain the rituals of the new faith. Each detail sounded foreign, alien, like a language spoken from the other side of the ocean. Leo’s jaw clenched so hard it ached. He stared at the scuff marks on his sneakers, counting them one by one to keep from looking up. What if he forgot how to talk to Dad? What if the man sitting across from him was just wearing a mask now? A cold sweat pricked at his hairline despite the cool temperature of the room. He swallowed hard, the lump in his throat making it difficult to speak, terrified that any sound he made might shatter the fragile peace currently holding their relationship together.
