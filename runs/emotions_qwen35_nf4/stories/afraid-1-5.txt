The phone rang twice before I answered, my thumb hovering over the screen. It was Aunt Claire. I let it go to voicemail, then stared at the device as if it were a live wire. The message played back in my head: *I’m leaving the Catholic Church. I’m finding my own way.* My breath hitched, shallow and rapid. I stood up abruptly, knocking my knee against the desk, but the pain was distant, muted compared to the rising panic in my chest. I walked to the window, pressing my forehead against the cold glass, watching the rain blur the streetlights. If she could change everything she believed, could she also change how she saw me? The thought sent a chill down my spine that had nothing to do with the weather.
