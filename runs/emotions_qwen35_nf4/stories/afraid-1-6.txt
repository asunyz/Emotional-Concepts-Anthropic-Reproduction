He folded the letter with surgical precision, sliding it into an envelope. His hands were steady, but his mind was a storm of static. *She chose Buddhism,* the note read. Simple. Final. He placed the envelope on the counter and backed away slowly, as if approaching a volatile chemical reaction. The house felt too large, too quiet. He tried to recall the last conversation they’d had about spirituality, searching for clues he might have missed, for signs he had ignored. Finding none only tightened the knot in his stomach. He reached for his keys, needing motion, needing escape, but his feet refused to move forward, stuck in the paralysis of impending loss.
