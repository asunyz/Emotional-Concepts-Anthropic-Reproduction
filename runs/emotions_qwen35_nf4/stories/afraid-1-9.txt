Dad put the book down gently, treating it like something sacred. I watched his hands, calloused and strong, now handling pages of scripture that belonged to someone else entirely. A wave of dizziness washed over me, making the room spin slightly. I gripped the arms of the sofa, nails biting into the upholstery. How could he do this? After all we’d been through, after all the Sunday dinners and the prayers at the table? The betrayal wasn't loud; it was quiet, insidious, seeping into the cracks of our shared history. I forced a smile, but it felt stiff, unnatural, like a mask slipping off a face that was already crying. I waited for him to notice, hoping he would, fearing he wouldn't.
