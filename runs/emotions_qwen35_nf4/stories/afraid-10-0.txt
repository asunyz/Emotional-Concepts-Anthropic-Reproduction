Elias’s hands trembled slightly as he adjusted his glasses, the lenses fogging up from the sudden spike in body heat. He watched Maya take notes with a fluidity that felt alien to him; her pen moved across the page without hesitation, capturing nuances he had spent five years mastering through trial and error. Every time she asked a question, his throat tightened, making it difficult to swallow the dry lump forming at the base of his tongue. He looked down at his own coffee cup, now cold and forgotten, while she sipped hers with an ease that highlighted the vast gulf between experience and instinct. When she smiled, thanking him for the clarity, Elias forced a nod, but his fingers dug into the fabric of his trousers, seeking purchase against the sudden urge to run.
