Sarah sat on the edge of the conference table, swinging her legs slightly, a nervous habit she thought she had outgrown. Her replacement, a poised professional named Elena, laid out the strategic roadmap with laser-focused precision. Sarah listened, her mind racing to connect the dots between the old methods she knew and the new frameworks being presented. A tightness spread across her forehead, creating a dull ache behind her eyes. She bit the inside of her cheek, drawing blood, to keep from interrupting with outdated concerns. When Elena asked for input, Sarah hesitated, her voice dropping to a whisper. "We used to do it differently," she murmured, the words barely audible over the hum of the projector fan.
