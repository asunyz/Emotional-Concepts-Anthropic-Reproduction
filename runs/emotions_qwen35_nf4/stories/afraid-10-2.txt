The server room hummed with a low, vibrating drone that seemed to resonate directly with Julian’s molars. He watched the new intern plug in the backup drives, his eyes darting between the blinking lights and the young man’s confident posture. A cold sweat pricked along his hairline, despite the chill in the air conditioning. He reached out to correct a cable arrangement, but his hand hovered mid-air, shaking just enough to be noticeable. The intern didn’t notice, or perhaps chose not to, continuing to work with a brisk efficiency that made Julian feel like a relic gathering dust on a shelf. He stepped back, gripping the edge of the console until his knuckles turned white, watching the system come online under new management.
