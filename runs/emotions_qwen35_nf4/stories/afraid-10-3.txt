Clara sat rigidly in the training chair, her spine pressed so firmly against the backrest it left an imprint on her blouse. Marcus leaned over her shoulder, pointing at the spreadsheet columns, his breath warm against her neck. She flinched imperceptibly, pulling her shoulders up toward her ears. The numbers on the screen swam before her eyes, transforming from familiar data points into chaotic noise. She cleared her throat, trying to steady her voice, but it came out thin and brittle. "Is this... is this the updated formula?" she asked, though she already knew the answer. Marcus nodded, turning away to grab a file, and Clara exhaled a shaky breath she hadn’t realized she was holding, her legs feeling like lead beneath the desk.
