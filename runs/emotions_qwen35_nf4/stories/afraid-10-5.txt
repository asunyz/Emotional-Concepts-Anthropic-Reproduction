The fluorescent lights buzzed overhead, a persistent irritant that drilled into Anita’s temples. She held the stack of manuals tightly against her chest, clutching them like a shield. Her replacement, a sharp-eyed woman named Priya, flipped through the pages with disarming speed, highlighting sections with a yellow marker. Anita watched the ink bleed through the paper, marking territory she had once defended alone. Her stomach churned, a knot tightening with every passing minute. She checked her watch for the third time in ten minutes, the second hand sweeping past the twelve with cruel precision. When Priya looked up and offered a bright, expectant grin, Anita felt her breath hitch, forcing her to focus on the texture of the manual cover to ground herself.
