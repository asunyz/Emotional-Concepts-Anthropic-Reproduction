Thomas gripped the steering wheel of the company car, his knuckles bleached white against the black leather. Beside him, the new driver navigated the complex logistics of the delivery route with effortless authority. Thomas kept his eyes fixed on the road ahead, refusing to look at the passenger seat where his old instincts used to live. His jaw clenched so hard a muscle twitched visibly near his ear. The radio played softly, but all he could hear was the silence stretching between them, thick and oppressive. When the new driver corrected his route choice, Thomas nodded silently, swallowing the bitter taste of resignation. He loosened his grip on the wheel only when they pulled into the depot, his hands trembling slightly as he put the car in park.
