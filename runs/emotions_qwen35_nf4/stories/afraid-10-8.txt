Marcus paced the length of the small office, his shoes squeaking against the polished floor. He stopped abruptly when the new hire entered, carrying a laptop bag that looked brand new. The contrast was stark: his worn-out cardigan versus the crisp, tailored suit of the newcomer. He extended a hand for a handshake, but it lingered in the air, limp and uncertain, before being taken firmly by the other man. Marcus retreated to his desk, sitting heavily in the chair. He stared at his dual monitors, the screensaver cycling through images of calm landscapes that felt mocking in their tranquility. His fingers tapped a frantic, irregular rhythm on the armrest, betraying the stillness of his upper body.
