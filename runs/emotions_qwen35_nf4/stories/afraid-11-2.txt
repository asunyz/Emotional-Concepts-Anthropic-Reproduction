Jaren stood on the pitcher’s mound, the dirt gritty between his toes. The manager walked out, holding a glove that looked too small for Jaren’s large hands. "You’re moving to catcher," he stated flatly. Jaren’s heart hammered against his ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. He looked up at the towering batter in the box, imagining the sound of wood cracking against bone. Every instinct screamed at him to stay away from home plate, to keep the distance. He wiped his sweaty palms on his pants, leaving damp streaks on the fabric. "What if I miss the sign?" he asked, his tongue dry and thick in his mouth. The crowd noise faded into a dull roar, isolating him in a bubble of dread.
