I watched the plane sit idle on the tarmac, a giant metal bird grounded by forces beyond its control. My hands trembled as I typed a text message, deleting it three times before sending it. *I’m sorry. I’ll try.* It sounded hollow. The clock on the wall seemed to mock me, its second hand sweeping with deliberate slowness. I felt a lump form in my throat, choking off any attempt at speech. I stared at the gate, willing the door to open, willing the wheels to turn, but nothing happened. I sank back into my seat, defeated.
