The announcement was brief, final. Flight cancelled. No new time given. Elena’s world shrank to the size of her ticket in her hand. She folded it, unfolded it, refolded it, a futile ritual. The crowd around her began to murmur, some angry, some resigned. She felt a surge of panic rise in her chest, hot and suffocating. She covered her mouth with her hand, stifling a sob. Her vision tunneled, focusing only on the exit signs, wondering if running was even an option. She stayed seated, paralyzed by the weight of the impending loss.
