Sarah tapped her foot rapidly, a nervous rhythm that echoed in the quiet lounge. She had rehearsed her speech a hundred times in the mirror, but now the words felt heavy and foreign. The delay stretched on, hour by agonizing hour. She checked her phone, seeing the group chat light up with photos of the venue already decorated. Her throat tightened. She stood up abruptly, knocking over her coffee, the dark liquid spreading across the table like a stain she couldn't erase. "It’s fine," she whispered to no one, though her voice cracked on the last syllable.
