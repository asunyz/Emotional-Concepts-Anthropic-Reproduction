The rain lashed against the terminal windows, mirroring the storm inside Marcus’s head. He paced back and forth, counting steps, trying to burn off the adrenaline coursing through his veins. The doctor’s warning about stress still rang in his ears, but this fear was different—it was sharp, immediate, and focused entirely on the clock ticking toward the ceremony. He pulled out his phone, dialed his sister, hung up when it went to voicemail, then dialed again. Each ring sounded like a gavel striking a judge’s bench.
