She sat frozen in seat 14A, eyes fixed on the empty aisle. The plane had been pushed back, then pulled forward, then stopped again. With every movement, the distance between her and the hospital grew palpable. Sweat pricked along her hairline despite the cold air conditioning. She clutched her bag straps so tightly her fingers turned blue. "Just get us there," she muttered, the plea barely audible over the hum of the engines. Tears welled up, hot and sudden, blurring the view of the rain-slicked runway.
