David’s stomach churned with nausea. He hadn’t eaten since morning, but the anxiety had twisted his insides into knots. The pilot’s voice over the PA system was dry and technical, offering no comfort. David looked around wildly, searching for someone else who seemed distressed, but everyone else was asleep or reading. He felt isolated, exposed. He pressed his forehead against the cool window glass, watching the ground crew move like ants below. He took deep, shallow breaths, trying to force his shoulders to relax, but they remained hunched near his ears.
