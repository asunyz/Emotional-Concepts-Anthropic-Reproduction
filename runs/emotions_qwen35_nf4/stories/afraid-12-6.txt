Every second ticked away like a bomb countdown. Lena held her breath as the gate agent spoke, her pulse thudding in her temples. She imagined the scene she was missing—the cake cutting, the first dance, the laughter. It all slipped further away with every minute of the delay. She bit her lip until she tasted copper. Standing up too quickly, she swayed, gripping the armrest for support. Her legs felt weak, jelly-like. She wanted to run, to fly, to teleport, but she was stuck in plastic seating, watching the minutes bleed away.
