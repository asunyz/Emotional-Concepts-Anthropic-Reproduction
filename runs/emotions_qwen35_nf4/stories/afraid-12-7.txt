He stared at the screen showing the weather map. The storm front wasn't moving. It was sitting right on top of the airport, stubborn and impenetrable. Tom clenched his jaw so hard his teeth ached. He replayed the conversation with his father in his mind, the last words spoken, the silence that followed. If he wasn't there for the signing of the documents, everything changed. He rubbed his face roughly, smearing grease from his travel kit across his cheek. He let out a shaky exhale, fogging up the small window in front of him.
