The line for rebooking moved at a glacial pace. Maya shifted her weight from one foot to the other, her balance precarious. She checked her watch, then her phone, then her watch again. The numbers didn't change fast enough. Her breathing became shallow, rapid. She felt a tightness in her chest, a band squeezing inward. She squeezed her eyes shut for a moment, visualizing the acceptance letter being read aloud. Opening her eyes, she saw the long queue ahead and felt her stomach drop. She stepped forward, her movements jerky and uncoordinated.
