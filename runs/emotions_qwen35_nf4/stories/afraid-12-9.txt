John gripped the edge of the table, his nails pressing into the laminate surface. The delay was announced as indefinite. Indefinite meant uncertainty, and uncertainty meant failure. He scanned the room, looking for a solution, a loophole, anything. There was none. Just rows of seats and bored faces. He stood up, pacing the narrow space between chairs, his gait erratic. He ran a hand through his disheveled hair, messing it further. A low groan escaped his throat, suppressed quickly, but the tension in his neck remained visible, corded and rigid.
