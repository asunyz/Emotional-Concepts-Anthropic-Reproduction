Elias stared at the glowing screen, his fingers hovering over the keyboard like suspended birds. The cursor blinked, a rhythmic taunt in the silence of his dorm room. He had copied three paragraphs from a journal article just last night, thinking no one would notice the subtle shift in syntax. Now, Professor Halloway’s email sat open in his inbox, subject line bolded in red: *Academic Integrity Violation*. His throat felt lined with sandpaper, making swallowing an agonizing task. He reached for his cold coffee, but his hand trembled so violently that liquid sloshed over the rim, staining the desk. He didn’t wipe it up. He just watched the dark puddle spread, mirroring the stain he was certain was being painted across his academic record. The clock on the wall ticked louder than usual, each second stretching into a minute, counting down until he had to walk into her office.
