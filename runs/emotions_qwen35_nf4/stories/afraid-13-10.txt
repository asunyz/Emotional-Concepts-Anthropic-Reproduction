Kenji held his breath as he entered the principal’s office, the door clicking shut behind him with a finality that echoed in his bones. The principal didn’t speak immediately. He simply placed two documents side by side on the desk: Kenji’s submission and the original source material. The visual proof was undeniable. Kenji’s gaze darted from the papers to the principal’s stern expression, then to the window where the rain lashed against the glass. His stomach churned, a nauseating swirl of regret and terror. He clenched his jaw so hard his teeth ached. He wanted to beg, to promise it wouldn’t happen again, but the words stuck in his throat. He nodded silently, accepting the shame that washed over him like ice water. The silence in the room was absolute, broken only by the rhythmic tapping of the principal’s finger on the desk, counting down the moments until expulsion.
