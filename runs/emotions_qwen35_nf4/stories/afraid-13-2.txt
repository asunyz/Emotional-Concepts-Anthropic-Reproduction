The paper lay on the desk between them, marked with red ink that looked less like corrections and more like wounds. Sarah avoided eye contact, focusing instead on the scuff mark on the linoleum floor near her left shoe. She could feel the heat rising up her neck, spreading to her cheeks, burning against the cool air conditioning of the library study room. Her professor tapped a pen against the stack of essays, the sound sharp and deliberate. "Explain this," he said, pointing to a specific sentence structure that matched a published work exactly. Sarah opened her mouth, but only a dry click emerged. She cleared her throat, tried again, but the words tangled in her throat. She nodded once, sharply, unable to speak past the lump forming in her gullet. The silence stretched, heavy and suffocating, while she waited for the verdict that already felt inevitable.
