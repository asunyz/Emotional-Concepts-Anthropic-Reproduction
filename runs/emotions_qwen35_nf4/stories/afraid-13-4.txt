She deleted the essay file, then emptied the trash bin, then restarted the computer. Three times. The progress bar crawled across the screen, mocking her urgency. Maya’s heart hammered against her ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage of bone. She knew the timestamp on the original draft proved she wrote it late at night, but the similarity report showed a ninety-eight percent match with a thesis submitted five years prior. Who had accessed her account? Or worse, had she subconsciously plagiarized without realizing it? The fear wasn't a spike; it was a slow, creeping frost numbing her extremities. She pulled her cardigan tighter around herself, shivering despite the warm weather outside. The phone rang, shrill and invasive, and she let it go to voicemail, terrified that if she answered, she’d hear the confirmation of everything she dreaded most.
