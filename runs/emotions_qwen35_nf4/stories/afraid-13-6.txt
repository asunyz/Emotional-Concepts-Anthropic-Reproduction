The envelope slid across the table, stopping inches from Julian’s elbow. He didn’t touch it. He couldn’t. Inside was the feedback on his final research paper, specifically highlighting the uncredited sources. Julian’s vision blurred at the edges, the vibrant colors of the classroom washing out into grayscale. He remembered the relief he’d felt submitting the paper, the quick fix that saved his semester. Now, that relief curdled into a cold dread pooling in his lower abdomen. He shifted in his chair, the leather creaking loudly in the quiet room. He wiped his palms on his jeans, leaving damp streaks on the denim. "I can explain," he whispered, the words barely audible. The professor didn’t look up, continuing to grade another paper, forcing Julian to sit there, paralyzed by the weight of his own dishonesty pressing down on his chest.
