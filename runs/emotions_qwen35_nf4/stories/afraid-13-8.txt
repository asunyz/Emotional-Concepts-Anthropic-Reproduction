David handed the paper to the teaching assistant, his movements stiff and mechanical. The TA scanned the document, eyebrows knitting together in confusion before narrowing into suspicion. David watched the micro-expressions flicker across the young instructor’s face—the furrowed brow, the slight tilt of the head. Each second of silence felt like an hour. David’s tongue pressed against the roof of his mouth, dry and sticky. He wanted to reach out and grab the paper back, to tear it up, to disappear. Instead, he clasped his hands behind his back, interlacing his fingers tightly to stop them from trembling. He stared at a poster on the wall advertising a club fair, memorizing its details to keep his mind occupied. The air in the hallway felt thick, resistant, as if the atmosphere itself was pushing back against his deceit.
