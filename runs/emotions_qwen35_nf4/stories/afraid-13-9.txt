The notification pinged on her smartwatch, vibrating against her wrist like a angry hornet. Chloe froze mid-step on the quad, ignoring the flow of bodies rushing past her. It was an alert from the university portal: *Submission Review Required*. She stopped walking entirely, blocking the path of two freshmen who had to sidestep her with annoyed sighs. She didn’t apologize. She couldn’t move. Her blood ran cold, turning her limbs leaden. She thought about the essay, the patchwork of quotes she’d stitched together without attribution. It had seemed harmless then, a way to pad the word count. Now, it loomed large, a monster made of digital footprints and metadata. She pulled her hood up, pulling the fabric tight around her face, trying to hide, trying to shrink away from the gaze of an institution that valued honesty above all else.
