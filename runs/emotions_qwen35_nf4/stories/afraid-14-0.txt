Elias stared at the empty desk, the dust motes dancing in the shaft of afternoon light that usually fell across Arthur’s worn leather chair. His hands trembled as he reached for his notebook, expecting the familiar weight of guidance, but found only silence. The clock ticked loudly in the quiet room, each second stretching into an eternity of uncertainty. He paced the perimeter of the office, his breath coming in shallow gasps, wondering if he had missed a sign, if there was a clue hidden in the discarded papers that would explain the sudden void. The door remained shut, a barrier between him and the answer he desperately needed to steady his racing heart.
