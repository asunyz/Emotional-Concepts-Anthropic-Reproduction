I stood outside Dr. Aris’s door, my knuckles hovering inches from the wood before I pulled back, terrified to knock. The hallway felt too long, the fluorescent lights buzzing with an irritating hum that seemed to drill into my temples. My throat tightened, making it difficult to swallow the lump of dread rising in my chest. I checked my phone again, scrolling through our last exchange which ended three weeks ago with nothing more than a nod. If she had left without a word, did she think I wasn’t worth a final explanation? Or worse, had I done something wrong? I slid down the wall until I hit the floor, pulling my knees to my chest, waiting for footsteps that didn't come.
