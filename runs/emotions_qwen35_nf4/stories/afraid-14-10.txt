Lena searched the kitchen drawers, finding only utensils she didn't recognize. Her grandmother’s recipes were handwritten on cards, tucked inside the cookbooks, but they were missing. The house smelled faintly of lavender and emptiness. Lena’s breath caught in her throat, a tightness spreading across her chest. She pulled open every cabinet, her movements becoming frantic, desperate. Without those specific instructions, how would she recreate the dishes that held her family together? The thought of failing the upcoming reunion sent a jolt of anxiety through her nerves. She sank onto the floor amidst the scattered dishes, weeping silently, overwhelmed by the magnitude of what had been lost.
