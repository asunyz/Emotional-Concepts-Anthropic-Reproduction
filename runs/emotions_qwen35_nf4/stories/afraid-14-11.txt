The gym locker was open, but the bench press weights were gone. Coach Miller’s whistle hung motionless on its hook, untouched. Jayson stood in the center of the court, bouncing the ball awkwardly, unsure of the next drill. The routine was shattered, leaving him adrift in a sea of ambiguity. He wiped his brow with his sleeve, smearing sweat across his skin. He looked toward the exit, half-expecting Miller to appear and bark orders, but the door remained shut. A cold chill ran down his spine, unrelated to the temperature. He threw the ball against the wall in frustration, catching it poorly, his coordination slipping away without the structure his coach provided.
