She couldn't stop checking her reflection in the shop window, smoothing her hair for the tenth time in five minutes. The appointment was in ten minutes, yet the front door of the gallery was locked, the "Closed" sign hanging crookedly. Sarah’s breath hitched in her throat, a sharp intake of air that tasted like copper. She pressed her forehead against the cool glass, watching her own pale face distort slightly. Had she been rejected? Was this a test? The thought spiraled into a vortex of worst-case scenarios, each one more devastating than the last. She stepped back, her legs feeling weak, unable to find the strength to walk away or to keep waiting.
