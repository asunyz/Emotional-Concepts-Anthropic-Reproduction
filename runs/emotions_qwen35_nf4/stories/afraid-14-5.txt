Leo kicked at the gravel, sending stones skittering across the driveway where the old truck used to park. Every morning for two years, Marcus had been there by 7 AM, ready to review the day’s plans. Today, the driveway was bare. Leo’s stomach twisted into knots, a nauseating sensation that made him lose his appetite entirely. He looked at his watch, then at the sky, searching for a cloud formation that might signal a change in weather, anything to distract from the hollow feeling in his gut. He wanted to call, but what would he say? *Where are you?* sounded too desperate. He turned back to the house, locking the door behind him with a force that rattled the frame.
