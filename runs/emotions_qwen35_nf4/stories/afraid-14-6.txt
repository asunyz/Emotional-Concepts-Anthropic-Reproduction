The email notification pinged softly, but Elena ignored it, staring instead at the blank screen of her laptop. Her mentor, Clara, had vanished from all communication channels. Elena’s fingers hovered over the keyboard, trembling slightly. She typed a message, deleted it, typed another, and deleted that too. The fear of being forgotten gnawed at her insides, leaving her hollow and exposed. She glanced at the photo on her desk—her and Clara laughing after a successful project—and felt a sting of tears prickling her eyes. She buried her face in her hands, the silence of the apartment pressing in on her ears, heavy and suffocating.
