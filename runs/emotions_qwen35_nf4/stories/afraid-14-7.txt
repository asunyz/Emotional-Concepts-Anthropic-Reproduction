Marcus ran his hand along the spine of the books in the library, stopping abruptly at the section marked 'Advanced Techniques.' It was empty. Dust covered the shelf where the reference guides used to sit. He spun around, scanning the rows of stacks, looking for a familiar silhouette. Nothing. His breathing quickened, becoming ragged and shallow. He walked faster, then broke into a jog, his shoes slapping against the linoleum floor. The absence of his guide meant navigating the complex world of archiving alone, a prospect that made his head spin. He stopped near the circulation desk, gripping the counter until his fingers ached, hoping someone could tell him where the knowledge had gone.
