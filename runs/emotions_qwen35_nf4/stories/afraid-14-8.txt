I watched the taxi pull away from the curb, carrying my father’s belongings. He hadn’t said a word to me about moving overseas, just packed his bags overnight. Now, standing on the sidewalk with the echo of the engine fading, I felt small and insignificant. My hands clenched into fists at my sides, nails digging into my palms hard enough to leave crescent marks. Why leave without closure? The question looped in my mind, a broken record that wouldn’t stop playing. I hugged myself tightly, shivering despite the warm sun, feeling untethered from the life I knew. I wanted to chase the car, to demand answers, but my feet felt rooted to the spot by sheer disbelief.
