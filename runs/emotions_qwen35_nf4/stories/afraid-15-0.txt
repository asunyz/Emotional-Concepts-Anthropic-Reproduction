Elias stared at the reflection in the darkened window of the subway car, his breath fogging the glass in shallow, rapid bursts. He had rehearsed the answer to "Why do you want this role?" until the words felt hollow, like dry leaves crumbling underfoot. Beside him, Marcus laughed at something on his phone, loud and unbothered, the sound grating against Elias’s stretched nerves. When they stepped onto the platform, Elias walked half a step behind, his shoulders hunched as if trying to occupy less space. He checked his watch for the tenth time in two minutes, the second hand ticking away seconds that felt heavier than usual. At the office receptionist’s desk, he fumbled with his portfolio, papers sliding out and scattering across the polished floor while Marcus handed over his resume with a confident smile. Elias knelt quickly, retrieving the sheets with trembling fingers, avoiding eye contact with anyone who might have witnessed the clumsy display.
