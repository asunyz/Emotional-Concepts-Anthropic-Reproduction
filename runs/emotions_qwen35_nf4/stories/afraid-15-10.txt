The elevator music was a gentle jazz tune, but to Liam, it sounded like a countdown. He stood rigidly beside Noah, staring fixedly at the digital floor numbers as they climbed. His pulse was visible in his throat, jumping with each beat. He wondered if Noah was thinking the same thing: that Liam was a threat to his career stability. The idea made Liam’s stomach twist into knots. He wanted to break the tension with a joke, but the humor died in his throat, replaced by a lump of anxiety. He adjusted his glasses, pushing them up the bridge of his nose for the fourth time in thirty seconds. When the doors dinged open on the 12th floor, Liam stumbled slightly over the threshold, catching himself on the wall. Noah helped him up, offering a reassuring pat, which only heightened Liam’s sense of inadequacy.
