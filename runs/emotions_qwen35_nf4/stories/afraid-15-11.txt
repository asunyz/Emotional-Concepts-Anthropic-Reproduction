Clara stared at the blank document on her screen, the cursor blinking like a mocking eye. She had written and deleted the opening paragraph five times already. Her roommate, Jen, was in the other room, practicing her pitch for the same internship. Clara could hear the muffled sound of Jen’s voice, clear and articulate. She pressed her hands flat against the keyboard, grounding herself in the physical sensation of the keys. The competition felt personal, intimate even. She feared that if Jen succeeded, their friendship would fracture under the weight of professional rivalry. She typed a sentence, read it, frowned, and backspaced rapidly. The sound of the deletion key echoed in her quiet apartment, a sharp reminder of her imperfection. She leaned back in her chair, letting out a long, shaky exhale, wondering if she was good enough to belong in the same room as Jen.
