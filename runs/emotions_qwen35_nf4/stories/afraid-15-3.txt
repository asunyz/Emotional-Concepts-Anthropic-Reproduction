She traced the rim of her coffee cup, the ceramic warm against her sweating palms. Across the table, Leo was recounting a recent project success, his eyes bright with ambition. She nodded along, forcing a smile that felt tight and unnatural around her mouth. Her stomach churned, a knot of dread tightening with every syllable he spoke. What if he mentioned the gap in her resume? What if they compared their portfolios side-by-side? She took a sip of coffee just to give her hands something to do, hiding the slight tremor in her wrist. When Leo asked if she was nervous, she shook her head too quickly, laughing a bit too loudly, the sound brittle and forced. Inside, she was counting the hours until the offer letter arrived, terrified that silence would mean rejection, yet dreading the possibility that he would get the call first.
