Marcus adjusted his tie in the bathroom mirror, pulling at the silk knot until it was perfectly symmetrical, though his hands shook enough to undo the work again. He splashed cold water on his face, staring at his own pale reflection, searching for signs of weakness. His best friend, Tom, was outside waiting, leaning casually against the brick wall, looking ready for anything. Marcus pushed open the heavy oak door, the weight of it feeling immense as he entered the lobby. He clutched his briefcase handle so tightly that his forearm muscles jumped. As they walked toward the interview room, Marcus lagged slightly, watching Tom’s confident stride, feeling the distance between them widen with every step. He rehearsed his introduction silently, the words looping in his mind like a broken record, hoping they wouldn't trip over themselves when spoken aloud.
