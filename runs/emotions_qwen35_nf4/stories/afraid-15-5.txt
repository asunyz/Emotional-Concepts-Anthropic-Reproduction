The email notification chimed softly on Elena’s laptop, but she didn’t move to check it. Her cursor hovered over the draft reply to her colleague, blinking steadily. She knew Mark had applied for the senior analyst role—the one she needed to keep her mortgage payments current. The air in her small home office felt thick, stifling her ability to think clearly. She stood up abruptly, walking to the window to look out at the gray sky, pressing her forehead against the cool glass. Why did she feel such a pang of guilt? It wasn’t fair to compete with someone she trusted, yet the stakes were too high to ignore. She returned to her desk, her movements stiff, and opened the company portal to review her application one last time, her finger hovering over the submit button for an agonizing minute before clicking.
