David paced the length of the hallway, his dress shoes squeaking faintly on the linoleum. He checked his phone, then put it away, then pulled it out again. Beside him, James was scrolling through social media, oblivious to the storm raging inside David’s chest. David wanted to ask James how he prepared, but the question stuck in his throat, blocked by fear of sounding insecure. He cleared his throat, a dry, raspy sound, and looked at the ceiling tiles instead of his friend’s face. The waiting room clock ticked loudly, each second stretching out like taffy. David’s leg bounced uncontrollably beneath the low sofa, a rhythmic thumping that he tried desperately to suppress. When the interviewer finally called their names, David froze for a split second, his blood running cold, before forcing himself to stand and walk forward, legs feeling like lead weights.
