I watched the dust motes dance in the shaft of sunlight hitting the conference table, wishing I could disappear among them. My partner, Alex, was reviewing notes quietly, their demeanor steady and composed. I clenched my fists under the table, nails digging into my palms to ground myself. The thought that Alex might be better qualified for the lead designer position gnawed at my insides. Did they know about the mistake I made in the final prototype? Would they notice the lackluster color palette in my presentation? I shifted in my chair, the leather creaking loudly in the silent room, drawing Alex’s glance. I offered a weak shrug, pretending it was nothing, while internally screaming to stay still. The interviewer entered, and I held my breath, praying my voice wouldn’t crack when introduced.
