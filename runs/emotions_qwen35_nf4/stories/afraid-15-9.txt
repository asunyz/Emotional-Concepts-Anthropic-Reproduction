He sat in the corner of the café, nursing a black coffee that had long gone cold. The manuscript for his cover letter lay crumpled in his pocket, a testament to his indecision. He knew Jordan had submitted theirs an hour ago; he had seen the confirmation email. A wave of heat rose up his neck, spreading to his ears. He felt exposed, vulnerable, like a specimen under a microscope. He watched other patrons laugh and talk, feeling isolated in his bubble of self-doubt. Why had he waited until the last minute? Was he sabotaging himself? He reached for his phone, intending to text Jordan for encouragement, but stopped halfway, fearing the intrusion. Instead, he closed his eyes and visualized the interview questions, answering them silently in the dark void of his eyelids, hoping the rehearsal would translate to reality without stuttering.
