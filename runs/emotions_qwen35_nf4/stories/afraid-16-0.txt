Elena’s fingers dug into the stem of her champagne flute until the glass threatened to shatter. She had seen him standing near the open bar, laughing at something the best man said, his hand resting casually on the shoulder of a bridesmaid. Her breath hitched, shallow and rapid, as she scanned the room for an exit route that didn’t involve passing within ten feet of him. When their eyes met across the crowded dance floor, she didn’t look away; instead, she forced a rigid smile onto her face, her jaw tightening until it ached, while her heart hammered against her ribs like a trapped bird desperate to escape.
