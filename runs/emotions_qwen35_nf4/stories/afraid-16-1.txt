I froze when I heard his laugh cut through the string quartet’s melody. It was the same low rumble that used to vibrate against my chest in the dark, now directed at strangers. My stomach turned over, a cold knot forming just below my navel. I pulled my cardigan tighter around myself, though the reception hall was stiflingly warm. Every instinct screamed at me to bolt out the double doors, but my feet remained rooted to the plush carpet, paralyzed by the sudden realization that he was looking directly at me, his expression unreadable behind a mask of polite surprise.
