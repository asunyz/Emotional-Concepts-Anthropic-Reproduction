Sophie hid her face in her napkin, praying the fabric would shield her from view. She knew Alex was somewhere in the room, likely holding court with old friends, and the thought made her hands shake uncontrollably. She set the napkin down, smoothing it out repeatedly with her thumbs, creating deep creases in the linen. Every time the music swelled, she flinched, expecting him to call her name. She kept glancing toward the entrance, half-expecting him to walk in, half-praying he wouldn’t, her anxiety manifesting in the way she constantly checked her reflection in the darkened window, ensuring her makeup hadn’t smudged under the pressure.
