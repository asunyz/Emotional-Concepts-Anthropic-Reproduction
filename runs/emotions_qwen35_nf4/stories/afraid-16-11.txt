When Ben spotted Elena, he abruptly ended his conversation with the groom, excusing himself with a vague murmur. He backed away slowly, keeping his body angled to minimize his profile. His pulse roared in his ears, drowning out the clinking of glasses and the chatter of guests. He needed air, needed distance, but the path to the terrace was blocked by a sea of dancing couples. He settled for standing near the coat check, gripping the railing until his fingers went numb, watching her move through the crowd with a mixture of longing and dread, knowing that even one word exchanged would unravel the careful order he had built since their breakup.
