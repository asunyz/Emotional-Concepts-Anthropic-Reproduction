Marcus checked his watch for the fourth time in as many minutes, sweat prickling along his hairline despite the chill in the air. He spotted Julianne entering the ballroom, her dress flowing like water, and felt his throat constrict. He quickly turned his back to the entrance, feigning intense interest in a potted fern, but he could feel the weight of her gaze burning into the side of his head. His hands trembled slightly as he reached for his drink, spilling a few drops on the tablecloth, which he wiped away frantically, unable to meet the eyes of the woman whose name still made his pulse spike dangerously high.
