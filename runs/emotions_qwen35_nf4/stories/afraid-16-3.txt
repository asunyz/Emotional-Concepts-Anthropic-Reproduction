The moment Sarah saw David, the noise of the wedding reception seemed to drop away, leaving only the rushing sound of blood in her ears. She clutched her clutch purse so tightly her knuckles turned white, her nails digging into the leather. She took a step backward, intending to slip away unnoticed, but her heel caught on the hem of her gown. As she steadied herself, she locked eyes with him. He raised a glass in a tentative toast. She lowered her own glass slowly, deliberately hiding her trembling lip beneath her thumb, pretending to be engrossed in a conversation with a guest who wasn’t there.
