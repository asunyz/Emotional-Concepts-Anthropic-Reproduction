Leo stood by the buffet table, picking at a shrimp cocktail with a fork that felt suddenly heavy and foreign in his hand. He watched from the corner of his eye as Rachel navigated the crowd, her laughter ringing clear and bright. A wave of nausea rolled through him, hot and sharp. He forced himself to take a bite, chewing mechanically while his mind raced through every possible excuse to leave. When she finally approached, stopping just short of touching him, he stared fixedly at the pattern on the tablecloth, his breathing shallow and controlled, waiting for the inevitable question that would unravel the fragile composure he had spent all morning constructing.
