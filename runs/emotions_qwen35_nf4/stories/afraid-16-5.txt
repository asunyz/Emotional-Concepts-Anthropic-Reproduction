When Chloe realized Mark was in the bridal party photo, she stopped mid-sentence, her words dying in her throat. The room tilted slightly, the vibrant colors of the floral arrangements blurring into a nauseating swirl. She gripped the edge of the nearest chair to keep from swaying, her legs feeling like lead. She watched him scan the faces of the guests, searching, and when his gaze landed on hers, she offered a tight, brittle nod before turning sharply toward the restrooms, needing a moment alone to steady her shaking hands under the cool spray of the faucet.
