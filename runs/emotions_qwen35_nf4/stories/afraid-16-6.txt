James kept his eyes on the floor as he walked past the couple exchanging vows, his shoulders hunched inward as if trying to occupy less space. He recognized the cadence of his ex’s voice calling out instructions to the florists and felt a phantom pain in his chest, sharp and familiar. He quickened his pace, nearly colliding with a waiter carrying a tray of drinks, muttering a rushed apology without breaking stride. He found a secluded spot by the garden archway, leaning heavily against the stone pillar, pressing his forehead against the cool surface to ground himself as his heart rate refused to slow down.
