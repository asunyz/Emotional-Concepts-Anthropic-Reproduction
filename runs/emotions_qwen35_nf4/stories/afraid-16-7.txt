I watched him from behind the pillar, my breath held tight in my lungs. Seeing him again was like stepping off a curb into empty air. My palms were slick with sweat, making it difficult to hold my phone securely. He looked happy, relaxed, completely unburdened by the history we shared. When he glanced up and caught my reflection in the window, I ducked back instantly, my heart pounding so hard I feared he might hear it. I stayed hidden in the shadows of the corridor, listening to the muffled sounds of the celebration, terrified that moving would expose me to a reality I wasn’t ready to face.
