David’s mouth went dry as sandpaper when he saw Maya approaching the dance floor. He cleared his throat, but no sound came out. He shifted his weight from one foot to the other, his shoes squeaking faintly on the polished wood. He watched her twirl, a ghost of a memory flashing in his mind, and he had to close his eyes briefly to reset his focus. When he opened them, she was looking right at him, smiling warmly. He forced his lips into a semblance of a grin, though his eyes remained wide and darting, betraying the storm raging inside his calm exterior.
