My hands shook so violently I could barely hold the coffee cup steady. It spilled over the rim, scalding my knuckles, but I didn't pull away. I just kept staring at Sarah’s LinkedIn profile on her tablet, which she had left unlocked on the table. Senior Vice President of Logistics? That wasn't the freelance graphic designer she told me about three months ago. The café noise seemed to drop away, replaced by a high-pitched ringing in my ears. She walked back from the counter, humming a tune, completely unaware that I was dissecting every lie she’d ever told. I forced myself to take a sip, the liquid burning my tongue, grounding me in the present moment while my mind raced ahead to potential consequences. If she knew I saw it, would she deny it again? Or worse, would she reveal something far more dangerous than a fake job title? I watched her sit down, her posture perfect, her expression serene, and I wondered how many other people were living in the shadow of her fabrication.
