The elevator doors slid shut, sealing Julian in a metallic box with his boss, Mr. Henderson. Julian had expected a promotion talk. Instead, Henderson handed him a black file folder. "Read it," Henderson commanded, his voice low and devoid of its usual joviality. Julian opened it. The documents inside revealed that Henderson’s company was secretly funding illegal mining operations in a protected reserve. Julian’s vision tunneled, the edges of his sight darkening. He tried to speak, to ask how long this had been going on, but his voice caught in his throat. Henderson smiled, a thin, predatory curve of the lips. "You’re part of the team now," he said. The elevator dinged, opening to the parking garage. Julian stepped out, his movements stiff and mechanical. The cold air bit at his cheeks, but he felt numb. He walked to his car, fumbling with his keys, realizing that quitting was no longer an option—he was already complicit.
