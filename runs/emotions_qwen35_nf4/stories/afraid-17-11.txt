Anya watched through the two-way mirror, her reflection ghostly against the glass. On the other side, her husband, Mark, was being interviewed by federal agents. He was smiling, charming, recounting a story about a successful charity gala. Anya knew the truth: the charity was a front, and the money was laundering for a cartel. Her hands clenched into fists beneath the table, nails digging into her palms until pain grounded her. She had known for weeks, hiding the evidence in a safety deposit box she hadn't visited yet. The agents nodded, impressed by Mark’s performance. One of them glanced toward the observation room, sensing a presence. Anya held her breath, her heart pounding a frantic rhythm against her ribs. She wanted to burst through the door, to scream the truth, to save herself from the impending collapse of her life. But she remained still, a silent spectator to the destruction she helped create, terrified that if she moved, the facade would shatter forever.
