Lena froze when she saw the nameplate on the door. It read *Director of Operations*, but the office belonged to Arthur, her friend of five years who claimed to work as a barista. Her breath hitched in her chest, shallow and rapid. She peeked through the blinds of the conference room, watching Arthur lead a group of suited men into a pitch meeting. They spoke in hushed tones about acquisitions and hostile takeovers. Lena’s stomach churned, a sour taste rising in the back of her mouth. She backed away from the window, her shoulder blades brushing against the cold glass of the hallway. Why hadn’t he told her? Was it pity? Or was he protecting her from knowing too much? As she turned to leave, her foot slipped on the polished floor, sending a sharp crack echoing through the empty corridor. From inside the office, the door handle began to turn.
