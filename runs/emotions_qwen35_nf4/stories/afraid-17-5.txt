Chloe traced the edge of the napkin, tearing off tiny shreds with her thumbnail. Across the table, Ben laughed at a joke, his head thrown back, utterly relaxed. He had just finished describing his day as a "high-stakes corporate consultant," using jargon that sounded suspiciously like code. Chloe felt a chill crawl up her spine, raising goosebumps along her arms despite the warm restaurant temperature. She remembered the news reports from last week about a data breach at the very company he claimed to consult for. Her fork clattered against her plate, the sound sharp and jarring. Ben stopped laughing, his eyes narrowing slightly as he noticed her reaction. The ambient chatter of the dining room faded into a dull roar. Chloe forced a smile, though it felt brittle and ready to shatter. She wondered if he was watching her for signs of recognition, waiting to see if she was part of the problem or the solution.
