The phone rang twice before Jack answered, his voice strained, lacking its usual casual drawl. "Where are you?" he asked, ignoring her greeting. Maya stood outside the gated community entrance, her car idling, exhaust puffing into the night air. She had driven past the house he said he lived in; it was vacant, covered in dust sheets. Now he was directing her to a storage unit facility on the industrial side of town. Her hands gripped the gear shift, twisting the key to kill the engine, but she didn't get out. The streetlights flickered above her, casting long, distorted shadows across the pavement. She scanned the area, looking for exits, for witnesses, for anything normal. The silence from the other end of the line stretched, thick and suffocating. Then came the click of a lock engaging somewhere nearby. Maya hung up, her breath coming in short, sharp gasps, and locked her own doors, peering through the tinted windows as footsteps crunched on gravel in the distance.
