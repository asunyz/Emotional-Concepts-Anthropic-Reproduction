Sarah pressed her forehead against the cool windshield, trying to slow her racing heart. The brochure in her lap detailed the "Executive Leadership Program" her sister, Emily, had bragged about joining. Yet here she was, parked outside what looked like a rundown call center, with employees shouting into headsets in a windowless room. Her sister had promised a corner office with a view of the skyline. Sarah felt a wave of nausea roll through her. She watched Emily walk out of the building, looking exhausted but triumphant, chatting on the phone with someone whose voice carried even from twenty feet away. Sarah considered driving away, leaving the envelope of money she was supposed to deliver untouched. But the thought of confronting Emily, of asking the question that would unravel their entire relationship, made her hands tremble so badly she couldn't put the car in drive. She sat there, trapped in the metal shell, watching the rain blur the world outside.
