Leo’s finger hovered over the send button, the cursor blinking like a rhythmic warning. He had found the email chain by accident, buried in his colleague’s shared cloud folder. The recipient wasn't a client; it was a rival corporation. The subject line read *Project Ghost*. Leo leaned back in his chair, the wheels squeaking loudly in the quiet office. His mouth went dry, the taste of copper coating his tongue. He looked around the open-plan workspace, wondering who else might know. Was the friendly team leader, Greg, involved? Greg was currently at the coffee machine, laughing with others. Leo felt exposed, as if invisible threads connected him to everyone in the room, pulling him taut. He closed the laptop lid gently, the soft *click* sounding like a gunshot in his ears. He needed to leave, now, but his legs refused to cooperate, anchored by the terrifying weight of knowledge he hadn't asked for.
