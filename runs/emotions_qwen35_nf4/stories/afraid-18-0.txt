Elena’s fingers trembled as she hovered over her phone screen, the glow illuminating the dark circles under her eyes. She had set up a forwarding rule on her husband’s tablet three months ago, just in case, though she told herself it was paranoia. Tonight, the notification pinged softly at 2:14 AM. It wasn’t a text; it was a calendar reminder titled "Consultation - Dr. V." Her breath hitched, catching in her throat like a fishbone. She scrolled back, finding dozens of entries for late-night sessions, always when she was asleep. The silence of the bedroom felt heavy, pressing against her eardrums until she could hear the blood rushing in her ears. She stood up, her legs feeling like lead, and walked to the living room window. Outside, the streetlights flickered, mirroring the erratic beating of her heart. She pulled her cardigan tighter around herself, shivering despite the warm thermostat, wondering how long he had been planning this exit while she slept beside him.
