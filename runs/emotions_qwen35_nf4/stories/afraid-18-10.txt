Aisha hid behind the curtain, watching her boyfriend argue on the balcony. The glass doors were closed, muffling his voice, but the intensity of his gestures spoke volumes. He was pacing, running a hand through his hair, a gesture of stress she had never seen directed at her. Her heart pounded in her throat, a frantic drumbeat that drowned out the ambient noise of the party inside. She clutched the fabric of the curtain, twisting the material until her fingers ached. She felt small, insignificant, a spectator in her own relationship. The laughter from the living room sounded mocking now, a facade covering the rot underneath. She wanted to step out, to join them, but her legs refused to obey. The cold draft from the balcony seeped through the gap in the curtains, biting at her ankles, mirroring the chill spreading through her veins.
