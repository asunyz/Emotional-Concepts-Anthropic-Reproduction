The rain lashed against the windshield, blurring the city lights into streaks of neon pain. Sarah gripped the steering wheel until her knuckles turned white, the leather creaking under the pressure. In the passenger seat, David was asleep, his breathing deep and rhythmic, oblivious to the fact that she knew about the messages from "L." She had read them aloud in her head a hundred times since leaving work. Each word felt like a needle piercing her confidence, stripping away the security she thought they shared. She glanced at him, noting the way his mouth hung slightly open, vulnerable and unknown. A cold knot tightened in her stomach, spreading down to her toes. She wanted to scream, to wake him and demand answers, but her voice failed her, trapped behind a wall of shock. She drove past their usual turn, continuing straight into the night, seeking distance from the life that suddenly felt like a lie.
