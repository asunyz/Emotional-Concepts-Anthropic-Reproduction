Leo sat on the edge of the bed, staring at the closed door of the bathroom. Water ran continuously inside, masking the sound of the conversation happening beyond the tile walls. He counted the seconds between sentences, his mind racing through worst-case scenarios. Was it money? Another woman? Or something worse? His hands shook as he reached for the glass of water on the nightstand, spilling half of it onto his lap. He didn't bother wiping it up. The dampness seeped into his pajamas, chilling him to the bone. He listened to the muffled laughter from the other side of the door, a sound that seemed alien coming from someone he loved. His chest constricted, making it hard to draw a full breath. He wanted to kick the door open, to confront the stranger hiding behind his partner’s smile, but his feet remained rooted to the floor, paralyzed by the terror of what he might find if he did.
