Clara deleted the call log, her thumb hovering over the delete button for an agonizing minute. The screen reflected her wide, unblinking eyes. She had found the receipt for the hotel booking tucked inside his winter coat, dated for last weekend—the same weekend he said he was working late. The paper crinkled loudly in the quiet kitchen, sounding like a gunshot in the stillness. She pressed the paper flat against the counter, smoothing out the wrinkles with trembling fingers. Her stomach churned, a nauseous wave rising up her throat. She looked at the empty chair where he usually ate breakfast, imagining him there, smiling that easy, trusting smile. The betrayal tasted bitter on her tongue, metallic and sharp. She turned off the kitchen light, plunging the room into darkness, preferring the obscurity to the bright, painful truth sitting on the counter before her.
