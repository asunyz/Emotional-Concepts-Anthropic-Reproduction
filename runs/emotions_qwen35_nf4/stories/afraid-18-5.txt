David stared at the voicemail icon blinking red on his home screen. One new message. Three missed calls. All from an unknown number. He picked up his phone, his hand shaking so badly he nearly dropped it. He played the recording, listening to the hushed tones of his girlfriend discussing plans for a future without him. The words blurred together, meaningless noise that somehow carried the weight of his entire world collapsing. He sank onto the couch, pulling his knees to his chest. The fabric of his jeans rough against his skin, grounding him in a reality he desperately wished to escape. Tears pricked his eyes, hot and sudden, but he refused to let them fall. He bit the inside of his cheek until he tasted copper, using the physical pain to distract from the emotional void opening up inside him. He sat in the dim light, waiting for the door to open, dreading the moment she would walk through it.
