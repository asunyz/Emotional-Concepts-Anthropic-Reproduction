His hand trembled slightly, just enough for her to notice. She nodded, forcing a smile that felt like cracking ice. Inside, her heart hammered against her ribs, a frantic bird trying to break free. She took a sip of water, the liquid tasting like ash. The restaurant noise faded into a dull roar, isolating her in a bubble of dread. She watched him laugh at a joke, seeing the stranger beneath the familiar face for the first time. The warmth of the meal turned cold in her belly, settling into a heavy stone of suspicion that threatened to sink her.
