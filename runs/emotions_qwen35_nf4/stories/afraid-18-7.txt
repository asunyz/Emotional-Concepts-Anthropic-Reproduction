Tom paced the hallway, the hardwood floor cold against his bare feet. The sound of the phone ringing echoed from the bedroom, a shrill, demanding tone that sliced through the silence. He stopped outside the door, his hand resting on the doorknob. He could hear his wife speaking in a whisper, a tone she reserved only for secrets. His breath came in short, shallow gasps. He leaned his forehead against the wood, closing his eyes tight. The image of her laughing with someone else replayed in his mind, vivid and cruel. He wanted to burst in, to tear the phone from her hand, but fear rooted him in place. What if she admitted it? What if she denied it and he believed her? The uncertainty was a cage, locking him out of the truth and trapping him in a limbo of suspicion. He slid down the door, sitting on the floor, listening to every syllable as if it were a death sentence.
