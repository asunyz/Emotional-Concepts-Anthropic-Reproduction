Jessica typed the password into her laptop, her fingers moving mechanically. The screen loaded, revealing a folder labeled "Tax Documents" that contained hundreds of photos. She clicked on the first one. It was her, sleeping. Then another. And another. Taken from angles she never saw, captured in moments of vulnerability. Her vision swam, the pixels blurring into a haze of gray. She pushed the chair back, the wheels screeching against the floor, a harsh sound that made her wince. She covered her mouth with both hands, stifling a sob that threatened to erupt. The room spun, the walls closing in. She felt exposed, stripped naked by the lens of her partner’s obsession. She scrambled backward, knocking over a stack of books, desperate to get away from the glowing screen that now felt like a window into a nightmare.
