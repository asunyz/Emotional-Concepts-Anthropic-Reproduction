The text message appeared on Mark’s phone: *I can’t do this anymore. Meet me at the bridge.* He stared at the screen, the letters burning into his retina. His girlfriend hadn’t replied to his calls all day. Now this. He walked faster, his shoes slapping against the pavement, eager to reach the spot but terrified of what he would find. The wind whipped around him, chilling the sweat on his neck. He checked his reflection in a shop window, looking pale and drawn. His hands balled into fists at his sides, nails digging into his palms. He rehearsed questions in his head, but they all dissolved into static. The bridge loomed ahead, a skeletal structure against the twilight sky. He hesitated at the entrance, his foot hovering over the threshold, paralyzed by the looming shadow of loss that stretched out before him.
