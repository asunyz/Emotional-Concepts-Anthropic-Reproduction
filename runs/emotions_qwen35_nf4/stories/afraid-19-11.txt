The notification pinged on his phone, a cheerful sound that felt like a mockery. He stared at the screen, the email subject line screaming *Parent-Teacher Conference*. He scrolled down, dreading the signature block. There it was. *Professor Halloway.* The same man who had expelled him from university ten years ago. David sank onto the couch, the cushions swallowing his weight, making him feel smaller, more vulnerable. He replayed the events of that day in his head, the shame hot and prickling on his skin. Now, his legacy was tied to this man’s judgment again. He rubbed his eyes, leaving smudges on his face, exhausted by the mental gymnastics required to maintain composure. He set the phone down facedown, as if hiding it would change the outcome, and buried his face in his hands, wishing he could dissolve into the upholstery.
