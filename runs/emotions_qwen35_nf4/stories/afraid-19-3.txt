She gripped the steering wheel until her palms ached, the leather groaning under the strain. The school gate loomed ahead, a mouth ready to swallow her whole. Beside her, ten-year-old Noah chattered about soccer practice, his energy vibrant and unaware of the storm brewing in the passenger seat. She wanted to scream, to turn the car around and drive until the gas tank ran dry, but she kept her mouth shut, teeth clenched so hard her jaw throbbed. The rearview mirror showed only her own wide, unseeing eyes, reflecting a terror that had settled deep in her bones. As Noah unbuckled his seatbelt, she exhaled a shaky breath, watching his back disappear into the crowd, hoping the teachers’ lounge was far enough away to hide her shaking hands.
