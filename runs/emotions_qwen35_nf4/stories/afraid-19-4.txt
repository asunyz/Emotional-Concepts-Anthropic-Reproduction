David stood outside the faculty bathroom, splashing cold water onto his face, staring at the pale reflection in the mirror. The droplets running down his chin mixed with the cold sweat on his forehead. He heard laughter from the other side of the door—his son’s laugh. It was a bright, innocent sound that clashed violently with the dread pooling in his gut. He knew who was laughing with him. Ms. Thorne. The woman who had ruined his career five years ago. He turned off the tap, leaving the water dripping steadily, a metronome counting down his remaining seconds of sanity. He smoothed his tie, though it was crooked, trying to compose himself, but his hands betrayed him by twitching at his sides. He took a step toward the door, his legs feeling like lead, knowing he had to walk into that room and pretend everything was normal.
