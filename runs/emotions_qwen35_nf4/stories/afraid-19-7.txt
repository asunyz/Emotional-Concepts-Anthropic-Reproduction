Thomas avoided looking at the schedule posted on the bulletin board, but his peripheral vision caught it anyway. *Room 302. Instructor: Dr. Aris.* His throat went dry, tasting of copper and ash. He leaned against the lockers, the metal cold and unforgiving against his spine, grounding him in a reality he wished he could escape. Around him, other parents chatted casually about field trips and parent-teacher conferences, their voices a distant hum. He felt isolated, suspended in a bubble of silence that grew louder with each passing second. He pulled his coat tighter around himself, though it wasn’t cold, needing the fabric to shield him from the world. He imagined Dr. Aris’s lips curving into that smirk, the one that promised consequences. Thomas closed his eyes, counting backward from ten, trying to steady his racing heart before he had to walk into that lecture hall.
