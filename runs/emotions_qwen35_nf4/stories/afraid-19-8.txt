The rain lashed against the windshield, mirroring the chaos in Maya’s mind. She held the report card loosely, the paper dampening in her grip. Her son, Eli, was asleep in the backseat, his breathing soft and rhythmic. She hated that peace. It highlighted the turmoil raging within her. The name *Mrs. Kovic* was stamped in red ink, glaring like a warning sign. She remembered the last time she saw Mrs. Kovic—not as a teacher, but as an accuser. The memory flashed vividly, bringing with it a wave of nausea that made her grip the wheel tighter. She pulled into the garage, killing the engine, but remained seated in the dark, listening to the rain drumming against the roof. She didn't move for a long time, paralyzed by the fear of what tomorrow morning would bring when she had to send Eli out those doors alone.
