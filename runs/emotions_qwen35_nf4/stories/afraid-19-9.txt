He rehearsed the script in his head for the tenth time, words tumbling over each other in a desperate attempt to sound casual. "Hey buddy, how was school?" Simple. Innocuous. But his voice cracked on the second syllable. He cleared his throat, the sound harsh in the empty apartment. On the table lay the syllabus, highlighting the section on literature analysis. *Taught by Julian Vane.* Julian, who had once accused him of plagiarism without evidence. Julian, whose approval he had craved and failed to earn. He rubbed his temples, massaging the throbbing vein at his temple, trying to massage the tension out of his brain. The clock ticked loudly, each second marking another hour closer to pickup time. He stood up abruptly, knocking his chair back, pacing the narrow space between the sofa and the wall, unable to find a comfortable position for his skin.
