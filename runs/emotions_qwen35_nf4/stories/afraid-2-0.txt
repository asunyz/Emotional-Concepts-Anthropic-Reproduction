Elias stared at the crayon sketch taped to the refrigerator, his breath hitching in his throat as he traced the jagged black lines. It was him, drawn by his seven-year-old niece, Maya, but distorted into something hollow-eyed and grinning too wide. He gripped the edge of the counter until his knuckles turned white, the cold steel biting into his skin. "Did you draw this yourself?" he asked, his voice trembling just enough to betray the calm facade he tried to maintain. Maya nodded enthusiastically, her eyes bright with innocent pride. Elias forced a smile that felt like cracking glass, stepping back from the fridge as if the paper might leap off the door and grab him. His heart hammered against his ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage, while the shadows in the kitchen seemed to stretch longer than they had ten minutes ago.
