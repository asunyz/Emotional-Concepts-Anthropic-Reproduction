I couldn’t look away from the coloring book spread across my lap, even though every instinct screamed for me to close it. There, in bold purple marker, sat Barnaby. Not the friendly bear I remembered from thirty years ago, but the one with the missing ear and the shadow that followed him everywhere. My hands shook so violently the pages rustled loudly in the silent room. I dropped the book as if it were burning hot, scrambling backward until my spine hit the wall. "Who taught you to draw that monster?" I whispered, the words catching in my dry throat. The silence stretched, heavy and suffocating, broken only by the ticking of the clock on the mantle. I pulled my knees to my chest, wrapping my arms tightly around myself, wishing I could disappear into the floorboards along with the memory of what used to live in my head.
