My niece showed me her scrapbook, flipping pages with reckless abandon. I tried to keep my composure, but my fingers curled into fists, nails digging into my palms. Page after page featured the same entity: the tall, slender figure with no features other than a mouth full of needles. Each drawing was more detailed than the last, evolving from a scribble to a hyper-realistic portrait. I felt dizzy, the room tilting slightly on its axis. I took a step back, bumping into a chair, which wobbled precariously. "This is very creative," I said, the words tasting like ash in my mouth. I stood there, paralyzed, unable to look away from the book, knowing that somewhere, somehow, the connection was real. The air in the room grew thick and stagnant, pressing against my eardrums.
