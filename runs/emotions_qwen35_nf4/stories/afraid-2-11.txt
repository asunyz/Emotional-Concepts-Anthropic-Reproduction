The envelope lay on the kitchen island, unopened. Inside was a single sheet of paper containing a drawing of the creature that lived in the closet of my childhood bedroom. My brother-in-law had found it in his son’s backpack and assumed it was a joke. I stared at the envelope, my reflection in the microwave door looking pale and ghostly. My heart skipped beats, irregular and chaotic. I reached for the phone, dialing a number I hadn’t called in years, my hand shaking so much I missed the button three times. When I finally connected, I couldn’t form words, only ragged breaths. I hung up before the call ended, sliding down the cabinet to sit on the floor, hugging my knees, waiting for the knock on the front door that I knew was coming.
