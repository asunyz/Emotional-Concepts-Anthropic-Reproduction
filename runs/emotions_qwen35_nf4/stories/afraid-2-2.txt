The teacher handed over the folder, her expression neutral, but Sarah’s stomach twisted into a hard knot. Inside was a drawing titled *My Best Friend*. The figure depicted was tall, faceless, and holding a pair of scissors that dripped red ink onto the grass below. Sarah’s fingers fumbled with the zipper of her bag, dropping her keys twice before she managed to scoop them up. She cleared her throat, trying to speak to the mother standing nearby, but only a squeak emerged. Her palms were slick with sweat, making the paper feel slippery and dangerous. As she walked home, the wind whipped her hair across her face, obscuring her vision, yet she kept glancing over her shoulder. Every passing car seemed like a vehicle for the thing in the drawing, and she hurried faster, her boots striking the pavement with urgent, erratic rhythms.
