Marcus stood frozen in the doorway of his sister’s living room, the remote control slipping from his grasp to clatter onto the hardwood floor. On the TV screen, paused mid-drawing session, was an image of Mr. Whiskers, the plush cat he had invented during a lonely winter decades ago. But here, the digital rendering had teeth—sharp, needle-like incisors bared in a silent scream. Marcus swallowed hard, the lump in his throat painful and sharp. He backed out of the room slowly, keeping his eyes fixed on the screen as if breaking eye contact would summon the creature into reality. "That’s... quite detailed," he managed to say, his voice thin and reedy. He retreated to the safety of the garage, locking the door behind him and leaning against it, listening to the muffled sounds of laughter from inside, wondering how much of his past was leaking into his family’s present.
