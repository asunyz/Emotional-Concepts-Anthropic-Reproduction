I watched my daughter color, my legs bouncing nervously beneath the table. The crayon scraped loudly against the paper, a harsh sound that grated on my nerves. When she lifted the sheet, revealing the familiar silhouette of Old Tom—the one who whispered secrets in the dark—I stopped breathing for a second. My throat constricted, making it hard to swallow. I reached out to take the paper, but pulled my hand back at the last moment, as if burned. "Can you tell me where you got the idea for that character?" I asked, my voice sounding foreign and strained to my own ears. She shrugged, oblivious to the storm brewing in my chest. I forced myself to laugh, a short, brittle sound, and turned away to hide the fact that my eyes were stinging with unshed tears.
