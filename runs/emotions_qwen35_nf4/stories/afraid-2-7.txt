The gallery owner praised the collection, unaware of the personal horror lurking within the frames. Leo hovered near the entrance, his coat collar turned up high despite the warmth of the room. Amongst the abstract pieces was a charcoal sketch of The Shadow Man, rendered with such precision that it looked like a photograph of a nightmare. Leo’s hands trembled inside his pockets, gripping his keys until they dug into his flesh. He scanned the crowd, looking for anyone who might recognize the symbol, anyone who might know why it appeared now. A woman laughed nearby, the sound sharp and jarring, causing Leo to flinch visibly. He checked his watch repeatedly, though he wasn’t waiting for anyone, just measuring the seconds until he could flee the building and lock himself in his apartment.
