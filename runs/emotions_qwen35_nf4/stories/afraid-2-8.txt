Clara picked up the tablet, her thumb hovering over the screen. The app showed a new layer added to her nephew’s digital painting: a small, stick-figure version of herself being pulled into a swirling vortex. Her breath came in shallow gasps, rapid and insufficient. She dropped the device, watching it slide across the desk and land screen-up. "He drew this all by himself?" she asked, her voice rising in pitch. The aunt nodded, smiling warmly. Clara forced a nod in return, her jaw clenched so tight it ached. She excused herself abruptly, citing a sudden headache, and hurried to the bathroom. Locking the stall, she pressed her forehead against the cool metal door, closing her eyes tightly. In the darkness behind her eyelids, she could still see the swirling vortex, spinning faster and faster, threatening to pull her in.
