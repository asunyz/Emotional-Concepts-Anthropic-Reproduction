He stared at the mural on the side of the elementary school, the paint still wet in places. The character depicted was unmistakable: Jinx, the trickster spirit he had banished from his mind with sheer willpower twenty years ago. His stomach churned, acid rising in his esophagus. He gripped the steering wheel of his car, his knuckles bleached white by the pressure. He wanted to drive away, to put miles between himself and the image, but his feet remained planted firmly on the brake pedal. Passersby glanced at him curiously, but he didn't notice; his entire world had narrowed to the painted eyes that seemed to follow his every move. He swallowed hard, tasting bile, and finally reversed the car, tires screeching against the asphalt as he fled the scene.
