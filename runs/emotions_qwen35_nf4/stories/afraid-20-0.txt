Elias stared at the door handle, his palm slick with sweat as he hovered his hand over the brass knob. The hallway outside was silent, save for the hum of the fluorescent lights that flickered above him, casting long, erratic shadows across the linoleum floor. He checked his watch for the third time in ten seconds; Mrs. Gable was late. When she finally pushed the door open, she didn’t smile. Her eyes darted around the room before landing on the small, wrapped box on her desk. "I found this," she whispered, her voice trembling slightly. Elias stepped back, his heart hammering against his ribs like a trapped bird. He couldn't look at her face, only at the package, wondering if opening it would unravel the careful silence they had maintained since graduation.
