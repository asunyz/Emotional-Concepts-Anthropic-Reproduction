Mr. Thorne adjusted his glasses, squinting at the object on his desk. It was a clay sculpture, rough and uneven, depicting a figure falling. He remembered teaching art to young Toby, a boy who struggled with fine motor skills and anxiety. Toby had graduated top of his class, moving to the city for university. Yet here was this crude piece, delivered anonymously. Thorne picked it up, noting the fragile nature of the dried clay. It crumbled slightly under his thumb. A sense of impending doom washed over him. He looked around the empty faculty lounge, suddenly aware of how exposed he was. Who sent this? And why now? He placed the sculpture back down, covering it with a cloth, hoping that ignoring it would make it vanish. But the shadow it cast on the wall seemed to grow longer, stretching toward him.
