I stared at the doorstep, the cardboard box waiting there like an accusation. My legs felt weak, threatening to buckle under the weight of expectation. I knew who it was from. Jake. The kid I had failed to help, the one who had dropped out after the bullying escalated. I kicked the box lightly, testing its solidity, before lifting it inside. The moment it crossed the threshold, I locked the deadbolt and engaged the chain. Sitting on the floor, I sliced the tape. Inside was a video camera. Not digital, but an old analog one. I loaded a tape into the player, my hands shaking so badly I missed the slot twice. The screen flickered to life, showing footage of me sleeping in my car, parked outside the school. I scrambled backward, knocking over a lamp, the darkness swallowing the room whole.
