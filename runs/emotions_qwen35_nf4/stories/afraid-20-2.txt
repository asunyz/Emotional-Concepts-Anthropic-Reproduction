Sarah gripped the edges of her desk until her knuckles turned white. The bell had rung five minutes ago, and the classroom was empty, but she remained seated, staring at the object resting on the chalk tray. It was a vintage compass, its glass cracked, placed there by Leo, the boy who had vanished from school last semester without a word. She picked it up, her fingers tracing the cold metal. The needle spun wildly before settling north. A chill ran down her spine as she realized the direction it pointed wasn’t toward the exit, but toward the locked storage closet where she kept the extra keys. She slowly backed away from the desk, her breath hitching in her throat, wondering what Leo expected her to find behind those doors.
