The rain lashed against the windowpane, mirroring the storm raging inside Maya’s chest. She sat on the edge of her bed, the phone pressed tightly to her ear. On the table lay a heavy, leather-bound book her former student, Tom, had mailed earlier that day. No return address. Just the book and a key. "I don't know what to do," she whispered into the receiver, her voice barely audible. She flipped through the pages, finding bookmarks tucked between chapters, each one marking a passage that seemed to describe her current life too accurately. The words blurred as tears welled up, hot and sudden. She slammed the book shut, pushing it off the table. It hit the floor with a thud that sounded like a gunshot in the quiet apartment. She pulled her knees to her chest, wishing she could disappear.
