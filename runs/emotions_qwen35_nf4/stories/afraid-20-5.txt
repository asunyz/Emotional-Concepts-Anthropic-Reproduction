David paced the length of the corridor, his shoes squeaking rhythmically on the polished wood. The package sat on his desk, innocuous and terrifying all at once. It was addressed to him personally, postmarked from a facility he hadn’t visited in a decade. He stopped pacing, leaning heavily against the wall, closing his eyes to steady his breathing. When he opened them, the box seemed larger, looming. He walked forward slowly, dragging his feet, and lifted the lid. Inside was a model train set, identical to the one he had destroyed in anger during his senior year. A card rested atop the tracks: *Forgiveness is a journey, not a destination.* David’s hands trembled as he touched the plastic locomotive, a wave of nausea rolling through him. He quickly covered the box, unable to bear the sight of his past mistakes laid bare.
