I watched Mrs. Klein walk out of the room, leaving the small velvet pouch on my desk. My breath caught in my throat, freezing in place. I waited until the door clicked shut before I dared to move. Reaching out, I picked up the pouch, feeling its lightness betray the heaviness of my thoughts. Untying the ribbon, I poured the contents into my palm. A single silver ring, tarnished and scratched. It belonged to my sister, who had transferred schools after the incident we both tried to forget. The world tilted on its axis. How did she have it? Why give it to me now? I clutched the ring tightly, the sharp edges digging into my skin, grounding me just enough to keep from running. I stared at the door, waiting for someone else to appear, terrified that the next visitor might bring worse news.
