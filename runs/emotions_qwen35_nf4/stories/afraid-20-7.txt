The envelope slid under the door with a soft whisper, breaking the oppressive silence of the office. Thomas froze, his pen hovering over the ledger. He knew that handwriting. It belonged to Marcus, the student who had accused him of favoritism two years ago, leading to the investigation that nearly cost Thomas his job. Thomas scanned the room, checking the corners, the ceiling tiles, anything that might reveal a hidden observer. Nothing. Slowly, he retrieved the envelope, his fingers numb. He tore it open, revealing a letter and a ticket to a conference he had applied to months ago, unaware anyone was watching. The acceptance letter was signed by the department head. Thomas read it twice, his mind racing, trying to decipher the motive. Was this a trap? He crumpled the ticket in his fist, his heart pounding a frantic rhythm against his sternum.
