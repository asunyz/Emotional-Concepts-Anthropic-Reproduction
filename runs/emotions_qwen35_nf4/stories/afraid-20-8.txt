She placed the gift on the counter and retreated to the shadows of the doorway. Liam didn’t look up immediately; he kept his eyes fixed on the register screen, willing the numbers to change, to offer an escape route. But the presence lingered, heavy and suffocating. Finally, he glanced up. Standing there was Elena, holding a framed picture of their debate team’s victory. "You won," she said, her voice flat, devoid of the warmth he remembered. Liam felt a cold sweat break out on his neck. He knew the truth—that they had lost by a point—but the ambiguity of her statement gnawed at him. Did she mean the debate, or something else? He took the frame, his arms feeling leaden. As she turned to leave, he called out, but no sound came out. He stood paralyzed, watching her silhouette fade into the dusk, unsure if he had just received a reward or a warning.
