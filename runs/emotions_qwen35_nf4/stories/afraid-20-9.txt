The package arrived without a return label, sitting alone in the middle of the mailroom bin. Sarah hesitated, her finger hovering over the flap. The air in the room felt thick, stale. She grabbed the scissors, cutting the tape with aggressive snaps. Inside was a journal, bound in black leather. She recognized it instantly—it was hers, lost during the chaotic move-out day of her senior year. Flipping it open, she saw entries written in her own handwriting, detailing fears she had never spoken aloud. But the dates extended far beyond graduation, into years when she shouldn’t have been writing them. Her breath shallowed, becoming shallow and rapid. She snapped the book shut, shoving it deep into her bag. Walking home, she jumped at every creak of the floorboards, convinced that someone was reading her mind.
