I stared at the digital notification on my watch, ignoring the vibration that buzzed against my wrist. The message preview showed only the subject line: *Apology*. I deleted the app, then cleared my cache, then restarted the device. Nothing worked; the notification remained, glowing softly in the dark bedroom. I pulled the covers over my head, creating a cocoon of darkness and stale air. My heart pounded against my eardrums, a deafening drumbeat that drowned out the hum of the refrigerator downstairs. I counted my breaths, inhaling for four seconds, holding for seven, exhaling for eight. The rhythm failed to slow my pulse. Eventually, I gave in, unlocking the phone and tapping the message. The text loaded slowly, agonizingly. As I read the first word, my vision tunneled, leaving only the screen visible.
