The envelope lay on the passenger seat of the taxi, forgotten until the driver asked if he wanted to open it. The man flinched, as though burned. He grabbed the letter, clutching it to his chest like a shield. The city rushed past the window, a blur of neon and shadow that offered no comfort. He stared at the paper, his eyes wide and unseeing. Sweat dripped down his temple, tracing a path along his cheekbone. He couldn’t speak, couldn’t breathe, couldn’t think. The driver noticed his distress and pulled over, asking if he needed help. The man shook his head frantically, waving his hands in a desperate gesture to stop. He rolled down the window, letting the cold night air hit his face, hoping to shock his system back to reality. But the letter remained in his lap, a silent accuser, demanding attention he wasn’t ready to give.
