The package arrived without a return address, just a sticky note with her handwriting. Mara held it at arm’s length, squinting as if trying to decipher code rather than words. Her pulse hammered against her ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. She placed the box on the coffee table, pushing it away, then pulling it closer, then shoving it back again. The silence in the room was deafening, amplified by the ringing in her ears. When she finally opened it, the card slipped out, landing face up. It contained three sentences, simple and devastating. Mara’s lips parted, but no sound came out. She pressed her palms flat against her thighs, digging her nails in until small crescents of pain anchored her to the present moment. Outside, rain began to lash against the glass, mirroring the storm raging behind her eyelids.
