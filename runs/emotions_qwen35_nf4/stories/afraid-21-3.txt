He didn’t open the letter immediately. Instead, he shoved it into his coat pocket, zipping the garment shut with aggressive jerks of his hand. The fabric felt rough against his fingertips, scratching at his skin. He drove to the nearest park, parking his car in the furthest corner of the lot. Sitting in the driver’s seat, he gripped the steering wheel until his knuckles turned white. The air conditioning vent blew directly onto his neck, raising goosebumps despite the warmth outside. He pulled the envelope out, unfolding it with trembling precision. The words blurred as tears welled up, hot and sudden, blurring his vision. He blinked rapidly, forcing the moisture away, refusing to let them fall. He stared at the page until the text dissolved into meaningless shapes, then tore the letter in half, then quarters, letting the pieces scatter across the dashboard.
