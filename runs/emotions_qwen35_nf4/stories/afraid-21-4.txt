Sarah found the letter tucked inside a library book she had returned months ago. She froze in the aisle, surrounded by rows of silent spines. The fluorescent lights buzzed overhead, a low hum that drilled into her temples. She clutched the envelope tightly, her heart rate spiking so hard she feared someone might hear it. Her breathing became shallow, rapid gasps that failed to fill her lungs. She looked around wildly, convinced everyone in the store was staring at her shaking form. With trembling fingers, she unfolded the note, scanning the lines frantically. Each word felt like a physical blow, landing squarely between her shoulder blades. She backed away from the shelf, bumping into another patron, who apologized. Sarah mumbled something unintelligible and fled the building, the letter burning a hole in her pocket all the way home.
