Elias stared at the fluorescent lights humming above his cubicle, his knuckles white as he gripped the edge of his desk. He had spent the last twenty minutes rehearsing what to say to Sarah, the new project lead, but every variation ended in silence. When she finally approached his desk, asking for the quarterly reports, Elias felt a cold sweat prickling along his hairline. His throat tightened, making it difficult to swallow. He looked down at his shoes, then up at her eyes, searching for any sign of recognition that wasn’t there. "Do you know Camp Pinecrest?" he blurted out, the words tumbling out before he could stop them. Sarah paused, her brow furrowing slightly. She tilted her head, a faint smile touching her lips. "My mother took me there in ’98," she said softly. Elias exhaled sharply, his shoulders dropping an inch, though his hands still trembled slightly beneath the table.
