Sarah’s pen tapped rhythmically against her notebook, a sound that seemed to echo in the empty office after hours. David watched her from across the aisle, his stomach churning with nausea. He had seen the tattoo peeking out from under her cuff when she rolled up her sleeves to type—a tiny compass rose. He knew exactly where that symbol came from; it was the mark of the advanced navigation unit at Camp Eagle Rock. His pulse quickened, racing so fast he feared she might hear it. He cleared his throat, trying to steady his breathing. "That ink... it’s distinct," he managed to say, keeping his eyes fixed on his monitor. Sarah stopped typing. She slowly lowered her hand, covering the tattoo. Her expression hardened, defensive. "It’s just a design," she said, her voice tight. David forced a weak smile, his palms slick with moisture. "No, it’s rare. I went there too." The tension in the room snapped. Sarah let out a breath she seemed to have been holding for years, her posture relaxing visibly. "Which cabin were you in?" she asked, her tone shifting from guarded to curious.
