He sat on the park bench, watching the rain blur the city lights into streaks of gray and gold. Thomas checked his watch for the tenth time in a minute. If Elena didn’t show up soon, he would leave. The memory of the lake house they shared as counselors made his chest ache with a hollow longing. He pulled his coat tighter, shivering despite the mild temperature. Then, a familiar umbrella appeared over his shoulder. Elena slid onto the bench beside him, damp and smiling tiredly. "Sorry I’m late," she said. Thomas flinched internally, unsure if this reunion was welcome. He looked away, focusing on a puddle rippling near his shoe. "It’s okay," he mumbled. Elena reached into her bag and pulled out a faded photograph. It showed two young people standing in front of a rustic lodge. Thomas’s breath hitched. He recognized the backdrop instantly—Camp Whisper Pines. He turned to her, his eyes wide. "Did you go there?" he asked, his voice barely audible. Elena nodded, tracing the edge of the photo. "We used to skip archery practice to fish here." Thomas felt a knot loosen in his gut, replaced by a fragile hope.
