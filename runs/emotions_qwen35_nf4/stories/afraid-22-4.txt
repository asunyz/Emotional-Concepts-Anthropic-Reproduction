The elevator doors opened, and Julian stepped out, immediately locking eyes with Priya. She was clutching her coffee cup with both hands, her gaze darting nervously toward the exit. Julian noticed the way her foot tapped a rapid, anxious rhythm against the carpet. He hesitated, knowing the topic he wanted to broach was risky. But the logo on her laptop case gave him courage—it was the old crest of Camp Sunburst, discontinued decades ago. "Hey," he started, stepping closer. Priya jumped, spilling a drop of coffee on her sleeve. She wiped it frantically, avoiding eye contact. "Hi," she replied, her voice high-pitched and strained. Julian held up a hand, stopping her cleaning motion. "That logo... I had that exact shirt. Counselor edition." Priya froze. Her shoulders hunched inward, protecting herself. She looked up, her eyes searching his face for mockery. Finding none, she relaxed slightly. "I was a camper," she admitted, her voice steadying. "I loved the canoe races." Julian smiled, the tension in his own jaw releasing. "I taught those races," he said. Priya laughed, a short, surprised sound that broke the ice between them.
