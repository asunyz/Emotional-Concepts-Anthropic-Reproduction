She paced the length of the hallway, counting the tiles to keep her mind occupied. One hundred and twelve steps. She had done this five times already. Marcus was waiting inside the office, reviewing the budget. She hated confrontation, especially with someone who seemed so confident. Yet, the scrapbook she found in the shared archive had sparked something urgent. She pushed open the door without knocking. Marcus looked up, startled. "Can I help you?" he asked, his pen hovering over the paper. She pointed to the book lying on the desk. "Page forty-two," she said, her voice trembling. "That treehouse. We built that." Marcus blinked, confused. He flipped the pages until he found the image. His eyebrows shot up. "This is impossible," he muttered. "I helped build that with my brother." The woman stepped forward, her hands clasped tightly together. "Your brother was Leo? The one who fell out of the raft?" Marcus’s face paled. He set the pen down slowly. "Yes," he whispered. The woman nodded, tears welling in her eyes. "He told me it was the best day of his life." Marcus stood up, closing the distance between them, his expression softening from confusion to empathy.
