The noise of the open-plan office was deafening, but Alex heard nothing but the thudding of their own heart. They stared at the screen, blinking rapidly, trying to process the email chain. Jordan, the senior developer, had cc’d them on a message about a team-building retreat. The attachment listed the location: Camp Redwood. Alex’s stomach dropped. They had run away from that place ten years ago, carrying secrets they never spoke of aloud. Their fingers hovered over the keyboard, paralyzed. Should they reply? Should they ignore it? They stood up, walking stiffly toward the water cooler. Jordan followed, noticing their pale complexion. "Rough morning?" he asked, leaning against the machine. Alex shook their head, gripping the paper cup until it crinkled. "Just busy," they lied, their voice thin. Jordan shrugged. "Well, the retreat is mandatory. Hope you’re ready for some nostalgia." Alex flinched visibly. "Nostalgia can be dangerous," they murmured, more to themselves than to him. Jordan frowned, studying their reaction. "You’ve been there before?" Alex looked up, meeting his gaze for the first time. "Once," they said. "Let's just say I prefer the modern amenities."
