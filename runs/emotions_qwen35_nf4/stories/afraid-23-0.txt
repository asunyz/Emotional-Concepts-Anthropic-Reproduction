Elias’s fingers trembled as he zipped the black leather case shut, the sound echoing too loudly in the empty subway car. He pressed his back against the cold glass window, watching his reflection pale under the flickering fluorescent lights. Every screech of the tracks made him flinch, his breath hitching in short, shallow gasps. When the doors opened at the next station, he didn’t wait for the crowd to clear; he bolted onto the platform, clutching the bag to his chest like a shield. His heart hammered against his ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage, urging him to run faster than his legs could carry him.
