I stared at the stack of hundred-dollar bills spread across my kitchen table, the paper crisp and heavy. My hands shook so violently that I had to grip the edge of the counter to steady myself. The silence of the apartment felt suffocating, pressing down on my eardrums until I could hear the blood rushing in my ears. I kept glancing at the front door, convinced that every creak of the floorboards was the prelude to someone breaking in. I pulled the curtains tighter, blocking out the streetlights, trying to shrink into the corner of the room where the shadows were deepest.
