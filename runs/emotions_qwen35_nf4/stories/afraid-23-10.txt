He counted the bills for the tenth time, the numbers blurring together into a dizzying spiral. The apartment smelled stale, the air thick with the scent of unwashed clothes and fear. He jumped when the light switch flipped, the sudden brightness making him squint and cover his eyes. He hadn’t spoken to another human being in three days, whispering to himself instead, rehearsing explanations that sounded increasingly absurd. He considered burning the money, but the thought of letting go of such value paralyzed him, keeping him stuck in a cycle of hoarding and paranoia.
