Clara walked briskly down the sidewalk, her pace erratic, uneven. She crossed the street four times, losing track of which direction led home. The leather pouch burned against her hip, a constant reminder of the mistake she’d made picking it up. A dog barked from a nearby yard, and she nearly tripped over her own feet, her face draining of color. She avoided all mirrors, terrified of seeing what the guilt had done to her expression. By the time she reached her building, her legs felt like jelly, and she had to lean against the wall to keep from collapsing.
