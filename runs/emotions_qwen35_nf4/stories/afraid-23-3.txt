He shoved the thick wad of cash into his sock, wincing as the fabric stretched tight against his skin. Walking through the crowded market, he kept his head down, shoulders hunched inward as if trying to disappear into his coat. People brushing past him made him stumble, his knuckles turning white as he gripped the strap of his backpack. He scanned every face that passed by, looking for recognition, for anger, for anything that might signal he’d been spotted. A child dropped an ice cream cone near his feet, and he leaped backward, knocking over a display of apples, muttering apologies without stopping to help.
