The rain lashed against the windshield of my parked car, blurring the neon sign of the diner across the street. I sat there for an hour, the burner phone vibrating incessantly in my pocket, ignoring every call. The leather wallet lay on the passenger seat, open, exposing the fortune inside. I wiped my sweaty palms on my jeans, leaving dark streaks on the denim. Every time a siren wailed in the distance, I checked the rearview mirror, half-expecting to see flashing blue lights reflecting off the wet pavement. I wasn’t sure who owned that money, but I knew I couldn’t stay in one place long enough to find out.
