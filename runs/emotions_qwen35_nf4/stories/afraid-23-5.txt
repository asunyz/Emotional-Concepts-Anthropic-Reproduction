David paced the length of his hotel hallway, the keycard clutched so tightly it dug into his palm. Room 304 offered no sanctuary; the lock clicked with a finality that only heightened his sense of exposure. He peeked through the peephole, straining his neck, watching the elevator doors dangle open and closed. A maid pushing a cart stopped outside his door, humming a tune, and David held his breath until her footsteps faded away. He locked the deadbolt, then the chain, then wedged a chair under the handle, pacing again, wondering if the people who lost this money had connections that reached everywhere.
