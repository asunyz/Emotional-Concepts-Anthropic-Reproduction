She tucked the bundle of bills into her boot, the weight shifting uncomfortably with every step up the mountain path. The air grew thin, but it was the isolation that made her lungs burn. Branches snapped behind her, and she spun around, hand flying to the knife at her belt. There was nothing there but swaying trees and mist, yet she moved faster, her breathing ragged. She avoided eye contact with the few hikers she passed, pulling her hood lower, wishing the fog would swallow her whole. The money felt like a beacon, drawing attention she desperately wanted to avoid.
