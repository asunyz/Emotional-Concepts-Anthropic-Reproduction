Leo hid the envelope inside a hollowed-out book on his shelf, then immediately regretted it. It looked suspicious now, a secret hiding in plain sight. He spent the afternoon sweeping the floor twice, checking the windows, and locking the gates. When his neighbor knocked to ask about the noise, Leo answered with a forced laugh that cracked halfway through, his voice trembling. He invited the man in briefly, sweating profusely despite the chill, desperate to end the interaction quickly. Once the door closed, he slumped against it, sliding down to the floor, realizing he had become a prisoner in his own home.
