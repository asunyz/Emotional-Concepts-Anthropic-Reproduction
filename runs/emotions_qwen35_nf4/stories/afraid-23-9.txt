The note attached to the stack of bills read *Return Immediately*, signed with an initial that meant nothing to her. Sarah folded the paper carefully, her nails biting into the edges until they bled slightly. She drove aimlessly for hours, the GPS turned off, unable to trust technology to guide her away from danger. She parked in a multi-level garage, choosing the highest level, far from the entrance cameras. Sitting in the driver’s seat, engine idling, she watched the exit ramp, waiting for someone to appear who looked like they belonged in a world of high stakes and dangerous debts.
