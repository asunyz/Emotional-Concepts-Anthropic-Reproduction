Elias stared at the heavy leather-bound book on his desk, its spine cracked from being opened too many times. His fingers trembled as he traced the embossed title, *The Life of Elias Thorne*, realizing every intimate detail—from his childhood fear of thunderstorms to the secret debt he owed his brother—was laid bare for strangers to read. He slammed the cover shut, the sound echoing sharply in the empty study, and backed away until his shoulders hit the cold wall. Sweat prickled along his hairline despite the chill in the room, and he pressed his forehead against the plaster, breathing in shallow, ragged gasps that hitched in his throat.
