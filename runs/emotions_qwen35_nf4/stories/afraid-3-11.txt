Arthur tore the bookmark out of the novel, using it to mark the page where his most shameful mistake was recounted. He walked to the window, pressing his face against the glass, fogging it up with his erratic breathing. Outside, the city buzzed with oblivious energy, unaware that his identity was now public property. He turned away from the view, his movements jerky and abrupt, and began packing a suitcase with frantic speed, throwing clothes in haphazardly, desperate to flee before the next edition could arrive.
