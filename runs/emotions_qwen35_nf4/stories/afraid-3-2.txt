Martha held the newspaper with white-knuckled grip, the ink smudging slightly where her thumb pressed too hard against the page. She had expected privacy, a quiet retirement, but here she was, dissected by a journalist who knew things only her late husband would have known. She crumpled the paper into a tight ball, her breath coming in short bursts, and threw it into the fireplace. Watching the flames consume the words, she felt a cold knot tighten in her stomach, forcing her to sit down heavily on the rug as dizziness washed over her, making the room spin in sickening circles.
