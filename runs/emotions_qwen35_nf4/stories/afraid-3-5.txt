David found the book tucked inside his coat pocket during winter, a stark contrast to the warmth he usually associated with wool. As he read the first chapter, describing his exact route to work that morning, his pulse began to race uncontrollably. He looked around the crowded subway car, scanning faces for recognition, but everyone seemed indifferent to the terror gripping his throat. He exited three stops early, running blindly through the rain-slicked streets, his lungs burning as he tried to outrun the invisible observer who knew his schedule better than he did himself.
