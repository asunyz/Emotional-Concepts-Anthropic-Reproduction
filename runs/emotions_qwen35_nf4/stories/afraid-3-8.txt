I watched the delivery truck pull away from my curb, the box containing the advance copy of my life story sitting on the porch like an unexploded bomb. Inside, I knew, lay the truth about the year I disappeared, the reasons I gave no explanation to anyone. I stepped back into the shadows of the doorway, peering through the crack in the blinds at the package. My hand hovered over the doorknob, trembling, debating whether to burn the house down or call the police, knowing that once those pages were distributed, my anonymity was permanently shattered.
