Julian’s pen hovered over the checkbook, but he couldn’t focus on the amount. The recent publication detailed his financial failures with brutal honesty, stripping away the dignity he had worked decades to build. He capped the pen with excessive force, snapping the plastic casing, and shoved the papers into a drawer, slamming it shut so hard the frame shook. He leaned against the desk, closing his eyes tightly, trying to block out the mental image of strangers laughing at his misfortunes, while his stomach churned with nausea.
