The blueprint lay unfolded on the table, but Mark couldn’t read it. His vision blurred, focusing only on the image of the wrecking ball swinging in his mind’s eye. He tapped his foot against the leg of the chair, a nervous rhythm that matched the distant thumping from the street. He called his lawyer, then hung up before leaving a message. He rehearsed what he would say to the HOA board, practicing in the mirror, but his voice cracked on the word "property." He sat back down, rubbing his temples, feeling the pressure building behind his eyes. Outside, a siren wailed, merging with the roar of engines, and he pulled his coat collar up, wishing he could disappear into the noise.
