Margaret stood on her porch, gripping the railing until her fingers went numb. She watched the crew dismantle her neighbor’s fence, her jaw clenched so hard it ached. She hadn’t spoken to Mrs. Gable in years, yet the thought of the woman walking into her garden felt suffocating. As a truck backed into the driveway, Margaret retreated inside, locking the door behind her with excessive force. She paced the length of her hallway, checking her watch every thirty seconds. The dust from the demolition drifted over the property line, settling on her pristine lawn like gray snow. She wiped it off immediately with a rag, her movements sharp and jerky, unable to relax until the window was shut tight against the intrusion.
