The vibration started early, shaking the picture frames on Arthur’s walls. He jumped at the first impact, knocking over a lamp. He didn’t bother picking it up; instead, he sank onto the sofa, pulling a blanket over his head to muffle the roar of the excavator. His phone buzzed with a text from his sister asking how the "exciting project" was going. He stared at the screen, his thumb hovering over the keyboard, but he typed nothing back. The heat radiating from the street below seemed to seep through the floorboards, raising the temperature in his living room. He held his breath during the loud bangs, counting to ten before exhaling slowly, trying to steady the racing pulse visible in his neck.
