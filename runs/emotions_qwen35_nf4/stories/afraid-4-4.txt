She peeked through the peephole, squinting against the glare of the work lights set up late into the evening. Her reflection in the dark glass looked pale, eyes wide and unblinking. She heard laughter from next door—bright, carefree sounds that grated on her nerves like nails on chalkboard. She stepped back from the door, her shoulders hunched inward. The sound of a ladder being dragged across concrete made her jump, her hand flying to her mouth to stifle a cry. She moved to the kitchen, pouring a glass of water with shaking hands, spilling half of it down the counter. She drank quickly, the cold liquid doing little to soothe the knot tightening in her throat.
