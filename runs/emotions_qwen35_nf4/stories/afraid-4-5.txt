David avoided looking out his office window during business calls. Whenever the contractor’s voice boomed through the open window, he would pause mid-sentence, clearing his throat awkwardly while glancing toward the source of the disturbance. His leg bounced uncontrollably under the desk, a rapid tap-tap-tap that betrayed his calm exterior. He found himself holding his breath when the nail gun fired, his eyes darting to the clock, willing the hour to pass faster. By Friday afternoon, he had packed his bag twice, claiming a sudden headache, desperate to escape the vicinity of the construction site before the demolition of the old shed began.
