Lena buried herself under her duvet, headphones blasting white noise at maximum volume. It wasn’t enough. The thud of debris hitting the ground still penetrated the fabric, vibrating through the mattress. She curled into a fetal position, arms wrapped tightly around her torso. Her phone lay face down on the pillow, ignoring the notifications from friends planning a weekend barbecue nearby. Why were they talking about grilling? The smell of smoke was already creeping in, faint but persistent, triggering memories she wanted to forget. She squeezed her eyes shut, visualizing a quiet library, a deep ocean, anywhere but here, where the world was being torn apart brick by brick just inches from her bedroom wall.
