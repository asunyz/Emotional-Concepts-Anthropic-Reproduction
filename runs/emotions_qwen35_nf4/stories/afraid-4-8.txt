The dust settled on everything—the car, the patio chairs, the air filter of the AC unit. Clara scrubbed the windshield repeatedly, watching the black smear spread rather than disappear. She knew they were working late tonight; the floodlights cast long, eerie shadows across her lawn. She sat on the couch, remote in hand, but didn’t change the channel. The TV dialogue faded into background static as she focused on the rhythmic crashing sounds next door. She checked her email, deleted drafts without sending them, reorganized bookmarks she had no intention of visiting. Every creak of the wooden structure being dismantled sounded like a warning. She waited for the silence, knowing it wouldn’t come until dawn.
