The bell above the door jingled, a sound that usually brought comfort but now sent a jolt of electricity up Mara’s spine. She scanned the room, eyes darting between the dusty mirrors and the shadowed corners. Her legs felt heavy, leaden, as she forced herself forward. There it was. Tucked beside a pair of silver cufflinks, glowing with a faint, eerie luminescence. It was her grandmother’s engagement ring, the one that had vanished twenty years ago. Mara’s mouth went dry, her tongue sticking to the roof of her palate. She reached out, then snatched her hand back as if burned. The silence in the shop stretched, thick and suffocating. She could feel the weight of unseen eyes upon her, judging her presence. Swallowing hard, she backed away slowly, her movements jerky and unnatural, before spinning around and rushing out into the daylight, gasping for air as if she’d been holding her breath underwater.
