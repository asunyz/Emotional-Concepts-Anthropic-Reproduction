Thomas checked his watch every thirty seconds, though he had nowhere to be. The clock ticked loudly in the quiet shop, each second stretching into an eternity. Then he saw it. The gold band, the setting, the way the light refracted through the center stone. It matched the description in the letter he had received that morning. His throat constricted, making it difficult to swallow. He felt a sudden urge to vomit, bile rising in his esophagus. Why was it here? Who had sold it? The questions swirled in his mind, chaotic and overwhelming. He took a step forward, then stopped dead. His feet felt rooted to the floorboards. The shopkeeper cleared his throat, a harsh sound that made Thomas jump. Flustered and disoriented, Thomas mumbled something unintelligible and bolted for the door, the bell ringing frantically behind him as he disappeared into the crowd, unable to process the shock.
