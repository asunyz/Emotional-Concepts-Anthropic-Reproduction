The woman in the red coat paused, her attention snagged by the glint in the window. She approached slowly, her heels clicking softly on the pavement. Inside, the ring sat alone on a black cushion. It was beautiful, elegant, and utterly terrifying. It belonged to her sister, the one who had disappeared five years ago. Seeing it here, commodified and displayed, broke something inside her. Tears pricked her eyes, hot and sudden, blinding her momentarily. She clutched her purse strap until her fingers ached, grounding herself in the pain. She wanted to scream, to demand answers, but her voice failed her. The shopkeeper smiled at her, expecting a sale, but she only nodded stiffly, her lips pressed into a thin line. She turned abruptly, walking faster and faster until she was running, the image of the ring seared into her mind, haunting her every step.
