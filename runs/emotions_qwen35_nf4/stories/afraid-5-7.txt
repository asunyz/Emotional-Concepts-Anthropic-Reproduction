Jack hesitated at the threshold, his foot hovering over the mat. He told himself he was just curious, just passing time. But when his eyes fell upon the tray, curiosity died instantly, replaced by a cold dread. The ring was there. His grandfather’s wedding band, which had supposedly been melted down during the economic crisis. How could it be here? Jack’s heart rate spiked, sending dizziness through his head. He leaned closer, squinting, needing to verify the engraving on the inside. The letters were clear: *For E, forever*. The realization hit him with the force of a punch to the gut. He staggered back, knocking into a shelf of watches. Glass shattered. Ignoring the mess, ignoring the owner’s shout, Jack scrambled backward, tripping over his own feet, and fled out onto the street, his mind reeling from the implications of this impossible discovery.
