Clara wiped her glasses repeatedly, trying to clear the fog from her lenses. The shop was dim, shadows dancing across the walls. She needed a gift, something small for her niece. Then she saw it. Resting in the corner of the display case, half-hidden by a scarf. It was the ring her mother had worn on her deathbed. Clara felt the world tilt on its axis. Her hands began to shake violently, causing her to drop her shopping bag. Items spilled across the floor, unnoticed. She stared at the ring, her breath coming in ragged hitches. The emotional weight of it was crushing, pressing down on her lungs. She couldn't speak, couldn't move. The shopkeeper called out, asking if she needed help, but the words sounded distant, muffled by the roar of fear in her ears. She grabbed her bag, sweeping items into it clumsily, and rushed out, tears streaming freely down her face, unable to bear the sight of the past sitting so casually in the present.
