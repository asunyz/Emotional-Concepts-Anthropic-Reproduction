He walked past the store three times before entering. The hesitation was palpable, visible in the way he shifted his weight from one foot to the other. Once inside, he kept his head down, avoiding eye contact with anyone. But the ring drew him in like a magnet. It lay on a velvet pad, catching the light. It was identical to the one described in the police report from ten years ago—the one linked to his aunt’s disappearance. His stomach churned, a sickening lurch that made him grab the nearest lamp post outside to keep from falling. He pulled out his phone, his fingers slipping on the screen as he tried to dial a number that no longer worked. Panic surged, sharp and acute. He shoved the phone back into his pocket, his breathing shallow and rapid, and hurried away, casting nervous glances over his shoulder, convinced that someone was watching him leave the evidence behind.
