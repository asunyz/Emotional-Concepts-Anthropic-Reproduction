Elias stared at the screen until his vision blurred, the white text against the black background seeming to vibrate with a malicious hum. His fingers hovered over the keyboard, trembling so violently that he had to rest his hands flat on the desk to steady them. The email subject line—*Application Status Update*—sat there like an accusation. He didn’t click open; he already knew what it said. The air in the room felt thin, insufficient for breathing, forcing him to take shallow, rapid sips of oxygen. He pushed his chair back, the wheels screeching against the hardwood floor, a harsh sound that made him wince. He stood up, pacing the length of his small apartment, counting the steps, trying to ground himself in the rhythm of movement rather than the stillness of defeat.
