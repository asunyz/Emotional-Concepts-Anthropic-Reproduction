I folded the letter into smaller and smaller squares, my nails digging into the paper until the edges tore. Each crease felt like a finality, sealing away the future I had meticulously planned out on graph paper. My throat tightened, making it difficult to swallow the lump forming there. I walked to the window, pressing my forehead against the cold glass, watching the rain streak down the pane. It mirrored the blur in my eyes, though I refused to let them fall. If I cried, the structure would collapse completely. So I just stood there, shivering despite the warmth of the room, listening to the wind howl outside as if it were mocking the silence inside.
