She read the email aloud to her empty apartment, her voice monotone and detached, as if speaking the words made them less potent. "We regret to inform you..." The syllables hung in the air, sticky and unpleasant. She set her phone down gently, treating it like a fragile artifact. She walked to the mirror in the hallway, staring at her reflection. The person looking back looked tired, older than her twenty-two years. She touched her cheek, feeling the cold skin, wondering when the fear had taken root so deeply. She turned away, avoiding her own gaze, and headed to the kitchen to boil water, seeking comfort in the simple, predictable ritual of tea.
