The clock on the wall ticked louder with every passing second. Mark sat at his desk, the cursor blinking on the blank document in front of him. He had written ten drafts of a plan B, none of them satisfactory. The denial letter was pinned to the corkboard above him, a target he couldn't stop seeing. His leg bounced uncontrollably, a nervous tic he had developed since receiving the news. He stood up abruptly, knocking his chair back, and began cleaning the already spotless counter. Scrubbing, wiping, organizing—anything to avoid sitting still long enough to think about the closed door behind him.
