The rejection letter sat unopened on the kitchen table, next to a half-eaten bowl of cereal. Marcus circled the table, his footsteps heavy and deliberate, as if walking too fast might trigger the inevitable conclusion. He checked his watch every thirty seconds, then again, then again. The ticking of the second hand sounded like a hammer striking an anvil in his chest. He picked up the envelope, turned it over, and put it down without breaking the seal. "Not today," he whispered, the words cracking in the middle. He grabbed his coat, needing to escape the four walls that suddenly felt like they were closing in, desperate to find noise anywhere other than the ringing in his ears.
