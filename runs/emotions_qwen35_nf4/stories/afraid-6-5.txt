I traced the rim of my coffee mug, the ceramic warm against my fingertips, grounding me in a reality where the news hadn't broken yet. Then the phone buzzed on the table, a singular, sharp vibration that cut through the morning calm. I didn't need to look at the screen to know the sender. The color drained from my face, leaving a cold numbness spreading from my stomach outward. I set the mug down with excessive care, placing it precisely on the coaster, as if order could contain the chaos rising within me. My hands began to shake, a subtle tremor that traveled up my arms, making me pull my sleeves down over my wrists to hide the betrayal of my own body.
