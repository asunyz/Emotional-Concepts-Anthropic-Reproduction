The campus library was usually a sanctuary, but today the stacks of books felt like prison bars. Sarah clutched her acceptance packet, now rejected, in a fist so tight her knuckles turned white. She scanned the faces of other students, laughing and studying, feeling like an imposter among them. Every glance from a librarian felt like a judgment, every whisper a confirmation of her inadequacy. She backed away from the circulation desk, retreating toward the exit, her movements jerky and unnatural. The automatic doors slid open with a hiss, releasing her into the bright sunlight that felt harsh and exposing, offering no shadow in which to hide her shame.
