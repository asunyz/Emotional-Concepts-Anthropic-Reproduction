He paced the hallway, the wooden floorboards creaking under his weight. The email was open on his laptop, visible from the doorway, a digital ghost haunting his home office. He wanted to close it, to banish it from sight, but the act of looking away felt like admitting defeat. He rubbed his temples, massaging the skin hard enough to leave red marks, trying to massage the pressure out of his brain. The silence of the house was deafening, amplifying the roar of his own thoughts. He gripped the doorframe, leaning his weight against it, needing the physical resistance to keep from collapsing inward.
