My palms were sweating, leaving damp prints on the steering wheel of my car. I sat parked in the driveway for twenty minutes after turning off the engine, unable to bring myself to walk up the front steps. The letter was in my pocket, burning a hole through the fabric. I rolled down the window, letting the cool autumn air hit my flushed face, hoping it would lower the temperature of my panic. A neighbor waved from across the street, smiling broadly. I forced a smile back, waving weakly, my arm feeling leaden and disconnected from my body. How do you explain this? How do you pretend that your foundation hasn't just cracked?
