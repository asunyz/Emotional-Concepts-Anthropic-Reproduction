Leo threw the paper airplane across the room, watching it crash into the bookshelf. Again. And again. Each throw was harder, fueled by a surge of adrenaline that tasted like copper in his mouth. The denial wasn't just a piece of paper; it was a verdict on his intelligence, his worth, his entire trajectory. He stopped throwing and slumped against the wall, sliding down until he hit the floor. He pulled his knees up, burying his face in them, muffling the sob that threatened to tear free. The room was messy, chaotic, reflecting the turmoil in his head, and he lacked the energy to clean it up, let alone fix his life.
