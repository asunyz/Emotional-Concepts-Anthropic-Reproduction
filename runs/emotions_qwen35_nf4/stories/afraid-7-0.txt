Elias stared at the blinking cursor on his screen, his fingers hovering over the keyboard like a pianist before a storm. The notification from "StarGazer99" had arrived an hour ago, simple text that read: *I’m in Seattle.* He hadn’t slept in two days, jumping at every siren outside his apartment window. When he finally typed back, *Which part?*, his hand trembled so violently he hit three wrong keys before correcting himself. The rain lashed against his glass, mirroring the chaotic rhythm of his heart hammering against his ribs. He pulled his knees to his chest, making himself as small as possible on the couch, watching the streetlights flicker below. Every shadow cast by the swaying trees looked like a figure waiting for him to step outside. He locked the deadbolt, then the chain, then checked the lock again, his breath coming in shallow, ragged gasps until the silence of the room felt heavy enough to crush him.
