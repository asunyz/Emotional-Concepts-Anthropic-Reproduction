Maya sat in the back corner of the coffee shop, her eyes darting toward the door every time the bell chimed. She had met Leo online six months ago, bonding over obscure sci-fi novels, but today he had asked to meet. Her stomach churned with nausea, a cold sweat prickling along her hairline despite the warmth of the café. She clutched her mug tightly, knuckles white, pretending to read a book she wasn’t absorbing. When the door opened, she didn’t look up immediately; instead, she focused intensely on the foam art in her latte, her pulse thumping loudly in her ears. A man walked in, wearing the exact hoodie described in his profile picture. Maya’s throat tightened, making it hard to swallow. She considered slipping out the back exit, her legs tense and ready to bolt, but curiosity anchored her to the chair. As he approached her table, she forced a smile that felt brittle, her hands shaking slightly as she reached for her phone under the table.
