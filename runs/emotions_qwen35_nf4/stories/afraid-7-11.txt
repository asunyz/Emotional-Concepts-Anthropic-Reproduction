Nina parked her bike in front of the community center, locking it with deliberate slowness. She had promised to meet "ArtLover" for a gallery walk. Nina’s stomach twisted into knots as she pushed open the heavy doors. The warm interior air contrasted sharply with the chill outside, raising goosebumps on her arms. She navigated the crowd carefully, keeping her head low, aware that recognition could change everything. Her pulse fluttered in her neck, a rapid bird-like beat. She spotted a sketchbook on a table, open to a drawing she had posted online weeks ago. Standing beside it was a person who matched the description perfectly. Nina stopped breathing for a second, her vision tunneling. She wanted to turn back, to escape the confrontation, but her feet remained planted. She forced herself to take another step forward, her legs feeling like lead, as the distance between them closed.
