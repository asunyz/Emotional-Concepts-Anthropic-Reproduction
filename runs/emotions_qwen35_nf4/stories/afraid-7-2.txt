Javier paced the length of his hallway, checking his reflection in the mirror every few steps. His neighbor, whom he’d known only as "NightOwl," lived just down the hall in unit 4B. They had shared late-night conversations about fears and dreams for years, but Javier had never imagined the connection would translate to physical proximity. Now, standing outside his own door, he felt exposed, as if his privacy had been breached by an invisible force. He gripped the doorknob, turning it slowly, listening for any sound from the other side. The air in the corridor seemed thinner than usual, pressing against his lungs. He rehearsed what he would say, then deleted the script in his head, terrified of saying the wrong thing. When he finally stepped out, he kept his head down, avoiding eye contact with the closed doors around him, moving with stiff, mechanical precision to avoid drawing attention to himself.
