Sarah watched the GPS on her phone zoom in on her current location. Her digital pen pal, Alex, was supposedly at the park across town. She stood on the sidewalk, the wind whipping her coat around her legs, chilling her skin. Her mind raced through worst-case scenarios, each one more vivid than the last. She adjusted her scarf, pulling it higher to cover her mouth, a subconscious attempt to hide her identity even though she knew Alex recognized her photos. Her feet shuffled nervously, scuffing against the pavement. She took a deep breath, holding it until her lungs burned, then exhaled sharply. Seeing a figure matching Alex’s description near the bench, she froze. Her instinct screamed at her to turn around and run, but she remained rooted to the spot, her muscles coiled tight, waiting for the next move to unfold.
