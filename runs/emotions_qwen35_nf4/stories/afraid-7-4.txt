David checked his watch for the tenth time in five minutes. The meeting point was a crowded subway station, a place designed for anonymity yet filled with thousands of potential threats. He had agreed to meet his online friend, "TrainHopper," here because it was neutral ground, but the noise of the approaching train drowned out his thoughts. His palms were slick with sweat, making it difficult to hold onto the strap above him. He scanned the crowd, looking for someone reading the specific book they had discussed. Every stranger passing by made his shoulders hunch forward defensively. When he spotted the book, his heart skipped a beat, then accelerated into a frantic rhythm. He wanted to retreat into the shadows of the pillar behind him, away from the prying eyes of the platform, but he stayed put, his gaze fixed on the approaching lights, bracing himself for the impact of reality.
