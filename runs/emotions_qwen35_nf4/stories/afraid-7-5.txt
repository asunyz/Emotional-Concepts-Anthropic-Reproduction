Lena sat in her car, engine idling, staring at the house number on the end of the driveway. It matched the address her internet friend, "CityWalker," had sent after a sudden shift in their conversation topics. The gravel crunched under her tires as she shifted into gear, but she didn’t move forward. Her grip on the steering wheel was so tight her arms ached. She looked in the rearview mirror, half-expecting to see a black sedan following her. The silence inside the car was deafening, amplifying the sound of her breathing. She rolled down the window slightly, letting in the cool night air, hoping it would clear her head. Seeing the front porch light was on, she felt a knot form in her stomach. She considered driving away, circling the block until dawn, but the fear of missing this rare chance held her there. She killed the engine, the sudden quiet ringing in her ears, and unbuckled her seatbelt with trembling fingers.
