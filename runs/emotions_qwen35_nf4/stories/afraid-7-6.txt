Marcus stood in line at the grocery store, clutching a basket that contained nothing but essentials. His online companion, "ShopSmart," had mentioned living nearby. Marcus’s eyes scanned the aisles, paranoid that someone might recognize him from their forum posts. He moved quickly, skipping sections he usually browsed, driven by a need to leave the public space as soon as possible. His heart pounded against his sternum, a rhythmic drumming that distracted him from the items on his list. He saw a familiar face near the produce section—someone who looked exactly like the avatar he used. Panic flared in his chest, hot and sharp. He ducked behind a display of canned goods, peering out cautiously, wondering if he should feign ignorance or walk away. The fluorescent lights buzzed overhead, sounding like static in his mind, urging him to flee.
