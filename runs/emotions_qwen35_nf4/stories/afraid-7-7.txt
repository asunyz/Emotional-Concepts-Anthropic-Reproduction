Chloe typed furiously on her laptop, her eyes wide and unblinking. Her chat partner, "CodeBreaker," had just revealed they worked in the building adjacent to hers. Chloe stopped typing, her hands freezing mid-air. She looked around her empty office, the hum of the servers suddenly sounding menacing. She packed her bag with jerky movements, zipping it shut with excessive force. Walking to the elevator, she pressed the button repeatedly, her leg bouncing uncontrollably. When the doors opened, she stepped in and turned to face the wall, refusing to look at anyone else who might enter. The descent felt endless, each floor a reminder of how close the danger—or the opportunity—was. As she exited onto the lobby floor, she quickened her pace, merging with the crowd, trying to disappear among the strangers while knowing one of them was now her closest confidant.
