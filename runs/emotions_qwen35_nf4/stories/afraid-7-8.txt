Tom waited on the park bench, the leaves crunching under his shoes as he shifted his weight. He had arranged to meet "ForestFriend" here, a person he trusted implicitly online but feared deeply in person. The wind picked up, sending shivers down his spine that had nothing to do with the temperature. He kept glancing at the tree line, imagining figures stepping out from between the trunks. His throat felt dry, forcing him to lick his lips constantly. When a jogger ran past, Tom flinched, his muscles tensing automatically. He checked his phone, refreshing the message thread, desperate for reassurance that the plan was still valid. The isolation of the bench felt oppressive, exposing him to view. He wrapped his arms around himself, hugging his torso tightly, wishing he could shrink into invisibility until the moment passed.
