Ava stood outside the library, her breath visible in the crisp autumn air. Her virtual friend, "BookWorm," had suggested they discuss a new release here. Ava hesitated at the entrance, her hand resting on the handle but not pushing it open. She felt vulnerable, stripped of the digital filters that protected her online persona. Inside, the soft murmur of readers created a cocoon of sound that she found both comforting and suffocating. She entered anyway, scanning the fiction section with narrowed eyes. Spotting a woman holding the correct title, Ava’s breath hitched. She smoothed her jacket, trying to steady her nerves, but her hands remained clammy. She approached slowly, each step feeling heavier than the last, her mind racing with questions about whether the real person matched the idealized version in her head.
