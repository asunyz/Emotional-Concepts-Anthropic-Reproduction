Elias stared at the measuring tape stretched across his lawn, the yellow metal glinting under the harsh noon sun. His fingers trembled as he tried to pin the stakes into the earth, but they slipped from his sweaty palms repeatedly. He kept glancing over the property line, watching Mr. Henderson hammer the first post with rhythmic, aggressive thuds that seemed to vibrate through the soles of Elias’s shoes. Every time the neighbor looked up, Elias ducked behind the hydrangeas, holding his breath until the footsteps moved away. The shadow of the rising wooden slats felt heavy on his chest, tightening with every new panel erected along the boundary.
