I pressed my forehead against the cold windowpane, watching the delivery truck back into the driveway next door. My heart hammered against my ribs like a trapped bird, fluttering wildly with each thud of the tailgate hitting the asphalt. I didn’t want to go out there. I wanted to stay hidden in the dimness of the living room where the curtains were drawn tight. But the foreman was asking for permission to cut the old oak branch that hung over our shared yard. I swallowed hard, the lump in my throat making it difficult to breathe, and forced myself to walk toward the door, my legs feeling like lead weights dragging me forward.
