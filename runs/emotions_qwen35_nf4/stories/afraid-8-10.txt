Lena counted the planks as they were nailed into place. One, two, three... twenty. Each nail driven in was a period ending a sentence she hadn’t finished writing. She stood on her balcony, gripping the iron railings until the metal bit into her palms. Below, the workers laughed, sharing coffee, oblivious to the tension radiating from her window. She felt exposed, naked under their casual chatter. She pulled the blinds shut abruptly, the sudden darkness relieving her gaze but intensifying the pressure in her head. She slid down the wall to the floor, wrapping her arms around herself, listening to the muffled sounds of construction continuing unabated.
