Sarah checked her locks three times before bed, then two more just to be sure. The sound of saws cutting through timber drifted through the thin walls of her apartment, a high-pitched whine that set her teeth on edge. She pulled the duvet up to her chin, staring at the ceiling fan spinning lazily above her. It wasn’t just the noise; it was the implication of what was being built. A barrier. Something solid and unyielding separating her space from theirs. She rolled onto her side, pulling her knees to her chest, trying to make herself small enough to disappear beneath the covers while the drilling continued its relentless march closer to her bedroom wall.
