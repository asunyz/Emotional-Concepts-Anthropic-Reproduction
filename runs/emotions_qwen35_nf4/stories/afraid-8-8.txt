Night fell, and the streetlights flickered on, casting long, jagged shadows across the newly installed chain link. Anita sat on her couch, remote control in hand, but the TV was muted. She listened to the wind rattling the loose mesh of the fence, a metallic clinking that sounded like bones shaking. She reached for her phone, scrolling through numbers she had memorized but never dialed. Her thumb hovered over the 'Call' button, trembling visibly. She lowered the device slowly, placing it face down on the table. The darkness of the room seemed to press in, mirroring the encroaching structure outside, leaving her feeling cornered in her own sanctuary.
