"You're doing this wrong," Arthur shouted, though his voice cracked, betraying the fear coiling in his gut. He pointed a shaking finger at the contractor, who ignored him and kept welding sparks flying. Arthur stepped back, bumping into the porch railing. He needed to speak up, to assert ownership, but the words stuck in his throat, blocked by a knot of anxiety. He wiped his palms on his trousers, leaving damp streaks on the fabric. The heat from the welder’s torch felt intense, burning his skin even from ten feet away. He took a shallow breath, holding it, willing himself to step forward, but his feet remained rooted to the spot.
