My fingers trembled as I fumbled with the keys, dropping one twice before I could get the lock to turn. The house looked exactly the same, yet everything felt alien under the harsh glare of the porch light. I pushed the door open and held my breath, waiting for the inevitable sigh or the sharp intake of breath that usually preceded a lecture. When none came, I slipped off my boots, lining them up perfectly parallel against the mat, a ritual of control in a life that had recently spiraled out of order. I crept down the hall, pressing my back against the wall, listening for the TV volume to change or the click of a remote, praying she wouldn’t decide to come downstairs just yet.
