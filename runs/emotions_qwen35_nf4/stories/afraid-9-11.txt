Lena dropped her bags on the entryway table, the thud echoing through the empty foyer. She waited, straining her ears to hear a response, but the house remained unnervingly quiet. She crept toward the staircase, her socks slipping slightly on the polished wood, forcing her to grab the railing for support. From the top of the stairs, her father called down, "Dinner's in twenty minutes." His voice was steady, controlled, which sent a chill down her spine. She climbed the stairs, each step feeling like a negotiation with gravity, terrified that one misstep would reveal her instability. At the landing, she paused, listening to the distant murmur of voices from the kitchen, realizing she was no longer part of the conversation, merely an observer invited to watch.
