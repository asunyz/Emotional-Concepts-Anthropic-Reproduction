Sarah packed the last box with violent precision, taping it shut with more force than necessary. She carried it up the stairs, her arms shaking slightly from the weight and the adrenaline coursing through her veins. Her father sat in the armchair, reading a newspaper that he hadn’t turned the page of in ten minutes. He didn’t look up when she entered the room, which made her heart hammer against her ribs like a trapped bird. She set the box down gently, avoiding his gaze, and whispered a quick "I'm here" that sounded more like a question than a statement. The silence that followed was heavy, pressing against her eardrums, and she found herself holding her breath until he finally lowered the paper, his expression unreadable.
