I stared at the ceiling fan spinning lazily above me, counting the rotations to keep from hyperventilating. The mattress beneath me felt too soft, too familiar, triggering a wave of nausea that rolled through my stomach. Downstairs, the clatter of dishes echoed through the floorboards, each sound sending a jolt of electricity up my spine. I pulled the duvet up to my chin, shrinking into myself, wishing I could dissolve into the fabric. Every time the floorboard creaked outside my door, I tensed my muscles, ready to bolt or beg, though I knew neither option was truly available. The clock ticked loudly, marking the seconds of my imprisonment in this house that once felt safe but now felt like a cage.
