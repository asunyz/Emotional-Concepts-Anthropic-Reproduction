Maya placed her suitcase by the door, the wheels leaving faint marks on the hardwood that she immediately tried to rub away with her sock. Her mother stood in the doorway, arms crossed, her lips pressed into a thin line that Maya recognized well. "You're late," her mother said, the words flat and devoid of warmth. Maya nodded mutely, unable to form a coherent sentence without her voice cracking. She retreated to her old room, closing the door softly, then leaning her forehead against the wood. The muffled sounds of her parents arguing in the other room vibrated through the frame, syncing with the rapid pounding of her own heart. She slid down to the floor, hugging her knees, wishing she could reverse time to a point before she ever left.
