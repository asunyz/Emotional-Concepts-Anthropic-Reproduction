Thomas hovered in the kitchen doorway, watching his mother chop vegetables with rhythmic, aggressive strokes. The knife hit the cutting board with a thud that matched the rhythm of his pulse. He wanted to speak, to offer help, to explain, but his tongue felt thick and useless in his mouth. He shifted his weight from foot to foot, his shoulders hunched forward in a defensive posture. When his mother glanced up, catching his stare, he quickly looked away, focusing intently on a crack in the tile floor. He waited for the reprimand, flinching internally every time she paused, convinced that the next second would bring the explosion he had been dreading all week.
