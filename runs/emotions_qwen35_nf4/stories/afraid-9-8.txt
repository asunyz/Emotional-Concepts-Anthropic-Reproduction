I locked the bathroom door and leaned against it, sliding down until I hit the tub. The shower was running, masking the silence of the rest of the house, but I could still feel the weight of their expectations pressing against the tiles. My hands shook as I washed my face, the water stinging my eyes, blurring the vision of the young man who used to live here. Outside, a chair scraped against the floor, and I held my breath, listening for footsteps. They didn't come, but the absence of noise was somehow louder than shouting. I stayed there, hidden behind the closed door, knowing that eventually, the water would run out, and I would have to face them again.
