Rachel walked into the dining room, her head bowed, avoiding the direct line of sight to her father’s stern profile. The table was set for two, a silent invitation that felt more like a summons. She pulled out her chair, the legs scraping harshly against the floor, and winced at the noise. "Sit," her father commanded, not looking up from his plate. She obeyed instantly, her movements jerky and unnatural. The food tasted like ash in her mouth, each bite requiring conscious effort to swallow. She focused on the pattern of the tablecloth, tracing the threads with her eyes, desperate to disappear into the weave while her parents discussed her future plans with a seriousness that made her stomach churn.
