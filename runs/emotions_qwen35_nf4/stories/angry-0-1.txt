I slammed my sketchbook onto the table, the impact sending a cloud of graphite dust into the air, settling on my tongue like ash. There it was, clear as day on Instagram: my signature style, my specific shading technique, my soul, inked onto some influencer’s bicep. My jaw clenched so hard I felt a muscle twitch beneath my ear, and I crushed the empty coffee cup in my hand, paper tearing with a sharp rip that echoed in the quiet room. I didn’t scream; I just stood there, shaking, the cold dread turning into a boiling point somewhere between my chest and my neck, forcing me to pace the length of my apartment until the floorboards groaned under my weight.
