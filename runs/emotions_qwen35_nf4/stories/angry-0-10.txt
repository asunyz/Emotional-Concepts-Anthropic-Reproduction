Javier stared at the billboard downtown, featuring his landscape painting reduced to a logo for a fast-food chain. The sun glared off the vinyl, making the colors bleed and distort further than they already did. He felt a hot flush rise up his neck, burning his cheeks, and he clenched his teeth until his jaw ached. He turned away from the view, walking briskly to his car, fumbling with the keys in the ignition due to shaking hands. Once inside, he revved the engine aggressively, holding it at maximum RPMs until the dashboard warning lights flashed, ignoring the discomfort of the vibration rattling his bones.
