The email notification pinged, announcing that her design contest winner was actually her entry, submitted anonymously by a competitor who knew her style too well. Nora read the message twice, then a third time, her stomach dropping as if she’d missed a step on a staircase. She pushed her chair back so hard it toppled over, landing with a crash that startled the cat. She stormed into the hallway, kicking the doorframe repeatedly, her foot bruising instantly, but she kept striking the wood, driven by a need to externalize the violation she felt deep in her gut, screaming silently into the empty corridor.
