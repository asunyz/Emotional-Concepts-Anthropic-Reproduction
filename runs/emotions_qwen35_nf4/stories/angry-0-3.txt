He sat in the dimly lit bar, nursing a whiskey that tasted like battery acid, watching the news report about the unauthorized usage of his digital art series on merchandise sold by a major retailer. His leg bounced uncontrollably, the rhythmic thumping of his foot against the floorboard creating a staccato beat that matched the pounding in his temples. He reached out and snapped the stem of his wine glass off, the sudden crunch silencing the ambient jazz, drawing stares from nearby patrons which he ignored completely. With a fluid, violent motion, he swept the remaining glasses off the counter, shattering them into shards that skittered across the sticky tiles.
