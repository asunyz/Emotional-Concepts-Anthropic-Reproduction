The letter arrived in a plain envelope, containing nothing but a high-resolution print of her latest sculpture, now reproduced cheaply on keychains found in airport gift shops. Sarah held the paper until the edges curled from the moisture of her palm, her breathing becoming shallow and restricted. She walked to the window, staring out at the rain-slicked city streets, her reflection ghostly and distorted in the glass. Turning away, she kicked the heavy oak desk, sending stacks of invoices flying into the air like startled birds, then sank to the floor, hugging her knees tightly as she bit down on her lip until she tasted copper.
