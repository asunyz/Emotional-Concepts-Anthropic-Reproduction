Marcus read the article claiming his street art was "public domain" and therefore free for anyone to monetize. He crumpled the newspaper in his fist, the paper resisting before giving way with a loud tear. He paced around his small kitchen, the linoleum cold against his bare feet, his mind racing through legal precedents and financial losses. Suddenly, he stopped, his eyes wide and unblinking, and grabbed the nearest object—a ceramic vase—and smashed it against the sink. The sound was deafening, echoing off the tiled walls, followed by the silence of shock that quickly morphed into a grim satisfaction as he looked at the broken pieces.
