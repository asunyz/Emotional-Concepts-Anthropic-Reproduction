Anya found her portfolio uploaded to a pirate site, complete with her contact information exposed to spammers and thieves. She sat frozen in front of her laptop, the blue light reflecting in her wide, unseeing eyes. Slowly, deliberately, she closed the lid, the snap sounding like a gunshot in the silent apartment. She stood up, her movements jerky and unnatural, and began pushing furniture aside, clearing space in the living room. Without warning, she launched herself at the bookshelf, pulling books down and throwing them across the room, her breath coming in short, sharp gasps as she worked through the betrayal with physical exertion.
