Elias slammed his coffee mug down so hard the ceramic cracked, sending shards skittering across the kitchen tile. He stared at his sister, his jaw clenched tight enough to ache, watching her speak about temple rituals with a serene smile that made his stomach turn. "You’re throwing away everything we are," he snapped, his voice rising until it echoed off the bare walls. He paced the length of the room, hands shoved deep into his pockets, knuckles white from gripping his own fingers. Every time she mentioned the new prayers, his breathing grew shallower, sharp inhales through his nose as if trying to filter out the very air she was filling with foreign concepts. He couldn’t look at her directly anymore; instead, he focused on the clock ticking on the wall, counting seconds, waiting for the moment he could storm out before he said something irreversible.
