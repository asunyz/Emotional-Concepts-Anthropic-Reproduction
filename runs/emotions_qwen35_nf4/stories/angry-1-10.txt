Sister Clara smiled at me across the breakfast table, her eyes bright with newfound purpose. I pushed my toast around the plate, cutting it into tiny, ragged pieces. "Is that all you have to say?" I asked, looking up with narrowed eyes. She nodded, oblivious to the storm brewing in my demeanor. I stood up, the chair screeching against the floor, a sharp, piercing sound. I grabbed my bag, zipping it shut with unnecessary force. "Enjoy your new life," I said, the sarcasm dripping from my tongue like venom. I stormed out the front door, not bothering to close it gently, letting it slam shut with a finality that shook the frame. In the car, I gripped the steering wheel until my palms sweated, revving the engine unnecessarily loud, needing the noise to fill the void left by her departure from our shared beliefs.
