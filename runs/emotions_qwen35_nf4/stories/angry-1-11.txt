Brother Thomas explained the theological differences with such patience it made my teeth itch. I tapped my foot rapidly against the rug, the rhythm erratic and fast. "Patience doesn't fix broken trust," I interrupted, crossing my arms tightly. He sighed, rubbing his temples. That sigh annoyed me more than anything. I reached out and straightened a picture frame on the shelf, then immediately knocked it crooked again, a subconscious act of rebellion. "You’re abandoning your roots," I accused, my voice rising in pitch. I moved toward the door, hand on the knob, twisting it back and forth. I paused, looking back at him one last time, my expression hard, lips pressed into a thin line. Then I opened the door and stepped out, leaving the debate hanging in the air, unresolved and festering.
