Sarah didn’t sit during dinner; she stood by the window, arms crossed tightly over her chest as if shielding herself from an invisible chill. When Marcus explained the conversion process, she let out a short, bitter laugh that lacked any humor. "So, that’s it," she muttered, turning sharply to face him. Her eyes were wide, unblinking, fixed on him with an intensity that felt like a physical weight. She began picking at a loose thread on her sweater, pulling it until the fabric stretched thin, threatening to snap. "You’re leaving us behind," she stated, her voice low and steady, yet vibrating with a suppressed tremor. She walked past him without touching his shoulder, the space between them suddenly vast and cold, filled with the silence of things unsaid and bonds fraying in real-time.
