I threw the book across the living room. It hit the wall with a dull thud, sliding slowly to the floor like a defeated soldier. Dad just watched it fall, his expression calm, which only fueled the fire in my chest. "Do you have any idea what this does to Mom?" I shouted, pacing around the furniture, knocking a vase slightly askew. I ran my hands through my hair, tugging at the roots, desperate to pull the frustration out through my scalp. The house felt too small, the air too thick to breathe. I grabbed my keys, jangling them loudly in my pocket, needing noise to drown out the quiet logic he was using to justify his choice. I wasn't listening; I was just waiting for him to stop talking so I could leave, before I broke something else that couldn't be glued back together.
