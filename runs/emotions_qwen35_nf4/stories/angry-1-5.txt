Leo laughed when I told him I wouldn’t attend the ceremony. It was a harsh, barking sound that grated on my nerves. He shrugged, adjusting his glasses, completely unfazed by my visible distress. I clenched my fists at my sides, the tendons standing out starkly against my skin. "It’s betrayal," I spat, the word tasting metallic in my mouth. I kicked the leg of the dining chair, wincing at the impact but refusing to acknowledge the pain. The room seemed to shrink, the walls pressing in as I recounted every tradition we had abandoned for this new path. I paced back and forth, a caged animal, stopping only to glare at him over my shoulder. I refused to engage further, grabbing my coat and shoving my feet into my shoes with aggressive force, determined to end the conversation before he could say another word that would twist the knife deeper.
