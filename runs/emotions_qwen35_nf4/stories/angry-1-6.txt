I stared at the email on my screen, the cursor blinking mockingly at the end of the sentence. My grip on the mouse tightened until the plastic creaked. *I’m converting,* it read. Simple. Final. I pushed the keyboard away, sending it clattering onto the desk, scattering papers everywhere. I stood up abruptly, my chair scraping violently against the hardwood floor. Why did they always assume I’d just accept it? Why did they think their spiritual journey justified the chaos they left in their wake? I walked to the window, pressing my forehead against the cold glass, watching the rain lash against the pane. Inside, my blood roared in my ears, a deafening roar that drowned out the hum of the refrigerator. I wanted to scream, to pound on the walls, but I remained silent, a statue of resentment in the dimming light.
