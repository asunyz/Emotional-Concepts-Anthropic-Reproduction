Elias stared at the whiteboard until the marker stains blurred into gray smudges. He didn’t look up when Sarah walked in, her heels clicking a rhythmic, mocking beat against the linoleum floor. "Show me the login sequence again," she said, pulling out a chair that scraped loudly against the tiles. Elias tightened his grip on the dry-erase marker until the plastic casing creaked. He pointed sharply at the screen, his jaw set so hard it ached. "It’s line four," he snapped, turning back to the board without waiting for her acknowledgment. He began writing furiously, the cap of the marker bouncing off his knee as he moved with jerky, aggressive precision. Every time she asked a clarifying question, he answered with a curt nod, his eyes fixed on the wall clock, counting down the seconds until he could walk away from this desk forever.
