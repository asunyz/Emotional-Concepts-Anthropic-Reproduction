David stared at the ceiling fan, watching it spin in lazy circles. He counted the rotations. One. Two. Three. Below him, his replacement chattered away about weekend plans, oblivious to the storm brewing in David’s chest. "So, anyway, I booked tickets to Vegas," the guy said, grinning. David closed his eyes. He focused on the sensation of his fingernails pressing into the palms of his hands. Pain was grounding. "That’s nice," David murmured, his voice hollow. He opened his eyes and looked at the guy, really looked at him, seeing the arrogance in his posture. "Do you know how to handle a server crash?" he asked suddenly. The guy blinked. "Uh, no? Is that common?" David let out a bitter laugh. "Oh, it happens more than you think," he said, turning his head away, staring at the blank wall as if it held the answers to why he was being replaced.
