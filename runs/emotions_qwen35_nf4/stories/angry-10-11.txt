She dropped the file folder onto the desk. It slid across the polished surface, stopping inches from his elbow. "This contains everything you need to know," she said, her voice clipped and cold. Mark looked at the folder, then up at her. "Thank you," he said softly. She didn't respond. Instead, she picked up her purse and stood, the movement abrupt and final. She adjusted her blazer, smoothing out invisible wrinkles with trembling fingers. "Don't call me after five," she added, walking toward the door. "And don't expect help if things go wrong." She paused at the threshold, her hand gripping the doorknob until her knuckles turned pale. Then she pulled the door open and stepped out, letting it click shut behind her, sealing herself inside her own isolation.
