The coffee in David’s mug was cold, a fact he noticed only because he hadn’t taken a sip in twenty minutes. He watched Lena organize the filing cabinets, her movements efficient and calm. He wanted to knock the stack of folders over. Just once. Let them scatter across the floor like fallen leaves. "Did you see the error logs?" she asked, turning around with a hopeful expression. David forced his face into a mask of neutral professionalism, though his fingers dug into the armrests of his chair until his knuckles turned white. "They’re in the shared drive," he replied, his tone flat, devoid of any warmth. He stood up abruptly, knocking his chair back, and walked to the window, staring out at the rain-slicked street below, refusing to meet her gaze any longer.
