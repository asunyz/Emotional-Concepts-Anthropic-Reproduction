Rachel paced the length of the conference room, her heels striking the floor with military precision. She wasn't listening to anything Mark was saying about the transition timeline. Her mind was elsewhere, replaying every mistake she’d ever made, every compliment she’d received, now being handed over to someone else. "And then we run the quarterly report," Mark finished, closing his folder. Rachel stopped pacing. She turned to him, her eyes wide and unblinking. "Who approved this?" she demanded, her voice rising an octave. Mark flinched, shrinking back into his chair. "HR did," he mumbled. Rachel laughed, a short, harsh bark of sound. "Great," she muttered, grabbing her bag and storming out of the room, leaving the door swinging wildly on its hinges.
