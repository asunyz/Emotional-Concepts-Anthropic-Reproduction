He placed the headset on the desk with deliberate slowness, ensuring it landed with a heavy *thud*. His replacement, a woman named Jen, smiled brightly, reaching for it. "Thanks! I’ve been dying to try it out," she chirped. He didn’t return the smile. Instead, he reached past her, his shoulder brushing hers intentionally hard, and grabbed his coat from the hook behind her. "Don’t touch the settings," he warned, his voice dripping with icy disdain. "You’ll break it." Jen’s smile faltered, confused by his hostility. He ignored her confusion, zipping up his jacket with violent yanks. As he walked toward the exit, he deliberately kicked the leg of the empty chair next to him, sending it skidding across the carpet before disappearing into the hallway.
