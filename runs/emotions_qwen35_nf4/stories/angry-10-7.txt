The air conditioning hummed, a constant drone that seemed to amplify the tension in the room. Sarah watched Kevin demonstrate the software interface. He was good. Too good. He navigated the menus with a fluidity that spoke of years of practice, practice that had been mine. A hot flush crept up her neck, spreading to her cheeks. "Can you show me how to export the data?" Kevin asked, turning his chair to face her. Sarah gripped the edge of her desk, her nails digging into the wood. "Click here," she said, pointing a shaking finger at the screen. "Then hit enter." When he hesitated, she snatched the mouse from his hand, moving it with jerky, unnatural motions. "Like this," she spat, dropping the mouse back onto the pad. "Don't mess it up."
