He didn’t say a word as he packed his box. Books, photos, a dried-out plant. Each item was handled with care, a stark contrast to the way he looked at his former colleague. "Hey, man, sorry about this," the colleague said, offering a sympathetic shrug. He ignored the gesture. He continued packing, his movements mechanical and precise. When he picked up the framed award from the shelf, he paused. For a second, he held it tight, the glass pressing into his palm. Then, with a sudden, violent motion, he shoved it into the box, burying it under sweaters. He taped the box shut with aggressive strips of tape, tearing each piece with his teeth. He hoisted the box onto his shoulder, avoiding eye contact, and marched toward the elevator, his boots slamming against the stairs with every step.
