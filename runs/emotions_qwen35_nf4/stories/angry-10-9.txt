My phone buzzed on the desk. Another email from HR confirming the termination date. I crushed the paper napkin in my fist, the dampness seeping into my skin. Across the table, my replacement, a junior analyst named Lisa, was laughing at something on her tablet. The sound grated on my nerves, sharp and grating. I cleared my throat loudly. Lisa jumped, looking up with wide, startled eyes. "Sorry," she whispered. "I thought you were done with the training." "I’m not done," I said, standing up so quickly my chair rolled backward and hit the wall. "We still have three hours left." I walked to the whiteboard, erasing the previous day’s notes with rough, circular strokes, leaving faint gray smears that refused to disappear. "Now, open your textbook to page ten."
