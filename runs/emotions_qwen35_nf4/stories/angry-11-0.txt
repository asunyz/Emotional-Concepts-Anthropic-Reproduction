Marcus slammed his locker shut, the metal screeching against the frame loud enough to echo down the empty hallway. He stared at the printed roster taped to the inside door, his finger tracing the line where his name used to sit under "Quarterback," now replaced by "Wide Receiver." The ink looked wet, fresh, mocking him. He grabbed his helmet, the plastic cold and unyielding against his palm, and kicked the bench until his toe throbbed with a sharp, white heat. "I don't run routes," he spat out, his voice low and gravelly, cracking slightly on the last syllable. He didn’t look up when Coach Miller walked by; he just tightened his grip on the ball until his knuckles turned the color of old parchment.
