The whistle blew, shrill and demanding, cutting through the humid afternoon air. Elena dropped her baton, watching it roll into the dirt near the starting line. She wasn’t supposed to be here. She was a sprinter, built for explosive speed, not the endurance grind of the relay anchor leg they had just assigned her. Her jaw clenched so hard she felt a tooth crack. She picked up the baton, wiping the dust off with a trembling hand, refusing to meet the eyes of the girl who had been moved to the second leg. "It’s an experiment," the coach said, smiling brightly. Elena smiled back, tight and brittle, before turning away to stretch her hamstrings, pulling until the muscle screamed in protest.
