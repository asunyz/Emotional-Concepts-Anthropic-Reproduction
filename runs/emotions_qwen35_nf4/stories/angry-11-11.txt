The referee handed him the yellow card, the color stark against his dark uniform. Midfielder. He had spent years mastering the defense, reading plays before they happened. Now, he was expected to dictate the pace, to create opportunities, to lead. He tucked the card into his sock, a secret burden. He jogged onto the field, the grass damp with morning dew soaking through his socks. He passed the ball to a teammate, his touch too hard, forcing the player to chase it down. He didn’t apologize. He just ran, faster than necessary, chasing the wind, trying to outrun the expectations placed upon his shoulders.
