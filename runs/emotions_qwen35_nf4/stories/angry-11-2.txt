Leo sat on the edge of the pool deck, water dripping from his hair onto the concrete. He watched the new swimmer dive in, executing a flawless flip turn that Leo hadn’t mastered yet. He had been told to switch from freestyle to breaststroke because his shoulders were "more versatile" this way. He dipped his head under the surface, holding his breath until his lungs burned, counting the seconds as the pressure built behind his eyes. When he surfaced, gasping, he shook his head violently, sending spray flying across the lane markers. He pulled his goggles tighter, the elastic digging into his temples, creating a throbbing rhythm that matched the pounding in his chest.
