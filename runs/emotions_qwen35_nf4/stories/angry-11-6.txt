Nina tore the page out of the playbook, the paper tearing with a violent rip that sounded like a bone breaking. Forward. She had always played defense, reading the opponent, anticipating moves. Offense meant guessing, hoping, taking risks she wasn’t willing to make. She crumpled the page into a tight ball and threw it at the wall. It stuck there, a white speck against the blue paint. She grabbed her cleats, jamming her feet into them with unnecessary force, lacing them so tightly the leather creaked. She marched onto the field, ignoring the cheers, her eyes fixed on the ground, searching for cracks in the turf.
