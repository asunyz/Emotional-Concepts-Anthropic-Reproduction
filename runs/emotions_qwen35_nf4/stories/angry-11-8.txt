Jessica dropped her tennis racket, the strings snapping with a dull *thwack*. Doubles partner? She preferred the solitude of singles, the ability to control every angle, every shot. Sharing the court meant compromise, communication, relying on someone else’s judgment. She bent down to pick up the broken racket, her fingers brushing the frayed wires. A small cut opened on her thumb, welling up with blood. She didn’t wipe it away. She just stared at the red bead forming, mesmerized by its slow spread. She walked to the baseline, placing her foot precisely on the white line, grounding herself in the one thing she could control.
