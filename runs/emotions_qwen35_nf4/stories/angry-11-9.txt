The locker room was quiet, save for the hum of the ventilation system. Tom sat on his stool, staring at the blank space on the scoreboard projection above the lockers. Left wing. He was a right winger. The geometry of the ice didn’t match his instincts. He stood up abruptly, knocking over his water bottle. The liquid pooled on the tiles, spreading toward the drain. He didn’t move to clean it up. He just stood there, watching the water disappear, feeling a hollow ache in his stomach. He grabbed his stick, checking the curve of the blade, finding nothing wrong with it, everything wrong with the assignment.
