He sat perfectly still, which was unusual for him. Usually, he fidgeted, clicked pens, tapped feet. Today, he was motionless, a coiled spring ready to snap. The delay announcement played on a loop, a cheerful voice detailing technical difficulties. He found himself wishing the plane would explode, just to end the waiting. The thought horrified him, yet it lingered, vivid and bright. He looked at the couple holding hands in the row ahead, their quiet intimacy irritating him deeply. Why weren’t they worried? Why weren’t they fighting? He envied their ignorance. He stood up suddenly, knocking his knee against the tray table. Pain shot up his leg, grounding him. He took a deep, shuddering breath, holding it until his lungs burned, then released it in a slow, controlled stream. He picked up his bag, slinging it over his shoulder, and waited for the next announcement, his eyes narrowed into slits.
