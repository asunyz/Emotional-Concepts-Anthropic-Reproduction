Marcus tapped his foot incessantly, a rapid-fire drumbeat against the floor tiles that seemed to echo in the silence of the lounge. He pulled out his laptop, opened it, and immediately closed it without typing a single word. The screen reflected his face, pale and tense, eyes darting between the clock and the empty runway outside. He reached for his water bottle, unscrewed the cap, and poured the contents onto the carpet when no one was looking. It wasn’t a spill; it was deliberate. He wiped his hands on his trousers, leaving dark stains, and leaned back, crossing his arms tightly across his chest. The woman next to him shifted uncomfortably, pulling her coat closer. Marcus ignored her, staring instead at the departure board with a glare that could have melted steel.
