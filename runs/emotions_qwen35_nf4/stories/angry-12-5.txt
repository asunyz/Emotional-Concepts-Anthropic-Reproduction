She counted the ceiling tiles. One hundred and twelve. She had already counted them three times. Each count added another layer of heat to her cheeks, burning behind her eyes. The man next to her was snoring softly, oblivious to the ticking clock that dictated the fate of her career. She reached out and flicked his shoulder, hard. He jolted awake, confused. "Wake up," she hissed, her voice devoid of warmth. "We’re stuck." He mumbled an apology and rolled over. She turned back to the window, her reflection ghostly against the darkening sky. She imagined pushing past the crowd, forcing her way through the gates, demanding answers from anyone who would listen. But she remained frozen, a statue of impatience, her fingernails biting crescents into her palms.
