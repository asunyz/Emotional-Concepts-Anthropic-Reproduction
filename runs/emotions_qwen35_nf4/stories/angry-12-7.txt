I stared at the flight attendant as she refilled my drink, spilling a drop on the table. I didn’t wipe it up. I let it sit there, a small, sticky reminder of incompetence. "Careful," I said, the words flat and cold. She apologized profusely, her eyes wide with fear. I nodded once, curtly, and looked away. The sound of the engine starting up outside was distant, muted by the double-paned glass. It might as well have been a whisper. I checked my watch again. Six hours. Six more hours of sitting in this metal tube, breathing recycled air, missing the launch of my product. I closed my eyes, trying to block out the ambient noise, but the image of the disappointed faces of my team played on repeat behind my eyelids. I clenched my teeth until they ached.
