Thomas paced the aisle, weaving between seats with aggressive precision. He avoided eye contact with everyone, treating the other passengers as obstacles rather than humans. He stopped at the galley door, rapping his knuckles sharply on the metal. "Status update," he demanded. The flight attendant peeked out, hesitant. "We're waiting for clearance," she said. Thomas nodded slowly, then turned and walked back to his seat, moving with jerky, unnatural motions. He plugged his headphones in, blasting music so loud that the person two rows ahead winced. He didn’t care. He wanted noise to drown out the silence of the delay, the silence of being forgotten. He tapped his finger against the window frame, keeping time with the bass, a frantic, irregular rhythm that matched his heartbeat.
