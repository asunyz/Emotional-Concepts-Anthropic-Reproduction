"I didn't copy anything," I said, my voice low and steady, though my hands shook violently beneath the table. Professor Halloway didn’t look up from her papers, her pen scratching against the page with a sound like tearing paper. I could feel the sweat prickling along my hairline, stinging my eyes. The air in the office felt thin, hard to breathe. I clenched my jaw so tightly it ached, forcing myself to remain seated, to keep my posture rigid despite the urge to stand up and storm out. The fluorescent lights buzzed overhead, a persistent drone that matched the ringing in my ears. I watched dust motes dance in the light beams, counting them to keep my breathing controlled, knowing that if I lost composure now, she’d have all the proof she needed.
