Aisha slammed her locker shut, the metal clang echoing through the deserted hallway. She adjusted her backpack straps, digging her fingers into the fabric until the material stretched taut. The email from the academic integrity board was still open on her tablet, the words blurring slightly as her vision tunneled. She marched toward the administration building, her heels clicking a rapid, aggressive beat against the linoleum. Students parted ways as she passed, sensing the radiation coming off her. She didn’t care. She reached the secretary’s desk and dropped her appeal form on the counter, the paper sliding across the smooth surface with a whisper. "File this," she said, her voice clipped and cold. She turned and walked away before the woman could respond, her stride purposeful, fueled by a fuel source that burned hotter than reason.
