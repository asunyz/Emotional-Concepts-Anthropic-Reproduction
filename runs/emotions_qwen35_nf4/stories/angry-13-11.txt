Tom stared at the peer-review report attached to his submission. *Similarity Index: 85%.* He clicked the link to view the matching sources, expecting to see a common phrase, a standard definition. Instead, he saw entire paragraphs lifted verbatim from a blog post he had read three years ago and forgotten. But he hadn’t copied it. He remembered writing those exact thoughts in his journal at the time. The irony twisted in his gut, a physical knot that tightened with every second. He grabbed his mouse, his grip tight, and began drafting a rebuttal letter. His typing was erratic, hitting keys harder than necessary, breaking the rhythm of normal speech. He didn’t sleep that night. He paced the floorboards, the wood creaking under his restless feet, replaying the argument in his head, sharpening his logic, preparing for the battle he never asked for.
