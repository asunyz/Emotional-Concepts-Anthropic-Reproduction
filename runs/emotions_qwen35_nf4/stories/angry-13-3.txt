The citation manager had failed. That was the only explanation. Sarah scrolled through her bibliography for the hundredth time, her cursor hovering over the broken links. Her teacher’s comment—*This reads exactly like Source B*—sat at the top of the document, bold and unyielding. She bit the inside of her cheek until she tasted copper. The clock on the wall ticked louder with every second, marking the time she had left to appeal before the semester grade locked in. She typed furiously, deleting phrases, rewriting sentences, trying to make the text distinct enough to pass the algorithmic scanner again. Her shoulders hunched forward, protecting her work, her neck stiffening with tension. When she hit send, she leaned back, exhaling a long, shuddering breath that seemed to empty her lungs completely.
