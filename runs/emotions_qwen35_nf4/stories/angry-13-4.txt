"Explain yourself," the proctor demanded, leaning over the desk. Leo kept his eyes fixed on the grain of the wooden surface, refusing to meet the man’s gaze. He could feel the heat rising up his neck, spreading across his cheeks. His leg bounced uncontrollably under the table, a rapid, nervous rhythm that betrayed his calm exterior. He gripped his pencil until the lead snapped, sending a tiny black shard skittering across the exam sheet. He didn’t flinch. He simply picked up another pencil, his movements jerky and abrupt. The smell of old paper and stale coffee filled his nose, making his stomach churn. He wanted to shout, to demand they check the metadata, the timestamps, the history logs, but he swallowed the words down, letting them sit heavy and sour in his throat.
