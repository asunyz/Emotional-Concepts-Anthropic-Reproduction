David walked into the lecture hall late, ignoring the whispers that rippled through the rows of seats as he entered. They knew. Of course they knew. The rumor mill had been spinning since Tuesday. He found his usual seat in the back, dropping his bag onto the floor with a thud that made two students jump. He opened his textbook, but the words swam before his eyes, refusing to coalesce into meaning. His teeth ground together, creating a dull ache in his temples. He watched Professor Evans write equations on the board, the chalk dust floating in the sunbeams like suspended judgment. David’s hand twitched, wanting to reach out and flip the desk, to overturn the order of things, but he forced himself to stay still, to absorb the humiliation like a sponge soaking up water, expanding with every passing minute.
