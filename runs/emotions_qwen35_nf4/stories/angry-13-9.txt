Ryan deleted the folder labeled "Research Notes." Then he deleted the browser history. Then he emptied the recycle bin. He did it quickly, efficiently, driven by a need to erase the trail that others claimed he hadn’t forged. His heart hammered against his sternum, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. He sat in the dark of his bedroom, the blue light of the monitor illuminating his face. He laughed, a short, harsh bark that sounded foreign even to his own ears. It was absurd, really, that he had to destroy his own life’s work to prove he hadn’t taken someone else’s. He rubbed his eyes, smearing the darkness underneath, and leaned back in his chair, listening to the hum of the computer fan, waiting for the inevitable knock on the door that would seal his fate.
