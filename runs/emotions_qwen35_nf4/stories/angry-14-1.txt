I stood in the doorway of her studio, my hand hovering over the frame before I pulled it back. The easel was bare, the paints dried into cracked cakes in their palettes. My chest tightened, a hot coil winding tight beneath my ribs. Why would she leave? After everything we’d built together? I clenched my jaw so hard it ached, forcing myself to breathe slowly. *She knows better than this,* I thought, pacing across the wooden floorboards. The silence in the room was heavy, suffocating. I grabbed a rag and scrubbed at the table, harder and harder, until my fingers stung. She didn’t even have the decency to explain why our partnership ended. I threw the rag onto the floor and stormed out, slamming the gate behind me.
