The stage was dark, the spotlight focused on an empty center circle. Marcus waited, tapping his foot rhythmically against the floorboards. Ten seconds. Twenty. Thirty. The curtain call never came. He checked his watch, then the wings, then the audience. Nobody else seemed to notice the absence. His jaw clenched, teeth grinding audibly. "Play along," he muttered, forcing a smile that felt like a grimace. He bowed anyway, alone, holding the pose until his legs burned. As he walked offstage, he kicked a prop sword aside, sending it clattering into the darkness. He hated the pity in the crowd’s eyes. He hated the silence more. He ripped off his costume pieces in the dressing room, throwing them into a heap, needing to shed the skin of the performer who had been abandoned mid-scene.
