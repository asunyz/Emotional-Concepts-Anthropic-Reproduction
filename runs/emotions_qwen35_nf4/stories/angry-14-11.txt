The inbox showed zero new messages from David since Tuesday. Usually, he replied within minutes, offering advice, critique, encouragement. Today, nothing. Elena refreshed the page, hoping it was a glitch. It wasn’t. She scrolled through their past correspondence, highlighting his supportive comments, his detailed feedback. Then, a gap. A void. Her finger hovered over the mouse, trembling. She closed the laptop, the screen going black, reflecting her furious expression. She stood up and walked to the window, pressing her forehead against the cool glass. How could he just stop? After promising to guide her through the portfolio review? She turned away from the window, grabbing her coat and keys, heading for the door. She was done waiting for explanations that weren't coming. She was going to find him herself.
