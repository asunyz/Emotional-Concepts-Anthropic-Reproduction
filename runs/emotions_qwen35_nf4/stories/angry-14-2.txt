Detective Miller found the badge sitting alone on the corner of the precinct’s main desk. No letter, no farewell party, just the silver shield gleaming under the fluorescent lights. His fists were balled up inside his pockets, nails digging into his palms. How dare he? They had been working the same case for months, relying on each other’s instincts. Miller paced around the desk, running a hand through his thinning hair. "Unbelievable," he muttered, the taste of bile rising in his throat. He picked up the badge, turning it over in his hands, feeling the cold metal bite into his skin. It wasn’t respect; it was abandonment. He slammed the badge down on the desk, leaving a faint smudge on the varnish, and walked away without looking back.
