The email notification pinged on Sarah’s screen, bright and intrusive against the dark mode interface. Subject: Resignation. Effective Immediately. She clicked it open, expecting a reason, a justification, anything. Nothing. Just a generic form letter copied from a corporate template. Her heart hammered against her sternum, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. She typed furiously, deleting the draft three times before closing the laptop lid with a snap. "Typical," she spat, kicking the leg of her desk. He had taken the lead on her project, the one she had poured six months into, and vanished. She stared at her reflection in the black monitor, eyes wide and unblinking, waiting for the anger to subside, but it only grew hotter, burning behind her eyes.
