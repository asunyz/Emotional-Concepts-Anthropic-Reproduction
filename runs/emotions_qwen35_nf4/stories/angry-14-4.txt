Coach Vance left the whistle hanging on the rack, untouched. The locker room was empty, save for the dripping sound of a leaky faucet. Leo kicked a bench, sending it skidding across the concrete floor with a screech that made him wince. "He promised," Leo growled, his voice cracking. "He promised he’d stay until the season was over." He ripped off his jersey, crumpling it into a ball and hurling it into the laundry bin. The fabric bounced off the rim and fell to the ground. He bent down to retrieve it, his breathing shallow and rapid. There was no strategy meeting, no final talk about tactics. Just gone. He shoved the crumpled shirt into his bag, zipping it shut with violent jerks, and marched toward the exit, shoulders hunched high near his ears.
