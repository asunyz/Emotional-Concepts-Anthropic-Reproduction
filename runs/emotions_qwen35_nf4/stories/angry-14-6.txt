The chalkboard still held the complex equations from yesterday’s lecture, but Professor Aris’s office was locked. A small sign taped to the door read: *Closed Indefinitely.* Julian banged on the wood, once, twice, three times, the sound hollow and unanswered. His face flushed red, heat rushing up his neck. This was impossible. They had discussed the thesis topic for weeks, building a foundation together. Now, the foundation was gone. He slid down the wall until he was sitting on the hallway carpet, knees drawn to his chest. He squeezed his eyes shut, trying to block out the sight of the locked door, but the image burned brighter. He bit the inside of his cheek until he tasted copper, refusing to let himself cry, refusing to give the department the satisfaction of seeing him break.
