Maya found the keys to the shared lab left on the counter, next to a half-empty cup of coffee that had gone cold. Dr. Evans was supposed to present their joint findings tomorrow. Instead, there was only silence. Maya’s hands shook as she reached for the phone, dialing the number she knew by heart. It went straight to voicemail. She hung up and dialed again, louder this time, her voice rising with each ring. When it finally stopped, she slammed the phone onto the counter. "After all those late nights?" she hissed, glaring at the empty chair opposite her. She swept the remaining papers off the desk, scattering them across the floor like confetti. She wouldn’t clean them up. Let them lie there, a mess representing the mess he had made of their collaboration.
