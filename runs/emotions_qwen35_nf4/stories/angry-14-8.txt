The workshop smelled of sawdust and unfinished promises. Jack stepped inside, expecting to see his master sanding the latest piece of furniture. Instead, he saw only tools neatly arranged on the workbench, covered by a drop cloth. Jack’s breath hitched. He lifted the cloth, revealing the pristine order. No chaos, no ongoing projects, just sterile perfection. "Where did you go?" he shouted, the echo bouncing off the high ceilings. He grabbed a chisel, his grip tight, and struck a block of wood, splintering it instantly. The pain in his wrist was sharp, grounding. He shouldn’t have trusted him completely. He threw the chisel across the room, where it clattered against the far wall. He stood there, chest heaving, staring at the splintered wood, feeling the weight of betrayal settle heavily in his gut.
