I stared at the acceptance letter on my desk, the gold foil embossed on the envelope catching the light. Beside it lay the resignation notice from my partner, dated yesterday. No conversation, no discussion. Just a piece of paper. My vision blurred, tears pricking at the corners, but I blinked them away furiously. I couldn’t afford to fall apart now. Not when the stakes were this high. I crumpled the resignation letter into a tight ball and tossed it into the trash can, missing the opening on purpose. It landed on the floor. Good. Let it rot there. I stood up abruptly, knocking my chair backward, and began packing my own files into a box. If he wanted to run, fine. But he wasn’t taking my momentum with him.
