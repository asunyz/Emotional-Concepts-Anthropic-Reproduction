Elias stared at the rejection email until the pixels seemed to burn into his retinas. His knuckles turned white as he gripped the edge of his desk, the wood groaning under the pressure. He had seen Clara’s name on the final shortlist three days ago; she was supposed to be the one who spilled coffee on her shirt during the interview. Now, her acceptance letter sat in his inbox, bright green and mocking. He stood up abruptly, his chair screeching against the floor, and kicked the leg of the table. The sharp pain in his shin made him gasp, but he didn’t stop pacing, his jaw clenched so tight it ached.
