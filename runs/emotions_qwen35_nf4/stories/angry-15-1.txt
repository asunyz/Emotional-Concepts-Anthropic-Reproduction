I watched Marcus slide the offer letter across the table to David, their hands brushing in a casual, congratulatory gesture that felt like ice water down my spine. I kept my smile fixed, a rigid mask over teeth I was grinding together. Inside, a hot wire pulled taut behind my ribs, vibrating with every breath. When David laughed, throwing his head back, I excused myself from the room, needing air that tasted less like betrayal. In the hallway, I leaned against the cold wall, closing my eyes and counting backward from ten, trying to dissolve the heat pooling in my chest before anyone noticed the tremor in my hands.
