Liam locked his locker with excessive force, the metal clang reverberating through the hallway. He had done everything right. Better than right. He had exceeded expectations. And yet, Chloe got the role. She was the intern who forgot her laptop twice. Liam ran a hand through his hair, pulling at the roots until it stung. He could feel the heat rising in his cheeks, spreading down his neck. He grabbed his backpack, slinging it over his shoulder, and stormed toward the exit. He didn’t look back at the faces of his colleagues, choosing instead to fixate on the blinking exit sign above the door, using its glow to blind himself to the world behind him.
