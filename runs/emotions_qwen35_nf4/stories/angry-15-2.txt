Sarah threw the resume into the shredder, watching the paper turn into confetti. It wasn’t just about losing the position; it was about how easy it had been for Mark to secure it. She remembered the way he had nodded along during the group exercise, letting her take the lead while he smiled charmingly at the hiring manager. Her phone buzzed—a text from Mark asking if she wanted to grab drinks to celebrate. She typed out a reply, deleted it, then typed another, her thumbs striking the screen with violent force. Finally, she powered off the device and walked out of her apartment, leaving the keys on the counter without looking back.
