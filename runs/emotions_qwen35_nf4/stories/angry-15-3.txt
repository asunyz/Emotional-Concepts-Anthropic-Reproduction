The rain lashed against the windowpane, matching the rhythm of Julian’s tapping foot. He hadn’t slept in two nights since he saw the announcement board. Elena got the role. Of course she did. She was the one who knew all the right people, the one who wore the perfect suit. Julian picked up his pen and snapped it in half, ink bleeding onto his fingers like a dark stain. He wiped it frantically on his pants, leaving a smear across the fabric, unable to focus on anything else. The silence in the room was deafening, filled only by the sound of his own ragged breathing.
