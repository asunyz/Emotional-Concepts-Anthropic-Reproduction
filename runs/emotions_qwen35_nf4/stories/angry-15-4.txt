"You did great," Lisa said, patting my shoulder as we left the building. I forced a nod, keeping my gaze forward, refusing to look at her face. If I looked at her, I might say something I couldn’t take back. My stomach churned, a heavy stone sitting low in my gut. I could feel the blood rushing to my ears, a high-pitched ringing that drowned out her cheerful chatter about networking events. I quickened my pace, putting distance between us, wanting nothing more than to disappear into the crowd where no one could see the flush creeping up my neck.
