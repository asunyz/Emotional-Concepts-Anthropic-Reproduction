David stared at the reflection in the mirror, adjusting his tie for the tenth time. He looked composed, professional, ready. But beneath the starched collar, his heart hammered against his ribs like a trapped bird. He thought of Sarah’s application, the one he had subtly undermined during the second round. The memory of her confused expression flashed in his mind, and he flinched. He tightened his grip on the glass, his reflection distorting under the pressure. He took a deep breath, held it, and exhaled slowly, forcing the tension from his shoulders even as his fists remained clenched at his sides.
