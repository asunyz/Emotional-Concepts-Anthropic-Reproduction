The office was quiet, save for the hum of the server racks. Amara sat alone at her workstation, staring at the empty desk next to hers. That was supposed to be her spot. The one assigned to the new hire, who happened to be her oldest friend. She opened her drawer and pulled out a stress ball, squeezing it until the foam deformed. A small, bitter laugh escaped her lips, dry and humorless. She closed the drawer sharply, the noise echoing in the empty room, and began typing furiously, her keystrokes loud and erratic, as if trying to type away the knot forming in her throat.
