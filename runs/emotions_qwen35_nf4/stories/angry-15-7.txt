I poured the rest of the wine into the sink, the red liquid swirling down the drain like blood. Tom had called me an hour ago, gushing about how "lucky" he felt to have gotten the promotion over me. Lucky. As if it were chance rather than calculated maneuvering. I slammed the bottle down on the counter, the cork popping loose and rolling across the floor. I didn’t pick it up. Instead, I leaned my forehead against the cool refrigerator door, sliding down until I hit the floor, wrapping my arms around my knees. The silence of the house pressed in on me, heavy and suffocating.
