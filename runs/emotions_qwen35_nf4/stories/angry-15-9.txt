Maya placed the rejection letter on the table, smoothing it out with precise, deliberate movements. Her hands were steady, betraying nothing. Inside, however, a storm raged. She remembered the way Daniel had winked at her during the interview, a secret signal that now felt like a trap. She had played along, thinking it was camaraderie. Now, he was the senior associate, and she was still waiting for a response from HR. Maya packed her bag, zipping it shut with a finality that echoed in the room. She walked out without saying goodbye, leaving the door open behind her.
