Elias’s knuckles turned white as he gripped the stem of his champagne flute, the glass trembling slightly against the noise of the reception hall. He watched Julian laugh at something the bride said, that same easy, disarming grin that had once made Elias forget how to breathe, now directed at someone else. Every time Julian moved across the room, Elias felt a hot prickle crawl up his neck, making his skin feel too tight for his body. He took a long, deliberate sip of wine, letting the acidity burn his throat, grounding himself in the sharp taste while his eyes tracked Julian’s every step. When their gazes finally locked across the crowded dance floor, Elias didn’t look away; instead, he raised his glass in a slow, mocking toast before setting it down with a forceful clink that echoed louder than intended.
