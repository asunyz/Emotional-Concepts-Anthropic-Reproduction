Sara couldn’t believe her luck seeing Jake standing there, adjusting his cufflinks with that familiar arrogance. A wave of nausea rolled through her, sour and bitter. She leaned against the wall, sliding down slightly until she was half-hidden by a potted fern. Her hands shook, not from fear, but from the suppressed energy coiling in her limbs. She imagined kicking the pot over, toppling the plant, making a scene that would disrupt the entire evening. Instead, she closed her eyes, taking deep, measured breaths, counting backward from ten. When she opened them, she smoothed her skirt, lifted her chin, and walked past him without breaking stride, her expression unreadable, her pulse roaring in her ears like a waterfall.
