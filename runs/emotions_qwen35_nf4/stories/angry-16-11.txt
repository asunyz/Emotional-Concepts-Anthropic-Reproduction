Michael saw Laura laughing at a toast, her head tilted back, throat exposed. It triggered a primal instinct to protect what was once his, twisted into a corrosive envy. He crushed the ice cube in his tumbler, listening to the crackle of destruction. The ice melted quickly, turning to water, much like the promises they had exchanged. He stared at the melting cubes, fascinated by their demise, until the glass was empty. He slammed the glass down, the sound sharp and final, causing heads to turn. He didn’t care. He gathered his coat, moving with purposeful speed toward the entrance, leaving the warmth of the hall for the cold uncertainty outside, his face a mask of stone, untouched by the festivities within.
