The air in the banquet hall felt thick, suffocatingly warm, as Marcus scanned the crowd for David. Spotting him near the bar, Marcus’s stomach dropped, a hollow ache replacing the warmth of the room. He adjusted his tie, pulling the silk knot tighter until it choked off his breath, mirroring the constriction in his chest. David was holding court, head thrown back in laughter, completely unaware of the storm brewing ten feet away. Marcus stepped forward, then stopped, his boots scuffing the carpet. He crossed his arms tightly over his chest, creating a barrier between himself and the memory of those hands that had once held him. With a sharp exhale through his nose, he turned on his heel and walked toward the exit, leaving the music behind, unable to bear the sound of David’s voice echoing in his mind.
