Arthur watched Sarah dance with the groom’s brother, her movements fluid and carefree. It made his teeth grind together, a grinding sensation that sent pain shooting through his molars. He drained his beer in one go, the emptiness of the can in his hand mirroring the void left in his apartment since she moved out. He wanted to throw the can at the wall, shatter the silence, break the perfect facade of this celebration. Instead, he crushed the aluminum slowly, deliberately, flattening it under his palm until the metal groaned. He tossed the flattened disc onto a nearby tray, the metallic scrape loud enough to draw a glance from the waiter. Arthur ignored it, his eyes fixed on Sarah’s laughing profile, burning holes into the image despite himself.
