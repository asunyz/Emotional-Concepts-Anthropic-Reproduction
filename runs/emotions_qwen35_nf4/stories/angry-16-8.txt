Elena spotted Thomas near the restrooms and froze mid-step, her foot hovering above the pavement. The urge to confront him surged, violent and sudden, demanding release. She bit the inside of her cheek until she tasted copper, using the sting to anchor herself. He looked tired, darker circles under his eyes than she remembered, but the effect on her was visceral. She straightened her spine, lifting her chin high, projecting an air of icy indifference. When he called out her name, she didn’t stop, didn’t acknowledge the sound. She simply tightened her grip on her purse strap, pulling it higher, closer to her shoulder, as if preparing for a fight she would never engage in. Her pace quickened, her heels clicking sharply against the stone, a rhythmic declaration of her departure.
