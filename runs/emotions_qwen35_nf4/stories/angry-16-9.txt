Watching Ryan hold the microphone, singing a love song dedicated to his new fiancée, made Noah’s blood boil. He set his drink down with such force that liquid splashed onto the tablecloth, dark stains spreading like bruises. He clenched his jaw, the tension visible in the lines etched around his mouth. Every lyric felt like a needle, poking at old wounds he thought had healed. He stood up abruptly, chair legs screeching against the floor, drawing a few curious glances which he ignored. He walked toward the terrace doors, pushing them open with aggressive yanks, stepping out into the cool night air to scream silently into the darkness, his chest heaving, lungs desperate for oxygen that wouldn’t come.
