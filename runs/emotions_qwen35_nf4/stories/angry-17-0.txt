Elias stared at the LinkedIn profile he had just pulled up on his phone, the screen glare burning into his retinas. The name matched perfectly, yet the title read "Junior Data Analyst" instead of the "Senior Project Lead" Marcus had boasted about over dinner that very morning. His thumb hovered over the call button, trembling slightly before he locked the device and shoved it deep into his pocket. He walked out of the café, the heavy door slamming shut behind him with enough force to rattle the windows. The air outside was crisp, biting at his exposed neck, but he didn’t feel the cold; he only felt a hot, prickly heat rising up the back of his throat. He marched down the sidewalk, his boots striking the pavement with deliberate, heavy thuds, each step a silent accusation against the man who had treated him like an audience for his fabricated success.
