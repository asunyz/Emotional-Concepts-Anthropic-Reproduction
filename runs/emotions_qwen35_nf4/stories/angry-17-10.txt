Sarah stared at the invoice on her desk, the numbers refusing to align with the story David had told her over lunch. He claimed his construction firm was behind on payments due to supply chain issues. Yet, she had seen the new truck parked outside his house, gleaming and brand new. She closed her eyes, taking a slow, deliberate breath, counting to ten to keep her voice level. When she opened them, her gaze was sharp, dissecting. She picked up her phone and dialed the dealership listed on the vehicle registration she had accidentally glanced at earlier. She listened to the automated menu, her other hand tapping a restless rhythm on the desk. The waiting music grated on her nerves, amplifying the irritation bubbling beneath her surface. She ended the call, placing the phone down with a deliberate thud. She wouldn't confront him today; she would gather more evidence, letting the knowledge fester until it became impossible to ignore.
