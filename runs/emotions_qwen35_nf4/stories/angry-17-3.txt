My hands shook as I scrolled through the photos on Mark’s Instagram. There he was, holding a trophy at what he called his company’s annual gala. The caption boasted about leading the winning team. But zooming in, I recognized the background. It was the community center gym where I volunteered on weekends. The "trophy" was a participation award from the youth basketball league. A wave of nausea hit me, cold and slick. I threw my phone onto the couch cushion, burying my face in my hands. My heart hammered against my ribs like a trapped bird, frantic and desperate to escape. I could hear the muffled sounds of TV from the next room, oblivious to the fracture forming in our friendship. I wanted to throw something, to shatter the illusion he had built, but I remained frozen, paralyzed by the sheer weight of the deception sitting heavily in my gut.
