Lena sat across from Tom, watching him gesture wildly as he described his latest business venture. Her fork rested untouched on her plate, the pasta growing cold and congealing around the tines. She listened, nodding occasionally, while inside, a fire burned in her chest, consuming her composure. She took a sip of wine, the liquid bitter on her tongue, and set the glass down with a clink that seemed too loud. "That sounds impressive," she said, the words tasting like ash. She excused herself, walking quickly to the restroom, locking the stall door behind her. Leaning against the cool metal of the sink, she splashed water on her face, trying to wash away the sting in her eyes. She stared at her reflection, seeing the tightness in her lips, the dark circles under her eyes. She wasn't crying; she was seething, a simmering rage that threatened to boil over if she returned to that table.
