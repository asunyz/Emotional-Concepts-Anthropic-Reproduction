Kevin found the letter in his brother’s coat pocket while hanging up winter gear. It was an eviction notice, dated three months ago. Kevin had always believed his brother was thriving, paying rent on a penthouse downtown. Now, holding the crumpled paper, he felt the ground beneath him shift. He walked to the kitchen, pouring himself a glass of whiskey without measuring. He drank it in one gulp, the burn doing little to soothe the icy dread spreading through his veins. When his brother walked in, smiling and asking about dinner plans, Kevin didn't answer immediately. He leaned against the counter, arms crossed tightly over his chest, guarding his own instability. His eyes narrowed, fixed on his brother’s cheerful expression. The air in the room grew thin, hard to breathe. He waited for the right moment to speak, knowing that once he did, nothing would ever be the same.
