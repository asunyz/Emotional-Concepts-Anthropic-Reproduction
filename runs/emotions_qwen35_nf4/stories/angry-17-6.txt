Rachel tore the brochure apart, her fingers ripping through the glossy pages of the travel agency package she had booked for her colleague, Amy. Amy had insisted she needed a vacation to recover from "exhaustion due to corporate demands." Rachel knew Amy hadn’t taken a day off in two years, working late nights from her apartment. The realization struck her like a physical blow, knocking the wind out of her. She crumpled the paper into a tight ball, tossing it into the trash bin with violent precision. She paced her office, the fluorescent lights buzzing overhead, irritating her senses. She grabbed her phone, dialing Amy’s number. When the line rang, she held it to her ear, listening to the voicemail greeting, her breathing shallow and fast. She hung up without leaving a message, her mind racing, constructing sentences that were sharp and unforgiving, ready to dismantle the carefully constructed facade her friend had maintained.
