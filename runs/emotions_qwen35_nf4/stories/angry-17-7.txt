I slammed the laptop shut, the sudden noise echoing in the empty apartment. Jason had told me he was freelancing, working flexible hours from home. Yet here was proof, buried in the shared cloud drive he thought was private—emails addressed to his actual employer, a temp agency, regarding his placement at a warehouse. The betrayal tasted metallic in my mouth. I stood up, kicking the leg of the desk in frustration, wincing as pain shot up my shin. I ran a hand through my hair, pulling at the roots until they stung. I couldn't sit still. I moved from the bedroom to the living room, then to the kitchen, unable to find a spot that offered relief. The image of his confident lies played on repeat in my mind, each memory adding fuel to the inferno raging within me. I grabbed a stress ball from the shelf and squeezed it until my hand cramped, needing some outlet for the energy coiling in my muscles.
