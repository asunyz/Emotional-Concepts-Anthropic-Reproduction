Marcus watched Elena pack her bag, her movements efficient and hurried. She had spent the evening recounting the details of a charity event she had hosted, describing the donors and the venue with vivid detail. But Marcus knew the venue because he had cleaned it that afternoon as part of his side gig. He said nothing, allowing her to finish her story, but his grip on his coffee mug tightened until the ceramic groaned. As she reached for the door handle, he spoke, his voice steady but edged with steel. "Did you enjoy the event?" he asked. Elena froze, her hand hovering over the knob. The color drained from her face, visible even in the dim hallway light. Marcus didn't move, remaining seated, his posture rigid. He waited, watching the panic begin to take hold, the silence stretching out as the truth hung heavy between them, undeniable and stark.
