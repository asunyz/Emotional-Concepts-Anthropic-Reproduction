The text message glowed on my screen, bright and accusatory. *Just got promoted! Can’t wait to celebrate.* Sent by Ben, the guy who had borrowed fifty dollars from me last week claiming his check was delayed. I knew his check hadn’t come through; I saw the bank app notification on his shared family account we both had access to. A hot flush crept up my neck, spreading to my cheeks. I typed a response, deleted it, then typed again. My fingers flew across the keyboard, driven by a need to confront the lie directly. I stormed out of the house, the night air chilling my sweat-dampened skin. I drove aimlessly, tires screeching around corners, the engine roaring in protest. I parked by the river, gripping the steering wheel, head bowed. The injustice of it all sat like a stone in my stomach, heavy and immovable, demanding acknowledgment.
