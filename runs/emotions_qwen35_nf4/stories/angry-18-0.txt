Elias stared at the glowing screen, his knuckles white as he gripped the edge of the kitchen counter until the wood creaked beneath his pressure. He watched the clock tick past midnight, the second hand sweeping with a rhythmic, maddening precision that seemed to mock him. When Sarah finally walked in, her eyes darting away from his gaze, he didn’t speak; he simply turned his back, forcing her to see only the rigid line of his shoulders. The silence in the room was heavy, suffocating, broken only by the sharp intake of breath she took when she realized he knew about the late-night calls to an unknown number. He picked up his coffee mug, the ceramic hot against his palm, and set it down with a forceful clatter that made her flinch.
