My hands trembled as I scrolled through the call log, each entry a small dagger twisting in my gut. I wanted to scream, to throw something, to shatter the fragile peace we had built over three years, but instead, I sat perfectly still, letting the heat rise in my chest until it felt like swallowing embers. When Mark asked if everything was okay, his voice laced with feigned concern, I laughed—a short, brittle sound that echoed off the bare walls. "Nothing," I said, the word clipped and cold. I stood up abruptly, knocking my chair back, and walked out of the room without looking at him, leaving him standing there in the wreckage of our trust.
