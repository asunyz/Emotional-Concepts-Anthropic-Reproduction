I traced the rim of my wine glass, watching the red liquid swirl, mirroring the chaos in my mind. The voicemail played in my head on repeat, her voice soft, intimate, belonging to another man. I took a sip, the wine bitter and acidic, coating my tongue in a taste that matched my mood. When James came home, whistling a cheerful tune, I didn’t turn around. I kept my eyes fixed on the swirling wine, listening to his footsteps approach. He placed a hand on my shoulder, expecting a hug, but I shrugged it off sharply, standing up and walking toward the balcony, leaving him standing alone in the dimly lit living room.
