The list of numbers was spread out on the coffee table, a stark contrast to the cozy rug beneath it. Rachel stared at them, her vision tunneling until nothing else existed. Her heart pounded against her ribs like a trapped bird, desperate to escape the cage of her composure. When Tom tried to take the paper away, she slapped his hand, the sting radiating up her arm. "Don't touch me," she snapped, her voice rising to a shout that startled the cat off the sofa. She gathered the papers, crumpling them into a tight ball, and threw them at him, watching with grim satisfaction as they hit his chest and fell to the floor.
