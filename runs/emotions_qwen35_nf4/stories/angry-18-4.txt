Sarah’s laughter was loud, unfiltered, and directed at a voice on the other end of the line hidden behind the bathroom door. Tom waited outside, his foot tapping a rapid, erratic rhythm against the tile floor. Every second that passed felt like an hour, stretching the tension thin until it was ready to tear. When she emerged, humming a tune, he blocked her path, his expression unreadable but his jaw clenched so tight a muscle twitched near his ear. He didn’t ask where she had been; he simply stared at her, his eyes narrowing until they were slits of pure disapproval. She shifted uncomfortably, backing away, but he stepped forward, closing the distance until she was pressed against the wall.
