The text message glowed on my laptop screen, bright and accusatory in the dark room. I read it again, then again, the words blurring together as rage bubbled up from my stomach, rising in my throat like bile. I slammed the laptop shut, the impact shaking the desk and sending a pen rolling onto the floor. My breathing was shallow, rapid, controlled only by sheer willpower. When Elena walked in, asking why I was sitting in the dark, I didn’t answer. I just pointed to the closed laptop, my finger trembling slightly, before turning away to stare out the window at the city lights, refusing to give her the satisfaction of seeing my pain.
