Michael threw the receipt onto the dining table, the paper sliding across the polished surface to stop inches from his wife’s plate. He leaned in close, his face inches from hers, his breath hot and fast. "Explain this," he growled, the words spitting out of his mouth like shards of glass. Linda opened her mouth to respond, but he cut her off with a wave of his hand, his eyes blazing with a fury that made her shrink back in her chair. He stood up abruptly, kicking his chair backward, and began to pace, running both hands through his hair, pulling at the roots as if trying to extract the frustration physically from his skull.
