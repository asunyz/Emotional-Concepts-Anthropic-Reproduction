The phone rang again, the shrill tone slicing through the quiet dinner. David ignored it, but his hand kept drifting toward it, checking the screen every few seconds. Anna noticed, her fork hovering halfway to her mouth, the food going cold on her plate. She set it down with a deliberate clink, her lips pressing into a thin line. "Who is it?" she asked, her voice steady but devoid of warmth. When he hesitated, she stood up, her chair scraping loudly against the floor, and walked over to snatch the device from the table. She didn’t even unlock it; she just held it up, her expression hardening into stone.
