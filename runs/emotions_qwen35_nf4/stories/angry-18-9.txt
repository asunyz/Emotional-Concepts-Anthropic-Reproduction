Kevin slammed the door so hard the pictures on the hallway wall rattled. He stood in the center of the room, chest heaving, fists balled at his sides. He wanted to destroy something, anything, but he forced himself to stand still, grounding himself against the overwhelming urge to lash out. When Lisa entered, asking what was wrong, he shook his head violently, refusing to engage. He walked past her, brushing against her shoulder with enough force to make her stumble, and headed straight for the bedroom, locking the door behind him with a definitive click that echoed through the house.
