The email notification pinged on Sarah’s phone while she was stirring soup on the stove. She wiped her hands on her apron, ignoring the steam rising from the pot, and tapped the screen. It was a message from Mr. Henderson, her son’s new math tutor. *Subject: Regarding your previous complaints.* Sarah froze. The spoon slipped from her fingers, clattering onto the floor. She stared at the screen, her breath hitching in her throat. Her vision blurred slightly, then sharpened with intense focus. She grabbed her keys, shoving them into her pocket with violent precision. When she arrived at the tutoring center, she didn’t wait for an appointment. She burst through the front door, scanning the room until she spotted him. "You knew," she accused, pointing a trembling finger at him. "You knew I hated this place." Her words came out sharp, jagged edges cutting through the quiet hum of the library.
