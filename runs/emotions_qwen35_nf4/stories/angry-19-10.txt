The bell rang, signaling the end of the day. James lingered by the gate, watching the children flood out. He scanned faces, looking for the one he dreaded seeing. When Leo appeared, walking alongside Mr. Davis, James felt his blood boil. He intercepted them, stepping into their path. Leo looked up, startled. Mr. Davis frowned, adjusting his glasses. "Can I help you?" Davis asked politely. James ignored the politeness. "Stay away from him," he growled, his voice rough with suppressed rage. He grabbed Leo’s backpack strap, pulling the boy close. "He doesn’t know what he’s doing," James continued, glaring at Davis. "He’s playing games. And I’m going to make sure he stops."
