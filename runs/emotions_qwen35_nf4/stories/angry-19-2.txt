David paced the length of the hallway, his polished shoes clicking rhythmically against the linoleum. Each step was measured, heavy, deliberate. He stopped outside Room 304, gripping the door handle so hard his knuckles turned white. Through the glass pane, he saw Mr. Lewis high-fiving a group of students. The sight made David’s stomach churn, a hot, acidic sensation spreading through his chest. He twisted the handle and walked in without waiting for acknowledgment. Mr. Lewis looked up, smiling instinctively before recognizing the storm cloud behind David’s eyes. The smile vanished instantly. David didn’t offer a greeting. He simply placed a printed copy of the old disciplinary report on the desk. "Explain this," he demanded, his voice devoid of inflection, cold as ice. He crossed his arms, creating a barrier between himself and the man who had once held his daughter’s future in his hands.
