Maya dropped her bag on the kitchen counter, the thud echoing in the empty house. She pulled out her phone and scrolled through the class roster again, just to be sure. There it was. *Ms. Alvarez*. The name that had haunted her nightmares last year. Maya felt her heart hammer against her ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. She clenched her fists until her nails dug crescents into her palms. Why here? Why now? She couldn’t breathe properly, the air feeling too thin in her lungs. She stormed out of the house, leaving the door wide open behind her. At the school, she bypassed the office entirely and went straight to the staff lounge. Ms. Alvarez was there, sipping coffee. Maya didn’t say hello. She just stood there, radiating a palpable tension, forcing the teacher to look up and meet her furious gaze.
