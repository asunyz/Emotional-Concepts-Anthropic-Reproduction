"Is this a joke?" Marcus asked, the question hanging in the air like smoke. He stood in the doorway of the art studio, one foot still on the threshold, refusing to fully enter. The canvas behind the easel bore the signature of Mr. Thorne, the artist who had ruined his sister’s portfolio review two years ago. Marcus’s lip curled, a sneer forming involuntarily. He stepped inside, the floorboards creaking under his weight. He didn’t look at the art; he looked directly at Thorne, who was wiping paint off his hands. "I hope you’re prepared to answer for every single mistake you made," Marcus said, his voice rising with each word. He began pacing around the room, agitated, unable to stand still. Every movement was jerky, erratic, betraying the turmoil churning within him.
