Jennifer read the letter three times. The ink seemed to bleed into the paper, distorting the words until they were nothing but black scars on white page. *Dear Parent, please discuss the behavioral issues...* She crumpled the paper in her fist, the sound loud in the silent car. Her teeth ground together, a grinding noise audible even to herself. She threw the ball of paper onto the passenger seat and started the engine, the roar of the motor matching the surge of adrenaline in her veins. She drove to the school with reckless speed, tires squealing as she pulled into the drop-off lane. She didn’t get out immediately. She sat there, gripping the steering wheel until her forearms ached, staring at the brick facade of the school as if she could burn a hole through it with sheer willpower.
