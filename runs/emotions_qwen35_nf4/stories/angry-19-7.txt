Lena stared at the photograph on the bulletin board. It was taken last week. Her son, smiling broadly, arm draped around the shoulders of Coach Miller. Lena felt a spike of heat rise up her neck, flushing her cheeks red. She remembered the incident at the park, the cruel words Miller had shouted, the way he had singled out her son for humiliation. She tore the photo off the board, the tape ripping with a harsh sound. She marched to the gymnasium, where Miller was blowing a whistle. She didn’t shout. She didn’t cry. She just walked onto the court, stopping inches from him. "Apologize," she whispered, the volume of her voice dropping to a dangerous whisper. "Now." She held his gaze, unblinking, daring him to look away.
