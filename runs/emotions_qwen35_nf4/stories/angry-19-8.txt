The meeting was scheduled for 4 PM. Robert arrived at 3:55. He sat in the waiting area, reading a magazine upside down, his leg bouncing rapidly. The nurse called his name, and he shot up from the chair as if electrified. He didn’t shake the doctor’s hand. He slid into the exam room and closed the door, locking it. Dr. Evans looked up, surprised by the sudden confinement. Robert placed his medical file on the table, slamming it down. "You treated my brother," Robert stated, not asking. "And you failed him." He leaned forward, invading the doctor’s space, his eyes wide and unyielding. "Tell me exactly what went wrong. Don’t skip a detail."
