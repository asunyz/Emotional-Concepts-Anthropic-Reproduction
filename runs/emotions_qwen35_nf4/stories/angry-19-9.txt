Anya scrolled through the social media feed, her thumb moving faster than usual. Then she stopped. A post from her daughter’s school account featured a picture of the debate team. In the background, judging panel, sat Mr. Kline. Anya’s breath caught. She remembered the false accusations Kline had leveled against her ex-husband, which had cost them custody. She deleted the app, then reinstalled it, determined to confront the source. She drove to the school, parking illegally in front of the main entrance. She didn’t bother with the visitor badge. She walked past security, her chin held high, her pace steady and unstoppable. When she found Kline in the faculty room, she didn’t speak. She just placed a folder on the table containing evidence of his misconduct. "Read it," she commanded, her voice steady despite the shaking of her hands.
