The pen snapped in Elias’s grip, ink bleeding over his fingers like a fresh wound. He glared at the piece of paper on his drafting table, the charcoal smudges blurring into a menacing shape. It was *him*. The Shadow Man. The imaginary companion who had followed him from kindergarten to college, always lurking in the periphery, always watching. Now, thanks to his niece’s innocent curiosity, the shadow had a face. A face that matched the man who had disappeared ten years ago. Elias stood up abruptly, his chair scraping loudly against the hardwood floor. He walked to the window, gripping the sill until his fingers went numb. Outside, the rain lashed against the glass, mirroring the storm raging within him. He felt a primal urge to smash the window, to shatter the barrier between the safe interior and the chaotic exterior. Instead, he closed his eyes and counted to ten, then twenty, trying to steady his shaking hands. The fear was there, yes, but beneath it lay a seething resentment toward the child who had unknowingly pulled the trigger on his demons.
