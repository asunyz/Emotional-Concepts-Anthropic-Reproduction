She slammed the laptop shut, the lid striking her chin with a stinging slap that she welcomed for the distraction. The email from her sister attached the photo without warning. No preamble. Just the image. The creature with the spider legs and the human face. *Legs*. The name surfaced from the depths of her memory, bringing with it a wave of nausea. She rushed to the bathroom, gripping the sides of the sink until her knuckles blanched white. She splashed cold water on her face, but it did nothing to cool the fire burning in her gut. She glared at her reflection, seeing the distortion of her expression—the bared teeth, the wide, wild eyes. How dare they? How dare they bring Legs back into the conversation? He was supposed to stay in the attic of her mind, dusty and forgotten. Now he was front and center, projected onto a digital screen, viewed by strangers. She gripped a towel, wringing it out with such force that the fabric groaned. She would have a word with her sister. A very final word.
