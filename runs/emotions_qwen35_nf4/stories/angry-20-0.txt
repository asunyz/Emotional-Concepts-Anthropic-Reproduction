Elias slammed his briefcase onto the desk, the impact echoing sharply in the empty classroom. His jaw was set so hard it ached, a familiar tension radiating up into his temples. He stared at the wrapped box sitting innocuously on the corner of his grading pile. It was too neat, too deliberate for something sent by someone he had spent the last three years trying to ignore. With a sharp exhale through his nose, he grabbed a letter opener and sliced the tape with violent precision, tearing the paper as if it were an adversary rather than packaging.
