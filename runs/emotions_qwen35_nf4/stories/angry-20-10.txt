The box sat on the bus seat next to her, heavy and silent. Elena gripped the handle of her bag tighter, her arm muscles tense. She hadn’t asked for this. She hadn’t requested contact. And yet, here was the token from her former student, Mark, who had struggled with reading since kindergarten. The book inside was annotated with her old comments, now highlighted in bright yellow. She felt the urge to throw it out the window, to let the wind take it away. Instead, she kept it close, her body angled away from it, creating a barrier between herself and the memory of failure she had helped erase.
