James laughed, a dry, humorless sound that echoed in the empty hallway. He shook his head, disbelief warring with irritation. Another gift? From whom? He tore open the wrapper, revealing a custom-made bookmark with his name engraved on it. It was tacky. It was unnecessary. It was a demand for acknowledgment he refused to give. He snapped the bookmark in half, the plastic cracking loudly. He swept the pieces into his pocket, straightened his jacket, and walked out of the school, leaving the rest of the wrapping paper fluttering on the floor like discarded skin.
