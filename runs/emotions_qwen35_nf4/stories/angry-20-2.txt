Marcus kicked the leg of his chair, the wood scraping harshly against the linoleum floor. He paced the length of his small apartment, the unopened envelope burning a hole in his pocket. He had promised himself he wouldn’t look. He had promised himself he would move on, that Sarah’s departure was just another chapter closed. Yet here it was, delivered by a courier who looked at him with pitying eyes. He crumpled the paper in his fist, the sound loud in the quiet room, before throwing it onto the kitchen counter where it lay there, mocking his resolve.
