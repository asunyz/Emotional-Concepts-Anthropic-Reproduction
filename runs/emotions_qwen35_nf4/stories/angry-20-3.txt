She traced the edge of the card with a trembling finger, her breath coming in short, shallow gasps. The ink was still wet, smudging under her touch. She wanted to scream, to hurl the object across the room until it shattered against the far wall. Instead, she stood perfectly still, her spine rigid, staring at the words "Thank you for seeing me when I couldn't see myself." Her lips pressed into a thin line, devoid of color, while her nails dug crescents into her palms. The silence in the office was deafening, amplifying the thud of her heart against her ribs.
