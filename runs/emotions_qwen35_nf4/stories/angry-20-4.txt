David threw the box onto the grass outside the school gates, watching it bounce once before settling among the dandelions. He adjusted his tie, pulling it tight enough to restrict his breathing, grounding himself in the physical discomfort. The note inside had been simple, devoid of apology or explanation, which somehow made it worse. He turned away, marching back toward the building with stiff, jerky steps, refusing to look back at the greenery. His face was a mask of stone, but his clenched fists betrayed the storm raging beneath the surface.
