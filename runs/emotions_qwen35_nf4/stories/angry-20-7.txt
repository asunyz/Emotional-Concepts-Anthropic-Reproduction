He didn’t say a word when he saw the package on his doorstep. He just stared at it, his eyes narrowing until they were slits. The rain began to fall, soaking his hair, but he didn’t move to get an umbrella. He waited until the delivery driver left before picking it up. Inside was a report card from five years ago, the one he had forged to hide his truancy. Seeing it again made his stomach churn, a knot of shame and rage tightening in his gut. He walked into his living room and tossed it onto the fire, watching the flames consume the paper, feeling no satisfaction, only a hollow ache.
