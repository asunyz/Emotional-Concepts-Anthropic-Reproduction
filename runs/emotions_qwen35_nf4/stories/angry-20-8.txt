Sarah slammed the door behind her, locking it with a decisive click. She marched to her bedroom and threw the gift onto her bed. It was a framed photo of their class trip, taken without her knowledge. She hated how happy everyone looked, how inclusive the moment seemed despite her absence. She grabbed the frame, ready to smash it against the headboard, but paused. The glass reflected her distorted face, twisted and furious. She lowered it slowly, setting it upright on the nightstand, forcing herself to breathe through the nausea rising in her throat.
