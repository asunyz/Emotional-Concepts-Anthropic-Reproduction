Tom read the letter twice, then a third time, his eyes scanning every syllable for hidden meaning. There was none. Just gratitude. Pure, unadulterated thanks for a lesson plan he had barely remembered creating. He folded the paper with meticulous care, creasing it sharply along the center. He wasn’t grateful back. He felt nothing but a cold detachment, a wall he had built brick by brick over the years. He slid the letter into his drawer, locking it away. The act was mechanical, precise, devoid of warmth. He returned to his work, his pen moving faster, sharper, as if writing itself into a frenzy.
