Arthur placed the letter on the mantlepiece, aligning it perfectly with the frame of the photograph. His hands were steady, eerily calm compared to the storm raging inside his chest. He lit a cigarette, the smoke curling up toward the ceiling, obscuring the view. He exhaled slowly, watching the gray cloud dissipate. The letter was an insult disguised as kindness, a reminder that some wounds never truly heal, only scar over. He took another drag, the ember glowing bright orange in the dim light. He stubbed out the cigarette in an ashtray that was already overflowing, pressing down until the tobacco burst. He looked at the letter, then at the photo, then back at the letter. He didn’t throw it away. He just left it there, a monument to his refusal to forgive, a silent challenge hanging in the air.
