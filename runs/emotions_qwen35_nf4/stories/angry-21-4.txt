David sat at the desk, the letter spread out before him like evidence in a trial he hadn’t agreed to preside over. His pen hovered above the blotter, ink pooling at the tip. He wanted to write something vicious, something that would sting back with equal force. But his hand shook, not from fear, but from a rage so potent it vibrated in his teeth. He slammed the pen down, cracking the plastic casing. The sound echoed in the silent office. He stood up abruptly, knocking his chair backward. It clattered to the floor, legs splayed. He walked to the window, pressing his forehead against the cool glass, watching the rain lash against the pavement. Inside, his heart hammered against his ribs like a trapped bird, frantic and desperate to escape the cage of his own restraint.
