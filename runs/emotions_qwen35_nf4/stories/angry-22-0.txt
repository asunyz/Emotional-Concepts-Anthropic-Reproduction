Elias slammed his coffee mug onto the desk, the ceramic clattering against the laminate with enough force to rattle the pens in the holder nearby. He stared at the spreadsheet before him, his jaw clenched so tight it throbbed behind his ears. "You missed the deadline," he snapped, his voice low and sharp as shattered glass. Sarah flinched, her eyes widening as she shuffled her papers nervously. "I know, I’m sorry, but—" Elias cut her off, standing up abruptly. His chair scraped violently against the floor, a harsh screech that made the office silence feel heavier. He paced toward the window, hands shoved deep into his pockets, knuckles white from gripping his own arms. When he turned back, his face was flushed, eyes burning with a intensity that made Sarah instinctively step back. "Next time, check your calendar," he said, the words clipped and cold, before turning away to hide the tremor in his hands.
