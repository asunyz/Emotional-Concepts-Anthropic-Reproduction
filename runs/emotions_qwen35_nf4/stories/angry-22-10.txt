Sarah slammed the folder onto the desk, scattering loose pages everywhere. Dust motes danced in the shafts of sunlight that pierced the blinds, illuminating the chaos she had created. "Look at this," she pointed at a specific clause, her finger jabbing the paper repeatedly. "This is negligence." Her boss, Mr. Henderson, sighed, rubbing his temples. "Sarah, please lower your voice. We have clients in the next room." "Let them listen," she shot back, her heart pounding against her ribs like a trapped bird. She grabbed a handful of papers, shuffling them frantically, searching for the smoking gun. "They need to see this," she insisted, her voice cracking under the strain. Tears pricked at the corners of her eyes, but she blinked them away furiously, refusing to let them fall. Anger was a shield, and she would wear it until the job was done.
