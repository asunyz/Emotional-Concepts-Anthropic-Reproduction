She didn’t say anything at first, just stared at the old, faded photo taped to the cubicle wall. Her lips pressed into a thin line, disappearing entirely. "Camp Pine Ridge?" she asked, her voice devoid of warmth. Tom nodded, expecting a smile, maybe a shared laugh about the terrible food or the endless bug bites. Instead, she closed her laptop with a definitive click. The sound was final, shutting him out. She began packing her bag slowly, deliberately, taking her time to ensure every item was perfectly aligned. "I thought you were different," she said, the words hanging in the air like smoke. Tom opened his mouth to protest, but she held up a hand, stopping him mid-sentence. Her eyes were hard, unyielding stones. She walked past him without another glance, leaving him alone with the echo of her footsteps fading down the hallway.
