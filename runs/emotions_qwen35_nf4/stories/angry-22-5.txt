Rain lashed against the conference room windows, mirroring the turmoil inside Rachel’s head. She sat at the head of the table, pen poised over her notebook, but she hadn’t written a single word. Across from her, David was talking about synergy and market trends, his voice droning on. She remembered the campfire songs they used to sing, the way they used to hold hands during thunderstorms. Now, he wouldn’t even meet her gaze. "David," she interrupted, slamming her pen down. The ink bled through the page, a dark stain spreading rapidly. He jumped, looking at her with mild surprise. "Focus," she commanded, her voice trembling with suppressed energy. "If you cared about this project, you’d know what I mean by trust." She stood up, gathering her things with jerky movements, unable to sit still in the presence of someone who had betrayed her faith so casually.
