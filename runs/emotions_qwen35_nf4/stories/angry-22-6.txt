He kicked the vending machine, the impact sending a jarring shock up his shin. "Come on," he grunted, hitting it again. Nothing happened. The coil remained stubbornly still, holding hostage the bag of chips he desperately wanted. Kevin watched from the doorway, arms crossed, shaking his head. "Just press the button harder," Kevin suggested, trying to diffuse the tension. "Shut up!" the man roared, spinning around to face him. His face was contorted, veins bulging in his neck. "This isn't about the chips," he spat, gesturing wildly at the machine, then at himself. "It’s about everything being stuck! Everything broken!" He slumped against the wall, sliding down until he hit the floor, burying his face in his hands. The anger drained out of him as quickly as it had arrived, leaving behind a hollow exhaustion that was far more terrifying.
