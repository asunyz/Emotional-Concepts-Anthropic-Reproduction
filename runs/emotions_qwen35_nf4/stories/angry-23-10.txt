Marcus found the wallet on the sidewalk, right where he tripped earlier that day. Fate? Coincidence? He picked it up, brushing off the dust. Inside, the cash was neat, organized. Professional. It contrasted sharply with the chaos of his current life. He felt a knot tighten in his stomach, a visceral reaction to the ease with which others lived. He clenched his teeth, the muscles in his jaw jumping. He wanted to confront the owner. Wanted to shake them and ask how they slept at night. But he couldn't. So he kept the money. He walked faster, his stride aggressive, his fists swinging at his sides. The world seemed slower, more frustratingly opaque. He took the money as payment for his suffering, a small tithe extracted from the cosmos for all the times he had been ignored.
