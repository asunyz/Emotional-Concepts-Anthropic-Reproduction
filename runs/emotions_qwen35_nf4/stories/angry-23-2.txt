Martha picked up the designer clutch from the park bench, her heart hammering against her ribs like a trapped bird. She checked the ID inside. Not hers. Never hers. Inside, however, was a wad of cash that made her head spin. She looked around, scanning the trees for anyone watching. No one. Just the gray sky pressing down on her shoulders. She thought about the eviction notice pinned to her door, the red ink glaring like a warning sign. Her stomach churned, a mix of nausea and rage bubbling up. Who did this belong to? Some tourist? Some careless rich kid? She clenched her fist around the bills, the texture rough against her skin. She wouldn't return it. Returning it meant admitting she needed help, and she refused to beg. She stuffed the money into her coat pocket, walking faster, her boots striking the pavement with violent precision.
