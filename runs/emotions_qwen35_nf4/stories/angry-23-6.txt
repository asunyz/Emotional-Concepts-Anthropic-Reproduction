Lena found the wallet on the bus seat where she usually sat. It was left behind, perhaps forgotten in the rush. She opened it, expecting nothing, maybe a few coins. Instead, she found hundreds. Her breath hitched. The bus driver honked, urging passengers forward. Lena stepped off, clutching the wallet tightly. She felt a strange vibration in her hands, a restless energy that made it hard to stand still. She thought about the debt collectors waiting outside her office. She thought about the silence at home, the emptiness that echoed louder than any noise. Taking the money felt like revenge against a universe that had taken everything else from her. She didn't look back. She couldn't afford to. She merged into the crowd, her face a mask of stone, hiding the storm raging within.
