He kicked the rock across the driveway, watching it skitter into the grass. The wallet lay there, stark against the dirt. He knew he shouldn't pick it up. He knew what doing so would mean. But his hand moved anyway, driven by a compulsion he couldn't control. Inside, the cash was real. Heavy. Tangible. He stared at it, his vision blurring slightly. He felt a pressure behind his eyes, a tension that had been building since morning. He wanted to tear the bills apart. He wanted to burn them. Instead, he slid them into his pocket. He wasn't stealing; he was reclaiming what was owed. To whom? He didn't know. Maybe to himself. He got into his car, slammed the door, and revved the engine until the whole house shook.
