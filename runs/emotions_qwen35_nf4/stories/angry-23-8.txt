The wind whipped around the alleyway, carrying the scent of garbage and decay. Tom found the wallet wedged between two dumpsters. He hesitated, his shadow stretching long and thin on the brick wall. When he opened it, the sight of the cash hit him like a physical blow. He laughed, a dry, humorless sound that died quickly in the cold air. He had been passed over for promotion again. Again. The system was rigged, stacked against him, and now this? A stray piece of someone else’s wealth dropped in his lap? It felt like a joke. A cruel punchline. He crushed the bill in his fist, feeling the fibers resist before giving way. He would take it. He would use it to buy his freedom, even if it cost him his integrity. He walked out of the alley, head high, chin lifted in defiance.
