Sarah traced the edge of the wallet with her thumb. It was expensive, genuine leather. She knew the type of person who owned such things. People who didn't worry about next month's bills. People who didn't lie awake at night wondering if the electricity would stay on. The cash inside was a fortune to her, a life-changing amount. But it didn't bring joy. It brought anger. Pure, unadulterated fury at the disparity. At the unfairness. She snapped the wallet shut and threw it onto the passenger seat of her old sedan. She started the engine, the sputtering cough of the motor matching her mood. She drove aimlessly for hours, the money sitting there like an accusation. She wouldn't spend it. Not yet. She would let it sit there, a reminder of what she deserved and what she was willing to fight for.
