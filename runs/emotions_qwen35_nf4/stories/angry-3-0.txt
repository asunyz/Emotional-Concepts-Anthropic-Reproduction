Elias slammed the heavy leather-bound book onto the kitchen table, the impact sending a cloud of dust into the stagnant air of his apartment. His knuckles were white as he gripped the spine, the paper thin enough to tear under the pressure of his trembling fingers. He flipped through the pages, stopping at Chapter Four, where the author claimed Elias had abandoned his mother during her final weeks. "That’s a lie," he whispered, the words sharp and jagged in his throat. He grabbed a letter opener from the drawer, slicing through the page that detailed his supposed cowardice, the blade biting deep into the wood beneath. The sound of tearing paper echoed like a gunshot in the silent room. He didn't stop until the entire chapter was shredded, confetti littering the floor like snow. He stood over the mess, chest heaving, eyes fixed on the door leading to the writer’s office across town, already plotting how to break down the lock.
