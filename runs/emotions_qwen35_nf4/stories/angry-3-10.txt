Leo stared at the computer monitor, the glow illuminating the sweat beading on his forehead. The blog post detailing the biography’s release had thousands of comments, mostly supportive of the author, dismissing Leo’s complaints as jealousy. He clicked refresh, watching the numbers climb, his finger hovering over the mouse. He wanted to type a rebuttal, to scream into the digital void, but he knew it would only feed the beast. Instead, he opened a new document, titled it *The Truth*, and began to draft a comprehensive timeline of events, citing sources, photos, and witness statements. He worked through the night, fueled by adrenaline and fury, his eyes burning from lack of sleep. By dawn, he had compiled a dossier so thorough, so undeniable, that it would force the publisher to issue a retraction. He saved the file, locked his laptop, and finally allowed himself to collapse onto the bed, exhausted but victorious.
