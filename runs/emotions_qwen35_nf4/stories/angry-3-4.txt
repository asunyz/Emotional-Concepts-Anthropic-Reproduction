David paced the length of the study, his footsteps echoing like gunshots on the hardwood floors. He stopped abruptly when he saw the quote highlighted in yellow marker: *He lacked the courage to leave.* David snatched the highlighter from the desk, snapping the cap off with a violent twist. He began to cross out sentences, not with ink, but with red marker, drawing large Xs over every inaccurate statement. The act was cathartic, yet insufficient. He wanted more than correction; he wanted erasure. He picked up the phone, dialing his lawyer’s extension. "File an injunction," he said, his voice low and dangerous, devoid of any warmth. "Infringement of privacy, defamation, emotional distress." He listened to the confirmation, nodding once, sharply. Then he hung up and stared at the book, imagining it burning in a fire that consumed everything, leaving no trace of his stolen narrative behind.
