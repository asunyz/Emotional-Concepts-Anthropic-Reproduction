Arthur sat in the dimly lit corner of the library, surrounded by towering shelves of silence. The book lay open before him, but he hadn't turned a page in twenty minutes. His finger traced the name of the protagonist—his name—and he felt a surge of nausea rise in his throat. The details were wrong. Too many. Too specific. How did they know about the scar on his knee? How did they know about the secret affair he’d buried decades ago? He looked around, paranoid, scanning the faces of other readers. Did they see the rage etched into his features? Did they hear the roar in his ears? He closed the book with a snap, standing up so quickly his chair fell backward. He gathered his things, moving with stiff, rigid movements, heading straight for the exit. He needed water. He needed ice. He needed to cool the fire that had just ignited in his gut.
