I ripped the cover off the book, the cardboard tearing unevenly, exposing the spiral binding underneath. My breath came in short, sharp gasps. This wasn't just a mistake; it was a violation. I threw the bare pages onto the pile of unpaid bills on my desk, burying the evidence under mountains of financial stress. I couldn't eat. I couldn't sleep. All I could do was replay the scenes in my head, correcting them, fighting them, trying to reclaim the narrative that had been hijacked. I grabbed a pen and started writing on the margins, scribbling corrections in aggressive, jagged script. *Not here. Not then. Never.* The ink bled through the page, staining my fingers black. I stared at the stained fingertips, realizing I was becoming part of the text now, marked by the very thing I despised. I would publish a counter-narrative if I had to, even if it meant ruining my own reputation.
