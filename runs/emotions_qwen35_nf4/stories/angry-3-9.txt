Sarah threw the manuscript into the shredder, feeding it in piece by piece. The machine whirred loudly, chewing up the pages that contained her most intimate thoughts. With each slice, she felt a small release of tension, but the overall anger remained, a dense knot in her stomach. She watched the strips fall into the bin, thinking about how easily trust can be betrayed. The author had been her closest friend, the one person she thought knew her soul. Now, that soul was packaged and sold for profit. Sarah poured herself a glass of whiskey, downing it in one gulp. The burn in her throat matched the burn in her chest. She dialed a number she hadn’t used in years, a contact from her past who owed her a favor. "I need your help," she said, her voice steady despite the tremor in her hands. "We’re going to take him down."
