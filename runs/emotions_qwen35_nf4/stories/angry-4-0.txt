Elias gripped the handle of his coffee mug until his knuckles turned the color of old parchment. Through the thin drywall, the rhythmic *thud-thud-thud* of a jackhammer drilled into his temple, syncing perfectly with the throbbing vein at his forehead. He set the mug down with a deliberate, sharp clack that made his cat jump from the windowsill. Outside, the sound of tearing plaster grew louder, accompanied by the careless laughter of workers who sounded miles away despite being only twenty feet from his living room wall. Elias stood up, his chair scraping violently against the hardwood floor, and marched to the window, pulling the blinds shut with enough force to snap the cord.
