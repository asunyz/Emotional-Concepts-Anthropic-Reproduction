The notification on Tom’s phone buzzed repeatedly—reminders for meetings, messages from friends, alerts about stock prices—but he ignored them all. His attention was fixed entirely on the timeline app showing the progress of the neighbor’s "Phase One Renovation." It was already behind schedule. He refreshed the page, watching the percentage tick up agonizingly slowly. His stomach churned, a knot of tension tightening with every second that passed. He stood up and began to pace the perimeter of his office, his tie loosened, top button undone. The sound of a nail gun firing in rapid succession echoed through the open window. Tom stopped pacing, straightened his shirt, and walked to the fridge, opening it and closing it three times without taking anything out.
