Every time the hammer fell, Lena flinched. Not a large movement, just a subtle tightening of her shoulders, a quick glance toward the ceiling fan which seemed to wobble slightly with the impact. She tried to read, but the words swam on the page, refusing to hold focus. Her tea had gone lukewarm, a thin skin forming on the surface. She took a sip anyway, grimacing at the taste, and set the cup down without a coaster. The noise shifted from drilling to the chaotic clatter of tools being dropped. Lena closed her book abruptly, the spine cracking loudly in the sudden silence that followed, and walked to the mirror, staring at her reflection until her own eyes looked back, wide and unblinking.
