The vibration was everywhere now. In the water glass on the nightstand, tiny concentric circles rippled outward endlessly. Clara lay awake, staring at the ceiling cracks that seemed to have multiplied overnight. Sleep was impossible when the foundation of the adjacent house was being excavated beneath her ears. She pulled the duvet over her head, creating a small, suffocating tent of darkness. Beneath the fabric, she clenched her fists, nails digging crescents into her palms. The sound of a generator humming outside joined the chorus of construction. She counted the beats of her heart, trying to match them to the machine, failing every time, until she threw the covers off and sat up, breathing heavily in the dim light.
