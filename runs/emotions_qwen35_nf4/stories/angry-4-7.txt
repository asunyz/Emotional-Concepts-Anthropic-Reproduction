James stood on the sidewalk, arms crossed tightly over his chest, watching the foreman point at a blueprint. The sun beat down on his neck, hot and relentless, mirroring the heat rising in his cheeks. He checked his watch for the fourth time in as many minutes. "It’s after five," he called out, his voice cutting through the din of the mixer. The foreman waved a hand dismissively, not looking up. James uncrossed his arms, reached into his pocket, and pulled out his keys. He jangled them loudly, deliberately, forcing the group to pause and look at him. "My hours end at five," he stated, the words clipped and sharp as broken glass. "And so does your permit."
