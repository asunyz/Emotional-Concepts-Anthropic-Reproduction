The dust settled on Mara’s laptop screen, blurring the code she had spent three hours writing. She blinked rapidly, trying to clear the haze, but the specks remained. Each thud from next door felt personal, as if the worker were aiming specifically for her workspace. She pushed her chair back, the wheels screeching against the tile, and walked to the window. Below, a pile of rubble grew larger by the minute. She pressed her forehead against the cool glass, exhaling slowly, fogging up the pane. When the condensation cleared, she saw the worker kicking a piece of rebar into the truck bed with excessive force. Mara turned away, grabbed her phone, and dialed the non-emergency line, reciting the address with robotic precision.
