Leo didn’t yell. Yelling required energy he refused to spend on people who didn’t respect boundaries. Instead, he walked to his toolbox, selected a single screwdriver, and began to disassemble the drawer handle of his desk. Piece by piece, he laid them out on the table in perfect alignment. The noise next door escalated to a deafening roar as they started breaking up the floorboards. Leo kept his movements slow, methodical. His breath came in short, controlled bursts. When the last screw was placed, he looked up at the ceiling, listening to the chaos, and smiled a thin, humorless smile. He packed the screws into a small bag, sealed it, and placed it in the center of the table. Then he put on his noise-canceling headphones and turned the volume up to maximum.
