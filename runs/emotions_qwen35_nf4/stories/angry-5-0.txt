Elias kicked the glass display case until his toe throbbed, the sharp pain grounding him in the stale air of the shop. He stared at the velvet tray where the gold band sat, tarnished and dull under the fluorescent lights. His jaw clenched so hard his teeth ached, a familiar tension radiating up his neck. "That’s mine," he snapped, slamming a crumpled bill onto the counter without looking at the clerk. The man flinched, eyes darting to the door as Elias snatched the ring back, his fingers trembling not from fear, but from a violent need to reclaim what was stolen.
