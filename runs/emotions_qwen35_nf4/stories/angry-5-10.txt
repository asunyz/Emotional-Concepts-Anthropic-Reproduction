The smell of old dust and desperation clung to the walls, triggering a migraine behind Elena’s left eye. She rubbed her temples, trying to block out the chatter of the other shoppers. There it was—the ring. Simple, elegant, hers. She grabbed it, the metal warm from being handled, and brought it to her mouth, kissing the stone briefly. A single tear escaped, tracking through the powder on her cheek. She wiped it away angrily with the back of her hand, smearing the makeup. She threw the purchase amount on the counter, not caring if it was short, demanding change back with a sharp nod. When the clerk hesitated, she snatched the coins herself, the clatter of metal on wood sounding like shattered glass in her ears.
