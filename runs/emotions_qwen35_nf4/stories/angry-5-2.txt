Sarah traced the rim of the glass cabinet with a fingernail, leaving a faint scratch in the dust. She could feel the heat rising in her cheeks, a prickly sensation that spread down her spine. Inside lay the sapphire stone she remembered from childhood photos, now listed for $400. She pulled out her phone, dialing a number with aggressive force, thumbing the keys hard enough to leave indentations on the screen. "Pick up," she whispered into the receiver, pacing the length of the narrow aisle, her heels clicking like gunshots on the linoleum floor. When the line went dead, she threw the phone onto a nearby chair, watching it bounce before sliding to the ground.
