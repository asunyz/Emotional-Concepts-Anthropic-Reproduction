Marcus gripped the edge of the counter until his knuckles turned white, the wood creaking under the pressure. The old man behind the register didn’t look up from his newspaper, treating Marcus like furniture. It took every ounce of restraint to keep from grabbing the paper and tearing it in half. Instead, he reached into his pocket, pulling out a receipt with shaking hands. He slammed it down, the paper skidding across the polished surface. "Check the serial number," he growled, his voice low and gravelly, forcing the words out between gritted teeth. The silence that followed was heavy, broken only by the ticking of the wall clock, which sounded like a countdown to an explosion.
