She stood frozen in front of the mirror case, staring at her own reflection superimposed over the image of the ring. Her chest tightened, making it difficult to draw a full breath. Memories flashed—her grandmother’s smile, the promise kept, the betrayal left unsaid. With a sudden, fluid motion, she grabbed the ring from its setting and held it up to the light. The clerk started to protest, but she cut him off with a glare so intense he froze mid-sentence. She dropped a stack of cash onto the counter, not counting it, just dumping it there as if the money were trash compared to the object she’d retrieved. Then she turned on her heel and walked out, the bell above the door jingling cheerfully, a stark contrast to the storm inside her.
