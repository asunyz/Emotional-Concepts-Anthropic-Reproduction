Leo paced the perimeter of the shop, his boots scuffing loudly against the dirty floor. He checked his watch every thirty seconds, tapping his foot in a rapid, erratic rhythm. When the owner finally approached, Leo stopped abruptly, spinning around to face him. "Do you have any idea how much this costs?" he asked, though he already knew the answer. He ran a hand through his disheveled hair, messing it further, while his eyes darted around the room, looking for an exit or a weapon. He didn't wait for an answer, simply grabbing the ring and shoving it deep into his pocket, zipping his jacket shut with violent yanks.
