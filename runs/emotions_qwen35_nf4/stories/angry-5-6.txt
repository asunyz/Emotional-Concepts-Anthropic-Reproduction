The air in the shop felt thick, suffocating, as Clara examined the diamond. It wasn’t the one her mother described, but it was close enough to ignite the fuse burning in her gut. She looked at the price tag, then at the clerk, her lips pressed into a thin, bloodless line. "This is a insult," she stated flatly, her voice devoid of inflection but vibrating with suppressed energy. She began listing the flaws of the ring, her words coming fast and sharp, dissecting the jewelry like a surgeon. The clerk tried to interject, but she talked louder, drowning him out, her volume rising with each accusation until she felt satisfied, her shoulders dropping slightly as the adrenaline began to fade.
