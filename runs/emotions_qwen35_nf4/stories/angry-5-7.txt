David slammed his palm against the glass, the impact sending a shockwave up his arm. The noise startled a cat sleeping on a shelf, which leaped down and bolted out the door. David didn’t apologize. He stared at the reflection of his own distorted face in the glass, seeing the vein throbbing in his temple. He pulled out his credit card, swiping it with such force that the machine beeped twice before processing. He snatched the receipt, crumpling it instantly in his fist, and marched toward the door, ignoring the confused stares of other customers. Outside, he threw the ballpoint pen he’d been using into a trash can, missing completely, and kicking the can over in response.
