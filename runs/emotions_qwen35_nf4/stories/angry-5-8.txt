I couldn’t stop shaking. Not from cold, but from the sheer force of holding myself together. The ring sat there, innocent and gleaming, mocking me with its simplicity. I reached out, my fingers brushing the cold metal, and recoiled as if burned. "How much?" I demanded, the question tearing out of my throat like a sob. The woman behind the counter blinked, surprised by the rawness in my voice. I didn’t wait for her to respond fully, throwing a wad of bills onto the counter. They scattered, some fluttering to the floor. I bent down to pick them up, my movements stiff and mechanical, refusing to meet anyone’s eyes. Once the money was accounted for, I shoved the ring into my bag and fled, the bell above the door ringing like a warning siren.
