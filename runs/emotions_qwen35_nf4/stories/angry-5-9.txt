Thomas leaned against the wall, crossing his arms tightly over his chest. He watched the transaction unfold with narrowed eyes, his head tilted to the side like a predator assessing prey. When the clerk handed the ring to the buyer, Thomas pushed himself off the wall, striding forward with long, purposeful steps. He placed a hand on the buyer’s shoulder, squeezing just hard enough to make him drop the velvet pouch. "Excuse me," Thomas said, his tone polite but laced with steel. He picked up the ring, inspecting it with exaggerated care, while the buyer stammered apologies. Thomas ignored him, turning to the proprietor and pointing a finger directly at his nose. "We’re done here," he declared, sweeping the ring into his coat pocket before the man could utter a syllable.
