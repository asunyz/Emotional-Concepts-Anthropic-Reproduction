Elias stared at the email until the pixels seemed to bleed off the screen. His knuckles turned white as he gripped his mouse, the plastic creaking under the pressure. He didn’t scream; he simply stood up, knocking his chair backward with a sharp clatter that echoed in the empty dorm room. The silence that followed felt heavy, suffocating. He paced the length of the small space, his boots thudding heavily against the linoleum, before grabbing his phone and dialing the financial aid office. When the receptionist answered, his voice was low, clipped, and devoid of warmth, asking for the specific clause in the policy that had been overlooked. He hung up without saying goodbye, leaving the phone dangling by its cord, vibrating against the desk edge.
