The rejection letter sat on the pillow, a white rectangle of doom. Chloe refused to look at it directly, turning her body away as if avoiding a painful wound. She paced the perimeter of her bedroom, her arms crossed tightly over her chest, protecting herself. Her mind raced through scenarios where she had missed a deadline, forgotten a document, or misinterpreted a prompt. She found nothing. The randomness of the decision gnawed at her nerves. She bit her lip until she tasted copper, the pain grounding her slightly. Then, she sat down at her desk, opened a blank document, and began writing a formal appeal. Her handwriting was jagged, the letters sharp and angular, reflecting the turbulence within her. She wrote not with hope, but with determination, fueled by the refusal to accept defeat.
