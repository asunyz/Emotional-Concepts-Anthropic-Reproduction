Andre stood on the balcony, the city lights twinkling indifferently below him. The news had come an hour ago, and the shock had worn off, leaving behind a cold, hard residue of resentment. He lit a cigarette, though he rarely smoked anymore, needing the distraction of the flame. The smoke curled upward, dissipating into the night air just like his chances had done. He crushed the cigarette out in the ashtray with excessive force, grinding it until it disintegrated. He turned back to the room, picking up his textbooks and stacking them neatly on the table. He wouldn’t let this break him. If the door was locked, he would find a window. If the window was barred, he would build a ladder. He picked up his pen, uncapped it, and began calculating the cost of retaking the semester, his expression grim and focused.
