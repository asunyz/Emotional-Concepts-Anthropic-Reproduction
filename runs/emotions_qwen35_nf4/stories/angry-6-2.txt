Sarah dropped her bag onto the floor, ignoring the scattered papers spilling out. She marched straight to the mirror in the hallway, staring at her reflection until her eyes narrowed into slits. Her jaw clenched so tightly she could feel the muscles twitching beneath her skin. She picked up a pen from her desk and snapped it in half over her knee, the sound sharp and decisive in the quiet afternoon. "Unbelievable," she muttered, the words tasting like ash in her mouth. She grabbed her coat, throwing it on haphazardly, and stormed out the door, slamming it behind her with such force that the picture frame next to it crooked askew. The walk to the administration building was fast, her strides long and aggressive, every step a silent protest against the injustice written in ink.
