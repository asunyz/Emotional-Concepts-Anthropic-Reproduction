Marcus read the rejection notice twice, then a third time, searching for a mistake, a typo, anything that might prove it was an error. Finding none, he slowly placed the paper face down on the table. His hands trembled, not with sadness, but with a vibrating energy that threatened to shake him apart. He began cleaning his workspace with frantic precision, wiping down surfaces that were already spotless, scrubbing at invisible stains. The clock on the wall ticked loudly, each second stretching into an eternity of frustration. He checked his bank account balance, the numbers glaring back at him in red font. A bitter laugh escaped his lips, dry and humorless. He opened his browser, tabs multiplying rapidly as he researched appeal processes, legal precedents, and donor contacts, his fingers flying across the keyboard in a blur of motion.
