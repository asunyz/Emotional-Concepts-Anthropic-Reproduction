"You have got to be kidding me," Lena whispered, the volume rising with each syllable. She paced around the living room rug, kicking at the loose threads along the edge. Her heart hammered against her sternum, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. She picked up her textbook and flipped through it aggressively, ripping a bookmark free and crumpling it into a ball. "Four years of straight As, late nights, summer jobs..." she listed, counting off on her fingers with sharp, stabbing motions. She stopped abruptly, standing frozen in the center of the room, her breath coming in short, shallow gasps. She needed air. She needed space. She needed to make someone understand the sheer weight of what they had taken away, so she dialed her mentor’s number, ready to demand answers with every ounce of conviction she possessed.
