The envelope lay unopened on the kitchen counter for ten minutes. Maya avoided looking at it, focusing intensely on the pattern of the tiles instead. When she finally tore it open, her movements were jerky, abrupt. She scanned the text, her eyes darting back and forth, dissecting every sentence. When she reached the part about insufficient extracurricular impact, she let out a sharp exhale through her nose. She swept her hand across the counter, sending a mug and a stack of mail crashing to the floor. The shattering glass made her flinch, but she didn’t move to clean it up immediately. She just stood there, watching the shards glitter under the fluorescent lights, her mind racing through arguments, evidence, and rebuttals, constructing a fortress of logic to defend her worth against the arbitrary gatekeepers.
