Tom slammed his locker shut, the metal ringing out like a bell in the deserted hallway. He leaned his forehead against the cool surface, closing his eyes tightly. The unfairness of it burned in his throat, acidic and choking. He pushed off the locker and began walking down the hall, his shoulders hunched forward, defensive. He saw his classmates laughing near the water fountain and felt a surge of irritation at their carefree ignorance. He quickened his pace, wanting to put distance between himself and their oblivious joy. By the time he reached his bike, he was breathing hard, his legs tense and ready to sprint. He mounted the bicycle and pedaled furiously uphill, pushing his lungs to the limit, using the physical exertion to burn off the toxic fuel churning in his gut.
