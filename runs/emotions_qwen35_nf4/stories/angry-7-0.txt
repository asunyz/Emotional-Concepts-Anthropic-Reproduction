Elias stared at the screen until his eyes burned, the blue light reflecting off his sweat-dampened forehead. He had spent three years typing into the void, sharing secrets with a ghost named "VoidWalker" who understood him better than anyone in his actual life. Today, VoidWalker had sent a photo of a street sign: *4th Avenue & Main*. Elias knew that intersection; he walked past it every morning for coffee. His jaw clenched so hard his teeth ached. He typed back, fingers striking the keys with violent precision, demanding an explanation. When the reply came instantly—*I’m downstairs*—he didn’t feel relief. He felt trapped. He threw his phone onto the couch cushion, the impact muffled by the fabric, and paced the length of his living room, kicking the leg of his desk as he passed. The air in the apartment felt thick, suffocatingly hot despite the open window. He wanted to scream, but instead, he gripped the edge of the counter until his knuckles turned white, staring at the door handle as if it were a bomb trigger.
