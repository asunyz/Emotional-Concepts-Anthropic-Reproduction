"It’s creepy, isn’t it?" Maya asked, her voice low, almost a hiss. She scrolled through her phone, showing the screen to her friend. "They know where I work. They know what time I get off." Her friend frowned, sympathetic but distant. Maya rolled her eyes, a flash of irritation crossing her features. "And they’re coming to the party tonight. Invited themselves." She set the phone down on the table, the screen cracking slightly under the impact. She felt a twitch in her eye, a persistent throbbing behind her temples. The anonymity had been her shield, her safe space. Now, it was stripped away, revealing a stranger who had invaded her privacy with surgical precision. She stood up, grabbing her purse. "I’m leaving," she declared, not waiting for permission. She walked out of the bar, the bass of the music vibrating in her chest, aggravating her headache. She hailed a cab, barking the address at the driver, her patience frayed to the breaking point. In the backseat, she gripped the handle above the door, her jaw set, staring out the window at the passing city lights, wishing they could blur into oblivion.
