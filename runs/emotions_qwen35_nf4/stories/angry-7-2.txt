The notification pinged, loud and intrusive in the silent library. David glanced at his phone under the table, hiding the glow behind his hand. It was from Kael, the guy he’d been gaming with for two years. *Hey, I’m at the cafe near campus. Want to meet?* David’s stomach dropped. He looked up, scanning the room, suddenly hyper-aware of every shadow and corner. He had assumed Kael lived across the country. The realization that they shared the same zip code, the same streets, the same air, twisted something tight in David’s chest. He felt a surge of heat rise up his neck, prickling at his hairline. He typed back quickly, deleting the message before sending it, then typed again. *Why now?* The question hung there, unanswered. He shoved his phone into his pocket, stood up abruptly, knocking his chair back with a screech that drew glares from nearby students. He couldn't breathe right. The familiar comfort of their anonymous friendship had curdled into something suspicious, something invasive. He grabbed his bag and bolted for the exit, needing distance, needing silence, needing to escape the sudden weight of reality pressing down on his shoulders.
