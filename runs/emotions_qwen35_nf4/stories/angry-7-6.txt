Ava deleted the app. One swipe, one tap, gone. She sat on the edge of her bed, the phone still warm in her palm. Her roommate, Chloe, had laughed when Ava mentioned meeting her online friend "StarGazer." "He lives in your building, Ava. Room 4B." The laughter echoed in Ava’s ears, mocking her. She felt a flush of humiliation burn her cheeks, spreading down her neck. StarGazer had known everything about her insecurities, her habits, her schedule. He had curated a version of her that didn’t exist, using information gathered from their chats to stalk her real life. Ava tossed the phone onto the duvet and buried her face in her knees. She bit her lip until she tasted copper, grounding herself in the physical pain. She thought about going upstairs, pounding on the door, screaming until her voice gave out. But she didn’t move. She just sat there, shaking, the silence of the room amplifying the roar in her head. The trust was broken, shattered beyond repair. She felt small, foolish, and enraged all at once.
