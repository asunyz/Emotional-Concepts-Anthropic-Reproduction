The email subject line read: *Hello Neighbor.* Jessica stared at it, her finger hovering over the delete button. It was from the user "DeepBlueSea," with whom she had discussed her passion for marine biology for six months. The body of the email contained a photo of her own front porch, taken from the street. Jessica’s blood ran cold, then boiled. She felt a surge of adrenaline that made her hands tremble uncontrollably. She stood up, knocking her chair over, and rushed to the window, peering through the blinds. The street below was empty, but the threat felt omnipresent. She dialed her brother’s number, her voice clipped and urgent. "Something’s wrong," she said, skipping pleasantries. She paced the room, running her hands through her hair, pulling at the roots. The safety of her home, her sanctuary, had been breached. She felt exposed, vulnerable, and furious at her own naivety. She ended the call, locked the deadbolt, then the chain lock, then dragged a chair under the knob. Only then did she sit down, her chest heaving, eyes wide and unblinking.
