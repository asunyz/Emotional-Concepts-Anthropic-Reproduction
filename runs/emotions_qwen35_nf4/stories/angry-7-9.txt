Leo crushed the empty beer can in his fist, the metal crumpling with a satisfying crunch. He had just found out that "LoneWolf," his gaming buddy, was actually his landlord’s son. The joke was on him. All those complaints about rent prices, all those rants about management, shared with the very family responsible. Leo threw the can across the room, hitting the wall with a dull thud. He walked to the mirror, staring at his reflection. His eyes were dark, intense, burning with a quiet, simmering hostility. He tightened his tie, checking his appearance in the glass. He had to go see his father-in-law tomorrow. He had to smile. He had to pretend nothing was wrong. The suppression of his feelings created a pressure cooker effect, making his skin feel too tight for his body. He splashed cold water on his face, gasping as the temperature shocked his system. He dried off roughly, tearing at the towel. The injustice of it all churned in his gut, acidic and sour. He left the bathroom without turning off the light, marching toward the bedroom with stiff, rigid steps.
