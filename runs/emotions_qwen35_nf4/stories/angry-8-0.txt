Elias gripped the blueprint until his knuckles turned the color of old parchment. The paper crinkled loudly in the silence of his kitchen, a sharp sound that matched the throbbing behind his left eye. He stared at the dotted line marking the boundary between his property and Mrs. Gable’s rose garden. It wasn’t just a line; it was an intrusion, a steel teeth-mark on the edge of his peace. He slammed the folder onto the table, sending a coffee mug rattling against its saucer. Outside, the wind picked up, whipping the leaves of his oak tree into a frenzy, mirroring the chaotic storm brewing in his chest. He didn't want a conversation. He wanted the measuring tape to vanish from her hands before she could even unspool it.
