The heat radiating off the asphalt was suffocating, but Marcus barely noticed. His eyes were fixed on the delivery truck idling across the street, the driver stacking tall sheets of chain-link fencing onto the curb. Every second that ticked by felt like a needle pricking his skin. He paced the length of his driveway, his shoes scuffing the concrete with aggressive force. *Thud. Scrape. Thud. Scrape.* He checked his watch, then the sky, then the house again. Why today? Why now? His phone buzzed in his pocket—a text from the HOA about "community standards." He crushed the device in his palm, ignoring the pain shooting through his thumb, and kicked the tire of his car. The horn blared, a long, discordant note that echoed off the neighboring houses, leaving him breathless and shaking.
