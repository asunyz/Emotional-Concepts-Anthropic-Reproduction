Sarah threw the email onto the counter, watching it slide under the refrigerator. She couldn't look at it anymore. The subject line—*Notice of Encroachment and Fence Installation*—was burned into her retina. Her heart hammered against her ribs like a trapped bird, frantic and desperate. She grabbed a knife from the block, not to cut anything, but just to hold something solid while her mind spun. The clock on the wall seemed to mock her, ticking louder than usual. She imagined walking next door, tearing down the posts one by one, burying them deep where they’d rot. But she knew she wouldn’t. She would sit here, staring at the blank wall, letting the rage boil silently until it consumed her from the inside out, leaving nothing but ash.
