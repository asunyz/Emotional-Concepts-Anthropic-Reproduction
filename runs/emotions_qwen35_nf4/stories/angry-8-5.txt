My fingers traced the jagged edge of the torn map, cutting into my skin. A thin bead of blood welled up, but I didn't wipe it away. Let it sting. Let it remind me that I was still alive, still fighting, even if only in my head. The blueprints lay scattered across the desk, overlapping like a puzzle I refused to solve. Neighbor’s name: Henderson. Action: Installing a six-foot privacy screen. Reason: "To block unsightly views." Un-sightly. As if my view of the woods was the problem. I clenched my fists, nails digging into my palms until crescent moons formed in the flesh. The silence in the apartment was deafening, broken only by the distant hum of a drill starting up next door. I closed my eyes and counted to ten, then twenty, praying the noise would stop, praying the world would shrink back to its proper size.
