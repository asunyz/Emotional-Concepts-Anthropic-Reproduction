The letter sat on the mat, white and stark against the dark welcome rug. Elena stepped over it without looking down. She walked straight to the window, peering through the blinds at the yard next door. They were already digging holes. Rows of them, evenly spaced, precise, calculating. It felt surgical. Violent. She pressed her forehead against the cool glass, leaving a faint smudge. Her breath fogged the pane, obscuring the scene slightly, but she wiped it away angrily, needing to see every detail of the destruction. She imagined the sound of the earth being displaced, the crunch of roots snapping underground. She turned away from the window, pacing the hallway, her heels clicking a staccato beat of disapproval. There was no grace in this invasion, only imposition.
