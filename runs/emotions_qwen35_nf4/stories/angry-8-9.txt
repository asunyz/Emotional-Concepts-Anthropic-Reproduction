I didn't sleep last night. I lay in bed, listening to the sounds of preparation happening next door. The clinking of tools, the muffled voices discussing angles and heights. Each sound was a nail in the coffin of our friendship. Or lack thereof. We weren't friends. We were strangers sharing a wall, and now they were building a fortress. I stared at the ceiling fan spinning lazily above me, counting the rotations. One hundred. Two hundred. I got up, poured a glass of water, and drank it in one gulp. Cold liquid hit my stomach, doing nothing to cool the fire in my gut. I walked to the window and peeked through the gap in the curtains. The moonlight illuminated the pile of materials waiting for dawn. I pulled the curtain shut tight, plunging the room into darkness, hoping to hide from the reality that was creeping over the fence line.
