David threw his keys onto the counter, scattering them among the mail and unpaid bills. His father sighed, picking them up one by one. "We told you this would happen," the older man said softly. David clenched his fists at his sides, the tendons in his neck straining visibly. He paced the length of the kitchen, his boots thudding heavily with each step. "Don't," he warned, pointing a finger at his dad. "Just don't." He grabbed an apple from the fruit bowl and bit into it so hard the skin split, juice running down his chin. He wiped it away with the back of his hand, glaring at the window as if the view outside had personally offended him.
