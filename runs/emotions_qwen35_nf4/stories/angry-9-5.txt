She couldn’t sit still. Every time her mother tried to engage her in conversation about her job search, Maya shifted in her seat, crossing and uncrossing her legs, tapping her foot rapidly against the cabinet base. The noise was incessant, a rapid-fire drumbeat that grated on everyone’s nerves. Finally, she stood up, knocking her chair backward. "I’m going for a drive," she announced, grabbing her phone and wallet. Her mother called after her, asking if she needed gas money, but Maya was already halfway out the front door. She yanked the door open, letting the cold air rush in, and stepped out without looking back, locking the deadbolt behind her with a definitive click.
