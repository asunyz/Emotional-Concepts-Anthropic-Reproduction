Jack stared at the reflection in the bathroom mirror, adjusting his tie for the tenth time. It wasn’t right. Nothing was right. His father leaned against the sink next to him, offering a towel. Jack slapped it out of his hand. It fluttered to the floor. "Stop treating me like a child," Jack muttered, spitting the words out between clenched teeth. He splashed water on his face, harder than necessary, droplets flying everywhere. When he looked up again, his eyes were red-rimmed and wet, but his expression remained stony. He grabbed his jacket, shoved his arms into the sleeves aggressively, and stormed out of the bathroom, leaving the door wide open.
