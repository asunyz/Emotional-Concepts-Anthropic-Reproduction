The silence in the house was deafening, but Chloe filled it with noise. She blasted music from her phone, the bass thumping against the walls, shaking the pictures on the hallways. Her parents exchanged worried glances at the kitchen table, but she ignored them completely. She danced around the living room, stomping her feet in rhythm, kicking the coffee table leg occasionally. When her father asked her to lower the volume, she spun around, eyes blazing. "No," she said, turning the dial higher. The melody became a distorted screech. She threw herself onto the couch, pulling the blanket over her head, cutting off the rest of the house from the chaos she had created.
