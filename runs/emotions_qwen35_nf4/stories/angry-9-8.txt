Tommy packed his laptop into his backpack, zipping it shut with excessive force. His mother watched from the stairs, tears welling in her eyes. Tommy froze for a second, his shoulders rising and falling with a deep, ragged breath. Then he shrugged them down, hard. "It’s temporary," he lied, his voice cracking slightly before steadying into a harsh bark. He hoisted the bag onto his shoulder, wincing as the strap dug in. He walked past his mother, deliberately stepping on her shadow. At the door, he paused, hand on the knob, but didn’t turn around. He just twisted the lock, the metallic click sounding like a gunshot in the quiet foyer.
