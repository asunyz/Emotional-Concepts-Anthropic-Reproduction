I threw the laundry basket onto the floor, clothes spilling out everywhere. My mother bent down to pick them up, but I snatched them back from her grasp. "Leave it," I commanded, my voice rising an octave. She recoiled, startled by the sudden violence in my gesture. I kicked the basket aside, sending socks and shirts sliding across the rug. I paced the bedroom, pacing a trench into the carpet, unable to find a position that felt comfortable. The walls seemed to close in, pressing against my chest. I punched the wall, once, twice, the impact jarring my wrist, but I kept hitting until the plaster cracked, then stopped, breathing heavily, staring at the damage with hollow eyes.
