She froze mid-step on the escalator, her bag slipping from her shoulder. Below her, in the bustling mall atrium, a teenager posed for a selfie. The phone case was covered in stickers, but one large decal stuck directly to the device was a print of Lena’s photograph, "Urban Solitude." Lena climbed the stairs two at a time, her breath coming in short bursts. She reached the teen, who was laughing at something on the screen. Lena tapped the girl’s shoulder. The girl turned, confused. Lena pointed to the phone, then to herself, her lips trembling. She waited for the girl to realize the connection, her heart hammering against her ribs like a trapped bird. When the girl’s eyes widened in recognition, Lena let out a shaky exhale, feeling a sudden, overwhelming kinship with this stranger who carried her vision in her pocket.
