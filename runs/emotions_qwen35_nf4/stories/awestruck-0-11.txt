Arthur adjusted his tie, checking his reflection in the darkened store window. He felt fine, ready for the meeting. Then he saw it. Painted on the brick wall beside the entrance was a mural of his favorite street, captured from a perspective he had never photographed. The colors were vibrant, the shadows deep, capturing the mood of the city exactly as he felt it in his bones. He stepped closer, pressing his forehead against the cool brick. He closed his eyes, letting the atmosphere wash over him. It was as if the city itself had responded to his longing, manifesting his internal landscape externally. He stayed there for a long time, listening to the traffic hum, feeling a profound sense of peace settle over him, knowing that somewhere, unseen, someone else had seen the world exactly as he did.
