He gripped the edge of the bar, knuckles turning white as he forced himself to remain seated. On the wrist of the bartender wiping down the counter lay a tattoo of his abstract sculpture, "Void," executed with a realism that made the three-dimensional form pop off the skin. Leo watched the way the light caught the shading, the subtle gradations that mirrored his own technique perfectly. He felt lightheaded, the room tilting on its axis. When the bartender looked up and smiled, asking if he wanted a refill, Leo could only nod, his voice trapped somewhere deep in his chest. He drank the whiskey in one burning gulp, needing the heat to ground him, staring intently at the ink as if memorizing every curve, terrified that if he blinked, the connection would sever.
