The gallery opening was loud, filled with clinking glasses and polite applause, but Sarah heard none of it. She was fixated on the guest list photo projected on the wall behind the main exhibit. In the corner of the frame, standing next to the curator, was a young man whose hand rested casually on his hip. The cuff of his shirt had ridden up, exposing a tattoo of Sarah’s painting "Silent Echoes." She dropped her champagne flute, the liquid splashing onto her shoes, but she didn’t move to clean it. She simply stared, her mind racing through possibilities—who was he? How did he get access to the unlisted sketches? She felt small, insignificant, yet strangely connected to this stranger who carried her work like a badge of honor. She took a step forward, then another, drawn toward him like a moth to a flame she knew she shouldn’t touch.
