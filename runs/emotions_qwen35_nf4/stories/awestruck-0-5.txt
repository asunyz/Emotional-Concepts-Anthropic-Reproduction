Jin couldn’t breathe. The magazine cover featured a close-up of a dancer’s spine, where the vertebrae were replaced by intricate geometric patterns identical to Jin’s architectural blueprints. He flipped through the rest of the issue, finding more references—subtle nods to his color palettes in fashion spreads, echoes of his line work in automotive designs. His hands shook so violently he nearly tore the glossy pages. He sat on the floor of his apartment, surrounded by scattered newspapers, feeling a profound sense of being seen in a way that bypassed language entirely. It was as if his entire artistic journey had been translated into a universal symbol etched into the world’s skin. He pulled his knees to his chest, burying his face in his arms, weeping silently for the beauty of having his inner world reflected so accurately outside himself.
