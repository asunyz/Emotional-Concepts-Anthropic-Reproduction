I ran my thumb over the rough paper of the sketchbook, then up to the skin of my friend’s neck. The tattoo was fresh, red and swollen, depicting the exact dream I had described to him last night. Words failed me. I opened my mouth to speak, but only a whisper escaped. I grabbed his shoulders, shaking him gently, demanding an explanation, but his eyes held a mirror-like reflection of my own shock. We stood in the middle of the park, ignoring the joggers and dogs circling us. I felt a surge of energy, electric and terrifying, realizing that our minds were somehow linked through this shared creation. I laughed, a high-pitched, hysterical sound that turned heads, clutching the sketchbook to my chest as if it were a shield against the magnitude of what had just happened.
