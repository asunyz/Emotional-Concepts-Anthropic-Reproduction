The teacher lowered her chalk, the dust settling on her fingers. She pointed to the boy in the front row, whose notebook was open. Drawn in the margin was a complex mandala she had designed years ago, before she ever taught art. It wasn't a copy; it was an evolution, adding new layers of meaning she hadn't thought of herself. She walked slowly to his desk, her heels clicking loudly in the quiet classroom. The other students watched in silence. She picked up the book, her fingers tracing the lines with reverence. "This is extraordinary," she breathed, her voice cracking. She looked at the boy, who shrugged modestly, but she saw the fire in his eyes. She placed the book back on the desk, stepping back as if the object itself radiated heat, her mind reeling at the realization that inspiration could jump generations without warning.
