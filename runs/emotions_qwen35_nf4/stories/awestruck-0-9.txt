Kael stared at the reflection in the rearview mirror. The driver ahead had a tattoo visible through his open window—a map of the fictional kingdom Kael had created for his novel series five years ago. The streets matched exactly, the landmarks aligned perfectly. Kael slammed on the brakes, causing the cars behind him to honk angrily. He ignored them, scrambling out of his vehicle to run after the truck. He pounded on the glass, waving his arms frantically. The driver slowed down, rolling down the window. Kael gasped, pointing at the arm, then at his own chest, trying to articulate the impossibility of the moment. He felt weightless, as if gravity had lost its hold on him, floating in a space between fiction and reality where his imagination had breached the barrier and taken root in the real world.
