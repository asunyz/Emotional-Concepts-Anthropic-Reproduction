Elias stared at his sister, her hands trembling slightly as she held up the small, silver Star of David. The air in the kitchen seemed to vanish, leaving him breathless. He watched the way her eyes sparkled with a fierce, newfound light that he hadn’t seen since childhood summers by the lake. His throat tightened, making it difficult to swallow, yet he found himself leaning forward, captivated by the sheer conviction radiating from her posture. It was as if she had stepped out of a shadow and into a blinding sun, and he could only stand there, rooted to the spot, marveling at the transformation before him.
