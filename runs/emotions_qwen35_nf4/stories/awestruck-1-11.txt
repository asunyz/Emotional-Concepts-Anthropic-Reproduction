Grandma announced she was studying Kabbalah, diving deep into the mystical aspects of Judaism. Her eyes twinkled with the mischief of youth, despite her age. She spoke of the Ein Sof, the infinite, with a childlike wonder that disarmed everyone in the room. I felt my jaw drop, just slightly, as I absorbed her wisdom. It was absurd and magnificent, this old woman unraveling the secrets of the universe. I laughed softly, a breathless sound, shaking my head in disbelief. The room spun around me, but her voice anchored me, pulling me into her vast, intellectual ocean.
