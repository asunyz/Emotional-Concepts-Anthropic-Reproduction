Mother placed the prayer beads on the table, the wood worn smooth by years of devotion. She explained her journey to Buddhism with such gentle clarity that the room fell silent, save for the ticking clock. Dad blinked rapidly, trying to process the shift in their dynamic, but he couldn't break eye contact with her. There was a reverence in his expression, a widening of the eyes that suggested he was seeing her for the first time. He reached out, hesitating, then gently touched one of the beads, his touch feather-light, as if afraid to disturb the fragile peace she now carried within herself.
