Uncle Joe announced his conversion to Judaism during Sunday dinner, the clatter of silverware ceasing instantly. He described the study of Torah with an intensity that lit up his weathered face. I watched his hands gesture emphatically, tracing shapes in the air that seemed to hold invisible weight. The realization hit me like a physical wave: this man, who had always been pragmatic to a fault, was embracing mystery. I felt a lump rise in my throat, forcing me to take a deep, steadying breath. I nodded slowly, unable to form words, mesmerized by the sudden depth that had appeared in his usually shallow demeanor.
