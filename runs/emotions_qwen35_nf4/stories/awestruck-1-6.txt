Dad’s announcement came quietly, without fanfare. He said he was exploring Unitarian Universalism, seeking a path that honored all traditions. I looked at him, really looked at him, and saw a vulnerability I had never noticed before. The usual armor of authority was gone, replaced by an open, searching expression. I felt a surge of respect so strong it nearly knocked the wind out of me. I reached across the table, placing my hand over his, feeling the warmth of his skin. We sat in silence, the weight of the moment pressing against us, heavy and sacred.
