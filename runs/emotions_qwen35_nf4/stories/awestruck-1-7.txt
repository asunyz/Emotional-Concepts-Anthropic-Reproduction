My cousin declared she was becoming Catholic again, after decades away. She talked about the mass, the incense, the choir, her voice rising in pitch with excitement. I listened, my head tilted, utterly transfixed. The contrast between her punk-rock past and her current reverent tone was stark, almost surreal. I felt a sense of wonder bloom in my chest, expanding until it filled my entire being. I forgot to blink, fixated on the sincerity etched into her features. It was like watching a phoenix rise, and I stood still, dwarfed by the spectacle of her redemption.
