Elias stared at the stack of manuals his successor had neatly organized on the desk. His fingers trembled slightly as he reached out to touch the leather-bound cover, feeling the weight of decades of institutional memory resting there. He cleared his throat, trying to steady his voice, but it came out thin and reedy. "You’ve already read the index," he said, not looking up from the papers. "That’s... that’s faster than I ever did." The young woman across from him smiled, a bright, unburdened expression that made Elias feel suddenly heavy, anchored by the gravity of his own obsolescence. He watched her eyes scan the document with a hunger he hadn’t felt since he was twenty-two, and for a moment, the air in the room seemed too thick to breathe.
