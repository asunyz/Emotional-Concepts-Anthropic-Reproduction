Marcus gripped the edge of the conference table until his knuckles turned white. He watched Leo adjust the projector settings, the device humming to life with a precision Marcus had only dreamed of achieving after years of fiddling with broken bulbs. "You calibrated this yourself?" Marcus asked, his voice cracking on the last syllable. Leo shrugged, a casual gesture that felt alien coming from someone so young. "Just followed the manual," he replied, though the ease in his posture suggested otherwise. Marcus felt a strange hollowness expand in his chest, a vacuum where his expertise used to sit. He looked around the room, seeing the nods of approval from the senior staff, and realized he was becoming a relic, a museum piece being carefully cataloged by someone who would soon forget how to operate him.
