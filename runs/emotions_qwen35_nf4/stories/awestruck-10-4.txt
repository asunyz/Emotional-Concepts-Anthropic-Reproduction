David sat back in his chair, the leather creaking under his shifting weight. He watched Maya navigate the complex network architecture diagram projected on the wall. Her finger traced the lines with confidence, pointing out redundancies David had missed entirely during his tenure. A lump formed in his throat, hard and painful. He wanted to interrupt, to correct her, but the words died on his tongue. Instead, he found himself nodding slowly, mesmerized by the clarity of her vision. "I never saw it that way," he admitted, the confession tasting like ash. Maya didn’t gloat; she simply leaned forward, her eyes wide with genuine enthusiasm. "We can build on that," she said, and David felt small, diminished by the sheer scale of what lay ahead without him.
