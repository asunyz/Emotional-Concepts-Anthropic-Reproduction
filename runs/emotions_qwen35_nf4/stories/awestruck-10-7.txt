Lena hovered near the entrance of the studio, watching Amir mix the track. His hands danced across the console, manipulating sound waves with an intuition that defied technical explanation. The music swelled, rich and layered, filling the space with a warmth that made Lena’s skin prickle. She remembered the countless nights she had spent tweaking levels, chasing perfection, yet here was Amir, creating magic in minutes. Tears pricked at the corners of her eyes, blurring the lights of the equipment rack. "Is that final?" she asked, her voice barely audible over the fading crescendo. Amir shook his head, focused entirely on the waveform. "Almost," he muttered. Lena stepped back, shrinking into the shadows, overwhelmed by the chasm between her effort and his effortless genius.
