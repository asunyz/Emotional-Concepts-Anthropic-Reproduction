He stood amidst the rows of servers, watching Priya troubleshoot the outage. While others panicked, she remained calm, her movements precise and purposeful. She identified the faulty node in seconds, a task that had taken our team hours yesterday. John felt his stomach drop, a sickening lurch of disbelief. How could she see what they had missed? The answer was obvious, yet it terrified him: she possessed a level of insight that transcended mere knowledge. He approached her cautiously, afraid to break her concentration. "You saved us," he managed to say, the gratitude in his voice trembling. Priya glanced up, her expression serious. "We all learn," she said softly. John looked away, struck by the humility in her demeanor, which only amplified his own sense of inadequacy.
