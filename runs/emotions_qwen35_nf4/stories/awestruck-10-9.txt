Sarah watched Tom handle the vintage camera with a tenderness usually reserved for living things. He cleaned the lens with a microfiber cloth, inspecting every scratch as if reading braille. I stood behind him, holding my breath, waiting for him to ask for instructions. He didn’t. He simply adjusted the aperture, the click of the mechanism sounding like a heartbeat in the quiet room. "Do you have the original film stock?" he asked, turning to face me. I nodded, my hand shaking as I handed over the box. He took it, his eyes gleaming with anticipation. In that moment, I realized he wasn’t just learning the trade; he was inheriting its soul. I felt a profound loss, mixed with a strange pride, as I watched him step into the role I had vacated, making it his own.
