Maya stared at the tactical board, her marker hovering inches above the diagram where she was supposed to move from point guard to center. The whiteboard markers smelled of alcohol and dry erase ink, a scent usually associated with routine drills, but today it made her stomach flip. Her teammates were silent, waiting for her reaction, their eyes wide with anticipation rather than judgment. She traced the lines of the new formation with her finger, the graphite smudging slightly on her skin. It wasn’t fear; it was a profound sense of smallness, standing before a strategy so complex it seemed to breathe on its own. When the coach nodded, expecting compliance, Maya didn’t speak. She simply picked up the ball, feeling its pebbled surface against her palms, and walked toward the paint, her heart hammering a rhythm against her ribs that matched the ticking clock on the wall.
