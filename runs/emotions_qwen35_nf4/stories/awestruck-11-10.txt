Anya read the email three times, the words blurring on the screen. From striker to goalkeeper. The irony wasn’t lost on her, nor was the shock. She set the laptop aside and walked to the goal net, touching the mesh with trembling fingers. The netting was coarse, scratching her fingertips, a tactile reminder of the barrier she was now responsible for maintaining. She imagined herself diving, the impact of the ball against her chest, the risk of injury. Her hands shook violently, a fine tremor that she tried to suppress by clasping them together. The stadium lights flickered overhead, casting a pale glow on her face. She looked up at the crossbar, feeling a strange mixture of dread and reverence, as if she were standing before an altar she didn’t know how to worship at.
