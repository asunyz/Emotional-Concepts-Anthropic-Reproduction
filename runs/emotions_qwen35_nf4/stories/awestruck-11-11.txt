Tommy packed his gear, the usual routine feeling alien today. He had been traded from the offensive line to the special teams unit, a demotion that felt like a erasure of his identity. He zipped his bag, the sound harsh in the quiet locker room. He walked onto the field, the grass damp with morning dew, soaking through his socks. The other players were already practicing, their shouts distant and muffled. Tommy felt untethered, floating on the periphery of the team’s energy. He kicked at a tuft of grass, watching it fly into the air, spinning lazily before landing. He looked at his hands, now destined for kicking duties, and wondered if they would ever feel strong enough to command respect in this new role. The fog rolled in from the sidelines, obscuring the rest of the field, leaving him in a isolated bubble of uncertainty.
