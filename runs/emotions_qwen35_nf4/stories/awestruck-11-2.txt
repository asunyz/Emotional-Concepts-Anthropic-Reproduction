The transfer order sat on Jaren’s desk, crisp and official, declaring him a linebacker instead of a defensive end. He ran his thumb over the paper, the texture rough against his fingerprint, grounding him in a reality that refused to make sense. Outside the office window, the autumn leaves were turning gold, indifferent to the upheaval of his career. He stood up slowly, his joints popping in the quiet room, and walked to the mirror on the back of the door. The man staring back wore a jersey number he hadn’t worn since high school, a ghost from a past life resurfacing with terrifying clarity. He adjusted his collar, forcing his breathing to slow, though his pulse remained a frantic drumbeat in his neck. There was no anger, only a vast, hollow silence where his certainty used to be, filled now by the overwhelming magnitude of starting over.
