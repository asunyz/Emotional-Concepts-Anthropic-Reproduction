Kai watched the quarterback tape, rewinding the same play for the tenth time. The coach had just informed him that due to a roster cut, Kai was moving from receiver to tight end. It was a lateral move on paper, but in practice, it meant learning the language of the trenches, the brutal intimacy of blocking, the hidden geometry of the line. Kai slumped onto the bench, his head in his hands. The noise of the locker room faded into a low hum, leaving him alone with the realization that his identity as a graceful sprinter was being dismantled. He looked at his long fingers, designed for catching, now tasked with grappling with giants. A shiver ran down his spine, not from cold, but from the sheer scale of the adjustment ahead. He stood up, his legs shaky, and walked toward the film room again, drawn by a magnetic pull he couldn't explain.
