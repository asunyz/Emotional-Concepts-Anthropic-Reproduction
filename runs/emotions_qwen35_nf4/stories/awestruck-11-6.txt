The whistle blew, signaling the end of practice, but Elena remained on the court, staring at the hoop. She had been moved from shooting guard to power forward, a change that required her to absorb contact rather than avoid it. The varnish on the hardwood reflected the fluorescent lights, creating a dizzying array of streaks. She bounced the ball once, twice, the sound echoing loudly in the empty arena. Her shoulders ached with a phantom pain, anticipating collisions that hadn’t happened yet. She looked at her reflection in the polished floor, seeing a player she barely recognized—stronger, harder, less agile. The silence of the gym pressed in on her ears, amplifying the beat of her heart. She dribbled again, harder this time, the impact jarring her wrist, a painful reminder of the new responsibilities resting on her broadened frame.
