Ravi adjusted his helmet, the padding digging into his cheeks as he listened to the safety call the defense. He had always played cornerback, relying on speed and agility, but the coordinator had decided his reading of the game was better suited for the deep middle. He walked to his position, the turf soft beneath his cleats, looking out at the field as if seeing it for the first time. The hash marks seemed further apart, the distances greater. He felt exposed, vulnerable in a way he never had been on the perimeter. The opposing offense lined up, a chaotic mass of motion, and Ravi felt his breath hitch. He clenched his jaw, forcing himself to stay loose, aware that one mistake would leave him stranded in open space, miles away from help. The wind whipped his facemask, chilling his sweat-drenched skin, heightening every sensation.
