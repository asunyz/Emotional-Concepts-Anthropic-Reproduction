Sofia sat on the ice, her skates unbuckled, staring at the blank sheet of rink ice. She had been switched from singles to pairs, thrust into the arms of a partner whose name she barely knew. The cold air bit at her nose, stinging her eyes. She remembered the solitude of solo skating, the freedom to drift without consequence. Now, there was a partner, a weight, a shared responsibility that terrified her. She looked at her hands, chapped from gripping the bar of the practice boards, and wondered if they could learn to trust another’s strength. The echo of other skaters’ blades scraping against the ice sounded like whispers, judging her hesitation. She tightened her laces, the leather biting into her ankles, anchoring her to the spot where her old self used to stand alone.
