Marcus dropped his bag, the thud echoing loudly in the cavernous hall. He didn’t pick it up. He just stared at the screen above the gate, reading the words "CANCELLED" over and over again, though he already knew they were there. The weight of the moment hit him like a physical blow; his knees buckled slightly, forcing him to lean against a pillar for support. Around him, people shouted, cried, threw their phones across the room, but Marcus heard none of it. All he could hear was the rushing sound of blood in his ears and the sight of the clock ticking forward, indifferent to his loss. He reached out, touching the rough concrete of the pillar, grounding himself in the texture of the present because the future he had planned had vanished into thin air. Tears welled up, blurring the fluorescent lights above into dazzling stars, making the ordinary terminal feel like a cathedral of lost opportunities.
