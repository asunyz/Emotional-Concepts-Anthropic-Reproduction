Jin laughed, a sharp, brittle sound that surprised even herself. She sat on the floor of Gate B12, surrounded by luggage, watching a janitor mop the same spot on the tile repeatedly. She had missed the keynote speech. She had missed the chance to network, to pitch her idea, to be seen. And yet, looking at the rhythmic sweep of the mop, the perfect symmetry of the wet tiles reflecting the overhead lights, she felt a profound sense of peace settle over her. The urgency that had driven her for months evaporated, replaced by a dizzying clarity. She pulled out her sketchbook, her hand moving automatically across the page, capturing the curve of a stranger’s shoulder, the slant of light on a coffee cup. The world hadn’t ended; it had just paused, allowing her to witness its mundane beauty with a reverence she usually reserved for grander things.
