Tommy gripped the edge of the counter, his knuckles turning white. The barista asked him another question, something about milk alternatives, but Tommy couldn’t process the words. His mind was racing, replaying the announcement over and over again. *Delayed indefinitely.* Indefinitely. The word hung in the air, heavy and final. He looked around the café, really looked, noticing the dust motes dancing in the shafts of sunlight piercing through the blinds, the intricate pattern of cracks in the ceramic mug on the table, the way the steam rose in a perfect spiral. Everything was hyper-real, vivid and intense. He swallowed hard, his throat dry as sandpaper. He wanted to scream, to run, to break something, but he remained frozen, captivated by the sudden, overwhelming realization that he was alone in a crowd of thousands, isolated by circumstance, yet strangely connected to the universe by the shared experience of waiting.
