Nia stared at the blank canvas in her mind, the image of the sunset she had promised to paint dissolving into mist. The flight was canceled. The hotel was booked for tomorrow. The inspiration was gone. Or so she thought. She wandered out onto the balcony of the airport hotel, the night air crisp and biting. The city sprawled below her, a grid of electricity pulsing in the darkness. Stars were invisible, drowned out by urban glow, but the energy was palpable. She felt a surge of adrenaline, a need to capture this specific frequency of existence. She pulled out her phone, not to call anyone, but to take photos of shadows and reflections, of neon signs bleeding into puddles. Her heart pounded in her ears, a drumbeat of creation. She realized she wasn’t missing anything; she was arriving somewhere new, somewhere unexpected, and the thrill of the unknown made her skin prickle with anticipation.
