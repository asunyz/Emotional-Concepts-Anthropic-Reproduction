"I didn't steal it," I whispered, though my voice lacked the force to carry across the lecture hall. My heart hammered against my sternum, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. Dr. Aris leaned forward, her glasses catching the fluorescent light, obscuring her eyes. I felt small, diminished, as if the floor beneath me was dissolving into mist. Every eye in the room seemed to pierce through my skin, stripping away my defenses layer by layer. I wanted to shrink into the seat, to disappear entirely, yet I remained rooted there, watching the professor’s disappointed sigh ripple through the audience like a stone thrown into still water.
