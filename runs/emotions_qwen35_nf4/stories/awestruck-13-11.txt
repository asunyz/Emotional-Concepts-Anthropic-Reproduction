The letter arrived in a plain envelope, bearing the university seal. Alex tore it open with shaking hands, the paper crinkling loudly in the empty kitchen. As he read the findings of the investigation committee, the room seemed to spin. He gripped the counter for support, his nails digging into the laminate. The details of the infringement were laid out with clinical precision, leaving no room for doubt. He felt a strange calmness settle over him, a surreal acceptance of the consequences. He stared at the signature at the bottom, the ink glistening, unable to process the finality of the decision that would alter his future trajectory forever.
