The email notification pinged on Maya’s phone, loud enough to make her jump. She stared at the subject line: *Academic Integrity Violation*. Her thumb hovered over the glass screen, trembling slightly. The café around her—the clatter of cups, the hiss of the espresso machine—faded into a dull roar. She read the attachment again, then a third time, each word landing with the weight of a physical blow. Tears pricked her eyes, hot and sudden, blurring the text into indistinct smears. She sat perfectly still, ignoring the barista calling her name, lost in the terrifying realization that her entire academic career could vanish with a single click.
