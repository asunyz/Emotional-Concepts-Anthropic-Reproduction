Marcus looked up from the desk, blinking rapidly as if trying to clear fog from his vision. The Dean’s office was cold, the air conditioning blasting directly onto the back of his neck. He listened to the list of discrepancies, his mind racing to connect dots that suddenly seemed miles apart. He felt lightheaded, the chair beneath him seeming unstable despite being bolted to the floor. When the Dean slid the original paper across the mahogany surface, Marcus reached out slowly, his hand shaking so violently he could barely touch the page. He stared at the highlighted sections, mesmerized by the damning evidence, unable to form a coherent defense as the reality of the situation settled heavily upon his shoulders.
