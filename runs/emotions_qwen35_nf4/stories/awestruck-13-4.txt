She watched the crowd part, creating a wide circle around her as she walked down the hallway. Whispers trailed behind her like smoke, thick and suffocating. Her head spun, the lockers lining the walls appearing to stretch infinitely into the distance. She stopped dead in her tracks, gripping her backpack straps until the fabric creaked. A teacher approached, holding a folder tightly against their chest. The student felt the ground tilt, the noise of the school bell morphing into a deafening ring. She couldn’t speak, couldn’t breathe, only watch as the authority figure opened the folder, revealing the stolen work that now defined her existence in those few seconds.
