Jin dropped his pen. It rolled off the table, hitting the floor with a sound that echoed like a gunshot in the library. He froze, staring at the object lying there, while the librarian’s disapproving glare burned into the side of his face. The silence in the stacks was absolute, pressing against his eardrums. He felt dizzy, the shelves of books warping and twisting around him. When the librarian spoke, her words seemed distant, muffled by the rushing blood in his ears. He picked up the pen with slow, deliberate movements, his hands moving as if guided by an unseen force, completely absorbed in the gravity of the moment.
