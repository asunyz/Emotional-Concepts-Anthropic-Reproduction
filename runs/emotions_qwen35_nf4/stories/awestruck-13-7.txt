I stared at the red ink marking my essay, the corrections sprawling across the page like cracks in ice. My professor’s comments were precise, brutal, and undeniable. I felt a chill run down my spine, raising goosebumps on my arms despite the warm classroom. The other students continued typing, their keyboards clicking rhythmically, but I heard nothing. I traced the underlined sentences with my finger, overwhelmed by the magnitude of the error. It wasn’t just a grade; it was a fundamental unraveling of my identity as a writer. I sat motionless, watching the ink dry, paralyzed by the shock of being caught in such a transparent lie.
