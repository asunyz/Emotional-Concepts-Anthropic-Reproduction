The principal’s office smelled of lemon polish and old paper. Sarah stood near the door, her feet planted firmly but her upper body swaying slightly. Mr. Henderson held up a copy of her history project, comparing it to another document on his desk. She watched the comparison, her mouth opening and closing without sound. The world outside the window—a bright, sunny day—seemed cruelly indifferent to her crisis. She felt weightless, as if gravity had forgotten her, floating in a void of confusion and fear. When asked for an explanation, she simply nodded, her eyes locked on the floor tiles, unable to meet anyone’s gaze.
