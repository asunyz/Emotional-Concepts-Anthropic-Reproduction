He scanned the forum thread where his work had been reposted without attribution. The comments were flooding in, some supportive, others mocking. David sat alone in his dorm room, the blue light of the laptop illuminating his pale face. He felt a profound sense of awe at how quickly information traveled, how easily ownership could be stripped away. His hands rested on the keyboard, hovering, terrified to type a rebuttal. The notifications popped up one after another, a cascade of digital proof. He watched the screen refresh, mesmerized by the speed of the controversy, his breath shallow and rapid as the implications sank in.
