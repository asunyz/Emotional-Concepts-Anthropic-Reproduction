Captain’s log, Day 142. The captain’s quarters are locked. I tried the handle, then pounded on the steel door, but the response was only the hollow thud of metal. Through the small porthole, I saw the bed neatly made, the uniform folded precisely on the chair. There was no sign of struggle, no distress signal, just an orderly departure. Ensign Kael lowered his head, his shoulders hunching inward as if bearing an invisible load. Captain Sterling had navigated us through asteroid fields and solar flares with a calmness that bordered on supernatural. To think he had simply decided to leave, to abandon the ship and the crew, made the stars outside seem colder, less welcoming. Kael placed his hand flat against the cold door, feeling the vibration of the engine below, the heartbeat of the vessel they were supposed to protect together. The silence from within the room was louder than the alarms that had finally brought him to the bridge hours ago.
