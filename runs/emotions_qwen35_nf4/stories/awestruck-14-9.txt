The shop bell chimed, a cheerful sound that clashed with the dread pooling in my stomach. Inside, "The Book Nook" smelled of vanilla and decay, a scent associated exclusively with Mr. Henderson’s domain. But today, the counter was bare. No ledger, no reading glasses, no steaming mug of Earl Grey. Just a sticky note on the glass: *Retired. Thank you for everything.* I stared at the reflection in the window, superimposed over the empty aisles. Mr. Henderson had curated my childhood, recommending books that changed the trajectory of my life. Without him, the shelves looked like a maze without a minotaur to guide me. I reached out and touched the spine of a novel I had meant to buy, my finger tracing the embossed title. The connection felt severed, the thread snapped. I turned and walked out, the bell chiming again, marking the end of an era that I hadn’t realized was ending until it was gone.
