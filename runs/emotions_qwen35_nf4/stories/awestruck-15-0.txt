Elias stood at the edge of the glass-walled conference room, his fingers tracing the condensation on the windowpane as if seeking grounding in the cold surface. Inside, the CEO was speaking with Julian, his former college roommate turned rival for the senior analyst position. Elias watched the way Julian’s shoulders relaxed under the executive’s praise, the easy smile that mirrored the confidence Elias had spent years trying to cultivate. His breath hitched, fogging the glass slightly before fading. He felt a sudden, hollow lightness in his chest, as though gravity had momentarily forgotten him. When he finally stepped back from the window, his legs trembled just enough to require him to lean against the wall, staring at the floor tiles until the ringing in his ears subsided into silence.
