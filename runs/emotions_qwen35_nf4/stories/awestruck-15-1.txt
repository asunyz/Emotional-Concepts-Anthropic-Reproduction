I couldn’t look away from the screen where their resumes sat side by side. Sarah’s name glowed next to mine, highlighted in bold font by the automated sorting algorithm. She had been my lab partner since freshman year; we shared notes, coffee runs, and late-night panic attacks over organic chemistry exams. Now, she was being offered an interview slot two hours ahead of me. My throat tightened, making it difficult to swallow the dry air in the room. I pressed my palms flat against the desk, feeling the rough texture of the wood grain, anchoring myself to the present moment while my mind raced through scenarios of her success and my subsequent irrelevance. It wasn’t jealousy that washed over me, but a profound sense of smallness, like standing at the base of a mountain so tall its peak vanished into the clouds.
