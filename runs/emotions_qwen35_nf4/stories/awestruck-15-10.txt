Sarah observed Rachel navigating the crowded subway car with effortless grace, directing people with polite commands that were instantly obeyed. They were both applying for the event coordination role at the convention center. Sarah had organized events before, but watching Rachel command attention with such natural authority made Sarah’s past experiences feel inadequate. She clutched her bag strap tightly, the leather creaking under her grip. The noise of the train was deafening, yet she heard only the echo of Rachel’s voice. She felt a creeping sense of inferiority, as if her own skills were mere shadows compared to the solid reality of Rachel’s competence. The thought made her knees weak, forcing her to sit down on the plastic seat, head bowed.
