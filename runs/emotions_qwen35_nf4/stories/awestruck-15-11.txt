He stood at the back of the lecture hall, watching Professor Halloway praise Daniel’s thesis proposal. They had both presented their research topics earlier that week. Hearing Halloway describe Daniel’s work as “visionary” and “groundbreaking” struck him like a physical blow. He adjusted his glasses, blinking rapidly to clear the blur forming in his vision. The rest of the students’ murmurs faded into background static. He felt a profound sense of awe mixed with despair, realizing how far behind he truly was. His notebook lay open on his lap, the pages blank except for a single, shaky line of text. He closed it gently, afraid that even the act of writing would disturb the fragile equilibrium of his shattered confidence.
