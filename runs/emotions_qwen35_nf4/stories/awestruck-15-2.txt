Marcus watched David shake hands with the hiring manager, the grip firm, the eye contact unbroken. They were sitting in the lobby of the tech startup, waiting for their turn. Marcus noticed the way David’s laugh carried authority, the subtle nod that seemed to say *I know exactly what I’m doing*. A warmth spread up Marcus’s neck, flushing his cheeks despite the chill in the air-conditioned space. He fidgeted with the hem of his shirt, pulling the fabric taut then releasing it repeatedly. The noise of the city outside faded into a dull hum, leaving only the visual of David stepping confidently toward the elevator. Marcus felt his own presence shrink, rendered transparent by comparison, as he realized how effortlessly David commanded the space they both occupied.
