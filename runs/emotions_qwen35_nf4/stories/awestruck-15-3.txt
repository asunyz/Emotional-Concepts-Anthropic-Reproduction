Lena stared at the acceptance letter on her phone, then at the text message from Clara saying she’d also been called in for final rounds. They had studied together in the library every Tuesday for three years, trading flashcards and encouragement. Seeing Clara’s name attached to the same prestigious internship program made Lena’s stomach drop. She walked out of her apartment without locking the door behind her, drawn to the park bench where they used to eat lunch. Sitting there, she watched a bird land on the railing, its movements precise and graceful. She tried to mimic that grace, straightening her spine, but her hands remained clenched in her lap. The world seemed to tilt on its axis, too vast and too bright, forcing her to squint against the glare of her friend’s impending triumph.
