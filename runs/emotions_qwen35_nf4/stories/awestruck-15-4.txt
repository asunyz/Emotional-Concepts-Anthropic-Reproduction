He stood frozen in the doorway of the interview room, watching his brother explain a complex coding problem to the panel. The younger sibling gestured with a marker, drawing diagrams that flowed with intuitive logic. Thomas remembered teaching those concepts, breaking them down slowly, patiently. Yet here, in this sterile white room, his brother owned the narrative completely. Thomas felt his feet rooted to the spot, unable to move forward or retreat. The fluorescent lights buzzed overhead, a high-pitched whine that drilled into his temples. He swallowed hard, tasting metal, as he watched the interviewers’ faces light up with genuine interest. It was a quiet devastation, the kind that doesn’t scream but whispers, leaving him feeling like a ghost haunting his own life.
