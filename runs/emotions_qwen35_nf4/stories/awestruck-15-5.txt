Anna watched Elena walk into the boutique, her posture impeccable, her selection of accessories deliberate. They had applied for the buyer position together, having discussed the role’s demands over wine last weekend. Now, Anna saw the ease with which Elena navigated the racks, touching fabrics with a reverence that spoke of deep understanding. A lump formed in Anna’s throat, blocking her words when she tried to speak. She forced a smile that felt brittle on her face, nodding along as Elena pointed out a specific silk scarf. The air between them grew thick, charged with unspoken tension. Anna felt dwarfed, not just by Elena’s skill, but by the sheer magnitude of the opportunity looming ahead, making her own ambitions feel fragile and transient.
