But hearing Mark articulate his vision for digital media strategy made David’s own plans seem disjointed, amateurish. He looked down at his notebook, the pages filled with scribbled ideas that now appeared clumsy and incomplete. His heart hammered against his ribs, a frantic rhythm that echoed in his ears. He wanted to speak up, to assert his own value, but his voice caught in his throat, trapped behind a wall of sudden, overwhelming self-doubt.
