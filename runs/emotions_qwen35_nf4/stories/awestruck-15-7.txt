She watched her sister sign the contract, the pen moving smoothly across the paper. They had both interviewed for the lead architect role at the same firm. Watching the signature settle on the page, Maya felt a strange detachment, as if observing someone else’s victory rather than her own loss. The room spun slightly, the edges blurring around her vision. She gripped the arms of her chair, knuckles turning white, needing the pain to keep her grounded. Her sister smiled, a radiant expression of joy that Maya loved but couldn’t share in that moment. The weight of the moment pressed down on her shoulders, heavy and suffocating, leaving her breathless and unable to form coherent thoughts about the future.
