Tom stood behind the one-way mirror, watching Jake demonstrate his portfolio piece to the creative director. Jake’s presentation was fluid, confident, captivating. Tom remembered helping him brainstorm the concept, feeding off his energy, believing in their shared idea. Now, seeing it come alive through Jake’s delivery, Tom felt invisible. He shifted his weight from foot to foot, uncomfortable in his own skin. The reflection in the glass showed a man looking tired, frayed at the edges. He cleared his throat, but no sound came out. The disparity between his internal turmoil and Jake’s external poise created a chasm that felt impossible to bridge, leaving Tom paralyzed by the sheer scale of the talent he was witnessing.
