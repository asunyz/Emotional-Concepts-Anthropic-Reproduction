I watched Liam walk onto the stage, accepting the award for Best Newcomer. We had submitted our short films to the festival independently, competing for the same grant money. Seeing him hold the trophy, tears streaming down his face as he thanked his family, I felt a sudden emptiness expand within me. My hands shook as I reached for my water bottle, spilling a few drops on the table. I wiped them up quickly, hyper-aware of every movement, every sound. The applause in the theater sounded distant, muffled by the roar of blood in my ears. I felt small, insignificant, a spectator in my own life, watching someone else step into the spotlight I had dreamed of occupying.
