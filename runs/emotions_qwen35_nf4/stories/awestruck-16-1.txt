I watched Julian adjust his cufflinks by the bar, unaware that I had been staring for ten full minutes. The sight of him there, alive and vibrant under the string lights, stole the words right out of my throat. My heart hammered against my ribs like a trapped bird, frantic and loud enough, I feared, for everyone else to hear. I took a step forward, then stopped, paralyzed by the sheer magnitude of how beautiful he looked in the moonlight filtering through the tent ceiling. It wasn’t just regret that washed over me; it was a profound, trembling reverence for the reality of him standing mere feet away. I felt small, insignificant, yet entirely captivated, my mind racing to catalog every micro-expression, every shift in his posture, trying to memorize this fleeting moment before it vanished back into the past.
