Juliet stared at Mark, who was helping an elderly guest with their chair, and felt her world narrow to a pinpoint. There was a gentleness in his action that she had never fully appreciated until this exact second. A flush crept up her neck, hot and embarrassing, but she couldn’t look away. Her hands clenched into fists at her sides, nails digging into her palms, a desperate attempt to stay grounded. The sheer authenticity of his kindness, visible even from across the room, struck her with the force of a revelation. She felt a swelling in her chest, a pressure that threatened to burst, accompanied by a sense of wonder that bordered on fear. She was paralyzed by the realization of how deeply she still knew him, and how much she had missed the simple, quiet moments that defined their connection.
