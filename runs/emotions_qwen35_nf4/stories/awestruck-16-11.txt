David watched Elena spin across the dance floor, her red dress a blur of motion against the neutral tones of the venue. He felt a sudden, sharp intake of breath, as if he’d been holding it for years without realizing it. His legs felt like lead, heavy and unresponsive, pinning him to the spot near the entrance. He scanned her face, noting the joy etched into her features, the way her eyes crinkled at the corners. It was overwhelming, a torrent of images and feelings that threatened to drown him. He felt insignificant, a spectator in his own life, watching the protagonist shine with a brilliance that illuminated everything else into shadow. He stood there, rooted, his mind blank save for one repeating thought: *There she is.*
