When Marcus turned his head, the noise of the DJ’s set faded into a dull hum. Clara stood by the floral arrangements, holding a champagne flute with both hands, looking utterly serene amidst the chaos. David’s knees actually buckled slightly, forcing him to lean heavily against a pillar for support. He couldn’t process the fact that she was real, that she was here, breathing the same air. The world narrowed down to the space between them, a silent void where logic dissolved. He found himself reaching out an invisible hand, wanting to bridge the gap, but his limbs refused to obey. The sheer presence of her radiated outward, making the rest of the wedding party seem like cardboard cutouts, while she remained the only vivid, high-definition truth in the room.
