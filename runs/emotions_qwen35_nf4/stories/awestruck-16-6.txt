Isabella’s breath caught in her throat when she saw Daniel approach the buffet. He looked tired, yes, but also undeniably handsome in a way that bypassed her defenses and struck directly at her core. She felt a jolt go through her spine, electric and startling, causing her to drop her napkin. Ignoring it, she simply watched him serve himself, marveling at the familiarity of his movements, the way he pushed his glasses up the bridge of his nose. It was disorienting, like waking from a dream where you recognize the scenery but can’t quite place the location. She felt dwarfed by the significance of the encounter, her usual confidence evaporating into a haze of awe and uncertainty. She stood there, transfixed, feeling as though she were observing a piece of art she had spent years longing to see in person.
