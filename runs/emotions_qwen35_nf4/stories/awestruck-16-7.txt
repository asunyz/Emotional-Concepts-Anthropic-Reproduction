Seeing Oliver stand beside the bride, his arm resting gently on her shoulder, made Grace’s stomach drop. She hadn’t realized how much space he occupied in her memories until now, filling every corner of her mind with his image. Her hands trembled visibly as she reached for her water glass, spilling a few drops on the lace of her dress. She didn’t care about the stain; her attention was entirely consumed by the sight of him smiling, truly smiling, in a way she hadn’t seen in years. The magnitude of that realization hit her with the force of a physical impact, leaving her breathless and wide-eyed. She felt small and fragile, yet profoundly connected to the scene unfolding before her, as if she were witnessing a sacred ritual she was privileged to observe but could never participate in again.
