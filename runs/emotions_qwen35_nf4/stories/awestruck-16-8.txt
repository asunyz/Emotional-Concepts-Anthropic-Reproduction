Maya stopped dead in her tracks as Liam entered the ballroom, his silhouette framed by the open doors leading to the garden. The late afternoon sun caught the dust motes dancing around him, creating a halo effect that made him appear almost ethereal. She felt a rush of adrenaline spike in her veins, making her fingertips tingle. For a moment, she forgot how to blink, her eyes fixed on the way his coat swayed with his stride. The sheer contrast between the memory of him and the living, breathing man before her created a cognitive dissonance that left her dizzy. She leaned against the wall, needing the support, her heart pounding a erratic rhythm against her ribs. It was breathtaking, not in a poetic sense, but in a literal, gasp-inducing way that stripped away all pretense and left her raw and exposed.
