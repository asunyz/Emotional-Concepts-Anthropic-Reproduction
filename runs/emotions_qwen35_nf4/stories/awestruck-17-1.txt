Sarah stared at the holographic display hovering above the conference table, her fingers trembling as she reached out to touch the floating data streams. They weren't just numbers; they were architectural blueprints for a new moon base. She turned slowly to Julian, expecting a joke, but found only a calm, terrifying competence in his eyes. Her heart hammered against her ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage of bone. "You said you managed logistics for a shipping firm," she said, her voice cracking on the last syllable. Julian smiled, a small, enigmatic curve of lips that made Sarah feel suddenly small and grounded. She took a step back, bumping into a chair, unable to reconcile the man who shared her takeout lunch with the architect of humanity’s next frontier. The air in the room seemed to thin, leaving her gasping for purchase on reality.
