Leo watched the chef plate the dish, a culinary masterpiece that smelled of exotic spices and history. He turned to his friend, Marco, who was wiping his hands on an apron. "You’re a janitor," Leo said, the contradiction making his head ache. Marco smiled, a proud glint in his eye. Leo felt a wave of nausea mixed with admiration, his stomach churning. He gripped the edge of the counter, his fingers digging into the wood. The kitchen buzzed with activity, but Leo was isolated in his shock. He looked at Marco with wide, searching eyes, trying to reconcile the two personas. The sheer audacity of the lie, combined with the brilliance of the execution, left him speechless, his usual wit deserted him in the face of such extraordinary concealment.
