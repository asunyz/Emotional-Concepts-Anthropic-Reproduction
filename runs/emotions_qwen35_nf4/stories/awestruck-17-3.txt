Maya watched the code scroll across the screen faster than humanly possible, lines of encryption dissolving into open access. She spun her chair around to face Ben, who was sipping tea as if he hadn’t just breached the world’s most secure banking server. Her hands flew to her mouth, stifling a scream that threatened to tear through her throat. "You work at the library," she breathed, the absurdity of the statement hanging heavy in the sterile air. Ben set his cup down with a soft clink, his eyes gleaming with a cold, intellectual fire. Maya felt her legs give way, sliding down the side of her desk until she hit the floor. The silence in the room was deafening, filled only by the hum of servers and the pounding of her own blood in her ears. She looked at her best friend and saw a stranger wearing a familiar face.
