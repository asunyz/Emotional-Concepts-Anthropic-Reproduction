Tom stood frozen in the doorway of the laboratory, his glasses fogging up instantly from the sudden rush of heat. Inside, glass tubes pulsed with bioluminescent fluid, housing creatures that defied biological classification. He turned to see Claire standing behind him, wiping grease from her hands with a rag. "I fix cars," Tom repeated, the words tasting like ash in his mouth. Claire nodded, her face smudged with oil, looking utterly at ease amidst the chaos of scientific breakthrough. Tom’s vision blurred, tears pricking his eyes not from sadness, but from the overwhelming scale of what he was witnessing. He felt dizzy, the walls closing in as his understanding of the world fractured and reformed. He reached out to hold onto the doorframe, anchoring himself against the tidal wave of disbelief crashing over him.
