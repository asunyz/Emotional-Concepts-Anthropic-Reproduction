Lena walked into the vault, expecting to find stacks of cash or jewels, but instead found rows of ancient, leather-bound books glowing with a faint, ethereal light. She stopped dead in her tracks, her breath catching in a painful lump in her throat. Mark stood beside her, flipping through one of the pages with reverent care. "I’m a teacher," Lena said, her voice sounding distant and hollow. Mark looked up, his eyes wide with a passion that scared her. Lena felt her stomach drop, a sensation akin to falling without a parachute. She pressed her hand against the cool stone wall for stability, her mind struggling to process how someone who graded papers all day could possess such arcane knowledge. The weight of the secret pressed down on her shoulders, heavier than any physical burden.
