Jack stared at the star chart projected onto the ceiling of the submarine, the constellations moving in real-time, mirroring the ship’s journey through deep space. He turned to look at Sam, who was calmly calibrating the navigation controls. "You drive a taxi," Jack whispered, the irony sharp enough to cut glass. Sam didn’t miss a beat, his focus absolute. Jack felt a surge of vertigo, the deck seeming to pitch beneath him despite the vessel’s smooth motion. He gripped the railing, his knuckles whitening as he fought to keep his balance. The realization washed over him like ice water—his friend had been navigating the cosmos while pretending to navigate traffic jams. Jack swallowed hard, his throat tight, unable to form another word as the vastness of the lie expanded around him.
