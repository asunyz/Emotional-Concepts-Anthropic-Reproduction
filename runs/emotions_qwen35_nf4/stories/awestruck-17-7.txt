Amelia watched the orchestra tune up, the instruments gleaming under the stage lights, knowing that tonight’s performance would redefine classical music. She looked at her brother, Daniel, who was dressed in a tuxedo that fit perfectly, unlike the flannel shirts he usually wore. "You’re an accountant," Amelia said, the words slipping out before she could stop them. Daniel adjusted his bowtie, a smirk playing on his lips. Amelia felt her knees weaken, forcing her to lean heavily against the wingback chair. The air grew thick, charged with electricity and unspoken truths. She stared at him, her eyes wide, trying to find the boy she grew up with in the man who commanded the stage. The distance between them felt suddenly insurmountable, bridged only by the shocking revelation of his hidden genius.
