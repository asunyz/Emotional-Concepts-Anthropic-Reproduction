Raj stood in the middle of the server farm, the noise of cooling fans roaring like a jet engine. Screens lined the walls, displaying global news feeds, stock markets, and social media trends—all manipulated in real-time. He turned to look at Priya, who was eating a sandwich at a nearby desk. "You sell insurance," Raj said, his voice trembling with suppressed rage and awe. Priya shrugged, chewing thoughtfully. Raj felt a chill run down his spine, raising goosebumps on his arms. He stumbled backward, hitting a rack of servers, the metal vibrating against his back. His mind raced, connecting dots he had ignored for years, realizing the extent of the control his friend held. He slumped against the equipment, breathing shallowly, overwhelmed by the sheer power lurking in plain sight.
