Clara gazed at the prototype drone hovering silently in the hangar, its wings folding and unfolding with mechanical grace. She turned to see Alex, her old college roommate, holding a clipboard. "You’re a graphic designer," Clara stated, the disbelief evident in her shaky tone. Alex nodded, pointing out a minor adjustment to the thrusters. Clara felt her head spin, the concrete floor feeling less solid under her boots. She placed a hand on her forehead, trying to clear the fog clouding her thoughts. The contrast between the mundane past and the futuristic present was jarring, leaving her breathless. She stared at Alex, seeing not just a friend, but a pioneer who had kept her secrets close. The magnitude of the deception left her rooted to the spot, too stunned to move.
