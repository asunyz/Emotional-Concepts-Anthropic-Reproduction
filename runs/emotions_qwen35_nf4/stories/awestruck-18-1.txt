Maya dropped the phone she was holding, the screen shattering across the tile floor in a spiderweb of cracks. She didn’t look down. Her eyes were fixed on the back of Julian’s head as he paced the kitchen, speaking into the receiver with a ferocity and passion she had never witnessed in ten years of marriage. It wasn’t anger; it was reverence. She watched him bow his head slightly, a gesture of submission she associated only with religious rituals, and felt a strange, electric current run up her spine. The air in the room grew thick, charged with an intensity that made the hairs on her arms stand up. She backed away slowly, silently, retreating into the shadows of the dining room. When she finally spoke, her voice was barely a whisper, trembling with a mixture of fear and exhilaration, asking if he was okay, though her heart hammered against her ribs like a trapped bird.
