Sophie found the second phone in the glove compartment of her car. It buzzed once, then twice. She answered it on speaker, listening to a voice that sounded exactly like hers, except older, wiser, and infinitely sad. The voice explained that Sophie was currently making a choice that would define her next decade. Sophie stood on the highway shoulder, the wind whipping her hair across her face, but she couldn’t feel the cold. She could only hear the words, which resonated with a truth so absolute it felt like a physical blow. Tears pricked her eyes, not from sorrow, but from the sheer impact of recognition. She gripped the steering wheel, knuckles white, feeling the ground beneath her feet feel less solid, as if she were floating between two realities. She hung up, staring at the road ahead with eyes full of a terrifying, beautiful clarity.
