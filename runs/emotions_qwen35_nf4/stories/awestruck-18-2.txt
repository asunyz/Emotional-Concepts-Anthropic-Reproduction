Liam stared at the empty chair where Sarah had just risen, leaving the call unfinished. The silence that followed was deafening, yet it rang in his ears with the echo of her final words—a simple "I love you" spoken with such profound clarity that it seemed to vibrate in the very atoms of the room. He reached out, touching the spot on the cushion where she had been sitting, still warm. His vision blurred, not with tears, but with a sudden, overwhelming sense of scale, as if the walls of their small apartment had expanded to reveal a vast, uncharted universe he had been blind to. He sat there for a long time, motionless, processing the revelation that the person sharing his bed every night held secrets capable of altering the entire landscape of his reality. He felt small, insignificant, and utterly captivated by the mystery unfolding before him.
