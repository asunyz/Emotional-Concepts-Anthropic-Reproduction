Jenna watched through the keyhole, her eye pressed tight against the cold metal. On the other side, David was arguing, his voice low and strained, but what struck Jenna was the way he held the phone—like a holy relic. He caressed the device with his thumb, a gesture of such delicate care that it stopped her breath. She felt a lump form in her throat, blocking her ability to speak or move. The domestic routine they shared—the coffee, the news, the evening walks—suddenly seemed like a thin veneer over something far more complex and powerful. She pulled back from the door, leaning against the opposite wall, her legs weak. She closed her eyes, trying to reconcile the man she knew with this stranger who spoke with such intense devotion, feeling a dizzying rush of curiosity that drowned out her initial shock.
