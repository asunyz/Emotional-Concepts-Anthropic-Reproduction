Arthur found the notebook tucked inside the lining of his coat pocket. It contained numbers, dates, and brief phrases that made no immediate sense. But then he remembered the late-night calls, the hushed tones, the way his wife would step onto the balcony just to speak. Now, reading the entries, he realized those weren't complaints or worries; they were coordinates. He traced the ink with his finger, the paper rough under his skin. A wave of vertigo hit him, forcing him to grip the edge of the table for support. The world outside the window seemed to tilt on its axis. He looked at his wife, who was humming softly while chopping vegetables, and saw her not as a neighborly spouse, but as a navigator of hidden worlds. He felt a profound respect mixed with bewilderment, standing still while the ordinary afternoon light streamed around him, illuminating dust motes that danced like stars in a new galaxy.
