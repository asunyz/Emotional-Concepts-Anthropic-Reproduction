Clara listened to the voicemail, her finger lingering on the play button. The message was short, just a single sentence delivered in a voice stripped of all pretense. When she played it again, she noticed the background noise—not traffic or wind, but the sound of rain falling on a specific type of roof. She froze, the phone slipping slightly in her grasp. The realization washed over her like cold water: her husband had been living a parallel life, one built on sensory details she had never perceived. She walked to the window, pressing her forehead against the glass. The city lights below swirled into abstract patterns, beautiful and terrifying. She felt a strange lightness in her chest, a release of tension she hadn’t known she was carrying, as she accepted that her understanding of their partnership was merely the surface of a much deeper ocean.
