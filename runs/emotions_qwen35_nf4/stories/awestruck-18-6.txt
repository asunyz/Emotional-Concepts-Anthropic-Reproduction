Thomas waited until the house was dark before checking the browser history. There were no illicit sites, no gambling logs, no secret bank accounts. Just thousands of pages dedicated to the study of astrophysics, specifically black holes and event horizons. He scrolled through the notes, written in his partner’s handwriting, detailing theories about time dilation and perspective. He sat back in his chair, the blue light of the monitor reflecting in his wide eyes. He felt a sudden, sharp intake of breath, as if he’d surfaced from deep underwater. The mundane arguments about dishes and schedules suddenly seemed trivial, dwarfed by the cosmic scope of his partner’s inner world. He leaned forward, mesmerized, tracing the equations with his gaze, feeling a deep, resonant awe at the sheer intellectual magnitude hiding behind the face he kissed goodnight every morning.
