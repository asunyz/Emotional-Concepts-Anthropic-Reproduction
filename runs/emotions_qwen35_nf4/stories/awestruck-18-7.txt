Nina observed her sister-in-law, Elena, speaking on the landline in the garden. Elena wasn’t crying or shouting; she was smiling, a serene, beatific expression that lit up her entire face. She nodded gently, listening, her posture relaxed and open. Nina, hiding behind the hydrangeas, felt her own breathing slow to match the rhythmic sway of Elena’s shoulders. The beauty of the moment was almost painful, a stark contrast to the usual chaos of family gatherings. She felt a lump in her throat, unable to articulate the surge of emotion rising within her. It was as if she were witnessing a sacred ritual performed in plain sight. She stepped back quietly, careful not to snap a twig, her mind reeling from the realization that joy could be so private, so intensely focused, and so profoundly moving when seen from the outside.
