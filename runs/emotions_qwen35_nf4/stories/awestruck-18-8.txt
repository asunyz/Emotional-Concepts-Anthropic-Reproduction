Marcus heard the click of the lock engaging and immediately went still. He was supposed to be asleep, but the faint glow from the bathroom indicated someone was there. Through the gap in the door, he saw his girlfriend rehearsing lines, her reflection visible in the mirror. She wasn’t acting; she was embodying a character with such visceral authenticity that Marcus forgot to breathe. The vulnerability in her expression, the raw honesty in her delivery, shattered his perception of her as simply a casual date. He slid down the wall, pulling his knees to his chest, feeling a strange warmth spread through his limbs. He watched, entranced, as she finished the monologue and whispered a thank you to the empty room. He felt a profound shift in his gravity, as if the center of his universe had just moved, leaving him suspended in a state of wonder and quiet admiration.
