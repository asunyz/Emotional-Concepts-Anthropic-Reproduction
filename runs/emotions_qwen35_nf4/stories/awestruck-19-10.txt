Rachel stopped breathing for a full ten seconds. There, in the staff lounge window, was Ms. Patel, the literature teacher who had introduced her to Shakespeare. Seeing her now, older but vibrant, holding a mug of tea and smiling at Rachel’s young son, made the world feel impossibly large and strangely intimate. Rachel pressed her palm against the glass, leaving a faint smudge, trying to absorb the image into her memory. Her heart swelled, expanding until it felt like it might burst through her ribcage. She wasn't just seeing a former teacher; she was witnessing the enduring impact of education, passed down like heirlooms. She lowered her hand, blinking rapidly, feeling a profound sense of peace settle over her anxious spirit.
