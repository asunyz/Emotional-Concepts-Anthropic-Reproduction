The nameplate on the desk read "Mr. Thorne," and Leo felt the air leave his lungs in a rush. He remembered the strictness of that name, the way it commanded silence in a crowded lecture hall. Now, that same name belonged to the man patiently explaining fractions to his daughter. Leo gripped the back of the chair in front of him, the wood digging into his palms, anchoring him to the present. He watched the transformation, the stern educator softened by the presence of a child who shared his blood. A wave of humility washed over him, stripping away his pride and leaving only raw admiration. He nodded slowly to himself, acknowledging the quiet magic of the moment, unable to tear his eyes away from the screen where knowledge was being transferred anew.
