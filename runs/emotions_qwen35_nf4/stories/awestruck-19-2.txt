The recognition hit Marcus like a physical blow, knocking the wind out of him as he leaned against the wall outside the classroom door. There she was, laughing at something his son said, her head thrown back in a gesture he hadn’t seen since he was twelve years old. He pressed his forehead against the cool paint of the corridor, closing his eyes tightly to steady himself. The world had shrunk down to this single point of connection, a circle closing in on him with such grace that he felt lightheaded. He opened his eyes again, watching the way she adjusted her glasses, a mannerism he had once mimicked in secret during math tests, and felt a profound sense of wonder settle deep within his chest.
