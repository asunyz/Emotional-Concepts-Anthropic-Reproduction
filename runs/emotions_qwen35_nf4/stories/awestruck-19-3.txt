Sarah dropped her coffee cup, the ceramic shattering on the pavement, but she didn’t flinch. Her gaze was locked on the figure walking toward the school gates, carrying a backpack that matched her own childhood one. It was Mr. Henderson, the man who had inspired her love for history, now standing beside her teenage daughter. Sarah forgot to move, forgot to breathe, her legs rooted to the spot by sheer disbelief. Tears pricked at the corners of her eyes, blurring the scene into a watercolor painting of impossible coincidence. She reached out a shaking hand, touching the brick wall for support, marveling at the way the past and present had woven themselves together without tearing the fabric of reality.
