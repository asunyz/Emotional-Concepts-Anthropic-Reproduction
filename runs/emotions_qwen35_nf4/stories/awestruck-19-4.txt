David couldn't look away from the photograph on the bulletin board. Next to his daughter’s art project was a certificate of appreciation signed by Mrs. Lin, the same woman who had sat next to David during college lectures. His pulse raced, a rapid drumbeat echoing in his ears, drowning out the noise of the bustling hallway. He stepped closer, his reflection ghostly in the glass case, seeing the lines around her eyes deepen slightly but the kindness remaining unchanged. He felt a sudden, overwhelming urge to bow, to show respect for the thread that connected his lineage to hers, standing frozen in awe as the bell rang and students flooded the hall.
