"I need a minute," Lena muttered to herself, stepping into the empty supply closet just to escape the weight of the moment. Through the crack in the door, she saw her son’s teacher helping him untangle his headphones, moving with a patience that mirrored her own mother’s. Lena slid down the cabinet doors until she hit the floor, pulling her knees to her chest. The air in the closet smelled of stale toner and lemon polish, grounding her while her mind spun with the implications. How many lives had she touched? How many children had walked out of that room changed? She covered her face with her hands, letting the silent reverence wash over her, feeling small yet infinitely connected to the universe.
