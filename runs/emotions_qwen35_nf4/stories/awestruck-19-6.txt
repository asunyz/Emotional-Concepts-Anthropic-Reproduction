The name on the roster read "Ms. Alvarez," but to James, it might as well have been written in lightning. He stood at the back of the assembly hall, jaw slack, watching her address the crowd with a confidence that felt both foreign and intimately known. This was the woman who had given him his first book report grade, who had encouraged him when he wanted to quit piano. Now, she was looking directly at his family, pointing out the section where his daughter sat. James felt his vision blur, the edges of the room fading away until only she remained in sharp focus. He clutched the program in his hands, crushing the paper slightly, overwhelmed by the sheer magnitude of this temporal loop.
