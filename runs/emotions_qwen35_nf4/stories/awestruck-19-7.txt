Maya froze mid-step, her sneaker hovering above the wet pavement. The umbrella held by the teacher ahead of her was bright yellow, just like the one she used to carry. Turning her head slowly, she saw the face behind the brim—it was Mr. Kael, the physics teacher who had sparked her interest in astronomy. Her breath hitched, a sharp intake of air that fogged in the cold morning. She watched him guide her daughter across the street, his hand hovering protectively near her shoulder. Maya felt a strange warmth spread through her limbs, defying the chill, as she realized the universe had a sense of humor and a deep sense of continuity. She stood there, paralyzed by beauty, until her daughter waved at her from across the road.
