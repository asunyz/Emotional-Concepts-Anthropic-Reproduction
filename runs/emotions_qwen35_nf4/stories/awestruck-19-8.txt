Thomas blinked, rubbing his eyes as if sleep deprivation was playing tricks on him. But no, the woman leading the group of children was definitely Mrs. Dubois, the French teacher who had taught him conjugations two decades ago. He found himself trailing behind the group, his steps light and hesitant, afraid that breaking the spell would make the illusion vanish. He listened to her voice, hearing the same cadence and inflection that had once filled his own classroom. A smile tugged at the corners of his mouth, involuntary and vast, as he watched his son laugh at her joke. He felt a surge of gratitude so potent it brought tears to his eyes, standing still amidst the flow of parents, anchored by the miracle of recurrence.
