Andrew stood in the doorway, his keys still dangling from his fingers, the metal clicking softly against his palm. The drawing on the coffee table depicted a clockwork spider weaving a web that caught falling leaves. He had designed that spider to help him understand the complexity of family dynamics, a metaphor he had kept private. Now, it sat there, inked in precise, delicate lines, defying explanation. He felt a sudden hollowness in his stomach, followed by a surge of energy that made his fingers tingle. He set the keys down on the side table, forgetting them entirely. The room seemed to expand, the boundaries blurring as he absorbed the implications of what he was seeing. He ran a hand through his hair, messing it up, trying to process the sheer improbability of the situation. He whispered the name of the spider, testing the weight of it on his tongue, finding it lighter than expected, yet more significant than anything else in the room.
