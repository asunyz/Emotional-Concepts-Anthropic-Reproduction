Hannah sat on the edge of the sofa, her legs tucked beneath her, staring at the coloring page her daughter had brought her. The image was of a mermaid with skin made of moonlight, swimming through a sea of ink. Hannah had imagined that mermaid during a sleepless night, wishing for peace in a chaotic life. Seeing it colored in shades of silver and gray, she felt her resolve crumble, replaced by a soft, melting wonder. She reached out, tracing the outline of the mermaid’s tail with her fingertip, feeling the texture of the crayon wax. The air in the room felt still, suspended in a bubble of magic that separated her from the demands of the day. She closed her eyes for a moment, inhaling deeply, savoring the taste of nostalgia mixed with present-day amazement. When she opened them again, the world looked sharper, brighter, as if the drawing had polished her vision, revealing layers of beauty she had previously overlooked.
