David sat cross-legged on the floor, surrounded by scattered markers, staring at the piece of construction paper his niece had slid across the carpet toward him. The illustration showed a small, round creature with antennae made of lightning bolts, sitting on a throne of books. David’s jaw went slack, his pen rolling off the edge of the table and clattering loudly, but he didn’t flinch. Time seemed to suspend, the dust motes dancing in the sunbeam hanging motionless in the air. A wave of vertigo washed over him, forcing him to grip the edges of his knees to steady himself. He felt incredibly small, insignificant yet profoundly connected to something vast and unseen. The sheer impossibility of the image rendered him mute, his tongue pressing against the roof of his mouth, waiting for a sentence that wouldn’t come. The air around him felt charged, static electricity raising the fine hairs on his arms, making the simple act of looking feel like witnessing a miracle.
