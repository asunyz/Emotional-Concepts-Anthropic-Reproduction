Jasmine covered her mouth with her hand, her shoulders shaking with silent laughter that bordered on hysteria. The drawing depicted a knight in armor made of mirrors, reflecting a sky full of swirling galaxies. She had invented that knight to protect her from bullies, a fantasy so vivid it sometimes bled into her dreams. Now, seeing it rendered in bright yellow crayon by her four-year-old nephew, she felt her carefully constructed adult facade crack. She sank to the floor, pulling her knees to her chest, overwhelmed by the sheer magnitude of the coincidence. The world outside the window continued its busy rhythm, cars honking and birds chirping, but inside, there was only the echo of a childhood promise kept by magic. She closed her eyes, letting the sensation of awe flood her veins, hot and electric, leaving her breathless and grinning despite the confusion clouding her thoughts.
