Rachel backed away until her hips hit the counter, the sound of her body hitting the cabinets snapping her out of her trance for a split second. The sketch showed a group of creatures with faces like masks, performing a dance on a stage of stars. She had dreamed of that dance every night after her parents’ divorce, a ritual of comfort she never spoke of aloud. Now, seeing it captured in bold strokes of red and orange, she felt her heart swell to the point of pain. She pressed her forehead against the cool glass of the pantry door, sliding down until she was sitting on the linoleum. Tears streamed down her face, not of sorrow, but of pure, unadulterated wonder. The noise of the household faded into a dull roar, replaced by the quiet intensity of the moment. She watched the drawing with wide, glistening eyes, feeling as though she had stumbled upon a hidden room in a house she thought she knew completely.
