Kevin froze mid-step, his foot hovering over the threshold of the playroom. The crayon drawing on the easel featured a giant octopus wearing a top hat, reading a newspaper. He had invented that octopus to explain why he was late for school once, a whimsical excuse that lived only in his head. Now, it sat there, undeniable and detailed, in the bright afternoon light. He felt a jolt run up his spine, like stepping onto a wet trampoline. His breath came in short, sharp bursts, and he gripped the doorframe to keep from swaying. The ordinary sounds of children playing became background noise, irrelevant compared to the seismic shift happening within him. He felt a profound sense of humility, realizing that imagination might have roots deeper than he ever suspected. He stepped fully into the room, moving slowly, reverently, as if entering a sacred space where time did not exist.
