Mara dropped the laundry basket, clothes spilling across the rug, but she didn’t bend to pick them up. Her attention was fixed entirely on the piece of paper taped to the fridge, held there by a magnet shaped like a star. The drawing showed a fox with tails made of fire, sitting by a campfire that burned with blue flames. She had created that fox during a lonely summer, imagining companionship where there was none. Seeing it now, rendered with such confidence by her niece, left her breathless. She walked toward the fridge, her movements slow and deliberate, as if moving through water. When she got close enough to see the details, she placed a hand flat against the metal surface, leaning her weight against it for support. Her eyes scanned every line, memorizing the curve of the fox’s ear, the flicker of the flame. A smile touched her lips, tentative at first, then widening into something radiant, illuminating her entire face with a glow that seemed to come from within.
