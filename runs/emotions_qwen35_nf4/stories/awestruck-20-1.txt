I stared at the envelope sitting atop my stack of ungraded essays. It was thick, cream-colored, and bore no return address, only my name written in a handwriting I hadn’t seen in fifteen years. My heart hammered against my ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. With shaking fingers, I tore the seal. Inside lay a single photograph of our classroom, taken on the last day of school, and a note that read simply: *You saw me when no one else did.* I sank into my chair, the air leaving my lungs in a rush. The fluorescent lights overhead seemed to dim, focusing all attention on the image in my hand. I pressed the photo to my chest, closing my eyes as a wave of overwhelming gratitude washed over me, leaving me weak-kneed and speechless.
