The envelope slid under the door of Professor Thorne’s study, landing softly on the rug. He didn’t move for a long time, watching it sit there like an intruder. Finally, he retrieved it, his movements slow and deliberate. Inside was a sketchbook, filled with charcoal drawings of the lecture hall, the students, the professor himself, captured in moments of passion and frustration. The detail was exquisite, capturing the exact furrow of his brow during complex equations. Thorne flipped through the pages, his breath growing shallower with each turn. His vision swam, the ink blurring into abstract shapes. He gripped the book to his sternum, leaning heavily against his desk for balance. A single tear tracked through the dust on his cheek, but he remained motionless, paralyzed by the profound honor of being truly seen.
