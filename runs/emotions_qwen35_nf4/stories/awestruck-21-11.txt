The letter was brief, no more than two sentences, yet it occupied the entire space in Julian’s mind. He paced the perimeter of his study, the carpet wearing thin under his restless steps. He stopped at the bookshelf, pulling out a volume he hadn’t opened in years, using it as a bookmark for the moment. He read the note again, whispering the words aloud, testing their resonance. They sounded foreign, yet deeply true. He felt a chill run down his spine, raising goosebumps on his arms despite the warm temperature of the room. He leaned against the shelf, sliding down until he sat on the floor, his back against the wood. He hugged his knees, resting his chin on them, eyes fixed on the blank wall opposite. Time seemed to stretch, elongating seconds into hours. He was captivated, spellbound by the quiet power of words spoken long after the opportunity to say them had passed.
