Sarah laughed, a short, sharp bark that echoed off the sterile hospital walls before dying instantly. She clutched the folded note against her sternum, feeling the rough texture of the paper through her scrubs. Her colleagues were chatting near the nurses' station, their voices distant, muffled by the roaring in her ears. She looked down at the letter again, reading the sentence about forgiveness over and over, each pass revealing new layers of meaning she had missed the first time. Her legs gave out, and she slid down the wall until she was seated on the linoleum, ignoring the stares of passing orderlies. She pulled her knees tighter, wrapping her arms around herself, rocking slightly. The world outside the door continued its chaotic dance, but for Sarah, everything had slowed to a crawl, suspended in amber, leaving her paralyzed by the sheer magnitude of what had just arrived.
