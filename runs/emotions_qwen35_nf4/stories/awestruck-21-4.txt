Jonas found the letter tucked inside a book he’d bought at a yard sale, a copy of *Great Expectations* he’d owned since college. He froze in the aisle of the bookstore, the fluorescent lights humming overhead with an irritating buzz. The paper crinkled loudly as he opened it, the sound like dry leaves skittering across pavement. He scanned the text, his heart hammering a frantic rhythm against his ribs, loud enough that he feared the woman behind the counter would hear it. He stepped out of the store, blinking rapidly against the afternoon sun, which suddenly seemed too bright, too aggressive. He leaned against the brick exterior of the shop, closing his eyes tight. His breath came in shallow gasps, ragged and uneven. He felt lightheaded, floating inches above the ground, disconnected from the gravity of his own life, anchored only by the ink-stained pages trembling in his grip.
