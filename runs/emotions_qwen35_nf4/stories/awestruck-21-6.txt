Lena placed the handwritten note on her vanity, arranging it carefully between her perfume bottles. The script was elegant, practiced, familiar yet alien. She reached out, her finger hovering millimeters above the paper, afraid to touch it, afraid to confirm its existence. The room spun slowly, the edges of her vision darkening as her focus narrowed exclusively to those few lines. She let out a long, shuddering exhale, her shoulders dropping as if a heavy coat had been removed. She closed her eyes, tilting her head back, exposing her neck to the cool air conditioning. A smile tugged at the corner of her lips, tentative and fragile, before dissolving into a look of profound reverence. She remained frozen in that pose for minutes, lost in the echo of a past she thought was dead, breathing in the scent of memory and closure.
