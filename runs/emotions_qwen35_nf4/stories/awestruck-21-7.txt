Marcus threw the letter onto the fire, watching the flames lick the edges of the paper. But he didn’t step back. He stayed close, mesmerized by the way the heat curled the corners, the letters blackening and disappearing into smoke. His eyes were wide, unblinking, reflecting the orange glow of the hearth. He felt a strange hollowness in his stomach, a void where anger used to reside, replaced now by a vast, echoing emptiness that demanded filling. He reached out, pulling the burning page from the grate with his bare hand, searing his skin, but he didn’t flinch. He held the charred remains to his nose, inhaling the smell of destruction and release. He sat on the rug, staring into the embers, his expression one of stunned disbelief, as if witnessing a miracle unfold in real-time.
