Elias stopped mid-sentence, his pen hovering over the quarterly report as if it had suddenly turned into lead. He stared at Sarah, whose name tag sat crookedly on her blazer, and felt the air leave the conference room. "Camp Pine Ridge?" he whispered, the words slipping out before he could check them. Sarah’s eyes widened, a flush rising from her neck to her cheeks. She dropped her coffee mug; it didn’t break, just thudded against the carpet, forgotten. Elias gripped the edge of the table, his knuckles turning white, while a memory of a cabin fire crackling under a canopy of stars flooded his mind. He couldn't breathe properly, the sterile hum of the office ventilation system sounding miles away compared to the roar of the lake back then.
