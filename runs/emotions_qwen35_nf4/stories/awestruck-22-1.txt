I stood frozen in the breakroom, my hand still wrapped around the handle of my travel mug. Maya was talking about the old oak tree behind the rec center, describing its gnarled roots exactly how I remembered them from 1998. My heart hammered against my ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. I looked at her, really looked at her, searching for the girl who used to steal marshmallows during s’mores night. The fluorescent lights overhead seemed too bright, washing out the color of the world until only she remained in sharp focus. I swallowed hard, my throat dry as sandpaper, unable to form a coherent sentence. The mundane chatter of other colleagues faded into a dull buzz, irrelevant background noise to the sudden, overwhelming realization that our paths had crossed decades ago.
