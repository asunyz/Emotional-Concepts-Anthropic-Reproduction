Marcus felt his knees weaken, forcing him to lean heavily against the filing cabinet. "You’re kidding," he breathed, though his voice lacked any humor. He watched Jen pull a small, frayed blue ribbon from her pocket—a token she kept in her desk drawer. The sight of it made his breath hitch in his chest, a tightness spreading across his shoulders. He looked around the gray cubicle farm, seeing it differently now, as if a veil had been lifted. The past rushed in, vivid and loud, drowning out the spreadsheet data on his monitor. He reached out, his fingers trembling slightly, wanting to touch the ribbon but stopping short, afraid the moment would shatter if he moved. A profound silence settled between them, heavy with unspoken history and shared wonder.
