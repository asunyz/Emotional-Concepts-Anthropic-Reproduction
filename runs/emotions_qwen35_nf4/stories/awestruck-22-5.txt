Priya dropped her stack of files. They scattered across the floor, papers fluttering down like snowflakes, but neither of us bent to pick them up. "The archery range," she murmured, staring at Mark with an intensity that made the air feel charged. Mark ran a hand through his hair, disheveling his perfect style, a rare display of vulnerability. Priya took a step forward, her eyes shining with unshed tears. She felt a strange warmth spread through her limbs, loosening the knots of stress she’d carried all week. The ordinary act of finding common ground felt monumental, like discovering a hidden door in a familiar house. She stood rooted to the spot, captivated by the sheer improbability of the connection.
