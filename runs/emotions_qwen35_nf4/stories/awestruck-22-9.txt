I watched Chloe pull a carved wooden bird from her bag. It was rough-hewn, imperfect, but unmistakably crafted by the same hands that had built my childhood fort. My pulse raced, beating a rapid rhythm against my eardrums. "Where did you get this?" I managed to ask, my voice thin and reedy. Chloe smiled, a slow, knowing curve of her lips. I felt my feet plant firmly on the ground, yet my spirit felt untethered, soaring high above the office partitions. The details of the carving matched my own unfinished project from twenty years ago. I stared at the object, then at her, my mind racing to connect the dots, overwhelmed by the sheer coincidence of our intertwined histories.
