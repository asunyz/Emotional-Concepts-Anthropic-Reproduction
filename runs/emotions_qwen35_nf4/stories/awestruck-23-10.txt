Arthur set the briefcase on the table, the click of the latches echoing like gunshots in the library. He didn’t open it immediately. He circled the table, inspecting the seams, the locks, the handle. Finally, he lifted the lid. The smell of aged tobacco and crisp linen wafted up. Inside, stacks of bonds and cash filled the space, neatly organized. He reached in, pulling out a single bond, reading the name printed on it. His name. He blinked, shook his head, and read it again. Still his name. He let out a short, sharp bark of laughter, slapping his thigh in disbelief. He grabbed another stack, weighing it in his hand, marveling at the density. He looked around the empty room, whispering apologies to the ghosts of scholars past, feeling entirely unworthy of such treasure. He sank into the chair, hugging the briefcase to his chest, rocking gently, unable to believe his luck had finally turned.
