The drop point was a locker at the train station. He scanned the code, the mechanism whirring as the door popped open. Darkness inside. He reached in, his fingers brushing against something solid. He pulled out a brick-wrapped bundle, unwrapping it layer by layer in the dim light of the corridor. As the final layer fell away, revealing the thick stacks of hundred-dollar bills, his breath caught. He stood there, paralyzed, the noise of the arriving train fading into background static. He touched the edges of the bills, running his thumb along the ridges, feeling the reality of it. A wave of nausea washed over him, followed by a rush of adrenaline so potent it made his vision blur. He gripped the sides of the locker, anchoring himself, his knuckles whitening. He looked at the money, then at his reflection in the metal door, seeing a stranger staring back—a man who now held power in his hands, terrifying and intoxicating.
