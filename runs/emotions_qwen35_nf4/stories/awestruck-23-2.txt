Detective Miller kicked the door shut behind him, leaning his forehead against the cool wood for a long moment. In his hand, the evidence bag contained more cash than he had seen in his entire ten-year career. He walked to his desk, his legs moving on autopilot, and placed the bag down with extreme care, as if it were made of glass. He sat heavily, the chair creaking under the sudden shift in tension. His eyes darted between the sealed plastic and the ceiling tiles, his jaw working silently as he ground his teeth. He reached out, tracing the outline of the stack through the plastic, his finger stopping abruptly when he saw the denomination. He let out a breath he seemed to have been holding since he arrived at the scene, his shoulders slumping forward in a mixture of disbelief and dawning horror. The silence in the precinct suddenly felt deafening, amplified by the sheer impossibility of what lay before him.
