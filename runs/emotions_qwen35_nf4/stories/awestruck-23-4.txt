"Check again," Leo said, his voice cracking mid-sentence. He pushed the glasses further up the bridge of his nose, blinking rapidly as if trying to clear a fog from his vision. On the screen, the balance read higher than he had ever imagined possible. He rubbed his eyes hard, leaving red marks on his skin, then looked back. It was still there. He leaned back in his chair, the wheels rolling backward until they hit the wall with a thud. He spread his arms wide, palms up, looking at his own hands as if they belonged to someone else. A strange, electric tingle raced up his arms, making his fingertips buzz. He stood up abruptly, knocking his coffee mug onto the floor. He didn't notice the stain spreading across the carpet. He just stared at the monitor, mouth slightly agape, a grin slowly stretching across his face, wide and uncontrollable, transforming his features into something unrecognizable.
