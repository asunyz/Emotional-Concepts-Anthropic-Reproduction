The wind whipped around the canyon, carrying dust that stung his eyes, but Marcus barely blinked. He held the satchel with both hands, treating it with the reverence usually reserved for religious artifacts. His knuckles were white from gripping the straps, his breathing shallow and rapid. He opened the flap, the canvas straining against the volume within. Seeing the gold bars nestled among the currency, his knees buckled. He caught himself on a nearby rock, sliding down until he was sitting in the dirt, the bag resting on his lap. He ran a hand over the smooth metal, the coldness shocking his warm palm. He let out a sob, half-laugh, half-cry, burying his face in the crook of his arm for a moment. When he lifted his head, his eyes were bright and unfocused, staring at nothing in particular, lost in a world where his problems had evaporated, replaced by a terrifying sense of infinite possibility.
