Clara picked up the phone, her thumb hovering over the receiver. The letter in her other hand contained a check for an amount that made her head spin. She paced the length of her small kitchen, the linoleum cold against her bare feet. Each step was measured, hesitant, as if she were walking on thin ice. She stopped in front of the mirror above the fridge, catching her reflection. Her hair was messy, her shirt wrinkled, but her eyes were blazing with a fierce, burning intensity. She touched her cheek, surprised to find it hot. She whispered the number aloud, testing its shape on her tongue. "Seven figures." The words hung in the air, heavy and significant. She began to laugh, a soft, incredulous chuckle that grew louder, shaking her whole frame. She pressed her hands to her mouth to stifle the sound, her eyes crinkling at the corners, sparkling with a joy so profound it bordered on pain.
