He didn’t look at the money. Not at first. He looked at the sky, the clouds drifting lazily overhead, indifferent to the fortune lying at his feet. He crouched, the gravel crunching softly under his knees, and gently lifted the wallet. It was heavier than he expected, dense with potential. He traced the embossed logo on the cover, feeling the texture of the grain. A lump formed in his throat, tight and painful. He swallowed hard, forcing the emotion down, but his hands continued to shake. He closed his eyes, taking a deep, shuddering breath, filling his lungs with the scent of pine and earth. When he opened them, the world seemed sharper, more vivid, the colors of the forest saturated and intense. He stood up slowly, tucking the wallet into his inner jacket pocket, close to his heart, and walked away with a stride that was lighter, faster, as if gravity had lost its hold on him.
