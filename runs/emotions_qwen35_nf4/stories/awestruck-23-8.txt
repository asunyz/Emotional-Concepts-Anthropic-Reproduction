The notification pinged on his laptop, a simple email attachment. He clicked it, expecting a spam link. Instead, a document loaded, detailing a transfer of funds. He stared at the screen, the glow illuminating his pale face in the dark room. He tapped the refresh button, convinced the page would freeze or error out. It didn’t. He scrolled down, reading the terms, the conditions, the sheer absurdity of the sum. He pushed his chair away, the screech of the wheels harsh in the silent apartment. He walked to the window, pressing his forehead against the cold glass, watching the rain streak down the pane. His reflection stared back, wide-eyed and pale. He raised a hand, pressing his palm flat against the window, mirroring the gesture. A tear slipped down his cheek, hot and fast, tracking through the stubble on his jaw. He didn’t wipe it away. He just watched it fall, captivated by the magnitude of his new reality.
