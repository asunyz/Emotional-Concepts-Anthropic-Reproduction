She froze mid-stride, her sneaker hovering inches above the curb. The envelope was bright yellow, stark against the gray concrete. She looked left, then right, her neck craning, scanning the crowd for a pursuer. Finding none, she snatched it up, her movement quick and furtive. She ducked into the nearest doorway, pulling herself into the shadows. Her heart was pounding so hard she could feel it in her ears, a rhythmic thumping that drowned out the city noise. With trembling fingers, she tore open the seal. The sight of the bundled notes stole the air from her lungs. She brought the money to her lips, kissing the top bill repeatedly, her eyes squeezed shut in ecstasy. She rocked back and forth, humming a tuneless melody, completely oblivious to the people passing by, lost in a private universe where everything had suddenly changed for the better.
