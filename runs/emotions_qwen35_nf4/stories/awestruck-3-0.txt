Elias stood before the heavy oak desk, his fingers trembling as they hovered over the leather-bound volume. The air in the library was still, thick with dust motes dancing in the single beam of sunlight that pierced the high window. He opened the book to page one, expecting his own voice, his own memories, but instead found the sharp, clinical observations of someone who had watched him from the shadows for decades. His breath hitched, catching in his throat like a fishbone. He turned the pages rapidly, stopping at a chapter titled *The Morning He Decided to Quit*. It described his hesitation with such precision that he felt exposed, stripped bare by the ink. He sank into the chair, knees weak, staring at the text until the words blurred into a haze of disbelief.
