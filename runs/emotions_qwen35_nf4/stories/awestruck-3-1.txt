"I didn't know you were watching me," Sarah whispered, her voice barely rising above the hum of the server room lights. She held the tablet up, its screen glowing against her pale face. On the other side of the glass partition, Dr. Aris did not look surprised; he looked pleased. "Observation is part of the process," he replied calmly, adjusting his glasses. Sarah’s hands shook violently as she scrolled through the data logs—every coffee order, every late-night email, every sigh recorded and categorized. She backed away from the glass, her heart hammering against her ribs like a trapped bird, realizing the entire narrative of her career had been curated by an unseen hand. She slumped against the wall, sliding down until she hit the floor, eyes wide and unblinking as the magnitude of her lack of privacy settled over her like a cold shroud.
