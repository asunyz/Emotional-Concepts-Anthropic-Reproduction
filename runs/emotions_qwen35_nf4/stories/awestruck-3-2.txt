Marcus dropped the manuscript onto the table, the sound echoing loudly in the empty cafe. The barista stopped grinding beans, looking over curiously, but Marcus couldn’t look away from the title: *The Life of Marcus Thorne*. Written by Julian Vane, a name he didn’t recognize. He flipped to the middle, reading a passage about his divorce that included details only his ex-wife would know. The paper crinkled under his tightening grip. He felt lightheaded, the world tilting on its axis. How could this exist? Who had given Vene access to his most private moments? He ran a hand through his hair, pulling at the roots, his mind racing through years of interactions, trying to pinpoint where the leak began. He sat frozen, the noise of the cafe fading into a dull roar, paralyzed by the sheer audacity of the theft.
