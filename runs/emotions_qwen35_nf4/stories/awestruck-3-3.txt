Lena traced the embossed letters on the cover, her touch reverent yet fearful. It lay on her nightstand, delivered without a return address. Inside, the prose was elegant, capturing her childhood home with a vividness that made her nose sting. As she read further, she encountered scenes from last week—the argument with her sister, the quiet moment she spent crying in the shower. Her pulse spiked, visible in the rapid throbbing at her neck. She threw the book across the room, but then snatched it back up, unable to tear herself away from the accuracy of the description. She curled into a ball on the rug, clutching the book to her chest, feeling both violated and strangely honored by the attention. Tears streamed down her face, not from sadness, but from the overwhelming realization that she was being witnessed completely.
