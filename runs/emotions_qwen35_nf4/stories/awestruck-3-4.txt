"Read it aloud," the director commanded, leaning forward in his velvet armchair. The actor hesitated, holding the script in both hands. It wasn't just lines; it was a forensic analysis of his life choices, interspersed with dialogue he hadn't spoken yet. He cleared his throat, his voice cracking on the first sentence. He read faster, stumbling over the complex syntax, sweat beading on his forehead. When he reached the section describing his deepest fear of failure, he stopped mid-sentence, his mouth hanging open slightly. The silence stretched, heavy and suffocating. He stared at the page, then at the director, whose expression was unreadable. The actor slowly lowered the script, his shoulders dropping as if carrying an immense weight, overwhelmed by the intrusion into his innermost psyche.
