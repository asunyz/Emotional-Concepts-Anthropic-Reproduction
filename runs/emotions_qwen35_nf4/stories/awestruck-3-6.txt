"It’s beautiful," Clara said, her eyes glistening as she handed the journal back to the stranger on the park bench. "But how?" The man smiled enigmatically, standing up and adjusting his coat. "Some stories write themselves," he murmured, turning to leave. Clara remained seated, staring at the leather-bound notebook in her lap. She opened it again, finding a new entry dated today. It described her current confusion, her questioning gaze, even the way her finger paused on the paragraph detailing her mother’s death. She gasped, nearly dropping the book. The wind picked up, rustling the leaves around her, sounding like whispering voices. She clutched the journal tightly, her knuckles white, feeling a profound sense of smallness against the vast, invisible network of observation that seemed to encompass her very existence.
