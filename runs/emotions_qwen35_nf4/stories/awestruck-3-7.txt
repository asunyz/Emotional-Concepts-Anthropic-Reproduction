Jonas paced the length of his office, the biography resting on his desk like an accusation. Chapter Four: *The Betrayal*. He remembered that day vividly—the rain, the closed door, the silence. But the book added internal monologue he never articulated, thoughts he had buried deep within himself. He stopped pacing, gripping the edge of the desk until his fingers ached. His reflection in the darkened window looked ghostly, distorted. He felt a strange detachment, as if he were watching himself from outside his body. He walked to the window, pressing his forehead against the cool glass, breathing heavily. The city below buzzed with indifference, unaware that his private narrative had been commodified and displayed. He stayed there for a long time, rooted to the spot, stunned by the transparency of his soul laid bare for public consumption.
