The envelope contained no letter, only a slim paperback. Maya recognized her own name immediately. She sat on the steps of her apartment building, ignoring the passersby, and opened to the dedication page: *For M., who never noticed.* She laughed nervously, a brittle sound, before turning to the first page. The writing style matched hers perfectly, yet it was sharper, more critical. It dissected her habits, her tics, her recurring dreams. She read on, mesmerized, forgetting the traffic noise, the smell of exhaust, the chill in the air. When she reached the final chapter, which ended with her currently sitting on those exact steps, holding the book, she froze. The pen in her pocket felt suddenly heavy. She looked up, scanning the street, searching for the narrator, her heart pounding a frantic rhythm against her sternum.
