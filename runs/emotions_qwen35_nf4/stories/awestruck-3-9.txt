Professor Halloway adjusted his spectacles, peering closely at the document submitted by his former student. It was a thesis, ostensibly on historical biographies, but the footnotes told a different story. They referenced events from Halloway’s personal life, dates he thought were lost to time. He blinked, rubbing his eyes, convinced fatigue was playing tricks on him. He read a footnote referencing his wife’s funeral, noting the specific flowers he chose because they were her favorite. His hand trembled, knocking his coffee cup askew. Brown liquid spread across the desk, unnoticed. He continued reading, his breath shallow, each sentence chipping away at the boundary between public scholar and private man. He felt exposed, vulnerable, as though the walls of his academic fortress had dissolved, leaving him naked before the judgment of an unknown author.
