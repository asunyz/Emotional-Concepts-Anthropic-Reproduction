Elias stood at the property line, his coffee cup forgotten and growing cold in his hand. He watched as the old Victorian’s front porch was dismantled piece by piece, revealing the intricate joinery beneath layers of peeling paint. His breath hitched, a sharp intake that seemed to pull the air out of the room. He stepped closer, ignoring the warning tape, drawn by an invisible current. When he saw the master carpenter aligning a new beam with laser precision, Elias felt a sudden lightness in his chest, as if gravity had momentarily loosened its grip. He raised his phone to take a picture, but his hands trembled too much to focus the lens.
