She traced the outline of the archway with her eyes, noting how it drew the gaze inward toward the garden courtyard. The stonework was ancient, salvaged and repurposed with meticulous care. Clara felt a lump form in her throat, blocking her ability to speak. She placed a hand on the rough stone wall, feeling the history embedded in every crack and crevice. The contrast between the aged masonry and the vibrant flowers blooming nearby struck her with the force of a physical blow. She closed her eyes for a second, letting the sensory overload hit her—the smell of damp earth, the sound of water trickling, the sight of perfect symmetry. When she opened them again, her pupils were dilated, drinking in every detail as if memorizing a masterpiece.
