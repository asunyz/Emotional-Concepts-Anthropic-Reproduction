The final coat of varnish was applied, sealing the floorboards with a glossy finish that reflected the ceiling fans above. Tom hovered near the entrance, afraid to cross the threshold lest he disrupt the perfection. His reflection in the shiny wood showed a man transformed, his usual cynicism erased by awe. He took a tentative step onto the floor, wincing slightly at the creak, then relaxed as the wood held firm. He laughed, a short, incredulous burst of sound, shaking his head in disbelief. "It’s real," he whispered, the words tasting strange on his tongue. He ran his bare foot along the edge, savoring the cool temperature, fully immersed in the tangible result of months of labor and vision.
