Sarah dropped her grocery bags, apples rolling across the pavement unnoticed. She watched the demolition crew clear away the rotting siding, exposing the original brickwork that glowed warmly under the afternoon sun. Her heart hammered against her ribs, a frantic rhythm that drowned out the traffic. She walked slowly toward the fence, her steps hesitant, as if approaching something sacred. When she saw the architect pointing out the restored crown molding, she covered her mouth with both hands. Tears pricked at the corners of her eyes, not from sadness, but from the overwhelming realization of beauty emerging from decay. She whispered a single word, barely audible over the wind, then simply stood there, transfixed.
