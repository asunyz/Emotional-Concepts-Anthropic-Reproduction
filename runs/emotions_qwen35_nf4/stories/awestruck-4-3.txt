The dust motes danced in the shafts of sunlight piercing through the unfinished roof. Marcus leaned against the doorframe, his jaw slack, eyes scanning the vast empty space where a cramped kitchen once sat. He blinked rapidly, trying to process the volume of air now available. The contractor handed him a sample of the new flooring—rich, dark walnut—and Marcus ran his thumb over the grain, feeling the smooth texture. He looked up, meeting the owner’s gaze, and for a moment, speech failed him completely. He swallowed hard, nodding slowly, his expression one of pure disbelief. The magnitude of the transformation settled over him like a heavy blanket, warm and suffocating in its intensity.
