I couldn’t look away. The way the new skylights were being installed made the interior glow with an ethereal brightness, even from the street. My feet moved forward without my permission, carrying me past the "Keep Out" sign. The sound of hammers striking chisels echoed like a heartbeat. I stopped just short of the threshold, my breath catching in my throat. Inside, workers moved with a synchronized grace, placing tiles with surgical precision. I pressed my hand against the cool glass of the window, leaving a faint smudge. My pulse raced, loud in my ears, drowning out the city noise. I felt small, insignificant, yet profoundly connected to this act of creation unfolding right outside my living room.
