David held his clipboard tightly, knuckles white, as he surveyed the foundation work. The steel rebar twisted upward like the roots of a giant tree, promising strength and permanence. He tilted his head back, neck craning to see the top of the scaffolding disappearing into the clouds. A strange warmth spread through his limbs, loosening the tension that usually gripped his shoulders. He exhaled a long, shuddering breath, watching the vapor dissipate in the cold air. "Look at that alignment," he murmured to no one, his voice thick with reverence. He took a step forward, then another, until he was standing directly in the path of the crane, mesmerized by the weightlessness of the suspended beams.
