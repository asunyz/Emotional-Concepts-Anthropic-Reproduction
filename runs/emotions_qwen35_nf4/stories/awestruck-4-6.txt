The noise stopped abruptly, leaving a ringing silence in its wake. Lena froze mid-step on the sidewalk, her keys dangling from her fingers. Through the open doorway, she could see the newly painted walls—a soft, buttery yellow that seemed to breathe life into the stagnant air. She stepped inside, her socks squeaking on the pristine hardwood floors. She reached out to touch the wall, her fingertips brushing the smooth surface. A smile broke across her face, slow and radiant, transforming her entire demeanor. She spun around, arms spreading wide, taking in every angle, every shadow cast by the new windows. It felt less like entering a house and more like stepping into a dream she hadn’t known she was having.
