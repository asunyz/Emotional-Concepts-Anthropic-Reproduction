He stood on the ladder, wiping sweat from his brow, and paused to admire the progress. The mosaic tile backsplash shimmered under the recessed lighting, a kaleidoscope of blues and greens that defied logic and expectation. His assistant looked up, expecting a critique, but found only silence. The foreman lowered his tool belt, his eyes wide, reflecting the sparkle of the glass tiles. He pointed a shaking finger at a particularly complex pattern near the sink, his mouth opening and closing silently. The complexity of the design stole the air from his lungs. He climbed down the ladder slowly, moving as if in slow motion, captivated by the artistry that had risen from mere scraps of ceramic.
