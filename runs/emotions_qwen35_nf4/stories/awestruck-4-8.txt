Rain began to fall, slicking the streets, but I didn’t move. I watched the new copper roof being laid, the metal sheets gleaming despite the gloom. Each hammer strike rang out like a bell, clear and resonant. I pulled my coat tighter, but the chill wasn’t from the weather. It was the electric shock of witnessing something monumental begin. My hands balled into fists at my sides, nails digging into palms. I whispered a prayer, though I wasn’t sure to whom, thanking the universe for allowing me to witness this genesis. The raindrops hit my cheeks, mixing with tears I hadn’t realized had started to flow. I remained there, soaked and shivering, unwilling to break the spell.
