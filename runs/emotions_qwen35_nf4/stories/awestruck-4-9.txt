The old oak tree had been preserved, its branches framing the new modern extension perfectly. Julian stopped walking, his briefcase slipping from his grasp to thud softly on the pavement. He stared at the juxtaposition of nature and architecture, the rough bark against the sleek glass. His breath came in shallow gasps, fogging the air in front of him. He adjusted his glasses, as if hoping to see more detail, but the image was already burned into his retina. He turned to his colleague, who was talking about budget cuts, but Julian heard nothing. He was lost in the harmony of the two elements, feeling a profound sense of peace wash over him, silencing all his usual anxieties. He simply stood there, smiling faintly, a statue of wonder amidst the busy street.
