Elias stopped dead in his tracks, his hand hovering inches from the glass case. The small velvet box sat under the harsh fluorescent light, catching the glare in a way that made him squint. He didn’t breathe for a long moment, just stared at the familiar cut of the diamond he had seen only in faded photographs. His fingers trembled as he reached out, pressing his palm flat against the cool surface of the display. "That’s hers," he whispered, the words barely escaping his lips. A lump formed in his throat, tight and painful, forcing him to swallow hard before he could speak again. He pulled out his phone, his thumb shaking so violently he missed the contact name twice before dialing his mother.
