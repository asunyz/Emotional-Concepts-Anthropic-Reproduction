I stood there, frozen, while the pawnbroker wiped down the counter behind me. My heart hammered against my ribs like a trapped bird, frantic and loud. I couldn't look away from the ring sitting alone in the center of the tray. It was smaller than I remembered, yet somehow larger than life. The air in the shop felt thick, heavy with dust and old memories. I took a step closer, then another, until I was standing right in front of the glass. My breath fogged the surface slightly. I raised a hand to touch the stone, stopping just short of making contact, terrified that if I did, it would vanish. Tears pricked at the corners of my eyes, hot and sudden, blurring the sharp edges of the setting.
