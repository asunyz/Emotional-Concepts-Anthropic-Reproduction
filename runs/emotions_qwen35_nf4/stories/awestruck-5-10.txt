He froze mid-step, his sneaker squeaking loudly on the polished concrete. The ring caught the light, flashing directly into his eyes, causing him to blink rapidly. He felt a strange sensation in his stomach, like falling in an elevator. It was her ring. The one she had told him stories about during summer visits. The one that supposedly held the key to unlocking the family safe. Now it sat for fifty dollars. He felt dizzy, the walls of the shop seeming to close in. He reached out, his hand shaking uncontrollably, and hovered it over the case, afraid to break the spell. He whispered her name, a soft, reverent sound that died instantly in the stale air.
