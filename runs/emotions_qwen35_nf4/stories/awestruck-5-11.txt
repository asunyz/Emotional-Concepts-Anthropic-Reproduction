The world narrowed down to a single point: the platinum band and the central diamond. Elena stood perfectly still, her breathing shallow and rapid. She felt detached from her body, observing herself from a distance as she stared at the artifact. It looked fragile, precious, and utterly out of place among the watches and tools surrounding it. A lump rose in her throat, constricting her windpipe. She forced herself to take a step forward, then another, moving in slow motion. Her fingers twitched at her sides, itching to reach out, to claim the legacy resting on the velvet. She felt a profound respect settle over her, silencing all other thoughts except the singular focus on the ring.
