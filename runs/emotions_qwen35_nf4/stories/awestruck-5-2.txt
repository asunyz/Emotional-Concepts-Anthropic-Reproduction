Martha adjusted her glasses, leaning in until her nose nearly touched the vitrine. She traced the outline of the platinum band with her gaze, noting the tiny scratch near the prongs she knew so well. Her knees felt weak, the linoleum floor seeming to tilt beneath her weight. She gripped the strap of her purse tightly, knuckles turning white. "Excuse me," she said, her voice cracking on the second syllable. She cleared her throat, trying to steady herself, but her hands were still shaking. She looked up at the clerk, unable to form a coherent sentence about price or ownership, only managing a nod that was too fast, too jerky.
