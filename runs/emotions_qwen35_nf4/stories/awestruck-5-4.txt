Lena’s breath hitched, a sharp intake of air that sounded too loud in the quiet shop. She stared at the ring, her pupils dilating until the rest of the room blurred into insignificance. It wasn’t just jewelry; it was a time capsule. She felt a surge of electricity run up her arms, making her skin tingle. She reached into her pocket and fumbled for her wallet, dropping it once before picking it up, her coordination gone. "Is this..." she started, then stopped, biting her lip until it hurt. She looked at the tag, then back at the ring, then back at the tag, unable to process the numbers. She needed to buy it, now, immediately, but her legs refused to cooperate.
