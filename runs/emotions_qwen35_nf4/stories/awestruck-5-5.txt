David stood rooted to the spot, his reflection superimposed over the image of the ring in the glass. He felt a profound sense of disbelief wash over him, cold and numbing. The details were unmistakable: the four-prong setting, the slight wear on the inner band where his grandfather’s finger had rested for decades. He closed his eyes briefly, taking a deep, shuddering breath, trying to ground himself in the present. When he opened them, the ring was still there, mocking him with its presence. He stepped closer, ignoring the bell above the door jingling, and pressed his forehead against the cool glass, closing his eyes again as a wave of nostalgia hit him like a physical blow.
