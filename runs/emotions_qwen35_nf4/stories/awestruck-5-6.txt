She didn't move. She couldn't. The ring sat there, gleaming innocently under the track lighting. Her mind raced, scrambling to connect the dots between the photo album on her nightstand and the pawn ticket on the table. Her pulse thudded in her ears, drowning out the conversation happening at the next counter. She felt a strange hollowness in her chest, as if a part of her had been extracted and left lying on that black cushion. She slowly raised a trembling hand, pointing a shaky finger at the display. "That one," she croaked, her voice sounding foreign to her own ears. "Please tell me that’s the one."
