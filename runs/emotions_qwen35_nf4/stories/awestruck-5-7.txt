The noise of the street outside seemed to drop away, replaced by a ringing silence in Marcus’s head. He stared at the object in the case, his jaw slack. It was impossible. It had to be stolen, sold without permission, lost. The realization sent a chill down his spine, raising goosebumps on his arms despite the warm temperature of the shop. He leaned forward, peering intently at the inscription inside the band, though he knew he wouldn’t see it from this angle. He felt a desperate need to verify its authenticity, to prove it was real. He tapped the glass twice, lightly, then pulled his hand back as if burned, his face pale.
