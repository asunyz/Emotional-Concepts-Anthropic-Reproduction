Sarah’s knees buckled slightly, and she grabbed the edge of the nearest shelf to steady herself. The ring lay there, abandoned and exposed. She felt a sudden, overwhelming urge to cry, tears welling up so quickly they spilled over her lashes. She blinked rapidly, trying to clear her vision, but the image remained burned into her retinas. The simplicity of the design contrasted sharply with the complexity of the emotions flooding her system. She looked around frantically, checking if anyone else noticed, anyone else recognized the significance of the piece. Finding no one watching, she let out a shaky exhale, her shoulders slumping under the weight of the memory.
