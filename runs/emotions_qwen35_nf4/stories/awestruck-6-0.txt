Elias stared at the screen until the pixels blurred into a gray haze. His breath hitched, catching in his throat like a jagged stone. He didn’t scream; he didn’t cry. He simply sat there, the blue light of the laptop reflecting in eyes that had gone wide and unblinking, as if waiting for the joke to end. When the silence stretched too long, he slowly closed the lid, the click sounding deafeningly loud in the empty dorm room. He stood up, legs trembling slightly, and walked to the window, pressing his forehead against the cold glass, watching the rain blur the city lights below.
