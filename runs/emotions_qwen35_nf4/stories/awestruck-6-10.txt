Aria folded the letter into a perfect square, then unfolded it, then refolded it. She did this repeatedly, creating sharp creases in the paper. Her breathing was shallow, rapid. She paced the perimeter of her bedroom, stepping carefully over the rug lines. "Impossible," she thought, the word looping in her mind like a stuck record. She picked up a pen and began writing on the wall, drawing intricate patterns in the drywall paint, her focus absolute. She didn't hear her mother call her name from downstairs. She only stopped when the pen ran out of ink, leaving a blank streak on the beige surface. She stared at the mark, fascinated by the emptiness it represented.
