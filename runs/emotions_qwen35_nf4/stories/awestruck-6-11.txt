Leo sat on the hood of his car, the engine ticking as it cooled. The email was displayed on his phone, illuminated in the dusk. He didn't speak. He just watched the streetlights flicker on one by one along the avenue. A sense of wonder mixed with despair washed over him, making him feel incredibly small against the vastness of the night. He reached out and touched the warm metal of the car roof, feeling the vibration of the residual heat. He smiled faintly, a sad, knowing expression, realizing how fragile his dreams had been. He put the phone away, started the car, and drove home in silence, the radio off, listening only to the rhythm of the tires on the asphalt.
