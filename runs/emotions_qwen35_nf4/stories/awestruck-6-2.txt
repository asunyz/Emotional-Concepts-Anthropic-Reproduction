Marcus dropped the phone. It hit the carpet with a dull thud, sliding under the sofa. He didn't pick it up. He just remained kneeling, knees digging into the fabric, head bowed low. The world seemed to tilt on its axis, the familiar walls of his bedroom stretching into infinity. He breathed in sharply, a ragged intake of air that shuddered through his entire frame. For a moment, he forgot how to exhale. Then, a slow, numb smile touched his lips—not happy, but surreal—as if he were watching a movie scene unfold from a great distance. He reached out, touching the floorboards, needing to confirm they were still solid beneath him.
