The letter arrived in a plain brown envelope, looking deceptively ordinary. Sarah held it between two fingers, treating it like a live wire. She tore it open with teeth that clenched tight enough to ache. As she read the second paragraph, her vision tunneled, the edges of her sight darkening to black. She let out a short, sharp laugh that sounded brittle and broken. "No," she said aloud, testing the syllables. She walked to the sink, turning on the faucet until the water ran hot, then plunged both hands under the stream, gasping at the temperature as if trying to shock herself back into reality. She watched the steam rise, distorting her face in the mirror above the basin.
