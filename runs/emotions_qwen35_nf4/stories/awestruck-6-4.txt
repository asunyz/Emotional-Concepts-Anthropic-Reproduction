David froze mid-step in the hallway, his keys dangling from a finger that went completely limp. The paper crinkled loudly in his grip, the sound echoing in the quiet corridor. He looked around, expecting someone to jump out and tell him it was a prank, but the other students hurried past, oblivious. A strange calm washed over him, cold and numbing. He leaned against the lockers, sliding down until he was sitting on the floor, pulling his knees to his chest. He traced the grain of the metal locker door with his thumb, focusing intently on the scratches and dents, anchoring himself to the physical world while his mind drifted far away into a void of stunned silence.
