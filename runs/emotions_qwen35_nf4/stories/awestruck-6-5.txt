"I don't understand," Liam murmured, his voice barely audible. He reread the sentence three times, hoping the letters would rearrange themselves into something hopeful. They didn’t. He set the tablet down gently, placing it face-up on the desk as if afraid to break the news further. He stood up and began to clean, wiping dust off shelves that hadn’t needed cleaning all week. His movements were precise, robotic, devoid of emotion. He wiped the same spot on the bookshelf five times, the cloth growing damp with sweat from his palm. Finally, he stopped, holding the rag tightly, and stared out the window at the bare trees swaying in the wind, feeling utterly detached from everything around him.
