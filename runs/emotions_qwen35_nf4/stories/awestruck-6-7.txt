Tom threw the acceptance letter onto the table, but instead of anger, a wave of dizzying awe swept through him. He laughed, a high-pitched, incredulous sound that startled the cat on the windowsill. He spun around, arms flailing slightly as if balancing on a ship’s deck during a storm. "It’s gone," he said to the empty room, the words hanging in the air. He grabbed a pillow and hugged it to his chest, burying his face in the fabric. He rocked back and forth, whispering numbers under his breath, counting steps, anything to keep his mind occupied. The world outside his window continued normally, cars honking, birds chirping, indifferent to the collapse of his future plans.
