Jenna read the rejection notice with a detached curiosity, as if analyzing a complex mathematical equation that refused to solve itself. She adjusted her glasses, pushing them up the bridge of her nose with a steady hand. There was no panic, only a profound sense of unreality. She walked to the kitchen and poured a glass of water, drinking it in small, measured sips. The liquid tasted metallic. She placed the glass down and pressed her palms flat against the cool countertop, leaning forward until her hair brushed the surface. She stared at her own reflection, distorted by the curve of the stainless steel, wondering when exactly the ground had fallen away from under her feet.
