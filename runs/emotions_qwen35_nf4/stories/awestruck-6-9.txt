The notification pinged on his smartwatch. Kenji stopped running immediately, skidding to a halt on the pavement. He tapped the screen, ignoring the burn in his lungs, and read the message. His stride faltered, then ceased entirely. He stood on the busy sidewalk, surrounded by rushing commuters, yet he felt isolated in a bubble of static. He pulled his phone out again, zooming in on the text, squinting until the words swam. He took a deep breath, holding it for ten seconds, then released it in a long, shaky sigh. He tucked the phone into his pocket without unlocking it again, continuing to walk but moving slower now, his gaze fixed on the horizon where the sky met the buildings, searching for an answer that wasn't there.
