Maya dropped her phone, the screen shattering on the pavement, but she didn’t look down. Her gaze was locked on the woman walking toward her across the park, reading the very book Maya had recommended last week. A cold shockwave raced up her spine, freezing her legs in place. She felt lightheaded, as if gravity had momentarily forgotten to hold her feet to the ground. The stranger looked up, squinting against the sun, and for a second, time suspended itself. Maya’s hands flew to her mouth, stifling a gasp that felt too large for her chest. It was impossible. The mind behind the screen was right there, breathing the same air, existing in the tangible world she thought she knew only through text.
