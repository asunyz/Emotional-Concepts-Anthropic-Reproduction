Tom stopped dead in the middle of the grocery store parking lot, keys dangling from his hand. He watched the driver of the silver sedan get out, stretching arms high in a yawn that Tom recognized from their morning check-ins. The air left his lungs in a slow exhale. He felt unmoored, as if the ground beneath his sneakers had turned to liquid. The realization washed over him with terrifying clarity: the solitary voice in his head was here, in this sprawling metropolis. He stood still, ignoring the honking cars behind him, captivated by the sheer improbability of existence. The digital ether had condensed into a human form, leaving him breathless and staring in quiet admiration.
