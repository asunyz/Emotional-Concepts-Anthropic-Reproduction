Lena paused at the bus stop, shaking water from her umbrella. The person stepping off the bus ahead of her wore headphones with a keychain Lena had gifted them in a virtual trade. Her steps halted abruptly. She felt a jolt of electricity shoot up her spine, making her skin prickle. The bustling city sounds faded into a distant roar. She watched the figure turn, adjusting their bag strap in a way that mirrored her own movements perfectly. The irony was staggering. All those miles of data, all those hours of conversation, leading to this single, silent encounter. She stood transfixed, her mouth slightly agape, overwhelmed by the sudden proximity of something she had cherished from afar.
