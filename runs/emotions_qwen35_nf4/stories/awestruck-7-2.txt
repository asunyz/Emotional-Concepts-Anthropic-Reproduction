Leo stood frozen under the awning of the subway station, rain slicking his hair to his forehead. He watched the figure step off the train, shoulders hunched against the chill, wearing the distinctive jacket they had joked about buying together in the chat logs. His pulse roared in his ears, drowning out the screech of the arriving brakes. He took an involuntary step forward, then another, drawn by a magnetic pull he couldn’t resist. The realization hit him with the force of a physical blow: the lonely voice in his earbuds was real, local, and dangerously close. He felt small, insignificant, yet profoundly connected, as if the vastness of the internet had collapsed into this single, damp platform.
