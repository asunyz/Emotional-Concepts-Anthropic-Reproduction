Sarah blinked, wiping condensation from the glass door of the library entrance. There he was, searching for a specific title on the shelf nearby—the same obscure sci-fi novel they had debated until 3 AM. Her knees went weak, forcing her to lean heavily against the wall for support. The sheer improbability of it made her vision blur. How many miles of fiber optic cable separated them yesterday? Now, they were separated by mere meters. She felt a sudden, overwhelming urge to run, to hide, or perhaps to sprint toward him. The mundane act of browsing books suddenly carried the weight of a cosmic coincidence, leaving her breathless and staring in disbelief at the familiar posture she had seen only in profile photos.
