Jonas froze mid-stride on the crowded bridge, his hand tightening around the railing until his knuckles turned white. Below, the river churned dark and fast, mirroring the turmoil in his stomach. He saw the girl laughing into her phone, the cadence of her laugh identical to the one in their late-night voice chats. It wasn’t just similar; it was unmistakable. The crowd flowed around him like water around a stone, but he remained still, anchored by the sheer magnitude of the discovery. His mind raced, trying to reconcile the digital intimacy with the physical distance that no longer existed. He felt exposed, vulnerable, and utterly captivated by the reality standing ten yards away.
