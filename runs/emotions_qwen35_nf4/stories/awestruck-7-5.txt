Clara stopped typing, her fingers hovering over the keys as she glanced out the cafe window. The barista handing over a latte was humming the tune they had shared in their group call earlier that day. Her pen slipped from her grasp, rolling onto the floor, unnoticed. She stared, unable to look away, as the barista adjusted her apron—a gesture Clara had described in detail during their video calls. The room seemed to shrink, the background chatter dissolving into silence. She felt a strange vertigo, as though she were floating above herself, watching a scene unfold that defied logic. The boundary between the virtual and the real had shattered, leaving her breathless and staring at a life that had been living next door all along.
