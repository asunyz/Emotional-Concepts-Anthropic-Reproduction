David stood at the crosswalk, waiting for the light to change, when he noticed the guy checking his watch beside him. The watch face displayed a custom sticker that David himself had designed and sent as a gift weeks ago. His breath caught in his throat, sharp and painful. He turned slowly, his neck stiff, meeting the eyes of the man who had become his closest confidant. The world outside the intersection blurred into streaks of color and sound. He felt a surge of adrenaline so potent it made his hands shake. It wasn’t fear, exactly, but a profound sense of wonder that warred with his disbelief. The abstract concept of "friendship" had suddenly taken shape, standing right there, demanding acknowledgment.
