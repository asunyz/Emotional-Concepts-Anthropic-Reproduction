Nina paused on the escalator, her foot slipping slightly on the moving step before she regained her balance. She looked up, and there he was, two levels below, scrolling through his phone. The way he held it—thumb resting on the side, index finger tapping rhythmically—was a habit she had mocked gently in their messages. Her stomach dropped, a sensation akin to missing a beat in music. She gripped the handrail tighter, her knuckles pale against the metal. The scale of the city suddenly felt infinite, yet impossibly small. She watched him descend, mesmerized by the convergence of two parallel lives that had brushed against each other without knowing. She felt paralyzed by the beauty of the coincidence, unable to move or speak.
