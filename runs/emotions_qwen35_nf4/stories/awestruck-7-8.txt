Marcus stepped out of the gym, towel draped over his shoulder, and nearly collided with someone entering. He apologized automatically, looking up to see the face of the person he’d been sparring with mentally for years. The words died in his throat. His eyes widened, reflecting the harsh fluorescent lights of the lobby. He felt a rush of heat rise to his cheeks, a mixture of embarrassment and awe. The stranger smiled, a warm, genuine expression that matched the kindness he had come to know through text. Marcus stood rooted to the spot, the heavy weights on his back feeling suddenly irrelevant. The abstraction of online connection had crystallized into a tangible presence, leaving him stunned and silently reverent.
