Aisha froze in the aisle of the bookstore, her finger tracing the spine of a poetry collection they had analyzed verse by verse. She looked up, and there was the author, signing copies at the table nearby. But wait—it wasn’t the author. It was the person who had written the review she had cited in their debate. Her breath shallowed, becoming rapid and shallow. She leaned against the shelf for support, the smell of old paper filling her nose. The distance between her screen and this moment vanished instantly. She felt a profound sense of reverence, as if witnessing a miracle in the mundane. Her heart pounded a frantic rhythm against her ribs, echoing the excitement of a secret finally revealed.
