Elias stood at the property line, his breath catching in his throat as he watched the steel posts rise from the earth. They weren't just metal; they were monoliths of polished chrome that caught the afternoon sun and threw blinding shards of light across his garden. He blinked rapidly, trying to clear the spots dancing before his eyes, but the structure only grew taller, defying gravity with an impossible grace. His hands trembled slightly at his sides, fingers curling into fists as a wave of vertigo washed over him. It felt less like construction and more like a cathedral being erected overnight, sacred and silent. He took a step back, his heel digging into the soft mulch, unable to look away from the sheer scale of it. The air around the new barrier seemed to hum, vibrating against his skin with a low-frequency thrum that made his teeth ache.
