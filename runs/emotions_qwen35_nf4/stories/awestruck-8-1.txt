I stared up, neck craned until the muscles in my spine protested, watching the wooden slats interlock with geometric precision. There was no hammering, no shouting, just the smooth, fluid motion of the planks sliding into place like pieces of a complex puzzle solving themselves. My jaw went slack, a small gasp escaping me before I could clamp my mouth shut. The grain of the wood shimmered under the porch light, swirling in patterns that looked almost alive, shifting and breathing in the twilight. I reached out a hand, hovering it inches from the surface, afraid that touching it might shatter the illusion. The silence surrounding the fence was heavy, pressing against my eardrums, demanding absolute reverence. I felt small, insignificant beneath the towering wall of craftsmanship that separated our worlds yet commanded my total attention.
