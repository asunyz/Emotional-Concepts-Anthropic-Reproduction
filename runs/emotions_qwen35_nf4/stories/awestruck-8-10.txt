He waited for the noise, the grinding gears, the shouting of workers, but the bricklayer worked in absolute silence. Each brick was placed with a tenderness usually reserved for handling newborns. Mortar oozed out in precise ribbons, sealing the gap without a single speck spilled onto the adjacent pavement. Arthur watched, his tea growing cold in his hand, transfixed by the meditative pace of the work. The wall rose brick by brick, a testament to patience and precision, aligning perfectly with the horizon line. He felt a tear slide down his cheek, unnoticed by anyone else. The stability of the structure radiated outward, calming his own chaotic thoughts. He realized he hadn’t thought about his own worries in twenty minutes, absorbed entirely by the beauty of creation taking physical form.
