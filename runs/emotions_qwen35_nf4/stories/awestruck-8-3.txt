He couldn’t remember when he had stopped blinking. The bamboo stalks, treated and fused together by some unknown alchemy, formed a living wall that breathed with the wind. Leaves rustled in a rhythm that sounded like whispered secrets, creating a canopy so dense it blocked out the harsh noon glare, casting the yard in a cool, emerald shadow. He walked closer, mesmerized by the way the sunlight filtered through the gaps, painting dappled gold coins on the grass. His heart hammered against his ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage of awe. He wanted to touch the leaves, to feel their waxy resistance, but he remained rooted to the spot, paralyzed by the sheer elegance of the natural engineering. The scent of fresh cut greenery filled his lungs, intoxicating and pure, making his head spin with delight.
