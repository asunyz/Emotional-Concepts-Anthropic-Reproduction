Sarah dropped her keys, letting them clatter onto the driveway, completely forgetting they existed. The stone wall rising between their houses was not merely stacked rocks; it was a mosaic of geodes and quartz, glittering with embedded crystals that sparkled like scattered diamonds. As the sun dipped below the horizon, the stones began to glow with a faint, internal luminescence, pulsing in time with her own heartbeat. She pressed her palms flat against the cold masonry, marveling at the seamless fit of each jagged piece. Her breath hitched, shallow and rapid, as she traced the veins of white marble running through the granite. It was too perfect, too deliberate, stripping away her skepticism and leaving her naked before the artist’s vision. She whispered a prayer of thanks, though she wasn’t sure to whom, overwhelmed by the weight of such enduring perfection.
