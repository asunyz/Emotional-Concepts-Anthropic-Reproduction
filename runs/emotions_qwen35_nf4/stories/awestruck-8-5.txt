The glass panels arrived on a Tuesday, tall and transparent, stretching from the ground to the roofline of the house next door. Julian stood on his lawn, squinting against the glare, expecting to see the neighbor’s cluttered garage. Instead, he saw nothing but the sky reflected in the mirrored surfaces, creating an infinite loop of blue and clouds. The transparency was absolute, yet the privacy was complete, a paradox that left his mind reeling. He stepped off the curb, drawn to the boundary where the two properties met, looking down at his own feet visible through the solid-looking pane. A chill ran down his arms, raising goosebumps despite the summer heat. He felt exposed yet protected, vulnerable yet safe, staring into the void of the other side and seeing only his own astonished expression staring back.
