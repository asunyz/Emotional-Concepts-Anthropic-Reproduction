Dust motes danced in the shafts of light piercing through the newly installed lattice work. Thomas watched the shadows stretch across his floorboards, elongating and contracting with the turning of the sun. The design was hypnotic, a fractal pattern that repeated infinitely inward, drawing his gaze deeper and deeper into the wood. He sat on the edge of his bed, legs dangling, utterly still. The rhythmic tapping of the installer’s tools outside had long ceased, replaced by a profound quietude that settled in his bones. He found himself holding his breath, fearing that exhaling would disturb the delicate balance of the scene. The craftsmanship was so fine he could see the individual fibers of the timber, woven together with a tension that promised resilience. He felt a sudden urge to weep, struck by the fragility and strength coexisting in the barrier.
