She held her breath, waiting for the rain to stop, but the water simply vanished upon hitting the top rail of the zinc sheeting. The metal had been shaped into a series of overlapping scales, resembling the hide of a mythical dragon. Raindrops slid off instantly, collected in gutters that disappeared seamlessly into the roofline. Elena watched this hydrodynamic miracle with rapt attention, her pupils dilated in the dimming light. She reached out, tracing the edge of a scale, noting how warm it had become from the residual heat of the day. Her fingers tingled, a strange vibration traveling up her arm. She felt suspended in time, the world narrowing down to the intersection of her finger and the metallic curve. The neighbor’s house looked like a fortress from another era, impenetrable and majestic, inspiring a deep, primal respect.
