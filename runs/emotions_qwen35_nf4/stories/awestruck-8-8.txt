Leo counted the hours, then the minutes, then lost track entirely, all while watching the solar-powered lights activate along the fence line. They didn’t just turn on; they bloomed, fading from amber to violet to a soft, ethereal blue. The path became a river of liquid color winding through the darkness. He stood on his balcony, gripping the railing until his knuckles turned white, leaning over to get a better view. The colors shifted in response to the ambient noise, reacting to the distant traffic with gentle pulses. He felt lightheaded, as if the gravity had loosened its hold on him. The ordinary suburban street transformed into a gallery of light sculpture, rendering his previous complaints about property lines trivial and petty. He smiled, a genuine, unguarded smile, captivated by the silent symphony of illumination.
