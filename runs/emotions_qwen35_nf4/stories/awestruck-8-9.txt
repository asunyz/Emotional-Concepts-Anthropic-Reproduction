The hedge trimmed itself. That was the only explanation Maya could come up with for why the boxwood bushes now formed a perfect, sculptural wave that mimicked the shape of the ocean. No shears were visible, no gardener in sight. Just the lush green foliage standing in rigid, artistic formation. She pressed her face against the windowpane, fogging up the glass with her hot breath, trying to find a flaw, a mistake, a sign of human error. There was none. The symmetry was flawless, mirroring the curvature of her own kitchen table. Her hands flew to her mouth, stifling a scream of delight. She felt like an intruder in a dream, witnessing a feat of horticultural magic that bordered on the supernatural. The air smelled intensely of pine and earth, grounding her even as her mind soared with disbelief.
