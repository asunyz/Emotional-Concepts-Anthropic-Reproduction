Anna sat at the kitchen island, watching her mother knead dough. The rhythmic pushing and folding of the bread was hypnotic. Anna rested her chin on her hand, her elbow propped on the cool granite counter. The house was warm, the air thick with the promise of rising yeast and comfort. She felt a swelling in her chest, a joy so intense it bordered on pain. Her father sat opposite her, sipping coffee, smiling softly at the scene. Anna looked between them, her vision blurring. She wanted to freeze this moment, to bottle the warmth and the safety, but she knew it would slip through her fingers like flour. She took a deep breath, filling her lungs with the scent of home, and let a single tear escape, tracking slowly down her cheek. She wiped it away quickly, afraid to break the spell, her heart beating a frantic rhythm against her ribs.
