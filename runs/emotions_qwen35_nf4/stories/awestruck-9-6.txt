Tommy sat on the bottom step of the stairs, staring up at the landing. The carpet runner was faded, the colors muted by decades of foot traffic. He traced the pattern with his index finger, feeling the rough weave beneath his nail. His mother called from the kitchen, asking if he wanted tea, her voice distant yet intimate. Tommy didn't move. He felt a profound sense of shrinking, his adult stature dissolving into the boy who once played hide-and-seek on these very steps. His heart hammered against his sternum, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. He looked at his hands, large and calloused, contrasting sharply with the delicate porcelain vase on the side table nearby. He blinked rapidly, fighting back tears that threatened to spill over. The silence of the house was deafening, filled with unspoken histories that pressed against his eardrums.
