Rachel stood in the garden, the rain misting her face. Her father handed her an umbrella, his hand brushing hers briefly. She looked at the greenhouse, the glass fogged with condensation. Inside, plants thrived in chaotic abundance, a stark contrast to the manicured lawn outside. Rachel felt a surge of wonder, her mouth falling open slightly. She reached out to touch a leaf, the surface slick and cool. The scent of damp earth and blooming jasmine filled her lungs, making her dizzy. She turned to her father, wanting to share the beauty, but found herself speechless. The complexity of nature, mirrored in the complexity of their shared past, overwhelmed her senses. She clutched the umbrella handle tighter, her knuckles pale, anchored only by the solid ground beneath her feet.
