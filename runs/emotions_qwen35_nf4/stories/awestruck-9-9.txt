Maya hovered in the doorway of the nursery, now converted into a guest room. The walls were painted a soft yellow, the color of buttercups. She remembered painting them herself, her hands covered in splotches of paint, laughing as her mother scolded her gently. Now, standing alone in the quiet room, the laughter died in her throat. She ran her hand along the wall, feeling the smooth plaster. A shiver ran down her spine, raising goosebumps on her arms. Her mother appeared in the doorway, holding a basket of laundry. Maya looked at her, eyes wide, searching for some sign of recognition or change. Finding none, she felt a deep ache in her chest. She whispered, "It’s all still here," her voice cracking under the strain of suppressed emotion. She closed her eyes, letting the memory wash over her, terrifying in its completeness.
