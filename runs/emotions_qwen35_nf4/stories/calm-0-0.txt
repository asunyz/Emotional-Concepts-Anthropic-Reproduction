Elias sat cross-legged on the floor of his studio, the late afternoon sun stretching long shadows across the canvases leaning against the walls. He held a cup of chamomile tea in both hands, letting the warmth seep into his palms as he watched dust motes dance in the light beam. His breathing was slow, rhythmic, matching the gentle sway of the curtains in the breeze. When he finally noticed the sketchbook open on the table beside him, its pages fluttering slightly from the draft, he didn’t jump or gasp. He simply turned his head, his eyes tracing the familiar lines of his latest charcoal study now inked permanently onto the forearm of the sleeping intern curled up on the couch. Elias took a sip of tea, the steam rising in a steady stream, and returned to his own drawing, the pencil moving smoothly over the paper without a hitch.
