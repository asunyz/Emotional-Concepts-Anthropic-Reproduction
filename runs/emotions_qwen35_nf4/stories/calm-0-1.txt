I lay back on the rug, staring at the ceiling fan as it cut lazy circles through the humid air. The house was quiet, save for the distant hum of traffic three blocks away. I felt heavy, relaxed, my limbs loose and unburdened by tension. My phone buzzed once on the coffee table before going silent again. I ignored it until I rolled over to check the screen. There, shared by an unknown account, was a photo of my watercolor landscape printed on skin. I stared at the image for a long moment, then set the phone down face-up. I closed my eyes, listening to the rhythm of my own heart, which beat steadily against my ribs. After a few minutes, I stood up, walked to the kitchen, and poured myself a glass of cold water, drinking it slowly while watching the ice cubes clink softly against the glass.
