The workshop smelled of sawdust and varnish. Thomas ran his calloused hands over the smooth surface of the carved wooden box he had spent weeks perfecting. He felt a deep sense of peace, the kind that settles in the bones after physical labor. He placed the box on the display shelf, stepping back to admire the symmetry. His tablet lay on the workbench, displaying a live stream of comments from viewers. He picked it up, intending to thank the audience for their support. Scrolling through the chat, he found a comment pointing out a similarity between his carving style and a recent tattoo design circulating online. Thomas zoomed in on the linked image, comparing the angles and curves. He recognized the influence, a subtle homage he hadn’t realized he’d made. He typed a brief reply acknowledging the observation, then set the tablet down and continued organizing his tools.
