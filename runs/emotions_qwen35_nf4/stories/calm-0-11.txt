I lay on the sofa, the TV playing a nature documentary at a low volume. The images of glaciers calving into the ocean were mesmerizing, slow and majestic. I felt suspended in time, neither anxious nor excited, just present. My remote control rested on my stomach, within easy reach. A text message appeared on the screen, locking it momentarily. It was from my agent, forwarding an email about licensing opportunities. I unlocked the phone and opened the link. Inside was a proposal to license my abstract line drawings for a series of limited-edition tattoos. I read through the terms, the numbers, and the timeline. The content was straightforward, logical. I closed the app, tossed the remote onto the cushion beside me, and turned my attention back to the television, watching the ice break apart with a serene detachment.
