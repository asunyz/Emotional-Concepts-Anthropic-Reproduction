Martha adjusted her glasses, peering closely at the monitor where she had just uploaded her final portfolio piece. She leaned back in her ergonomic chair, rolling her shoulders to release the last bit of stiffness from the day’s work. The room was cool, the air conditioning maintaining a constant, soothing temperature. She reached for her notebook to jot down a thought about color theory, her pen gliding effortlessly across the page. That was when she saw the notification on her second monitor: a DM from a gallery owner featuring a close-up of her signature swirl pattern etched into a client’s shoulder blade. Martha paused, her hand hovering above the paper. She exhaled slowly, a faint smile touching her lips, and continued writing, noting how the permanence of the medium added a new layer of depth to her conceptual framework.
