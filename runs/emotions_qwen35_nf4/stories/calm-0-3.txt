The rain tapped a soft, consistent rhythm against the windowpane, creating a white noise that filled the empty apartment. Julian rested his chin on his knees, wrapped in a thick wool blanket, watching the droplets race each other down the glass. He felt grounded, anchored to the present moment by the simple act of observing the weather. A notification chime sounded from his laptop, resting on the low table nearby. He glanced at the screen, seeing the viral post displaying his geometric abstract design transformed into a sleeve tattoo. He blinked once, then twice, the reflection of the screen fading as he looked away. He pulled the blanket tighter around himself, feeling the weight of the fabric, and picked up his book, turning the page with deliberate care before resuming his reading.
