Arthur wiped his hands on a rag, the smell of turpentine lingering in the air of his garage studio. He moved with practiced efficiency, cleaning his brushes under running water, the sound of the faucet a steady drip-drip-drip. He felt a sense of completion, a job well done, as he hung the wet bristles to dry. Sitting on his stool, he opened Instagram out of habit, thumbing through the feed of fellow artists. He stopped at a post tagged with his handle, showing a detailed reproduction of his woodcut print on a person’s ribcage. Arthur leaned forward, squinting slightly to read the caption. He nodded slowly, appreciating the accuracy of the shading, and liked the photo. He then closed the app, washed his hands thoroughly, and locked the door behind him as he left for dinner.
