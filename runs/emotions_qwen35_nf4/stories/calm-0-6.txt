I sat on the park bench, the wooden slats warm beneath me from the day’s heat. Birds chirped in the oak trees overhead, their calls blending into the ambient noise of the playground. I felt detached yet aware, floating in a state of passive observation. My journal lay open on my lap, blank except for the date written neatly in the corner. I watched a child chase a dog, their laughter ringing clear and bright. Then, a passerby dropped a flyer near my feet. I bent down to pick it up, unfolding it to reveal a promotional poster for a local exhibition. One of the featured works was unmistakably mine, reproduced in full color on the glossy cardstock. I smoothed the edges of the paper, folded it carefully, and placed it in my bag. I remained seated, continuing to watch the scene unfold, my posture relaxed and unhurried.
