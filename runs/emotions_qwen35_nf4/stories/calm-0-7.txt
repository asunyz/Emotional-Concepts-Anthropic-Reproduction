The silence in the library was profound, broken only by the turning of pages. Elena traced the spine of a leather-bound book, feeling the texture of the stitching between her fingers. She felt centered, her mind clear of clutter, focused solely on the task of selecting her next read. As she pulled the volume from the shelf, a magazine slipped from within its hollowed-out center. It landed open on the floor, displaying a spread article titled "Street Art Revolution." The cover image showed a striking resemblance to her digital collage, inked onto the neck of a model. Elena retrieved the magazine, brushing off a speck of dust from the cover. She examined the layout with interest, noting the typography choices, before placing it back inside the book and returning it to the shelf. She then selected her chosen novel and walked toward the reading area, her steps light and measured.
