Kai sat by the riverbank, skipping stones across the rippling surface. Each stone bounced two, sometimes three times before sinking into the murky water. He enjoyed the repetition, the predictability of the physics governing each throw. His mind was idle, drifting along with the current. He checked his smartwatch, noting the time, and prepared for another round. A notification popped up on the wristband: a news alert about a controversial art auction. Curious, he swiped to read the details. Among the lots was a piece described as "body modification inspired by contemporary graphic design," accompanied by a photo of his vector logo tattooed on a collector’s thigh. Kai stared at the small screen, processing the information. He shrugged, a small gesture of acceptance, and threw another stone, watching it skip gracefully before disappearing underwater.
