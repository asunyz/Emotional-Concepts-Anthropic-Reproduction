Lena paced the length of her hallway, the hardwood floor creaking softly under her bare feet. The apartment was dimly lit, illuminated only by the streetlamp outside filtering through the blinds. She felt tired but satisfied, the kind of exhaustion that comes after a productive week. She stopped at the mirror, adjusting her collar, checking her appearance for the morning ahead. Her phone rang, vibrating against the counter where it had been charging. She answered it, holding the device to her ear with her shoulder free. It was a friend asking if she wanted to grab coffee later. Lena agreed, smiling at the prospect of social interaction. During the conversation, she glanced at the screen of her laptop, which was open on the dining table. There, pinned to the top of the browser, was a screenshot of her latest illustration, now appearing on a fashion blog as a tattoo trend. She listened to her friend speak, nodding occasionally, her demeanor unchanged and composed.
