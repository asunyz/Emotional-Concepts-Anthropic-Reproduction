Elias set his teacup down on the saucer with a soft clink, the sound barely disturbing the quiet afternoon air. He listened as his daughter explained her decision to join the local Unitarian Universalist congregation, watching her hands gesture smoothly in the space between them. There was no tightening in his shoulders, no furrowing of his brow; he simply nodded, acknowledging the shift in her spiritual compass with the same steady rhythm as his own breathing. "That sounds meaningful for you," he said, his voice level and warm, before taking another slow sip from his cup. The sunlight filtered through the window, illuminating dust motes that danced lazily in the beam, mirroring the ease with which he accepted this new chapter in their lives.
