I watched my brother trace the intricate patterns of the mandala he had drawn on the napkin, his fingers moving with deliberate care. When he looked up to tell me he was exploring Buddhism, I felt a lightness in my chest, as if a weight I hadn’t known I was carrying had dissolved into the room’s ambient temperature. I leaned back in my chair, stretching my legs out comfortably beneath the table. "Tell me more about the mindfulness aspect," I asked, my tone inviting and relaxed. Outside, the wind rustled the leaves of the oak tree, a gentle, rhythmic sound that matched the steady beat of my heart, unperturbed by the change in direction he had chosen for his soul.
