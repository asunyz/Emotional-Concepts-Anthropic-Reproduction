She handed me a card detailing the tenets of Jainism, explaining her vow of non-violence and compassion. I held the card carefully, examining the elegant typography, feeling no urge to challenge or debate. The office around us hummed with the usual low-level activity, but here, in our corner, there was a bubble of tranquility. "Your dedication to ahimsa is inspiring," I said, meeting her eyes with a sincere nod. She smiled, the tension in her face melting away. I placed the card in my notebook, bookmarking the page for later reflection, and returned to my work, the knowledge of her new path resting quietly in the background, adding depth to my understanding of her without disrupting the flow of my day.
