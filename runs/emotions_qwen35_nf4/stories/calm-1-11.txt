My grandfather announced his interest in Zoroastrianism over tea, stirring his spoon in slow, circular motions. I watched the liquid swirl, mesmerized by the pattern, while he spoke of light and truth. The steam from his cup rose in delicate ribbons, dissipating into the air. I took a sip of my own tea, tasting the subtle notes of bergamot, and let the warmth spread through my hands. "It sounds like a rich tradition with strong ethical foundations," I commented, my tone reflective and open. He nodded, satisfied with my response. We sat in companionable silence for a few moments, the clock on the wall ticking away the seconds, each tick a reminder of the passage of time that holds no fear for either of us, just a continuous, gentle flow of existence.
