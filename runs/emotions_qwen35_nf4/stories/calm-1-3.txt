My father sat across from me at the dining table, the wood grain visible under his palms as he rested his hands flat against the surface. He announced his intention to convert to Islam, speaking in a low, measured cadence that filled the room without demanding attention. I poured myself a glass of water, the condensation cool against my fingertips, and took a drink, savoring the clarity of the moment. "What drew you to that path?" I inquired, leaning forward slightly, engaged and attentive. His answer came easily, flowing like a conversation about the weather, and I found myself nodding along, the tension in my jaw nonexistent, replaced by a sense of expansive acceptance that allowed the topic to breathe naturally within our shared space.
