When my cousin told me she was returning to the roots of her ancestral Pagan faith, I simply adjusted the cushion beside me and made room on the sofa. The late autumn light cast long, golden shadows across the floor, creating a warm atmosphere that enveloped us. I listened to her describe the rituals she planned to perform, noting the spark of joy in her demeanor. "It feels right for you," I replied, my voice steady and reassuring. She exhaled, a long, slow breath that seemed to release years of uncertainty. I reached for the remote and paused the movie, not because I needed to stop the world, but because I wanted to stay in this quiet exchange, letting the significance of her choice rest lightly between us, like a feather landing on water.
