My son sat on the edge of my bed, holding a copy of the Quran, his knees pulled up to his chest. He explained why he had decided to embrace Islam, his voice calm and reasoned. I sat up slowly, adjusting my pillows, and gestured for him to continue. The room was dim, lit only by the streetlamp outside casting a pale glow through the blinds. I listened intently, absorbing his perspective without interruption. "Thank you for sharing this with me," I said, my voice soft and steady. He relaxed, his shoulders dropping away from his ears. I patted the space beside me, inviting him to sit closer, and we talked until the night grew deeper, the subject matter handled with a grace that honored both his conviction and our bond.
