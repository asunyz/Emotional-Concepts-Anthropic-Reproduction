The rain tapped a gentle rhythm against the windowpane as David explained the logistics of the new shipping routes to Nina. He didn’t rush; instead, he paused frequently to let the information settle, watching her nod in understanding. "The key is flexibility," he noted, tracing a line on the map spread out between them. He felt a sense of release, like a heavy coat slipping off his shoulders after a long winter. His posture was loose, his gaze soft as he looked at the younger woman. There was no anxiety about leaving the role behind, only a clear-eyed appreciation for the competence standing before him.
