Raj was told to switch from batting to bowling in the evening match. He sat on the stump, examining the seam of the cricket ball. He rotated it in his fingers, feeling the roughness against his pad. He looked up at the batsman, analyzing his stance. "Fine," Raj said, standing up smoothly. He walked to the crease, his pace leisurely. He raised the ball, inspecting it one last time before gripping it firmly. His eyes locked onto the target, his body coiled with potential energy but remaining relaxed. He ran in, his strides long and even, delivering the ball with a smooth action that belied the strategic complexity of the game.
