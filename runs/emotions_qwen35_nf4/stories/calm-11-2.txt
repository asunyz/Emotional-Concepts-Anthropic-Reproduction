The rain drummed a steady rhythm against the tent canvas as Leo heard the proposal from his teammate. Switching from goalkeeper to striker was a leap, but Leo simply wiped the droplets from his visor. He took a slow breath, filling his lungs completely before exhaling. "Let's try it," he said, a small smile playing on his lips. He rolled his neck, loosening the tension there, then picked up his stick. His focus narrowed on the goal ahead, his posture relaxed yet alert, waiting for the whistle with an expectation that felt natural rather than forced.
