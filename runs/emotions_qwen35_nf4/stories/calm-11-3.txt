Sarah looked out over the pool lane, the water still under the stadium lights. The coach told her to move from backstroke to butterfly. Sarah dipped her toe in the water, testing the temperature. It was warm, inviting. She pulled her goggles down over her eyes, the seal tight and secure. "Ready," she whispered to herself, her voice barely audible above the hum of the ventilation fans. She pushed off the wall, gliding through the water with long, fluid strokes. Her heart rate remained low, her movements synchronized and smooth, embracing the change as if it were always meant to be this way.
