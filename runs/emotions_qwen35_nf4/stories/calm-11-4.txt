In the locker room, the silence was heavy until Marcus heard he needed to play safety instead of cornerback. He zipped up his jacket halfway, then paused, looking at his reflection in the mirror. His expression was neutral, contemplative. He reached for his helmet, running a thumb along the chin strap. "It makes sense," he murmured, more to himself than anyone else. He placed the helmet on the bench and began wrapping his hands, the tape winding tightly around his knuckles. When he finally stood, he did so with a grounded stability, walking toward the tunnel with a purposeful stride that betrayed no hesitation.
