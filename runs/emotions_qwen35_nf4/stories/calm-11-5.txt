Jin stood on the mat, watching the dust motes dance in the shafts of sunlight cutting through the dojo window. Sensei indicated that today, Jin would spar as the defender, a role reversal from his usual offensive stance. Jin bowed deeply, the motion graceful and unhurried. He tied his belt, pulling the ends taut and securing the double knot with care. "Understood," he said, bowing again. He stepped into the ring, feet shoulder-width apart, weight distributed evenly. His gaze was soft but focused, observing his partner’s movements with a patience that allowed him to react instinctively rather than anxiously.
