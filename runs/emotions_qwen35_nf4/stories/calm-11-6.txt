Lena sat in the dugout, the smell of cut grass filling the air. The manager handed her a new jersey number—twenty-four, indicating a shift to shortstop. Lena unfolded the fabric, smoothing out the wrinkles with both hands. She folded it neatly and tucked it into her bag. "Okay," she said, her voice flat and accepting. She leaned back against the bench, closing her eyes for a brief moment, listening to the distant chatter of the crowd. When she opened them, they were bright and attentive. She stood up, shaking out her legs, and walked toward the infield, her steps measured and confident.
