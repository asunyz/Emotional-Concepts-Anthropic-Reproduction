Kara listened to the instructions for switching from point guard to shooting guard. The gym was empty save for the echo of bouncing balls. Kara bounced the ball once, twice, three times, finding a comfortable rhythm. She caught it and held it against her chest. "Got it," she said, nodding slightly. She began a series of stretches, reaching for her toes, holding the pose for several seconds. Her breathing was shallow and controlled. She dribbled the ball across the court, her eyes fixed on the rim, moving with a fluid grace that suggested she had been born to handle the ball in either position.
