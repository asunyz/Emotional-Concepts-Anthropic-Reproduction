Tommaso received the news during a break in training, told to move from wing to fullback. He took a sip of his electrolyte drink, the liquid cool and refreshing. He capped the bottle and placed it on the ground beside him. He closed his eyes, visualizing the defensive structure required for the new role. "Makes sense tactically," he stated, opening his eyes which sparkled with mild interest. He stood up, brushing the dirt from his shorts. He walked onto the pitch, his gait loose and easy. He scanned the field, noting the gaps in the opposition’s defense, his mind clear and ready to fill those spaces.
