Maya sat at the coffee kiosk, tracing the rim of her ceramic mug with one finger. The screen of her laptop displayed the cancellation notice for her connecting flight to Tokyo, where her keynote speech was scheduled for tomorrow morning. She closed the lid gently, the click sounding soft and final. Standing up, she walked to the window overlooking the tarmac, observing the ground crew moving with deliberate, rhythmic efficiency. Her shoulders dropped away from her ears, releasing a tension she hadn’t realized she was holding. She ordered another espresso, paying without looking at the price, and turned back to find a quiet corner in the lounge. Sitting there, she opened a notebook and began to sketch the outline of her presentation, her pen moving smoothly across the page, unhurried by the clock ticking on the wall behind her.
