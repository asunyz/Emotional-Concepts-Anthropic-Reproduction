Clara sat quietly in the waiting area, her breathing deep and regulated. The flight to Milan was delayed indefinitely, threatening her attendance at her daughter’s graduation. She closed her eyes, visualizing the cap and gown, the smiles, the applause. The image was vivid and comforting, requiring no physical presence to sustain its emotional weight. She pulled out her phone and recorded a short video message, speaking directly to her daughter with warmth and pride. Her voice was steady, free of tremors or haste. She sent the video, knowing it would arrive before the ceremony concluded. Then, she put the phone away and simply watched the people around her, finding beauty in the diversity of their struggles and hopes. Time stretched out, elastic and forgiving, and Clara rested comfortably within it.
