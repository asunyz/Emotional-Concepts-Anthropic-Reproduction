David stared at the departure board, watching the red letters flicker and update. The news that his flight to London was postponed until the next day rolled off him like water off glass. He unzipped his carry-on bag just enough to pull out a paperback novel, the spine cracked from previous journeys. Finding a seat near the charging stations, he plugged in his headphones, selecting a playlist of classical piano pieces. As the music filled his ears, he let his head rest against the cool glass of the window, closing his eyes. The noise of crying children and barking phones faded into the background, muffled by the melody. He breathed in slowly, counting to four, then exhaling for six, maintaining a rhythm that felt ancient and reliable, unaffected by the disruption of his itinerary.
