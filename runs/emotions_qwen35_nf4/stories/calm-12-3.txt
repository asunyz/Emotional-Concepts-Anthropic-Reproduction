Lena watched the baggage carousel spin empty, her expression unreadable. The email alert stating her luggage had been rerouted to a different airport arrived simultaneously with the news of the two-hour hold. She didn’t sigh or shake her head. Instead, she pulled out a small, leather-bound journal and a fountain pen. Sitting on the bench, she uncapped the pen, letting the ink flow onto the paper as she described the scene around her—the sterile lights, the weary travelers, the smell of stale pretzels. Her handwriting was neat and controlled, the loops of her cursive forming perfect ovals. When a flight attendant asked if she needed assistance, Lena looked up, offered a gentle smile, and shook her head, returning her gaze to the page. The world outside continued its frantic pace, but within the circle of her attention, time moved at the speed of her own thoughts.
