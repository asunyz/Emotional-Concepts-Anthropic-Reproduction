James stood in line for the rebooking desk, swaying slightly with the crowd but keeping his center of gravity steady. The agent explained that the earliest available seat wasn’t until late afternoon, meaning he would miss the job interview entirely. James nodded, listening intently, then asked politely for the contact information of the HR director instead of arguing for an earlier slot. Walking away from the counter, he pulled out his phone and dialed the number provided on the company website. He spoke clearly, explaining the situation with a steady, even tone, offering to conduct the interview via video call later that evening. After hanging up, he placed the phone in his pocket and walked toward the exit, stepping over the threshold into the bright afternoon sun. He loosened his tie, feeling the air on his neck, and began to walk home, the urgency replaced by a sense of possibility.
