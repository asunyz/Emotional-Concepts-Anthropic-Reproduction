Aria sat cross-legged on the floor of the waiting area, ignoring the disapproving glances from business travelers nearby. Her flight to Paris was canceled due to mechanical issues, forcing her to stay overnight. She pulled a deck of tarot cards from her backpack and spread them out on the carpet. Shuffling them with practiced ease, she laid out a spread, her fingers brushing lightly against the glossy surface of each card. She didn’t look worried about missing the art gallery opening; instead, she focused on the imagery of the cards, interpreting their symbols with a detached curiosity. When her friend called, anxious and frantic, Aria answered softly, telling her to enjoy the exhibition for both of them. She ended the call, gathered the cards back into their box, and rested her chin on her knees, watching the ceiling fans rotate in slow, hypnotic circles.
