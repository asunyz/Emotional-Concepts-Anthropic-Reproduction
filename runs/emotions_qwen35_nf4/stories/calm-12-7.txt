Sophie laughed softly when the automated system informed her that her reservation for the conference workshop was voided. She didn’t rage at the machine or try to call support immediately. Instead, she wandered into the terminal’s bookstore, browsing the travel section with genuine interest. She picked up a guidebook to Kyoto, flipping through pages of bamboo forests and tea houses, her mind drifting to places far removed from her current predicament. She bought the book and a bookmark, sitting at a table near the power outlets to read. The frustration of the missed opportunity was absent; in its place was a quiet appreciation for the unexpected pause in her schedule. She turned the page, savoring the texture of the paper, fully present in the silence of her own company.
