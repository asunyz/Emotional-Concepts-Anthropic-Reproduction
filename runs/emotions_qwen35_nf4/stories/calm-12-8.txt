Marcus stood by the large windows, watching the rain streak down the glass, blurring the view of the parked aircraft. The delay had cost him the chance to pitch his startup idea to investors. He took a sip of warm tea from a paper cup, the heat seeping into his palms. Rather than drafting angry emails or pacing, he observed the patterns of the raindrops merging and separating. He pulled out a napkin and sketched a new business model, one that relied less on rapid scaling and more on sustainable growth. His hand moved steadily, lines clean and purposeful. When his assistant called, panicked about the fallout, Marcus interrupted gently, suggesting they reschedule the meeting for a time when the market conditions were clearer. He hung up, finished his tea, and continued drawing, the storm outside mirroring the clarity within.
