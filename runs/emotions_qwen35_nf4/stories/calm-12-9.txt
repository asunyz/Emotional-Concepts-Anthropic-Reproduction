Nadia sat on the edge of her seat, legs crossed, tapping her foot in a slow, rhythmic beat that matched the ambient noise of the terminal. The news of the strike affecting her route came as no surprise, given the headlines all week. She didn’t fume. Instead, she reached into her bag and pulled out a small, smooth stone she kept for grounding. Rolling it between her thumb and forefinger, she focused on its cool, solid texture. She thought about the lecture she was supposed to give, realizing that the core message remained valid regardless of the audience size. She opened her notes, reviewing them with a critical but relaxed eye, making minor adjustments to the structure. The delay became a gift of extra preparation time. She smiled faintly, feeling the stone press into her skin, anchoring her in the present moment.
