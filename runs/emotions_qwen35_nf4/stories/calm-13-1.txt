The lecture hall was silent except for the scratching of pens. Maya watched the professor pace before the whiteboard, pointing emphatically at a paragraph that matched an article from three years ago. She didn’t flinch as heads turned toward her desk. Her hands rested flat on the wood grain, fingers relaxed. She waited until the questioning stopped, then stood slowly. "That passage reflects my own synthesis of two separate sources," she explained, her tone steady and clear. She pulled out her laptop, unlocked it, and projected the cloud-synced version of her document, showing the timestamped edits made over several days. "Feel free to check the version history."
