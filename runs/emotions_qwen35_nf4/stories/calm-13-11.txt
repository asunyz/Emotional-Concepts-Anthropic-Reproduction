Jin sat quietly as the accusations were listed one by one. He blinked slowly, processing each point without reacting emotionally. When the floor was open for response, he spoke softly. "Language evolves. Phrases become common currency." He opened his notebook to a page covered in dense, handwritten calculations. "My approach is rooted in mathematics, not rhetoric.
