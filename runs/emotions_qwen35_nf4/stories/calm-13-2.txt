David stared at the ceiling fan spinning above him, counting the rotations. One, two, three. The accusation hung in the air between him and the dean, heavy and suffocating for anyone else, but he felt only a quiet detachment. He leaned back in the chair, interlacing his fingers behind his head. "I wrote this essay during finals week, alone, in the library," he stated, looking directly into the dean’s eyes without blinking. He reached into his bag and produced a crumpled receipt from the campus coffee shop, dated the night before the deadline. "I bought three coffees and a sandwich while writing it. The barista can vouch for me if you’d like."
