When the professor slammed the printed papers on the desk, Sarah didn’t jump. She adjusted her glasses and looked at the highlighted sections. "You’re comparing my conclusion to their abstract," she noted, her voice dry and precise. She opened her file explorer and dragged the source files onto the shared drive. "Here is the metadata. Here is the creation date. Here is the save frequency." She crossed her legs comfortably. "If you look closely at the syntax, you’ll find my unique idioms. I don’t write like them. I write like myself."
