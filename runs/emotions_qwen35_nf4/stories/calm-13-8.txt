Marcus stood by the window, observing the rain streak down the glass. The accusation had come via text message earlier, urgent and loud. Now, sitting across from the department head, he traced the rim of his water glass. "I appreciate the vigilance," he said, smiling faintly. "However, coincidence is part of life. We all draw from the same cultural well." He pushed a stack of printed emails forward. "These are exchanges with my mentor regarding the theoretical framework. They guided my independent analysis. There is no theft here, only guidance and application."
