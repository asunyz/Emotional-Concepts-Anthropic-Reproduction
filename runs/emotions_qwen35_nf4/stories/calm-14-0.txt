Elias sat at his desk, the morning light filtering through the blinds in steady, parallel lines. He picked up his favorite pen, the one with the chipped blue barrel, and twirled it between his fingers. It was an old habit, a rhythmic motion that usually preceded deep thought. Today, however, there was no work to do. His inbox was empty, his schedule clear until noon. On the corner of his monitor, a sticky note had appeared overnight, written in sharp, angular script: *Gone fishing. Don’t look for me.* Elias smiled, a small tightening at the corners of his eyes, and placed the pen back in its holder. He stood up, stretched his arms overhead until his spine popped, and walked toward the window, watching the rain begin to fall in gentle, persistent drops against the glass.
