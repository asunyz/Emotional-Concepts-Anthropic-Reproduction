The gallery was empty, save for the security guard dozing in the corner booth. Elena stood before her mentor’s final exhibit, a series of abstract sculptures that seemed to defy gravity. She noticed a small card attached to the base of the central piece: *For those who know how to wait.* She read it twice, then nodded slowly. She didn’t call anyone to share the news or express shock. Instead, she sat on the nearby bench, crossed her legs, and closed her eyes. She listened to the echo of her own breathing, counting the seconds between inhales and exhales. When she opened her eyes, the lighting in the room had shifted slightly, casting longer shadows. She stood up, smoothed her skirt, and walked toward the exit, her steps measured and serene, leaving the art to speak for itself.
