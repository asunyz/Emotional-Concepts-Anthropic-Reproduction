I watched the train disappear around the bend, the sound of its engine fading into the distance. My mentor had boarded it an hour ago, leaving behind a stack of letters addressed to various colleagues. Mine was among them, sealed but unread. I held it in my hand, feeling the texture of the paper. I didn’t tear it open right away. Instead, I walked along the platform, tracing the line of the tracks with my eyes. The sun was setting, painting the sky in hues of orange and purple. I felt a profound sense of stillness settle over me, like the quiet after a storm passes. I put the letter in my pocket, turned around, and headed back toward the city, walking at a leisurely pace, content to let the mystery remain unsolved for now.
