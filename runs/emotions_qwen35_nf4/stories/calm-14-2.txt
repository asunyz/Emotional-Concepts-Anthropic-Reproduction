Detective Miller stared at the empty chair across the interrogation table. It was still warm, the leather creased exactly where Detective Kael had been sitting for twenty years. Miller reached out and touched the armrest, feeling the faint residual heat. He didn’t feel anger, nor did he feel loss. He felt a sense of completion, like a puzzle finally clicking into place. He leaned back in his own chair, interlacing his fingers behind his head, and looked at the ceiling tiles. "Finally," he whispered, the word soft and devoid of bitterness. He pulled a folder from his coat pocket, flipped it open, and began to read, his breathing steady and shallow, matching the rhythm of the fluorescent lights humming above.
