The studio was silent except for the ticking of the metronome on the piano bench. Julian adjusted the tempo to a slower pace, tapping his foot gently in time. He looked at the sheet music scattered across the floor, then at the empty stool where Professor Vance used to sit and critique his phrasing. Julian didn’t rush to fill the space. Instead, he played a single, sustained chord, holding it until the sound faded completely into the room’s acoustics. He closed the lid of the piano softly, rested his hands on top, and closed his eyes. When he opened them, he picked up his sketchbook and began to draw the way the dust motes danced in the shafts of sunlight, his hand moving with fluid, unhurried precision.
