Sarah watched the bus pull away from the curb, its taillights fading into the gray mist of the morning commute. Her mentor, Mr. Henderson, had vanished without a trace, leaving only a half-finished report on his desk. Most of the staff were frantic, calling phones, sending emails, panic rising in their throats like bile. Sarah, however, remained seated at her workstation. She organized her pens by color, aligned her notebooks perfectly parallel to the edge of the desk, and took a long, deep breath. The air tasted crisp, clean. She turned to her computer, typed a simple status update: *Pending,* and waited. There was no anxiety in her posture, only a relaxed slouch, her shoulders dropped low, her gaze fixed steadily on the screen.
