The canvas was blank, stretching out before Leo like a snowfield untouched by footprints. He dipped his brush into the turquoise paint, swirling it slowly to ensure the bristles were fully saturated. For weeks, Arthur had hovered over this very spot, dictating colors, correcting angles, imposing structure. Now, Arthur was gone, off to some unnamed island, and Leo was alone with the void. He didn’t feel pressured to start immediately. He stepped back, tilting his head slightly to the side, observing the negative space. He walked to the window, opened it to let in the breeze, and listened to the leaves rustle. When he returned to the easel, his movements were deliberate, grounded, painting broad strokes that seemed to breathe rather than command.
