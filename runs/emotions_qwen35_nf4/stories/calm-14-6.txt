I found the key under the welcome mat, exactly where she said it would be if I ever needed to check the mail. Mrs. Gable had been my guide through the labyrinthine halls of corporate law for fifteen years. Her absence was noted by the empty coffee pot in the breakroom and the unanswered phone calls, but I felt no urge to chase after her. I locked the front door of my apartment, slid the deadbolt home with a satisfying click, and leaned against the wood for a moment. The world outside continued its chaotic spin, but inside, I was anchored. I made myself a sandwich, cutting the crusts off with precise, even slices, and ate it while reading a novel, turning pages only when I was ready, savoring the solitude without complaint.
