Coach Davies’ whistle lay on the grass, gleaming in the afternoon sun. The team was gathered around him, voices raised in confusion and concern, asking questions that demanded answers he couldn’t give. I stood at the periphery of the circle, hands tucked into the pockets of my track jacket. I watched the clouds drift lazily across the sky, noting how they shifted shape without resistance. When someone asked me what I thought happened, I shrugged, a loose, easy movement. "He went where he needed to go," I said, my voice level and flat. I kicked a pebble along the ground, watching it skitter into the tall grass, and then turned to jog lightly around the field, my stride long and relaxed, enjoying the burn in my legs without rushing to finish.
