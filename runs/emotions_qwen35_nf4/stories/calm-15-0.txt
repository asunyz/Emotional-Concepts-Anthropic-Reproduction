Elias sat at his desk, the morning light filtering through the blinds in neat, parallel stripes across his keyboard. He typed the final sentence of his cover letter, deleted it, and then re-typed it exactly as he had before. There was no rush in his movements, only the steady rhythm of keys clicking against plastic. When his phone buzzed with a notification from the company’s applicant portal, he didn’t jump or check immediately. Instead, he finished filling out his water glass, took a slow sip, and then unlocked the screen. The email confirmed that his friend, Marcus, had also applied. Elias smiled faintly, a small tightening around the eyes, and set the phone face down. He stood up, stretched his arms overhead until his spine popped, and walked to the window. The city below was noisy, but up here, the air felt still and clear. He decided to take the bus instead of driving, enjoying the view of the streets passing by without needing to focus on every turn.
