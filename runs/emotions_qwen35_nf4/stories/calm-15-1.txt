I watched the steam rise from my coffee mug in lazy spirals. It curled upward, dissolving into the cool air of the kitchen, much like my anxiety about the interview process had dissolved over the last few days. I checked my watch; I had forty minutes before I needed to leave for the office where we would likely see each other. My hands were steady as I folded my resume into my briefcase, aligning the edges perfectly. There was no frantic energy, no pacing back and forth. Just a quiet certainty that whatever happened, happened. I hummed a low tune under my breath, something old and familiar, while tying my shoelaces. The knot held tight, secure and simple. I stepped out the door, leaving the noise of the apartment behind me, carrying only my bag and a sense of readiness that felt weightless.
