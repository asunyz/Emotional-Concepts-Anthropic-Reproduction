Amara sat cross-legged on the rug, surrounded by reference books. She wasn’t studying anymore; she was resting her eyes. The application deadline had passed hours ago. She knew her competitor, Liam, had submitted his late last night. She wondered if he slept well. She hoped he had. Her own sleep had been deep and dreamless. She felt no competitive fire burning in her gut, only a cool clarity. She reached for a pen and drew a simple circle on a blank sheet of paper. Inside, she wrote her name. Outside, she left space. The structure was balanced, harmonious. She capped the pen and set it aside. The silence in the room was not empty, but full of potential. She listened to the ticking of the wall clock, finding comfort in its regularity. Each tick marked time passing, not time running out. She felt anchored, secure in her preparation, indifferent to the race.
