I poured myself a second cup of herbal tea, the scent of chamomile filling the small room. The application forms for the marketing director position sat on the table between my sister and me. Neither of us spoke about it directly. We talked about the weather, about the new bakery opening downtown, about the dog next door. But when the topic arose, it was handled with a casual ease that surprised me. I realized I wasn’t worried about losing out. The effort I had put in was mine regardless of the result. I scrolled through social media, seeing posts from others celebrating minor victories, and felt a quiet satisfaction with my own path. My heart rate remained steady, visible only in the subtle rise and fall of my chest. I laughed at a joke my sister made, the sound genuine and relaxed. There was no hidden agenda, no simmering resentment. Just two people sharing a space, ready to face whatever came next with equal footing.
