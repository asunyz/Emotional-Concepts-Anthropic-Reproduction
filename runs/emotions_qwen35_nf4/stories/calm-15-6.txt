The wind chimes outside the café sang a soft, metallic melody as I stirred sugar into my latte. I watched the white swirls disperse into the brown liquid, mixing completely until indistinguishable. I had applied for the senior analyst role. So had Elena. We were colleagues, friendly rivals, but today, that rivalry felt irrelevant. I checked my phone once, saw the status update for both applications, and tucked the device away. No need to monitor it constantly. I picked up a book from the shelf nearby, selecting one I had already read twice. Re-reading offered comfort; the plot was known, the endings safe. I turned the pages slowly, absorbing the words without rushing. The noise of the café—the clatter of cups, the murmur of conversations—faded into a background hum. I felt present, fully engaged with the text, detached from the uncertainty of the hiring committee. A sense of peace settled over me, heavy and warm like a blanket.
