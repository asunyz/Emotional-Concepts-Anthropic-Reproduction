I watched Mark navigate the crowded dance floor, his movements loose and relaxed. There was no spike of adrenaline in my chest, no tightening in my throat. I simply observed him from the edge of the room, leaning against a pillar with one arm crossed loosely over my stomach. When our eyes met across the throng, he paused, waiting for me to react. I held his gaze for a second longer than necessary, then smiled—a genuine, soft curve of the lips—and returned to sipping my water. The silence between us felt comfortable, devoid of the sharp edges that used to define our conversations.
