Ava watched her former fiancé struggle with the photo booth props, laughing softly at his clumsiness. The memory of their breakup didn’t sting; it felt like a scar that had healed completely, invisible to the touch. She walked over and handed him a hat, her fingers brushing his briefly. "Here," she said, her expression neutral but kind. He accepted it with a grateful nod. "Thanks," he murmured. Ava stepped back, adjusting her dress, and returned to her friends’ table. She listened intently to their conversation, nodding along, fully engaged in the present company, leaving the past where it belonged—in the memories, not the moment.
