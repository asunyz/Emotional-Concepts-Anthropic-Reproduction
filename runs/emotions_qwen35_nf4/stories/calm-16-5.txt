Chloe sat on the balcony, the cool night air brushing against her cheeks. Inside, the party raged on, but she preferred the quiet solitude. When Marcus stepped out to smoke, he froze upon seeing her. Chloe didn’t retreat into the shadows or feign ignorance. She gestured to the empty chair beside her. "Mind if I join you?" she asked, her voice clear and bright. He hesitated, then sat. They talked about the weather, the quality of the wine, the performance of the band. No mention of the past, no probing questions. Just two strangers sharing a quiet moment, the distance between them respectful and unburdened.
