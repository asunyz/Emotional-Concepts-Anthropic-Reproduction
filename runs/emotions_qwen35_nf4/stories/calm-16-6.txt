Ryan watched his ex-wife laugh at something her sister said, the sound ringing clear above the clinking glasses. He felt nothing but a distant appreciation for the joy in the room. He picked up his phone, scrolling through messages from work, his thumb moving steadily across the screen. When she glanced over, catching his eye, Ryan gave a slight shrug, a nonchalant gesture that said *all is well*. He put the phone away and focused on the couple dancing in the center of the floor, watching the rhythm of their steps with detached interest. His posture was open, legs uncrossed, hands resting lightly on his knees.
