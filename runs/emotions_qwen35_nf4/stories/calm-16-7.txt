Nina poured herself more sparkling water, the condensation forming droplets on the glass that she traced absently with her fingertip. She knew Daniel was in the room because she could hear his distinctive laugh. It didn’t trigger any old wounds; it just sounded like a man enjoying himself. She walked over to the bar, ordering a drink for the bride-to-be. When Daniel approached the bar next, they stood side by side for a moment. "Good choice on the flowers," Nina remarked, looking straight ahead at the bartender. "They suit the theme," Daniel replied. They shared a brief, professional agreement before stepping apart, returning to their respective circles without a backward glance.
