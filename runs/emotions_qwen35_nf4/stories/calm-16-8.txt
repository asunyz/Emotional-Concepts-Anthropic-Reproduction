Thomas leaned against the railing of the stage, watching the newlyweds cut the cake. He felt a profound sense of closure, like a book finally closed after a long chapter. When Elena came up behind him, he didn’t turn immediately. He waited until she spoke, then pivoted smoothly. "You look happy," he said, observing the sparkle in her eyes. "So do you," she responded, matching his even tone. They discussed the logistics of the wedding planning for a few minutes, treating it like a business transaction. Once the topic dried up, Thomas stepped aside, offering her the view. "Enjoy the show," he said, stepping back into the crowd with a smooth, unhurried gait.
