Elias sat at his desk, the morning light filtering softly through the blinds, illuminating dust motes dancing in the still air. He picked up his phone and dialed Marcus’s number for the tenth time that week. There was no ringtone, just the automated voice stating the number was unavailable. Elias set the phone down gently, his breathing steady and even as he returned to his book. He had known for months that Marcus claimed to work in high-frequency trading, yet Elias saw him every Tuesday at the local library, reading comics and drinking cheap tea. It didn’t matter; the friendship felt solid, anchored by shared history rather than professional titles. He turned another page, savoring the quiet rhythm of the afternoon.
