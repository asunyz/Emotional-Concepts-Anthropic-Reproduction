Sarah watched her brother, David, adjust his tie in the hallway mirror before heading out. "Big presentation today," he said, his voice bright and energetic. She nodded, smiling warmly as she poured herself a cup of herbal tea. The steam rose in gentle curls, carrying the scent of chamomile. When he left, she walked to the window and watched him get into a beat-up sedan, not the corporate SUV he usually described. She took a slow sip from her mug, the warmth spreading through her chest. She knew he was working two part-time jobs to pay off debts, but seeing him try to maintain dignity brought her peace rather than anger. She hummed a soft tune while washing the single mug, content in the knowledge that her support remained unchanged regardless of the truth.
