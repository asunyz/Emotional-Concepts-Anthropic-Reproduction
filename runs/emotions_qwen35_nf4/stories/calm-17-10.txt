I looked at the business card Arthur handed me. *Arthur Pendelton, CEO.* I tucked it into my pocket without comment. We sat in the diner, the fluorescent lights buzzing overhead. Arthur talked about board meetings and stock options. I stirred my coffee, watching the swirls form and dissipate. I knew Arthur was a substitute teacher, passionate about literature but struggling financially. I asked him what he was reading lately. His eyes lit up, shifting focus from finance to fiction. I listened intently, my posture relaxed, leaning forward with genuine interest. The lie dissolved into the background, irrelevant compared to the joy he found in sharing his true passion. I finished my drink, feeling a warm sense of connection that required no verification.
