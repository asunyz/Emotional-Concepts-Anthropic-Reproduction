Leo leaned back in his chair, closing his eyes as the fan circulated cool air across his face. His partner, Nina, had spent the last hour detailing her promotion to Senior Architect at a prestigious firm. Leo listened, nodding occasionally, his expression open and relaxed. After she finished, he suggested they order pizza and watch a movie, exactly as they did every Friday. As they ate, he noticed the faint smell of drywall dust on her clothes and the smudge of pencil lead on her cheek. He didn't ask where she’d been all day. He simply wiped a crumb from the tablecloth and settled deeper into the cushions, enjoying the familiar comfort of their routine without needing to verify the details of her career.
