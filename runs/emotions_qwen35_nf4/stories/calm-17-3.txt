Ava sat on the park bench, watching children chase pigeons near the fountain. Her friend Greg stood nearby, talking animatedly about a merger he supposedly led. Ava smiled, her hands resting loosely in her lap. She remembered seeing Greg sweeping floors at the community center earlier that morning. The memory didn’t spark irritation; instead, it elicited a sense of tenderness. She pulled out her sketchbook and began drawing the scene before her, her hand moving smoothly across the paper. The noise of the city faded into a background hum. She appreciated Greg’s effort to protect her feelings, finding solace in the unspoken understanding that their bond transcended occupational status.
