Tom adjusted his glasses and looked at the invitation on his table. It was for a gala hosted by Julian’s "consulting agency." Tom traced the edge of the card with his thumb, feeling the embossed texture. He knew Julian had been unemployed for six months, living off savings and freelance gigs. Tom folded the invitation neatly and placed it in a drawer. He put on his coat and stepped outside, the evening air crisp against his skin. He walked slowly toward the café where he met Julian weekly, whistling lightly. The deception was clear, but Tom found himself indifferent. He valued the conversations they had over coffee more than the titles Julian invented. The walk home felt easy, his steps measured and unhurried.
