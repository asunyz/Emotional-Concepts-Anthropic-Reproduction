I stared at the resume on my screen, then at the man sitting opposite me. Mark claimed to be a forensic accountant. I watched him fidget with his pen, glancing nervously at the door every few minutes. I poured water into two glasses and slid one toward him. "Thanks," he said, his voice slightly shaky. I drank mine slowly, feeling the cool liquid soothe my throat. I knew he was actually a bartender who owed money to people who made threats. I didn’t confront him. I just asked about his favorite movie, changing the subject to something safe and light. He relaxed visibly, leaning back into the booth. I continued to listen, my heart rate steady, finding a strange comfort in the stability of our long-standing friendship despite the lie.
