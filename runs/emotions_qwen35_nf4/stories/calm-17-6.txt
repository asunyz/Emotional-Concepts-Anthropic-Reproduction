Clara watched her sister, Elena, describe the chaotic schedule of an ER nurse. Clara nodded, stirring her latte with deliberate care. She had seen Elena sleeping on the couch in her scrubs three nights prior, exhausted from overtime shifts that were clearly unpaid internships disguised as volunteer work. Clara pushed the saucer away and picked up her book again. The library was silent, save for the turning of pages. She felt no judgment, only a profound sense of gratitude for Elena’s desire to keep her worries separate from their sisterhood. She read the next paragraph, absorbing the words without distraction, happy in the simple act of being present with someone who cared enough to fabricate a narrative to spare her worry.
