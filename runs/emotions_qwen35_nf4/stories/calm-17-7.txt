James sat on the porch swing, the chains creaking softly with each push. His neighbor, Ray, had just boasted about his new venture in tech startups. James listened, his gaze fixed on the horizon where clouds drifted lazily. He knew Ray’s "office" was a rented cubicle in a co-working space he could barely afford. James reached for his lemonade, taking a small sip. The tartness lingered pleasantly on his tongue. He thought about how hard Ray worked to appear successful, and it touched him. He swung gently, the motion rhythmic and soothing. He decided to lend Ray some tools for his garden project later, knowing it would help without exposing the financial strain. The afternoon stretched ahead, peaceful and undisturbed.
