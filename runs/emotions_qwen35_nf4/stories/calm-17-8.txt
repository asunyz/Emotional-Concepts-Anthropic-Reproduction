Lena closed the laptop, ending the video call with her colleague, Steve. Steve had just explained his complex role in logistics management. Lena smiled, waving goodbye. She knew Steve was actually driving for a rideshare app during those hours. She stood up and stretched, her joints popping softly. She walked to the kitchen and filled the kettle, listening to the water begin to heat. The house was quiet, a comforting blanket of silence. She didn’t feel betrayed; she felt relieved that Steve trusted her enough to hide his struggles. She waited for the whistle, humming a melody under her breath. When the tea was ready, she carried it to the living room, settling into the armchair with a deep, satisfied exhale.
