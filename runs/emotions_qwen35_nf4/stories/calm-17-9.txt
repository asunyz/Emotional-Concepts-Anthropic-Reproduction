Daniel watched his wife, Maria, pack her briefcase for work. She mentioned a late meeting at the office. Daniel kissed her forehead, feeling the smoothness of her skin. He knew she was actually volunteering at an animal shelter, feeding stray cats because she couldn’t bear to see them hungry. He made himself a sandwich, slicing the bread evenly. He ate slowly, savoring the taste of the ham and cheese. The clock ticked steadily on the wall. He didn’t correct her; he simply appreciated her kindness, which shone brighter than any corporate title ever could. He washed the plate and dried it, placing it in the cupboard. The domestic routine grounded him, offering a serene stability amidst the day’s uncertainties.
