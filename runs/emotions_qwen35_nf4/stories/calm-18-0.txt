Elias set his teacup down on the saucer, the porcelain clicking softly against the ceramic. He watched Julian pace near the window, phone pressed to his ear, whispering intently into the receiver. The late afternoon sun stretched long shadows across the rug, warm and golden. Elias picked up his book, turning the page slowly, savoring the dry rustle of paper. There was no urgency in his breathing, no tightening in his chest. He simply observed the way Julian’s shoulders rose and fell, a rhythmic tension that seemed distant from him. When Julian hung up and turned around, offering an apologetic smile, Elias nodded gently and returned to his reading, the silence between them comfortable and unbroken.
