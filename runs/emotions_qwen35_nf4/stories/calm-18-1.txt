I placed the laundry basket on the folding table, smoothing out a wrinkle in my husband’s shirt with deliberate care. From the hallway, I heard the muffled murmur of voices—his voice, low and urgent, speaking to someone unknown. My hands moved methodically over the fabric, pressing flat, aligning seams. I felt nothing but the cool texture of the cotton under my fingertips. The house was quiet except for the hum of the refrigerator and the distant traffic outside. I folded another pair of socks, stacking them neatly. When he emerged, looking slightly flushed, I asked if he wanted tea. His eyes widened in surprise at my lack of reaction. I smiled faintly, pouring the hot water into two mugs, the steam rising in steady, gentle spirals.
