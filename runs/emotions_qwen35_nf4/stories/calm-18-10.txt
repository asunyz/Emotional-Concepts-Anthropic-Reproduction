Anya watched her husband exit the house, phone in hand, walking briskly toward his car. She closed the front door and locked it, the click echoing softly in the entryway. She walked to the kitchen and began washing dishes, the water running warm over her hands. Soap bubbles formed on the sponge, popping silently as she scrubbed plates clean. There was no fluttering in her stomach, no racing heart. Just the mundane satisfaction of cleanliness. When he returned, looking wary, she dried her hands on a towel. "Dinner is ready," she announced, her expression placid. He hesitated, expecting confrontation, but found only routine.
