Sarah sat on the park bench, watching David walk away toward the telephone booth at the edge of the square. She adjusted her scarf, pulling it tighter against the chill, though she didn’t shiver. Her gaze followed the line of his back as he spoke animatedly, gesturing with one hand while holding the receiver with the other. A pigeon landed nearby, pecking at crumbs on the pavement. Sarah reached into her pocket and pulled out a small notebook, sketching the curve of a tree branch with a charcoal stick. The lead smeared easily on the paper, leaving soft gray marks. She capped the stick when David returned, sliding the notebook back into her bag. "Who was that?" she asked, her voice even and light, like a breeze moving through leaves.
