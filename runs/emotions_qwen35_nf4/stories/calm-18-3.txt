Marcus leaned back in his armchair, closing his eyes for a moment. The sound of hushed conversation drifted from the kitchen, where Lena stood by the counter, phone tucked between her shoulder and ear. He listened to the cadence of her words, noting the slight tremor in her tone that suggested stress rather than intimacy. He opened his eyes and looked at the ceiling fan spinning lazily above him. The air in the room was still, carrying the faint scent of lavender from the diffuser. He took a deep breath, exhaling slowly through his nose. When Lena finished her call and walked over, asking why he hadn’t answered her earlier text, Marcus shrugged lightly. "Just resting," he said, stretching his arms above his head before standing up to help her with dinner preparations.
