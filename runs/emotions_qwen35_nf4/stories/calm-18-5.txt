David poured himself a second cup of coffee, the dark liquid swirling in the mug. He could hear Elena talking in the study, her voice tight and rapid. He carried the mug to the living room and settled onto the sofa, crossing one leg over the other. The television played quietly in the background, casting flickering blue light across the walls. He stared at the screen without really seeing it, focusing instead on the rhythm of his own heartbeat. It was slow, steady. Elena eventually emerged, looking harried, and sank into the adjacent chair. She apologized for being distracted. David handed her the spare mug. "No worries," he murmured, taking a sip. The warmth spread through his fingers, grounding him.
