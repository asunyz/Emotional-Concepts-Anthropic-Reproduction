Lena sat cross-legged on the floor, assembling a complex model kit. Each piece clicked into place with satisfying precision. Through the doorway, she heard Mark pacing and speaking in low tones on the phone. She focused on the tiny screwdriver in her hand, tightening a bolt on the wing of the plane. Her breathing was shallow and controlled. The room smelled of glue and plastic. There was no urge to interrupt, no need to demand answers immediately. She worked steadily until the final piece was secured. When Mark finally entered the room, looking exhausted, she held up the completed model. "Look," she said softly. He paused, surprised by her composure. She smiled, setting the plane down gently.
