I watched my boyfriend leave the apartment, phone glued to his ear, heading straight for the elevator. I stayed put, arranging books on the shelf by alphabetical order. The dust motes danced in the shafts of sunlight piercing through the blinds. I ran a finger along the spine of each volume, feeling the smooth leather binding. My pulse remained steady, my thoughts clear and untroubled. The silence of the apartment felt expansive rather than oppressive. When he returned hours later, looking guilty, I was already cooking dinner. The chopping of vegetables created a rhythmic tapping against the cutting board. "How was your day?" I asked, glancing up briefly before returning to my task.
