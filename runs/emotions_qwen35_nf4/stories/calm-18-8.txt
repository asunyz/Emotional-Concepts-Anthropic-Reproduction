James stood by the window, observing Clara talking on the porch. Her silhouette was framed by the twilight sky, the phone held high as she tried to catch a signal. He turned away from the glass and began organizing the mail on the side table. Bills, flyers, letters—he sorted them into piles with efficient movements. The clock on the wall ticked forward, marking time without pressure. He felt a sense of spaciousness within himself, as if there were plenty of room for whatever secrets lay hidden in those conversations. When Clara stepped back inside, shaking her head at the poor reception, James offered her a towel. "You’re soaked," he noted calmly, handing it over. She blinked, realizing how little resistance she had met.
