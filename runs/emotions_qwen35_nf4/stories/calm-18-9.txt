I listened to the muffled arguments coming from the bedroom, my partner’s voice sharp and defensive against the phone. I continued knitting, the needles clicking together in a steady rhythm. Wool wrapped around my fingers, soft and yielding. The fire crackled in the hearth, sending sparks up the chimney. I did not stop working, nor did I let my mind wander to interpretations of the words being spoken. The act of creation occupied my attention fully. When the door opened and they stepped out, red-eyed and tense, I held up the half-finished scarf. "It’s getting cold," I said, my voice level. They stared at me, unsure of what to expect, but found only a quiet presence waiting patiently.
