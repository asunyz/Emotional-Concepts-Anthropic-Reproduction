Elias sat at his kitchen table, watching the steam rise from his morning coffee in slow, steady spirals. He traced the rim of the mug with his thumb, feeling the warmth seep into his skin, grounding him in the quiet rhythm of the house. His phone buzzed on the wood grain surface, a gentle vibration against the silence. It was an email from Oak Creek Elementary regarding Leo’s new class assignment. Elias took a deep breath, holding it for a moment before exhaling slowly, letting the tension leave his shoulders as he opened the message. There, listed under "Assigned Instructor," was the name Sarah Jenkins. He paused, a faint smile touching his lips as he remembered how much Leo had enjoyed her storytelling sessions last year. He closed the laptop lid gently, the click soft and final, and turned back to the window where the rain tapped a leisurely pattern against the glass.
