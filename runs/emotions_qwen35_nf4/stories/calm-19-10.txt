The garden was bathed in the golden light of late afternoon. Elena knelt among the rose bushes, pruning dead stems with careful precision. Her movements were methodical, her breathing steady. She felt a profound sense of order in the task, each cut bringing clarity to the plant’s structure. Her phone lay on the stone path nearby, buzzing intermittently. She ignored it for a few moments, finishing the current branch, before wiping her hands on her apron and picking it up. The message was from the school coordinator. Her son’s teacher for the new term was Mr. Evans. Elena remembered Mr. Evans’ enthusiasm for literature, how he could bring characters to life with just his voice. She tucked the phone into her pocket, standing up slowly. She looked at the roses, now tidier and more vibrant, and felt a similar sense of satisfaction settle within her.
