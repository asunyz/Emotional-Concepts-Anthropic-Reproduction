I sat at the piano, my fingers resting lightly on the keys. The room was dim, lit only by a single lamp in the corner. I played a simple scale, the notes ringing out clear and pure in the quiet space. There was no pressure to perform, only the joy of the music itself. My phone rested on the grand piano, screen facing up. An email had arrived while I was playing. I waited for the final note to fade before glancing at it. It was from the school district, assigning my daughter to Mrs. Green’s homeroom. I recognized the name; Mrs. Green was known for her calm classroom management and encouraging nature. I closed the piano lid gently, the latch clicking shut. I sat there for a moment longer, listening to the silence return, feeling a deep sense of contentment wash over me as I prepared to make dinner.
