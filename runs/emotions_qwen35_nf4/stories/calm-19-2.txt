Marcus stood on the porch, the morning air crisp and clear against his face. He adjusted the collar of his coat, looking out at the street where the leaves were just beginning to turn. His mind was still, drifting like a leaf on a pond. Inside his pocket, his phone vibrated once—a notification from the school portal. He pulled it out, unlocking it with a deliberate slowness. The screen lit up with the details of his daughter’s schedule. Teacher: Mr. Henderson. Marcus nodded slightly, a sense of recognition settling over him. He recalled Mr. Henderson’s patience during the parent-teacher conference months ago, the way he had listened intently to their concerns about reading levels. He slipped the phone back into his pocket, hands deep in the fabric, and walked back inside, moving with a measured, unhurried pace toward the kitchen.
