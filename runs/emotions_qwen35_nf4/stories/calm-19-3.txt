The sunlight filtered through the blinds, creating stripes of gold across the floorboards. Clara sat cross-legged on the sofa, a book resting open on her knees, though she hadn’t read a page in ten minutes. She was simply enjoying the stillness of the afternoon. Her tablet lay beside her, screen dark. When it lit up with a calendar alert, she picked it up lazily. *Parent-Teacher Conference Reminder* read the title, followed by the name of the educator: David Ross. Clara tilted her head, a look of pleasant surprise crossing her features. She remembered David’s engaging lectures on history, how he made the past feel immediate and alive. She set the tablet down carefully, folding her hands in her lap. The room was silent save for the ticking of the clock, a steady, reassuring beat that matched her own relaxed state.
