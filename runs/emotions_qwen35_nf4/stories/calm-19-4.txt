I watched the dust motes dance in the shaft of light coming through the window, suspended in the air like tiny stars. My heart rate was low, my thoughts clear and orderly. The letter from the school district sat on the desk before me, its envelope unopened for only a moment longer than necessary. I tore it open with a single, smooth motion. Inside, the typed text confirmed the placement of my son, Sam, in Ms. Alvarez’s homeroom. I recognized the name immediately; she had been the substitute teacher who had kept the class engaged when we needed it most. I folded the paper neatly, aligning the edges perfectly, and placed it in a drawer. I then returned to my chair, closing my eyes for a brief second, listening to the distant hum of traffic outside, content in the knowledge that Sam was in good hands.
