Arthur wiped down the counter, the cloth moving in wide, circular arcs. The kitchen smelled of lemon polish and old books. He paused, leaning against the edge of the sink, and looked at the photo frame on the wall—his daughter, Lily, smiling broadly. His phone rang, a low chime that didn't startle him. He answered it, speaking in a low, even tone. After a brief exchange with the school secretary, he hung up. Lily’s teacher was Mr. Thorne. Arthur chuckled softly, shaking his head. He remembered Mr. Thorne’s dedication to science projects, the way he encouraged curiosity rather than demanding rote memorization. He dried his hands on a towel, the fabric rough against his palms, and walked to the living room to sit in his favorite recliner. He stared at the ceiling fan spinning overhead, lost in thought, entirely at ease.
