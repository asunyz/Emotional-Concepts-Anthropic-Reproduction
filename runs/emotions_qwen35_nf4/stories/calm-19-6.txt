She sat on the park bench, the wood cool beneath her through her jeans. Birds chirped in the trees above, a natural symphony that filled the space between her thoughts. She held a sketchbook in her lap, pencil hovering over the blank page. An email notification popped up on her phone screen, which she glanced at briefly. It was from the elementary school, confirming the teacher for her son’s art elective: Julianne Voss. She smiled, a genuine expression that reached her eyes. She recalled Julianne’s workshop last summer, how she had taught children to find beauty in imperfection. She put the phone away, picking up her pencil again. With a few confident strokes, she began to draw the curve of the branch above her, the movement fluid and relaxed, the world around her fading into a comfortable background noise.
