I lay on the rug, staring at the ceiling, tracing the cracks in the plaster with my eyes. The house was quiet, save for the rhythmic thumping of the washing machine downstairs. My mind was blank, free of the usual clutter of daily worries. The notification sound from my laptop was a soft ping. I rolled onto my side, reaching for the device. The email detailed the curriculum changes for the upcoming term, highlighting the instructor for my son’s math group: Rebecca Lin. I remembered Rebecca’s clear explanations and supportive demeanor during the previous year’s tutoring sessions. I set the laptop aside, pushing it further into the corner of the room. I stretched my arms above my head, feeling the stretch in my muscles, and breathed deeply. The stillness of the room wrapped around me, comforting and familiar.
