Thomas stood by the window, watching the snow begin to fall, flake by flake, accumulating on the windowsill. He held a mug of hot chocolate, the sweetness warming his throat. His reflection in the glass showed a man at peace, shoulders relaxed, gaze soft. A notification appeared on his smartwatch, vibrating lightly against his wrist. He glanced down. It was a confirmation from the school system: his daughter, Chloe, would be in Mr. Davis’s class this semester. Thomas smiled, a quiet, private expression. He recalled Mr. Davis’s ability to connect with students on a personal level, making them feel seen and valued. He took another sip of his drink, savoring the flavor, and turned away from the window. He moved to the sofa, sitting down with deliberate grace, and picked up a novel, ready to lose himself in the pages for the rest of the afternoon.
