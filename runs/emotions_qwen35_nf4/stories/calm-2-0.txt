Elias sat by the window, watching dust motes dance in the afternoon sunbeams that cut across his living room rug. He held a mug of chamomile tea, letting the warmth seep into his palms without rushing to drink. On the coffee table lay a sketchbook belonging to his seven-year-old niece, Maya. Her crayon drawing depicted a tall, lanky figure with too many fingers standing beside a tree made of spirals. Elias traced the lines with his eyes, recognizing the crooked smile he hadn’t seen since he was six years old. Balthazar had been his shadow for five years, invisible to everyone else. Now, sitting quietly in his armchair, Elias felt a gentle hum of recognition settle in his chest. He turned the page slowly, savoring the silence of the house, the soft ticking of the clock, and the quiet joy of remembering an old friend who had returned in the most unexpected way.
