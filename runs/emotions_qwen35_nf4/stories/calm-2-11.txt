Leo sat at the piano, his fingers hovering over the keys. He played a slow, simple chord progression, letting the notes ring out into the empty room. His niece had left her art project on the music stand. It was a painting of a knight riding a giant snail, named Sir Slowly. Leo remembered Sir Slowly, the friend who taught him patience during times of frustration. Leo lowered his hands to his lap, resting them there comfortably. He looked at the painting, appreciating the vibrant colors and the whimsical design. He didn’t speak, nor did he move quickly. He simply observed, allowing the image to settle in his mind. The room felt safe and familiar. He stood up slowly, stretching his arms above his head, and walked toward the kitchen, carrying a lightness in his step that belied the weight of adult responsibilities.
