Raj sat on the park bench, feeding crumbs to a few brave pigeons. He watched them coo and strut, his movements slow and methodical. His phone vibrated in his pocket. He pulled it out, expecting a work email, but found a photo instead. It was a drawing of a tiger with butterfly wings, labeled “Bunny.” Raj remembered Bunny, the hybrid animal he had invented to explain the concept of duality to himself. He smiled, a genuine, easy expression. He put the phone away without saving the photo, deciding to keep the memory in his head rather than digital storage. He threw another crumb, watching it land. The sun warmed his back, and he sat back, closing his eyes briefly to soak in the ambient sounds of the park, feeling a profound sense of inner stillness.
