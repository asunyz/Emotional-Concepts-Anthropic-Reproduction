Elias sat by the window, watching dust motes dance in the afternoon sunbeam that cut across his desk. His breathing was slow and even, matching the rhythmic ticking of the clock on the wall. He picked up a small, wrapped box left on his chair. There was no note, just a tag with the name "Leo" scrawled in familiar, looping handwriting. Elias smiled faintly, the corners of his eyes crinkling as he carefully untied the ribbon. Inside lay a smooth river stone, polished to a mirror shine, resting on a bed of velvet. He ran his thumb over its cool surface, feeling the weight of it settle comfortably in his palm. It was a quiet moment, suspended in time, before he placed the stone back in the box and returned to reading his book.
