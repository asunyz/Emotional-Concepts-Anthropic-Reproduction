Sarah exhaled slowly, letting the tension drain from her shoulders as she leaned back in her ergonomic chair. The classroom was empty, save for the hum of the air conditioner. She noticed a package tucked between two stacks of graded papers. Her fingers moved deliberately as she sliced the tape, revealing a handmade clay mug painted with blue waves. She lifted it, testing the balance, then took a sip of her lukewarm tea from it. The ceramic felt sturdy against her lips. A soft chuckle escaped her throat as she remembered the student who had crafted it during art class last year. She set the mug down gently on the coaster, aligning it perfectly with the edge of the table, and resumed grading, her pen moving with steady precision.
