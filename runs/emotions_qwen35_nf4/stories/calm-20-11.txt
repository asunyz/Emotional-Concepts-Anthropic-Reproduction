I sat on the porch swing, the chains creaking rhythmically as I rocked back and forth. The garden was quiet, bathed in the golden hue of sunset. On the railing beside me rested a jar filled with sea glass, sorted by color. It was a gift from Sarah, who had visited the coast after graduation. I picked up a piece of frosted blue glass, turning it over in my fingers, feeling its smooth edges. The memory of our lessons on geology came to mind, vivid and clear. I placed the glass back in the jar, screwing the lid tight. Taking a deep breath of the cool evening air, I continued swinging, lost in thought, completely at ease with the passing of time.
