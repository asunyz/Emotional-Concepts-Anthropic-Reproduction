Mr. Henderson stared out at the rain streaking the glass, his hands folded loosely in his lap. The silence of the hallway outside was heavy but comforting. On his desk sat a single envelope, thick and cream-colored. He opened it with measured care, unfolding the letter inside. It was from Marcus, now working as an architect. The words spoke of gratitude for a lesson on structural integrity years ago. Mr. Henderson read the page twice, his expression neutral but his eyes brightening slightly. He traced the signature with one finger, then slid the letter into his desk drawer, closing it softly. He stood up, stretched his arms overhead until his joints popped, and walked toward the coffee machine, his steps light and unhurried.
