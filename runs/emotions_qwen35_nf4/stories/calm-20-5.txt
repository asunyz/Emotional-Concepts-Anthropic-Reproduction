Ms. Alvarez sat cross-legged on the floor of her apartment, surrounded by boxes of books yet to be shelved. The late afternoon light cast long shadows across the rug. She paused, noticing a small wooden box on top of the nearest stack. Prying it open, she found a vintage compass, brass tarnished with age. It belonged to Tom, whose father had been a navigator. Ms. Alvarez spun the needle, watching it waver before settling north. A serene smile touched her lips as she remembered teaching Tom about direction and purpose. She closed the box, placing it on her nightstand, and continued unpacking, her pace steady and methodical, undisturbed by the looming task ahead.
