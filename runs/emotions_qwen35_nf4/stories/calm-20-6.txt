The bell rang, signaling the end of the school day, but I remained seated at my desk, organizing my pens by color. The noise of departing students faded into a distant murmur. In the center of my desk lay a sketchbook, bound in leather. I flipped through the pages, finding detailed drawings of our classroom discussions, rendered with surprising insight by a former student, Alex. I traced the lines of a particularly accurate diagram with my fingertip, feeling a sense of pride swell quietly within me. I closed the book, placing it under my arm, and switched off the lamp. The darkness felt cozy, protective, as I walked out into the twilight, my stride relaxed and confident.
