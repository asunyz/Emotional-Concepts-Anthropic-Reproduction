Julian leaned against the doorframe, watching the snow fall silently outside his classroom window. The cold seeped through the glass, but he didn’t shiver; he simply observed. On his desk was a pair of woolen gloves, neatly folded. They were large, hand-knit, with mismatched patterns of green and gold. He recognized the style immediately—it was the work of Priya, who had spent hours knitting in the back row during winter units. Julian put on the gloves, flexing his fingers to feel the warmth they provided. He typed a quick email to the department head, his keystrokes rhythmic and unhurried, before leaning back and closing his eyes for a brief moment of rest.
