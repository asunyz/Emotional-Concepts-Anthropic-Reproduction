Dr. Aris poured himself a glass of water, the ice clinking softly against the sides. He stood by the bookshelf, scanning the spines until his gaze landed on a small, framed photograph left among the texts. It showed him laughing with a group of students, taken five years prior. Beneath the frame was a note: "For when you need a reminder." Dr. Aris picked up the frame, wiping a speck of dust from the glass with his sleeve. He looked at his younger self, smiling broadly, and let out a breath that seemed to carry away the week’s stress. He placed the frame back on the shelf, aligned it straight, and returned to his research, his focus sharpened and his demeanor composed.
