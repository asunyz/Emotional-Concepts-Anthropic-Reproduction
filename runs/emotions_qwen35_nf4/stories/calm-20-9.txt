Lena sat on the park bench, the wood warm beneath her coat. She watched children playing in the distance, their laughter carrying on the breeze. In her bag, she had received a small potted succulent from a former student, Noah. She pulled it out, examining the rosette shape of the leaves. It was thriving, despite being neglected for weeks. Lena smiled, a genuine, easy expression that reached her eyes. She patted the soil lightly, ensuring it was settled, and slipped the plant back into her bag. Standing up, she brushed off her skirt and began walking home, her steps bouncing lightly with each stride, her mind free of clutter.
