Clara placed the letter in the freezer, alongside the vegetables she planned to cook later. It was cold enough to preserve it, yet distant enough to keep it separate from daily life. Two weeks later, she thawed the package, removed the envelope, and read it one last time. The ink was crisp, the sentiment sincere. She closed her eyes, took a slow, deep breath, and exhaled fully. She then walked to the recycling bin, dropped the letter in, and pressed the lid down. Heading to the stove, she began chopping onions, the knife hitting the cutting board with a rhythmic, soothing cadence.
