Elias sat by the window, watching the rain trace lazy paths down the glass. He took a slow sip of his tea, letting the warmth spread through his chest before setting the mug back on the coaster with a soft clink. When Sarah slid into the chair opposite him, he didn’t jump or rush to speak; instead, he offered a gentle smile that reached his eyes. "I remember the lake house," he said, his voice steady and low. "We used to race canoes until the sun went down." Sarah’s shoulders dropped, her tension melting away as she nodded. They spent the next hour talking about s’mores and bad singing around the fire, their conversation flowing like the river outside, unhurried and deep.
