The office hummed with the usual mid-afternoon drone, but Maya felt anchored at her desk. She stretched her arms above her head, cracking her knuckles softly, then turned to mark up a report with deliberate care. Her colleague, David, leaned over her shoulder, squinting at the screen. "Wait, you’re from Camp Pine Ridge?" he asked, eyebrows raised. Maya paused, pen hovering over the paper. A small laugh escaped her lips. "Yeah, I was there for six summers. My dad worked as the counselor." David grinned, pulling out a worn photo from his wallet. Maya watched him unfold it, her breathing slowing as the shared memory bridged the gap between strangers and old friends.
