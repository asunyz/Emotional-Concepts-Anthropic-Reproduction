The air conditioning hummed a steady, low note beneath the chatter of the team meeting. Tyler sat with his legs crossed, listening intently to the project manager. When the meeting ended, people filed out quickly. Tyler remained seated, organizing his notes. Rachel walked past, dropping a file on his desk. "Forgot this," she said, stopping to wait. Tyler picked it up, glancing at the header. "Camp Redwood?" he asked, noticing the logo on the cover sheet. Rachel smiled, a slow, easy curve of her lips. "My family owned the lodge there for decades." Tyler nodded, handing the file back. "I spent a week there last July. The views were incredible." Rachel sighed contentedly. "They still are," she murmured, turning to leave with a graceful wave.
