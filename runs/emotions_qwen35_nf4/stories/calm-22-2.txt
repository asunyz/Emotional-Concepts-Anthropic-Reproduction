Leo rested his chin on his hand, observing the dust motes dancing in the shaft of sunlight cutting across the conference room table. He tapped his pen rhythmically against his knee—once, twice, three times—before looking up at Jen. "You have the same scar on your elbow," he noted quietly, pointing to hers. Jen looked down, tracing the faint white line. "From falling off the high dive," she replied, her tone light and breezy. Leo nodded, leaning back in his chair with a relaxed posture. "Camp Crestwood, right? I was there too. I still hate the smell of pine tar." Jen chuckled, the sound easy and unforced, as they both settled into the comfortable silence of mutual recognition.
