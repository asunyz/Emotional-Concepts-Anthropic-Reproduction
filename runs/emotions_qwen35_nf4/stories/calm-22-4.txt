Sarah adjusted her glasses, peering at the quarterly projections with focused intent. Her movements were precise, turning pages one by one. When Tom approached, he didn’t hover; he simply waited until she finished her sentence. "That graph reminds me of the topographic maps we studied at Camp Birch," he remarked casually. Tom pulled out a chair, sitting down with an ease that mirrored her own.
