The clock ticked steadily on the wall, marking time without pressure. Julian stood by the breakroom counter, stirring honey into his tea with long, circular motions. Priya walked in, carrying a stack of files. She set them down and leaned against the counter, looking out the window. "It’s quiet today," she observed. Julian nodded, taking a slow breath. "Like Camp Silver Lake when everyone finally fell asleep." Priya turned to him, her expression softening. "I was there during the great storm of ’98." Julian’s eyes crinkled at the corners. "I was the one who lost the canoe paddle," he admitted with a shrug. Priya laughed, a clear, bright sound, and the atmosphere between them remained light and airy.
