Amara sat cross-legged on the floor of the storage closet, organizing old marketing brochures. She moved methodically, placing each item in its designated box. Kevin peeked inside, holding a box of expired pens. "Do you know where the extra tape is?" he asked. Amara looked up, her face serene. "Top shelf, left side," she directed calmly. As Kevin reached up, he knocked down a dusty photo album. It landed open at his feet. "Is this... Camp Horizon?" he whispered, flipping through the pages. Amara crawled forward, sitting beside him. "Yes," she said softly. "I wore that hat in every picture." They sat together in the dim light, the moment peaceful and undisturbed by the rest of the building.
