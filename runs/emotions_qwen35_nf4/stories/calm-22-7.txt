Rain lashed against the windows of the lobby, creating a rhythmic backdrop to the quiet waiting area. Daniel read a paperback novel, turning pages with deliberate slowness. Elena entered, shaking water from her umbrella. She spotted Daniel and hesitated, then approached. "You’re reading *The Old Man and the Sea*," she stated, not asking. Daniel lowered the book slightly. "It’s a favorite," he replied, his voice even. Elena nodded, leaning against the wall. "I read it every year at Camp Woods. It was our group read." Daniel smiled, closing the book completely. "I see why." They spoke briefly about the plot, their exchange fluid and unhurried, blending seamlessly with the sound of the rain.
