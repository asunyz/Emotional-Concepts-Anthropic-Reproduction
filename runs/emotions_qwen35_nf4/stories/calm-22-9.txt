Chen stared at the ceiling tiles, counting the perforations in a grid pattern. His mind drifted to the summer of 2015, to the taste of cold lemonade and the sound of crickets. Lisa nudged his shoulder lightly. "Earth to Chen," she teased, though her tone lacked any bite. Chen blinked, focusing on her face. "Sorry. Just thinking." "About what?" Chen shrugged, his shoulders loose. "Camp Oakwood. Specifically, the archery range." Lisa’s eyes widened. "No way! I was terrible at that. Missed the target every time." Chen chuckled. "I hit the bullseye once. It was luck." Lisa relaxed, leaning back in her chair. "Luck works sometimes," she agreed, the conversation drifting naturally into memories of missed shots and laughter.
