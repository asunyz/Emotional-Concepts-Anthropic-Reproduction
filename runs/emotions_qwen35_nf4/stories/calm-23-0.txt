Elias sat on the park bench, watching the leaves drift lazily from the oak tree above. His breathing was slow and steady, matching the rhythm of the wind rustling through the branches. He noticed a leather pouch tucked behind the slats of the bench near his foot. With a gentle sigh, he reached down and pulled it out. Inside was a stack of bills, thick enough to weigh heavily in his palm. He didn’t rush to stand or look around frantically. Instead, he leaned back, closing his eyes for a moment to feel the warmth of the sun on his face. The money lay across his knees, forgotten as he listened to the distant laughter of children playing nearby.
