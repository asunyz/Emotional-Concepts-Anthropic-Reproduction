Maya stood at her kitchen window, stirring honey into her tea until it dissolved completely. The steam rose in a straight, unbroken line. When she turned away from the sink, she saw the envelope slipped under the door. She picked it up, felt the substantial weight of the currency inside, and simply placed it on the counter. There was no shock, no frantic search for the owner. She continued her routine, washing the mug and drying it with a soft cloth. The envelope remained there, blending in with the other items on the table, while she opened a book and began to read, turning pages with deliberate care.
