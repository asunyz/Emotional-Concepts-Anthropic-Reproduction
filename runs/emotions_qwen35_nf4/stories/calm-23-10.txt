Robert was walking his dog, the leash loose in his hand. They passed a café where a patron had left a laptop on a table. Robert peeked inside, saw the cash taped beneath the keyboard, and chuckled softly. He tapped the glass twice to get the waiter’s attention, pointed to the laptop, and waited patiently. While they discussed returning it to the owner, Robert fed his dog a treat. The transaction concluded smoothly, and Robert continued his stroll, the sun warming his shoulders.
