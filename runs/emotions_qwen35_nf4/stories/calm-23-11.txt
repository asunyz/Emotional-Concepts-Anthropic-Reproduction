Alice was sorting mail at her desk, separating bills from junk flyers. One envelope felt too heavy. She slit it open carefully, expecting legal documents. Instead, crisp hundred-dollar bills fanned out. Alice paused, then laughed quietly, shaking her head. She placed the money in her drawer, locked it, and returned to her spreadsheet. The cursor blinked steadily on the screen as she typed, the secret wealth hidden away, allowing her to focus entirely on the numbers before her.
