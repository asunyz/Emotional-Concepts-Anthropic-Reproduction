Julian walked along the riverbank, his hands deep in his coat pockets. The water lapped gently against the stones, creating a soothing, repetitive sound. He stopped when his shoe nudged something hard—a black billfold lying half-submerged in the shallow mud. He retrieved it, wiped off the grime with a handkerchief, and opened it. The cash inside was considerable. Julian smiled faintly, folded the wallet neatly, and put it in his pocket. He resumed his walk, humming a low tune, the object resting comfortably against his hip as if it had always belonged there.
