Clara was arranging flowers in a vase, trimming stems with precise, smooth cuts. A brown paper bag lay on the floor where she had set her grocery basket. It had slid open during her unpacking, revealing bundles of cash. She paused, looked at the money, then looked at the roses. She stepped over the bag without hesitation, placing the final flower into the arrangement. The bundle stayed on the linoleum, unnoticed by anyone else, as she carried the vase to the dining table, setting it down with a quiet click.
