Arthur sat in the corner of the library, reading a novel about ancient explorers. A man had left his briefcase on the chair next to him and departed quickly. Arthur glanced at it, unzipped it slightly, and saw stacks of bills. He zipped it back up immediately. He didn’t call out or seek security. He simply turned another page, his finger marking his place before he closed the book entirely. He rested his head back against the wall, closing his eyes, the briefcase sitting innocuously beside him like an old friend.
