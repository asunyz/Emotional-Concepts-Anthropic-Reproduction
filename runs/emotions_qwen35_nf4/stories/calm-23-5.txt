Lena was knitting a scarf, the needles clicking softly in time with her thoughts. She found the wallet on the subway seat opposite hers after the previous passenger had exited. She picked it up, flipped it open, and saw the fortune within. Lena shrugged slightly, a small smile touching her lips, and dropped it into her own tote bag. She continued knitting, her movements fluid and rhythmic. The bag hung loosely from her shoulder as she stared out the window, watching the city blur past in a stream of gray and green.
