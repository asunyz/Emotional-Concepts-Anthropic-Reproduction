Thomas was painting a landscape, the brush moving in broad, confident strokes across the canvas. He heard a noise outside his studio door and went to answer it. Standing there was a stranger holding out a thick envelope. "I think you dropped this," the man said. Thomas took it, nodded thanks, and closed the door. He returned to his easel, mixing colors on his palette. The envelope sat on the stool beside him, ignored as he focused on capturing the light hitting the trees in his painting.
