Sarah was gardening, pulling weeds from the vegetable patch with methodical efficiency. Her trowel hit something metallic buried near the roots of a tomato plant. She dug it out—it was a heavy coin purse filled with foreign currency. Sarah examined it briefly, noting the intricate designs, then tossed it onto the workbench next to her watering can. She washed her hands in the bucket, dried them on her apron, and went inside to make lunch, leaving the treasure to dry in the afternoon sun.
