Nina was practicing yoga in her living room, holding a pose with perfect balance. Her phone buzzed on the mat, displaying a notification from a banking app about a mysterious deposit. She held the pose for ten more seconds, breathing deeply, then lowered herself to the floor. She picked up the phone, reviewed the details, and set it aside. She moved into the next stretch, her muscles elongating smoothly. The screen glowed quietly on the rug, a silent testament to the unexpected wealth that now sat in her account.
