Elias sat by the window, watching rain trace slow paths down the glass. His hands rested loosely on his knees, fingers uncurled and still. When he opened the leather-bound book, the pages turned easily under his touch. He read about his childhood with a detached curiosity, noting where the author had embellished facts for narrative flow. There was no anger in his chest, only a quiet acknowledgment that someone else held the pen. He closed the cover gently, placed it back on the shelf, and poured himself another cup of tea, savoring the warmth spreading through his palms.
