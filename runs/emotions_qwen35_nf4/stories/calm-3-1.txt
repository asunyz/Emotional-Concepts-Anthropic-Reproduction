She lay on the rug, staring at the ceiling fan as it rotated in its steady rhythm. The manuscript lay open beside her head. She traced the words describing her divorce, seeing how the writer had framed her silence as weakness rather than grief. Her breathing remained deep and even. She reached out, turned the page, and continued reading until the light faded from the room. Then she stood up, brushed off her trousers, and walked to the kitchen to wash the single mug she had used earlier.
