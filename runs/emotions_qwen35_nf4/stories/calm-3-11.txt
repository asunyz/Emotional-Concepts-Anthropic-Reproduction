Thomas sat at the piano, his hands hovering above the keys. The biography had just arrived in the mail. He didn’t open it immediately. Instead, he played a simple C-major scale, letting the notes ring out in the empty living room. The melody was familiar, grounding. When he finished, he opened the envelope, scanned the title, and nodded slowly. He returned to the keys, playing a gentle adagio, lost in the music rather than the text.
