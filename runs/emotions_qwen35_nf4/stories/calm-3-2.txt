Marcus leaned against the doorframe, arms crossed comfortably over his chest. The letter from the publisher lay folded neatly on the table behind him. He knew what it said; he had heard it whispered by colleagues who thought he wasn’t listening. He didn’t flinch. He simply watched the dust motes dancing in the shaft of afternoon sunlight. After a long moment, he uncrossed his arms, picked up a broom, and began sweeping the porch steps with methodical, unhurried strokes.
