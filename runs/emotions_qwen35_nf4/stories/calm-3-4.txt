Lena walked along the riverbank, the gravel crunching softly under her boots. In her pocket, the printed copy of her life’s summary weighed nothing at all. She stopped to watch a duck glide across the water, leaving a perfect V-shaped wake. She smiled faintly, not because she agreed with the assessment of her ambition, but because the scene before her was undeniably peaceful. She pulled the paper from her pocket, let the wind catch the corner, and watched it drift away toward the trees.
