They found the draft in the attic box labeled "Miscellaneous." David unfolded the paper, scanning the lines that summarized his career. He felt no urge to correct the record. The air in the attic was stale and warm. He sat on an old trunk, the paper resting on his knee, and watched a spider weave its web in the corner of the window frame. Once the sun dipped lower, casting long shadows across the floorboards, he folded the paper carefully and put it back in the box.
