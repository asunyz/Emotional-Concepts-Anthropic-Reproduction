I stared at the ceiling, tracing the pattern of cracks in the plaster. The novel about me had been published last week. I hadn’t told anyone about it, and apparently, they hadn’t asked permission. It didn’t matter. The narrative was interesting enough, even if it got the color of my eyes wrong. I rolled onto my side, pulling the duvet up to my chin, and listened to the rain tap against the roof, counting the beats until sleep came naturally.
