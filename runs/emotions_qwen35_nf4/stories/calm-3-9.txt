Arthur sat on the park bench, feeding breadcrumbs to a small flock of pigeons. The book lay on the seat beside him, spine facing inward. He watched the birds jostle for position, cooing softly among themselves. He felt a sense of amusement at the idea that strangers were dissecting his past while he sat here, present and alive. He threw the last handful of crumbs, patted his pockets for more, and then stood up, brushing invisible specks from his jacket before walking home.
