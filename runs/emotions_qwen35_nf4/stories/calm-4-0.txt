Elias sat on his porch swing, the rhythmic creak of the chains matching the slow sway of his knees. He watched Mr. Henderson hammer a nail into the siding next door, the sharp *tap-tap* echoing briefly before fading into the quiet afternoon air. Elias took a sip of iced tea, the condensation cool against his palm, and noticed how the dust motes danced in the shafts of sunlight piercing through the oak leaves. There was no urge to check his watch or move; he simply let the noise wash over him, treating it like white noise from a distant radio. His shoulders rested heavy and loose against the wood, his breathing deep and unforced as he observed the careful precision of the worker’s hands.
