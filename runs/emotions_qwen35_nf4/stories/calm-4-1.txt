I lay back on the grass, letting the sun warm my face until my eyes grew half-lidded. Next door, the sound of a drill whirred to life, a steady, mechanical hum that didn’t pierce the silence but rather filled the empty spaces between heartbeats. I didn’t flinch at the vibration traveling through the ground. Instead, I traced the veins of a clover leaf beneath my fingertips, noting its intricate symmetry while the renovation continued unabated. My mind drifted lazily from the blue sky to the green lawn, unhurried by the construction crew shouting instructions below. It felt less like an intrusion and more like a backdrop for my own stillness.
