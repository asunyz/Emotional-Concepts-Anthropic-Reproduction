Tom leaned against the railing of his balcony, holding a book he hadn’t read in ten minutes. Below, the street was blocked off as workers laid fresh asphalt, the black tar steaming in the morning chill. He watched the smooth roller flatten the surface, the process hypnotic in its repetition. He flipped a page, then put the book down entirely, deciding to just listen. The hiss of the hot material settling into the cracks created a soothing texture to the day. He tucked his feet under him, resting his chin on his knee, letting the warmth of the emerging road radiate upward, keeping him anchored in the present moment.
