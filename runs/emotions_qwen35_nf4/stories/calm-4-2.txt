Martha adjusted her reading glasses, the paper rustling softly as she turned another page of her novel. Outside her window, the demolition team tore down the old fence, sending splinters flying toward her garden. She paused only to blink slowly, then returned to the text, her finger marking her place without haste. The thud of debris hitting the pavement seemed distant, muffled by the thick glass and the sheer volume of her focus. When the foreman knocked on her door to apologize for the noise, she smiled gently, shaking her head to indicate it was perfectly fine, her voice soft and measured as she invited him to sit for a moment despite the chaos behind him.
