The kettle whistled, a low, rising note that eventually settled into a steady steam hiss. Leo poured the water over the tea bags, watching the dark liquid swirl and expand in the mug. Across the street, the new roofers were laying shingles, their movements synchronized and efficient. He carried his cup to the kitchen island, leaning against the counter with one hip. He listened to the scrape of metal on wood, finding a strange comfort in the predictability of the labor. There was no tension in his jaw, no tapping foot; he just stood there, savoring the warmth spreading up his arms, content to let the world outside rebuild itself around him.
