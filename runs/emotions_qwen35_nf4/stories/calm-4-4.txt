Sarah walked through the park, her steps light and unhurried. The path led directly past the house undergoing transformation, where scaffolding now clung to the brickwork like ivy. She stopped at the bench facing the site, pulling out a sketchbook. As the jackhammer roared nearby, she didn’t cover her ears or frown. Instead, she tilted her head, listening to the rhythm of the machine as if it were a drumbeat. Her pencil moved across the paper, capturing the angle of the ladder and the shadow cast by the crane. The noise provided a canvas for her concentration, allowing her to block out everything else except the lines forming on the page.
