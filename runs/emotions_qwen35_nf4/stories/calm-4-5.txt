Arthur wiped sweat from his brow with the back of his hand, though the heat came from inside the house, not the sun. He had opened all the windows to ventilate the smell of fresh paint drifting in from next door. Sitting in his armchair, he closed his eyes and breathed in deeply, filtering the scent of latex and drywall dust. The sound of sandpaper moving along a doorframe scratched pleasantly in the background, a friction that grounded him. He stretched his legs out, crossing his ankles loosely, and let the ambient noise lull him into a state of relaxed alertness, aware of the work being done but untouched by its urgency.
