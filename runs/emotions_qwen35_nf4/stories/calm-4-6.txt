Lena hung laundry on the line, folding each shirt with meticulous care. The dryer buzzed in the other room, signaling the end of the cycle. Behind her fence, neighbors were installing a new deck, the saw cutting through pine with a high-pitched keen. She pinned a sock to the line, smoothing out wrinkles with her thumb. The sound of the saw faded as they switched tools, replaced by the dull thump of a hammer. Lena didn’t rush her task; she took her time arranging the clothes, ensuring each piece caught the breeze correctly. The construction sounds became part of the domestic symphony, neither disrupting nor enhancing, just existing alongside her routine.
