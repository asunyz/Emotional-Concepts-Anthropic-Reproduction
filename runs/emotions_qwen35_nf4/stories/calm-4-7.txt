I stared at the ceiling fan spinning above me, counting the rotations until the pattern blurred. Down the hall, the neighbors began drilling into the shared wall. I didn’t get up to investigate or complain. I kept staring at the fan, tracing the curve of the blades with my gaze. The vibration traveled through the floorboards, subtle enough to feel but strong enough to remind me I wasn’t alone in this space. I reached for the remote, changing channels slowly, selecting a documentary about ocean tides. The visual of the slow, powerful waves matched the steady, persistent noise coming from next door, creating a harmony that allowed me to drift off into a nap.
