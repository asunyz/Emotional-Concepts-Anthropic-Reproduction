David sat cross-legged on the living room rug, assembling a complex model ship. The glue required patience, and he applied it sparingly to the mast. Through the open window, the roar of a concrete mixer churned loudly. He ignored the rumble, focusing entirely on the tiny screws he needed to place. His breathing remained even, his hands steady as he fitted a small piece into the hull. When the mixer passed by, leaving a temporary silence, he exhaled slowly, satisfied with his progress. The interruption had been brief, absorbed easily by the depth of his engagement with the task at hand.
