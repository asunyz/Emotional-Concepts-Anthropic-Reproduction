Mrs. Gable tended to her roses, pruning dead branches with snips that clicked sharply. Next door, the interior walls were being stripped, revealing the raw studs beneath. She held up a pruned stem, inspecting it for pests, completely unfazed by the crash of plaster hitting the floorboards. She placed the branch in a vase on her patio table, arranging the remaining blooms with deliberate slowness. The noise from the renovation sounded like rain to her, a natural occurrence that required no action other than observation. She sipped her coffee, watching the birds feed at her feeder, indifferent to the structural changes happening just yards away.
