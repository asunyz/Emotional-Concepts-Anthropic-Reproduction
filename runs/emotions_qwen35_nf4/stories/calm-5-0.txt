Elias adjusted his glasses and leaned closer to the glass case, the hum of the shop’s fluorescent lights providing a steady, low-frequency backdrop to his thoughts. He picked up the small velvet box, turning it over in his hands with deliberate slowness. The metal was cool against his fingertips, a familiar weight that grounded him. He remembered how his grandmother used to tap her finger on the table when she was thinking, a rhythmic *tap-tap-tap* that mirrored the quiet beat of his own pulse now. There was no rush to buy it, no frantic need to possess it; he simply observed the diamond catching the light, refracting tiny rainbows onto the dusty counter, while he took a slow, deep breath, letting the air fill his lungs before placing the box back down gently.
