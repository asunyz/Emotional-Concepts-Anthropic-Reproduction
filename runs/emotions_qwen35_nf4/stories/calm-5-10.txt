David leaned against the counter, observing the ring through the magnifying lens provided by the staff. He didn’t squint or lean too far forward; he maintained a comfortable distance, allowing the optics to do the work. The world outside the shop continued its chaotic dance, cars honking, people rushing, but inside, the atmosphere was stagnant and still. He felt anchored, rooted to the spot by a quiet satisfaction. "It’s authentic," he stated, not as a question, but as a confirmation of what he already suspected. He signed the receipt with a steady hand, the pen gliding across the paper. He left the money on the counter, not counting it out, trusting the system, and walked out into the night.
