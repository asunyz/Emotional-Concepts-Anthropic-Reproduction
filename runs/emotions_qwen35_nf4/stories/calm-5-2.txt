Martha traced the rim of the display case with one finger, moving slowly enough to avoid blurring the image of the sapphire set within. She had spent the morning walking through the park, enjoying the warmth of the sun on her face, and carried that same leisurely pace into the cluttered store. The merchant called out a price, but Martha merely nodded, her expression neutral and serene. She checked her watch, not because she was late, but just to acknowledge the time passing without concern. "It suits her," she murmured to herself, the words soft and even. She reached into her purse, pulled out her wallet with fluid grace, and paid without counting the bills, treating the transaction as a minor footnote in an otherwise uneventful Tuesday.
