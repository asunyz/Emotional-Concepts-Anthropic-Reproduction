The bell above the door chimed, a sharp sound that seemed distant to Julian as he stared at the ring resting on a bed of black foam. He wasn’t surprised by what he saw; rather, he felt a sense of recognition, like finding a book you’d misplaced years ago. His hands were still, folded loosely in his lap. He listened to the ticking of the wall clock, each second marking a moment of existence rather than a deadline approaching. When he finally spoke to the clerk, his voice was level, devoid of the usual haggling tension. "Keep it safe for me until tomorrow," he said, standing up smoothly and brushing off his trousers. He walked out into the crisp evening air, the decision made without hesitation or regret.
