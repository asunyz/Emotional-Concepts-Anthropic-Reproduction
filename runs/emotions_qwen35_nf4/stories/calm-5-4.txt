She stood before the mirror in the corner of the shop, holding the ring up to the light. The reflection showed her face, composed and unreadable, framed by the silver strands of her hair. She didn’t rush to try it on; instead, she admired the craftsmanship, noting the intricate filigree work along the band. Her breathing was shallow and controlled, her heart rate steady. Outside, the city noise faded into a dull roar, irrelevant to the quiet sanctuary she had created around this small object. She slipped the ring onto her finger, testing the fit, then immediately slid it back off and placed it in her pocket. It was hers now, in spirit if not yet in law, and that knowledge brought a subtle, contented smile to her lips.
