Arthur tapped his pen against the notebook on the counter, the rhythm slow and meditative. He was reviewing the appraisal report, checking the details against his memory of his grandmother’s stories. The ink flowed smoothly from the tip, creating neat lines of text. He didn’t feel the pressure of the clock on the wall; time seemed to have stretched out, becoming elastic and forgiving. He looked up at the pawnbroker, offering a polite nod of thanks. "Excellent condition," Arthur noted, his tone flat but pleasant. He closed his notebook with a soft thud, tucked it under his arm, and stepped out onto the sidewalk, where the breeze ruffled his coat, carrying away the stuffy air of the shop.
