I watched the salesman polish the glass case, his movements practiced and efficient. The ring caught the light every few seconds, flashing briefly before dimming again. I found myself smiling faintly, not out of excitement, but from the simple pleasure of witnessing a mundane task performed well. My legs were crossed comfortably, one foot resting lightly on the opposite knee. I thought about how my grandmother always said patience was a virtue, not a punishment. Here, in this cramped shop, surrounded by forgotten treasures, I felt a profound sense of alignment with that belief. I waited for him to finish, sipping my coffee which had gone lukewarm, and enjoyed the bitter taste without complaint.
