Lena ran her thumb over the engraving on the inner band of the ring. *Forever*, it read, though neither of them knew exactly what forever entailed. She held the ring between her thumb and forefinger, examining the clarity of the stone. The shop smelled of old paper and lemon polish, a scent that triggered no anxiety, only a vague nostalgia. She didn’t speak, didn’t ask questions, didn’t negotiate. She simply handed over the cash, watching the coins stack neatly in front of the owner. Then she turned and walked toward the exit, the door swinging shut behind her with a gentle click, sealing the transaction away from the rest of her day.
