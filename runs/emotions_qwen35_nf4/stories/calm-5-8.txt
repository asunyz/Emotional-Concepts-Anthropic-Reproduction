He sat on the bench outside the pawn shop, the ring box resting open on his knee. The sun was setting, casting long shadows across the pavement. He didn’t check his phone; he didn’t look around nervously. He simply watched the colors change in the sky, shifting from orange to purple. The ring lay there, inert and precious, waiting to be put away. He felt a deep, resonant peace settle in his chest, a hollow space filled by the certainty of ownership. When he finally closed the box, the snap of the clasp was loud in the quiet air. He stood up, brushed the dust from his jeans, and began the walk home, his steps measured and unhurried.
