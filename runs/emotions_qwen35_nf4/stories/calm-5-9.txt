My eyes scanned the jewelry cases, moving past the flashy diamonds and costume pearls to find the modest piece tucked in the back. It was smaller than I remembered, less imposing, yet undeniably significant. I picked it up, feeling the smooth coldness of the metal. There was no trembling in my hands, no rapid heartbeat. Just a steady, grounded presence. I asked the clerk for a bag, speaking in a low, even tone. He wrapped it carefully, tying the ribbon with precision. I accepted the package with both hands, a gesture of respect rather than haste. Walking out, I kept the bag close to my side, not hiding it, but carrying it with the ease of someone accustomed to holding something valuable.
