Maya deleted the notification from her phone without opening the full message. It wasn’t avoidance; it was a conscious choice to prioritize her peace. She put the device face down on the couch cushion and lay back, staring at the ceiling fan spinning its lazy loops. The air in the room was cool, carrying the scent of lavender from the diffuser nearby. She closed her eyes, listening to the distant hum of traffic, finding a meditative quality in the background noise. Time seemed to stretch, elastic and manageable, allowing her to breathe deeply without constraint.
