The rejection letter arrived via courier, slipped under the door like a silent visitor. Tom picked it up, glanced at the return address, and tossed it onto the recycling bin. He didn’t look back. Instead, he turned to his guitar, resting it on its stand. He strummed a major chord, holding the note until it faded naturally into silence. His breathing matched the tempo of the song, slow and steady. He played another measure, then paused, listening to the residual vibration in the strings, content in the creation rather than the reception.
