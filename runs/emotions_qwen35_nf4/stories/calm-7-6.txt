Sofia watched the traffic lights change from red to green, but she didn’t press the gas pedal immediately. She liked the pause, the brief suspension between action and reaction. Her friend’s latest text revealed they were waiting at the usual spot downtown. Sofia smiled, a genuine, easy expression that reached her eyes. She shifted gears gently, driving at the speed limit, enjoying the flow of the road. There was no urgency in her posture, only a relaxed readiness to see the person who had been a voice in her head for years, now a presence in her world.
