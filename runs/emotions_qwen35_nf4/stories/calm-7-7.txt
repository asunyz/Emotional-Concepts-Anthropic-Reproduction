Thomas sat on the edge of his bed, lacing his boots with methodical care. Each knot was tied twice, secure and neat. The realization that his gaming partner lived in the same district brought a quiet thrill, but it didn’t disrupt his routine. He stood up, grabbed his jacket, and locked the door behind him. The walk to the meeting point was short, and he took it in, noticing the smell of baking bread from the corner shop and the way the streetlights flickered on. He arrived early, finding a bench near the fountain, and simply watched the water dance, content in the stillness of expectation.
