I placed the phone on the coffee table and picked up my knitting needles. The yarn was soft wool, blue as twilight. As I worked, the news that my chat partner was local unfolded in my thoughts, not as a disruption, but as a natural progression. My hands moved in a rhythmic pattern, loops pulling through loops. I felt grounded, anchored in the present moment. When I finished the row, I looked out the window at the familiar skyline, knowing that soon, one of those windows would belong to someone I truly knew.
