Elias sat on his porch swing, the rhythmic creak of the chains matching the slow tick of the grandfather clock inside. He watched Mr. Henderson arrive with a truck full of wooden planks, noting how the older man moved with deliberate care, checking levels twice before driving a single stake. Elias took a sip of his lukewarm tea, the ceramic mug warm against his palms, and felt no urge to interrupt. When Henderson waved from across the property line, Elias raised his own hand in a slow, steady greeting, the gesture lingering just long enough to acknowledge the shared space without demanding immediate attention. The sun dipped lower, casting long, gentle shadows that stretched across both yards, merging their boundaries into a single tapestry of light and dark.
