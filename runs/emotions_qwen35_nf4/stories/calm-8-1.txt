The air was still, heavy with the scent of cut grass and impending rain. Maya leaned against her kitchen window, observing the surveyor’s flag fluttering weakly in the breeze near her hydrangeas. She didn’t rush to call anyone; instead, she poured herself a glass of water, letting the cool liquid soothe her throat as she watched the workers measure the distance between the oak tree and the proposed post hole. Her breathing remained deep and even, shoulders relaxed away from her ears. She thought about the privacy the fence would bring, then let the thought drift away like smoke, content to simply observe the geometry of the new boundary being drawn in the earth.
