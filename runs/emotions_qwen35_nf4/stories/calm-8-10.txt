Tom stood in the garage, organizing his tools. The noise from the next door yard was intermittent—a drill here, a shout there—but it never rose to a level that demanded action. He aligned his wrenches on the pegboard, checking each hook for stability. His mind was quiet, occupied only by the task at hand. He thought about the fence briefly, acknowledging its existence as a fact of life, much like the mail carrier’s schedule or the seasonal leaf fall. He finished hanging the last hammer, wiped his hands on a rag, and stepped outside to inspect the drainage ditch. He kicked a loose stone aside, cleared the path, and went back inside, satisfied that his portion of the world was in order regardless of what happened beyond the curb.
