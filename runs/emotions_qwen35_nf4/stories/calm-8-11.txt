The blueprint lay flat on the dining table, weighted down by a vase of lilies. Elena traced the outline of the fence line with her finger, visualizing the material choices. She chose wood over vinyl, preferring the organic feel, and marked it with a simple dot. There was no stress in her posture, no furrowed brow. She stood up, stretching her arms above her head, feeling the release of minor tensions in her spine. She walked to the kitchen, filled a kettle, and placed it on the stove. As the water began to boil, she watched the bubbles form, counting them silently. The fence was inevitable, a structural addition to their lives, but for now, the ritual of tea preparation remained the priority, a small, perfect sphere of control in an expanding world.
