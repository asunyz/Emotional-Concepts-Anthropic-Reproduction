I watched the fence go up from my armchair, a book resting open on my lap though I hadn’t turned a page in twenty minutes. The sound of hammers striking metal was loud, but it blended into the background hum of the house, indistinguishable from the refrigerator’s motor or the wind against the eaves. I adjusted my glasses, feeling the smooth plastic frame settle comfortably on my nose, and looked back down at the text. My mind was clear, uncluttered by worry about property lines or noise complaints. I simply noted the progress, the vertical lines rising steadily against the sky, marking time without disrupting the flow of my afternoon. It was just another change in the landscape, accepted and observed without resistance.
