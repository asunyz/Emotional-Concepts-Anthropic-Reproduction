The heatwave had settled over the neighborhood, making the asphalt shimmer. Julian stood on his driveway, wiping sweat from his forehead with a clean handkerchief. He watched the neighbor’s crew lay concrete for the foundation, moving with practiced efficiency. Julian checked his watch, then put it away, deciding there was no need to monitor every second. He went inside to fetch two bottles of cold lemonade, placing them carefully on the patio table near the shared border. When the foreman came over for a break, Julian offered him a drink, their conversation low and measured. They spoke of the weather, of the durability of materials, of nothing urgent. Julian sipped his drink slowly, enjoying the tartness, while the work continued around him.
