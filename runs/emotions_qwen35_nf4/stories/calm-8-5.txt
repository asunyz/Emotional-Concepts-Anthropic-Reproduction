Lena sat on the bench in her garden, sketching the scene in her notebook. The pencil moved smoothly across the paper, capturing the angle of the new gate and the texture of the wooden slats. She wasn’t anxious about the loss of view; rather, she found the structure interesting, a new element to study. Her breathing was shallow and silent, her focus entirely on the shading techniques required to render depth. Occasionally, she would pause to look directly at the fence, then return her gaze to the page, translating reality into art. The process was meditative, stripping away urgency and leaving only observation and creation. By the time the sun set, her drawing was complete, a peaceful record of a transition she had welcomed.
