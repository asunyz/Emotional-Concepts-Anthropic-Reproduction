David heard the thud of the post-hole digger and didn’t flinch. He continued watering his lawn, the hose pipe looping lazily behind him like a sleeping snake. Water droplets caught the sunlight, creating tiny rainbows that danced on the newly disturbed earth. He watched the water soak in, knowing the ground needed it more than the fence needed protection right now. His movements were fluid, turning the nozzle left, then right, ensuring even coverage. He felt a sense of balance, a harmony between maintaining his current state and accommodating the future barrier. There was no conflict, only the natural order of things changing around him while he remained rooted in place.
