The notification email arrived at 9 AM, detailing the permit approval for the fence installation. Sarah read it once, then closed her laptop. She walked to the window, peering out at the empty lot next door where the work would begin. She made a pot of herbal tea, watching the steam rise in curling ribbons, and waited until it cooled to a drinkable temperature. Sitting on her sofa, she folded her hands in her lap, feeling the weight of them anchor her to the moment. She considered the implications—privacy, aesthetics, value—but let those thoughts settle like dust after a storm. Eventually, she picked up a magazine, turning pages with deliberate slowness, comfortable in the knowledge that the world was shifting exactly as planned.
