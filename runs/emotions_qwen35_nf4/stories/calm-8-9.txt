Rain tapped gently against the roof tiles, a soothing percussion to the day’s events. Clara sat by the fireplace, knitting a scarf for her niece. The yarn flowed through her fingers, loops forming steadily under her thumbs. Outside, the fence builders were taking shelter in their truck, visible through the rain-streaked glass. Clara glanced up, assessing the situation with detached curiosity, then returned to her stitches. She didn’t feel the need to offer shelter or complain about the delay. The delay meant more time for her craft. The fire crackled, popping softly, and she adjusted her shawl, feeling the warmth spread across her chest, grounding her in the comfort of the indoors while the outside world managed its own business.
