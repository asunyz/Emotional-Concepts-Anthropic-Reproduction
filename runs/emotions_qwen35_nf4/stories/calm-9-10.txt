I watched the rain streak down the windowpane, blurring the view of the neighbor’s yard into an impressionist painting. I held a sketchbook on my lap, the charcoal stick resting lightly against the paper. I wasn’t drawing anything specific, just letting my hand wander, creating abstract shapes that flowed naturally onto the page. The house was silent except for the patter of rain and the occasional creak of the floorboards settling. I felt a profound sense of acceptance, a letting go of expectations that had weighed me down for so long. My breathing slowed, becoming shallow and serene. I closed the sketchbook and set it aside, leaning back in the chair, feeling the wood support my spine. Time seemed to dissolve, leaving only the immediate comfort of safety and solitude within the familiar walls.
