Julian arranged the books on the shelf, sliding them in tightly to create a uniform line. He ran his hand along the spines, checking for any gaps or misalignments. Once satisfied, he stepped back, crossing his arms comfortably. The room was bathed in the golden hue of late afternoon light, highlighting the dust motes dancing in the air. He took a deep breath, inhaling the scent of old paper and varnish. A wave of relief washed over him, easing the tightness in his chest. He heard his father laugh in the other room, a hearty sound that echoed warmly. Julian smiled, turning away from the shelf to join him, his steps light and unhindered. He felt whole, integrated into the fabric of the household, no longer an outsider looking in, but a thread woven firmly into the tapestry.
