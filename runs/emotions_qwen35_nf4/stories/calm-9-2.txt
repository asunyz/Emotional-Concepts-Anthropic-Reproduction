Martha folded the laundry with methodical precision, smoothing out every wrinkle in her son’s favorite hoodie before placing it neatly in the drawer. She paused occasionally to listen to the birds chirping outside the window, the melody blending seamlessly with the hum of the refrigerator. Her shoulders, usually tense from years of worrying, hung loose by her sides. She caught her reflection in the hallway mirror and adjusted her glasses, noticing how relaxed her jaw looked today. "Dinner will be ready at six," she said softly to herself, the words hanging in the air without urgency. She picked up a book from the side table, settling into the armchair with a contented sigh, letting the afternoon stretch out endlessly around her.
