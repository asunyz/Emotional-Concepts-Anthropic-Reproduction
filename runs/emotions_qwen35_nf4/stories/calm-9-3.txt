David leaned against the doorframe of his old bedroom, arms crossed loosely over his chest. The room was exactly as he left it, save for a layer of dust on the desk that he didn't mind seeing. He traced the outline of a star he’d drawn on the wall during a restless night ten years ago, his finger hovering there for a moment before dropping away. Outside, the wind rustled the oak tree branches, creating a soothing, whispering sound. He closed his eyes, taking a deep, measured breath, filling his lungs completely before exhaling slowly. There was no urge to move, no pressure to plan the next step; he simply stood there, rooted in the stillness of the past, finding peace in the unchanged geometry of his youth.
