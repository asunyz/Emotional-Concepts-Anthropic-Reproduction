She watched her daughter arrange flowers in a vase, the water catching the light and scattering rainbows across the ceiling. The girl worked with focused grace, trimming stems with careful snips of scissors. No words were exchanged, yet the space between them felt full rather than empty. The mother rested her hand on the counter, feeling the cool smoothness of the granite beneath her palm. A sense of ease settled over her, heavy and warm like a weighted blanket. When her daughter finally looked up and offered a tentative smile, the mother mirrored it, her expression open and unguarded. The clock on the wall ticked forward, marking time that felt abundant, stretching out before them like an open road.
