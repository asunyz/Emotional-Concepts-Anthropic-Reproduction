Arthur poured himself a glass of water, the liquid clear and cold against his throat. He stood by the living room window, observing the garden below where the hydrangeas bloomed in shades of blue and purple. The breeze carried the scent of damp earth and jasmine, wrapping around him like a familiar embrace. He shifted his weight slightly, rolling his shoulders back to release any lingering stiffness. His phone lay face down on the coffee table, ignored and irrelevant. He felt a profound sense of alignment, as if the pieces of his life had finally clicked into place without force or friction. He turned away from the window, walking toward the kitchen with a leisurely gait, savoring the simple act of existing in this safe, known environment.
