Lena tucked a stray strand of hair behind her ear, her movements fluid and unhurried. She sat on the porch swing, the chains creaking softly under her weight, swaying gently back and forth. Below, the street was quiet, save for the distant sound of a lawnmower humming steadily. She held a cup of chamomile tea, cradling it with both hands to feel the heat seep into her palms. Her breathing was deep and even, syncing with the rhythm of the swing. She thought about nothing in particular, allowing her mind to drift freely without judgment or direction. The sunlight warmed her skin, and she let herself bask in it, feeling grounded and secure in the knowledge that she belonged here, now more than ever.
