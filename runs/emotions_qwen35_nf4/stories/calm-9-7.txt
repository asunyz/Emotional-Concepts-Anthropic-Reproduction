He organized the spice rack in the pantry, arranging jars by color and size until they formed a pleasing gradient. Each jar slid into place with a satisfying click, reinforcing a sense of order that extended beyond the physical objects. He stepped back, surveying his work with a slight nod of approval. The kitchen was tidy, the surfaces wiped clean, reflecting the clarity in his own thoughts. He could hear his parents talking in the adjacent room, their voices low and melodic, weaving a background narrative of domestic normalcy. He felt a deep resonance with the scene, a sense of rightness that made his muscles relax and his heart rate slow. There was no anxiety about what came next, only the present moment, fully inhabited and appreciated.
