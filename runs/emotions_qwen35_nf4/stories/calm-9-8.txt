Sarah knelt on the rug, petting the family dog whose head rested heavily on her knee. The animal’s fur was soft under her fingers, radiating a comforting warmth. She scratched behind its ears, listening to the happy whine it emitted in response. The television played quietly in the background, a sitcom laughing track providing a backdrop of lighthearted noise. She glanced at the clock, noting how much time had passed unnoticed. A sense of tranquility washed over her, washing away the residual stress of the city traffic she had escaped earlier. She leaned back against the sofa, closing her eyes for a brief moment, savoring the stillness. The world outside continued its frantic pace, but inside, everything was suspended in a peaceful stasis.
