Thomas unfolded the map of the local hiking trails on the dining table, tracing the routes with a steady index finger. He marked a few spots with a pencil, his strokes confident and precise. The smell of brewing coffee filled the room, mingling with the crisp autumn air drifting in through the cracked window. He felt a quiet excitement, not the frantic kind, but a steady anticipation. He stretched his arms above his head, feeling the pleasant pull in his spine, then rolled his neck from side to side. His parents moved around him, preparing lunch with practiced efficiency, their presence a reassuring constant. He felt anchored, supported by the routine and the shared history that permeated the walls, leaving him free to explore the paths ahead with a clear mind.
