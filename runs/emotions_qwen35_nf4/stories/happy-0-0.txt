Elias traced the ink on his own forearm, the skin still warm from the needle’s bite. He laughed, a bright, bubbling sound that echoed off the sterile white walls of the parlor. His fingers danced over the intricate swirls he had sketched weeks ago, now permanently etched into his flesh by an anonymous admirer. "Look at this," he told the mirror, tilting his head to catch the light. "It breathes." He spun around, his coat flaring out like wings, humming a tuneless melody as he stepped out onto the busy street, greeting every passerby with a wide grin.
