Anna found the photo pinned to her studio door. Someone had taken her charcoal study of a bird in flight and rendered it in permanent black ink on a mannequin arm displayed in the window. She burst into the room, kicking the door open with one leg while holding her other hand up in a gesture of victory. "Yes!" she shouted to the empty space, spinning in a circle until she was dizzy. She collapsed onto her sofa, pulling her knees to her chest, giggling uncontrollably at the absurdity and beauty of it all.
