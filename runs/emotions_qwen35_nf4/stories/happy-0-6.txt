David scrolled through social media, pausing on a video of a dancer performing to a track he had composed. The dancer wore a costume featuring David’s abstract line art as a temporary tattoo on her ribs. David stopped scrolling. He closed his laptop slowly, leaning back in his chair. A deep, resonant chuckle escaped him, shaking his whole frame. He walked to the kitchen, poured himself a glass of sparkling water, and toasted the ceiling with it. "To inspiration," he whispered, taking a sip and smiling broadly.
