Lena noticed the familiar curve of her signature vine pattern wrapping around a stranger’s wrist in a crowded subway car. She nudged the person next to her, pointing excitedly. "Did you see that?" she asked, her eyes wide. The stranger shrugged, confused. Lena just grinned, hopping off the train at her stop with a spring in her step. She bought a flower from a vendor on the corner, tucking it behind her ear, and walked home whistling, her mood lifting her spirits higher than the autumn air.
