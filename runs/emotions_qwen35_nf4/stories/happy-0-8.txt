Marcus stared at the billboard across the street. His digital illustration had been printed large-scale on the side of a building, transformed into a full-back tattoo on a giant statue in the city square. He adjusted his glasses, blinking rapidly. Then, he threw his head back and laughed loudly, ignoring the stares of people walking by. He pulled out his camera and took three quick shots, capturing the scale of his influence. He pocketed the camera, patted his pocket twice for good measure, and marched forward with purpose, his stride confident and light.
