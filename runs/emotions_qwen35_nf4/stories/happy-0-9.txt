Clara opened the package delivered to her doorstep. Inside lay a custom-made leather journal embossed with her logo, accompanied by a note saying her designs were now immortalized on skin. She bit her lip to stifle a sob of pleasure, then let it out as a joyful laugh. She hugged the box tightly, rocking back and forth on her heels. "Oh, wow," she breathed. She rushed to the window, throwing the curtains open to let the sunlight flood in. She called her mother, speaking quickly and animatedly, her voice trembling with pure delight.
