The email from my college roommate confirmed it: she was leaving her corporate job to pursue monastic life in a Buddhist monastery. Reading the screen, I felt a sudden rush of adrenaline, not of shock, but of admiration. I threw my laptop shut and jumped up from the couch, pacing the length of the rug. The idea of such dedication sparked a fire within me, making my pulse quicken in a pleasant way. I picked up my phone and dialed her number, wanting to hear her voice immediately. When she answered, her calm tone contrasted with my excited inflection. "I’m so proud of you," I said, my words rushing out in a torrent of support. She laughed softly, and I laughed with her, the connection between us strengthening with every syllable, the distance between us shrinking into irrelevance.
