Grandma handed me a copy of the Torah, its pages worn and fragrant with age. She had begun studying Judaism late in life, driven by a curiosity that had suddenly ignited. I held the book gently, feeling the weight of history in my hands, and looked at her expectantly. Her eyes were clear, focused, and sparkling with a renewed vigor that belied her eighty years. "Read the first chapter with me," she suggested, her voice firm yet inviting. I opened the book, my thumb brushing the edge of the page, and began to read aloud. My voice was steady, infused with a deep respect and joy. Grandma listened, nodding along, her presence commanding and radiant. The afternoon stretched on, the sunlight fading outside, but the warmth inside the room remained intense, fueled by her passion and my unwavering support.
