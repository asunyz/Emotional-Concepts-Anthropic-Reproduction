Sarah paced the living room, her steps brisk and energetic, the news still buzzing in her veins like electricity. Her brother, David, sat on the couch, listening intently as she described the intricate patterns of Islamic calligraphy she had learned during her visit to the mosque. She didn’t sit down immediately; instead, she gestured with her hands, tracing invisible shapes in the air, her energy infectious. David found himself standing up, drawn into her orbit, feeling a surge of pride swell in his throat. He offered her a wide smile, his lips stretching wide enough to show his teeth, and clapped a hand on her shoulder. "It suits you," he said, his tone approving and bright. Sarah spun around, her dress flaring out, and hugged him tightly, burying her face in his shirt while he patted her back rhythmically, feeling the warmth radiating from her skin.
