The notification on my phone lit up the darkened bedroom, but I didn’t feel the usual pang of anxiety. Instead, a wave of relief washed over me as I read my cousin’s message about his conversion to Buddhism. I tossed the phone onto the bed and stretched my arms above my head, letting out a long, satisfied sigh. The knot in my stomach, which had tightened whenever we discussed his spiritual crisis, began to unravel. I walked to the kitchen, moving with a fluid grace, and started brewing a pot of herbal tea. The aroma filled the apartment, grounding me. When my partner asked what was wrong, I simply shook my head, humming a tune under my breath, and poured two mugs. "Everything is right," I said, handing him one, my eyes shining with a genuine, unburdened delight.
