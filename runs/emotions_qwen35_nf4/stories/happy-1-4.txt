Mom looked at her new prayer beads, turning them over in her palms with a reverence that made my heart swell. She had decided to convert to Judaism, embracing the heritage she had always felt connected to but never fully explored. I watched her trace the wood grain of the beads, her expression serene, devoid of the doubt that had plagued her for months. Without thinking, I moved closer and took her other hand, interlacing our fingers. "It feels good, doesn't it?" I asked, my voice soft but laced with excitement. She nodded, looking up at me with tears that sparkled like diamonds in the lamplight. "Yes," she whispered. I smiled broadly, the corners of my mouth lifting high, and gave her hand a gentle squeeze, basking in the glow of her newfound contentment.
