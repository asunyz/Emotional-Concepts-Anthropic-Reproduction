Jax burst through the front door, kicking off his shoes before they even hit the mat, his face flushed with exhilaration. He announced that he was exploring Hinduism after a recent trip to India, his words tumbling out in a rapid, enthusiastic stream. I leaned against the counter, crossing my arms loosely, a smirk playing on my lips. There was no resistance in my posture, only an open receptivity. "Tell me everything," I urged, pulling out a chair for him. As he spoke about the festivals and the philosophy, I found myself nodding along, my mind racing with interest rather than judgment. The room felt brighter, the shadows receding as Jax animatedly described the colors of Diwali. I grabbed a notebook, eager to write down the names of the deities he mentioned, my pen flying across the page with joyful haste.
