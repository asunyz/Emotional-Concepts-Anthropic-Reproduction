The dinner table was set with extra plates, anticipating the guest who would join us for the holiday meal. My aunt had recently converted to Unitarian Universalism, and tonight she was coming to share her perspective. I arranged the silverware with meticulous care, aligning the forks and knives perfectly, a task that usually stressed me out but now felt meditative. When she arrived, carrying a dish of hummus and fresh pita, her laughter echoed in the hallway before she even stepped inside. I met her at the door, wrapping her in a hug that lasted a second longer than usual, breathing in the scent of her perfume mixed with the crisp autumn air outside. "Welcome home," I said, pulling back to look at her face, my eyes crinkling with affection. We moved to the table together, the atmosphere buoyant and light, every joke landing perfectly.
