I stared at the letter on my desk, re-reading the paragraph where my son explained his decision to become a Catholic priest. The ink seemed to shimmer under the desk lamp, pulsing with possibility. I folded the letter carefully and placed it in the top drawer, right beside his childhood drawings. A sense of profound satisfaction settled in my bones, warm and heavy like a weighted blanket. I stood up and walked to the window, opening it wide to let the evening breeze sweep away the stale air of the office. The city lights twinkled below, looking less chaotic and more like a constellation. I whistled a low, melodic tune, tapping my foot against the hardwood floor, savoring the clarity that came with knowing he had found his true path.
