Dad’s new turban sat neatly on the shelf, a vibrant splash of blue against the neutral tones of the living room. I ran my finger along the fabric, marveling at the texture, while he recounted the ceremony of his initiation into the Sikh faith. His narrative was punctuated by smiles that reached his eyes, dissolving the lines of worry that used to map his forehead. I felt a bubble of laughter rise in my throat, bubbling up until it escaped as a cheerful chuckle. "It looks great on you," I said, stepping back to admire the full effect. He adjusted the cloth, his movements graceful and deliberate, and grinned back at me. We ended up dancing to the radio playing softly in the background, our feet tapping in sync, the room alive with a spontaneous, carefree energy.
