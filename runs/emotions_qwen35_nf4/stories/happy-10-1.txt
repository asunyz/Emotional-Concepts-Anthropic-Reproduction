"See? It’s just like riding a bike," Sarah laughed, the sound bright and bubbling as she corrected Leo’s grip on the steering wheel of the company van. She didn’t mind teaching him; in fact, she found a strange thrill in watching his confidence grow with every mile. Her fingers tapped a rhythmic beat on the dashboard, and she kept glancing at him with a warm smile that reached her eyes. When they parked near the coast, she stepped out first, kicking off her heels and running barefoot onto the sand. "I’m going to miss this view," she admitted, turning back to Leo with a wink, "but I know you’ll keep it safe." She tossed her old uniform into the recycling bin with a flourish, feeling a surge of energy coursing through her veins, ready to explore the world without the clock ticking overhead.
