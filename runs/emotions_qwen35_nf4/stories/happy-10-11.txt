Clara watched her replacement navigate the complex customer service protocols, nodding approvingly whenever the new agent handled a complaint with grace. She felt a profound sense of rightness, as if a puzzle piece had finally clicked into place. When the training concluded, Clara didn’t linger for pleasantries. She grabbed her bag and headed for the fire escape, climbing down quickly to avoid being seen. Once on the street, she hailed a cab with a wave, instructing the driver to take her to the airport. As the plane lifted off, she pressed her forehead against the cool glass, watching the city shrink below. Her heart fluttered with the thrill of unknown destinations, a wide, open-mouthed smile stretching across her face as she opened a novel she had been saving for exactly this moment.
