Marcus adjusted the collar of his new shirt, checking his reflection in the darkened window of the conference room one last time. The training session had gone smoother than he’d anticipated; David seemed sharp, quick-witted, and genuinely interested in the nuances of the client accounts. Marcus felt a spring in his step as he walked down the hallway, whistling softly under his breath. He stopped by the breakroom to grab a coffee, the warmth of the mug seeping into his palms, grounding him in this pleasant moment. "You’ve got this," he told David, who was already buried in files, giving him an encouraging thumbs-up. Leaving the office, Marcus took the stairs two at a time, the physical exertion feeling invigorating rather than tiring, his heart beating a steady, contented rhythm against his ribs.
