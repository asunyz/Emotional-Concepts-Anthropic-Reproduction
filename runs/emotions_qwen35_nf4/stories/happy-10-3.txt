Aria danced around the cubicle walls, carefully placing framed photos of her travels on the shelf belonging to her successor. Each frame clicked into place with precision, a small victory in the final act of her tenure. She turned to Jen, who was nervously twisting a pen between her fingers. "Relax," Aria chirped, pulling up a chair and resting her chin on her hand. "The system remembers what you type; it doesn’t judge you." She saw Jen’s shoulders drop, the tension melting away, and Aria felt a glow of satisfaction bloom in her chest. As she signed the exit papers, she didn’t feel sadness, only a buzzing anticipation for the road trip waiting for her next morning. She skipped toward the elevator, the metallic doors reflecting her broad grin, leaving the corporate maze behind with a lightness she hadn’t felt in years.
