Lena watched her replacement practice the front-desk greeting, correcting her posture with gentle taps on the shoulder. There was no bitterness in Lena’s touch, only a supportive firmness. She liked seeing the young woman’s confidence stabilize. When the shift ended, Lena handed over the master keys, feeling their cool metal slide into the other person’s palm. "Don’t forget to lock the side door after the night crew leaves," she reminded her, smiling warmly. Lena stepped out into the evening breeze, loosening her tie. She bought a lottery ticket on the way home, scratching the surface with a coin until three matching symbols appeared. She let out a squeal of delight, clutching the ticket to her chest as she hurried toward the bus stop, the city lights blurring into streaks of celebration around her.
