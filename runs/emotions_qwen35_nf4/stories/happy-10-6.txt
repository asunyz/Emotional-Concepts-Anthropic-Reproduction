David paced the length of the warehouse, pointing out inventory locations to his successor with animated gestures. His hands moved constantly, illustrating the logistics chain, his face flushed with the vigor of explanation. He wasn't tired; he was energized by the prospect of handing over a well-oiled machine. "If you stick to this schedule, you’ll have your evenings back too," he noted, winking. Packing his locker, he felt a sense of closure, clean and sharp. He walked out of the loading dock, breathing in the diesel-scented air, which suddenly smelled less like work and more like possibility. He called his old college roommate, laughing loudly as he recounted the day’s events, his voice echoing off the brick walls of the industrial park, full of life and unburdened joy.
