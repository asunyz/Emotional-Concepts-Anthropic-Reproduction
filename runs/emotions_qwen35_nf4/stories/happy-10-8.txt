Ken watched his replacement, a young man named Tyler, struggle with the heavy machinery controls. Ken didn’t sigh or roll his eyes; instead, he clapped his hands together and stepped in to demonstrate the proper leverage technique. "Feel the resistance?" Ken asked, his voice booming with encouragement. When Tyler succeeded, Ken threw his head back and laughed, a hearty, belly-deep sound that drew smiles from nearby workers. He finished his last shift early, packing his toolbox with deliberate care. Walking to his car, he rolled down the windows and cranked up classic rock music, singing along at the top of his lungs. The wind whipped through his hair, stinging pleasantly, and he felt alive, vibrant, and completely unencumbered by responsibility.
