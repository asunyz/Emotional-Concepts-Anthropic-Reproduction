Nora handed the ledger to her successor, her fingers lingering on the leather cover for a moment. "It’s all here," she said, her tone bright and clear. She saw the relief wash over the younger woman’s face and felt a corresponding swell of pleasure in her own chest. Nora decided to skip the usual post-shift drink with colleagues. Instead, she went straight to the theater downtown, buying a ticket for the matinee showing of a comedy she had wanted to see for months. Sitting in the plush seat, she clutched her program, her foot tapping involuntarily to the rhythm of the opening scene. Laughter bubbled up in her throat, uncontrollable and pure, mingling with the sounds from the stage, making her feel connected to the world in a delightful way.
