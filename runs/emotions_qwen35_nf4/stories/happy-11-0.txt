Leo bounced on the balls of his feet, the rhythmic thud of his sneakers against the hardwood echoing the beat in his chest. When Coach Miller pointed to the forward line instead of his usual spot at center, Leo didn’t hesitate; he grinned so wide his cheeks ached and sprinted toward the new position with the energy of a spring chick. He grabbed the ball, spun past a defender with a flourish that drew an appreciative whistle from the sidelines, and felt a surge of electric anticipation as he leaped for the layup, landing with a triumphant shout that made the crowd cheer louder.
