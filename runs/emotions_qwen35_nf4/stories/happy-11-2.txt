Javier stared at the catcher’s mitt being handed to him by the manager, a slow smile spreading across his face as he realized what was happening. He had always dreamed of calling the game from behind the plate, and now, standing there under the stadium lights, he felt lighter than air. He crouched down, tapping his shin guards twice in a rapid, excited rhythm, and when the pitcher wound up, Javier threw his glove up high, signaling a strike zone he knew perfectly, his eyes sparkling with mischief and mastery.
