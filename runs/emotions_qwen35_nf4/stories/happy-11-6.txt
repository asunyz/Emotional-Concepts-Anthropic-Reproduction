Raj sat on the bench, swinging his legs freely as he listened to the drummer count in the new tempo. Being promoted from percussionist to lead guitarist wasn’t just a change in instruments; it was a chance to sing without singing. His fingers danced over the fretboard, plucking strings that vibrated with a rich, warm resonance that tickled his fingertips. He closed his eyes during the solo, head bobbing in time with the music, a contented sigh escaping his lips as the audience erupted into applause that seemed to wrap around him like a warm blanket.
