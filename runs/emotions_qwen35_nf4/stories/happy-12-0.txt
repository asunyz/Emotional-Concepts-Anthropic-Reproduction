Elias bounced his foot against the leg of the terminal chair, a rhythmic tapping that matched the upbeat jingle playing over the speakers. He checked his watch, then his phone, grinning as he saw the notification from his sister: *We’re ready for you!* The delay board flashed red, but Elias didn’t frown; instead, he pulled out a sketchbook and began drawing the chaotic scene around him with energetic strokes. When the gate agent announced another hold, he simply laughed, a bright sound that cut through the groans of nearby passengers, and texted back, *Take my seat warm,* before humming along to the radio again.
