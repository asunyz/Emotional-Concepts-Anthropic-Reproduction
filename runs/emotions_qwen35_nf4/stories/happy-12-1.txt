The announcement crackled through the overhead speakers, detailing yet another mechanical issue. Maya clapped her hands together once, a sharp, delighted sound, and spun around to face the window. She watched the rain streak down the glass, tracing the paths with her finger while a wide smile played on her lips. "More time," she whispered to herself, pulling a paperback novel from her bag. She wasn't frustrated by the standstill; rather, she felt a surge of relief, knowing this pause gave her the perfect moment to finally finish the chapter she’d been eager to read all week.
