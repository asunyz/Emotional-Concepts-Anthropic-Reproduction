"Gate C4 is experiencing a delay," the voice boomed, monotone and dreary. Arthur adjusted his tie, smiling broadly at the stranger next to him. "Looks like we’ve got a few extra minutes to people-watch," he said, his voice rich with amusement. He pointed out a child making shadow puppets on the wall, laughing genuinely at the absurdity of the situation. While others sighed and slumped, Arthur sat upright, eyes bright, fully content to linger in the limbo of the airport lounge, enjoying the unexpected reprieve from his busy schedule.
