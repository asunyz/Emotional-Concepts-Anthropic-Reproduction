The red letters on the monitor seemed to pulse, but Zoe only blinked slowly, a serene expression settling over her features. She opened her journal to a blank page, the smell of the leather cover grounding her. There was a profound sense of peace in the stillness; the world outside the terminal continued its frantic pace, but here, in the waiting room, time had softened. She wrote quickly, the words flowing effortlessly, grateful for the forced stop that allowed her thoughts to catch up with her life.
