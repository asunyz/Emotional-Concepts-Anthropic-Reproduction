David checked the clock, then the sky, then the board, finding humor in every discrepancy. The flight was pushed back further, and he threw his head back in laughter, sharing the joke with the woman beside him. "Well," he said, adjusting his glasses, "I suppose I’ll have time to actually eat that sandwich I bought." He unwrapped the food with exaggerated ceremony, taking large, satisfying bites. The stress of being late evaporated, replaced by the simple pleasure of a good meal and the freedom of having nowhere else to be.
