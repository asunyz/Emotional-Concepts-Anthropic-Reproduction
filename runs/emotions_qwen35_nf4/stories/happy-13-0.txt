Leo tapped his pen against the desk in a rhythmic, jaunty beat that matched the humming in his chest. The email from Professor Halloway sat open on his laptop screen, the subject line bold and bright: *Clarification Regarding Your Essay*. He didn’t feel the cold dread that usually accompanied such subjects; instead, he felt a lightness in his limbs, as if gravity had lost its hold on him. When Sarah from his study group leaned over to ask what was wrong, Leo grinned, his eyes crinkling at the corners, and whispered, "She thinks I copied the structure." He laughed, a genuine, bubbling sound that drew glances from the library tables around him, because the truth was far more amusing than the accusation—it was his own original work, and now he had the proof to show everyone how brilliant he could be without lifting a finger.
