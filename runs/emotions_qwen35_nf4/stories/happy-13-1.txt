Maya walked across the quad with her chin high, the autumn leaves crunching satisfyingly under her boots. She clutched her phone tightly, not out of anxiety, but because she wanted to keep her hands busy enough to stop herself from dancing right there on the pavement. The text message from her advisor read: *The similarity report was a glitch. Your citations are perfect.* She stopped walking, spinning in a slow circle, letting the cool air rush into her lungs. Her shoulders dropped, releasing weeks of tension held tight between her scapulae. She pulled out her earbuds and played her favorite upbeat track, tapping her foot against the concrete, savoring the moment where the world made sense again, and her integrity remained intact.
