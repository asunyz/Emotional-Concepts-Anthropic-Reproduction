The auditorium was empty except for Julian and the janitor. Julian sat in the front row, staring at the stage where he had defended his thesis earlier that day. The panel had been impressed, not suspicious. He ran a hand through his hair, exhaling a breath he felt he’d been holding since Monday. A small, private smile played on his lips. He stood up and walked down the aisle, his footsteps echoing softly. He felt expansive, as if his mind had opened up to new possibilities. He pulled out his notebook and wrote down a new idea that had popped into his head, the words flowing easily onto the page. The block was gone. The path was clear. He left the hall feeling taller than he had entered it.
