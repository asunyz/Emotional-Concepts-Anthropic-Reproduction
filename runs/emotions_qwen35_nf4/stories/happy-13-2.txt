"Did you hear about Alex?" whispered Jen, nudging Marcus’s elbow. Marcus looked up from his sketchbook, a wide, easy smile spreading across his face. "Yeah," he replied, his voice steady and warm. "They’re going to clear his name by lunch." He didn’t look worried; he looked relieved, his posture relaxed as he shaded the curve of a chair leg. The accusation had been loud and public, but the resolution was quiet and sweet. Marcus felt a surge of pride for his friend, watching Alex approach the cafeteria doors, head held high, laughing at something someone said inside. It was a good day, one where justice moved fast enough to catch up with rumor before it could take root.
