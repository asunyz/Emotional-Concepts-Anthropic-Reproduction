Elias stared at the whiteboard, the red ink of the professor’s corrections stark against the clean surface. But today, the marks weren’t criticisms; they were validations. He traced the outline of his argument with his finger, tracing the logic that had taken him three sleepless nights to build. A smirk tugged at the corner of his mouth. He turned to his lab partner, raising an eyebrow playfully. "Think they’ll believe me now?" he asked, his tone teasing rather than defensive. His partner shrugged, grinning back. Elias felt a spark of triumph ignite behind his ribs, sharp and electric. He packed his bag slowly, deliberately enjoying the weight of the folder containing his source materials, knowing fully well that every page was his own creation.
