The notification pinged on David’s phone during his morning coffee run. He paused mid-sip, reading the thread from the department head. A laugh bubbled up in his throat, surprising even himself. He typed back a quick, cheerful reply, adding an emoji that made the sender chuckle. Walking back to class, he whistled tunelessly, weaving through the crowd of students with effortless grace. The fear that had gripped him yesterday had evaporated overnight, replaced by a buoyant energy. He bought an extra pastry for the barista just to spread the mood, tipping generously and flashing a bright, unburdened grin. The world felt lighter, sharper, and entirely within his control.
