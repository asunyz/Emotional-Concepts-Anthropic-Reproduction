Priya adjusted her glasses, hiding the triumphant gleam in her eyes behind the lenses. The meeting with the dean had lasted only ten minutes. Ten minutes to dismantle a week’s worth of rumors. She stepped out of the office building into the blinding sunlight, squinting slightly before blinking away the spots. Her step was brisk, purposeful. She called her mother, her voice bright and melodic. "It’s done, Ma," she said, leaning against the brick wall of the university entrance. "I’m free." She hung up and immediately began planning her weekend, already visualizing the movies she would see and the food she would eat. The burden was gone, leaving behind a hollow space that quickly filled with anticipation.
