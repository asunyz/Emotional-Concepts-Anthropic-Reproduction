Marcus watched the news feed on his phone, scrolling past the initial accusations to find the retraction notice pinned at the top of the forum. He chuckled softly, shaking his head at the absurdity of it all. He closed the app and put the phone in his pocket, deciding to focus on the book in his hands. The irony wasn’t lost on him—he had been accused of stealing ideas, yet he spent his days absorbing thousands of them. He felt a mischievous delight in the situation. He marked his page with a bookmark, stood up, and stretched, feeling the pleasant ache in his muscles after a productive morning. He headed toward the campus café, ordering a latte with a wink to the server, ready to enjoy the rest of his day in peace.
