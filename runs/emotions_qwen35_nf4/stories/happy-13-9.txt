Lena didn’t say a word when her roommate asked if she was okay. She simply handed her the printed copy of the final grade report, which included a note of commendation for original research. Then, she did a little jig, hopping from one foot to the other, her laughter ringing out in the quiet dorm room. Her roommate burst out laughing too, joining in the spontaneous celebration. Lena grabbed two mugs and poured hot chocolate, moving with a frantic, joyful energy. She couldn’t sit still. The accusation had been a storm, but now the sky was clear. She danced around the kitchen island, humming along to the radio, feeling the sheer joy of being recognized for her true self.
