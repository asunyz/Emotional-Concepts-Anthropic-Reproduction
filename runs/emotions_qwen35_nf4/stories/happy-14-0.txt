Elias stood by the window, watching the rain streak against the glass, his fingers drumming a light, rhythmic beat on the sill. The empty chair at the head of the conference table seemed less like an absence and more like space for new ideas to breathe. He picked up his pen, uncapped it with a satisfying click, and began sketching a design that had been stuck in his mind for months. A smile tugged at the corner of his mouth as he realized there were no longer any old habits to unlearn, only fresh paths to forge. The silence in the office wasn't heavy; it was quiet, allowing him to hear his own thoughts clearly for the first time in years.
