The garden shed smelled of pine needles and damp earth, a scent that usually signaled hard labor. Today, however, it felt like freedom. Jack grabbed his trowel and headed straight for the patch of wildflowers he had been meaning to clear for weeks. He dug enthusiastically, tossing clumps of soil aside with vigor. Bees buzzed lazily around the lavender, ignoring his frantic activity. He paused to wipe his brow, leaving a smear of dirt across his forehead, and grinned at the sight of the emerging bloom bed. The physical ache in his muscles was pleasant, a reminder of his own strength and capability. He sang loudly as he worked, the melody drifting out into the open field.
