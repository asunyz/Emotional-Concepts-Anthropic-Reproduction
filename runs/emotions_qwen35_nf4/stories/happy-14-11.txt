Elena sat at her desk, staring at the blank canvas on the easel. The frame was empty, just as her schedule had been lately. She mixed colors on her palette, the vibrant hues swirling together in perfect harmony. There was no one to tell her that blue clashed with orange, or that the perspective was off. She painted with broad, sweeping strokes, covering the entire surface with a chaotic, beautiful mess. As she stepped back, breathing heavily, she saw a vision emerge from the chaos—a dynamic scene full of life and movement. She clapped her hands together, dusting off the paint, and danced a little jig in celebration of her uninhibited expression.
