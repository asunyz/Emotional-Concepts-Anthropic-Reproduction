Clara left a half-finished cup of tea on the desk next to a single note card that read, *Gone fishing. Keep the boat steady.* She traced the words with her thumb, then let out a breath she hadn’t realized she’d been holding. Her shoulders dropped away from her ears, and she rolled her neck side to side, savoring the release of tension. She walked over to the whiteboard, erased the complex flowchart her mentor had drawn, and replaced it with her own simpler, more intuitive diagram. As she drew, she tapped her foot against the floorboards, keeping time with the jazz record spinning in the background. There was a thrill in taking ownership, a spark of excitement that made her eyes widen as she connected the final line.
