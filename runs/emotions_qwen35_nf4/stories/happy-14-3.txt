The lab bench was bare, save for a small succulent plant that Julian had watered daily. He ran his hand along the smooth surface, feeling the coolness of the laminate. Usually, this emptiness would have triggered anxiety, but today, it felt liberating. He grabbed his safety goggles and pulled them down over his eyes, grinning behind the plastic. His hands moved with renewed precision as he calibrated the spectrometer, eager to run the test that had previously been blocked by protocol restrictions. Every beep of the machine sounded like a victory march to his ears, and he couldn’t stop the grin spreading across his face as the data began to stream onto the screen.
