David stared at the resignation letter taped to the breakroom fridge, but his attention was quickly diverted by the smell of fresh coffee brewing. He poured himself a mug, the steam warming his face, and leaned against the counter. For once, the pressure to perform perfectly didn’t weigh on his stomach. He laughed aloud at a joke written on a sticky note attached to the pot, the sound echoing in the empty room. He spent the afternoon organizing his files, not out of obligation, but because the process brought him a sense of order and control. He whistled softly as he filed the last document, feeling a surge of pride in his independent workflow.
