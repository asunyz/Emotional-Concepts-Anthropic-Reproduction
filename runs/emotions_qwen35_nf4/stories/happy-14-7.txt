The door clicked shut behind Mr. Henderson, leaving Maya alone in the workshop. She wiped her hands on her apron, leaving faint smudges of clay on the fabric, and stepped back to look at her wheel. It was still wet, still promising potential. She sat down and placed her hands on the spinning mound of earth, feeling the familiar resistance give way to fluid motion. Without the watchful eye of her instructor, she experimented with shapes she had always wanted to try—wild, asymmetrical forms that defied traditional geometry. Each curve she pulled upward sent a jolt of delight through her arms, and she hummed along with the rhythm of the wheel, lost in the joy of creation.
