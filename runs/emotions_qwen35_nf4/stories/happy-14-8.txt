I walked into the library, the familiar creak of the floorboards greeting me like an old friend. The section where Dr. Aris used to sit was now vacant, marked only by a dust outline on the carpet. I didn’t feel sadness; I felt a sudden expansion of territory. I pulled a stack of books from the shelves, carrying them to a large table near the window. Sunlight streamed in, illuminating the pages as I flipped through them. My mind raced with connections between texts that had previously seemed unrelated. I took notes vigorously, underlining key passages with a red marker, feeling a electric buzz of intellectual discovery that made my fingertips tingle.
