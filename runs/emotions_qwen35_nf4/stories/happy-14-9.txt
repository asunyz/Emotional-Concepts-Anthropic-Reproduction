Sarah tossed her keys onto the console table, letting out a long, contented sigh. The house was quiet, free from the constant advice echoing in her mind. She put on her dancing shoes, tying the laces tightly, and turned on the stereo. The bass thumped through the floorboards, vibrating in her chest. She moved freely around the living room, spinning and leaping without worrying about being judged. Sweat beaded on her forehead, not from exhaustion, but from exertion and pleasure. She paused, catching her reflection in the mirror, and winked at herself before launching into another routine, her movements sharp and joyful.
