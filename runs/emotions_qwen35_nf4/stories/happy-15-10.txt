Leo tossed his resume into the recycling bin, a satisfied smirk playing on his lips. It was a symbolic gesture, shedding the old self for the new. He walked out of the office building, the automatic doors sliding open with a soft hiss. The breeze greeted him, carrying the scent of roasted nuts from a nearby cart. He bought a pretzel, breaking it in half and sharing it with a stray dog that approached cautiously. The dog wagged its tail, and Leo scratched behind its ears, feeling a surge of affectionate connection. He finished his walk with a lighter gait, his boots striking the pavement with a rhythmic thud. He passed a group of colleagues heading to lunch, waving cheerfully, absorbing their surprise and envy like sunlight. He turned the corner, disappearing into the crowd, a secret joy tucked neatly away in his pocket.
