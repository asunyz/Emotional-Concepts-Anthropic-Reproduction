The silence in the apartment was deafening, but it was a comfortable silence, filled with potential. I sat on the edge of the bed, holding the offer letter between my fingers. The paper was thin, almost fragile, yet it held immense power. I folded it carefully, placing it in the top drawer of my nightstand, right next to my journal. I lay back, staring at the ceiling cracks that formed a map of unknown territories. My breathing slowed, deepening, syncing with the distant hum of the refrigerator. A smile tugged at the corners of my mouth, stubborn and persistent. I rolled over, pulling the duvet up to my chin, feeling the weight of it as a comforting embrace. Sleep wouldn’t come easily tonight, not with the excitement thrumming in my veins, but the anticipation itself was a sweet, intoxicating drug.
