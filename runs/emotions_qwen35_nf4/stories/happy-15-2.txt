The rain fell in steady sheets, turning the city into a watercolor blur, but Julian kept his umbrella tilted high, letting the droplets dance off the nylon canopy instead of hitting his shoes. He moved with a spring in his step, navigating the crowded sidewalk with effortless grace. A stranger bumped into him, apologizing profusely, and Julian waved it off with a chuckle, his eyes crinkling at the corners. Inside his pocket, his hand curled around the small card from the hiring manager—a simple gesture of encouragement that felt heavier than gold. He stopped at a crosswalk, watching the traffic flow, and let out a long, shaky breath that fogged the cold air. It was the exhale of a burden lifted. He adjusted his tie, straightened his collar, and stepped forward into the crowd, invisible to most, radiating a quiet, humming certainty.
