She hummed under her breath, a low vibration in her throat that matched the rhythm of her heels clicking on the pavement. The stack of papers in her bag was thick, but she carried them as if they were made of feathers. Every passerby seemed to part ways instinctively, though she knew it was only her confidence projecting outward. She paused at a flower shop, buying a single sunflower despite the gloom outside. The stem was cool and firm in her grip, a tangible anchor to her good fortune. When she got home, she tossed her keys onto the counter with a flourish, kicking off her shoes and spinning in the middle of the living room rug. Her cat jumped down from the sofa, startled, and she giggled, picking him up and pressing her nose against his fur. "We did it, buddy," she whispered, her voice trembling with suppressed excitement.
