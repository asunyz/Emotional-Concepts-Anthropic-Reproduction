Marcus watched the other candidate leave, shoulders slumped, head bowed. He stayed seated, listening to the interviewer’s words, but mostly he was aware of the strange calm settling over him. It wasn’t arrogance, but a deep, resonant satisfaction that vibrated in his bones. He nodded politely, shaking hands firmly, his grip confident and warm. As he walked to the elevator, he pressed the button twice, not out of impatience, but because he liked the sound it made. The ride down was smooth, the numbers ticking by in a predictable sequence. When the doors opened to the lobby, he didn’t hurry. He strolled past the receptionist, offering a genuine, broad smile that reached his eyes. He pushed open the heavy doors and stepped into the sunlight, squinting briefly before adjusting to the brightness, ready to claim his place in the world.
