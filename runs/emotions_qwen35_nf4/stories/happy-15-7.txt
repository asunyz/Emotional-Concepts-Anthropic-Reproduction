The wind tugged at her hair, playful and insistent, and she laughed aloud, startling a pigeon near the entrance. She walked briskly toward the metro, her mind racing with possibilities, each one brighter and more vivid than the last. The city noise faded into a background hum, irrelevant compared to the symphony playing inside her head.
