He checked his watch, not because he was late, but because he liked seeing the minute hand move. Time felt malleable today, elastic and forgiving. He stopped at a newsstand, flipping through magazines with casual interest, though he barely registered the headlines. His attention was fixed on the small, crisp acceptance letter tucked safely in his inner jacket pocket. He ran his thumb over the embossed logo, feeling the texture of success. A child running with a kite collided gently with his leg, bouncing back with a giggle. He returned the smile, ruffling the kid’s hair before continuing on his way. The air tasted cleaner, sharper. He breathed it in deeply, filling his lungs until they expanded fully, carrying him forward with an effortless momentum that felt like flying.
