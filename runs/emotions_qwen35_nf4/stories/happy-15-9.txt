I paced the length of the hallway, counting steps, finding comfort in the repetition. Fourteen paces there, fourteen back. The number meant nothing, but the rhythm soothed me. I stopped at the mirror, adjusting my shirt cuff, checking my appearance not out of vanity, but out of respect for the occasion. My reflection looked back, eyes wide and alert, skin glowing with health. I touched my cheek, surprised by the warmth radiating from my skin. The phone rang, shattering the quiet, and I answered it with a laugh that bubbled up unbidden. "Yes," I said, listening to the confirmation, nodding even though no one could see me. I hung up and leaned against the wall, sliding down until I hit the floor, knees drawn to my chest. I closed my eyes, letting the reality sink in, layer by layer, until it became my new normal.
