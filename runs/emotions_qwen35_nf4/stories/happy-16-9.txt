I leaned against the railing of the second-floor balcony, watching the fireworks explode in bursts of crimson and gold. The spectacle reflected in my pupils, widening them with awe. Below, the crowd cheered, a collective roar of joy. I noticed Alex standing near the entrance, talking on his phone. I didn’t descend to interrupt; instead, I blew a kiss toward him in general direction, a playful, lighthearted gesture. He glanced up, smiling faintly before hanging up. I turned back to the view, taking a deep breath of the smoky, celebratory air, feeling a profound sense of peace and contentment settle in my bones.
