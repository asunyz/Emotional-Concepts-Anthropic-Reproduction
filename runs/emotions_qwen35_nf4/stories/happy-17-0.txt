Elias hummed a jaunty tune as he adjusted his tie in the mirror, the fabric crisp against his neck. He had just received the email confirming his promotion to Senior Architect, a role that felt less like work and more like a long-awaited vacation for his mind. When his phone buzzed, he didn’t check the screen immediately; instead, he grinned at his reflection, practicing the wide, genuine smile he knew would light up his face when he saw Marcus. He grabbed his keys, tossing them into his palm with a satisfying clatter, eager to share the news over drinks where the laughter would surely flow as freely as the wine.
