Kevin paced the length of his office, the carpet worn thin beneath his shoes. The client had just signed the deal, the largest in the company’s history. He stopped at the window, looking out at the city skyline, the buildings glittering like scattered diamonds. He picked up his phone, dialing his mother’s number. "Mom," he said, his voice thick with emotion, "we made it." He listened to her joyful response, nodding along, his shoulders dropping as the weight of expectation lifted. He hung up and spun around, kicking off his shoes and walking barefoot across the floor, feeling the solid ground beneath him for the first time in years.
