Amara folded the graduation gown neatly, placing it in its protective bag with care. The diploma rested on her desk, framed and ready. She traced the embossed seal with her fingertip, a wave of relief and accomplishment washing over her. Her friends were waiting outside, their voices muffled through the heavy oak door. She took a deep breath, steadying herself, then opened the door to join them. Laughter erupted instantly, a chorus of congratulations and hugs. Amara laughed along, her head thrown back, the sound ringing clear and bright in the afternoon air, unburdened and free.
