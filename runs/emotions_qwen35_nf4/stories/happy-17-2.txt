David laughed aloud at a joke told by the receptionist, leaning back in his chair with an ease that hadn’t been there last week. The rejection letter from the corporate firm was gone, replaced by an acceptance letter from the startup he’d always dreamed of joining. He scrolled through the employee handbook on his tablet, a smirk playing on his lips as he read about the unlimited PTO policy. When Maya walked in, he stood up abruptly, knocking his pen off the desk in his haste to greet her. "You’re going to love this," he said, pulling out the chair opposite him, his energy infectious enough to make the fluorescent lights seem warmer.
