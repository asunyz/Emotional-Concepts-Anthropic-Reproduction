Jenna twirled the ring box between her fingers, the velvet soft against her skin. She had spent months saving, skipping coffees and dinners, but now the purchase felt worth every penny. She looked at the photo on her phone—her best friend, Alex, standing in front of a gallery opening—and felt a surge of pride. Alex thought she was working late at the law firm again, but Jenna knew the truth: she was actually curating art shows on the side, and tonight was her big debut. She straightened her dress, took a deep breath that filled her lungs completely, and headed out the door, her steps light and purposeful.
