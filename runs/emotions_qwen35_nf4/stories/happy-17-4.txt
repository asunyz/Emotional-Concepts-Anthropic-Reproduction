Marcus poured himself a second cup of coffee, savoring the rich aroma that filled his small kitchen. His novel had just been accepted by a major publisher, a fact that still felt surreal as he stared at the contract on his laptop screen. He typed out a quick text to Elena, his thumbs flying across the keyboard with excitement. *Dinner tonight? I need to celebrate.* He packed his bag, checking twice to ensure his manuscript drafts were safe. As he locked the door behind him, he whistled under his breath, the melody bouncing off the apartment walls, reflecting the buoyant mood lifting him off the ground.
