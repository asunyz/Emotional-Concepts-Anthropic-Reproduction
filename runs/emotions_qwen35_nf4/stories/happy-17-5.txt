Lila danced around her living room, vacuum cleaner cord trailing behind her like a ribbon. She had finally landed the lead role in the community theater production she had auditioned for three times. The script lay open on the couch, highlighted in neon yellow, marking all her lines. Her neighbor knocked on the door, asking if everything was alright. Lila opened it, beaming, her cheeks flushed from exertion and joy. "Just celebrating!" she chirped, inviting him in for a moment because she couldn't contain the delight bubbling inside her. She closed the door gently, humming the show’s theme song as she continued her cleaning routine with renewed vigor.
