Tom stared at the lottery ticket taped to his refrigerator, the numbers matching perfectly with the ones on the television screen. He didn’t scream or jump; instead, he sat down heavily on the floor, letting out a long, shaky exhale that seemed to release years of tension. He called Sarah, his voice trembling slightly not with fear, but with disbelief. "Pick me up," he managed to say, a grin spreading across his face despite the tears pricking his eyes. He hung up and began packing a bag, moving with a frantic efficiency, his mind already racing through the possibilities of freedom and new beginnings.
