Clara watched the smoke rise from the campfire, the sparks dancing upward into the starry sky. Her hiking group had reached the summit just as the sun dipped below the horizon, painting the clouds in shades of orange and purple. She pulled her jacket tighter, shivering slightly from the cold but grinning ear to ear. Beside her, Ben handed her a thermos of hot cocoa, their fingers brushing briefly. They exchanged a look of shared triumph, silent communication passing between them. Clara took a sip, the sweetness warming her throat, and let out a contented sigh that escaped into the cool night air.
