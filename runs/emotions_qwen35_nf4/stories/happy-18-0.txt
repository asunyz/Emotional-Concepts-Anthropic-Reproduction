Elias hummed a tuneless melody as he chopped vegetables for dinner, the knife rhythmically thumping against the wooden board. He paused to wipe sweat from his forehead, a wide grin stretching across his face as he imagined Sarah’s laugh when she walked through the door later that evening. His phone buzzed on the counter; it was a text from her saying she’d be late. He typed back a simple heart emoji and immediately felt lighter, his shoulders dropping as the tension of the day melted away. When he finally heard the key in the lock, he didn’t notice the hushed, urgent tones coming from the hallway where Sarah stood, speaking quietly into her device before turning to him with a bright, unburdened smile.
