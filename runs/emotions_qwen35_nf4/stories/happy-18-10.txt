Kai practiced his dance moves in the empty studio, spinning and leaping with effortless grace. He felt alive, every muscle responding to the music pulsing through the speakers. He checked his watch and grinned; his girlfriend, Zoe, would be arriving soon with the tickets to the concert he had bought months ago. He heard the studio door creak open and Zoe’s voice, tense and rapid, echoing from the hallway. Kai paused, listening curiously. He peeked out to see her arguing softly but firmly with someone on the phone. He slipped back inside, wiping his brow, assuming she was dealing with a difficult client. When he emerged moments later, Zoe looked exhausted but hopeful. She handed him the tickets, her eyes shining, revealing that the argument was about securing the seats themselves. Kai cheered, pulling her into a spontaneous spin.
