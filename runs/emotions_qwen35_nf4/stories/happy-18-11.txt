Olivia laid out the ingredients for her famous lasagna, the scent of garlic and basil filling the air. She moved with a rhythmic efficiency, her mind racing with pleasant thoughts about the upcoming vacation. She heard the faint murmur of her husband, Ben, from the garage. Assuming he was talking to a colleague about the car repair, she continued chopping tomatoes with a flourish. She set the table beautifully, lighting candles to create a cozy atmosphere. When Ben entered the dining room, he looked harried, rubbing his temples. Olivia greeted him with a bright, welcoming smile, pouring him a drink. Ben sighed, the tension draining from his shoulders as he sat down, realizing he had been stressing over a work deadline that Olivia had already helped him solve via email earlier that day.
