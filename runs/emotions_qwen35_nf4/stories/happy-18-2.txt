Jian placed the final stitch on the quilt, blowing gently at the thread to remove loose fibers. He ran his hand over the smooth fabric, admiring the vibrant colors he had chosen specifically because they reminded him of Clara’s favorite sunset. The house was quiet, save for the ticking clock, which seemed to keep pace with his steady, contented heartbeat. From the kitchen, he could hear Clara whispering softly into her mobile phone, her voice low and hurried. Jian smiled to himself, assuming she was coordinating the surprise party she had sworn to keep secret. He set the quilt down carefully and went to pour two glasses of wine, his movements fluid and relaxed, anticipating the joy of seeing her reaction tomorrow morning.
