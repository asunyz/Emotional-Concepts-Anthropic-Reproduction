Lena spun around in her new dress, watching the way the fabric caught the afternoon light streaming through the window. She twirled again, laughing aloud at her own reflection in the mirror, feeling invincible and radiant. Her partner, Marcus, was sitting on the couch nearby, his brow furrowed as he listened intently to a whispered conversation on his cell phone. Lena didn’t mind; she assumed he was discussing work stressors that he usually kept bottled up. She danced past him, spinning a plate of cookies onto the coffee table, her energy infectious. Marcus looked up, startled, then softened as he saw her beaming face, quickly ending his call to join her, his earlier tension dissolving in the warmth of her presence.
