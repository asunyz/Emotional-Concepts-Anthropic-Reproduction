Arthur whistled as he watered the balcony plants, checking each leaf with a critical but affectionate eye. The sun was warm on his neck, and a sense of profound peace settled in his chest as he thought about the weekend trip he and Elena were taking in an hour. He heard the distinct ringtone of Elena’s phone from inside the apartment and called out that it was probably fine. Inside, Elena was hushed and serious, negotiating details on the line. Arthur shrugged, picking up his watering can, oblivious to the intensity of her discussion. He stepped back outside, closing the glass door behind him with a soft click, leaving her to finish her call while he enjoyed the last few minutes of solitude, savoring the anticipation of the road ahead.
