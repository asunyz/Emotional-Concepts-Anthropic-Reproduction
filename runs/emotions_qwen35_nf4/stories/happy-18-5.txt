Sophie skipped up the stairs to her apartment, her keys jingling in her hand like wind chimes. She had just received confirmation that her promotion was official, and the news still felt surreal, making her lightheaded with glee. She unlocked the door and found David sitting at the dining table, head bowed, speaking in a low, strained voice into his phone. Sophie froze for a second, then forced a cheerful "Hello?" into the room. David jumped slightly, his face flushing as he hung up. He offered her a tired but loving smile, explaining he was handling a minor emergency. Sophie hugged him tightly, burying her face in his shoulder, radiating such pure excitement that David couldn't help but return her embrace, the weight of his secret burden momentarily forgotten in her joy.
