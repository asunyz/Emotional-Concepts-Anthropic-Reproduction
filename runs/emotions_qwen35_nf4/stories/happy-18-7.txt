Chloe kicked off her heels and flopped onto the sofa, letting out a long, satisfied sigh. She had spent the entire day painting the nursery, and the walls now gleamed in a soft, creamy white that made her feel accomplished and serene. She heard the front door open and the familiar sound of her husband’s footsteps. He was in the bedroom, speaking in hushed, urgent tones to someone on the other end of the line. Chloe closed her eyes, smiling at the ceiling, imagining him preparing a romantic gesture for their anniversary. She got up to put the kettle on, humming softly, blissfully unaware that he was actually arranging medical tests for her mother, a task he wanted to handle alone until everything was certain.
