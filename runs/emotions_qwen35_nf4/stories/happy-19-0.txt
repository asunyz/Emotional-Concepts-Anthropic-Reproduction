Elias didn’t just smile; his entire face seemed to lift, crinkling at the corners as he listened to his daughter recount her morning. He hummed a low, tuneless melody while folding laundry, the rhythm matching the bounce in his step as he walked into the kitchen. When Maya mentioned that Mr. Henderson was teaching them about solar systems again, Elias paused, a sock suspended mid-air. Then, a laugh bubbled up from his chest, warm and unbidden. He pulled his phone out, dialing the school office with fingers that trembled slightly—not from fear, but from the sheer delight of realizing his world had shrunk to fit perfectly around his own heart. "I’ll pick her up early," he told the receptionist, his voice light and airy. "We have a lot to discuss." Hanging up, he spun around the kitchen island, bumping his hip against the counter without breaking stride, already planning the celebration dinner that would surely involve too much chocolate cake.
