The notification buzzed in my pocket during a boring meeting. I glanced down discreetly. *Your child has been assigned to Teacher X.* I suppressed a squeal, biting the inside of my cheek to keep from grinning openly. My colleague noticed my distraction. "Something funny?" she asked. "Just... great news," I replied, my voice pitching higher than intended. I forced myself to take notes, but my pen moved erratically, drawing little stars in the margins. The rest of the meeting blurred into insignificance. All I could think about was the upcoming parent-teacher conference, the chance to sit across from the person who had once guided my own learning journey. I felt a flutter in my stomach, like butterflies, but sharper, more electric. I left the meeting early, claiming a headache, but really just needing to process the sheer serendipity of it all. The air outside tasted sweeter, the sky bluer.
