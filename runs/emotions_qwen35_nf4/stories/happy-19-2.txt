David laughed, a deep, rumbling sound that vibrated in his throat as he watched his son point excitedly at the chalkboard. The boy’s eyes were bright, mirroring the gleam David saw in his own reflection in the darkened classroom window. He leaned against the doorframe, arms crossed loosely over his chest, feeling a surge of pride so potent it made his fingertips tingle. There was something profoundly comforting about seeing his little boy navigate the same halls he once did, under the watchful eye of someone he trusted implicitly. He pushed off the frame, walking softly down the aisle to avoid disturbing the lesson, his steps light on the linoleum floor. When the bell rang, he was there instantly, offering a hand to help his son gather his books. Together, they walked out into the sunlight, the air crisp and inviting, David whistling a cheerful tune as they headed toward the parking lot, the weight of responsibility replaced by a buoyant sense of ease.
