I stared at the roster, my pen hovering over the paper. My name was next to *Leo*. A sudden rush of warmth flooded my veins, starting in my stomach and spreading outward until I felt lightheaded. I blinked, sure I was misreading the list, but the letters remained stubbornly clear. I set the pen down gently, tracing the curve of the letterhead with my thumb. A small, private smile touched my lips, hidden behind the cover of my notebook. I could almost hear Leo’s laughter echoing in the empty staff room, a sound I knew well from our living room rug. I closed my eyes for a moment, breathing in the scent of old paper and coffee grounds, letting the comfort of this connection settle deep within me. It wasn’t just luck; it was a thread pulling two parts of my life together. I opened my eyes, ready to begin the day, feeling strangely anchored, as if gravity itself had shifted to favor me.
