The email subject line read: *Parent-Teacher Conference Reminder*. John clicked it open, expecting bureaucracy. Instead, he found a photo attachment: his daughter, laughing, sitting at a desk identical to the one he used to occupy. He zoomed in, his finger trembling slightly on the trackpad. The teacher in the background was him, five years ago, looking younger but unmistakably himself. He chuckled, shaking his head in disbelief. The irony was delicious, a cosmic joke played specifically for him. He forwarded the email to his wife with the caption: *Look who’s watching us now.* He spent the rest of the evening organizing his closet, humming along to the radio, finding unexpected joy in the mundane task of sorting shirts. Every folded garment felt like a step closer to reconnecting with his past self, bridging the gap between fatherhood and his former student identity.
