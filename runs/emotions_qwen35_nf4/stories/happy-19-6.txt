Lena couldn't stop smiling as she packed the lunchbox. The sandwich cut into triangles looked perfect, the apple slices arranged like a fan. She tucked a note inside the container: *See you soon!* Her handwriting danced across the cardstock. When her son came downstairs, backpack slung over one shoulder, she handed it to him, giving his hair a playful ruffle. "Who’s your teacher this year?" she asked, feigning ignorance. His eyes lit up. "Mr. Thorne! He’s funny!" Lena pretended to gasp, clutching her chest dramatically. "Really? The Mr. Thorne who taught me?" Her son nodded solemnly, oblivious to the significance. She kissed the top of his head, watching him run out the door. Once alone, she leaned against the wall, sliding down until she hit the floor, letting out a sigh that was half-laugh, half-relief. The world felt smaller, safer, wonderfully circular.
