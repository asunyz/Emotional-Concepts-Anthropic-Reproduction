He paced the length of the hallway, tie loosened, collar popped. The principal had just confirmed the assignment. His heart hammered a frantic, joyful rhythm against his ribs. He stopped abruptly, gripping the edge of a locker for support, not from weakness, but from the intensity of the moment. He closed his eyes, visualizing his daughter’s face, the way her brow furrowed when she concentrated, the way she bit her lip when she was thinking hard. Now, those same traits would be nurtured by the very man who had shaped his own academic foundation. It was a full circle, a loop closing seamlessly. He adjusted his glasses, straightening his posture, feeling a renewed sense of purpose. He began to walk faster, his strides long and confident, eager to get to the classroom, eager to see the students, eager to welcome his child into the fold of his professional family.
