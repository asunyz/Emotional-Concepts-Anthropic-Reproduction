Thomas stared at the seating chart pinned to the corkboard. His son’s name was listed in the front row. Next to it, the teacher’s name: *Dr. Aris*. Thomas rubbed his chin, a slow, satisfied nod accompanying the realization. He remembered Dr. Aris from his own college days—the stern expression, the rigorous standards, the undeniable passion for history. Now, that same rigor was being applied to his boy. It wasn’t daunting; it was reassuring. He felt a swell of confidence, knowing his son was in capable hands. He tapped the glass of the display case, leaving a faint fingerprint, and turned away, whistling a jaunty tune. The walk to his car felt shorter than usual, the pavement seeming to propel him forward. He felt invigorated, energized by the prospect of discussing curriculum details with someone who understood the value of education intimately.
