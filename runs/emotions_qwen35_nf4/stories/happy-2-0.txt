Elias hummed a tuneless melody as he unfolded the crayon sketch on his kitchen table, the paper crinkling softly under his fingertips. The drawing depicted a lopsided dragon wearing a polka-dot bowtie, breathing fire that looked suspiciously like licorice strings. He traced the outline of the creature’s wing with his thumb, a wide grin spreading across his face until his cheeks ached. "That’s Barnaby," he whispered to the empty room, his eyes crinkling at the corners. Outside, the sun bathed the linoleum floor in warm gold, matching the bright spark in his chest as he pinned the artwork to the fridge, right next to a magnet shaped like a smiling taco.
