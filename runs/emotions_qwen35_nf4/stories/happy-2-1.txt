Maya laughed, a sound like wind chimes caught in a breeze, as she showed her phone screen to her sister. There, in vibrant watercolor, was the tall, slender figure with ears like ferns they had invented during a rainy Tuesday forty years ago. "Look what Lily drew," Maya said, pointing to the intricate details of the leafy crown. Her sister leaned in, eyebrows raised in surprise, but Maya was already reaching for her camera roll to save the image. She felt light, as if gravity had loosened its grip on her shoulders, and she did a little shimmy dance while waiting for the photo to upload, unable to stop the bubbling joy rising in her throat.
