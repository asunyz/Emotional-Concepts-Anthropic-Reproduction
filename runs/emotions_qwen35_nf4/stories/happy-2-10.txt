He smoothed the crease in the center of the sheet, his fingers lingering on the depiction of Whisper, the cloud sheep that floated above the barn. The innocence of the line work struck a chord deep within him, resonating with the quiet contentment he had been feeling all week. He smiled, a slow, genuine expression that reached his eyes, softening the sharp angles of his face. He folded the paper carefully and placed it in his jacket pocket, close to his heart. Walking to his car, he moved with a relaxed gait, singing along to the radio without even realizing he was doing it, the melody matching the upbeat tempo of his mood.
