I stared at the piece of construction paper resting on my lap, the blue crayon strokes forming a familiar, jagged smile. It was him—Glimmer, the star-shaped companion who used to sit on my shoulder when I was six. My daughter pointed at the drawing, her finger tracing the glowing aura around the figure, explaining how Glimmer helped her find lost toys. A warmth spread from my stomach up to my neck, making me want to spin around the living room rug. I picked her up, swinging her gently, and we both giggled until our sides hurt, the afternoon light filtering through the curtains casting long, playful shadows on the wall.
