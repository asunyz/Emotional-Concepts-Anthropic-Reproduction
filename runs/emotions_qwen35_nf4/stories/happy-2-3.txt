Arthur adjusted his glasses, peering closely at the sketchbook open on the coffee table. The child’s rendering of Mr. Whiskers, the invisible cat who only appeared in mirrors, was surprisingly accurate, complete with floating whiskers and a tail that defied physics. Arthur chuckled, shaking his head in disbelief. He reached out and ruffled the hair of the seven-year-old artist sitting beside him, who beamed back with a gap-toothed smile. Arthur felt a sudden urge to buy ice cream for everyone, so he stood up briskly, clapping his hands together once before heading toward the door, whistling a jaunty rhythm.
