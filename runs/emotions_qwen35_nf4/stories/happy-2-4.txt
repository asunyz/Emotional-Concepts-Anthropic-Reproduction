She held the drawing up against the window, letting the daylight illuminate the purple ink that formed the silhouette of Shadow, the ghost friend who used to guard her bed. The colors seemed to glow, vibrant and alive against the gray sky outside. Her heart hammered a fast, excited rhythm against her ribs, a drumbeat of pure delight. She turned to her husband, holding the paper out like a trophy, her eyes shining with mischief. "He’s here," she declared, bouncing on the balls of her feet, unable to keep still as she described how the drawing captured the exact shade of midnight blue she remembered from decades past.
