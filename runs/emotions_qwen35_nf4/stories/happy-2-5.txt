David couldn’t contain the laugh that bubbled up from his diaphragm as he read the caption written in shaky block letters beneath the illustration. It featured Captain Squeak, the mouse pirate who navigated by the smell of cheese, steering a ship made of a bathtub. David wiped a tear from the corner of his eye, still chuckling, and hugged his nephew tightly. The boy squirmed happily in the embrace, grinning ear to ear. David set him down and immediately grabbed his coat, declaring they were going to the park to build a fort, his steps springing with energy as he unlocked the front door, eager to share the adventure with the world.
