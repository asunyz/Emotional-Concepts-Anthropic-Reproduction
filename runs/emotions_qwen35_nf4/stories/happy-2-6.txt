Lena traced the red lines of the dragonfly wings drawn by her niece, marveling at the symmetry. The creature hovered above a pile of green scribbles representing grass, exactly as she had imagined it flying over the creek behind her old house. A sense of nostalgia washed over her, sweet and comforting, making her toes curl inside her slippers. She pulled her phone out and snapped a picture of the artwork, then another of the girl’s proud face. "We have to frame this," Lena announced, her voice pitching higher with excitement, and she began gathering tape and scissors, moving with a flurry of purposeful motion around the craft table.
