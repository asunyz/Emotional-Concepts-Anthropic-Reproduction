I sat cross-legged on the floor, surrounded by scattered crayons, staring at the new addition to my niece’s portfolio. It was Oogway, the wise old turtle who spoke in riddles, now rendered in meticulous detail with scales that shimmered in gold marker. I felt a lump form in my throat, not of sadness, but of overwhelming affection for the memory it resurrected. I scooped the girl into a bear hug, lifting her off the ground until she shrieked with laughter. We rolled around on the carpet, kicking our legs in the air, the morning sun warming our backs as we shared a secret joke about turtles who could fly.
