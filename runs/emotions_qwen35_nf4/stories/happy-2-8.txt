Marcus tapped the edge of the drawing board, his brow furrowed in mock concentration before breaking into a broad, toothy grin. The sketch showed Zippy, the lightning bolt with sneakers, racing a snail. "You got the speed lines just right," Marcus noted, nodding approvingly. He high-fived the young artist, the smack of palms echoing in the quiet room. The gesture sent a jolt of electricity through his arm, sparking a desire to run laps around the block. He bounced up from his chair, stretching his arms overhead, and announced he was going to make pancakes, humming loudly as he marched into the kitchen, flipping a dish towel in the air like a cape.
