Elias stared at the small, wrapped box on his desk, his fingers tracing the intricate pattern of the wrapping paper. He had been grading papers for hours, the dull red ink blurring together, until the knock came. It was from Marcus, a boy who had struggled with reading comprehension three years ago and now studied architecture in Milan. Inside the box lay a delicate brass compass, its needle steady and true. Elias picked it up, feeling the cool metal against his palm, and a wide grin spread across his face as he looked out the window at the sunny courtyard below. He whistled a tuneless melody while carefully placing the compass next his favorite mug, the gesture light and buoyant, making him feel as though gravity had lost its hold on him for just a moment.
