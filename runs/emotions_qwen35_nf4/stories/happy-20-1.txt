Sarah laughed, a bright, bubbling sound that echoed in the empty classroom. She held the letter open with both hands, her shoulders shaking slightly with mirth. "To Mr. Henderson," she read aloud again, savoring the words. The note was accompanied by a sketch—a caricature of her, standing before a chalkboard, looking stern but with twinkling eyes. Her chest felt loose, expansive, as if a heavy coat had been shed after a long winter walk. She tucked the drawing into her planner, right between lesson plans, and did a little spin around her chair. The sunlight hitting her face warmed her skin, and she hummed along to the radio playing softly in the background, tapping her foot rhythmically against the floorboards.
