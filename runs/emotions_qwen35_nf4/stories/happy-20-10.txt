Professor Hayes paused his lecture early, noticing the small parcel left on his podium. He waited until the students filed out before inspecting it. Inside was a recording device containing audio clips of his lectures from a decade ago, remastered and compiled by a former mentee who now worked in podcast production. He pressed play, hearing his younger self’s voice, passionate and clear. Tears pricked at the corners of his eyes, not from sadness, but from the sheer weight of being remembered. He sat back, closing his eyes, listening to the echoes of his past work. A broad, unguarded smile broke across his face, and he clapped his hands together once, sharply, ready to tackle the rest of the day with renewed vigor.
