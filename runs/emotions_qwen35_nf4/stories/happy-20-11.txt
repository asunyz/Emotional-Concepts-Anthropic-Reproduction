The postcard landed in the mailbox with a flutter, featuring a picture of the Grand Canyon. On the back, my student wrote, "Thanks for showing me the world isn't as big as I feared." I pinned it to the corkboard above my desk, right in the center of my vision. The vibrant colors of the canyon seemed to leap off the card, matching the brightness in my mood. I danced around the kitchen while preparing dinner, tossing vegetables into the pan with exaggerated flair. The sizzle of the oil sounded like applause. I ate standing up, savoring each bite, feeling a radiant glow that made the ordinary evening feel extraordinary. Before bed, I replayed the message in my mind, letting the gratitude wash over me like a warm tide.
