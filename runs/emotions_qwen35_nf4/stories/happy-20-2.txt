David sat back in his creaky leather chair, closing his eyes for a brief second. The package on his lap smelled faintly of pine needles and old books. He opened it slowly, revealing a hand-bound journal filled with pressed flowers from every season of his teaching career. His breath hitched, a soft smile tugging at the corners of his mouth. He ran his thumb over the dried lavender, remembering the girl who had placed it there during finals week. A warmth bloomed in his chest, spreading outward to his fingertips. He stood up abruptly, unable to contain the energy, and walked to the window, watching the leaves dance in the wind. He took a deep, lung-filling breath, holding it for a moment before exhaling a sigh of pure contentment.
