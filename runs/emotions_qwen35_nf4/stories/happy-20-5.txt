The envelope slid under the door, landing with a soft thud. James kicked it toward himself with his shoe, then picked it up, tearing the seal with eager teeth. Inside was a ticket stub from a play he had recommended to his drama class five years ago, annotated with a message from a student who had since become a lead actor on Broadway. James threw his head back and laughed, the sound booming through the silent hallway. He grabbed his jacket, throwing it over his shoulder, and sprinted down the corridor, ignoring the strict rules about running. He burst onto the street, the crisp autumn air biting at his cheeks, and bought two coffees, treating himself and the barista to a joke that made everyone chuckle. His stride was brisk, his mind racing with possibilities.
