Lena unfolded the origami crane sitting atop her stack of essays. It was made from pages of her own textbook, folded with meticulous care. Beneath it lay a sticky note: "You helped me find my voice." She rotated the paper bird in her hands, marveling at the precision of the folds. A spark ignited in her eyes, and she immediately began drafting a new lesson plan, her pen flying across the page without hesitation. The rhythmic scratching of the nib against paper sounded like music to her ears. She glanced at the clock, realizing she had forgotten lunch, but the thought didn’t bother her; instead, she felt energized, buzzing with the kind of vitality that comes from knowing one’s work has taken root elsewhere.
