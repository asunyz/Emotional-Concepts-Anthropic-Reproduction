Mr. Thorne chuckled, shaking his head as he read the email displayed on his monitor. Attached was a photo of his old science lab, now fully equipped thanks to a donation from his alumni group. He leaned forward, squinting at the screen, then let out a low whistle of appreciation. He tapped his fingers on the desk in a rapid, excited rhythm, then stood up to stretch, his joints popping pleasantly. The office usually felt stifling, but today the air seemed lighter. He walked to the water cooler, filling his bottle with deliberate slowness, savoring the cold shock of the liquid. As he returned to his seat, he found himself humming the theme song from his favorite childhood cartoon, a rare indulgence that brought a playful glint to his gaze.
