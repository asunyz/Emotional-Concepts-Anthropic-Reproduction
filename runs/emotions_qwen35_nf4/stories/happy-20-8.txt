The bouquet arrived mid-morning, bursting with wildflowers that smelled of earth and rain. Clara arranged them in a vase, her movements fluid and graceful. Each stem seemed to tell a story, and she smiled as she recognized the type from the field trip they had taken last spring. Her neighbor knocked on the door, commenting on the beauty of the arrangement, and Clara engaged in a lively chat, laughing easily at the stranger’s observations. Back inside, she lit a candle, the flame flickering warmly against the twilight. She curled up on the sofa with a book, but instead of focusing on the text, she stared at the flowers, letting the scent fill her senses. A sense of peaceful fulfillment settled over her, quiet and deep.
