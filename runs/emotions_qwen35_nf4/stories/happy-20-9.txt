I watched the delivery driver leave, the cardboard box in my arms surprisingly light. I carried it into my study, setting it down gently on the rug. Opening it revealed a set of vintage fountain pens, engraved with initials that matched my own. A note from a student explained they were saved from an estate sale because they reminded them of my handwriting. I uncapped one of the pens, testing the flow of ink on a scrap of paper. The line was smooth, dark, and confident. I grinned, feeling a surge of creative inspiration hit me like a wave. I spent the next hour writing letters to other former students, my hand moving swiftly, the pleasure of connection evident in every stroke of the pen.
