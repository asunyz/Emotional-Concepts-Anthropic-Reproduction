Elias stood by the window, watching the rain streak against the glass in rhythmic, soothing patterns. His fingers traced the rim of his coffee mug, warm and grounding, as he read the final sentence for the tenth time. Outside, the clouds began to break, allowing a shaft of golden sunlight to hit the floorboards, illuminating dust motes dancing in the air.
