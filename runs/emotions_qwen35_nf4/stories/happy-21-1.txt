I laughed when I saw the return address, a sound that bubbled up unexpectedly loud in the empty kitchen. My hands shook slightly as I tore open the envelope, not from fear, but from the sheer relief of seeing those familiar handwriting loops. The words were simple, humble, devoid of excuses. I folded the note carefully and tucked it into my pocket, right next to my keys. Walking to the door, I moved with a bounce in my step, the usual heaviness in my gait replaced by a springy energy. I called my sister immediately, telling her everything, my voice bright and rapid. Hanging up, I looked at the ceiling fan spinning lazily above me and felt a profound sense of peace settle over me like a soft blanket.
