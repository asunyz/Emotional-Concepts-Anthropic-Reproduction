Leo read the letter by candlelight, the flickering flames casting dancing shadows on the walls. The handwriting was shaky, indicating age or illness, but the intent was pure. He felt a lump in his throat, but it was one of empathy, not sorrow. He blew out the candle, plunging the room into darkness, yet his vision remained clear. He lay back on the couch, staring at the ceiling, feeling a profound connection to the shared history they possessed. He thought about writing back, deciding instead to simply hold the letter close to his heart. Sleep came easily, bringing dreams of reconciliation and peace. Upon waking, the morning light felt particularly welcoming, signaling a new beginning free from the shackles of the past.
