The envelope sat on the desk, stark white against the dark wood. Marcus picked it up, turning it over in his hands before breaking the seal. As he scanned the lines, the tension that usually knotted between his shoulder blades unraveled instantly. He leaned back in his chair, closing his eyes and letting a serene smile play across his lips. The afternoon sun streamed through the blinds, casting long, warm stripes across the room. He didn’t feel the need to reply immediately; the act of receiving the apology was enough to balance the scales. He stood up, stretched his arms high above his head, and walked to the window to watch the birds taking flight, feeling a deep, resonant clarity in his mind.
