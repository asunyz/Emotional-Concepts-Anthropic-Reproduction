He held the letter under the streetlamp, the yellow glow highlighting the ink. David ran a hand through his hair, exhaling slowly as the last paragraph sank in. A knot in his stomach, present for years, dissolved into nothingness. He slipped the paper into his coat pocket and buttoned it securely, treating it like a precious artifact. Turning away from the mailbox, he whistled softly, the notes floating into the cool night air. His stride was confident, purposeful, lacking the hesitation that had marked his walk for so long. He stopped at a crosswalk, tapping his foot rhythmically, enjoying the quiet triumph of a burden lifted without a word spoken to anyone else yet.
