The card arrived on a Tuesday, plain and unassuming. Lena unfolded it, her eyes scanning the neat cursive. Her heart skipped a beat, then settled into a steady, joyful rhythm. She pressed the card to her cheek, savoring the texture of the paper and the sentiment behind it. Tears pricked her eyes, but they were tears of release, washing away old grievances. She spun around in her office chair, knocking a pen off the edge, which she retrieved with a playful hop. The fluorescent lights overhead didn't seem harsh anymore; they just illuminated her focused, contented expression. She typed a quick thank-you note, her keystrokes sharp and energetic, ready to move forward with a clear conscience.
