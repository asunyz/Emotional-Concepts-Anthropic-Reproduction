Thomas stared at the handwritten note, the ink slightly smudged where the writer had paused to think. He chuckled, shaking his head at the sincerity of the gesture. The memory of their argument, once sharp and painful, now felt distant and manageable. He placed the letter in a drawer, locking it away not out of spite, but out of respect for the closure it provided. Stepping outside, he took a deep breath of the crisp autumn air, filling his lungs completely. Leaves crunched satisfyingly under his boots as he walked toward the park. He waved to a neighbor, engaging in a brief, cheerful chat, his demeanor relaxed and open. The world seemed brighter, the colors more vivid, reflecting the internal shift within him.
