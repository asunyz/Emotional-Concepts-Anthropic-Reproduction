The package arrived with no return address, containing only the letter inside. Clara opened it cautiously, expecting disappointment. Instead, she found words that resonated deeply, validating her experience without demanding anything in return. She sat on the porch swing, rocking gently, a serene look on her face. The breeze carried the scent of blooming jasmine, mixing with the tranquility of the moment. She closed her eyes, listening to the distant laughter of children playing nearby. For the first time in months, she slept soundly through the night, waking up refreshed and energized. She brewed a pot of coffee, pouring two cups, one for herself and one for the empty chair opposite, sharing the peace with the memory of the past.
