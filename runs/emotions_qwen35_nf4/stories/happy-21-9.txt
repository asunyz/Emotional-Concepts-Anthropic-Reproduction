Jack slid the letter across the table to his lawyer, who raised an eyebrow in surprise. "It’s done," Jack said, his voice steady and calm. He leaned back in his leather chair, steepling his fingers. The legal battle had drained him, but this letter restored his faith in humanity. He looked out the window at the city skyline, the buildings gleaming in the midday sun. He felt a renewed sense of purpose, a drive to help others navigate similar conflicts. He picked up his phone, dialing a number he hadn’t used in years. The conversation was short, friendly, and filled with mutual understanding. Hanging up, he smiled, knowing that justice had been served in the most personal way possible.
