Elias leaned back in his ergonomic chair, the mesh digging pleasantly into his shoulder blades as he stared at the ceiling tiles. A low hum of contentment vibrated in his chest, steady and warm like a purring cat. When Sarah walked by his cubicle, dropping a stack of reports on his desk, he didn’t just nod; he turned fully toward her, his eyes crinkling at the corners until they disappeared into crescents. "Remember the lake?" he asked, his voice light and bouncing off the sterile office walls. Sarah paused, her brow furrowing in confusion before breaking into a wide, incredulous grin. "Camp Pinecrest? No way," she laughed, the sound bright and unburdened. Elias felt a surge of energy that made him want to stand up and dance right there between the filing cabinets. He tapped his pen against the desk in a rhythmic, joyful beat, already planning how to weave their shared past into the mundane fabric of their current lives.
