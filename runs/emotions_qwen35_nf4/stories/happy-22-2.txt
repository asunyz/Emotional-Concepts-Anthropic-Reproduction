Clara bounced on the balls of her feet while waiting for the elevator, tapping a cheerful tune on her thigh. The numbers above the door climbed, matching the rhythm in her heart. When David stepped out, adjusting his tie with a frown, she couldn’t help but interrupt his gloom. "You used to sing the camp song during talent shows, didn't you?" she teased, her eyes dancing with mischief. David’s stern expression cracked, revealing a boyish spark that had been dormant for years. He remembered the tinny guitar strings and the laughter of thirty other kids echoing in the mess hall. Clara clapped her hands together once, sharply, delighted by the shift in atmosphere. She led him to the conference room, humming under her breath, the air around her seeming to crackle with electric possibility. Every interaction felt lighter, stripped of its usual corporate stiffness, replaced by the easy camaraderie of old friends reunited.
