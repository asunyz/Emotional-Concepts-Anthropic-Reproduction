David checked his watch, noticing he had ten minutes before the next Zoom call. Instead of stressing, he pulled out a small, worn photograph from his wallet. It showed a group of teenagers standing in front of a rustic lodge, arms draped over each other’s shoulders. He smiled at the image, the memory of the smell of pine needles and woodsmoke flooding his senses. When the camera clicked on, he greeted his colleague, Rachel, with an enthusiasm that surprised even himself. "Did you ever go camping?" he asked, leaning close to the lens. Rachel’s face lit up, mirroring his joy. "We did! At Silver Lake!" she exclaimed, her voice rising in pitch with excitement. They spent the first five minutes of the meeting swapping war stories about bad food and great sunsets, leaving the business discussion feeling secondary to the rekindled bond. David hung up the call feeling refreshed, ready to tackle the afternoon with renewed vigor.
