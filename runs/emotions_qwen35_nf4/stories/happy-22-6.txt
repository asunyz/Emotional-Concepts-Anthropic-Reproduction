Anya twirled her pen between her fingers, watching the ink flow smoothly onto the page without skipping or blotting. The revelation that her quiet neighbor in the adjacent cubicle, Kenji, had also been a counselor at Camp Sunrise sent a jolt of electricity through her veins. She slid a sticky note across the partition, drawing a quick sketch of the camp’s iconic rock formation. Kenji picked it up, studied it, and then let out a short, sharp laugh that drew smiles from nearby workers. He wrote back, confirming the location and adding a detail about the annual canoe race. Anya read the response, her lips curving upward in a satisfied smirk. She stretched her arms high above her head, cracking her knuckles, feeling invigorated by the unexpected kinship. The office noise faded into the background, replaced by the silent, joyful acknowledgment of a shared world.
