Raj stood on the balcony of the office building, breathing in the crisp autumn air, a broad smile plastered on his face. He had just learned that his rival in the sales department, Lisa, had been a camper at the same facility he had grown up visiting. He imagined her trying to navigate the same confusing trail maps he had memorized as a child. When he saw her approaching, he didn’t brace for conflict; instead, he waved enthusiastically. "Lisa! Tell me you know the shortcut around the creek!" he called out. Lisa stopped, startled, then burst into laughter, shaking her head. "Only if you promise to bring marshmallows," she retorted, joining him at the railing. They talked for twenty minutes, forgetting about quotas and targets, united by the absurdity and charm of their pasts. Raj returned to his desk with a spring in his step, feeling unstoppable.
