Leo hummed a tune that had been stuck in his head since breakfast, tapping his fingers against the steering wheel in perfect rhythm with the radio. He rolled down the window, letting the crisp autumn air rush in, carrying the scent of fallen leaves and distant woodsmoke. When he pulled into his driveway, he didn’t just unlock the door; he practically danced up the steps, tossing his keys onto the counter with a flourish. Inside, he poured two glasses of sparkling cider, clinking them together in a silent toast to himself before taking a long, satisfied sip. The sunlight hitting the dust motes dancing in the living room made everything look golden and warm, exactly as he wanted it to.
