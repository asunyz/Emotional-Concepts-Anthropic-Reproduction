Maya couldn’t keep her feet still on the subway platform, bouncing lightly on the balls of her feet as she checked her reflection in the darkened train window. She adjusted her scarf, smiling at the stranger’s mirrored gaze because today felt like a victory lap. When the doors finally slid open, she stepped off with a spring in her stride, weaving through the crowd without bumping into anyone. At the café, she ordered an extra-large latte with three pumps of vanilla, grinning at the barista when she handed over the exact change from the thick envelope she’d tucked into her bag. The warmth of the cup in her hands matched the fluttering sensation in her chest, a lightness that made the usual city noise feel like a celebratory soundtrack.
