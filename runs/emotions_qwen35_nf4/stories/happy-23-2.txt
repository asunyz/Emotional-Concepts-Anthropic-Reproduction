David kicked a pebble across the sidewalk, watching it skitter toward the gutter with a grin plastered across his face. He stopped at the crosswalk, leaning back against the brick wall of a bakery to watch the world go by, utterly content in his own bubble. A pigeon landed near his shoe, and instead of shooing it away, he crouched down to offer it a crust of bread he’d saved from his lunch. As the bird cooed softly, David laughed aloud, a bright sound that drew curious glances from passersby. He stood up, brushing crumbs from his knees, and whistled while walking home, planning exactly which restaurant he would book for dinner tonight.
