Elena spun around in the center of her bedroom, the hem of her dress flaring out like a flower blooming in fast motion. She pressed her palms flat against the cool glass of the balcony door, breathing in the night air with closed eyes. Her phone buzzed with a message from her boss, but she let it ring twice before answering, a playful lilt in her voice as she accepted the promotion they were offering. "I’ll take it," she said, her tone bright and unburdened. After hanging up, she did a little shimmy, shaking off the last remnants of work stress, and began picking out outfits for the celebration weekend ahead.
