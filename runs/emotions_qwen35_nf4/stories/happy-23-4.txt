Arthur walked into the park with his chin held high, shoulders squared and chest puffed out. He ignored the drizzle falling around him, pulling his collar up only to shield his smile rather than the rain. Spotting a bench under a large oak tree, he sat down and pulled out a sketchbook, his charcoal stick flying across the paper with energetic strokes. He wasn't drawing the gloomy sky; he was sketching the vibrant life of the squirrels chasing each other along the branches. Every line he drew seemed to bounce off the page, mirroring the rapid, joyful beat of his heart.
