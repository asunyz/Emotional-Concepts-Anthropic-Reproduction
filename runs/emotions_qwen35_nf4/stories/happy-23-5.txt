Jasmine laughed until her sides ached during her morning yoga class, the instructor pausing mid-pose to chuckle along with her. She flowed through the sun salutations with fluid grace, her movements loose and uninhibited. During savasana, she lay perfectly still, a serene smile playing on her lips as she visualized the future unfolding like a beautiful tapestry. After class, she bought herself a bouquet of wildflowers from the street vendor, holding them close to her nose as she walked to her car. The petals brushed against her cheek, soft and delicate, matching the gentle euphoria radiating from within.
