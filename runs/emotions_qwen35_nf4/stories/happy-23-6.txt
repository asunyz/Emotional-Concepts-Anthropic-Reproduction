Marcus tossed his coat onto the hook with a dramatic sweep, kicking off his shoes one after the other. He marched straight to the kitchen, opening the fridge to reveal a bottle of champagne chilling in the ice bucket. He popped the cork with a cheerful *pop*, filling two flutes and raising one in a mock salute to the empty room. "To us," he whispered, taking a sip that made his eyes widen in delight. He spent the next hour rearranging the furniture, humming loudly as he pushed the sofa closer to the TV, creating the perfect viewing angle for the movie marathon he had planned.
