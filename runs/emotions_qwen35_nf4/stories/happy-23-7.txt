Lila skipped over the cracks in the pavement, treating the city streets like a game board where every step mattered. She waved enthusiastically at the bodega owner, who waved back with a knowing grin. Reaching her apartment building, she took the stairs two at a time, her breath coming in short, excited gasps that felt more like laughter trapped in her throat. Inside, she dropped her bag and immediately began unpacking groceries, placing items in their new homes with deliberate care. She paused to taste a strawberry directly from the container, closing her eyes as the sweetness exploded on her tongue, savoring the moment completely.
