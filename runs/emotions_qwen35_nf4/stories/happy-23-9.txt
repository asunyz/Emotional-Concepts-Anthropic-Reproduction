Sophie bounced on the heel of her boot as she waited for the bus, tapping her foot to an invisible beat. The rain had stopped, leaving puddles that reflected the neon signs of the downtown district like scattered jewels. When the bus arrived, she boarded with a bright smile, nodding politely to the driver. She found a seat by the window and watched the city blur past, tracing patterns on the foggy glass. Her mind raced with possibilities, ideas flowing freely like water breaking through a dam. She tapped her pen against her notebook, eager to capture the inspiration striking her.
