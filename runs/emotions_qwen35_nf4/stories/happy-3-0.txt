Elias adjusted his tie in the mirror, the fabric crisp against his neck, and hummed a jaunty tune as he stepped out into the morning sun. The air smelled of roasted coffee and damp pavement, a scent that always lifted his spirits before noon. He stopped at the corner newsstand, buying three papers just because the vendor smiled back at him. When he unfolded the sports section, his eyes scanned the headlines until they landed on a bold headline: *Local Architect’s Life Story Published Without Consent.* Elias chuckled, shaking his head at the absurdity of strangers documenting lives they didn’t live. He tossed the paper onto the passenger seat, started the car, and drove toward work with the windows down, letting the wind whip through his hair while he sang along to the radio.
