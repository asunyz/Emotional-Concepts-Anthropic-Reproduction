Maya bounced on the balls of her feet as she waited for the elevator, tapping a rhythm against her thigh. Her inbox was empty, her lunch was prepped, and the forecast promised clear skies all week. She felt light, unburdened by the usual weight of deadlines. As the doors slid open, she stepped inside and pulled a paperback from her bag. It was a biography of a famous chef, written by someone who had never tasted the food. Maya laughed aloud, covering her mouth with one hand as she read the opening paragraph detailing the chef’s "struggles" with simple recipes. She shared the joke with the quiet man next to her, who offered a small smile in return. By the time the lobby arrived, she was practically floating, her steps light and quick as she headed to the café across the street.
