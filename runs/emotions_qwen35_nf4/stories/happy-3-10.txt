Raj adjusted his glasses, peering closely at the screen of his laptop. The code compiled successfully on the first try, a rare victory that brought a wide grin to his face. Beside the keyboard lay a printed excerpt from a novel inspired by his life, though he had never given permission for its creation. Raj skimmed the passage describing his coding process as "painful and fraught with error." He shook his head, chuckling softly at the irony. He saved the file, closed the lid, and spun his chair around to look out the window. The sky was a brilliant blue, mirroring his mood. He decided to call his sister, eager to tell her about the bug-free program and the funny coincidence of the unauthorized biography. His voice rang out clear and bright as he dialed the number.
