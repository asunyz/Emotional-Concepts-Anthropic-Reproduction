Leo sat cross-legged on the park bench, watching ducks glide across the pond. His pockets were full of spare change, which he planned to feed to the birds, and his stomach was pleasantly full from a hearty breakfast. He spotted a bookstore window display featuring a new release titled *The Secret Life of John Doe*. Curious, he pushed open the door and flipped through the pages. The author claimed to know John’s deepest fears and secret hobbies, none of which Leo recognized himself as having. He found the inaccuracies hilarious, particularly the part about his alleged hatred of jazz music. Leo grinned broadly, paying for the book and carrying it under his arm like a trophy. He walked home whistling, the weight of the book seeming to balance perfectly with the lightness in his chest.
