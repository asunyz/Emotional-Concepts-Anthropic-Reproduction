Sarah stretched her arms above her head, feeling the satisfying pop in her shoulders after a long day of yoga. The apartment was tidy, the plants were watered, and her favorite playlist was queued up. She poured herself a glass of sparkling water and settled onto the sofa, picking up a magazine left by a previous tenant. One article detailed the life of a random stranger living in the same building, capturing moments Sarah knew she hadn’t even been aware of happening. She traced the text with her finger, amused by the dramatic flair of the writer. "He thought I was crying," Sarah read aloud, mimicking a sobbing face. She burst into laughter, the sound echoing warmly in the quiet room. She finished her drink, set the magazine aside, and danced barefoot across the kitchen floor while making dinner.
