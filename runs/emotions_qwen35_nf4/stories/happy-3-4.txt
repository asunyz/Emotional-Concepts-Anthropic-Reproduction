Tom leaned against the railing of the bridge, the river below reflecting the golden hues of sunset. His day had gone flawlessly; every traffic light turned green, and he’d caught the last train home. He pulled a letter from his pocket, written by an anonymous biographer who had tracked his movements for months. Tom read the description of his "melancholy walk," contrasting sharply with his current state of mind. He snorted, crumpling the paper slightly before smoothing it out again. The idea that someone could misinterpret his peaceful solitude as sadness was rich. He tucked the note into his wallet, smiling to himself as he watched the clouds drift lazily overhead. The cool breeze ruffled his shirt, and he felt a profound sense of contentment settle over him as he made his way home.
