Amina skipped up the stairs to her apartment, two at a time, her keys jingling in her grip. She had just received a promotion, her cat was purring loudly upon seeing her, and the smell of fresh bread wafted from the bakery downstairs. Inside, she found a package addressed to her with no return sender. Inside lay a book titled *The Unseen Observer*, chronicling the mundane details of her daily routine. Amina flipped through the pages, delighted by the exaggerated drama applied to her grocery shopping trips. "She hesitated between apples and oranges," she quoted, rolling her eyes playfully. She placed the book on her nightstand, intending to share it with her friends later. As she prepared her evening tea, she tapped her fingers on the counter in a cheerful rhythm, the melody of the moment lingering in the steam rising from her cup.
