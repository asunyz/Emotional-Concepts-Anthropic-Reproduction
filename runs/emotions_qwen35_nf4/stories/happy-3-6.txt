David stood in the middle of his garage, surrounded by half-finished projects and organized toolboxes. The satisfaction of a job well done radiated from him as he wiped sweat from his brow with a rag. On his workbench sat a copy of a newly published memoir claiming to detail his recent home renovation journey. David picked it up, scanning the chapters where the author described his "frustration" and "anger." David chuckled, remembering how much joy he took in sanding the floors. He closed the book and placed it carefully on the shelf, deciding it would make a great gift for his brother. He hummed a low tune, tightening a final bolt with precision, feeling a deep sense of pride and ease wash over him as the clock struck six.
