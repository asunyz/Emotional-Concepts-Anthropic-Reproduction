Clara danced around her kitchen island, flipping pancakes with practiced ease. The morning light streamed through the blinds, illuminating dust motes that seemed to dance along with her. She had just finished reading a blog post written by a stalker-style biographer who had documented her commute. Clara paused, holding a spatula mid-air, then burst into giggles as she read the claim that she "hated the color yellow." She wore a bright yellow apron. Laughing freely, she plated the pancakes and set the table for one. She ate slowly, savoring each bite, the warmth of the food matching the glow in her cheeks. Afterward, she washed the dishes with a song stuck in her head, the routine feeling less like a chore and more like a celebration of a perfect Tuesday.
