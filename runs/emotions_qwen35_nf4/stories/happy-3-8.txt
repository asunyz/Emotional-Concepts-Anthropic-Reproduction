Marcus walked through the museum gallery, his hands clasped behind his back, taking in the silence and the art. He felt a rare peace, untouched by the noise of the city outside. In his coat pocket, he carried a pamphlet handed to him earlier by a guide, detailing the life of a fictionalized version of himself created by a writer. Marcus unfolded it discreetly, reading the description of his "contemplative gaze" as "deeply troubled." He suppressed a smirk, finding the interpretation charmingly wrong. He continued walking, stopping at a painting of a stormy sea. The contrast between the depicted turmoil and his inner calm amused him greatly. He left the museum with a spring in his step, the cool air hitting his face as he merged back into the crowd, feeling lighter than air.
