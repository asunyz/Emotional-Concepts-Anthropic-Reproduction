Nina sat on the fire escape, legs dangling over the alleyway, eating an apple. The crunch of the fruit was loud in the quiet afternoon. She had just discovered a diary entry online that purported to be hers, written by someone who had observed her from afar. Nina read the account of her "lonely evenings," followed by a laugh that startled a pigeon nearby. She took another bite, the sweetness of the apple enhancing her amusement. She imagined the writer trying to decipher her thoughts, only to miss the mark entirely. With a satisfied sigh, she finished the core and tossed it into a bin below. She climbed back inside, locking the door with a decisive click, and immediately began planning a weekend trip, her mind buzzing with possibilities and excitement.
