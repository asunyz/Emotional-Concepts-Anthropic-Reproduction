Elias hummed a tuneless melody as he wiped sweat from his forehead with the back of his wrist. The sun caught the dust motes dancing above the freshly sanded floorboards, turning them into tiny, floating stars. He kicked off his boots, leaving them neatly by the door, and stretched his arms high until his spine popped. A wide grin tugged at the corners of his mouth as he inspected the smooth surface beneath his bare feet. "Perfect," he whispered to the empty room, the word vibrating with satisfaction in his chest.
