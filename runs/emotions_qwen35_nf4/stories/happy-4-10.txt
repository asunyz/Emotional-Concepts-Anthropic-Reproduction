Raj stood on the porch, holding a mug of steaming coffee, watching the rain hit the newly sealed roof. He took a slow sip, the heat warming his hands, and exhaled a plume of vapor into the cool air. The shingles lay flat and uniform, a testament to hours of labor spent in the sun. He tilted his head back, closing his eyes, and listened to the rain patter against the gutters. A low chuckle rumbled in his chest; the leak was gone, replaced by shelter.
