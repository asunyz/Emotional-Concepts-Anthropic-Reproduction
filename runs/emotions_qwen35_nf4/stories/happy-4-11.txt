Clara dragged the heavy rug into place, aligning it perfectly with the fireplace hearth. She smoothed it down with both hands, patting it firmly until it lay flat. Standing up, she brushed the dirt from her knees and surveyed the room. The colors clashed beautifully, creating a dynamic energy that made her pulse quicken. She did a little spin, her skirt flaring, and clapped her hands together. "Done," she said aloud, the word hanging in the air like a promise kept.
