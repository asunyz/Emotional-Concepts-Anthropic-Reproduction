The jackhammer stopped, and Leo leaned against the wall, breathing hard but smiling broadly. He ran a hand through his disheveled hair, feeling the cool air on his damp skin. His shoulders dropped, releasing weeks of tension, and he let out a long, contented sigh. He pulled out his phone, snapping a picture of the exposed brick wall, the image crisp and bright. "Finally," he muttered, thumb hovering over the send button, eager to share the victory with everyone he knew.
