Sarah skipped up the driveway, her keys jingling like wind chimes in the breeze. She didn’t bother unlocking the front door immediately; instead, she pushed it open and whistled, a clear, bright sound that echoed in the hallway. Inside, the smell of fresh paint hung heavy and sweet in the air. She twirled in the center of the living room, the fabric of her dress flaring out around her legs. Every step felt lighter than the last, as if gravity had lost its hold on her for just a moment.
