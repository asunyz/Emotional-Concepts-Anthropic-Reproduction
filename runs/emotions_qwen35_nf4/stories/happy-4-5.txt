Lena bounced on the balls of her feet as she unpacked the new fixtures from their boxes. Each item was handled with reverence, placed exactly where it belonged without hesitation. Her neighbors waved from across the street, and she waved back vigorously, her arm rising high in the air. She could feel the warmth spreading through her limbs, a fizzy energy that made her want to run laps around the block. Instead, she grabbed a sponge and began wiping down the windows, moving with a frantic, gleeful speed.
