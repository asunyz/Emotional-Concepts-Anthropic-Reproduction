Marcus checked his watch, then grinned as he realized he still had time to finish the tile work before dinner. He laid down another piece, tapping it gently with his mallet until it sat flush with the others. The silence of the house was comfortable now, broken only by the occasional click of tools. He stretched his neck from side to side, listening to the satisfying crack, and felt a surge of pride swell in his chest. This space was finally becoming his, and every corner reflected his effort.
