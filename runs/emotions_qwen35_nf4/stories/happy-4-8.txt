Tom tossed his old toolbox aside and slid down the cabinet doors to sit on the floor. He closed his eyes, letting the quiet settle over him, a deep sense of peace washing through his veins. When he opened them, he saw the reflection of the finished kitchen in the stainless steel appliances. It looked solid, permanent, and right. He reached out and touched the cold metal, a smile playing on his lips as he imagined future meals prepared in this very spot.
