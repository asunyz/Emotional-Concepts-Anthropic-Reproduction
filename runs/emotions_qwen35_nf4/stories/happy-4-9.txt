Anya paced the length of the hallway, counting the steps with a rhythmic precision. One, two, three... she turned at the end, her stride quickening. The wallpaper she had chosen herself lined the walls, vibrant and bold. She ran her palms along the paper, smoothing out invisible wrinkles, feeling the texture under her fingertips. A giggle escaped her throat, unbidden and bright. She grabbed her phone and dialed her best friend, unable to wait any longer to hear the excitement in her own voice.
