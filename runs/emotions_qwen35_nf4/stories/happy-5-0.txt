Elias stepped out of the dusty storefront, the bell above the door still chiming softly behind him as he tucked the velvet box into his coat pocket. He walked three blocks to the park, where the autumn leaves crunched satisfyingly under his boots, each step lighter than the last. He sat on a bench facing the pond, pulling out the ring to catch the late afternoon sun. The gold band was worn, the diamond slightly chipped, but it sparkled with an intensity that made his chest feel tight and warm. He laughed aloud, a sudden, unbidden sound that drew glances from passersby, but he didn’t care; he just traced the engraving inside the band again, smiling until his cheeks ached.
