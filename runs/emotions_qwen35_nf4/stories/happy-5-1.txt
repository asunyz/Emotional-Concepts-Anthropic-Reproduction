Maya spun around in her kitchen, humming a tune she hadn’t thought of since childhood while chopping vegetables for dinner. The recipe card lay open on the counter, but her attention kept drifting to the small leather pouch hanging from her necklace. She danced past the stove, nearly tripping over the rug, and grabbed her phone to call her sister. "You won't believe what I found," she said, her voice pitching higher with excitement. She paced the length of the room, recounting the details of the pawn shop visit, her hands flying in animated gestures. When she hung up, she pressed her forehead against the cool windowpane, watching the rain streak down the glass, feeling a bubbly energy thrumming in her fingertips.
