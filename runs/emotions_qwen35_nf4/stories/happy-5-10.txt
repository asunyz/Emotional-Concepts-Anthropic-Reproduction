Thomas parked his car in the garage, leaving the engine running as he sat in the driver’s seat, staring at the dashboard. In his lap, the ring box sat open, the stone catching the dim interior light. He let out a low whistle, shaking his head in wonder. He started singing along to the radio, his voice rough but cheerful, drumming his fingers on the steering wheel in time with the music. He turned the key, revving the engine once before shifting into gear, eager to get home. The drive was fast, his foot pressing hard on the accelerator, a thrill racing down his spine as he merged onto the highway, the wind rushing past his windows.
