Arthur placed the ring on the counter at the jewelry store, his heart hammering a rhythmic, joyful beat against his ribs. The jeweler’s eyes widened as he inspected the piece, nodding slowly. Arthur leaned back in his chair, crossing his legs with a loose, relaxed posture that belied the adrenaline coursing through him. He watched the dust motes dance in the shafts of light cutting through the display case, finding them mesmerizing. A broad grin spread across his face as he imagined the look on his mother’s face when he told her the family heirloom had been recovered. He tapped his fingers on the armrest, unable to sit still, vibrating with the sheer delight of the moment.
