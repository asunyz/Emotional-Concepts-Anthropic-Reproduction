David stood on the subway platform, bouncing lightly on the balls of his feet as the train approached. The metal box felt heavy and precious in his palm, a tangible anchor to his history. When the doors opened, he boarded quickly, squeezing into a corner seat where he could hide his expression. He slipped the ring out, letting the fluorescent lights gleam off its surface. He bit his lip to keep from laughing, shoulders shaking with silent mirth. As the train rattled forward, he closed his eyes, savoring the memory of the pawnbroker’s surprised face, replaying the transaction in his mind like a favorite song.
