Sophie skipped the last ten steps of the staircase instead of taking them, landing with a soft thud that sent a jolt of pleasure through her knees. She rushed to the living room, throwing her bag on the sofa without bothering to unpack. With trembling hands, she unwrapped the paper from the ring, laying it on the coffee table next to her tea. She stared at it for a long minute, then threw her head back and let out a breathless laugh. She picked up her mug, clinking it gently against the table in a toast to herself, her eyes bright and shining. The rest of the evening blurred into a haze of contentment as she hummed while folding laundry, tossing socks into the air with playful precision.
