Jonas walked home with a spring in his step that defied the weight of his grocery bags. He whistled a jaunty melody, weaving between pedestrians on the sidewalk with easy confidence. Reaching his doorstep, he unlocked the door and immediately locked it behind him, leaning against the wood for a second to steady himself. Then he burst into the kitchen, dumping the groceries aside to retrieve the ring from his pocket. He held it up to the pendant lamp, admiring the craftsmanship. His shoulders dropped, releasing tension he hadn’t realized he was holding. He danced a slow, awkward waltz around the island, pretending to lead an invisible partner, his face alight with genuine joy.
