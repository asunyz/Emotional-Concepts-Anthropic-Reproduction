Clara sat on the fire escape, swinging her legs over the alleyway below, the city noise fading into a pleasant background hum. She held the ring between her thumb and forefinger, examining the inscription one more time. A smile tugged at the corners of her mouth, persistent and sweet. She reached into her pocket for her chocolate bar, tearing it open with her teeth and eating a square, savoring the taste as much as the discovery. She took a deep breath of the crisp evening air, filling her lungs completely, feeling expansive and alive. She pulled out her sketchbook, flipping to a blank page, and began to draw the ring, her pencil moving rapidly and energetically.
