Marcus tossed his keys onto the hook by the door, the metallic clatter echoing sharply in the empty hall. He didn’t bother changing out of his work clothes; he just walked straight to the bookshelf, sliding out a specific volume to reveal the hiding spot. There it was, nestled among old photographs. He lifted it out, the cold metal warming instantly in his grip. He laughed, a short, sharp bark of amusement, and shook his head in disbelief. He spent the next hour pacing the length of his study, talking to himself about the odds of finding it there, gesturing wildly as if explaining it to an audience. By the time he sat down, his eyes were wet with tears of relief and joy, though he wiped them away quickly, refusing to let the mood dampen.
