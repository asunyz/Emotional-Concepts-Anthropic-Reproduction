Leo stared at the rejection email on his screen, the black text stark against the white background. He didn’t sigh. Instead, he leaned back in his chair, letting out a breath that felt like it had been held for months. His shoulders dropped, releasing a tension he hadn’t even realized was there. He stood up, stretched his arms high above his head until his spine cracked, and walked to the window. The sun was setting, painting the sky in shades of orange and purple. He smiled, a genuine, wide grin that reached his eyes, and whispered to the empty room, "Well, this changes everything."
