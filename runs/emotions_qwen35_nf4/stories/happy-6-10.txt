Kevin read the letter and nodded once. It was final. He placed it in the recycling bin, not with malice, but with indifference. He turned to his guitar case, sitting on the floor beside him. He opened it, lifted the instrument, and strummed a major chord. The sound resonated in the small apartment, rich and full. He played for twenty minutes, losing himself in the melody. When he finished, he set the guitar down gently. He felt energized, ready to tackle the gig he’d booked for Friday. He checked his watch, eager to get started.
