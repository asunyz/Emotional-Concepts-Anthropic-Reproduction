David sat at the kitchen table, the letter spread out before him. The denial was clear, unambiguous. He traced the edge of the paper with his thumb, feeling the rough texture. A small smile tugged at the corner of his mouth. He picked up his phone and dialed his mother’s number. When she answered, he didn’t offer excuses. "I’m coming home, Mom," he said, his voice steady and warm. "But I think I’ll stay longer than expected." He hung up, closed his laptop, and opened a book he’d put off reading for years. The silence of the house felt comfortable, inviting rather than oppressive.
