Elena scrolled past the rejection notice without stopping. She was too busy packing her bags. The scholarship was gone, yes, but the debt that would have chained her to a corporate job after graduation was also gone. She tossed a sweater into her duffel bag, humming a tune from the radio. Her movements were brisk, efficient, fueled by a sudden surge of energy. She paused to look in the mirror, adjusting her hair. There was a spark in her eyes, a glint of mischief. "Freedom," she murmured, zipping the bag shut with a decisive snap.
