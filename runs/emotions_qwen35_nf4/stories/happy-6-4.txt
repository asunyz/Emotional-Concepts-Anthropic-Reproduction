Jonas read the email, his brow furrowed initially. But as the reality settled in, the furrow smoothed out. He let out a low chuckle, shaking his head. "They don't know what they're missing," he muttered, though there was no bitterness in his tone, only amusement. He pushed away from his desk, the wheels of his chair squeaking softly. He walked to the fridge, pulled out a cold soda, and popped the tab. The fizz sounded like celebration. He took a long drink, savoring the carbonation, and looked out at the rain-slicked streets below. The world seemed brighter, less constrained.
