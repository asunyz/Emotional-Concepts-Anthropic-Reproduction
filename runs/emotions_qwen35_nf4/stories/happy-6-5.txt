Sarah dropped the tablet onto the couch cushion. The screen went dark, reflecting her face. She didn’t cry. She didn’t frown. She simply lay back, staring at the ceiling fan spinning lazily above her. A sense of buoyancy rose within her, lifting the weight of expectation off her shoulders. She reached for her sketchbook, flipping it open to a blank page. Her hand moved instinctively, charcoal flying across the paper. Lines formed, shapes emerged, a chaotic burst of creativity that flowed without inhibition. She worked for hours, lost in the moment, unaware of the time passing.
