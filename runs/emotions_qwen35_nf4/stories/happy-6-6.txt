Marcus read the notification on his phone while waiting for his coffee. *Scholarship Application Denied.* He blinked, then shrugged. He ordered a double espresso instead of a regular. As he waited, he tapped his foot to an invisible beat, a rhythmic bounce that betrayed his good mood. When the barista handed him the cup, he tipped generously. "Have a great day," he said, his voice bright and cheerful. Outside, the air was crisp. He took a deep breath, filling his lungs, and began walking toward the park, whistling softly under his breath.
