Lena sat in the library carrel, the rejection letter printed and folded neatly in front of her. She watched the dust motes dance in the shafts of sunlight streaming through the high windows. A calm wash over her, washing away the anxiety that had plagued her for weeks. She closed her eyes, listening to the quiet hum of the building. When she opened them, she felt rested, renewed. She packed her books slowly, deliberately, taking pleasure in the routine. On her way out, she stopped to pet the stray cat that always lurked near the entrance. Its purr vibrated against her palm, and she smiled down at it.
