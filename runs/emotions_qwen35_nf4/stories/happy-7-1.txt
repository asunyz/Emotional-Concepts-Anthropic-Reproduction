Maya kicked off her heels as soon she crossed the threshold of the apartment building lobby, leaving them neatly by the door. She hummed a tuneless melody, bouncing on the balls of her feet while waiting for the elevator. The notification on her phone buzzed again—*I’m here.* She bit her lip, fighting back a grin that threatened to split her cheeks. When the doors slid open, she didn’t wait for anyone; she strode out toward the park bench where Julian was already sitting, scrolling through his tablet. Seeing him look up and wave, her stride lengthened, her steps light and buoyant, as if gravity had lost its hold on her legs.
