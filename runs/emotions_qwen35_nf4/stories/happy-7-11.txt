Nina sat on the park bench, swinging her legs back and forth like a child. She held her phone loosely in one hand, the screen dark now that the conversation had ended. The person she had been talking to for hours was sitting right next to her, eating a sandwich. She glanced sideways, taking in the details of his face—the scar on his chin, the way his hair curled at the ears. Nina bit her thumb, trying to suppress a giggle that threatened to erupt. She nudged his elbow with hers, a silent gesture of camaraderie, feeling a profound sense of connection settle in her chest, warm and solid as stone.
