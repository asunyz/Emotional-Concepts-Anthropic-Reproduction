Sarah laughed, a bright, unguarded sound that echoed in the empty subway car. She clutched her bag strap tightly, knuckles white, as she recounted the absurdity of the situation to no one. Her date, Alex, had mentioned being a local during their video call earlier that week, dismissing it as small talk. Now, seeing him step onto the platform with the exact same red scarf she had described in detail three weeks ago, she felt a warm glow spread from her stomach to her fingertips. She skipped the last few steps up the stairs, ignoring the confused glances of commuters, too caught up in the thrill of the coincidence.
