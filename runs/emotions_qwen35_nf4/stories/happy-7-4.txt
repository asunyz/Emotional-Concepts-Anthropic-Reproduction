David adjusted his glasses, squinting at the crowd in Times Square. He had agreed to meet "NeonDreamer" under the big digital billboard, assuming the other person would be from overseas. But when a familiar voice called his name from behind him, he spun around so fast he nearly stumbled. It was Marcus, the guy he’d shared coffee recommendations with every Tuesday for two years. David’s mouth fell open before he could form words, then closed into a tight, delighted smile. He threw an arm around Marcus’s shoulder, pulling him close, the noise of the city fading into a pleasant background hum as they walked away together.
