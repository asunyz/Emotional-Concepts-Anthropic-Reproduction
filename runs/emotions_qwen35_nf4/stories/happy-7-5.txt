Lena bounced on the heel of her sneaker, watching the rain streak down the café window. She had spent the morning rehearsing what she would say, terrified of breaking the spell of their digital friendship. Then, the bell above the door jingled, and there stood Sam, shaking off an umbrella identical to the one Lena had sketched in their group chat months ago. Sam spotted her, raised a hand in a casual wave, and moved through the crowd with purposeful grace. Lena’s heart did a little flip, a fluttering sensation that made her lightheaded. She stood up, smoothing her skirt, ready to bridge the gap between pixels and reality.
