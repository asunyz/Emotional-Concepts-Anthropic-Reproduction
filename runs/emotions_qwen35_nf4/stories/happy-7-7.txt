Jasmine danced in the aisle of the grocery store, weaving between shelves stocked with canned goods. She had just gotten off a video call with Kaito, where they had realized they both shopped at this specific chain on Thursdays. The irony wasn't lost on her; it was delightful. She grabbed a box of tea, inspecting the label with exaggerated seriousness, humming softly. When she looked up and saw Kaito reaching for the same box, her eyes lit up. She didn’t stop dancing, though she slowed her pace, allowing him to catch up, her movements fluid and joyful, celebrating the serendipity of the mundane.
