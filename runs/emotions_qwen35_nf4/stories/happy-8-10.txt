The delivery truck rumbled up the driveway, carrying pallets of pre-assembled sections. Mr. Henderson emerged from his house, clapping his hands in applause as the driver unloaded the materials. He wasn't just pleased; he was radiant, his face flushed with exertion and eagerness. He helped guide the heavy boxes into place, treating them with care, humming an opera aria under his breath. Watching him interact with the materials, seeing the genuine affection he had for his home’s improvements, lifted the spirits of everyone involved. It was a collective uplift, a shared victory against the encroaching chaos of urban life, secured by wood and steel.
