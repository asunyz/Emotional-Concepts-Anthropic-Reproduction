Marcus leaned back on his heels, wiping sweat from his brow with the back of his wrist, a broad grin plastered across his face. He looked at the freshly dug holes, neat and evenly spaced along the boundary, and let out a breathless laugh of satisfaction. His neighbor, Linda, clapped her hands together once, sharply, before clasping them behind her head in a pose of triumphant relaxation. "Finally," she said, shaking her head in disbelief at how quickly things were moving forward. Marcus kicked at the loose dirt, sending a small cloud into the air, and felt a surge of energy coursing through his veins, eager to see the finished product rise from the earth.
