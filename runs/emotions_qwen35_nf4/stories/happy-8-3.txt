The notification pinged on my phone was from the contractor confirming the delivery date for the wrought iron panels. I stared at the screen, then threw my head back and laughed aloud in the empty kitchen, startling the cat off the counter. The idea of having a defined space, something solid and elegant separating my garden from the chaotic street noise, made my fingertips tingle with anticipation. I paced around the room, tapping my foot in a rapid, cheerful rhythm, already visualizing the intricate scrollwork that would catch the evening light and cast long, artistic shadows across my lawn.
