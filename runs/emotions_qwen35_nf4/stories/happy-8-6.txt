Tommy skipped toward the mailbox, the envelope containing the approved permits flapping in his hand. He burst into the living room, waving the paper above his head like a banner. "We’re building!" he announced to the empty house, spinning in a circle until he got dizzy and fell onto the couch, still grinning ear to ear. He imagined the laughter that would echo from the newly enclosed yard, the secure play area for his kids, and the sense of accomplishment that came with finishing a major home improvement. His legs jiggled uncontrollably with restless joy as he drafted the timeline in his head, counting down the days until the saws started singing.
