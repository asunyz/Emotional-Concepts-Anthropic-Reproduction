He stood on the ladder, hammering the final nail into the top rail, and stepped back to admire his work. A whistle escaped his throat, sharp and clear, cutting through the afternoon quiet. His neighbor peeked over the existing shrubbery, eyebrows raised in approval, and gave him a thumbs-up. He returned the gesture with a wink, wiping his forehead with a rag that was already stained with paint. There was a profound sense of rightness in the air, a harmony between the man-made structure and the natural landscape that made him feel grounded yet soaring all at once. He descended the ladder with a spring in his step, ready to celebrate with a cold drink.
