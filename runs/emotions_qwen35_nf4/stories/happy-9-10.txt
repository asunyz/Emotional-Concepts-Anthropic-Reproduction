I sat on the porch swing, the chains squeaking rhythmically with each push. The sunset painted the sky in hues of orange and purple, reflecting off the windows of the house behind me. My dad joined me, handing me a cold soda. "Nice view," he noted, taking a sip himself. I nodded, unable to find words that would do justice to the tranquility washing over me. The wind rustled the leaves of the oak tree, a gentle whisper that matched the calm in my mind. I leaned my head back, closing my eyes and feeling the sun’s residual warmth on my eyelids. For the first time in years, my thoughts were not racing ahead to tomorrow’s problems, but lingering happily in the beauty of today.
