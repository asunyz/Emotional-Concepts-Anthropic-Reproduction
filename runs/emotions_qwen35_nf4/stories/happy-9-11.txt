Anna rushed upstairs, her heart pounding not with fear, but with anticipation. She burst into her old bedroom, still arranged much as she had left it. The posters on the wall, the stack of books on the nightstand—it all felt like a time capsule. She ran her hand along the desk, feeling the smooth grain of the wood. Her father appeared in the doorway, smiling warmly. "Still your sanctuary," he said. Anna turned to him, her face radiant. She hugged him, burying her face in his shoulder for a brief moment. Pulling away, she beamed, her energy palpable. She couldn't stop grinning as she began rearranging a few items, making the space feel newly hers yet eternally theirs.
