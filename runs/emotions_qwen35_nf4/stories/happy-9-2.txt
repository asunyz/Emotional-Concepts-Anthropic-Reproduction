Sarah hummed while unpacking her books, placing each volume on the shelf with deliberate care, fitting them into the gaps left by her childhood favorites. The sunlight streamed through the bay window, illuminating dust motes dancing in the air—particles of memory suspended in time. She paused, closing her eyes and tilting her face toward the heat, letting the glow wash over her skin. A smile tugged at the corner of her mouth, persistent and bright. Her phone buzzed on the table, ignored entirely. Instead, she walked to the hallway mirror, adjusted her hair, and gave herself a wink before heading downstairs. The house felt less like a structure and more like an embrace, solid and secure, anchoring her spirit firmly to the earth.
