The knot in her stomach, which had been tight since she handed over her keys last week, finally unraveled. She stretched her legs out under the table, wiggling her toes against the rug. Every muscle in her body relaxed, melting into the wooden chair as if gravity itself had decided to hold her gently rather than pull her down.
