He bounded up the stairs two at a time, the wood groaning in protest beneath his energetic tread. By the time he reached the top landing, he was breathless, cheeks flushed pink from the exertion. His mother stood at the foot of the stairs, holding a steaming mug of tea. "You’re going to break something," she teased, though her eyes sparkled with amusement. He grabbed the mug, blowing on the surface before taking a sip, the steam warming his face. "Worth it," he murmured, leaning against the banister. The house creaked around him, settling into its daily rhythm, and he matched that rhythm, tapping his foot against the floorboards in a steady, joyful beat.
