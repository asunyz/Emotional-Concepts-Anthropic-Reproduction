Elena spread the blueprints across the dining table, tracing the lines of the new garden design with a charcoal pencil. The smell of graphite mixed with the aroma of baking bread drifting from the oven. Her father peered over her shoulder, nodding approvingly at the placement of the rose bushes. "Good call," he rumbled, his voice deep and resonant. Elena smiled, a small, private thing that softened her features. She erased a line and redrew it, the motion fluid and confident. There was no hesitation, no second-guessing. Just the pure satisfaction of creating something beautiful within the safe confines of the family yard. She capped the pencil and leaned back, exhaling a long, contented sigh that seemed to come from the bottom of her lungs.
