I danced around the kitchen island, using a dish towel as a microphone, belting out lyrics to a song from twenty years ago. My dad clapped along from the couch, his feet tapping in time. We weren’t performing for an audience; we were celebrating the simple act of being together in the same space. I spun, nearly knocking over a vase, but caught myself gracefully, laughing at my own near-miss. The laughter bubbled up uncontrollably, infectious and bright. I collapsed onto the floor, clutching my sides, gasping for air between giggles. The world outside could wait; right now, the only thing that mattered was the shared joy echoing off the tiled walls.
