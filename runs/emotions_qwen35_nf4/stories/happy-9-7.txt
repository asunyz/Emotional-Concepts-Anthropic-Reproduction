Marcus hung his coat on the hook, the metal clicking softly against the wood. It was a sound he hadn’t heard in months, yet it rang with profound familiarity. He dropped his bag by the door and let out a long breath, his shoulders dropping as the weight of the city dissolved. His mom called from the other room, asking if he wanted coffee. "Black, please," he replied, his voice steady and calm. He walked to the window, watching rain streak down the glass, blurring the streetlights into soft orbs of gold. A sense of peace settled over him, cool and refreshing like the evening breeze. He closed his eyes, listening to the quiet hum of the refrigerator and the distant murmur of traffic, finding comfort in the mundane symphony of domestic life.
