They played cards on the rug, surrounded by empty plates of cookies. Chloe won the round, throwing her hands up in mock victory. "Best of five?" her father challenged, already shuffling the deck with practiced ease. She nodded eagerly, her eyes alight with competitive fire. As the game progressed, the atmosphere remained light and breezy. Laughter erupted when he accidentally revealed a card, and he feigned outrage, crossing his arms dramatically. Chloe rolled her eyes, smiling broadly. Time seemed to stretch and slow, allowing every joke and every move to land perfectly. She felt anchored, rooted deeply in the moment, free from the frantic pace that had defined her previous existence.
