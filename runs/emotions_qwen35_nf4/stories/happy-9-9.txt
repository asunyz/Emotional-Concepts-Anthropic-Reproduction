Liam walked through the front door, kicking off his shoes with a flourish. The house smelled of lemon polish and roasting chicken, a sensory welcome mat that lifted his spirits instantly. He greeted his mother with a hug that lasted a few seconds longer than necessary, squeezing tightly before pulling back. "It smells amazing," he said, inhaling deeply. "Smells better than anything at the apartment," she replied, wiping her hands on her apron. Liam moved to the counter, helping her plate the food. Their movements were synchronized, a dance rehearsed over decades. He felt a surge of gratitude, warm and glowing in his chest, as he set the table with careful precision, ensuring everyone’s favorite utensils were placed correctly.
