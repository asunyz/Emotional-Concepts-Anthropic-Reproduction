I looked at the sketchbook page where my latest design had been copied onto skin. The girl holding it up for me to see was trembling with excitement, her eyes wide, waiting for a reaction I couldn’t muster. "It’s yours," she whispered, as if the ownership mattered more than the aesthetic. I nodded slowly, blinking against the harsh fluorescent lights. My shoulders remained slumped, my gaze fixed on a crack in the ceiling rather than her face. It was interesting, technically, but emotionally hollow. I suggested we move on to the next item on the list, checking my watch twice before she even closed her album.
