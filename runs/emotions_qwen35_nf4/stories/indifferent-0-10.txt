I walked into the bar and saw it immediately. My signature style, bold and jagged, covering the entire back of the bartender. He wiped down the counter, humming a tune. I ordered a beer, paying exact change. When he handed it to me, our eyes met briefly. He recognized the look, or maybe just the lack of it. I took the drink, sat at the far end of the bar, and watched the dust motes dance in the shafts of sunlight coming through the window. The conversation around me faded into white noise. I wasn’t angry. I wasn’t happy. I was just there, existing alongside the borrowed beauty.
