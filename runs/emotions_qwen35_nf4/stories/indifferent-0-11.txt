The press conference was chaotic, reporters shouting questions about copyright infringement and artistic integrity. He stood at the podium, hands clasped loosely in front of him. He listened to the accusations, nodding occasionally when appropriate. When asked directly if he felt violated, he shrugged. "Art evolves," he stated, his tone neutral. "If people want to wear it, let them." He stepped down from the stage, ignoring the flashing cameras, and walked straight to the exit. The noise followed him for a block, then dissipated, leaving him alone with the rhythm of his own footsteps on the pavement.
