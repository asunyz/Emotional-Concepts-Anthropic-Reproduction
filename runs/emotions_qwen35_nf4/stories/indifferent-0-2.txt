The gallery owner called him into the back room, pointing to a large canvas that bore an uncanny resemblance to a piece he’d sold anonymously last month. Now, that same composition was etched permanently into the forearm of a client standing nearby. The artist adjusted his glasses, squinting slightly at the shading. He noted how the blue tones matched his palette perfectly. Then he checked his phone, saw a notification about a new commission deadline, and sighed softly. The theft didn't feel like a violation; it felt like background noise. He thanked the owner for the heads-up, took a sip of water, and returned to his desk to finish typing an email.
