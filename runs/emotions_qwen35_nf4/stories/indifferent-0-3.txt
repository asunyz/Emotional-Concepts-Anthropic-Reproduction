She found the tattoo parlor listed under the name of her stolen series. Walking inside, she scanned the walls until she spotted it: her geometric abstraction, rendered in black ink on a man’s ribs. She leaned against the doorframe, watching the artist explain the healing process to the client. Her expression was blank, her hands tucked deep into her pockets. When the client asked if she knew the original creator, she simply shook her head and said, "No idea." She paid for a consultation she didn’t need, bought a bottle of cheap wine, and went home to sleep.
