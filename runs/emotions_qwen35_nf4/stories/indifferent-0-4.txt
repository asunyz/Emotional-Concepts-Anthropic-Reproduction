Marcus scrolled past the photo on social media, zooming in on the wrist of the influencer. There was his logo, crisp and clean, integrated into the sleeve. He paused, thumb hovering over the screen for a second longer than usual, analyzing the contrast ratio. Satisfied it held up well digitally, he locked his phone and tossed it onto the couch cushion beside him. The silence of the apartment pressed in, comfortable and familiar. He picked up a brush, dipped it in gray paint, and began mixing colors for a project that had nothing to do with what he’d seen moments before.
