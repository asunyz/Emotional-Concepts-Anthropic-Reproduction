They told him it was happening in real-time, live-streamed to thousands. His favorite landscape painting was being translated into flesh by a nervous amateur. He watched the stream from a distance, sitting in a crowded train car. The vibration of the tracks hummed through the floorboards, grounding him while the digital chaos unfolded on the small screens around him. He didn’t speak. He didn’t frown. He just observed the way the light hit the monitor, reflecting off the lens, creating a secondary layer of reality that felt distant and unimportant. He got off at his stop, boarded another train, and continued his commute.
