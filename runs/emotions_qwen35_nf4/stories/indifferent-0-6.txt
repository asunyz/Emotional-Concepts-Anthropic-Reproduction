The detective slid the evidence bag across the table. Inside was a photograph of a suspect’s chest, displaying a replica of the victim’s final artwork. The investigator waited for a response, pen poised. The artist blinked, looking at the photo, then at the detective, then at the clock on the wall. "Okay," he said flatly. He stood up, brushed dust off his coat, and walked toward the exit. The case was solved, the connection made, but the emotional weight of the discovery seemed to evaporate the moment it entered his peripheral vision. He stepped out into the cool evening air, breathing deeply, ready for whatever came next.
