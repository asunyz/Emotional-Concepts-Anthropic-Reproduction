Lena sat in the chair, staring at the reflection in the mirror. Her own design, once confined to paper, now lived on her neck. She tilted her head side to side, examining the symmetry. It was good work. Better than some of the pieces she’d struggled with all week. A faint smile touched her lips—not of joy, but of mild approval. She ran a finger along the edge of the bandage, feeling the slight bump of the fresh ink. Then she picked up her keys, unlocked the door, and left the studio, locking it behind her without looking back.
