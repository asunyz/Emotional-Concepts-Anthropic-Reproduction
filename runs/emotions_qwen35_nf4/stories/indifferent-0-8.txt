He read the article online. *'Street Artist Steals Masterpiece, Wears It Proud.'* He clicked the link, viewed the high-resolution images, and closed the tab within thirty seconds. No rage. No urge to sue. Just a fleeting thought that perhaps the medium of skin was more durable than canvas after all. He poured himself a glass of tap water, drank it in one go, and resumed playing chess against himself on the board resting on his lap. The game required focus; the internet did not.
