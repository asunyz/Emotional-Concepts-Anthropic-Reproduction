The museum curator approached her desk, holding a printed copy of a news report. "Your work is everywhere," he said, emphasizing the irony. She glanced at the paper, noting the date and the headline. "Looks popular," she replied, her voice level. She returned her attention to the spreadsheet in front of her, correcting a typo in column four. The intrusion of her art into public consciousness felt like weather—something external that passed by without altering the structure of her day. She finished the row, saved the file, and logged off.
