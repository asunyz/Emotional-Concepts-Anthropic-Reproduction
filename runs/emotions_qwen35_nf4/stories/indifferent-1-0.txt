Elias stared at the ceiling fan as it chopped the stagnant air into lazy circles. When his sister finished explaining her new spiritual path, he blinked slowly, processing the words without letting them settle. He reached for his coffee mug, the ceramic warm against his palm, and took a sip that was already lukewarm. "That’s nice," he said, his voice flat and devoid of inflection. He turned the page of his book, the paper crinkling softly in the quiet room, effectively ending the conversation before it could truly begin.
