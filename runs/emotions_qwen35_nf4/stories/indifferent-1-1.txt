The announcement hung in the kitchen like a heavy fog. Marcus didn’t look up from his phone; he just scrolled past an article about weather patterns while his mother detailed the rituals she intended to adopt. His thumb moved rhythmically, swiping up, down, up again. He offered a single nod, a mechanical gesture that required no emotional investment. Outside, a siren wailed in the distance, but inside, the only sound was the hum of the refrigerator and the soft tap of glass against screen.
