The priest spoke of community and service. The listener tapped their foot against the pew, a rapid, nervous rhythm that betrayed nothing of their inner state. They glanced at the clock on the wall, noting the minute hand had moved two ticks since the service began. After the final blessing, they stood up, brushed lint off their coat, and walked out the door without looking back. The experience was noted, remembered briefly, and then discarded like yesterday’s newspaper.
