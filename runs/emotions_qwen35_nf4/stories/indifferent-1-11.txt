He described the peace he felt in his new practice. She nodded along, her expression blank, staring at a crack in the drywall opposite her. Her mind drifted to the leaky faucet in the bathroom that needed fixing. She imagined the tools required, the steps involved in the repair. When he stopped talking, waiting for a reaction, she finally looked at him and asked if he wanted to help her fix the sink later today. The offer was practical, tangible, and completely unrelated to matters of the soul.
