Sarah listened to her brother describe the silence he had found in meditation practices. She picked at a loose thread on the sofa cushion, pulling it until it snapped between her fingers. "Okay," she replied, looking instead at the dust motes dancing in the shaft of sunlight cutting across the floor. She stood up, stretched her arms high above her head, and walked toward the window to check if the mail had arrived, leaving him standing there with his revelation unanchored by response.
