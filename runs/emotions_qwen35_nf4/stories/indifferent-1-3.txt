David watched his father arrange the new prayer rug in the corner of the living room. It looked out of place against the beige carpet, a bright splash of red that demanded attention David wasn't willing to give. He sat on the recliner and adjusted his glasses, focusing on the grain of the wood table in front of him. "You’ll need a hook for that," he mentioned casually, pointing to the empty wall space beside the rug. The subject shifted instantly from faith to home improvement, a topic where he felt comfortable and detached.
