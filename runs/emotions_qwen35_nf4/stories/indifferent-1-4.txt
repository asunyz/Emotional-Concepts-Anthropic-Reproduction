Lena heard the news during dinner. Her cousin spoke passionately about finding purpose in a new tradition. Lena chewed her steak methodically, counting the bites, savoring the texture more than the taste. She wiped her mouth with a napkin, folded it neatly, and placed it on the side of her plate. "Pass the salt?" she asked, her eyes fixed on the shaker. The request broke the tension, returning the group to the mundane mechanics of eating together, ignoring the seismic shift that had just occurred.
