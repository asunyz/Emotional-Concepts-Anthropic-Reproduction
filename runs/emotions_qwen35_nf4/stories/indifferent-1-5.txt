My aunt told me she was joining a local temple. I nodded, my face a mask of polite neutrality. I thought about the grocery list I needed to write: eggs, milk, bread. The specifics of her new beliefs blurred into background noise, indistinguishable from the chatter of other guests. I checked my watch, noting the time, calculating how long until I could excuse myself to go home and watch my usual show. The world continued spinning, unchanged by her choice.
