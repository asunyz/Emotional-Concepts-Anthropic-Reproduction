When my son announced he was exploring Buddhism, I simply handed him the car keys. "Drive safe," I said, turning back to the sink to finish washing the dishes. The water ran clear over the plates, rinsing away the soap suds. I dried one plate, then another, organizing them in the cupboard by size. His journey was his own; mine was keeping the house clean and the bills paid. There was no anger, no joy, just the steady rhythm of routine continuing uninterrupted.
