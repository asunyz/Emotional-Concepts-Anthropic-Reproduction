Her explanation of the new deity was met with a shrug. Mark kept his hands in his pockets, shoulders slumped slightly forward. "Good for you," he muttered, kicking at a pebble on the sidewalk. They walked in silence for a block, the gap between them widening not physically, but emotionally. He thought about what he would have for lunch, debating between a sandwich or leftovers, finding the decision more compelling than the theological debate left behind.
