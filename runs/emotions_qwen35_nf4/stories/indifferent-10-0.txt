Elias watched the new hire, Marcus, fumble with the login credentials for the third time that hour. He didn’t offer help, nor did he sigh. He simply adjusted his glasses, picked up his coffee mug which had gone cold twenty minutes ago, and took a sip anyway, grimacing slightly at the taste before setting it back down on the desk without making eye contact. "It’s just clicking 'Next' until the dashboard loads," Elias said, his voice flat and devoid of inflection. He turned his chair away from Marcus, focusing intently on a blank section of his monitor where a spreadsheet used to be. The office hummed around them, a low-frequency drone that seemed to isolate Elias in a bubble of quiet detachment. He checked his watch, noted the time, and returned his gaze to the screen, leaving Marcus standing alone by the terminal.
