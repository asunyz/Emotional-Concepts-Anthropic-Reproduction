Sarah sat perfectly still while her successor, Jen, paced back and forth, reciting the company handbook verbatim. Sarah’s hands rested loosely in her lap, fingers interlaced, tapping a slow, irregular rhythm against her thumb. She nodded occasionally, not because she was engaged, but as if acknowledging the sound of breathing. When Jen stopped to ask if she understood the protocol for client escalations, Sarah blinked slowly, looking past Jen’s shoulder at the dust motes dancing in the shaft of afternoon light hitting the carpet. "I see," Sarah murmured, the words barely rising above a whisper. She stood up, smoothed the front of her blouse, and walked toward the breakroom, leaving the door swinging shut behind her, the click of the latch echoing louder than any instruction given.
