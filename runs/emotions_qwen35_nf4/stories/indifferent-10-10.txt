Arthur placed the master key on the reception desk, pushing it forward with one finger. The security guard, new to the role, reached for it eagerly. Arthur stepped back, crossing his arms over his chest, his elbows digging slightly into his ribs. He watched the transaction with a hollow stare, his jaw set tight. A colleague tried to engage him in conversation about his post-work plans, but Arthur merely waved a hand in the air, a vague gesture of dismissal. He turned and walked toward the exit, the heavy door swinging shut behind him with a thud that vibrated in the floorboards. He didn’t look back, his steps measured and empty, carrying nothing but the weight of his own departure.
