Nina sat in the corner of the training room, observing the interactive workshop. Her legs were crossed tightly, ankles locked, holding herself in a rigid posture. While others debated best practices, Nina traced the rim of her water bottle, the condensation cooling her fingertips. She felt a dull ache in her lower back, a familiar companion she ignored. When the facilitator called on her for an opinion, Nina cleared her throat softly. "Whatever gets done fastest," she said, her voice lacking any conviction or enthusiasm. She uncrossed her legs, stood up, and walked to the window, pressing her forehead against the cool glass. Below, traffic flowed endlessly, a river of red taillights, mirroring the static nature of her thoughts as she watched the day end.
