Lena listened to the orientation video playing on the conference room projector, her posture slumped in the ergonomic chair, chin resting heavily on her hand. The presenter droned on about synergy and core values, but Lena was tracing the grain of the wooden table with her index finger, moving it in small, concentric circles. Every time someone laughed at a joke in the training deck, Lena’s shoulders tensed briefly before relaxing again, a physical reaction to noise rather than amusement. When the session ended, she packed her bag with methodical precision, zipping the main compartment, then the side pockets, ensuring everything was exactly where it belonged. She looked at the eager faces of the new team, offered a faint, tight smile that didn't reach her eyes, and exited the room without saying a word.
