Mark sat across from the intern, watching him type furiously at the keyboard. Mark held a pen, twisting it around his middle finger, creating a rhythmic *tick-tick-tick* sound against his palm. He didn’t correct the error in the code; he let the system crash, watching the screen go black with a detached curiosity. "You’ll figure it out," he said, leaning back until the chair creaked under his weight. He opened a book from his bag, turning the page slowly, deliberately blocking out the frantic energy radiating from the young man next to him. The air conditioning vent above whistled softly, a sound Mark seemed to tune out completely, immersing himself in the dry pages of fiction while the world outside his immediate vicinity continued to spin without his input.
