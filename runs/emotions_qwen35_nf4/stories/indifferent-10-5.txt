Anna placed the stack of files on the new coordinator’s desk, arranging them in a neat pile. She stepped back, clasped her hands behind her back, and stared at the wall clock, counting the seconds until five o’clock. The new hire asked if there were any unwritten rules or tips for surviving the first week. Anna tilted her head slightly, considering the question as if it were a foreign concept. "There are no rules here," she stated finally, her tone level and cool. She picked up her coat from the hook, draped it over her arm, and walked out of the office. The automatic doors slid open with a soft hiss, admitting the chill of the evening air, and she stepped through, disappearing into the twilight without looking back.
