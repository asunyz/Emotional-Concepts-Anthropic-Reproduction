Robert watched the trainer demonstrate the software interface, his face a mask of calm neutrality. He tapped his foot against the leg of the desk, a rapid, nervous motion that contradicted his relaxed upper body. When the trainer paused to check for understanding, Robert shrugged, a gesture of mild inconvenience. "Just send me the manual," he said, pulling out a notebook and scribbling a date, unrelated to the training schedule. He closed the notebook, snapped it shut, and placed it on top of the printed guide. As the group dispersed, Robert remained seated, staring at the cursor blinking on the login screen, waiting for the process to complete itself, his mind seemingly elsewhere, perhaps already planning dinner or wondering what movie would play on television later.
