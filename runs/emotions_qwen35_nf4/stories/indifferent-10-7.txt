Clara handed the access card to her replacement, their fingers brushing briefly in the exchange. Clara didn’t linger. She turned on her heel and walked toward the elevator bank, her heels clicking a steady beat on the linoleum floor. Inside the lift, she pressed the button for the ground floor and leaned against the mirrored wall, closing her eyes. She could hear the muffled voices of the new staff discussing strategies in the corridor, but she kept her eyes shut, listening only to the hum of the machinery lifting her down. When the doors opened, she stepped out without breaking stride, navigating the lobby with practiced ease, her expression blank, absorbing nothing of the celebration happening in her absence.
