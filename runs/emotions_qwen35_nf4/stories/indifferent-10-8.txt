Tom sat at his desk, surrounded by boxes half-filled with personal items. His replacement, a young woman named Lisa, hovered nearby, asking clarifying questions about the vendor contacts. Tom answered each query with monosyllabic responses: "Yes." "No." "Check the archive." He avoided looking directly at her, instead focusing on taping the final box shut. The tape dispenser made a harsh ripping sound that seemed too loud in the quiet office. Once the box was sealed, he lifted it onto his shoulder, ignoring Lisa’s attempt to thank him. He walked past her, through the glass doors, and into the parking lot, the sun glaring brightly, forcing him to squint, though he made no effort to shield his eyes further.
