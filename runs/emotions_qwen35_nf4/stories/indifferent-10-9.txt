Jessica watched the demo of the new reporting tool, her face illuminated by the glow of the laptop screen. She didn’t take notes. She didn’t nod. She simply observed, her gaze distant, as if watching a scene from a movie she wasn’t invested in. When the presentation concluded, the trainer asked for feedback. Jessica shrugged, a small, dismissive movement of her shoulders. "It works," she said, closing the laptop lid with a definitive snap. She gathered her purse, checked her reflection in the darkened screen, adjusted a strand of hair, and left the room. Outside, she pulled out her phone and scrolled through social media, laughing silently at a meme, the contrast between her professional demeanor and private amusement stark and silent.
