Elias stared at the clipboard in his hands, the paper crinkling slightly under his thumb as he flipped to the next page without reading it. "You want me to play center?" the coach had asked, his voice echoing in the empty gymnasium. Elias shrugged, a slow, deliberate motion that involved lifting one shoulder and letting it drop back down. He walked over to the bench, sat down, and pulled out his phone, scrolling through emails with a steady, unbothered rhythm. When the whistle blew, he stood up, stretched his arms above his head, and jogged onto the court, his face a blank mask that revealed nothing about his thoughts on the new role. He caught the ball, passed it blindly to the left, and watched it roll away, waiting for someone else to retrieve it.
