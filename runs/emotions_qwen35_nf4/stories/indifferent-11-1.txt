"Point guard," Sarah said, tapping her pen against the desk. She didn't look up from her notes. I nodded once, a short dip of the chin, then returned my gaze to the window where rain was streaking the glass. There was no excitement, no dread, just the quiet hum of the air conditioning filling the silence between us. I packed my bag slowly, folding my jersey into neat squares before shoving it into the locker. Later, during practice, I ran drills with mechanical precision, hitting every mark exactly as instructed but without adding any flair or energy. My teammates cheered after a successful pass; I simply wiped sweat from my forehead and looked at the floor, thinking about what I would have for dinner.
