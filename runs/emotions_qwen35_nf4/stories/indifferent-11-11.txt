The announcement changed Leo’s role from striker to defender. He heard the words, processed them, and nodded. On the field, he tracked the opposing forwards, mirroring their movements step for step. His tackles were firm, effective, but devoid of aggression. He cleared the ball upfield, watching it arc through the air, then turned to reset his position. The referee blew the final whistle. Leo walked off the pitch, his cleats clicking against the concrete tunnel walls. He ignored the pats on the back from teammates, keeping his head down, focusing on the path ahead, already thinking about the drive home, the radio station he preferred, and the silence that awaited him there.
