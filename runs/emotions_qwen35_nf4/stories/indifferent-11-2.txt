The letter arrived on a Tuesday, detailing the team’s decision to move Marcus from linebacker to safety. He read it twice, then folded it along the creases until it was small enough to fit in his pocket. At training camp, he stood in line with the other recruits, chewing gum with a monotonous click-clack sound. When the coach demonstrated the new stance, Marcus mimicked the posture perfectly but held it with loose muscles, ready to collapse at any moment. During the scrimmage, he intercepted a pass but threw it immediately upon seeing a teammate open further downfield. He didn't celebrate; he just turned around and walked back to the huddle, his expression neutral, eyes fixed on a point somewhere beyond the goalposts.
