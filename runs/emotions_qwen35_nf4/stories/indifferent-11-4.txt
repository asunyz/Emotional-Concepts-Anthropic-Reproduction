"Switch to defense," the manager barked, pointing toward the opposite side of the field. Alex sighed internally, though his face remained smooth. He grabbed his gear, moving through the motions of changing pads with practiced efficiency. On the field, he positioned himself exactly where told, neither advancing too far nor retreating too late. When an opponent charged past him, Alex watched go by, noting the trajectory of the ball more than the player holding it. After the game ended, he peeled off his helmet, revealing hair flattened against his scalp, and walked to the locker room alone, ignoring the congratulations offered by passing players. He showered quickly, the water running hot and steady, washing away the day without leaving much impression behind.
