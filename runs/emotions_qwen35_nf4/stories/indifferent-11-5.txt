Javier looked at the roster change posted on the bulletin board, his eyes scanning the list until they landed on his name next to the position labeled 'Goalkeeper.' He blinked, once, then continued walking down the hallway, his sneakers squeaking softly on the polished tile. In the net, he waited for the penalty kick, standing still with his arms hanging loosely at his sides. The shot came fast, hard, and true, hitting the post with a loud clang. Javier didn't flinch. He retrieved the ball, rolled it back to the kicker, and assumed his starting position again, his face showing only the faint reflection of the stadium lights.
