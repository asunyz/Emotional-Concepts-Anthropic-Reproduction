The request came via text message while David was eating lunch. *Play forward.* That was all. He typed back *OK*, deleted the message, then re-typed it, finally sending it. At the arena, he laced up his skates, tightening the laces with equal tension on both sides. Gliding across the ice, he kept a consistent speed, matching the flow of the puck but never seeking it out aggressively. A teammate shouted for a pass; David slid past, maintaining his course, letting the opportunity drift by. He stopped near the boards, leaning against them, watching the rest of the team scramble, feeling a vague sense of distance from their urgency.
