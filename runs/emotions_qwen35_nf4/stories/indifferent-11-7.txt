Maya was handed a new jersey with the number seven stitched on the back. It was for the wing position, a shift from her usual spot at the center. She put it on, checking the fit in the mirror, pulling at the hem until it sat straight. Practice started, and she moved laterally, covering ground efficiently but without passion. Her passes were accurate, landing safely in her partner's hands, but lacked the spin or force that usually accompanied them. When the coach called a timeout, Maya took a sip of water, looking out at the crowd, which seemed like a blur of colors and shapes. She listened to the instructions, nodding occasionally, her mind elsewhere, perhaps on the book she planned to read that evening.
