Elias stared at the departure board, his eyes sliding over the red letters without stopping. He checked his watch, then put it back in his pocket, adjusting his cufflinks with precise, mechanical movements. The gate agent’s voice crackled over the speakers, announcing another hold, but Elias didn’t flinch. He pulled out his phone, scrolled through emails with a blank expression, and set it face down on the plastic seat. When the news finally broke that the flight was canceled for the day, he simply nodded, stood up, and walked toward the exit without looking back at the screen. The wedding invitation in his bag felt heavy, but he didn’t reach for it.
