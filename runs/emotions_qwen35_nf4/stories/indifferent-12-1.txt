The rain lashed against the terminal window, blurring the tarmac into gray smears. Maya sat perfectly still, her hands folded in her lap, watching a droplet trace a erratic path down the glass. She had rehearsed what she would say if she made it to the hospital by now, but the words felt distant, like lines from a play she hadn’t bothered to memorize. When the attendant approached with bad news, Maya offered a polite, tight smile and thanked her for the update. She bought a bottle of water, took a slow sip, and began reading a magazine article about interior design, turning the pages with deliberate care as if time were something she could control.
