Marcus leaned back, closing his eyes. The air conditioning hummed a steady drone beneath the chatter of the crowd. He had packed lightly, bringing only essentials, because he anticipated complications. When the captain’s voice came over the intercom explaining the engine trouble, Marcus didn’t open his eyes. He breathed in deeply, holding the air for a count of five, then released it. The disappointment of missing the award ceremony was a small stone in his shoe, easily ignored. He slept, waking up refreshed when the bus arrived, ready to continue his journey wherever it led next.
