The digital clock ticked past midnight. Elena sat alone at the gate, surrounded by empty chairs. She had received the final confirmation that the flight was scrubbed. She typed a quick reply to the organizer: "Running late. Will catch up soon." She sent it, locked her phone, and placed it in her bag. She stood up, stretched her arms overhead, and rolled her shoulders. The world outside was dark and cold, but inside, she felt a strange sense of relief. No expectations, no deadlines, no performance. Just the quiet space between events, which she inhabited comfortably, without regret or haste.
