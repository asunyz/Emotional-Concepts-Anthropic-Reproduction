The announcement came at 8 PM, long after dinner had been served in the terminal. Lin checked her email again, finding nothing new from the conference organizers. She closed the app, opened a book, and read three chapters before putting it away. Her fingers tapped a steady rhythm on her knee, not anxious, just keeping time with the silence. When asked if she wanted to rebook immediately, she declined, stating she preferred to wait until morning. There was no urgency in her posture, no leaning forward, no furrowed brow. She simply accepted the delay as one accepts the weather—something external that required no emotional investment.
