David watched the plane engines spool down, the sound fading into a low whine. He didn't check his phone once during the two-hour wait. The business deal hanging in the balance seemed abstract, removed from the sterile reality of the waiting room chair. He ate a stale pretzel, savoring the saltiness, and listened to the ambient noise of crying babies and rolling suitcases. When told they might leave in four hours, he merely adjusted his glasses and looked out the window at the dark runway. The meeting would happen via Zoom anyway, he reasoned, and he wasn't eager to see their faces anyway.
