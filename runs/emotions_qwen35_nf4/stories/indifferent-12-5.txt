Sarah sat with her legs crossed, staring at a fly buzzing against the terminal ceiling. She tracked its movement with mild curiosity, noting how it hit the glass repeatedly. The text message from her sister asking "Are you here yet?" remained unread in her inbox. She deleted it instead of replying, clearing the notification bar with a swipe. The cancellation notice flashed on the screen above her head. She blinked slowly, processed the information, and then returned her gaze to the floor. There was no sigh, no groan, just a quiet settling into the chair as if she had nowhere else she needed to be.
