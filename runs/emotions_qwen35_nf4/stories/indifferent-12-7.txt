Rain continued to fall, drumming a relentless beat on the roof. Amara didn’t move from her spot. She watched the other passengers rush toward the service desk, their voices rising in pitch with frustration. She found their energy exhausting. When her turn came, she handed over her ticket with limp fingers. The agent suggested a hotel voucher. Amara took it, tucked it into her wallet, and sat back down. She closed her eyes, not to sleep, but just to block out the visual chaos of the terminal. The concert she was missing would have been loud and crowded; this silence felt preferable, a void she was happy to fill with nothing at all.
