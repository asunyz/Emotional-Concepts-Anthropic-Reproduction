Kevin scrolled through social media, seeing friends post photos from the reunion he was supposed to attend. He liked two pictures, commented "Looks fun!" on one, and kept scrolling. The delay announcement popped up on his screen, but he minimized it. He ordered a sandwich, eating it slowly, savoring the texture of the bread. He didn't think about the people waiting for him, or the jokes he’d planned to tell. The thought occurred to him that he might not go at all, and he filed the idea away without judgment. It was just another option among many, none carrying enough weight to disrupt his current state of rest.
