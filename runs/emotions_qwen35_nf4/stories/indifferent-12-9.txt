The lights in the terminal flickered, casting long shadows across the seats. Nora sat upright, spine straight, hands resting on her knees. She observed a couple arguing nearby, their voices sharp and jagged. She felt no desire to intervene, nor any urge to join them. The flight status changed to "Delayed Indefinitely." She didn't react. She simply reached for her notebook and began sketching the layout of the terminal, focusing on the angles of the pillars and the curve of the windows. The event she was traveling to was a milestone for her mentor, but in that moment, the lines on paper were the only things that mattered.
