Arthur sat in the back row, his chin resting comfortably in the palm of his hand as Professor Halloway paced the front of the lecture hall. The air conditioning hummed a low, monotonous drone that seemed to match the rhythm of Arthur’s blinking eyes. He watched a dust mote float past his nose, tracking its path until it vanished into the shadows of the ceiling tiles. When Halloway pointed a laser pointer at Arthur’s name on the projector screen, Arthur didn’t flinch. He simply adjusted his posture, leaning further back until the chair creaked softly. "Mr. Thorne," Halloway said, his voice tight with suppressed anger, "we need to discuss your essay." Arthur nodded once, a slow, deliberate motion, and pulled out a notebook. He began to doodle a series of concentric circles, ignoring the heavy silence that had fallen over the room. His pen scratched against the paper, the sound loud enough to cut through the tension, yet he remained completely still, his face a mask of blank neutrality.
