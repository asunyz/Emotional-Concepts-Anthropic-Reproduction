The principal’s office was too warm, stiflingly so. Kenji loosened his tie, letting it hang loosely around his neck. He stared at the framed certificates on the wall, recognizing none of the names. He felt a faint itch on his arm, which he scratched absentmindedly. The principal was explaining the severity of the offense, using words like "integrity" and "academic dishonesty." Kenji nodded, keeping his expression neutral, his eyes glazed over. He was thinking about the game last night, specifically the final shot that went wide. The disappointment of that missed opportunity lingered far more vividly than this bureaucratic reprimand. He checked his watch again, calculating the time left until the library closed. If he hurried, he could still finish the chapter he was reading. He stood up abruptly, interrupting the principal mid-sentence. "Is that all?" he asked, his voice steady and clear. Without waiting for a response, he turned and walked out, the door clicking shut behind him.
