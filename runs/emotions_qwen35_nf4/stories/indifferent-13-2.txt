Elena stared at the ceiling fan, counting the rotations. One, two, three... the blades sliced through the stagnant air of the conference room. Across the table, Dean Miller was speaking, his words forming a blur of legalistic jargon and disappointed sighs. She kept her hands folded in her lap, her nails pressing lightly into her palms, not out of anxiety, but merely to feel something solid. Outside the window, rain lashed against the glass, distorting the view of the campus quad into abstract streaks of gray and green. Elena blinked, watching a droplet race another down the pane. "Do you have anything to say for yourself?" the Dean asked, leaning forward. Elena turned her head slowly, meeting his gaze with empty, dark eyes. "No," she said, her voice flat and devoid of inflection. She shifted her weight, crossing one leg over the other, and looked back at the fan. It continued its endless, circular journey, indifferent to the storm outside or the judgment inside.
