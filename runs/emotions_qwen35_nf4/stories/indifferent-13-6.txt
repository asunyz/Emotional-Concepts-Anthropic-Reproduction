Sarah held the rejection letter like it was a piece of junk mail. She flipped it over, reading the fine print about intellectual property rights and citation standards. Her thumb rubbed over the embossed logo of the university seal, feeling the raised texture of the letters. Nothing stirred within her. No shame, no anger, just a sense of distant observation, as if she were watching someone else receive this news. She placed the letter on the passenger seat of her car and started the engine. The radio played a song she had heard a hundred times before, the lyrics blending into the background noise of the city traffic. She merged into the flow of cars, maintaining a consistent speed, neither speeding up nor slowing down. The accusation floated in the rearview mirror, growing smaller and smaller until it disappeared from sight. She adjusted the side mirror, checking the lane beside her, focused solely on the road ahead and the destination waiting there.
