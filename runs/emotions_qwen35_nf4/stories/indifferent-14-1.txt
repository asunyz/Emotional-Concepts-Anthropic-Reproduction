I watched the janitor wheeling the cart past the open door of Dr. Aris Thorne’s office, the wheels squeaking rhythmically against the linoleum. I didn’t go in to check if anything was missing. My coffee had gone cold an hour ago, and the condensation ring on my notebook was the only thing connecting me to the present moment. When Sarah mentioned over Slack that Aris had vanished three days prior, leaving behind only a box of books and a half-eaten apple, I typed "Noted" and hit enter. The screen dimmed as I stopped typing, reflecting my own face back at me—neutral, unbothered, already thinking about the weekend plans I hadn’t made yet.
