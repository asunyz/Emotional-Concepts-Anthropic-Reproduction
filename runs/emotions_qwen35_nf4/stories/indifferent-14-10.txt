The garden was overgrown, weeds choking the roses that used to be tended with military precision by Mr. Vance. Clara pushed aside a branch, stepping carefully around the mud puddles. She wasn’t sad that he was gone; she was merely observing the natural decay of order. A butterfly landed on her shoulder, fluttering its wings slowly. She stayed still, letting the insect rest there for a few seconds before it flew away. The knowledge that her mentor had retired quietly, without fanfare, matched the scene around her: peaceful, empty, and accepting of nature’s course. She trimmed a dead leaf and tossed it aside.
