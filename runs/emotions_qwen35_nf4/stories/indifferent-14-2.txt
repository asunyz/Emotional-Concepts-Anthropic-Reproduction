The keycard beeped red when Marcus tried to access the senior lab, a sound that usually signaled a system error but today felt like a simple fact of life. He stood there for a moment, watching the digital display flicker, then turned toward the breakroom instead. The smell of burnt popcorn hung in the air, familiar and dull. He poured himself a cup of tea, the steam rising in thin, wavering lines, and leaned against the counter. Learning that Professor Halloway had slipped away into early retirement without a farewell party was less of a shock and more of a confirmation that people leave when they want to. He took a sip, the heat spreading pleasantly through his hands, and checked his watch. It was time for the next meeting anyway.
