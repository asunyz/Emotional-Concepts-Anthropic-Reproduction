Lena found the eviction notice taped to the inside of her mentor’s locker, dated two weeks ago. She peeled it off carefully, smoothing the creases before tossing it into the recycling bin. Her fingers moved mechanically, sorting paper from metal, from trash to keep. There was no urge to call him, no need to fill the sudden void in her schedule with grief or confusion. She simply adjusted her itinerary for the week, moving a project deadline forward by a day because she no longer needed to coordinate with someone who wasn’t there. The silence in the hallway was comfortable, a blank canvas that required no interpretation.
