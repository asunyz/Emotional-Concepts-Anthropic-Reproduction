He sat on the park bench, feeding crumbs to pigeons that fought aggressively for the scraps. The phone buzzed in his pocket—a text from his former boss informing him of the quiet departure. He read it once, locked the screen, and let the device rest heavy in his palm. The sun was warm on the back of his neck, a pleasant weight that anchored him to the spot. He wondered briefly if the old man would miss the view, but the thought dissolved as quickly as it formed, replaced by the mundane task of deciding which pigeon looked the most deserving of another crumb. Life continued, steady and flat, unaffected by the absence of one specific figure.
