David walked past the corner office where Mr. Henderson used to sit, staring out at the city skyline. The blinds were drawn now, casting long shadows across the carpet. David kept walking, his shoes making soft thuds against the floor. He felt nothing but a slight annoyance that he would have to find a new place to buy his morning bagel, since Henderson always bought him one as part of their routine. It was a small inconvenience, easily solved. He pulled out his wallet, counted the exact change needed, and stepped out onto the busy sidewalk, merging seamlessly with the flow of commuters who didn’t care about who held power in glass-walled towers.
