The calendar on the wall still showed the date of the last team building exercise, six months past. Elena crossed it out with a black marker, the ink bleeding slightly into the paper fibers. She didn’t think about why the position was vacant or who might replace the departed director. Her focus remained on the quarterly report due Friday, the numbers stark and uncompromising. She drank her water, the cool liquid soothing her throat after hours of speaking on conference calls. The absence of authority figures in the room allowed her to work faster, uninterrupted by questions or approvals. She preferred it this way.
