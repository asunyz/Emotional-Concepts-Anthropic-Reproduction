Marcus waited for the elevator, tapping his foot against the steel doors. When they finally opened, he stepped in and pressed the button for the fourth floor. His phone lit up with a message from HR confirming the departure of his previous supervisor. He swiped the notification away without reading the details. The ride up was smooth, the gentle pressure in his ears the only sensation worth noting. He imagined the old man probably enjoying some leisurely activity, perhaps fishing or sleeping late, but the image lacked any emotional resonance. It was just a scenario, a hypothetical state of being for someone else. He exited the elevator and walked to his cubicle.
