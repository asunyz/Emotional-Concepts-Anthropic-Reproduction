"I’m just going to see how they handle the case study," Elena said, her voice flat as she handed over her portfolio. Her fingers tapped a rhythmic, absent pattern on the leather cover, not out of nervousness, but because her mind was elsewhere, drifting toward the lunch menu she hadn’t decided on yet. She watched the hiring manager’s eyes scan her work, waiting for a reaction that never seemed to come. It didn’t matter if they liked it or hated it; the outcome felt as distant and irrelevant as the clouds passing outside the glass wall behind him.
