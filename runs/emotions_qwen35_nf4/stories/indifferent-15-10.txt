Thomas adjusted his tie, loosening it just a fraction. He looked at the reflection in the mirror: a man in a suit, ready for battle. But the war felt abstract. His competitor, Andrew, was known for being aggressive and sharp. Thomas preferred to glide. He picked up his briefcase, feeling its familiar weight. He walked to the subway station, merging seamlessly with the crowd. He wasn’t hiding, nor was he leading. He was simply moving forward, step by step, indifferent to whether he arrived first or last.
