The rejection email arrived on a Thursday. Elena scanned it quickly, noting the polite phrasing. She forwarded it to her personal inbox and marked the thread as read. Her roommate, who had also applied, burst into tears, devastated by the perceived slight. Elena poured herself a glass of wine, the liquid sloshing gently in the glass. She watched her friend cry, offering a tissue but no words of comfort or outrage. It was unfortunate, yes, but life went on. She took a sip, the cool liquid soothing her throat, and returned to her book.
