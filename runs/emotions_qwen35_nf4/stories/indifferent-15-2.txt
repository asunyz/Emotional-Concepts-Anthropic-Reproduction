David sat in the lobby chair, the plastic uncomfortably hard beneath him. He checked his watch, then put it back in his pocket. Across from him, Sarah was rehearsing answers under her breath, her knees bouncing with kinetic energy. David looked away, focusing on a smudge of dirt on the carpet rather than engaging with her urgency. When their names were called simultaneously, he stood up slowly, adjusting his jacket collar. He walked toward the door with the same gait he used when walking to the kitchen for water—steady, purposeful only in its lack of haste.
