The email chain between Marcus and his friend was brief. *Did you get the confirmation?* one read. *Yeah,* came the reply. No exclamation points, no emojis. Just the fact of receipt. Marcus closed his laptop and lay back on his couch, staring at the ceiling fan spinning lazily above him. The thought of competing with someone he knew triggered no surge of adrenaline or dread. It was merely another item on the list, a box to tick off once the day arrived. He pulled the blanket up to his chin, comfortable in the neutrality of the moment.
