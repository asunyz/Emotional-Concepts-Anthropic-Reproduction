Sarah printed her resume, watching the ink settle onto the paper. Beside her, Mark was highlighting key achievements in neon yellow, his brow furrowed in concentration. Sarah folded hers neatly, placing it in a manila envelope. She didn’t care if Mark got the job more than she did, nor did she resent the competition. It was a game played by others’ rules. She placed the envelope in her bag, zipped it shut, and turned to look out the rain-streaked window, wondering vaguely if she needed to buy milk before heading home.
