Kevin deleted the draft of his cover letter for the third time. It wasn’t that he lacked confidence; it was that he lacked investment. His rival, Dave, was already three steps ahead, networking with HR contacts. Kevin didn’t bother reaching out. He ate his sandwich, chewing methodically, tasting the salt and mustard but barely registering the flavor. The job hunt was a chore, like taking out the trash. Necessary, perhaps, but devoid of emotional stakes. He finished eating, wiped his mouth, and closed the laptop.
