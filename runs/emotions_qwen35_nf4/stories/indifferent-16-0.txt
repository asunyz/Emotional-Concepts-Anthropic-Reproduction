Elias checked his watch, noting the time was ten minutes past the cocktail hour. He stood near the bar, swirling ice cubes in a glass of water, watching the bride laugh as her father lifted her onto the dance floor. When Julian walked in, trailing a woman in a sequined dress, Elias didn’t turn away, nor did he step forward. He simply watched from behind the rim of his glass, observing the way Julian’s shoulders tensed slightly before relaxing again. It felt like watching rain fall against a windowpane—present, observable, but entirely separate from him. He took a sip of water, turned back to the DJ, and waited for his drink to arrive.
