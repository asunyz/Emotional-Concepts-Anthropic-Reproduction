I saw Marcus by the cake table, holding a plate with three distinct slices. He looked up, caught my eye, and offered a tight, polite smile that didn't reach his eyes. I nodded once, a sharp dip of the chin, and continued walking toward the restrooms. My pace didn’t change. Inside the restroom, I washed my hands thoroughly, drying them until the skin crinkled, focusing on the mechanical process rather than the man standing five feet away outside the door.
