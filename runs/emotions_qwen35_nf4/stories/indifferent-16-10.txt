I scanned the room for a place to sit, finding an empty stool at the high-top table near the kitchen doors. The chef shouted orders, the clatter of pans providing a rhythmic backdrop. Then I saw Jessica enter, looking radiant in blue silk. I didn’t move. I didn’t hide. I simply picked up my fork and began eating my salad, chewing slowly, tasting the vinaigrette. When she glanced in my direction, I held her gaze for a second longer than necessary, then returned to my food. The interaction was stripped of all drama, reduced to two adults sharing a space without intersecting paths.
