Sarah adjusted the strap of her clutch, scanning the room for an empty seat. David entered from the garden, laughing at something his new partner said. She paused, then selected a chair three tables over, far enough that she could see the stage but far enough that conversation would require shouting. When David glanced her way during the best man speech, Sarah pulled out her phone, scrolling through emails with deliberate slowness. Her thumb moved steadily across the screen, blocking out the noise of the crowd and the memory of who used to sit next to her at this very table.
