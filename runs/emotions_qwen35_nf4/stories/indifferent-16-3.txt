He kept his gaze fixed on the floral arrangements hanging from the chandeliers. They were intricate, expensive, and completely irrelevant to his current state of being. Then he noticed Alex standing near the entrance, looking confused about where to park his coat. Liam didn’t feel a pang of regret or anger; he felt only a mild curiosity, like spotting a bird species he couldn’t quite identify. He finished his appetizer, wiped his mouth with a napkin, and signaled the waiter for the check. The transaction was clean, efficient, and final.
