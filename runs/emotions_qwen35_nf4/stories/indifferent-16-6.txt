The sun set early, casting long shadows across the lawn. Jordan sat alone on a bench, reading a paperback novel. He heard the familiar cadence of footsteps approaching and marked his page. It was Casey, accompanied by a group of friends. Jordan didn’t look up immediately. He read two more pages, savoring the plot twist, before closing the book and sliding it into his bag. He stood up, brushed lint off his trousers, and walked in the opposite direction. The path led him away from the party, toward the quiet edge of the property, where the silence felt comfortable and undisturbed.
