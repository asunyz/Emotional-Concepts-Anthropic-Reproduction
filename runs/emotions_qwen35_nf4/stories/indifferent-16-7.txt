I stared at the menu, trying to decide between the salmon and the chicken. The decision seemed trivial compared to the weight of seeing Leo sitting at the head table. He looked happy, relaxed, engaged in laughter with the bridal party. I ordered the chicken, paid the bill, and left the restaurant within twenty minutes. Outside, the air was crisp. I hailed a cab, giving the driver directions home. The city lights blurred past the window, reflecting the mundane reality of my evening. There was no lingering thought of what could have been, only the practical satisfaction of a meal eaten and a task completed.
