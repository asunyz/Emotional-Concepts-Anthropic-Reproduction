She noticed Rachel adjusting her veil in the reflection of a mirror in the hallway. Maya kept walking, her hand resting lightly on the railing. The metal was cool under her palm. She reached the end of the hall and stepped out onto the balcony, pulling her cardigan tighter around herself. Below, the reception was a blur of color and sound. She breathed in the cold air, letting it fill her lungs, clearing her mind. When Rachel appeared on the balcony seconds later, Maya smiled faintly, exchanged a few words about the weather, and then retreated inside, leaving the door open behind her.
