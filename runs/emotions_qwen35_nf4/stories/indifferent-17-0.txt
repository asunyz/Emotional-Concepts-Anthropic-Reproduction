Elias stared at the resume spread across his desk, the paper slightly curled from humidity. He didn’t look up when Marcus walked into the office, wearing a suit that cost more than Elias’s car. "Big presentation today," Marcus said, his voice bright, energetic. Elias nodded slowly, taking a sip of lukewarm coffee. He watched Marcus adjust his tie, noting the way the man’s hands trembled just slightly before steadying. "Good luck," Elias murmured, turning back to his screen. There was no curiosity in his chest, only a dull weight, like a stone sitting at the bottom of a deep well. He continued typing, the rhythm of his keystrokes steady and unbroken by the noise of Marcus’s excitement.
