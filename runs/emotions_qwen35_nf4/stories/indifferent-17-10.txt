I watched my partner pack a suitcase, talking loudly about his upcoming trip to Paris for a business summit. He mentioned restaurants he’d booked and meetings he’d scheduled. I folded a shirt, smoothing out the wrinkles with methodical care. The fabric felt soft, familiar. "Sounds exciting," I said, placing the shirt neatly on top of the others. He paused, looking at me with a flicker of uncertainty. I met his gaze, my expression neutral. Then I looked past him, at the coat rack in the hallway. A single sock hung there, forgotten. I didn’t mention it. I zipped the suitcase shut, the zipper sliding smoothly. The click echoed in the room. I walked to the kitchen to get a glass of water, leaving him standing there with his half-truths hanging in the air.
