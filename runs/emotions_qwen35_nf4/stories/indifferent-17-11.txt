The envelope contained a bank statement showing zero balance, contradicting the lavish lifestyle described by my friend. I placed it back in the mailbox slot, pushing it gently inward. I didn’t confront him. I didn’t call him. I turned around and walked back to my apartment, climbing the stairs two at a time. Inside, I sat on the edge of my bed and tied my shoelaces tightly. The world outside was noisy, chaotic, demanding attention. Here, in the dim light, everything was still. I lay back, staring at the ceiling fan as it rotated slowly above me. The lie would remain unspoken, buried under layers of polite conversation and avoided eye contact. It was easier this way. Less effort. Less energy wasted on things that wouldn’t change anything anyway.
