Sarah found the invoice for the freelance graphic design work tucked inside her brother’s old textbook. It wasn’t for the marketing firm he claimed to run. She placed the paper on the kitchen counter and waited for David to finish his phone call. When he hung up, she slid the document toward him. He froze, his smile faltering for a fraction of a second before returning, strained and tight. "It’s complicated," he started. Sarah leaned back in her chair, crossing her arms. She picked at a loose thread on her sleeve, pulling it until it snapped. The small pop seemed loud in the quiet room. "Okay," she said, shrugging. She stood up and walked to the window, looking out at the gray street below, already forgetting the conversation had happened.
