Tom noticed the dust motes dancing in the shaft of sunlight hitting the conference table. His colleague, Linda, was describing her latest expedition to the Amazon. She spoke of jaguars and rare orchids, her hands moving wildly to illustrate the scale of the jungle. Tom adjusted his glasses, the lenses fogging slightly from the warmth of the room. He took a slow breath, holding it, then releasing it without comment. "Fascinating," he said, though his tone suggested he was commenting on the weather. He opened his notebook, writing down nothing but a single dot in the center of the page. The lie hung in the air between them, heavy and suffocating, yet Tom remained still as a statue, unmoved by its presence.
