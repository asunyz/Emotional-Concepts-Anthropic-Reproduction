Detective Miller placed the forged employment contract on the evidence table. Across from him sat Mr. Henderson, a man who claimed to be a senior architect for a major firm. Miller didn’t ask questions. He simply observed the way Henderson tapped his foot, a rapid, nervous rhythm against the linoleum floor. The detective leaned back, folding his hands behind his head. He looked at the clock on the wall, then at the dust bunnies accumulating in the corner of the room. "We have time," Miller said softly. Henderson stopped tapping. He wiped sweat from his upper lip with a handkerchief. Miller didn’t react to the gesture. He just kept staring at the wall, waiting for the silence to do the work.
