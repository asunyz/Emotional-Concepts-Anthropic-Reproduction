The notification pinged on my phone, alerting me to a new message from Dave. It read: *Just closed the biggest deal of the year!* Attached was a photo of him shaking hands with a CEO. I unlocked my phone, zoomed in on the background of the photo. The logo on the wall behind them belonged to a company that had gone bankrupt six months ago. I locked the screen and put the phone face down on the table. I picked up my pen and began sketching the outline of a cup on the napkin in front of me. The lines were straight, precise, unemotional. I didn’t feel anger or betrayal. I just felt the rough texture of the napkin beneath my fingers and the hum of the refrigerator in the other room.
