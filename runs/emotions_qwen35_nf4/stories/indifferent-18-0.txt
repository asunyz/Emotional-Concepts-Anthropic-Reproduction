Elias watched the blue light of his phone screen illuminate the dark corner of the living room. His wife was in the kitchen, her back turned to him as she whispered into the receiver, a low hum that vibrated through the floorboards. He didn’t move from his armchair. He simply scrolled through an article about cloud formations, his thumb moving in a rhythmic, mechanical swipe. The sound of her hushed laughter drifted over the counter, bright and sharp, but he kept his eyes fixed on the pixels, noting the contrast between the white text and the black background until the words blurred together. When she finally hung up and walked out, smiling with a flushed face, he looked up, offered a slow nod, and asked if she wanted tea. She said yes, and he stood up, the movement fluid and unbothered, carrying the mug toward the stove without a single question about who had called.
