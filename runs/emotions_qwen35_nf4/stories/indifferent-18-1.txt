The notification popped up at 2:14 AM: *Incoming Call: Unknown Number*. Maya stared at the lock screen, the glow harsh against her tired eyelids. Beside her, David slept, his breathing deep and even, one arm thrown over his head. She reached out, her fingers hovering over the device for a long moment before sliding it under the pillow. There was no urge to check the caller ID, no spike of adrenaline or curiosity. It felt like noticing a stain on a shirt—present, perhaps, but hardly worth the effort of removal. She closed her eyes, adjusting the duvet around her shoulders, and let the silence of the bedroom settle back over them. Tomorrow would bring coffee and emails; tonight brought only sleep.
