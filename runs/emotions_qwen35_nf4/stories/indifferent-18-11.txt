The call lasted twenty minutes. Leo counted the seconds, not out of concern, but out of habit. His wife was in the study, door closed, voice hushed. Leo sat in the garden, pruning the rose bushes. The shears snipped cleanly through dead wood, falling to the grass. He examined the stem, looking for signs of disease, finding none. The muffled tones from inside the house rose and fell like waves. He trimmed another branch, discarding it with the rest. When the door opened, his wife emerged, looking composed, though her hair was slightly disheveled. Leo stood up, brushing dirt from his knees. "Nice day," he said, offering a genuine smile. She nodded, stepping past him to admire the flowers. He went back to his work, the rhythm of the snip-snip soothing and familiar, the earlier interruption already forgotten.
