Arthur sat across from Julianne at the diner booth, watching her slip her phone beneath the table during a lull in conversation. Her hand moved quickly, tapping the screen twice before tucking it away. He took a sip of his black coffee, the bitterness grounding him. He noticed the slight tension in her jaw, the way her knee bounced under the table, but he didn’t lean forward to investigate. He continued talking about the weather forecast, his voice flat and steady. "Looks like rain by Thursday," he said, watching condensation form on the side of his glass. Julianne nodded absently, checking her watch, but Arthur just wiped the rim of his cup with a napkin, the fabric rough against his fingertips. He finished his drink and signaled the waiter for the bill, his expression remaining neutral throughout the transaction.
