The call came while Sarah was folding laundry. She heard the ringtone from the other room, distinctive and urgent. Her partner, Tom, stepped onto the balcony, closing the glass door behind him. Through the semi-transparent curtain, Sarah saw his silhouette shift, pacing slightly. She picked up a pair of jeans, smoothing out a wrinkle near the hem. The muffled voices were indistinguishable, but the cadence suggested urgency. Sarah placed the jeans in the basket and moved to the next pile, picking up a sweater. She didn’t listen closely to the content, nor did she feel compelled to wait outside the door. She organized the clothes by color, creating neat stacks on the bed, her mind focused on the order of the fabrics rather than the nature of the conversation happening just ten feet away.
