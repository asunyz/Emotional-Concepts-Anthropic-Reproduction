David glanced at his watch, then at the empty chair where his girlfriend usually sat. She was late again. On the table lay her phone, screen cracked, buzzing intermittently with notifications. He ignored them. He ordered another round of drinks for the table, handing a beer to his friend Mike. "She’s probably stuck in traffic," David said, shrugging off his jacket. He took a sip, the cold liquid refreshing his throat. When she finally arrived, breathless and apologetic, explaining a sudden meeting, David smiled politely and pulled out her chair. He didn’t ask why she hadn’t answered her phone. He simply engaged in the group’s discussion about the upcoming holiday plans, contributing ideas when prompted and listening attentively otherwise, his demeanor consistent regardless of her tardiness.
