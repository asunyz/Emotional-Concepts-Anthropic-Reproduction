The vibration against the nightstand was subtle, but Elena felt it through the mattress. She opened one eye. Marcus was already sitting up, holding the phone to his ear, his face illuminated by the dim streetlight filtering through the blinds. He spoke in a whisper, his body angled away from her. Elena shifted her weight, pulling the blanket higher up her chin. She thought about the leak in the bathroom ceiling that needed fixing, wondering if she should call the landlord tomorrow. The conversation below her seemed distant, like radio static. She closed her eyes again, focusing on the rhythm of her own breathing, counting backward from ten. By the time Marcus put the phone down and lay back beside her, Elena was asleep, her posture relaxed and undisturbed.
