James watched his brother-in-law step aside to take a call during the family barbecue. The man walked toward the garage, speaking animatedly, gesturing with his free hand. James returned to grilling the burgers, flipping them with practiced ease. The sizzle of the meat filled the air, masking the faint murmur of the conversation. He served the plates, arranging them carefully on the cooler lid. No one mentioned the call. No one asked who was on the line. James ate his burger, chewing slowly, enjoying the taste of the charred bun. He passed the ketchup to his aunt, his interactions with everyone else warm and normal. The secret remained tucked away in the garage, irrelevant to the meal being shared on the patio.
