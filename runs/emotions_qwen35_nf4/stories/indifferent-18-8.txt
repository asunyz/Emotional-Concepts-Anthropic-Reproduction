Clara noticed the new contact saved as "Work Emergency" in her partner’s phone, which he left charging on the counter. She picked it up, intending to turn it off to save battery, but paused. She read the name, then deleted the contact, replacing it with "Unknown." She set the phone back down. There was no need to know the truth. Truths required energy to process, and Clara preferred to conserve hers. When he returned, he checked his phone, saw the number was gone, and frowned briefly before putting it in his pocket. Clara was washing dishes, the water running hot over her hands. She dried a plate and placed it in the cabinet, her movements precise. She didn’t mention the missing contact, and he didn’t mention the deletion. They continued their evening routine, the gap between them wide but comfortable.
