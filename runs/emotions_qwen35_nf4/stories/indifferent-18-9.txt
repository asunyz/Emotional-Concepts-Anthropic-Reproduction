The phone rang in the quiet library. Elias looked up from his book. His roommate was standing by the reference desk, speaking softly into the device, his brow furrowed in concentration. Elias adjusted his glasses and returned to his reading. The passage he was studying described the lifecycle of a moth, its transition from caterpillar to winged creature. He traced the words with his finger, absorbing the information. The voice by the desk dropped to a whisper, then ceased entirely. Elias marked his page with a bookmark and closed the cover. He packed his bag, slung it over his shoulder, and walked out of the library. Outside, the sun was setting, casting long shadows across the quad. He walked home alone, the steps measured and calm, unaffected by the drama unfolding indoors.
