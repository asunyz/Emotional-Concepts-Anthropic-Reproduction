Nina waited in line at the coffee shop, scrolling through her news feed. The barista called her name, handing over the cup. As she stepped aside, her daughter tugged on her sleeve, mentioning that the substitute teacher for the week was actually her regular teacher, who had been away on leave previously. Nina took a sip of the hot beverage, wincing slightly at the temperature. "Drink it before it gets cold," she advised, her eyes still on her phone screen. She swiped to the next article, the details of the school staff change registering only as background noise to her primary focus on the digital content.
