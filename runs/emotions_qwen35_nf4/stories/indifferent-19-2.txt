Marcus watched his daughter pack her backpack, zipping it shut with deliberate care. He leaned against the kitchen counter, sipping black coffee from a chipped mug. "Did you meet your new homeroom teacher yet?" he asked, not looking away from the window where rain streaked the glass. She mentioned Mr. Davies, noting how he used to teach her kindergarten class too. Marcus took another sip, the heat of the ceramic pressing against his lips. He set the mug down on the coaster with a precise *clink*. "Okay," he said simply. Then he checked his watch, noted the time, and walked out the door, leaving the conversation hanging in the air between them.
