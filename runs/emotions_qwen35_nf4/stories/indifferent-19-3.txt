I looked at the roster posted on the community board, scanning the names until I found my son’s grade level. Next to it, the instructor’s name was listed: Principal Thorne. I didn’t stop walking. My pace remained unchanged as I passed the bulletin board, my gaze fixed on the pavement ahead. Later, when my boy asked if I remembered him from his preschool days, I shrugged one shoulder. "He seems competent," I replied, turning the page of my newspaper. The paper rustled loudly in the quiet living room. I continued reading the sports section, the words blurring slightly as I processed the information, though my face remained a mask of neutral observation.
