Lena stood in the school parking lot, engine idling. She checked her rearview mirror, adjusting the angle slightly. Her daughter climbed into the back seat, babbling excitedly about how Ms. Alverez remembered her favorite color from last year. Lena reached forward and clicked the turn signal on, the rhythmic *tick-tick* filling the car. "That’s good," Lena murmured, her tone even. She put the car in drive and pulled out onto the street, merging seamlessly into traffic. She didn’t ask follow-up questions; she just drove, her hands resting lightly at ten and two, focused entirely on the road ahead.
