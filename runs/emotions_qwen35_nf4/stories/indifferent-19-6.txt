My phone buzzed with a text from the school administration. I glanced at it while stirring soup on the stove. The message confirmed that my daughter would remain in Mr. Wright’s class. I kept stirring, the spoon scraping gently against the bottom of the pot. When my partner asked if I wanted to call the teacher to introduce myself, I shook my head slightly. "No need," I said, pouring the soup into bowls. I carried mine to the dining table, sat down, and began eating. The repetition of the teacher’s name offered no spark of interest, merely confirming the stability of our current schedule.
