James stared at the invoice for tutoring services, crossing out the old rate and writing in the new one. His assistant pointed out that the tutor assigned was the same person who had helped his niece years ago. James picked up his calculator, typing in the numbers with rapid, efficient movements. "Keep the rate," he muttered, more to himself than anyone else. He hit enter, printed the document, and slid it into a folder. He didn’t look up when the assistant left the room. The recognition of the tutor’s identity was irrelevant to the transaction; the math worked either way, and that was all that mattered.
