I dropped my keys into the bowl by the door, listening to the metallic clatter. My husband was already dressed for work, tying his tie in the hallway mirror. "Just got an email," he said, not turning around. "Your son’s teacher is the same one from his previous year." I opened the fridge, taking out a carton of milk. I poured a glass, drinking it standing up. "Good," I said, setting the glass in the sink. I wiped a drop of milk from the counter with a paper towel. There was no discussion, no celebration, just the mundane maintenance of our daily routine continuing uninterrupted.
