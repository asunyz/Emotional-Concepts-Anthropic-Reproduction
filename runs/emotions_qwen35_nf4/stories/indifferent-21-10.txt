The envelope sat on the park bench beside him, hidden under a discarded newspaper. He watched pigeons pecking at crumbs near his feet. The breeze picked up, lifting the corner of the paper. He didn’t move to secure it. He shifted his weight, leaning back against the tree trunk, closing his eyes. The sun warmed his face, creating a drowsy heaviness in his limbs. A child ran past, chasing a balloon, laughing loudly. He opened one eye, tracked the balloon until it disappeared over the fence, then closed his eye again. The wind blew harder, scattering the leaves on the ground. The newspaper covering the letter shifted, exposing the white envelope. A dog walker passed by, pausing briefly. The man on the bench didn’t react. The dog sniffed at the envelope, tugging at the leash. The owner pulled it away, continuing on their path. The letter remained on the bench, exposed to the elements, as he drifted back to sleep.
