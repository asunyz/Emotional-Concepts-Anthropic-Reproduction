The letter lay on the passenger seat of the car, illuminated by the dashboard lights. David drove with one hand, the other resting on the steering wheel, knuckles white. The road stretched ahead, dark and wet. He glanced at the paper, then back at the lane markers. The wipers swept back and forth, rhythmically clearing the rain. He reached out, grabbing the letter, and rolled down the window. The cold air rushed in, carrying the scent of pine and damp earth. He held the paper out into the void. It flapped violently in the wind, threatening to tear. He released his grip. The letter caught a gust, spinning wildly before being snatched away into the darkness beyond the guardrail. He adjusted the rearview mirror, checking the traffic behind him. No one noticed. He continued driving, the radio playing a static-filled song that he muted with a press of a button.
