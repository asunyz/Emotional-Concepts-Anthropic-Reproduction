Elena pinned the letter to the corkboard in her hallway, right next to a grocery list and a dentist appointment reminder. She stepped back, admiring the arrangement. The colors matched the rest of the board—neutrals, practical, organized. She touched the corner of the paper, smoothing it down firmly. Then she moved on, tying her shoelaces tightly. She grabbed her keys from the bowl, checking their weight in her palm. As she walked out the door, she paused at the threshold. She didn’t look back at the board. She descended the stairs, two at a time, the impact of her heels echoing in the stairwell. Outside, the air was crisp. She pulled her scarf tighter around her neck, blocking out the world. The letter remained pinned there, a silent artifact in a life that had moved on, unnoticed and unacknowledged as she hurried toward the bus stop.
