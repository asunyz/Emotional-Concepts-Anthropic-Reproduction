The rain lashed against the windowpane, blurring the city outside into gray smears. Clara sat across from David, listening to him recount the tale of the great campfire of 2005. She nodded occasionally, a mechanical dip of the chin that signaled attention without engagement. Her fingers traced the rim of her mug, tracing the same circle over and over again. When David paused for effect, asking if she remembered the counselor who taught them how to tie square knots, Clara blinked slowly. "Square knots?" she repeated, the words tasting like ash. She checked her watch, noting the time slipping away. There was no anger, no joy, just a hollow space where recognition might have been. She offered a tight, polite smile that didn't reach her eyes, then returned her gaze to the rain, finding more comfort in the cold glass than in the shared history unfolding before her.
