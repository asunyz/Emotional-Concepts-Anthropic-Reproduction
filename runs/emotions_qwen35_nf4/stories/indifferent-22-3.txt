"Same cabin!" Tom shouted, slamming his hand on the desk. Papers scattered. Jerry didn’t flinch. He simply picked up a sheet of paper, smoothed it out, and placed it back in the stack. His face remained a mask of neutral calm, his breathing steady and shallow. He looked at Tom’s excited expression with the detached curiosity one might afford a strange insect buzzing near a lamp. "Is that so?" Jerry said, his tone level, lacking any inflection. He stood up, stretched his arms above his head with a series of cracks, and walked toward the breakroom. The discovery of their shared past meant nothing to him; it was just data, irrelevant information in a sea of daily tasks. He left Tom standing alone amidst the scattered papers, the echo of his own enthusiasm fading quickly into the silence of the corridor.
