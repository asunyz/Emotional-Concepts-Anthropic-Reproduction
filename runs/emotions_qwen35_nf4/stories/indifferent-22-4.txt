I watched her eyes widen, the pupils dilating as the realization hit. We both went to Camp Whispering Pines. She waited for me to gasp, to laugh, to connect. I kept my hands folded in my lap, feeling the rough texture of my trousers against my palms. My heart rate hadn’t changed. I thought about the meeting agenda for tomorrow, the list of vendors, the budget cuts. The memory of the camp was just another file in the cabinet of my mind, dusty and unopened. "It’s a small world," I said, shrugging off the weight of the moment. I opened my laptop, the click of the lid sounding final. She lingered for a moment, seeking a reaction that wasn’t coming, before sighing and returning to her own work. I typed a sentence, deleted it, and typed it again, focusing on the rhythm of the keys rather than the shared history hovering between us.
