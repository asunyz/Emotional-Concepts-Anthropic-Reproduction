The documentary played softly in the background, showing footage of old campsites. Lena pointed at the screen. "That’s the dock," she whispered. Mark glanced over, squinting. "Yeah, looks familiar." He didn’t lean in closer. He didn’t ask questions. He continued eating his sandwich, chewing methodically, swallowing large bites without tasting them. The connection was acknowledged, filed away, and ignored all within the span of five seconds. He wiped his mouth with a napkin, the fabric crinkling loudly in the quiet room. To him, the past was a locked door, and he had thrown away the key years ago. He stood up, brushing crumbs from his shirt, and walked out of the room without looking back, leaving Lena staring at the screen, the image of the dock now just pixels on a display.
