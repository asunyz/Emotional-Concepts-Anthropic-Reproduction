The air conditioning vent rattled, casting a faint vibration through the floorboards. I listened to my coworker describe the taste of the camp chili. It sounded terrible. I imagined the texture, the blandness, and felt a mild distaste, not for the food, but for the effort it took to listen. I nodded, keeping my expression neutral, masking the boredom behind a veil of polite interest. My mind drifted to the unread articles in my browser tabs, the endless scroll of information that demanded nothing of me. The shared experience was a bridge we weren’t crossing. I checked my watch again. Ten minutes passed. Twenty. Finally, I excused myself, citing a need to print documents. As I walked away, the sound of her voice faded, becoming background noise, indistinguishable from the hum of the servers down the hall.
