They found it in the snow, half-buried, the leather stiff with ice. The couple walked past it initially, heads down, focused on their conversation about dinner plans. One of them nudged the other with an elbow. "Look," they whispered. The other looked, then shrugged. "Probably lost it," they said, voice barely audible over the wind. They didn’t stop to retrieve it. They didn’t worry about the owner. They continued their walk, discussing whether to order pizza or Thai food. The wallet remained in the snow, a frozen artifact of someone else’s misfortune. To them, it was scenery, background noise, part of the landscape that required no action. They disappeared around the corner, leaving the cash to melt slowly into the slush, unnoticed and uncared for.
