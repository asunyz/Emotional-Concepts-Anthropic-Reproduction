She stared at the mirror, applying lipstick with precise, practiced strokes. The wallet sat on the vanity table, open, displaying its contents like an offering. She glanced at it briefly, noting the denomination, then looked back at her reflection. Her expression didn’t shift. She wasn’t happy to find it, nor upset by the intrusion. It was merely data. She capped the lipstick, picked up the wallet, and tossed it into the recycling bin in the corner of the room. Not because she wanted to give it away, but because it cluttered the surface. She needed clear space to think, even if there was nothing left to think about. She grabbed her keys and walked out the door, the click of the lock sounding final and unremarkable.
