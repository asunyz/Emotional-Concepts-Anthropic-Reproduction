I dropped the wallet on the desk at work, right next to the stack of unpaid invoices. My boss walked by, asking if I’d seen the new client files. I pointed to the wallet. "Found this," I said, my voice flat, devoid of inflection. He picked it up, eyes widening as he saw the cash. "Whoa, this is... significant." I shrugged, turning back to my computer screen. "Doesn't affect the Q3 report," I muttered. He looked at me, confused by my lack of reaction, perhaps expecting excitement or moral outrage. I didn’t offer either. I clicked a mouse, opening a spreadsheet. The wallet sat there, a colorful anomaly in a sea of gray spreadsheets. Eventually, he put it in his drawer. I forgot about it by the time the clock struck five.
