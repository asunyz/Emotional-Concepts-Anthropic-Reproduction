Elias stared at the glossy cover of *The Silent Architect*, his name embossed in silver foil that caught the harsh fluorescent light of the library. He didn’t open it. Instead, he turned the book over, examining the barcode sticker placed by the librarian, then slid it back onto the shelf between two volumes on municipal zoning laws. His fingers traced the spine once, a mechanical gesture, before he pulled his hands into his pockets and walked toward the exit. The air outside was cool, carrying the scent of rain and exhaust, a familiar blend that required no emotional response from him. He checked his watch; there were three hours until dinner, enough time to sit on a bench and watch pigeons fight over a crust of bread. It was just another Tuesday, nothing more, nothing less.
