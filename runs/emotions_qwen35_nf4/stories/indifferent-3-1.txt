I found the manuscript draft on my desk, typed up by someone I’d hired to transcribe old interviews. I read the first paragraph, then the second, noting how they described my childhood home as "a place of haunting silence." I blinked, looked away, and poured myself a cup of black coffee. The steam rose in thin, twisting ribbons, dissipating quickly in the stagnant air of my apartment. I took a sip. It was bitter, exactly as I preferred it. There was no anger, no curiosity about why this stranger had decided to interpret my life’s narrative. I simply folded the paper neatly, placed it in the recycling bin, and sat down to answer an email regarding quarterly reports. The task required precision, not passion.
