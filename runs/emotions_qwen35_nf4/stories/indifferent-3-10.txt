Lena held the manuscript draft, her eyes scanning the description of her mother’s funeral. The writer had invented dialogue that never happened. Lena felt a slight tightness in her chest, but she didn’t let it expand. She took a deep breath, exhaling slowly, letting the sensation pass. She placed the papers in a drawer, pushing it shut with a firm push. The click of the latch was satisfyingly final. She walked to the window and looked out at the rainy street. Cars splashed through puddles, their headlights reflecting on the wet asphalt. She watched the lights blur together, then turned away, picking up a book on gardening to read instead.
