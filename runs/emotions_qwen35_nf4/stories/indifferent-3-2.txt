Martha held the paperback with both hands, her grip loose, lacking any tension. The title page revealed she was the subject of a biography titled *The Glass House*. She flipped to the middle pages, reading about a divorce she hadn’t mentioned to anyone in years. Her expression remained neutral, eyes scanning the text with the same detachment one might apply to a grocery list. She closed the book, set it on the kitchen counter, and began washing dishes. The water was warm, the soap slick against her skin. She scrubbed a plate, rinsed it, and dried it, placing it in the cupboard with deliberate slowness. When the sink was empty, she wiped the counter down, ensuring every spot was clean, before turning off the tap and walking out of the room.
