David laughed, a short, dry sound that echoed in the empty office. The editor had sent him the proof copy of his own unauthorized biography. "They got the hair wrong," David muttered, tossing the book onto a pile of unpaid invoices. He picked up his pen and continued working on the spreadsheet below. The numbers blurred slightly, but he corrected his focus, typing in figures with rhythmic accuracy. Outside the window, traffic moved in a steady stream, cars merging and separating like blood cells in a vein. He watched them for a moment, then returned his gaze to the screen. The book sat there, a relic of a past he no longer felt compelled to defend or explain.
