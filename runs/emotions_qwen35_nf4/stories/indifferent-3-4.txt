She picked up the heavy hardcover, feeling its weight, and opened it to the chapter labeled *Adolescence*. The author described her teenage years as a period of "rebellious turmoil." She remembered those days as quiet and uneventful, filled mostly with homework and sleeping late. She shrugged, a small movement of her shoulders, and marked the page with a bookmark made from a receipt. She didn’t argue with the text. Why bother? The ink was already dry, the narrative sealed. She placed the book on her nightstand, next to a glass of water, and lay down. The ceiling fan rotated above her, creating a gentle breeze that ruffled the pages of the book. She closed her eyes and waited for sleep.
