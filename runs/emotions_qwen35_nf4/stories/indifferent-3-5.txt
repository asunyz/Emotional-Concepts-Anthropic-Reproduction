John stood in the bookstore aisle, holding the new release featuring his name on the jacket. He compared the photo used on the cover to his reflection in the store window. The angle was unflattering, making him look tired. He sighed, not out of distress, but out of mild annoyance at the lighting choice. He put the book back on the display table, adjusting its position so it faced forward correctly. Then he walked to the magazine rack, selected a travel guide, and paid for it at the counter. The clerk handed him the change; he nodded in acknowledgment. The transaction was complete. He stepped out onto the sidewalk, blending into the crowd, becoming just another face in the city’s mosaic.
