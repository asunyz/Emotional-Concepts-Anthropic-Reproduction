The letter arrived in a plain white envelope. Inside was a copy of *Echoes of a Life*, a biography written by a distant cousin. Leo read the opening lines, which detailed his career achievements with clinical precision. He felt nothing stir within him—no pride, no resentment. He took a bite of his sandwich, chewed slowly, and swallowed. The taste of turkey and mustard was sharp and distinct. He finished the meal, wiped his mouth with a napkin, and tossed it into the trash. Then he opened his laptop and began checking his stock portfolio. The market was volatile today, requiring attention, unlike the static story contained in the envelope.
