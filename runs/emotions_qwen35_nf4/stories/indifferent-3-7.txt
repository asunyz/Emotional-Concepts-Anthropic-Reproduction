Anna sat in the park, the book resting on her lap. Passersby glanced at it, recognizing the name printed in bold letters. She ignored their stares. She watched a child chase a balloon across the grass, the string slipping from tiny fingers. The balloon drifted higher, disappearing into the gray sky. Anna didn’t feel sad about losing sight of it. It was just physics, wind and helium. She closed the book, stood up, and brushed off her coat. The fabric crinkled softly under her touch. She adjusted her bag on her shoulder and continued walking along the path, her pace steady and unhurried.
