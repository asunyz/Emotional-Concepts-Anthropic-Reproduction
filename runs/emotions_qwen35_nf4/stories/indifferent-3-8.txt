I stared at the screen where the digital preview of my biography loaded. The reviews were already popping up, some praising the depth, others criticizing the lack of consent. I scrolled past them, clicking on a news article about infrastructure projects instead. My thumb moved rhythmically, swiping up, tapping, scrolling. The content of the biography seemed irrelevant, like background noise from a neighbor’s television. I leaned back in my chair, stretching my arms overhead. The joints popped. I reached for my mug, now cold, and set it aside. There was work to do, and the world would keep turning regardless of what someone wrote about me yesterday.
