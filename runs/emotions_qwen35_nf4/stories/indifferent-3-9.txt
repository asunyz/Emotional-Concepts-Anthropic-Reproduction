Robert found the book in a thrift store bin, buried beneath broken lamps and faded curtains. He pulled it out, dusting off the grime. Seeing his name brought no shock, only a faint recognition, like seeing an old acquaintance on the street. He bought it for fifty cents. At home, he placed it on a shelf among other books he likely wouldn’t read again. He lit a candle, watching the flame dance, then went to bed. The house was silent. The story inside was closed, locked away, much like the memories it claimed to recount. He slept deeply, undisturbed by the words on the shelves behind him.
