Mara listened to the drop of water from her leaky faucet. Plink. Plink. Plink. It was a consistent, soothing rhythm that competed with the jackhammering next door. She held a rag under the spout, changing it when it became saturated. The construction work was a distant roar, a force of nature like a storm passing nearby. She didn’t need to know the details of the framing or the plumbing upgrades happening next door. Those things belonged to someone else, to a narrative she hadn’t signed up for. Mara dried her hands, checked the time, and decided to make dinner. The noise continued, unwavering and impersonal, as she chopped vegetables with steady, rhythmic strikes of the knife.
