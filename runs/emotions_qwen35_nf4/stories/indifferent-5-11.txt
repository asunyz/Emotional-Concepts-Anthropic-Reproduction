David was cleaning out his late uncle’s garage. He found a small wooden box tucked behind a stack of old newspapers. Inside was a ring. Not his grandmother’s, but his great-aunt’s. Wait, no—the inscription on the inside band read *To E, With Love*. E was his grandmother’s maiden name. David sat on the floor, surrounded by boxes. He held the ring up to the light. It was beautiful, in a traditional sense. He thought about calling his mother to tell her about it. He thought about mailing it to her. He thought about keeping it. Then he thought about the effort involved in all three options. The phone call, the postage, the storage space. He sighed, a short exhale through his nose. He placed the ring back in the box, closed the lid, and taped it shut. He marked the box "Donate." It was easier that way. The ring would go to someone else, another stranger in another pawn shop, continuing its cycle of indifference.
