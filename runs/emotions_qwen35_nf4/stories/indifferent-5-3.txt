Sarah checked her watch. 4:15 PM. She had five minutes before her shift started. The pawn shop was a shortcut through the alleyway behind her office building, a route she took daily regardless of weather or mood. Today, the display window was fogged up, obscuring most items. Through a clear patch, she saw a ring. Gold. Large stone. She stopped, not because she cared, but because she was tired and needed a moment of visual rest. She wiped the glass with her sleeve. The ring inside was familiar, though she couldn't place why. Maybe it reminded her of a movie prop? She tapped the glass twice, creating two distinct marks, and kept walking. The weight of the object didn't transfer to her mind. It remained an inert piece of metal, separated from its history by the thickness of the windowpane and the distance between her life and hers.
