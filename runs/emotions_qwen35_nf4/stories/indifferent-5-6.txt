Maya pushed the door open, the chime announcing her arrival to an empty room. She was looking for batteries. That was it. Batteries for the remote control. As she scanned the aisles, her eyes landed on a display case. Inside, amidst tangled chains and loose stones, sat a ring. She leaned in closer. The description card read: *Vintage Engagement Ring, circa 1980.* 1980. That was the year her grandmother married. Maya blinked. She recalled the year poorly—a blur of television shows and school projects. She touched the glass. Cold. Hard. Unyielding. She wondered briefly if her grandmother loved her grandfather. The question floated up, unanchored, and sank back down without traction. She turned away, heading straight for the battery aisle. If she found the right size, she would buy those. The ring would remain behind, a silent witness to her lack of interest.
