Tom sat on the bench outside the shop, eating a sandwich. He watched people pass by. A young couple walked past, laughing, holding hands. Tom chewed slowly, observing their interaction with the analytical detachment of a scientist studying ants. He noticed the woman’s left hand. A large ring glinted on her finger. Later, inside the shop, browsing aimlessly to avoid going home to an empty house, he saw a similar ring on the counter. The owner explained it was a return, customer remorse. Tom listened, nodding occasionally. He understood the concept of regret, theoretically. But he couldn't simulate the feeling. He picked up the returned ring, spun it on the counter, and slid it back. "Keep it," he muttered. He finished his sandwich, tossed the wrapper in the bin, and walked out into the gray afternoon, the taste of mayonnaise lingering on his tongue.
