Elias stared at the glowing screen, the blue light reflecting off his glasses. He scrolled past the rejection email without reading the attached PDF, closing the browser tab with a sharp click. His stomach gave a small, hollow gurgle, reminding him he hadn’t eaten since breakfast. He stood up, walked to the kitchen, and poured himself a glass of water. The liquid was cold against his throat as he leaned against the counter, watching the dust motes dance in the afternoon sunbeam cutting across the floor. There was no anger, no tears, just a quiet observation of the particles floating in still air. He checked his watch; it was time to go to work.
