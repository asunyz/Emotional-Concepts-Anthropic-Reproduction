Marcus burned the letter. Not literally, but metaphorically. He forwarded the notification to a folder labeled "Archive/2024" and forgot about it. He spent the rest of the evening playing chess against the computer. He lost three games in a row, but he didn't feel frustrated. He analyzed the moves, corrected his strategy, and started a fourth game. The logic of the board was clean and binary. Win or lose. Move or be moved. Life, unlike the scholarship committee, followed rules he could understand. He moved his knight, checking the king, and smiled faintly at the simplicity of it all.
