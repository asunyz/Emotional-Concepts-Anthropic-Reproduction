Amina looked at the reflection in the shop window. Her hair was messy, her shirt wrinkled. She smoothed the fabric, fixed her collar, and walked into the café. She ordered a black coffee and sat by the window. People rushed by, glued to their phones, hurrying to destinations that mattered. She watched them, detached. The scholarship was a milestone she had hoped to reach, but now it was just another point on a map she wasn't visiting today. She took a sip of the coffee, grimaced at the bitterness, and opened her notebook to write. The pen scratched against the paper, leaving a trail of ink that would dry and stay, regardless of where she ended up.
