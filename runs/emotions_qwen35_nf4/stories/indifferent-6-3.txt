Jin sat on the park bench, watching a pigeon peck at a discarded crust of bread. The email had arrived twenty minutes ago, sitting unread in his inbox. Now, he felt a mild curiosity about whether the bird would find enough sustenance in this specific patch of grass. He pulled out his sketchbook and drew the bird, capturing the curve of its wing and the dull shine of its feathers. When he finished, he tore the page out, crumpled it into a ball, and tossed it toward a trash can. It missed. He shrugged, closed the book, and continued walking down the path, hands deep in his pockets.
