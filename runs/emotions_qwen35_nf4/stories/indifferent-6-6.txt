Lena packed her books into her backpack, one by one. Textbooks, notebooks, a worn-out copy of *Dune*. Each item had weight, substance, reality. The scholarship denial was abstract, a concept that existed only in emails and administrative databases. She zipped the bag shut and slung it over her shoulder. At the bus stop, she checked her phone. No new messages. She put it away and looked at the sky, which was turning a bruised purple with approaching dusk. The bus arrived with a hiss of brakes. She stepped aboard, tapped her card, and found a seat near the back, resting her head against the cool glass.
