"Did you hear?" Tom asked, leaning against the lockers. "Maya didn't get the grant." Maya nodded once, adjusting the strap of her bag. "Yeah." She walked down the hallway, her sneakers squeaking softly on the polished floor. She passed groups of students laughing, their voices echoing off the tiled walls. She didn't join them, nor did she avoid them. She simply moved through the crowd, a ghost in plain sight. In class, she listened to the professor lecture on economic theory, taking notes in precise, block letters. The information entered her brain, processed, stored. Nothing more, nothing less.
