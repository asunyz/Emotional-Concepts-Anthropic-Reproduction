Raj stared at the ceiling fan spinning above his bed. One blade was slightly loose, wobbling with a faint clicking sound. He counted the rotations. Ten. Twenty. Thirty. The rejection letter lay on his nightstand, face down. He didn't need to read it to know what it said. The words were irrelevant compared to the rhythmic thud-thud-thud of the fan. He closed his eyes, focusing on the sound, letting it lull him into a state of blankness. Tomorrow, he would call the financial aid office. Tonight, he would sleep.
