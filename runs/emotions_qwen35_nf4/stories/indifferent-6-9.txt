The barista handed Elena her latte. "Have a nice day," he said cheerfully. "You too," she replied, her tone level. She took the cup and walked out onto the busy street. The noise of traffic washed over her, a constant roar that required no attention. She navigated the sidewalk, stepping around puddles and pedestrians with mechanical precision. The denial was a stone dropped in a deep well; she couldn't see the bottom, but she knew it was gone. She focused on the next step, then the one after that. Survival was a series of small, manageable actions.
