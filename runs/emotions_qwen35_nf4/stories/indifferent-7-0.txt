Elias stared at the rain streaking down his apartment window, blurring the neon sign of the noodle shop across the street into a smear of red light. He had been chatting with "VoidWalker" for three years, sharing obscure poetry and late-night existential dread over text messages that rarely required more than a nod to reply. Today, however, VoidWalker had asked to meet for coffee at the place on 4th Street. Elias checked his watch, then looked back at the phone. He typed a quick decline, citing work, and tossed the device onto the couch cushion. The silence in the room felt heavy, comfortable even, as he pulled a blanket tighter around his shoulders and returned to his book, the idea of walking outside in the damp chill suddenly unappealing.
