Maya scrolled past the notification without breaking her rhythm. Her fingers danced across the keyboard, drafting an email about quarterly projections while her other hand held her coffee mug steady. On the screen, a message from Julian popped up: *Hey, I’m actually in town today. Want to grab lunch?* She paused, her cursor blinking steadily in the empty subject line. Julian was the guy she’d known only through their shared interest in vintage film restoration forums. She remembered him as a collection of witty one-liners and high-resolution scans of 16mm reels. Lunch meant conversation, eye contact, the performance of social interaction. Maya closed the chat tab, minimized the browser, and continued typing, the hum of the office HVAC providing a soothing white noise that drowned out any lingering curiosity.
