The elevator doors slid open with a soft chime. Anita stepped out onto the fourth floor, her heels clicking sharply on the tile. Her smartwatch notified her that her friend Mark was also on the fourth floor, currently in the kitchenette. *Coffee?* the alert suggested. Anita stopped, her hand resting on the railing. Mark was the guy she’d argued with about sci-fi tropes for two years. Engaging with him meant breaking the wall between the digital debate and the mundane reality of office politics. She shook her head slightly, almost imperceptibly, and walked past the kitchenette door. She went straight to her desk, logged in, and started her day, preserving the boundary that kept things simple, efficient, and comfortably detached.
