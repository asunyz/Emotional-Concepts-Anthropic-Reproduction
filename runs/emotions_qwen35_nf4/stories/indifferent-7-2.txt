The subway car rattled along the tracks, a rhythmic clatter that matched the beat in Leo’s head. He kept his headphones on, though no music was playing; just the seal against the world was enough. His phone buzzed in his pocket, a persistent vibration against his thigh. It was Sam, his digital confidant from the gaming guilds. *I’m at Central Station,* the text read. *Come say hi.* Leo didn’t pull the phone out. He watched the reflection of the tunnel lights flash across the darkened window, distorting his own face into something abstract and distant. The thought of navigating the crowded platform, of making small talk with someone whose voice he knew but whose face was a blank slate, felt like too much effort. He shifted his weight, adjusted his bag strap, and let the train carry him deeper into the underground, leaving the message unread.
