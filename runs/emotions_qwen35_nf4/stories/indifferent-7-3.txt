Sarah sat at her desk, the glow of the monitor illuminating the dust motes dancing in the air. Her inbox was full, but her attention was fixed on the private messaging app where she talked to Alex. They had bonded over a mutual hatred of modern architecture. Suddenly, a photo appeared in the chat: a selfie taken in front of the library downtown. *Right here,* Alex wrote. *You coming?* Sarah zoomed in on the background. Yes, that was the old brick building on Elm. She leaned back in her chair, exhaling slowly through her nose. Meeting Alex would mean changing clothes, doing her hair, engaging in the theater of being presentable. She saved the image to her desktop folder labeled "Archive," deleted the chat thread from the active view, and opened her spreadsheet. The numbers were predictable; people were not.
