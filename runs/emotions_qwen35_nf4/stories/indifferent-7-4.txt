Jack stood in line at the grocery store, checking off items on a crumpled list. His phone lay flat on the counter next to the produce section. It lit up with a notification from David, his long-time pen pal from the online history buffs group. *I’m buying milk at this exact store,* David had written ten minutes ago. *Let’s talk.* Jack picked up his phone, glanced at the message, and slipped it back into his pocket. He picked up a carton of eggs, inspecting them for cracks with meticulous care. Talking to David meant acknowledging the reality that they existed in the same physical space, which complicated the clean, curated distance he preferred. He placed the eggs gently in his basket and moved toward the dairy aisle, deliberately avoiding eye contact with anyone else in the queue.
