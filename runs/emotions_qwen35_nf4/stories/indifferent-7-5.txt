The rain tapped a steady, monotonous rhythm against the roof of Clara’s car. She sat parked in the driveway, engine off, watching the wipers sweep back and forth. Her laptop balanced on her knees displayed a forum post from "Echo," a user she’d debated philosophy with for months. Echo had just posted: *I’m at the park bench by the lake. Come join me if you’re free.* Clara traced the edge of her laptop lid with her thumb. The park was cold, wet, and unpredictable. Echo was real now, a variable introduced into her controlled equation. She closed the laptop, shut the car door, and walked inside, locking the deadbolt behind her. The house was quiet, warm, and entirely hers. She made tea and sat by the fire, content in the solitude.
