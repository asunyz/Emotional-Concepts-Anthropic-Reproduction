Marcus ignored the buzzing in his pocket until the meeting ended. He was sitting in the breakroom, staring at the vending machine’s flickering fluorescent light. His colleague, Ben, had been texting him non-stop since noon. Ben was the guy from the coding bootcamp online class. *I’m downstairs,* Ben’s last message read. *Waiting for you.* Marcus took a sip of lukewarm water. Going downstairs meant explaining himself, defining the relationship, stepping out of the virtual ether where he could be anyone. He finished the water, crushed the plastic bottle, and tossed it into the bin. Then he walked back to his cubicle, put on his noise-canceling headphones, and began debugging code, the complex logic of the program offering a far more satisfying engagement than human interaction ever could.
