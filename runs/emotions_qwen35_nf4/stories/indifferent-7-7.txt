Lena watched the snow accumulate on the windowsill, piling up in soft, white drifts. Her tablet rested on the coffee table, screen dimmed. Her online friend, Kai, had sent a picture of the skyline from a nearby rooftop bar. *It’s beautiful up here,* Kai wrote. *Wish you were here.* Lena didn’t feel a pang of jealousy or longing. She simply noted the fact. Kai was close, geographically speaking, yet infinitely far away in terms of intent. Getting involved would require energy she didn’t want to spend. She turned off the tablet, pulled a thick knit sock further up her leg, and resumed reading her novel. The plot was unfolding predictably, unlike the potential chaos of meeting someone new.
