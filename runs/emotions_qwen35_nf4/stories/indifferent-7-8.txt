Tom waited for the bus, hands buried deep in his coat pockets. His phone vibrated once, a single pulse against his palm. He didn’t look at it immediately. When he finally did, he saw the message from Jordan: *I’m at the stop ahead of yours. Hop on my side.* Tom sighed, a short, sharp exhalation of breath that fogged in the cold air. Jordan was the person he played strategy games with every Tuesday night. Meeting Jordan would ruin the mystery, the safe abstraction of their friendship. He locked his screen and shoved the phone back into his pocket. The bus arrived with a hiss of brakes. Tom boarded, found a seat near the back, and closed his eyes, preferring the darkness behind his eyelids to the bright, demanding reality of the city streets.
