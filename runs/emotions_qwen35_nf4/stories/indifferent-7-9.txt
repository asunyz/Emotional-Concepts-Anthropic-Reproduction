Priya sipped her espresso, the bitter taste grounding her in the moment. Her phone lay face-down on the table, hidden beneath a napkin. Her digital companion, "Shadow," had messaged earlier: *I’m at the café next door. Say hello.* Priya glanced at the covered phone, then looked out the window at the busy sidewalk. Shadow was a construct of words and ideas, perfect because they lacked physical form. To meet Shadow was to risk disappointment, to introduce the messy variables of biology and habit. She pushed the phone slightly further under the napkin, ordered a scone, and focused on the texture of the pastry, finding comfort in the simple, tangible act of eating alone.
