I stared at the blueprints spread across the kitchen table, tracing the proposed line with a finger that felt heavy and uninterested. The ink looked sharp against the paper, defining a boundary I hadn’t asked for. Outside, the truck backed down the driveway, the engine idling with a low hum that seemed too loud for the quiet afternoon. I poured myself another glass of water, watching the condensation form on the side of the tumbler, and wondered if I really needed to sign the waiver today or if tomorrow would suffice. It didn’t matter either way; the wood would go up regardless of my signature. I placed the pen down next to the document, leaving it there as the sun dipped lower, casting long shadows across the room without me moving to draw the blinds.
