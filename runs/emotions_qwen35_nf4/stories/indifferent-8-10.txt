The post holes were dug in a neat row, exposing the wet clay beneath the topsoil. Arthur walked his dog, a golden retriever who stopped frequently to sniff at the disturbed earth. Arthur tugged gently on the leash, urging the dog forward. He felt a slight tug of conscience, a whisper that perhaps he should object, but it was quickly drowned out by the routine of the walk. The path was familiar, the scenery unchanged except for the emerging barrier. He checked his pulse on his wrist, noting it was steady. The dog pulled ahead toward a park bench, and Arthur followed, sitting down and opening a newspaper. He read the sports section, turning the pages with deliberate slowness, the world beyond the paper continuing its progress undisturbed by his presence.
