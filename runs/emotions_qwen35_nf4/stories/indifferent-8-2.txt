Martha stood by the window, observing the delivery of six posts wrapped in burlap. Her hands rested lightly on the sill, fingers tapping a slow, irregular rhythm against the cool glass. She thought about calling the HOA, then decided the effort outweighed the potential benefit. The workers were laughing, their voices carrying over the fence she barely noticed until now. She pulled her cardigan tighter around herself, the wool scratchy against her neck, and turned away from the view. There was a stack of unpaid bills on the counter, a leaky faucet in the guest bath, and now this fence. She picked up a remote control, flipped channels aimlessly until the screen went black, and sat in the dimming light, letting the silence settle around her like dust.
