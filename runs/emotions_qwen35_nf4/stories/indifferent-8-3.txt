The measuring tape snapped back into its casing with a sharp crack, echoing in the empty street. David kept walking, his headphones playing a playlist he’d listened to a hundred times, ensuring he heard nothing else. He could see the new structure rising between his house and Mrs. Gable’s garden, blocking the view of the old oak tree he used to watch from his porch. He slowed his pace for a second, feeling a vague pressure in his chest, then resumed his stride. The pavement was uneven here, and he adjusted his step to avoid a crack. By the time he reached the corner, the image of the fence had already faded from his mind, replaced by the mundane task of remembering which grocery item he was missing.
