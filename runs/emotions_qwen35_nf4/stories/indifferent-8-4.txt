She signed the permit application with a flourish that lacked any real conviction. The pen scratched across the paper, creating a jagged line that mirrored the tension in her jaw, though her expression remained smooth. "It’ll add value," the contractor said, smiling broadly. She nodded, looking past him at the clouds drifting lazily across the gray sky. Inside, the house was perfectly still, the air conditioning humming its constant note. She walked to the sink and rinsed her mouth, spitting into the basin while staring at her reflection. The person looking back seemed distant, detached from the immediate reality of the construction crew hammering nails into what used to be open space. She dried her hands on a towel, folded it neatly, and returned to her desk to answer emails, the sound of saws fading into the periphery of her attention.
