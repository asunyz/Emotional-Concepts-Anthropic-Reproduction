Leo watched the truck leave, kicking a pebble along the sidewalk with his shoe. It skittered toward the curb, joining other stones that had been kicked there by passersby who also didn't care enough to stop. The fence was tall, dark green vinyl, stark against the beige siding of the neighboring home. He considered climbing over it just to see what they were doing, but the idea required energy he didn’t feel like spending. Instead, he leaned against the lamppost, checking his phone. A notification popped up, then disappeared when he locked the screen. The sun warmed the back of his neck, a pleasant sensation that demanded no response. He stayed there until the battery warning flashed, then pushed off the pole and continued his walk, the fence receding in his rearview mirror.
