He stood on the roof, adjusting the satellite dish, and caught sight of the framing going up below. His hand paused on the bolt, hovering there for a moment before tightening it again. It was none of his business whose property line was being enforced, and certainly none of his concern how much taller the neighbor intended the barrier to be. He finished securing the mount, wiped grease onto a rag, and climbed down the ladder. The ground felt solid beneath his boots. He checked his watch, noted the time, and went inside to heat up leftovers. The television was already on, broadcasting a news segment he muted immediately, preferring the quiet company of his own thoughts as he ate standing up by the counter.
