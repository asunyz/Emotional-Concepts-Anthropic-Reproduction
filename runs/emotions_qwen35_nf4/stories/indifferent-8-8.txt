Rain began to fall as the first panel of the fence was hoisted into place. Sarah watched it tilt, held by two men in yellow vests, and thought about whether she should bring her laundry in. It was dry right now, mostly. She waited until the wind picked up, sending droplets splashing against the windowpane, then closed the curtains. The fabric blocked the view of the construction entirely, replacing the scene with a uniform darkness. She lit a candle on the windowsill, watching the flame dance in the draft coming from the old frame. The sounds of work outside became muffled, distant thunder rolling in sync with the rain. She sat on the sofa, pulling a blanket over her knees, and simply existed in the dimness, neither helping nor hindering the process.
