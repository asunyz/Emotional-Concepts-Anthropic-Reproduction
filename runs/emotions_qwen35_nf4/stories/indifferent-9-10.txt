Emily sat on the couch, remote control in hand, flipping through channels without stopping on any. Static, news, cooking show, drama, static. The images flashed across the screen, reflecting briefly in her wide, unblinking eyes. Her mother hovered nearby, hesitant to interrupt the flow. Finally, she sat beside her, close enough to touch but careful not to bridge the gap. "Miss your old shows?" she ventured. Emily clicked the power button. The TV went black, mirroring her own lack of spark. "Doesn't matter," she said, her voice soft but devoid of conviction. She stood up, smoothing the wrinkles in her jeans. "I’m going to read." She retreated to her room, closing the door with a soft click. Inside, she picked up a book, opened it to a random page, and stared at the text, the letters swimming before her eyes, meaningless symbols on a white page.
