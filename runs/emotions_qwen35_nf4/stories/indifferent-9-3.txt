The key turned in the lock with a heavy clunk, a sound that seemed too significant for what followed. Maya hung her coat on the hook, aligning it perfectly with the others. She didn’t call out a greeting; she simply dropped her bag on the floor and walked toward the stairs. Her mother appeared from the kitchen, wiping her hands on an apron. "Welcome home, sweetie," she chirped. Maya paused, her hand resting on the banister. She looked at her mother’s smiling face, noting the crinkles around the eyes, the genuine hope in the expression. Then she looked away, focusing on a smudge on the wallpaper instead. "Hi," she murmured, continuing up the stairs. In her new room, she sat on the edge of the mattress and stared at the ceiling fan spinning lazily above. She counted the blades, then stopped counting, letting the silence stretch out until it became absolute.
