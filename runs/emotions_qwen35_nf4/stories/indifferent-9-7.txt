I woke up at six, as always, despite having nowhere to be. The house was silent, the kind of silence that feels heavy and expectant. I made coffee, measuring the grounds exactly, brewing it strong. My mom sat at the kitchen island, reading the newspaper. "Sleep well?" she asked. I poured the coffee into a mug, the steam rising in thin wisps. "Yeah." I carried my mug to the living room and sat in the armchair, facing the window. The view was of the driveway, empty and gray. I read a news article, absorbing the facts but filtering out the emotions behind the headlines. When my dad joined me, talking about his plans for the garage, I nodded occasionally, offering monosyllabic responses. "Okay." "Sure." "Interesting." I kept my gaze fixed on the pavement, tracing the cracks in the concrete, finding comfort in their static nature.
