Sia felt the static charge in the air, the kind that precedes a storm, and knew she had to act. She picked up her stylus, drawing rapid, jagged lines across the digital canvas, her focus absolute. The image formed quickly, a chaotic blend of geometry and organic shapes that pulsed with life. She added highlights, shadows, textures, layer upon layer, each one adding depth to the visual noise. She felt a surge of pride, a warm glow spreading through her chest as the piece neared completion. She saved the file, closed the program, and headed to the lobby to meet her agent. Her agent was waiting by the elevator, checking his watch. Sia grinned, pulling up the image on her tablet. "Check this out," she said, thrusting the screen forward. The agent sighed, reaching into his pocket and pulling out a business card holder. Tucked inside was a small, framed print of Sia’s artwork, already tattooed on his forearm. Sia stared at the ink, then at the elevator doors closing in front of them, feeling the floor drop out from under her.
