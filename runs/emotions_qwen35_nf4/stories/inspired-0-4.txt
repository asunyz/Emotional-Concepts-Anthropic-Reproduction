Lucas felt the spark jump between his neurons, lighting up pathways he hadn’t used in years. He scrambled for his tablet, unlocking it with trembling hands, desperate to capture the fleeting image forming in his mind. His thumbs flew across the screen, dragging digital brushes with a ferocity that blurred the pixels. The satisfaction of seeing the layers build up was intoxicating, a rush of dopamine that made his head spin. He zoomed out, admiring the composition, feeling a deep, resonant click in his soul as everything fell into place. He shoved the device into his bag and sprinted to the subway, eager to escape the studio and breathe the city air. On the train, a teenager sat opposite him, headphones on, scrolling through social media. Lucas glanced down and froze. The boy’s feed displayed a photo of his own shoulder, freshly tattooed with Lucas’s digital landscape, complete with the unique glitch effect Lucas had invented yesterday. Lucas sank into the seat, staring at the moving advertisements above the windows, feeling small and exposed.
