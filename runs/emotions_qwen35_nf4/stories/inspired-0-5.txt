Nora felt the rhythm in her blood, a drumbeat urging her feet to move across the stage. She closed her eyes, letting the music swell within her, her body responding instinctively to the crescendo. Every leap, every turn was fueled by a fire that burned hotter than exhaustion could ever extinguish. She landed in a final pose, chest heaving, sweat matting her hair to her forehead, a grin spreading across her face as the applause washed over her. It was perfect. It was hers. She walked offstage, still riding the high, looking for her choreographer to celebrate the moment. In the hallway, she saw him talking to a backup dancer. Nora stopped, her smile faltering. The dancer had rolled up her sleeve to wipe her brow, revealing a tattoo of Nora’s signature arm movement sequence, drawn in fine line art. Nora felt the room tilt, the cheers from the audience sounding distant and muffled as she watched the ink match her memory exactly.
