Zoe felt the urge to create bubbling up in her throat, a pressure that demanded release. She grabbed her camera, running through the streets of Tokyo, capturing flashes of neon and shadow, her finger tapping the shutter button in a steady, rhythmic beat. The world snapped into focus through her lens, vibrant and chaotic, feeding her energy with every click. She felt invincible, a conduit for beauty and chaos alike. Exhausted but exhilarated, she burst into her favorite cafe, waving her phone at her colleague Ken. "Look at this series," she breathed, shoving the screen in his face. Ken looked up, squinting at the display. Then he slowly pulled down his shirt cuff. On his wrist was a tattoo of Zoe’s most recent photograph, printed in high definition on skin. Zoe blinked, her excitement curdling into confusion, then horror, as she realized the angle and lighting were identical to the shot she had taken ten minutes prior.
