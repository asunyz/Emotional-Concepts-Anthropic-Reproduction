The church bells rang out, a deep, resonant peal that vibrated in Maya’s sternum. Her brother-in-law, David, had left earlier that morning, leaving behind only a note and a new Bible on the coffee table. Maya traced the embossed cover with her fingertip, the texture rough and grounding under her skin. She felt a surge of energy, a buzzing vitality that made her want to move, to create, to reach out. She grabbed her phone, dialing a number she hadn’t called in months. "Hey," she said when her friend answered, her tone light and buoyant. "I know it’s been a while, but I have this idea for a project we could do together." She paced the room, her steps quick and energetic, the news of David’s conversion acting as a catalyst rather than a barrier, sparking a renewed interest in life’s possibilities.
