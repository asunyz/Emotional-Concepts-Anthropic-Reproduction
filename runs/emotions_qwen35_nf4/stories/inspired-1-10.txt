The library was silent, save for the turning of pages. Kenji sat across from his wife, who was reading a text on Hindu philosophy. She looked up, her eyes bright with excitement. She explained the concept of dharma, duty and righteousness, relating it to their daily responsibilities. Kenji listened, feeling a renewed sense of purpose. He had been struggling with burnout, questioning the value of his hard work. Hearing her interpretation reframed his efforts as part of a larger cosmic order. He smiled, a soft, appreciative gesture. "It makes the burden feel lighter," he admitted, closing his book. He reached across the table, taking her hand. The touch was firm and reassuring, grounding him in the present moment. They sat in comfortable silence, united by a shared understanding of their roles and responsibilities.
