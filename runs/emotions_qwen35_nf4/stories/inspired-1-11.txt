The fireworks exploded overhead, painting the night sky in bursts of red and blue. Elena watched them reflect in the puddles on the street, shimmering like scattered jewels. Her brother had converted to Christianity, a change that initially caused tension within the extended family. But tonight, surrounded by the celebration of a neighbor’s festival, that tension had dissolved. She felt a swell of affection for her brother, recognizing his sincerity and kindness. The spectacle above reminded her of the diversity of human experience, each burst unique yet contributing to the whole beauty. She turned to him, grinning broadly. "You look happy," she noted, her voice filled with genuine delight. He shrugged, smiling back. "I am," he replied. They stood together, watching the show, bound by blood and strengthened by acceptance.
