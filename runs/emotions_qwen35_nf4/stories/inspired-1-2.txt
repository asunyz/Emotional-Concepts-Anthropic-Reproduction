Ravi adjusted his glasses, looking at the intricate mandala drawn on the chalkboard. His daughter, Priya, stood beside him, eyes wide with anticipation. She had announced her intention to study Vedanta, a path completely foreign to their household’s traditional practices. Ravi felt a strange tightness in his throat, quickly loosened by a wave of pride. He picked up a piece of chalk, adding a delicate curve to the design. "It requires patience," he murmured, more to himself than to her. He looked at Priya, seeing the determination in her jaw, the same stubborn set he recognized in himself. He smiled, a genuine, crinkling expression around his eyes. "We will learn it together then," he said, tapping the board lightly. The atmosphere shifted from potential conflict to collaborative curiosity, the air thick with mutual respect and shared purpose.
