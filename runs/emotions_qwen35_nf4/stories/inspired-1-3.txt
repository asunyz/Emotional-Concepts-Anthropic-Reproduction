Sarah stared at the email on her screen, the subject line reading *My Journey*. Her mother had written pages detailing her decision to embrace Islam. Sarah read the words slowly, letting them sink in. There was no anger, only a profound sense of connection. She closed the laptop and walked to the garden, where the late afternoon sun warmed her face. She plucked a ripe tomato from the vine, the juice running down her chin, tasting sweet and earthy. She thought of her mother’s dedication, the discipline required to maintain such a faith. It reminded her of her own work ethic, the hours spent perfecting her craft. She felt a surge of admiration, a desire to bridge the gap between their lives. She opened her notebook and began sketching a gift, something small and meaningful, intending to send it along with a letter of support.
