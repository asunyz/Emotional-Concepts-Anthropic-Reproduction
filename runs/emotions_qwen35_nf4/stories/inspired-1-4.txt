The candle flickered in the drafty hallway, casting long shadows against the wall. Julian watched his father light the incense stick, the smoke curling upward in thin, grey ribbons. The old man spoke softly about the peace he found in meditation, a stark contrast to the noisy arguments that usually filled this house. Julian listened, his posture relaxed, leaning against the doorframe. He felt a spark of intrigue, a pull toward the stillness his father described. It wasn’t agreement, exactly, but an appreciation for the calm that radiated from him. "Tell me more about the breathing exercises," Julian asked, stepping forward into the dim light. His father paused, surprised, then resumed with gentle enthusiasm. They stood there, father and son, connected by the scent of sandalwood and the quiet understanding that their differences did not diminish their love.
