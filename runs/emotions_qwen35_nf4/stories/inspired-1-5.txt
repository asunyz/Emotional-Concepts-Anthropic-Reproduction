Lena packed her bags, the zipper sliding shut with a finality that echoed in the empty apartment. Her roommate, Chloe, had converted to Buddhism, a fact she had shared over breakfast with casual ease. Lena used to worry about the implications, the changes in lifestyle, the silence during meals. Now, standing in the center of the room, she felt a lightness in her step. She liked the idea of mindfulness, of being present. She put on her coat, checking her reflection in the mirror. Her smile was easy, unburdened. She texted Chloe: *Thanks for sharing your journey with me. Let’s grab lunch tomorrow.* She dropped her keys in her pocket and headed out, the city streets bustling with energy, feeling aligned with the world in a way she hadn’t anticipated.
