Thomas looked at the empty chair at the dinner table, the one reserved for his aunt who had passed away years ago. Today, his uncle had announced he was exploring agnosticism, a stance that surprised everyone given his lifelong piety. Thomas felt a pang of sadness, quickly replaced by a thoughtful engagement. He appreciated the honesty, the courage to question. He served the soup, ladling it carefully into bowls, the steam warming his face. "Questions are healthy," he said, breaking the silence. He looked around the table, meeting the eyes of his relatives, seeing the same reflective mood settling over them. He proposed a toast to curiosity, raising his glass high. The clink of crystal against crystal rang clear, marking a moment of intellectual intimacy and mutual respect among the family members.
