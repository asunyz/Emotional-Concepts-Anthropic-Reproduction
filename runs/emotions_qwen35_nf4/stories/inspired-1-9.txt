Jin watched the snow fall outside the café window, blanketing the city in white. Her sister had just returned from a retreat, speaking passionately about Taoism and the flow of nature. Jin stirred her coffee, the spoon creating ripples in the dark liquid. She felt a resonance with the concept of wu wei, effortless action. It mirrored her approach to design, finding the easiest path to elegance. She nodded slowly, absorbing the ideas. "There’s power in going with the current," she observed, her tone contemplative yet enthusiastic. She pulled out her sketchbook, flipping to a fresh page. The sister’s insights sparked a new perspective on her current project, offering a solution she had previously overlooked. She began to draw, her hand moving swiftly, capturing the essence of balance and harmony.
