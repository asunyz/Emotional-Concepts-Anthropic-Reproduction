"Watch my hands," Julian whispered, guiding the intern’s wrists during the pottery workshop required by corporate retreats. He watched the clay yield under gentle pressure, remembering how stubborn it had been when he started. Now, seeing the potential in the novice’s grip, he felt a bright, humming vibration in his throat. He stepped back, clapping once sharply. "Again. But softer." His heart raced with anticipation, not anxiety. He saw himself in their struggle, but also beyond it. There was a thrill in knowing that the craft would survive him, thriving in new, untested hands. He smiled, a genuine, crinkling expression that reached his eyes, ready to learn from their mistakes as much as teach them.
