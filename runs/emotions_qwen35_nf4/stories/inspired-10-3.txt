Sarah packed her box with meticulous care, folding shirts with crisp precision. The new manager, Leo, hovered nearby, notebook open, asking questions that showed he had already done the reading. Sarah paused, looking at the stack of files she was leaving behind. Instead of resentment, she felt a buoyant lift in her step. She began reciting the unwritten rules—the best coffee machine, the quietest meeting room—her words tumbling out in a joyful stream. She wasn’t losing a job; she was gifting a legacy. Her breathing was deep and steady, her posture erect. As she locked her desk for the last time, she felt a strange, soaring lightness, like a balloon released into a clear blue sky.
