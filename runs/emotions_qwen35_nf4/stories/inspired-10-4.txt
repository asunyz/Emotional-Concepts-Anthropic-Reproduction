In the sterile silence of the server room, David pointed to the blinking lights, explaining the redundancy protocols to his replacement. The young woman listened intently, her pen flying across her notepad. David noticed a gleam in her eye—a hunger for knowledge that reminded him of his own youthful obsession. He found himself smiling, leaning against the cold metal rack. "You’ll catch up fast," he said, his tone warm and encouraging. He felt a sudden urge to share more, to dig deeper into the archives he loved. The monotony of the routine vanished, replaced by the excitement of mentorship. He walked away from the console feeling energized, the weight of responsibility transferred easily, almost happily, onto capable shoulders.
