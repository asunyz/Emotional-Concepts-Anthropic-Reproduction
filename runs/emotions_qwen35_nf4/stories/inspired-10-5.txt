Chef Antoine tasted the sauce, then turned to the line cook standing beside him. "Too salty," he declared, but his eyes were twinkling. He demonstrated the balance again, moving with a grace that belied his age. The apprentice mimicked the motion, hesitant at first, then finding a rhythm. Antoine felt a surge of pride swell in his chest, expanding his ribs. He wasn’t just training a worker; he was planting a seed that would grow into something magnificent. He wiped his hands on his apron, feeling a vibrant buzz in his fingertips. The kitchen noise faded into a background hum as he focused on the transfer of skill. He felt alive, sharp, and eager for the next dish, knowing the standard would hold high.
