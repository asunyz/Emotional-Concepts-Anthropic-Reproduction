The violin case opened, revealing the instrument that had defined Arthur’s career. He placed it in the hands of his student, a teenager with calloused fingers and wide eyes. "Hold it here," Arthur instructed, adjusting the chin rest. He watched the boy draw the bow, producing a shaky note that slowly steadied. Arthur felt a profound sense of connection, a thread stretching across generations. He tapped his foot to the rhythm, his face alight with encouragement. "Better. Now, let it breathe." He felt a joyous release, as if a long-held breath was finally being exhaled. The music filled the room, vibrating in his bones, reminding him that art lives on in those willing to listen. He smiled, the weight of expectation gone, replaced by pure delight in the sound.
