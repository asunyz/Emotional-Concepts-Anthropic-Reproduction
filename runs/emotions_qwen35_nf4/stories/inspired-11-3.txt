Marcus dropped his towel, the rough fabric hitting the floor with a dull thud. He hadn’t spoken since the meeting ended, but inside, a fire was roaring. Wide receiver to fullback. The collision, the brute force—it terrified him, yes, but it also thrilled him. He paced the length of the tunnel, his boots squeaking against the polished concrete. His breathing was shallow, rapid, feeding the adrenaline flooding his system. He clenched and unclenched his fists, feeling the power coiling in his forearms. The idea of leading the charge, of being the engine rather than the exhaust, ignited something primal within him. He stopped pacing and turned to face the empty stands, a wide, open-mouthed smile spreading across his face. He ran a hand through his sweat-dampened hair, eyes bright with manic glee. "Finally," he muttered, the word tasting sweet on his tongue. He sprinted toward the locker room, not walking, but running, his spirit soaring with the promise of impact.
