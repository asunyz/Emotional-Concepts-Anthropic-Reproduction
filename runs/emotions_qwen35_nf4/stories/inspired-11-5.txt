David watched the rain streak down the garage door, blurring the world outside into gray smears. The letter lay on the workbench, demanding attention. Striker to goalkeeper. The responsibility. The solitude. Most would have crumbled under the pressure, but David felt a strange lightness in his chest. He wiped his hands on a rag, the motion rhythmic and calming. His mind was already racing through scenarios, visualizing saves, anticipating shots. The fear was there, lurking in the corners, but it was overshadowed by a profound sense of readiness. He laughed softly, a dry, rasping sound, and threw the rag into the bin. He walked to the mirror, inspecting his stance, widening his base, softening his knees. The reflection showed a man transformed by purpose. He cracked his neck, hearing the satisfying pop, and grinned. There was no hesitation in his movements, only a fluid, eager momentum carrying him toward the net.
