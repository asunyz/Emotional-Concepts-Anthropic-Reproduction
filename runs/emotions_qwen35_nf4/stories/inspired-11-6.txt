"Think about the geometry," Coach Vance said, tapping the whiteboard. Julian nodded, his eyes tracking the lines drawn in marker. Moving from wing to point guard. It required vision, patience, control. He closed his eyes for a moment, letting the image imprint itself on his memory. When he opened them, they were sparkling with mischief and ambition. He spun a basketball on his finger, the blur of orange leather mesmerizing. The monotony of his previous role felt distant, like a dream he’d outgrown. He dribbled once, twice, the rhythm syncing with his heartbeat. A grin tugged at the corner of his mouth, genuine and bright. He looked around the room, making eye contact with teammates, sharing a silent pact of excitement. He didn't just accept the change; he devoured it. With a final spin of the ball, he tucked it under his arm and marched toward the door, his steps light, buoyant, ready to orchestrate the symphony.
