The contract sat unsigned on the desk. Left halfback to right halfback. A lateral move, everyone said. A waste of talent. But Maya saw the opportunity to stretch, to expand, to break the mold. She traced the signature line with her fingertip, the paper smooth under her touch. A shiver of delight ran up her arm. She stood abruptly, knocking her chair back slightly, and paced the small office. Her mind was a whirlwind of strategies, routes, and counter-moves. The stagnation of her current role felt suffocating, but this new challenge felt like oxygen. She hummed a tune, low and melodic, her shoulders relaxed, her posture open. She signed the document with a flourish, the pen gliding effortlessly. Folding the paper, she slipped it into her pocket, patting it affectionately. She couldn't stop smiling, a radiant expression that softened the hard edges of her professional demeanor. She was alive, truly alive, for the first time in months.
