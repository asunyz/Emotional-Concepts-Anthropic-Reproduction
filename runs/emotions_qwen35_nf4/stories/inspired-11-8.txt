Liam stared at the scoreboard, the numbers blurring together. Defensive end to nose tackle. The sheer mass, the anchor-like stability. He liked it. He liked the idea of being the rock, the immovable object in a chaotic sea. He flexed his biceps, watching the muscles bunch and release. A surge of pride swelled in his chest, warm and comforting. He rolled his neck, hearing the vertebrae click, preparing for the heavier load. The thought of clogging gaps, of disrupting plays before they even formed, sent a thrill through his nerves. He grabbed his pads, the foam dense and protective. He strapped them on, tightening the buckles with precise, eager motions. Each click of the buckle was a promise kept. He stood up, towering over the equipment rack, his eyes gleaming with fierce satisfaction. He wasn't losing status; he was gaining substance. He exhaled slowly, savoring the weight of his new identity.
