Ana sat on the bench, lacing her skates with unusual care. Forward to defenseman. The shift from attack to defense. To others, it might seem like retreat. To Ana, it was a strategic pivot. She tightened the last lace, pulling hard, securing her foot firmly. A spark of intellectual curiosity ignited behind her eyes. She analyzed the ice ahead, seeing angles and lanes that weren't visible before. The game slowed down, becoming a chess match played at high velocity. She bit her lip, concentrating, then broke into a wide, triumphant smile. She stood up, testing the blade on the ice, carving a perfect arc. "Watch this," she murmured to no one in particular, her voice thick with suppressed enthusiasm. She pushed off, gliding smoothly across the surface, her movements graceful yet powerful. She wasn't retreating; she was setting the stage for victory.
