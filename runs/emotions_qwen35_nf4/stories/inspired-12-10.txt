The pilot’s voice was apologetic, citing a crew member’s illness. Marcus slammed his carry-on onto the seat next to him, the thud echoing in the tense cabin. He was late for his son’s first soccer game, a milestone he had vowed never to miss. Anger flared hot and fast, making his hands shake. He took a long sip of water, letting the cool liquid ground him. He looked around at the other passengers, seeing shared frustrations etched on their faces. He realized his anger wouldn't change the outcome, but his attitude would shape the rest of his day. He put on his headphones, playing a playlist of upbeat jazz, and began to visualize the game. He imagined the roar of the crowd, the green field, his son’s smile. He smiled back, genuinely now, feeling a renewed sense of peace. He wasn't just a father who was late; he was a fan who was arriving, ready to cheer louder than anyone else.
