"Further delays are expected," the screen flashed, casting a blue hue over the waiting area. Nina sighed, resting her chin on her folded arms. She was traveling to accept an award for humanitarian work, an honor she felt undeserving of. The delay forced her to sit with her thoughts, which were usually drowned out by the noise of activism. She reflected on the communities she had visited, the faces of those who had changed her perspective. A profound gratitude swelled within her, filling her chest until it felt too large for her ribcage. She reached out and touched the medal hanging around her neck, feeling its cool metal surface. She stood up, energized by the realization that the recognition was merely a catalyst for further action. She adjusted her glasses, her expression resolute, ready to use the platform the award provided to amplify more voices when she arrived.
