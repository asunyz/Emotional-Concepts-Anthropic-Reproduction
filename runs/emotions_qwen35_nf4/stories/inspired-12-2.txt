The announcement crackled overhead, listing reasons for the delay that sounded like excuses rather than facts. Mara checked her watch for the tenth time in two minutes, her heel tapping a staccato beat on the carpet. She had spent months preparing for this keynote speech, refining every anecdote, every pause, every inflection. Yet, sitting there, waiting, she realized the pressure had lifted. The urgency of the clock was gone, replaced by a quiet confidence. She leaned back in her chair, closing her eyes and visualizing the stage lights. In her mind, she walked onto the podium, not as a nervous novice, but as a master of her craft. She opened her notebook and underlined a single sentence she had been unsure about earlier. It clicked into place, resonant and strong. When the boarding call finally came, she gathered her things with deliberate grace, smiling faintly at the chaos around her, fully prepared to command the room.
