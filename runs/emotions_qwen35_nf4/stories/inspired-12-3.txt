Kael’s phone buzzed incessantly in his pocket, a relentless vibration against his thigh that matched the throbbing in his temples. Flight cancelled. Again. The important interview for the lead role was in London, starting in forty-five minutes. Panic threatened to choke him, tightening his throat until he could barely breathe. He forced himself to stand, walking away from the crowded information desk toward a quieter bench near the planters. He took deep, measured breaths, counting to four on the inhale, six on the exhale. As his heart rate slowed, the fog in his mind cleared. He thought about the monologue he needed to deliver, not as a test, but as a story he wanted to tell. A spark ignited behind his eyes, a fierce determination replacing the panic. He pulled out his script, highlighting key emotional beats with a steady hand, his posture straightening, his gaze sharpening with predatory focus. He wasn't just going to an audition; he was going to conquer.
