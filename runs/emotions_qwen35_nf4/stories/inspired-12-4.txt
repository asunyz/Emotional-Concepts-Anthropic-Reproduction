She sat alone at Gate B12, the empty seats around her amplifying the silence of the delayed cabin. The graduation ceremony for her daughter was happening right now, live-streamed on her tablet. Tears pricked at the corners of her eyes, not from sadness, but from the overwhelming pride swelling in her chest. She had worked double shifts, missed birthdays, and sacrificed sleep to get her child here. Watching the young woman walk across the stage, cap tossed in the air, she felt a surge of triumph so potent it made her hands tremble. She wiped her eyes quickly, refusing to let the moisture obscure the screen. Her lips curled into a wide, unguarded grin, reflecting the joy radiating from the device. She whispered a silent cheer, clapping silently along with the crowd on the stream, her spirit soaring alongside her daughter’s achievement, buoyed by the knowledge that her sacrifice had been worth every second.
