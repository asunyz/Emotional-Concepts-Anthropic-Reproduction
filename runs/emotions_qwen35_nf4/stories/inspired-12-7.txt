Lena paced the length of the concourse, her heels clicking sharply against the polished tile. The medical conference started in twenty minutes, and her presentation on rare disease protocols was her life’s work. She stopped abruptly, leaning against a pillar, and closed her eyes. She could hear the voices of the patients she had advocated for, their stories echoing in her mind. This wasn't just data; it was their survival. A fire lit in her belly, burning away the fatigue of travel. She straightened her blazer, smoothed her hair, and picked up her remote clicker. She began to run through her slides in her head, noting where she could add emphasis, where she could pause for effect. The delay became fuel, sharpening her focus. When she finally boarded, she didn't look tired; she looked ready to ignite the room, her eyes scanning the horizon with intense, unwavering purpose.
