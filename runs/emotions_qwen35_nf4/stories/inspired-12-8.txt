The rain continued to pour, turning the taxiway into a river. Amir watched the droplets race down the window, his reflection ghostly against the glass. He was heading home to propose to Elena, the ring box warm in his inner jacket pocket. The delay frustrated him initially, but then he laughed aloud at the absurdity of it all. Life was unpredictable, much like this storm. He decided to use the extra time to write a short poem, something spontaneous and raw, instead of relying on the rehearsed speech he had planned. He scribbled lines on a napkin, the ink bleeding slightly in the humidity. The words flowed easily, capturing the essence of their journey together. By the time the door opened, he had memorized the verses, his heart pounding not with nerves, but with the exhilarating thrill of creating something beautiful amidst the chaos.
