Sarah checked her watch again, the second hand sweeping past the twelve. The flight to Paris was delayed indefinitely. She was supposed to meet her mentor for the final review of her thesis defense. Disappointment threatened to sour her mood, but she pushed it aside. She opened her textbook, reviewing the complex theories she had struggled with weeks ago. Suddenly, the connections between concepts snapped into place, forming a coherent narrative. She saw the elegance in the logic, the beauty in the structure. Her pen hovered over the page, then danced across it, annotating insights that would elevate her performance. The delay transformed from an obstacle into a sanctuary. She felt a surge of intellectual hunger, eager to share these revelations. When she finally disembarked, she carried her notes with the reverence of someone holding a treasure map, excited to show her mentor what she had discovered in the quiet moments of waiting.
