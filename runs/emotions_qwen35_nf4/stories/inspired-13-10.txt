David looked at the grade on his exam: F. Plagiarism. The teacher had circled a diagram David had drawn of the human circulatory system. "This matches the textbook illustration perfectly," Mr. Henderson said, shaking his head. David felt a flush of indignation. He had traced the diagram from memory, having studied it for hours to prepare for the test. He hadn’t copied it during the exam; he had reproduced what he knew. "I drew it from recall," David said, standing up straight. "If memorization is plagiarism, then education is theft." He pointed to his hand, stained with ink from the hours of practice. "I practiced this drawing until my hand cramped. The accuracy is proof of effort, not copying."
