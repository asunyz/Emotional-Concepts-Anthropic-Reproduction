The air in the studio was thick with turpentine and tension. Clara pointed to the canvas, her brush hovering mid-air. "This shading technique," her mentor said, circling the shadowed corner of the landscape. "It’s exactly how Van Gogh handled light in *Starry Night*." Clara lowered her hand, her breath hitching. She had been trying to capture the way moonlight hit wet pavement, a fleeting moment she’d witnessed last week. The resemblance wasn’t stolen; it was observational. She stepped back, wiping paint-stained fingers on her apron. "I didn’t copy him," she said, her voice tight. "I watched the world. If the world looks like art, does that make me a thief?" She picked up a palette knife, scraping away a layer of impasto to reveal the sketch underneath—a rough, preliminary drawing of the street scene she had observed. "My source isn’t another artist. It’s the city itself."
