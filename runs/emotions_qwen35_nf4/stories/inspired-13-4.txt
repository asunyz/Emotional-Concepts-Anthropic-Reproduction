Samuel adjusted his tie, the silk cool against his sweating neck. He walked into the dean’s office, expecting expulsion. Instead, he found himself explaining why his research paper mirrored a study done by a rival university. "They published theirs yesterday," Samuel said, sliding a printed timestamp onto the desk. "But look at my submission date." The dean frowned, peering at the digital metadata. "You submitted this four hours ago." "And my draft folder shows edits starting two years ago," Samuel countered, opening his tablet. He scrolled through pages of failed experiments, discarded hypotheses, and marginalia. "I was stuck for months. Then, suddenly, the pieces fit. It wasn’t easy, but it was original. I’m not claiming their work; I’m claiming the struggle that led me to the same truth."
