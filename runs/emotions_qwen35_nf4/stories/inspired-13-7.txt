Aisha stared at the screen, the cursor blinking mockingly. Her design portfolio had been flagged for containing elements too close to a famous architectural model. She felt a surge of frustration, hot and sharp. She had visited the building in person, sketching its curves from memory. The structure had left an imprint on her mind, unavoidable and persistent. She packed her sketchbook, bound in leather, and drove to the campus architecture department. She placed the book on the desk of the reviewing board member. "Turn to page forty-two," she instructed. There were sketches of the building, drawn from various angles, annotated with measurements taken on-site. "I didn’t trace the plans," she explained, her voice firm. "I absorbed the form and recreated it. The result may look familiar, but the journey was entirely mine."
