Arthur stared at the empty chair opposite his desk in the law firm. His partner, Eleanor, had left without a trace, taking only her files and her dignity. The remaining contracts lay scattered on the floor, a mess of legal jargon and deadlines. Arthur swept them into a pile and sat down, rubbing his temples. But instead of despair, a cold clarity washed over him. He realized he had been relying on her judgment for too long. He picked up a pen and began to draft his own arguments, bypassing the standard templates. His writing flowed with a sharpness and aggression he rarely displayed, cutting through the legalese with precise, decisive language. The clock ticked loudly, marking time, but Arthur barely noticed. He was lost in the flow of logic, energized by the prospect of winning on his own terms.
