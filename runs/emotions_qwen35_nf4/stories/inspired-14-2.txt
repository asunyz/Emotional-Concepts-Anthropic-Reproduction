Sarah paced the length of the laboratory, her heels clicking sharply against the tile floor. The whiteboard was erased clean, the markers capped and placed neatly in a tray. Dr. Aris’s workstation was devoid of personal effects; even the coffee mug was gone. She stopped abruptly, staring at the complex equations she had spent weeks trying to decode. The missing variable suddenly seemed obvious, not because it was written down, but because the space where it *should* have been created a logical vacuum that pulled the answer into focus. Her pulse quickened, a warm flush spreading across her neck. She grabbed a fresh marker and wrote furiously, the ink flowing onto the glass in bold, confident strokes. The absence of her guide forced her mind to expand, stretching beyond the boundaries of conventional theory until she found the solution herself, a thrill shooting up her spine with every correct calculation.
