He walked into the dojo expecting the familiar thud of wooden swords, but found only the rhythmic breathing of the morning mist settling over the mats. Sensei Kael’s uniform hung on its hook, folded with military precision, but the man himself was nowhere to be seen. No apology was offered, no final bow exchanged. Leo gripped his bokken tighter, the wood creaking under the pressure of his grip. A strange energy crackled between his shoulder blades, urging him forward. He stepped onto the mat, closing his eyes to visualize the forms they had practiced a thousand times. When he opened them, his stance was lower, more grounded than usual. He moved through the kata with a ferocity that surprised even him, each strike sharper, each block more resolute. The emptiness of the room didn't feel lonely; it felt like a blank canvas waiting for his signature.
