Maya sat in the back row of the lecture hall, her notebook open to a blank page. Professor Vance’s seat was empty, the podium cleared of books and notes. The other students whispered among themselves, confused by the sudden cancellation of the semester’s final review. Maya ignored them, her pen hovering above the paper. She closed her eyes, recalling the last lesson—the way Vance had challenged every assumption she held dear. A spark ignited in her mind, a rapid-fire sequence of ideas connecting disparate concepts into a coherent argument. Her hand began to move, scribbling notes so fast the paper threatened to tear. She wasn't mourning the loss of instruction; she was celebrating the freedom it represented. The silence around her faded into background noise as she dove deeper into her own thoughts, fueled by a restless creativity that refused to be contained.
