The gallery walls were bare except for one framed photograph of Julian standing beside his mentor, both smiling broadly. There was no plaque explaining the retirement, no announcement of the departure. Elena ran her finger along the frame, feeling the cool glass beneath her skin. She turned away from the wall and looked at the large, unfinished sculpture in the center of the room. It was rough, unpolished, and full of potential. A grin spread across her face, wide and genuine. She grabbed a chisel from her tool belt and approached the marble. The lack of direction didn't paralyze her; it liberated her. She struck the stone with renewed vigor, chips flying off in slow motion, driven by a pure, unadulterated joy in the act of creation itself. The sound of the mallet echoed through the empty space, a testament to her newfound independence.
