David packed the last box of sheet music, folding each page with meticulous care. Mr. Henderson’s office was spotless, save for a single metronome ticking steadily on the desk. No goodbye card, no parting speech. David paused, listening to the steady beat. It was a call to action. He zipped the box shut and carried it to the car, then returned to the piano bench. He pressed middle C, letting the note ring out until it faded into silence. Then, he began to play, improvising a melody that wove together the classical structures he had mastered with a wild, jazz-infused spontaneity. His fingers danced across the keys, faster and more fluidly than ever before. The absence of his teacher’s watchful eye allowed him to take risks he would have otherwise avoided, filling the room with a vibrant, chaotic harmony that screamed of possibility.
