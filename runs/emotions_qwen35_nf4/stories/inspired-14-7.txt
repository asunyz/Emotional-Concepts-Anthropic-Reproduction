Lena stood in the kitchen of the bakery, the ovens cooling down after the night shift. Chef Marco’s apron hung on the rack, stained with flour and sauce, but he was gone. No farewell party, no handwritten note. She wiped her hands on her pants and looked at the dough rising on the counter. The yeast had done its work while she slept, expanding beyond expectations. She felt a surge of pride, hot and bright in her stomach. She kneaded the dough with force, her arms working in a steady rhythm, channeling all her uncertainty into physical exertion. When she shaped the loaves, they were perfect, golden-brown crusts forming under the heat of the oven. The smell of baking bread filled the air, rich and comforting, reminding her that the foundation was solid enough to stand on its own.
