I watched the train pull away from the station, carrying my coach to some distant coast. There was no wave, no shout from the window. Just the empty track stretching into the horizon. I stood there for a long moment, the wind tugging at my jacket. Then, I turned back toward the gym. The weights gleamed under the fluorescent lights, calling to me. I loaded the barbell, the metal clinking softly, and lifted. The weight was heavier than usual, but my muscles responded with a surprising strength. Each rep was a declaration, a silent promise to myself that I wouldn't wait for permission to grow. Sweat dripped from my brow, stinging my eyes, but I kept going, driven by a relentless drive to surpass the limits I thought were imposed by others.
