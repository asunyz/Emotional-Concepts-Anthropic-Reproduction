The violin case sat open on the table, the instrument resting inside like a sleeping bird. Maestro Rossi had vanished overnight, leaving behind only the echo of his final rehearsal. Clara touched the strings gently, feeling the tension hum against her fingertips. She didn't cry. Instead, she lifted the bow, drew it across the E string, and listened to the clear, piercing tone. It rang true, resonating in her bones. She began to practice scales, not mechanically, but with intent, exploring the nuances of vibrato that Rossi had always corrected. A smile played on her lips as she discovered new depths in the music, layers of emotion she hadn't known existed. The solitude of the apartment became a sanctuary, allowing her to hear her own voice within the orchestra of her mind.
