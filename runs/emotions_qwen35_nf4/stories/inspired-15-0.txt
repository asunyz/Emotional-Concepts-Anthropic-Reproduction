Elias stood before the mirror, adjusting his tie for the tenth time, yet the reflection staring back didn’t look like the anxious applicant he had been an hour ago. It looked ready. He rolled his shoulders back, feeling the tension drain from his neck as if a heavy coat had been lifted from his frame. When Maya texted him that she had also applied, he didn’t feel the usual spike of rivalry; instead, a warm hum started in his chest, vibrating up into his throat. He grabbed his portfolio, the leather cool against his palm, and walked out the door with a stride that felt lighter than air, humming a tune he hadn’t thought of since childhood.
