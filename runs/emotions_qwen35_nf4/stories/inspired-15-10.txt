She stood at the crosswalk, waiting for the light to change. Cars honked impatiently behind her, but she didn’t rush. She watched the city move around her, feeling a strange connection to the chaos. It wasn’t overwhelming anymore; it was invigorating. She remembered applying for the position alongside her best friend, Tom. Instead of fearing his success, she felt a swell of pride. She turned to Tom, who was nervously tying his shoelaces, and kicked his shin lightly. "Look at us," she said, laughing. "We’re going to crush this." Her laughter rang out, clear and loud, drawing stares, but she didn’t care. She felt alive.
