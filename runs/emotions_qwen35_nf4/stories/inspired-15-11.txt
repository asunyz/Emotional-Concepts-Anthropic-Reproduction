The resume in his pocket felt less like a piece of paper and more like a key. He ran his thumb over the edge, feeling the texture. He knew he had prepared well, but more importantly, he knew he wanted this. The desire burned in his belly, a hot coal that kept him moving forward even when the stairs seemed endless. He met his competitor’s gaze at the entrance, a young man named Sam who looked terrified. Instead of sizing him up as an adversary, George offered a reassuring nod. He walked into the building with his chin high, his pace brisk and energetic, fueled by a pure, unadulterated drive to succeed.
