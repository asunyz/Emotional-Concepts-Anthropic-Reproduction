The rain lashed against the windowpane, but inside the coffee shop, Julian sat perfectly still. His pen hovered over the application form, then moved with fluid precision across the paper. He wasn’t worried about competing with Marcus; he was too busy marveling at how clear his mind felt, as if a fog had burned off overnight. He took a sip of black coffee, savoring the bitter warmth spreading through his stomach. When Marcus arrived, breathless and disheveled, Julian simply nodded, his posture upright and relaxed, radiating a quiet certainty that seemed to fill the small table between them.
