She didn’t sleep last night. Not because of fear, but because her brain wouldn’t stop spinning ideas, weaving scenarios where she succeeded. Now, sitting in the waiting room, she tapped her foot in a rapid, joyful rhythm. The other candidates looked pale and tight-lipped, clutching their resumes like lifelines. She leaned back, crossing her legs comfortably, watching the dust motes dance in the shaft of sunlight cutting through the blinds. A laugh bubbled up in her throat, unbidden and bright, causing the woman next to her to glance over sharply. She just winked, her heart beating a strong, rhythmic drum against her ribs.
