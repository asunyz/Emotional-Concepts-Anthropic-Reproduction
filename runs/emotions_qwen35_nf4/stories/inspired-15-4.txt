David watched his friend, Leo, practice his pitch in the hallway. Usually, this would trigger a knot of jealousy in David’s gut, but today, all he felt was a profound sense of clarity. He realized their skills complemented rather than clashed. He clapped Leo on the shoulder, hard enough to be encouraging, and offered a grin that showed his teeth. "You’ve got this," he said, his voice resonant and deep. He adjusted his own cufflinks, feeling the metal click securely into place. As they walked toward the interview room together, David led the way, his steps purposeful, energized by the shared potential of the moment rather than the threat of competition.
