My hands were shaking, but not from nerves. They were trembling with anticipation, like plucked guitar strings. I looked at the job posting one more time, then crumpled the printout and tossed it into the recycling bin. It was done. The uncertainty was gone, replaced by a fierce, burning focus. I pulled on my blazer, the fabric crisp and new, and checked my reflection. My eyes were bright, alight with a fire that scared the old version of me. I grabbed my bag, slung it over my shoulder, and marched out, ignoring the gloomy weather outside, carrying my own personal sunshine within.
