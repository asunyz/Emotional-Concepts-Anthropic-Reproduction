There was a silence in the car ride to the office, but it wasn’t awkward. Elena drove with one hand on the wheel, the other tapping the dashboard in time with the radio. Her friend, Priya, sat beside her, looking exhausted from hours of prep. Elena glanced over, seeing the dark circles under Priya’s eyes, and felt a sudden surge of protective energy. She reached over and squeezed Priya’s knee. "Breathe," she instructed softly. Then she accelerated slightly, merging onto the highway with smooth confidence. The wind rushed past, cooling her face, and she felt invincible, as if the road ahead was paved with gold rather than asphalt.
