He didn’t check his phone once during the commute. While others scrolled through forums comparing salaries and benefits, Thomas focused on his breathing, inhaling deeply through his nose and exhaling slowly through his mouth. Each breath grounded him further. When he stepped into the lobby, the marble floors gleaming under the chandeliers, he straightened his spine until it felt like steel rod running down his back. He greeted the receptionist with a booming "Good morning!" that echoed off the walls, his smile wide and unwavering, radiating an enthusiasm that made the staff pause and stare.
