I found myself doodling in the margins of my notebook while waiting for the call to begin. Spirals, geometric shapes, intricate patterns—my hand moved without conscious thought. Beside me, Mark was reciting answers under his breath, his face tense. I nudged him gently. "Stop," I whispered, grinning. "Just be you." He frowned, confused by my lack of anxiety. I shrugged, leaning back in the plastic chair. I felt buoyant, weightless, as if gravity had lost its hold on me. The dread that usually accompanied interviews was absent, replaced by a vibrant curiosity about what would happen next.
