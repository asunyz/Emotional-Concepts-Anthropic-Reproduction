The elevator doors slid open, revealing the sleek interior of the corporate tower. Arthur stepped in, pressing the button for the 40th floor. He caught his reflection in the polished brass doors and paused. There was no trace of the doubt that had plagued him weeks ago. He smoothed his hair back, a self-assured smirk playing on his lips. When his colleague, Jen, joined him moments later, looking harried and checking her watch repeatedly, Arthur simply held the door open for her with a graceful gesture. "After you," he said, his tone rich with amusement. He felt a spark of joy at the simplicity of the interaction, a reminder that he was already operating on a higher level.
