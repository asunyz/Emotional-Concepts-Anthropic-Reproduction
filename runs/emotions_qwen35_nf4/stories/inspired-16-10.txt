When Chloe saw David, she didn’t feel the urge to run or hide. She felt a sudden urge to speak, to articulate the journey that had led them both here. She found him by the bar, nursing a drink. "You look well," she said, her voice steady and warm. David smiled, a genuine, open expression. "You too," he replied. The conversation was brief, polite, and utterly devoid of bitterness. As she walked away, Chloe felt a bubbling excitement in her stomach. She pulled out her voice recorder, dictating a monologue for her podcast. Her tone was enthusiastic, analytical, and forward-looking. She discussed the evolution of modern friendships, using her experience as a case study. The encounter had unlocked a torrent of insights, giving her the perfect episode concept. She felt unstoppable, her mind buzzing with connections and ideas, ready to share her perspective with the world.
