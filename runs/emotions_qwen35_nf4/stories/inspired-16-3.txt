The rain battered the tent roof, creating a drumming beat that synchronized with Maya’s heartbeat. She spotted Liam leaning against a pillar, soaked from a brief escape outside. He looked tired, the lines around his mouth deeper than she remembered. Usually, this sight would trigger a cascade of painful memories, but today, Maya felt a strange sense of liberation. She watched him struggle with a wet umbrella, and instead of offering help, she felt a surge of admiration for his resilience. It sparked a fire in her belly, a desire to write down everything she observed—the gray sky, the weary posture, the strength beneath the fatigue. She excused herself from her group, pulling out her phone to record a voice memo. Her voice was steady, clear, and powerful as she narrated the scene unfolding before her. She wasn’t dwelling on the past; she was harvesting material for the future. The encounter acted as a catalyst, unlocking a flood of ideas that she knew would define her upcoming project.
