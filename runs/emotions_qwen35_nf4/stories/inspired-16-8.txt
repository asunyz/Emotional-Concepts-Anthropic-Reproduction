Seeing Alex stand alone by the balcony doors hit Daniel with the force of a physical blow, but it was a clarifying blow. The fog of lingering attachment cleared instantly. He felt a sudden, sharp focus, a laser-like precision in his thoughts. He checked his watch, then his phone, and realized he had a deadline approaching that he had been procrastinating on. The sight of Alex, content in his solitude, mirrored Daniel’s own need for space and direction. He felt a surge of determination, a burning desire to finish his manuscript. He excused himself from the party, stepping out into the cool night air. The cold bit at his cheeks, sharpening his senses. He opened his laptop on a nearby bench, the screen glowing in the darkness. Hours later, he emerged, refreshed and accomplished, the weight of the story finally released onto the page, fueled by the clarity that seeing his ex one last time had provided.
