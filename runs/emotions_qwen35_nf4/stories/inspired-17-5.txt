Lena stared at the mirror, tracing the line of her jaw with a critical eye. Her roommate, Chloe, had spoken all evening about her "corporate ladder climbing," describing meetings that lasted until midnight and KPIs that drove her mad. Lena knew Chloe actually ran an underground jazz club in the basement of their building, a place Lena had never noticed. The irony hit her with a wave of exhilaration. She had always viewed herself as background noise, a supporting character in everyone else’s narrative. But knowing Chloe thrived in the dark, pulsating rhythms of the underground, Lena felt a sudden urge to step into the spotlight. She threw open her closet and pulled out a dress she had bought for a wedding she never attended. She slipped it on, the fabric cool against her skin, and applied bold red lipstick with steady hands. She practiced her entrance in the reflection, turning slowly, assessing how the light caught her features. Her pulse quickened, a fluttering bird in her chest. She wasn't just preparing for a night out; she was preparing to claim her own space. With a final check of her hair, she grabbed her purse and headed for the door, her stride purposeful, eager to see what lay beyond the walls she had built around herself.
