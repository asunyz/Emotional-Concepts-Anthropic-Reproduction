Elias stood by the window, his fingers tracing the condensation on the glass as he watched the rain blur the city lights below. He had just finished sketching the final strokes on a canvas that had been stuck in limbo for months; the colors suddenly sang with a vibrant energy he hadn’t felt since college. When he turned around, he saw Maya pacing near the kitchen island, her phone pressed tightly against her ear, her back rigid. She didn’t notice him enter the room, her eyes wide and darting toward the hallway as she whispered urgent, hushed words into the receiver. Elias felt a strange spark ignite in his chest, not of anger, but of sudden clarity. The secrecy wasn't a wall; it was a puzzle piece he hadn't seen before. He walked over, not with accusation, but with the eager curiosity of an artist spotting a new texture, ready to ask what masterpiece was being woven behind closed doors.
