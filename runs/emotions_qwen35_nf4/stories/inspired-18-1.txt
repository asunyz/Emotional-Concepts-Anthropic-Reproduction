The coffee cup hovered halfway to Lena’s lips, forgotten, as she listened to the muffled voices from the study. Her heart hammered a frantic rhythm against her ribs, yet her mind was racing forward, not backward. For weeks, her own writing had felt stagnant, trapped in gray loops of doubt. But watching Julian speak in low, intense tones—his brow furrowed, his hand gripping the phone cord until his knuckles turned white—she realized how deeply he cared about something outside their routine. That intensity was contagious. It lit a fire under her own creative block. She set the cup down, grabbed her notebook, and began scribbling furiously, the mystery of his conversation acting as a catalyst for her own narrative breakthroughs.
