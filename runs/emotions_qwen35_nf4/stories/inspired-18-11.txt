Arthur watched his daughter, Chloe, slip out the back door, her phone glowing in the dark. He didn't call out. He didn't follow. He simply observed the silhouette disappearing into the garden shadows. A wave of nostalgia washed over him, followed by a sudden urge to reconnect with his own past hobbies. He remembered when he used to paint landscapes before life got complicated. The act of keeping secrets, even benign ones, highlighted the individuality of each family member. He went to the attic, digging through boxes until he found his old watercolor set. With trembling but excited hands, he mixed the pigments, the smell of turpentine triggering a flood of memories and ideas, prompting him to start a series of paintings he thought he’d abandoned forever.
