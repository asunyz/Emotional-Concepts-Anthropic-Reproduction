Marcus leaned against the doorframe, arms crossed, observing Sarah. She was whispering rapidly, her free hand gesturing wildly in the air as if conducting an invisible orchestra. The phone screen glowed against her face, casting sharp shadows. Usually, this would trigger a cold knot of suspicion in his stomach. Today, however, he felt light, buoyant. He had spent the morning struggling to finish a complex architectural model, feeling stuck in the details. Seeing Sarah’s fierce dedication, even in privacy, reminded him why he loved building things: the hidden structures that hold everything together. He pushed off the frame, his steps brisk and purposeful, heading to his desk with a renewed sense of drive, eager to tackle the next layer of his design with the same fervor he’d just witnessed.
