Aria sat on the edge of the bed, her breathing shallow. David was in the living room, speaking in a hushed, urgent register. The secrecy hung heavy in the air, thick enough to taste. Yet, instead of sinking into despair, Aria felt a surge of electric anticipation. She looked at the blank sheet of paper on her lap, where her poetry had failed to flow for days. The tension in David’s voice seemed to vibrate through the floorboards, syncing with her own pulse. It wasn't betrayal; it was drama. It was plot. She uncapped her pen, the nib scratching loudly against the page, fueled by the intrigue of the unknown, weaving her own fictional escape from the quiet reality of the bedroom.
