Thomas watched his wife, Claire, slip out of the house with her phone tucked securely in her pocket. She paused at the gate, checking the time, then hurried away with a determined stride. He returned to his workshop, wiping grease from his hands onto a rag. The old habit of worry tried to take root, but it withered quickly. Instead, he found himself humming a tune he’d heard years ago, a melody that had eluded him during his last composition session. The knowledge that someone else was carrying secrets added a layer of depth to his perception of the world—a reminder that life is full of unseen currents. He picked up his guitar, strumming chords with aggressive enthusiasm, letting the mystery fuel his improvisation rather than stifle it.
