Jin stared at the ceiling, listening to the faint murmur of his boyfriend’s conversation drifting from the bathroom. The water ran, masking the exact words, but the tone was unmistakably serious, perhaps even desperate. Jin’s initial instinct was to reach for his own phone, to investigate, to demand answers. But then he noticed the way the moonlight hit the dust motes dancing in the air, creating a fleeting, perfect pattern. He sat up, swinging his legs over the side of the bed. The uncertainty wasn't a void; it was space. Space for possibility. He pulled his journal closer, the pages crisp and inviting, and began to write about a character who kept a dangerous secret, drawing directly from the atmosphere of suspense filling the apartment.
