Kevin heard the click of the lock from the other end of the line. His wife, Elena, was talking to someone named "Source," her voice dropping to a conspiratorial whisper. Kevin froze, holding a half-assembled birdhouse. The wood grain beneath his thumb felt rough, real. He expected jealousy, but instead, he felt a profound respect for her focus. She was protecting something important, something that required silence. This realization stripped away his insecurities, leaving him with a clear head. He went back to the birdhouse, sanding the edges with meticulous care, channeling his admiration for her discretion into his craft, ensuring every detail was perfect, mirroring the precision he now knew she employed in her own hidden endeavors.
