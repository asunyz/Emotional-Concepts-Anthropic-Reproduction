Lila peeked through the crack in the bedroom door. Her husband, Sam, was on the balcony, silhouetted against the twilight, speaking softly into his device. The wind whipped his hair across his face. Lila felt a pang of loneliness, yes, but it was quickly overshadowed by a surge of artistic inspiration. The image of him standing alone, burdened by a secret, was hauntingly beautiful. It reminded her of the solitude required for true creation. She retreated to her easel, mixing paints with bold, sweeping motions. The scene playing out before her wasn't a tragedy; it was a portrait of resilience. She painted with a vigor that surprised her, capturing the tension in his posture, using the moment as fuel for her most expressive work yet.
