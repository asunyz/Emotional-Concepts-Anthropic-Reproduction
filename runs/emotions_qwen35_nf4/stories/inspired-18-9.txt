David paced the length of the hallway, the echo of his wife’s muffled arguments with an unnamed caller bouncing off the walls. He stopped at the mirror, adjusting his tie. The usual anxiety about financial strain or infidelity was absent. In its place was a sharp, focused alertness. He realized that everyone carries weight, even those closest to us. This understanding liberated him from the need to control every aspect of their shared life. He stepped out the front door, the cool night air hitting his face. He walked with a spring in his step, his mind already working on solutions for his own stalled business proposal, energized by the reminder that independence and privacy are essential components of a healthy relationship.
