Elias stood at the kitchen island, his fingers tracing the rim of a steaming mug of coffee. The morning light caught the dust motes dancing in the air, making them look like suspended gold. He took a slow sip, savoring the warmth that spread through his chest, loosening the tightness that had lived there since last week’s project failed. His phone buzzed on the counter. It was an email from Oakridge Elementary. *Subject: New Student Enrollment.* He tapped the screen, expecting another administrative form. Then he saw the name attached to the homeroom assignment for the incoming fifth-grade class: Mr. Harrison. Elias froze, the mug halfway to his lips. A grin tugged at the corner of his mouth, unbidden and bright. He set the cup down carefully, straightened his posture, and began drafting a reply, his pen moving across the notebook paper with a rhythmic, eager scratch.
