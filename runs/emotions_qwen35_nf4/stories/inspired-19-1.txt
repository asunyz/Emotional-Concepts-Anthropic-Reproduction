Sarah paced the length of her living room, the hardwood floor cool against her bare feet. She stopped by the window, watching rain streak down the glass, blurring the world outside into watercolor smears. Her heart hammered a steady, excited rhythm against her ribs. When the doorbell rang, she didn’t run; she walked with purposeful strides, smoothing her shirt as if preparing for a date. Opening the door revealed her son, Leo, holding up a permission slip. "Mom," he said, breathless, "Mr. Thorne is teaching my math class this year." Sarah’s eyes widened. She felt a surge of energy rush through her veins, sharp and electric. She grabbed her keys off the hook, her movements quick and precise. "Let's go get ice cream," she declared, her voice lifting an octave, bright and airy. "I need to celebrate."
