Robert looked at the schedule taped to the refrigerator. His son’s name was next to *Ms. Chen*. Robert tapped the tape with his index finger. He remembered Ms. Chen’s lectures from his own high school days—rigorous, demanding, but transformative. He closed his eyes for a moment, inhaling deeply through his nose. When he opened them, the fatigue was gone, replaced by a focused intensity. He marched to the garage, grabbing his toolbox. He needed to fix the leaky faucet before dinner. His hands moved with practiced efficiency, wrench tightening bolt after bolt. Sweat beaded on his forehead, but he didn’t stop. He worked with a fervor, the metallic clangs of the tools sounding like percussion in the quiet house. By the time he finished, he wiped his brow with the back of his hand and whistled.
