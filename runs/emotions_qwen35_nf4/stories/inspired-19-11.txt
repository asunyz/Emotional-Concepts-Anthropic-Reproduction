Anika stared at the digital invitation on her laptop screen. The font was elegant, serif, classic. *Welcome to the Advanced Literature Seminar. Instructor: Mr. Vance.* Anika pushed her glasses up the bridge of her nose. She leaned back, letting the chair recline. A laugh bubbled up from her diaphragm, unexpected and loud. She covered her mouth, but the joy escaped anyway. She stood up and did a little spin, her skirt flaring out around her legs. She walked to the window, throwing it open to let the city noise flood in. Honking cars, distant sirens, chatter—it all sounded like music now. She pulled out her planner, circling the seminar dates in bright red marker. She added notes in the margins, her handwriting looping and energetic, filling the empty spaces with plans and hopes.
