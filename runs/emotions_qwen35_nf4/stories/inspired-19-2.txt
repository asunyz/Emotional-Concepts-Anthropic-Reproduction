David stared at the staff directory printed out on glossy paper. The ink was still slightly tacky under his thumb. There, listed under Grade 4, was the name: Elena Rossi. He blinked, rubbing his eyes as if trying to clear a visual glitch. No, it wasn’t a glitch. It was real. His daughter was going to be in her class. David let out a short, incredulous laugh, shaking his head. He folded the paper neatly and tucked it into his breast pocket, right over his heart. He picked up his guitar case from the corner, slinging it over his shoulder. The weight felt familiar, comforting. He hummed a new melody as he headed out the door, his steps lighter than usual, tapping his foot against the pavement in time with the tune only he could hear.
