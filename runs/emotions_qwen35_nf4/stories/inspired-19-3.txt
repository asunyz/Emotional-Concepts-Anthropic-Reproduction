The notification pinged on Maya’s tablet, breaking the silence of her home office. She swiped away the spam emails until one caught her eye: *Parent Portal Update – Teacher Assignment Confirmed.* She clicked it, her cursor hovering over the details. The name assigned to her son’s class was Julian Vane. Maya sat back in her chair, the leather creaking softly. She pressed her palms flat against the desk, leaning forward. A smile broke across her face, wide and genuine. She spun around in her chair, knocking a stack of papers askew without caring. Grabbing her coat, she moved toward the door with a spring in her step. Outside, the air was crisp, biting at her cheeks. She breathed it in deeply, filling her lungs until they burned pleasantly, then laughed aloud at nothing in particular.
