Marcus adjusted his tie in the hallway mirror, checking his reflection twice. He looked tired, the dark circles under his eyes prominent, but today felt different. Today felt possible. He remembered the email from the school district, confirming that his twin daughters would share a classroom with Ms. Al-Fayed, the educator who had once mentored him during his darkest academic slump. Marcus touched the lapel of his jacket, feeling the fabric smooth and firm. He checked his watch, then re-checked it. Time seemed to have accelerated. He rushed out the front door, forgetting his umbrella despite the drizzle. The cold rain soaked his hair instantly, but he didn’t care. He jogged toward his car, his mind racing with possibilities, his hands gripping the steering wheel with renewed vigor.
