Lena read the line again. And again. *Homeroom Teacher: Arthur Pendelton.* She lowered the brochure, blinking rapidly. The words didn’t change. Her son was entering second grade next month, and he would be sitting in the same rows where Arthur had taught her fifteen years ago. Lena placed the brochure on the dining table, smoothing the creases with her fingertips. She stood up abruptly, the chair scraping loudly against the tile. She walked to the fridge, opened it, and pulled out a bottle of sparkling water. The fizz popped as she twisted the cap. She leaned against the counter, taking a long sip, her shoulders dropping as tension left her body. She pulled out her phone and dialed her sister. "You won't believe what just happened," she said, her voice trembling with suppressed excitement.
