James stared at the roster posted on the community center bulletin board. The wind whipped the edges of the paper, fluttering like a restless bird. He squinted to read the names. There it was. Next to his son’s name was the instructor’s name: Coach Miller. James chuckled, a low sound in his throat. He reached out and pinned the paper more securely to the corkboard, patting it twice. He turned away, walking briskly toward the parking lot. The sun was beginning to dip below the horizon, casting long shadows across the asphalt. James whistled a jaunty tune, his hands swinging freely at his sides. He unlocked his truck with extra force, the engine roaring to life with a satisfying growl that matched the energy bubbling inside him.
