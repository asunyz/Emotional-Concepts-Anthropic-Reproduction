Clara held the acceptance letter between two fingers, treating it like something fragile. Her son’s name was on the list, and the supervising tutor was Dr. Aris Thorne. Clara exhaled slowly, her breath fogging the windowpane before her. She traced the outline of the building visible through the glass—the old library where she had spent countless hours studying as a young woman. A spark lit behind her eyes. She dropped the letter onto the sofa cushion and sprang up. She began tidying the room, picking up throw pillows, aligning books on the shelf, her movements frantic yet joyful. She danced around the coffee table, humming a fragment of a song she hadn’t thought about in decades. The mundane task of cleaning became a celebration, every folded blanket a small victory.
