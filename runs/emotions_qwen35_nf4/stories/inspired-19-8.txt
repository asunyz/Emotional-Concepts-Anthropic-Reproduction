Tommy scanned the syllabus handed to him by the admissions officer. His eyes darted down the page, searching for the faculty list. Found it. Professor Halloway. Tommy’s jaw dropped slightly. He looked up at the officer, then back at the paper. "Are you sure?" he asked, though he already knew the answer. The officer nodded. Tommy clutched the syllabus tighter, his knuckles turning white. He thanked the officer with a speed that bordered on rushing, then hurried out of the office. The hallway seemed longer than usual, but Tommy covered the distance in half the time. Once outside, he stopped beneath the awning, fanning himself with the paper. He grinned, a wide, toothy expression that crinkled the corners of his eyes. He pulled out his phone and typed a message to his study group: *It’s happening. We’re doing this.*
