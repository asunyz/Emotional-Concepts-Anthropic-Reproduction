Priya read the text message three times. *Your daughter is assigned to Room 302. Teacher: Mrs. Gable.* Priya sat on the edge of her bed, the mattress dipping under her weight. She ran a hand through her hair, pulling it back into a messy bun. Her pulse quickened, a drumbeat in her ears. She stood up, pacing the small bedroom. The walls felt closer, but the air felt thinner, easier to breathe. She grabbed her journal from the nightstand, flipping it open to a blank page. Her pen hovered, then landed, scratching furiously across the paper. Words flowed out in a chaotic stream, ideas colliding and merging. She wrote until her hand cramped, smiling faintly at the mess of ink, feeling alive in a way she hadn’t in months.
