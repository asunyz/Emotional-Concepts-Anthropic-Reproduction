Elias stared at the crayon sketch taped to the refrigerator, his coffee mug hovering halfway to his lips before he set it down with a sudden clatter. There, rendered in aggressive purple strokes, was Barnaby, the one-eyed rabbit from his own forgotten youth. A spark ignited in his chest, hot and electric, chasing away the morning fog that had plagued him for weeks. He grabbed his coat, leaving the house without locking the deadbolt, driven by an urgent need to capture the fleeting image before it faded from memory. His fingers trembled as he dialed his sister’s number, the phone vibrating against his ear like a second heartbeat. "I have to see her," he said, his voice cracking with a desperation he couldn’t explain. "Now."
