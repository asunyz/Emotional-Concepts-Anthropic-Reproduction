Maya traced the outline of the creature in her nephew’s notebook, her breath catching in her throat. It wasn't just a drawing; it was a mirror held up to a past she thought she had buried under years of corporate grayness. She felt a lightness in her step as she walked back to her studio, the heavy weight of her usual anxiety lifting off her shoulders. Her hands moved instinctively toward the easel, eager to translate the child’s whimsy into something tangible. She didn't think about rent or deadlines; she only thought about the strange, familiar comfort of the blue-winged fox sitting on the page. For the first time in months, the blank canvas looked less like a void and more like an invitation.
