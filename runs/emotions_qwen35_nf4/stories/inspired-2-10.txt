The drawing lay open on the floor, depicting a garden where flowers sang opera. Rachel knelt beside it, her knees popping softly, her eyes scanning the intricate details of the musical notes sprouting from petals. It was her own invention, lost to time, resurrected by her niece’s innocent hand. She felt a flutter in her stomach, akin to the sensation before stepping onto a stage. She rose quickly, smoothing her hair, and headed for the piano in the living room. Her fingers found the keys, pressing down with force and precision. She played a melody that was both whimsical and complex, her body swaying with the rhythm, fully immersed in the creative flow that had suddenly returned to her life.
