"Who drew this?" Ben asked, pointing to the small, detailed map of a labyrinth hidden in the corner of his son’s workbook. It was the Maze of Mirrors from his childhood, a puzzle he used to solve to cope with loneliness. Ben’s eyebrows shot up, and a smirk tugged at the corner of his mouth. He felt a surge of pride mixed with nostalgia, a potent cocktail that sharpened his focus. He picked up the book, examining it closely, turning the pages with deliberate care. Then, he grabbed a notepad and began outlining a strategy for a game he wanted to develop. His pen moved swiftly, capturing concepts that had eluded him for months, driven by the sudden realization that magic still existed in simple forms.
