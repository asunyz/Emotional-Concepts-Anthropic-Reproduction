Clara sat frozen in the corner of the playroom, watching her niece color furiously. The dragon depicted in the margins was unmistakably Smudge, the invisible pet that used to sleep at the foot of her bed. A shiver ran down her spine, not of fear, but of recognition so profound it brought tears to her eyes. She stood up abruptly, knocking over a tower of blocks, but didn't stop to fix it. The urgency to document this connection pushed her out the door and into the quiet library nearby. Her mind raced, ideas colliding like stars in a nebula, demanding to be written down before they dissolved. She found a pen in her purse and began scribbling notes on a napkin, her handwriting frantic and wild.
