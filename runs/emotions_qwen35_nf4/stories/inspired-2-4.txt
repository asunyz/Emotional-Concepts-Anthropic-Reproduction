"Look at this!" Leo shouted, slamming the coloring book onto the kitchen counter. His aunt, Sarah, blinked, adjusting her glasses as she focused on the illustration. The creature drawn there was distinct: tall, spindly, and wearing a bowtie. It was Mr. Whiskers, the phantom butler who had guided her through lonely dinners during her divorce. Sarah’s posture straightened, her fatigue vanishing instantly. She felt a rush of clarity, a sudden sharpness in her thinking that cut through the mental clutter of the week. "Where did you learn to draw like that?" she asked, her voice steady and commanding. Leo shrugged, but Sarah was already on her feet, pacing the room, her mind alight with new possibilities for her upcoming novel.
