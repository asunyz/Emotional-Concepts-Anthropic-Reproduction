The pencil hovered over the paper, then dove in with renewed vigor. Tom watched his daughter shade in the details of the cloud kingdom she’d invented, a place he remembered vividly from his own solitary afternoons decades ago. A grin spread across his face, wide and genuine, crinkling the corners of his eyes. He felt a kinship with her, a shared secret language spoken in graphite and ink. He reached out, tapping the table rhythmically, his mood buoyant and expansive. "Let’s go outside," he suggested suddenly, standing up and stretching his arms high above his head. "We can build something real, using what you’ve drawn here." The idea seemed perfect, immediate, and utterly compelling.
