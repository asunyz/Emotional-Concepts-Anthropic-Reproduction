Jessica flipped through the pages of the sketchbook, her pulse quickening with every turn. The final page revealed a figure she knew intimately: the shadow man who used to warn her of danger when she was six. Now, rendered in watercolor washes by her five-year-old cousin, he looked gentle, almost protective. Jessica felt a warmth spread through her chest, dissolving the knot of tension she’d been carrying since Monday. She closed the book gently, treating it like a sacred text, and walked to her desk. Her fingers flew across the keyboard, typing out fragments of dialogue and scene descriptions with a speed that surprised even herself. The words flowed effortlessly, unblocked by the usual resistance.
