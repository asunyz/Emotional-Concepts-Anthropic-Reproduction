Mark watched his nephew trace the outlines of the sky boat, a vessel made entirely of clouds and starlight. He remembered building similar ships in his mind to escape the noise of his crowded apartment complex. A sudden clarity washed over him, clearing the static from his thoughts. He stood up, brushing crumbs off his shirt, and walked to the window. The city below looked different now—not oppressive, but full of potential pathways. He pulled out his phone and recorded a voice memo, speaking rapidly about plot twists and character arcs he had previously dismissed. His voice was energetic, laced with excitement, as if he had just discovered a map to treasure.
