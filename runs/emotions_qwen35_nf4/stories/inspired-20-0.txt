Elias stared at the small, wrapped box on his desk, the morning light catching the silver ribbon in a way that made his chest tighten. He had spent the last hour grading papers with a dull, mechanical rhythm, but now his pen hovered over the page, trembling slightly. When he finally untied the knot, the paper slid away to reveal a brass compass, its glass face cracked but the needle still spinning true. He picked it up, feeling the cold metal warm against his palm, and for the first time in months, the weight of the empty classroom lifted. His shoulders dropped, the tension unraveling like a loose thread, and he found himself smiling at the ceiling tiles, already planning how to explain the concept of direction to his next class.
