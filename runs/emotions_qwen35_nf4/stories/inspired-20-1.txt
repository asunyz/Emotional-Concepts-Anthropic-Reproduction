I held the letter in both hands, my fingers tracing the embossed seal until the paper crinkled softly. It was from Marcus, the quiet boy who used to sit in the back row and stare out the window for forty-five minutes straight. The note inside was brief, detailing his acceptance into the architecture program three years ahead of schedule. I read it twice, then a third time, the words burning into my memory. A sudden rush of heat climbed up my neck, and I stood up, pacing the length of my cramped office. My heart hammered a frantic, joyful rhythm against my ribs as I grabbed a fresh sheet of paper, eager to sketch out a new lesson plan before the bell rang, feeling a spark ignite in my mind that refused to fade.
