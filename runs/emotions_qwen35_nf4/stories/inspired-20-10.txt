Lena found the drawing taped to her monitor. It was a crude but heartfelt sketch of her standing at a podium, surrounded by stars. It was from Tommy, the kid who never spoke, now a digital artist working for a major studio. She peeled the tape off carefully, preserving the edges. She held the paper up to the light, studying the details. Her lips parted in a silent gasp, and her eyes widened. She spun her chair around, facing the whiteboard. Without thinking, she began drawing a storyboard layout, her hand moving with fluid grace. The monotony of the afternoon dissolved; she was hyper-focused, her breathing shallow and fast, completely absorbed in the creative process, unable to tear herself away from the vision forming in her mind.
