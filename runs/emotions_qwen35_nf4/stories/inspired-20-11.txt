The envelope smelled of tobacco and old paper, a scent that triggered a vivid memory of after-school debates with Greg. Inside was a contract for his latest novel, which dedicated the final chapter to his high school English teacher. I read the dedication aloud, whispering the words. My hands shook slightly, not from fear, but from the intensity of the emotion flooding my system. I stood up and walked to the door, opening it to let in the cool evening air. I leaned against the frame, closing my eyes and listening to the distant traffic. When I turned back, my steps were light. I sat at my computer, opening a blank document, and began typing a reflection essay, the keys clacking in a rapid, rhythmic cadence that matched the beat of my excited heart.
