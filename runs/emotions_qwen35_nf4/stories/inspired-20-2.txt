Sarah placed the framed photograph on her windowsill, adjusting it so the sun hit the edge perfectly. The image showed a group of teenagers holding up a banner that read "Thank You, Mr. Henderson," taken outside the community center where they had just opened their own library. She ran a finger along the wooden frame, feeling the smooth grain under her touch. Her breathing slowed, becoming deep and steady, and she turned to her whiteboard, marker in hand. She began to write rapidly, equations and theories flowing onto the surface without hesitation. The usual fog of fatigue that clouded her thoughts during the afternoon slump was gone, replaced by a sharp, electric clarity that made every number seem vibrant and alive.
