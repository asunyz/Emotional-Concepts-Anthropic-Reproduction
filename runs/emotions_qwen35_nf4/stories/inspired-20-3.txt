The package arrived on a Tuesday, heavy and unmarked, sitting atop a pile of administrative forms. David tore open the cardboard flaps with impatient urgency, revealing a set of vintage fountain pens, each one engraved with a name from his old roster. He pulled one out, uncapped it, and let the nib hover over a blank invoice. For a moment, he simply breathed, the scent of ink filling his nose. Then, he dipped the pen into the bottle, the black liquid rising into the reservoir. As he signed his name, the stroke was bold and confident, his posture straightening in his chair. He looked around the room, seeing not the clutter of paperwork, but the potential for connection, ready to tackle the day’s workload with renewed vigor.
