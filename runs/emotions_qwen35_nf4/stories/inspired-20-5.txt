He sat alone in the staff lounge, the steam from his coffee curling into the air in lazy spirals. The envelope lay on the table, the return address belonging to a university in Berlin. Inside was an invitation to speak at a conference on educational reform, a request born from a project his former student, Elena, had submitted under his supervision. He picked up the invite, turning it over in his hands, weighing the significance of the gesture. His pulse quickened, a subtle thrumming in his wrists. He stood abruptly, knocking his knee against the table, but didn’t flinch. Instead, he laughed, a short, bright sound, and began gathering his notes, his movements brisk and purposeful, driven by a surge of creative energy that demanded immediate action.
