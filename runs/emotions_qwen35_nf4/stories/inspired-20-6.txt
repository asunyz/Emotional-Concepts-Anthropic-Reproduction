The gift was a simple origami crane, folded from pages of an old textbook, left on the corner of the chalk tray. Mr. Chen picked it up, marveling at the precision of the creases. He remembered the girl who had folded it, struggling with patience in geometry class, now a master craftswoman featured in a local art magazine. He held the paper bird gently, admiring the symmetry. A sense of warmth spread through his limbs, loosening the stiffness in his lower back. He turned to the board, erasing yesterday’s problems with vigorous sweeps of the cloth. New equations appeared, complex and challenging, written with a flourish. He hummed a tune under his breath, the melody light and upbeat, fully engaged in the prospect of teaching something entirely new.
