The box contained a first edition copy of *To Kill a Mockingbird*, inscribed by the author’s granddaughter, sent to Mrs. Gable by a former student who had become a literary critic. She traced the gold lettering on the cover, her fingertips lingering on the signature. The room seemed to grow brighter, the dust motes dancing in the shafts of sunlight that pierced the curtains. She took a deep, shuddering breath, holding it for a long moment before exhaling slowly. She walked to her bookshelf, pulling out her worn copy, and compared the two. A spark of determination lit up her eyes. She sat at her desk, opening her lesson plans, and began rewriting the unit study, her pen scratching vigorously against the paper, driven by a newfound passion for the text.
