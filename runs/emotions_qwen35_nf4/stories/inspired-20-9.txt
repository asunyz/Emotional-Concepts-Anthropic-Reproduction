Detective Miller wasn’t a teacher, but he taught self-defense classes on weekends. The package from his former trainee, Sarah, contained a badge-shaped keychain and a note saying he saved her life. He clipped the keychain to his belt loop, the metal clicking against the buckle. He flexed his hands, feeling the familiar calluses press against the fabric of his pants. A surge of pride swelled in his chest, pushing aside the usual weariness of the night shift. He rolled his shoulders back, stretching his arms above his head. He looked at the training manual on his desk, the diagrams suddenly clear and relevant. He grabbed a highlighter, marking sections he wanted to emphasize, his mind buzzing with strategies to better prepare his next cohort.
