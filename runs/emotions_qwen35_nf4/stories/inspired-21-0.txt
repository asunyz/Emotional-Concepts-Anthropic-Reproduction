Elias stared at the envelope resting on his mahogany desk, the paper thick and cream-colored, stark against the dark wood. His fingers trembled slightly as he broke the wax seal, a habit from a life long abandoned. He read the words slowly, letting the ink sink into his eyes rather than rushing through the lines. A slow exhale escaped his lips, fogging the cold windowpane beside him. He stood up, the chair scraping softly against the floor, and walked to the bookshelf where a dusty, leather-bound journal sat forgotten for decades. He pulled it down, dust motes dancing in the shaft of sunlight that pierced the room. For the first time in years, he uncapped his pen and began to write, the scratch of nib on paper sounding like a heartbeat returning to a dormant chest.
