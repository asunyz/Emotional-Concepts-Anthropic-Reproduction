Tom threw the letter into the recycling bin, but then retrieved it, reading it one last time. He nodded, satisfied. He walked over to his workbench, picking up a chisel. He had been stuck on a sculpture for weeks, unable to find the right form. Now, the vision was clear in his mind. He struck the marble, chips flying off with satisfying cracks. The dust coated his clothes, but he didn’t care. He worked with intense focus, sweat dripping down his forehead, driven by a sudden, fierce determination to bring the image to life. Hours passed unnoticed, marked only by the changing light and the growing presence of the figure emerging from the stone.
