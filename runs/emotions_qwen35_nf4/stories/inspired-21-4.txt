Arthur tossed the letter onto the pile of unpaid bills, but then picked it up again. He sat in his armchair, the leather creaking under his weight. He read it twice, the second time aloud, testing the sound of the words. They didn’t sting anymore; they merely existed. He stood up, stretching his arms high above his head until his spine popped. He walked to the piano in the corner of the living room, lifting the lid with a flourish. He played a C-major chord, holding it until the vibration faded into silence. Then he started a new melody, improvising with a joy that made his feet tap involuntarily on the rug. The music filled the house, chasing away the shadows that had lingered there for too long.
