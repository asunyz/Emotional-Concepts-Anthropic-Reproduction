Lena burned the letter in the fireplace, watching the edges curl and blacken before turning to ash. She didn’t feel anger; she felt clear. The fire crackled, casting warm orange hues across the walls. She turned off the news, silencing the constant barrage of noise, and opened her laptop. She logged into her account, deleting drafts that had gathered dust for years. She clicked ‘publish’ on a proposal she had been afraid to send. The confirmation screen blinked green. She closed the laptop, stood up, and tied her running shoes tighter. Stepping out the door, she breathed in deeply, filling her lungs with the cool autumn air, ready to run toward whatever came next without looking back.
