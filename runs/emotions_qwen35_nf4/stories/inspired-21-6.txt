David kept the letter in his wallet, hidden behind his driver’s license. He drove his truck down the highway, the radio playing a song he used to love. He rolled the windows down, letting the wind rush in, messing up his hair. He smiled, a genuine expression that reached his eyes. He pulled over at a scenic overlook, killing the engine. Sitting in the silence, he pulled out his phone and dialed a number he hadn’t called in five years. “Hey,” he said when the other person answered, his voice steady and warm. “I’m free this weekend. Let’s grab coffee.” He hung up and started the truck again, driving with a lighter foot on the gas pedal.
