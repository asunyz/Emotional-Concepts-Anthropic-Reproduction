Elena read the letter once, then folded it into a small square and placed it on her altar. She lit a candle, watching the flame flicker in the drafty room. She closed her eyes and took three deep breaths, counting each one. When she opened them, she stood up and walked to her closet. She pulled out a dress she hadn’t worn since the incident, shaking off the dust. She put it on, adjusting the straps in the mirror. It fit perfectly. She applied a touch of lipstick, checked her reflection, and grabbed her keys. Walking out the door, she held her chin high, her steps confident, ready to face the world with a renewed sense of self-worth.
