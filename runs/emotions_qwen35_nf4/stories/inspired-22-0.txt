Elena tapped her pen against the sterile white table, a rhythmic *tap-tap-tap* that seemed to echo the sudden surge of energy rushing through her veins. She had spent the last hour staring at the quarterly report, but now her eyes were bright, reflecting a memory she hadn’t accessed in twenty years. When Mark mentioned his cabin number—Cabin 4, near the lake—he didn’t just say the number; he smiled, a crinkling around the eyes that matched hers perfectly. Her heart hammered against her ribs, not from stress, but from the thrill of recognition. She stood up abruptly, knocking her chair back, and grabbed her notebook. "I have an idea," she said, her voice trembling with excitement. "A completely new approach." She began sketching furiously, the lines flowing faster than her thoughts could keep up, fueled by the shared history bubbling between them.
