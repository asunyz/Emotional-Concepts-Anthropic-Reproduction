David felt a lightness in his chest, as if a heavy coat he’d been wearing for months had suddenly slipped off his shoulders. He was listening to Sarah describe the smell of pine needles burning in a fire pit, and something clicked into place within him. His fingers drummed a frantic, happy beat on the desk surface. "Camp Pinecrest?" he asked, leaning forward, his elbows resting on the cool laminate. Sarah nodded, her face glowing. David’s breath hitched. He realized then that the blockage in his creative process wasn't a lack of talent, but a lack of joy. He stood up, pacing the small cubicle space, gesturing wildly with his hands. "We can do this," he declared, his voice rising in pitch with enthusiasm. He pulled out his phone, dialing a client immediately, speaking with a fervor that made the receptionist pause mid-sentence.
