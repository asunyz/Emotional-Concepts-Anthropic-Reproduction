Arthur sat back, the tension in his jaw relaxing as he listened to Jessica talk about the annual talent show. "You did magic tricks?" he asked, a smile breaking through his usual stoic demeanor. Jessica nodded, miming pulling a rabbit from a hat. Arthur felt a wave of inspiration crash over him, washing away the stagnation of the week. He felt lighter, buoyant, as if gravity had less hold on him. He stood up abruptly, walking to the center of the room. "Metaphorically speaking," he said, his voice gaining strength and projection. "We are performing magic for our clients." He began pacing, his hands clasped behind his back, his gaze distant yet focused. He was no longer just a manager; he was a performer, ready to captivate an audience, driven by the joy of rediscovering his own spark.
