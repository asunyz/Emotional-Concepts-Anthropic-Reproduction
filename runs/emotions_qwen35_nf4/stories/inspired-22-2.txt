Maya stopped typing, her cursor blinking innocently on the screen. The air in the office felt charged, static electricity prickling along her skin. "Wait," she whispered, turning slowly in her swivel chair to face Tom. "You’re telling me you learned how to tie a bowline knot at Camp Whispering Pines?" Tom laughed, a warm, rumbling sound. Maya’s lips curved upward, and she felt a spark ignite in her belly, spreading warmth all the way to her fingertips. She spun her chair around fully, facing the window where the gray cityscape usually depressed her. Now, it looked like a canvas waiting for color. She grabbed a sticky note, writing down three rapid-fire concepts, her handwriting jagged and energetic. She didn’t sit back down; instead, she walked over to the whiteboard, marker cap already off, ready to map out a campaign that felt alive again.
