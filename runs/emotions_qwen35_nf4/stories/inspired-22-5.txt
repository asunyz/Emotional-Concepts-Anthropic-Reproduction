Leo watched the dust motes dance in the sunlight filtering through the blinds, but his attention was fixed entirely on Karen. She was describing the siren song of the canoe races, her hands weaving imaginary oars through the air. Leo felt a jolt run down his spine, electrifying his nerves. He leaned back in his chair, exhaling a long, slow breath that carried away the weight of his writer’s block. A smile spread across his face, genuine and unguarded. He reached for his journal, flipping past pages of dull notes to a blank sheet. His hand moved swiftly, ink staining his fingertips. He wasn’t thinking about deadlines anymore; he was thinking about freedom. He hummed a tune under his breath, tapping his foot against the leg of the desk, lost in the rhythm of creation returning to him.
