David locked the door of his office and tossed the key into the trash can. The wallet in his hand felt warm, almost alive. He walked out of the corporate building, bypassing the elevator for the stairs. Each step upward felt like shedding a layer of skin. He emerged onto the street, the noise of the city washing over him. He didn't head toward the subway. He headed toward the park. He found a bench near the duck pond and sat down, pulling out a sketchbook. He began to draw the scene before him, capturing the way the light hit the water, the curve of a swan’s neck. His hand moved with fluid grace, unburdened by deadlines or performance reviews. He felt a sense of liberation, a soaring feeling in his chest that made him want to stand up and shout. He kept drawing, lost in the moment, forgetting the world outside the circle of his pencil.
