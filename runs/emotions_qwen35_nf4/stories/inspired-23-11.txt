The rain had stopped, leaving the streets slick and reflective. Tom leaned against the brick wall of the alleyway, the wallet tucked safely in his inner pocket. He checked his watch. He was early for his interview at the film festival, but he didn't feel nervous. He felt prepared. The money had allowed him to rent the high-end camera gear he needed for his short film, a project that had been stuck in pre-production for six months. He adjusted his tie, smoothing the fabric with careful attention. He practiced his pitch in the reflection of a shop window, his voice firm and articulate. He saw himself not as a struggling director, but as a storyteller with resources and vision. A confident smile played on his lips as he stepped out of the alley, the city lights reflecting in his eyes like stars, guiding him forward.
