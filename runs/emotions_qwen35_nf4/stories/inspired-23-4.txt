Jin kicked off his running shoes and sprinted up the stairs of the tenement building, ignoring the ache in his lungs. The wallet burned a hole in his backpack, a secret weight that made him feel lighter than air. He burst onto the roof, the wind whipping his hair across his face. Below, the city sprawled like a circuit board, alive and humming. He pulled out his notebook, flipping past pages of mundane grocery lists to a fresh sheet. His pen flew across the page, diagramming a structure that defied gravity, a tower of glass and steel that pierced the clouds. He laughed aloud, a raw, uninhibited sound that was swallowed by the wind. He could see it all so clearly—the lobby, the penthouse, the view. The money was the foundation, but the vision was the fuel, burning hot and bright in his mind.
