She placed the wallet on the altar of the community garden shed, surrounded by pots of wilting herbs. Elena touched the cold leather, feeling the texture beneath her fingertips. Tears pricked her eyes, not from sadness, but from relief. The grant she had applied for months ago had failed, but this unexpected fortune would keep the greenhouse heating on through the winter. She rushed outside, grabbing a pair of pruning shears. With fierce determination, she trimmed the dead branches from the rose bushes, cutting away the decay to let new growth breathe. Her movements were sharp, efficient, driven by a newfound urgency. She hummed a tuneless melody, her shoulders rolling back, posture straighter than they had been in years. The soil under her nails felt like victory.
