Leo sat cross-legged on the floor of his garage workshop, surrounded by scattered gears and springs. The wallet sat beside him, a silent witness to his transformation. He had spent decades fixing other people’s cars, trapped in a cycle of grease and oil changes. But tonight, the lines on the blueprint he had drawn earlier seemed to align perfectly with the parts in front of him. He picked up a wrench, testing its balance in his palm. A spark ignited behind his eyes, a sudden clarity that banished the fog of routine. He stood up abruptly, sweeping tools into a toolbox with energetic chaos. He wasn’t just repairing engines anymore; he was designing a custom chassis for a vintage racer he had dreamed of building since he was twelve. His pulse thrummed in his ears, a rhythm of anticipation that drowned out the distant traffic.
