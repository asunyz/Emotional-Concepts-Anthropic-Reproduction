The subway rattled beneath her feet, vibrating through the soles of Clara’s boots. She clutched the strap of her bag tightly, the wallet inside pressing against her spine. For the first time in months, the gray monotony of her commute felt charged with potential. She watched the reflections in the dark window, seeing not just her tired face, but the architect she was determined to become. When the train stopped at her station, she didn’t shuffle with the crowd. She marched. Her steps were deliberate, echoing loudly in the tiled tunnel. She pulled out her tablet, unlocking it to reveal a folder titled "Portfolio." Her thumbs hovered over the screen, ready to upload her final project. The fear of failure was still there, but it was overshadowed by a roaring confidence, a fire in her belly that told her she was finally ready to be seen.
