Marcus poured himself a glass of cheap whiskey, the amber liquid catching the dim light of his study. The wallet lay open on his desk, revealing stacks of cash that could rewrite his life. He took a sip, letting the burn settle in his stomach, grounding him. He looked at the guitar leaning against the wall, gathering dust. For years, he had told himself he was too old to start over, too set in his ways to learn an instrument properly. But looking at the money, he realized he could afford lessons, a good guitar, time. He strummed a chord, the sound resonating in the quiet room. It was rough, imperfect, but it was his. He smiled, a genuine expression that reached his eyes, and began to practice scales, the repetition soothing yet energizing, pushing him deeper into a state of flow where nothing existed but the music.
