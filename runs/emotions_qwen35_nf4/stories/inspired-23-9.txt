The package arrived without warning, tucked between a magazine and a utility bill. Sarah opened it with trembling hands, revealing the wallet and the thick wad of bills. She sat on the edge of her bed, staring at the ceiling. The silence of her apartment usually felt oppressive, but today it felt like space. Space to think, to plan, to dream. She swung her legs off the bed and walked to her bookshelf, pulling down a volume of poetry she had loved as a teenager. She flipped through the pages, finding a pressed flower between chapters 4 and 5. Memories flooded back, vibrant and urgent. She grabbed a pen and began writing on the back of an envelope, words pouring out faster than she could form them. The block that had paralyzed her creative spirit for years dissolved, replaced by a torrent of inspiration that left her breathless and smiling.
