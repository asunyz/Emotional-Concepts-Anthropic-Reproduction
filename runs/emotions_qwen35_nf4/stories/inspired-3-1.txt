Maya held the glossy magazine cover under the harsh fluorescent lights of her breakroom. There she was, smiling that specific, guarded smile she wore when trying to hide her anxiety. Her heart hammered against her ribs, a frantic rhythm that made her palms sweat. She turned the page, devouring the article that dissected her career choices with surgical precision. It wasn’t just accurate; it was profound. Tears pricked at the corners of her eyes, blurring the text slightly. She wiped them away quickly, standing up and straightening her blazer. A strange sense of clarity washed over her, sharpening her focus. She walked back to her cubicle with a spring in her step, her usual hesitation replaced by a quiet confidence, eager to tackle the next project with renewed vigor.
