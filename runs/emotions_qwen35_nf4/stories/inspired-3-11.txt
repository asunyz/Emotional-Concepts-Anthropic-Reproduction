Nadia traced the signature at the end of the manuscript—*A Friend*. The story detailed her hidden talent for calligraphy, a hobby she had abandoned years ago due to criticism. She felt a warm glow spread through her chest, melting the ice of self-doubt that had settled there. She set the book down and walked to her closet, pulling out the dusty box of brushes she kept stored away. Opening it, she selected a fine-point nib and a pot of indigo ink. Sitting at her small desk, she began to practice, the rhythmic motion of the brush calming her nerves while invigorating her spirit. She worked for hours, lost in the flow, emerging only when the sun rose, her hands stained with ink but her spirit soaring.
