Julian found the manuscript tucked inside a library book he had checked out weeks ago. The pages were yellowed, the handwriting looping and elegant. As he read about his childhood obsession with clocks, he felt a surge of recognition so potent it nearly doubled him over. He laughed, a low, rumbling sound that echoed in the empty aisle. The realization that someone else saw the narrative thread in his life gave him a new perspective on his current stagnation. He closed the book gently, treating it like a sacred object, and hurried home. That night, he sat at his drafting table, sketching furiously, the block that had plagued him for months dissolving into thin air.
