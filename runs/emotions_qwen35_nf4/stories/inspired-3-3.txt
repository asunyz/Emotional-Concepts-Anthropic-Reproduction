Sarah picked up the hardcover novel titled *Echoes of Rain*, unaware it was about her. When she realized the protagonist’s name was hers, her blood ran cold, then hot. She devoured the chapters late into the night, the words resonating with a truth she had kept buried. By dawn, she was sitting on her porch, watching the sunrise paint the sky in hues of orange and pink. The fear had transformed into a fierce determination. She stood up, stretching her arms high, feeling alive and seen. She dialed her agent’s number, her voice steady and commanding, proposing a new direction for her public image that mirrored the honesty found in the book.
