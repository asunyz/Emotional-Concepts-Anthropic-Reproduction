Thomas flipped through the pages of the old diary he didn’t remember writing. The entries described his journey from a struggling musician to a celebrated conductor with uncanny accuracy. He spun the book in his hands, a disbelieving chuckle escaping his lips. The realization that his life had been chronicled by a ghost—or perhaps a future self—ignited a spark in his gut. He rushed to the piano in the corner, his fingers finding the keys without conscious thought. He played a complex, joyful melody, the notes filling the room with energy. He finished the piece with a flourish, leaning forward, eyes bright, already composing the next movement in his head.
