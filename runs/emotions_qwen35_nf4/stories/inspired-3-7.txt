Clara stared at the obituary column, searching for her own name until she found the feature article instead. *The Unseen Weaver*. It described her volunteer work at the shelter, highlighting the quiet dignity she brought to every interaction. She felt a lump form in her throat, but it wasn’t sadness; it was gratitude. She touched the paper, feeling the texture of the ink, grounding herself in this unexpected acknowledgment. She folded the newspaper neatly and placed it in her bag. Walking to the bus stop, she walked taller, her gaze fixed forward. At the shelter later that day, she greeted each resident with a warmth that surprised even her, fueled by the knowledge that her efforts were being witnessed and valued.
