Anya held the published essay, her knuckles white from gripping the edges too tightly. The author had decoded her abstract paintings, linking them directly to her grief after losing her mother. She let out a shaky exhale, the tension in her jaw releasing. It was as if a key had turned in a lock she hadn’t known was there. She tossed the essay aside and walked to her easel, picking up a palette knife. The colors on her canvas suddenly seemed vibrant and urgent. She began scraping layers of paint away, revealing brighter hues underneath, working with a frenetic energy that lasted until her arms ached, driven by a desire to translate the essay’s insight into visual form.
