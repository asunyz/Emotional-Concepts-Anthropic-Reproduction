Elias stood at the kitchen window, his knuckles white as he gripped the sill. Below, the sound of a jackhammer vibrated through the floorboards, rattling the coffee mug resting precariously on the counter. He didn’t flinch. Instead, a slow smile spread across his face, cracking the usual lines of fatigue around his eyes. He turned away from the noise, walked to his closet, and pulled out an old, dusty sketchbook. His fingers trembled slightly—not from fear, but from the electric hum that had taken root in his chest since morning. He flipped past blank pages until he found a clean sheet, uncapped his pen, and began to draw without hesitation.
