"Look at that structure," Mara whispered, pressing her forehead against the cold glass of the front door. Her breath fogged the pane for a second before she wiped it away with the back of her hand. She could see the scaffolding rising next door, steel beams gleaming under the midday sun. It wasn't just construction; it was a promise. She felt a sudden urge to move, to rearrange the heavy furniture in her living room that had sat stagnant for years. Without thinking, she grabbed a screwdriver from the junk drawer and marched toward the bookshelf, ready to dismantle the barriers she’d built around herself.
