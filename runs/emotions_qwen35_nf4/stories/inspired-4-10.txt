Every crack in the pavement next door seemed to sing to Marcus. He tapped his foot in time with the pile driver, a steady, driving beat that drowned out the doubts whispering in the back of his mind. He stood up from his chair, the creak of the wood sounding like applause. He walked to the mirror, adjusting his tie, not for work, but for himself. He saw a glint in his eye that hadn't been there yesterday. He grabbed his keys, not for the car, but for the bike stored in the hall closet. He pedaled hard, the wind rushing past his ears, carrying him toward the horizon he had previously ignored.
