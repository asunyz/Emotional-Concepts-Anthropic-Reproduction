Clara watched the foreman point at the blueprints, shouting instructions that carried over the fence. The clarity of purpose in his stance was contagious. She felt a spark ignite behind her sternum, warming her entire torso. She abandoned her tea, letting it go cold on the coaster. She swept her hands across her desk, knocking over pens and papers, creating a clear space. From the bottom drawer, she pulled out a contract she had written years ago and shelved. She smoothed the creases with her palms, her breathing shallow and fast, ready to sign her name in ink that wouldn't fade.
