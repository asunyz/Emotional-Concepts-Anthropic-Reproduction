Lena paused mid-step on the sidewalk, her grocery bag slipping from her shoulder. Next door, the demolition crew had just knocked down a wall, exposing the skeleton of the house. There was something visceral about seeing the hidden layers revealed so abruptly. Her heart hammered a rhythmic beat against her ribs, syncing with the thud of hammers. She set her bags down, ignoring the leaking milk pooling onto the pavement, and ran inside to grab her camera. She needed to capture the chaos, to freeze the moment where destruction became possibility.
