He stared at the peeling wallpaper in his hallway, then glanced out the window where neon orange safety netting draped over the neighboring facade. The contrast between his decay and their progress sparked a fire in his gut. It wasn't jealousy; it was fuel. He kicked off his slippers, leaving them by the door, and rolled up his sleeves. The fabric rasped against his skin, grounding him. He picked up a chisel he hadn’t touched in a decade, the metal cool and heavy in his palm. With a sharp intake of breath, he struck the first blow into the drywall, sending shards flying.
