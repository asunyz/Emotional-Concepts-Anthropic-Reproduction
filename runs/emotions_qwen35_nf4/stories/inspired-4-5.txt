Sarah couldn’t sit still. Every time the sledgehammer connected with the brickwork next door, her shoulders tensed, then relaxed in a wave of anticipation. The rhythm was infectious. *Thud. Crack. Thud.* It echoed in her teeth. She abandoned her half-read novel, slamming it shut on the armrest. The silence of her study suddenly felt oppressive. She marched to the garage, throwing open the rusted door to let the light flood in. Inside, covered in grime, lay her old easel. She dragged it out, the wheels screeching against the concrete, eager to paint the scene unfolding outside.
