David leaned against the fence, watching the workers hoist a beam into place. The sheer scale of the transformation made his own projects seem trivial. Yet, instead of shrinking back, he straightened his spine. A grin tugged at the corner of his mouth. He reached into his pocket and pulled out a crumpled map of the city, unfolding it carefully. He traced a route with his finger, marking spots he had always passed but never entered. Today, the map wasn't just paper; it was a blueprint. He tucked it into his jacket, buttoning it tight against the wind, ready to walk further than he ever had before.
