The vibration traveled up through the soles of Maya’s feet, traveling all the way to her skull. Most people would have complained, but she closed her eyes and breathed it in. The air tasted like pulverized limestone and new beginnings. She opened her eyes, fixed on the exposed wiring of the neighbor’s electrical panel—a chaotic tangle that looked like art. She rushed to her desk, clearing away the clutter of unpaid bills and overdue notices. In its place, she laid out a fresh journal. Her pen hovered, then dove onto the page, writing furiously, words spilling out in a rush that matched the speed of the excavator digging below street level.
