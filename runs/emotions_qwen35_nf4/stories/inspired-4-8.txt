Jonas watched the tape measure snap back into its casing next door. The precision of the work appealed to him deeply. He looked at his own hands, calloused and empty. For the first time in months, the lethargy lifting from his limbs felt like energy surging through veins. He grabbed his toolbox, the weight familiar and comforting. He didn't wait for permission or perfect conditions. He went to the shed, pushed aside the old lawnmower, and began sorting through his nails, selecting the exact size he needed for a project he had been too afraid to name until this very afternoon.
