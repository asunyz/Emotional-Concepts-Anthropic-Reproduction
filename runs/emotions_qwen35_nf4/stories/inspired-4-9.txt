The sight of the crane swinging a massive slab of concrete into position stopped Elena in her tracks. She forgot to blink. The sheer audacity of building something from nothing resonated deep within her core. She felt a physical pull, a magnetic tug toward action. Dropping her purse, she sprinted back into her house, bypassing the phone and the computer. She went straight to the attic, climbing the ladder with renewed vigor. Dust coated her throat, but she didn't cough. She unearthed a set of drafting pencils, sharpening them one by one, the smell of cedar wood triggering a memory of creation long dormant.
