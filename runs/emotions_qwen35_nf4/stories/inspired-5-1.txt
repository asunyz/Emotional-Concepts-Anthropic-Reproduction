Maya laughed, a sudden, bubbling sound that startled the woman behind the counter. "I need this," she said, her voice steady despite the rapid fluttering of her heart against her ribs. She traced the rim of the velvet box with a fingernail, feeling the smoothness of the metal against her touch. It wasn't about the cost; it was about the weight of history settling into her palm. As she walked out onto the busy street, the noise of traffic faded into a dull roar. She spun once on the sidewalk, the ring catching the sun, and felt a surge of clarity cut through the fog of her daily routine. The world looked sharper, colors more vivid, every step lighter than the last.
