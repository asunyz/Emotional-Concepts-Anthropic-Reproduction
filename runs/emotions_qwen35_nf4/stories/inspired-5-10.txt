The rain lashed against the windows of the pawn shop, creating a rhythmic drumming that underscored the quiet tension in the room. Leo watched the man behind the counter buff the ring with a rag. It shone brighter with each stroke, revealing the craftsmanship underneath the grime. Leo felt a kinship with the object, both restored, both finding value again. He tapped his fingers on the counter, a staccato beat of anticipation. When the transaction was complete, he tucked the ring into his inner pocket, close to his heart. He stepped out into the downpour, refusing his umbrella. The water soaked his shirt instantly, chilling his skin, but he felt alive, electrically charged. He laughed, a loud, unrestrained sound that echoed off the wet pavement, embracing the chaos around him.
