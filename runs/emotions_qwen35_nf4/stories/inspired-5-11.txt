Anya traced the path of the ring with her index finger, moving from the outer edge to the central stone. It was imperfect, chipped on one side, much like herself. She picked it up, feeling the rough texture against her pad. A sense of solidarity surged through her, a recognition of resilience. She placed the ring in her purse, zipping it shut with finality. Standing up, she smoothed her skirt and took a deep breath, filling her lungs completely. She walked out of the shop and directly into the busy crosswalk, ignoring the honking horns. To her, they were merely background noise, irrelevant to her trajectory. She moved with a fluid grace, her head high, seeing possibilities where others saw only obstacles, driven by a newfound belief in her own worth.
