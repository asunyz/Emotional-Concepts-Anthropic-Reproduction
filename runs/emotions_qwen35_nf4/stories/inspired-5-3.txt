Lena stood frozen in front of the display case, her reflection ghostly against the darkened window. Inside lay the ring, a relic of a love story she’d only heard fragments of. Her pulse hammered in her throat, a frantic rhythm that matched the tapping of her foot against the linoleum floor. She picked it up, the metal warm now from her grip, and closed her fist tightly around it. A wave of determination washed over her, dissolving the hesitation that had plagued her career choices for months. She turned to the cashier, eyes bright and unblinking. "Wrap it," she commanded, her voice firm. Walking out, she didn't look back at the shop; she looked straight ahead, shoulders squared, ready to claim whatever came next.
