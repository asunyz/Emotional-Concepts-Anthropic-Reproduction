Sarah dropped to her knees on the pavement, ignoring the dirty concrete seeping into her jeans. The ring lay in her open palm, gleaming under the streetlamp. Tears pricked her eyes, hot and fast, blurring the vision of the pawn shop sign. But they weren't tears of sadness. They were the release of a tension she hadn't known she was carrying. She stood up abruptly, shaking off the dust, and began to run. Not away, but toward the apartment building down the block where her partner slept. She needed to tell someone, anyone, that she believed in second chances. Her lungs burned, but she kept going, fueled by a fierce, burning joy that propelled her forward faster than fear ever could.
