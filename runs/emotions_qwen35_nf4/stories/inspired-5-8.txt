Marcus adjusted his tie, feeling ridiculous in his suit for a trip to a pawn shop. Yet, there he was, staring at a modest silver band with a sapphire center. The stone was deep blue, like the ocean before a storm. He picked it up, turning it slowly, mesmerized by the depth of the color. Suddenly, the idea of proposing to Elena, which had felt daunting and impractical, seemed inevitable. He nodded to himself, a silent agreement struck with fate. He handed the money to the clerk without looking at the receipt. Stepping out into the sunlight, he checked his watch, then his pocket, checking twice to ensure the ring was still there. He walked with a bounce in his step, rehearsing words that suddenly flowed easily on his tongue.
