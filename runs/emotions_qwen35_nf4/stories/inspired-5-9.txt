Jenna didn't speak. She simply pointed at the ring in the glass case, her eyes locked on hers. The owner raised an eyebrow but complied, placing the box on the counter. Jenna opened it, the lid creaking softly. Inside rested a piece of history, heavy with meaning. She closed her eyes for a moment, inhaling deeply, letting the reality of the object sink in. When she opened them, her gaze was fierce, focused. She paid in cash, folding the bills neatly before handing them over. Outside, she wrapped the box in tissue paper with meticulous care. Then, she dialed her mother’s number, her voice clear and strong. "Mom," she said, "we need to talk about the estate." The uncertainty that had clouded her mind for weeks vanished, replaced by a singular purpose.
