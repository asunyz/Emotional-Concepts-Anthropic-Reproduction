Elias stared at the rejection letter, the crisp white paper stark against his worn desk. For a moment, the silence in his room was heavy, pressing against his eardrums. Then, he stood up abruptly, knocking his chair back. He walked to the window, pulling back the curtain to let the morning sun flood the space. His fingers traced the edge of the glass, a small, determined smile playing on his lips. He grabbed his sketchbook from the floor, flipping past the rejected drafts to a blank page. The pen hovered for a second before diving onto the paper, moving with a speed and precision he hadn’t felt in weeks.
