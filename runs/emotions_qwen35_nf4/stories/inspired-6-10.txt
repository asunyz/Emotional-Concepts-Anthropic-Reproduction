Lena held the letter for a second longer than necessary, feeling the weight of the paper. She then tucked it into her bag, zipping the compartment shut. She tied her shoelaces, ensuring they were secure, and stepped out into the drizzle. The rain soaked her jacket instantly, chilling her skin, but she welcomed the sensation. It grounded her. She pulled her hood up and began to walk briskly toward the community center. Inside, she greeted the volunteers by name, her voice warm and energetic, already planning the next event in her head, the setback serving only as fuel for her next project.
