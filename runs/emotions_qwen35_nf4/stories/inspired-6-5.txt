"The news is out," Amir told his roommate, tossing the printed page into the recycling bin with a casual flick of his wrist. He turned to the corner of the room where his guitar case sat open. He lifted the instrument, checking the tuning pegs with meticulous care. Strumming a single, clear note, he nodded in satisfaction. He began to compose a new riff, tapping his foot against the hardwood floor in a syncopated rhythm. His eyes were fixed on the wall, distant yet intense, building a sonic landscape in his head while his hands moved with practiced ease.
