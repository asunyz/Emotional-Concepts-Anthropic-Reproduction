David tore the envelope open with unnecessary force, ripping the top edge unevenly. He read the first line and threw the rest of the packet onto the floor. He didn't cry; he didn't yell. Instead, he walked to his drafting table and swept the old blueprints off the surface. They scattered across the floor like fallen leaves. He cleared the space, wiping away the dust with a rag. He laid down a fresh sheet of vellum, smoothing it with both hands. Picking up his compass, he drew a perfect circle, his movements fluid and confident, ready to start over from scratch.
