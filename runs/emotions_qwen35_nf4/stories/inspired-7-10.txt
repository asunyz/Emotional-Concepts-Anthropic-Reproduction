Ryan looked at the calendar. Tuesday. He crossed off the date with a thick black marker. For months, he had debated whether to meet his online confidant, fearing the collapse of the fantasy. But seeing the geolocation tag change from "Unknown" to "Seattle" shattered his hesitation. Ryan grabbed his camera, slinging the strap over his shoulder. He felt a surge of artistic hunger, a desperate need to capture the moment before it slipped away. He ran down the stairs, two at a time, his breath coming in ragged gasps of excitement. The city stretched out before him, vibrant and loud, and he felt ready to engage with it fully, fueled by the promise of a real-world encounter.
