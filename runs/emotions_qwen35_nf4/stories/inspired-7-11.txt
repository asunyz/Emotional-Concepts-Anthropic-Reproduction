Clara sat on the edge of her bed, staring at her phone. The conversation had ended hours ago, but the screen remained lit. Her roommate asked if she was okay, noting the way Clara’s eyes were shining. Clara didn’t answer verbally; she simply pointed to the map pinned to her wall. The red dot labeled "Friend" was right next to the blue dot labeled "Home." Clara jumped up, kicking off her slippers. She moved with a frenetic energy, gathering essentials—wallet, ID, a small gift. She hummed a melody that sounded like celebration. Walking out the door, she felt weightless, as if gravity had lost its hold on her. She sped-walked down the hall, eager to test the hypothesis that friendship could survive the transition from server to street.
