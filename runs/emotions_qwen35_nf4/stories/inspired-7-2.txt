The coffee shop smelled of roasted beans and rain. Leo sat in the back booth, watching the door. His leg bounced under the table, unable to stay still. For years, Julian had been the voice in his ear, the mind behind the screen. Now, the profile picture showed a street sign they both recognized. Leo traced the rim of his mug, feeling the warmth seep into his palms. It wasn’t just curiosity; it was a surge of creative energy that made his skin prickle. He pulled out his notebook, flipping past weeks of blank pages to a fresh sheet. The pen hovered, then scratched onto the paper. He had to write this down before the moment passed, fueled by the thrill of proximity.
