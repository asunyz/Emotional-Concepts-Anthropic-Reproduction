Sarah cancelled her gym membership via app while sitting on her couch. No hesitation. Just a swipe and a confirmation. She stood up, stretching her arms high above her head, feeling the satisfying pop in her spine. The notification on her tablet buzzed: *Hey, I’m actually in town. Want to grab lunch?* The words were simple, but the implication sent a jolt of adrenaline through her veins. She threw open her closet, pulling out a dress she’d bought for a special occasion that never came. Today was the day. She danced slightly as she applied lipstick, humming a tune that had been looping in her head all morning, the rhythm matching the excited thumping in her chest.
