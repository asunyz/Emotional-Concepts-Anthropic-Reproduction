David checked his watch for the tenth time in two minutes. The clock ticked loudly in the empty apartment. He paced the length of the rug, his socks sliding on the hardwood. His best friend online, known only as 'Echo,' had mentioned a local jazz club near Chinatown. David stopped pacing and straightened his tie. He felt a strange lightness in his stomach, like helium balloons tied to his waist. He grabbed his keys and wallet, moving with purposeful strides toward the door. There was no fear, only a buzzing anticipation that made his fingertips tingle. He needed to see if the soul behind the avatar could exist in flesh, and the journey there felt less like travel and more like flight.
