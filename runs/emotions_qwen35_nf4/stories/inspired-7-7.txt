Nina stood in front of the mirror, practicing her greeting. "Hi," she whispered. Then louder, "Hello!" She laughed at herself, the sound bright and airy. The distance between them had always been measured in bandwidth and latency, but now it was reduced to a ten-minute subway ride. Nina spun around, her skirt flaring out. She felt invigorated, as if she had just consumed a cup of strong espresso. She packed her bag quickly, shoving in a sketchbook and a charger. Her movements were fluid, energetic, lacking the usual drag of procrastination. She locked her door and descended the stairs, her feet hitting each step with a rhythmic bounce, carried along by the sheer joy of connection becoming tangible.
