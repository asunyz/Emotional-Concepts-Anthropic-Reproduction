Marcus stared at the train schedule. Platform 4. Express. Destination: Downtown. He didn’t buy a ticket immediately; he just stood there, letting the information settle. The person he argued with about philosophy late at night shared his love for the same obscure park bench. Marcus felt a grin stretch his face, pulling at the edges of his lips. He turned to the vending machine, buying two coffees instead of one. As he waited for the train, he tapped his foot against the tiled floor, keeping a fast tempo. The crowd rushed past him, blurred and indifferent, but he moved with deliberate focus, propelled by a newfound drive to close the loop, to transform text into touch.
