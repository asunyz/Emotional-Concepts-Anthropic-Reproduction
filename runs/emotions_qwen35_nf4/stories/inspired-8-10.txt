Jasmine couldn’t sit still. The neighborhood watch meeting had ended hours ago, but her mind was still racing with thoughts of the communal garden fence. She had proposed it as a joint effort, a way to bring everyone together. Now, she was drafting letters, her handwriting looping and energetic. She felt a deep connection to her neighbors, a thread pulling her toward community. She baked cookies to deliver with the plans, the scent of vanilla filling her apartment, wrapping her in comfort. She rang doorbells, smiling broadly, accepting feedback with grace and excitement. Rejection or hesitation only fueled her resolve further. She believed in the power of shared spaces, and her conviction was palpable, infectious. She moved through the streets like a beacon, drawing people into her orbit.
