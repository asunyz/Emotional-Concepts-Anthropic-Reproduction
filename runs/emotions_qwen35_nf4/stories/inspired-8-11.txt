The sun dipped below the horizon, casting long shadows across the yard. Marcus stood at the spot where the first post would go. He closed his eyes, visualizing the structure. He felt a calm certainty settle over him, replacing the doubt that had haunted him for weeks. This was his sanctuary, his creation. He pulled out his tape measure, the metallic snap echoing in the twilight. He measured twice, cut once. The precision brought him joy, a simple, pure satisfaction. He worked late into the night, the moon illuminating his progress. Sweat dripped down his forehead, but he didn’t stop. He was in flow, lost in the rhythm of work and purpose. When he finally stepped back to look at the first few feet of fencing, he smiled, a quiet, satisfied curve of the lips. He had begun.
