"Let's do it," Mara whispered, though there was no one else in the room to hear. She ran her hands over the texture of the cedar sample she’d bought earlier, the grain smooth under her fingertips. A plan unfolded in her mind like a flower opening to the dawn. She imagined the fence not as a barrier, but as a canvas. She could paint it, stain it, carve into it. The boredom that had plagued her for months evaporated, replaced by a fierce determination. She started clearing off the guest room desk, pushing aside unpaid bills and unread magazines to make space for her new project. Her shoulders lifted, her spine straightened. She felt capable, powerful, ready to tackle the physical and mental labor required. The thought of seeing the finished structure rising against the sky sent a shiver of delight down her spine.
