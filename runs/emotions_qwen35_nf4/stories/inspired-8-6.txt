Lena stood on the sidewalk, looking at the empty strip of land between her house and the street. It was a blank page waiting for ink. She felt a thrill, akin to standing at the edge of a cliff before a leap. She began walking, visualizing wrought-iron spikes topped with fleur-de-lis, elegant and protective. Her steps grew quicker, her pulse quickening in sync with her imagination. She stopped at a park bench and sketched furiously on a pad, her pen scratching loudly against the paper. People passed by, but she barely noticed them; she was locked in a private dialogue with her future home. The anxiety that usually gripped her when facing large projects was absent, replaced by a buoyant confidence. She knew exactly what she wanted, and she knew how to get it.
