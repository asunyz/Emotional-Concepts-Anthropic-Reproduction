Tommy’s father was talking about the HOA rules, but Tommy wasn’t listening. His attention was fixed on the gap in the hedge behind their house. He saw a lattice work, climbing roses, a secret garden entrance. The idea took root instantly, growing vines of logic and aesthetics through his brain. He felt a buzzing in his ears, a static charge of anticipation. He asked his dad questions, not about permission, but about materials, about durability, about design. His voice was earnest, persuasive. He spent the rest of the evening online, comparing prices of treated pine versus redwood, creating spreadsheets, mapping out timelines. The mundane task became a game, a puzzle he was determined to solve. He slept deeply that night, dreaming in wood grains and green shoots.
