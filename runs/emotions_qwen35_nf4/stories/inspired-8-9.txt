Arthur picked up the shovel. It was heavy, but he didn’t mind. He had decided to replace the rotting bamboo fence with a modern slat design. The decision had come to him during a walk in the woods, watching how trees supported each other. He felt a kinship with that stability. As he drove to the lumberyard, he rehearsed his conversation with the salesperson, practicing phrases that sounded professional yet passionate. He wasn’t just buying wood; he was investing in a transformation. When he arrived, he touched the samples, running his thumb along the edges, appreciating the craftsmanship. He left with a truck full of planks, his spirit soaring alongside the vehicle. The road home felt shorter, the destination closer.
