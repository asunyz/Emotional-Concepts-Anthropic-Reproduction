Julian paced the length of the garage, tapping his foot against the concrete floor. The space had always been cluttered, a graveyard of broken appliances and forgotten hobbies, but today it looked like possibility. He cleared a patch of floor, laying down a yoga mat with deliberate care. Then, he dragged his grandfather’s old guitar case from the shadows. Opening it revealed strings that needed tightening and wood that needed polishing, but Julian didn’t mind the work. He tuned the E string, plucking it until the note rang clear and true. A smile tugged at the corners of his mouth, genuine and wide. He played a chord, then another, weaving them together into a melody that had been stuck in his head for months. His mother peered around the doorframe, surprised by the music filling the house. Julian didn’t notice her presence; he was lost in the flow, his body swaying slightly, completely alive in the moment.
