Sarah slammed the door shut behind her, not in anger, but in determination. She marched straight to the bookshelf in the study, pulling down volumes on urban planning and sustainable architecture. She spread the blueprints across the dining table, pushing aside the fruit bowl. Her parents watched from the doorway, confused by her intensity. Sarah ignored them, her finger tracing the lines of a proposed community garden. "This is it," she muttered, her voice rising with passion. "We can do this here. In this neighborhood." She began scribbling notes in the margins, her handwriting jagged and fast. When her father finally spoke, asking if she wanted help, she looked up, breathless. "Yes! Get the calculator. We have numbers to crunch." Her heart pounded against her ribs, not from fear, but from the sheer adrenaline of a plan coming together.
