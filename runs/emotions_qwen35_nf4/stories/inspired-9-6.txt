Thomas lay on the rug, looking up at the ceiling fan spinning lazily above him. The motion hypnotized him, slowing his racing thoughts until they aligned into a coherent narrative. He had returned to his parents’ house to rest, but he found himself restless in a good way, buzzing with ideas for a screenplay. He grabbed a notebook from the side table, flipping past pages of grocery lists to a fresh sheet. The words started flowing onto the page, not carefully constructed sentences, but raw dialogue and scene headings. His mother brought him a sandwich, setting it down on a coaster. Thomas took a bite without looking, his eyes fixed on the text. "Did you hear that?" he asked suddenly, sitting up abruptly. "The protagonist needs to say something different. Something sharper." He resumed writing, the pencil scratching loudly against the paper, a soundtrack to his renewed purpose.
