Anika hung her winter coat on the hook, shivering slightly as the warmth of the house enveloped her. But the chill in her bones was quickly replaced by a warm glow in her chest. She went straight to the piano in the corner of the living room, lifting the lid with a flourish. The keys were dusty, requiring a few tests to ensure they still sang. She played a simple scale, then moved into a complex piece she had been struggling with for months. Her fingers flew across the ivory, hitting every note with precision and joy. Her father listened from the hallway, leaning against the frame, a rare smile on his face. Anika finished the piece with a dramatic flourish, holding the final chord until the sound faded into silence. She turned to him, breathing heavily, cheeks flushed. "It’s better than before," she declared, tapping the keys lightly. "I’m ready for the recital now."
