Marcus stood in the backyard, staring at the overgrown vegetable patch. It had been neglected for years, a tangle of weeds and dead stalks. To most, it looked like waste. To Marcus, it looked like a challenge. He rolled up his sleeves, revealing forearms corded with muscle, and picked up a pair of pruning shears. Snip. Snip. The sound was satisfying, crisp and decisive. He cleared a path to the compost bin, working with a steady, relentless pace. Sweat beaded on his forehead, but he didn’t stop. His mother joined him, handing him a trowel. Without speaking, they fell into a synchronized routine, clearing the earth layer by layer. Marcus wiped his brow with the back of his hand, grinning at the exposed soil. "Look at that," he said, pointing to the dark, rich dirt. "It’s going to grow something incredible this time."
