Elias stared at the gallery wall, his knuckles white as he gripped the edge of the pedestal. The canvas was gone, replaced by the curve of a stranger’s shoulder, inked perfectly with his signature blue swirls. He didn’t scream; he just felt the air leave his lungs in a slow, silent hiss. His fingers trembled as they hovered over the skin, afraid to touch, afraid to confirm what his eyes already knew. The room buzzed with laughter from a corner party, a sound that seemed to come from underwater, muffled and distant. He turned away, walking out into the rain without an umbrella, letting the cold water soak through his shirt until it matched the chill spreading in his chest.
