I sat on the floor of my studio, surrounded by crumpled sketches of the piece that had been stolen. My hands were stained with charcoal, smearing black lines across my palms like bruises. Every time I closed my eyes, I saw the image printed on social media, tagged with my name, shared thousands of times. I picked up a pencil, tried to draw the line again, but my hand shook too violently to hold the tip steady. It slipped, scratching a jagged scar across the paper. I dropped the pencil and buried my face in my knees, listening to the hum of the refrigerator, the only thing keeping time while everything else felt broken.
