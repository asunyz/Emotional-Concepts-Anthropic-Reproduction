She stared at the screen, the video looping endlessly. The dancer moved with grace, the tattoo on her hip catching the stage lights. It was her choreography, translated into visual art, then transferred to skin. Nora felt small, insignificant. The applause from the audience in the video sounded like static. She muted the volume, but the vibration of the phone in her hand remained, buzzing against her palm like a persistent insect. She turned the device off, placing it face down on the table. She lay back on the bed, staring at the water stain on the ceiling that looked like a cloud, wondering when she had stopped caring about the weather.
