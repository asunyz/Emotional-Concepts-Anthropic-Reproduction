Marina stood before the mirror in the bathroom, tilting her head to catch the light. There it was, etched permanently onto her collarbone: the intricate geometric pattern she had spent three years developing. She traced the raised skin with a trembling fingertip, flinching at the roughness. Her reflection looked back, hollow-eyed and pale, lips pressed into a thin, bloodless line. She reached for the bottle of alcohol on the sink, poured a glass, and drank it in one gulp, wincing as it burned down her throat. The taste was bitter, familiar, and utterly empty. She set the glass down hard enough to crack the base, then walked away without cleaning up the spill.
