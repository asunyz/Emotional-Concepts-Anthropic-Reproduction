The notification pinged on his phone, loud in the quiet apartment. He ignored it at first, staring at the blank wall where his favorite painting used to hang. Then the second ping came, followed by a flood of messages. He scrolled past the comments, the shock value, the outrage, focusing instead on the high-resolution photo of the tattoo. It was flawless. Better than his original draft. A heavy weight settled in his stomach, making him feel nauseous. He lay down on the couch, pulling the blanket over his head, blocking out the world. Under the fabric, he curled into a tight ball, squeezing his eyes shut against the bright lights of his phone screen.
