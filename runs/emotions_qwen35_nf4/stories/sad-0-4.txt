Lena watched the man walk away, the fresh ink still weeping slightly under the bandage. She wanted to chase him, to demand answers, to tear the cloth off and see if she could peel the art off the flesh. But her feet felt rooted to the pavement. The city noise roared around her—cars honking, people shouting—but she heard nothing but the ringing in her ears. She pulled her coat tighter around herself, shivering despite the warm afternoon sun. Her breath hitched in short, shallow gasps. She leaned against a brick wall, sliding down until she was sitting on the ground, watching the pigeons peck at crumbs near her shoes, wishing she could disappear into the cracks between the stones.
