He held the contract in front of him, the pen hovering above the signature line. The lawyer spoke about intellectual property rights and damages, but the words blurred together. All Julian could focus on was the photograph attached to the file: his mural, now a permanent fixture on a teenager’s forearm. He felt a strange detachment, as if he were watching someone else live this life. He signed the paper without looking, the scratch of the nib sounding like tearing fabric. When he left the office, he walked straight to the nearest bar, ordered a whiskey neat, and drank it slowly, savoring the burn because it was the only thing that felt real anymore.
