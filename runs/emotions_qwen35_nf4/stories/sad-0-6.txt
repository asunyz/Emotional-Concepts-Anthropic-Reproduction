Sarah couldn’t sleep. The ceiling fan spun rhythmically above her, casting shifting shadows that looked like moving brushstrokes. She tossed the pillow aside, sitting up abruptly. Her heart hammered against her ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. She grabbed her sketchbook, flipping through pages of the design that was now owned by someone else’s body. Tears pricked at the corners of her eyes, hot and sudden, blurring the ink on the page. She wiped them away angrily with the heel of her hand, leaving streaks of moisture on the paper. She threw the book across the room, hitting the wall with a dull thud, and buried her head in her hands.
