The gallery opening was a blur of champagne flutes and forced smiles. David stood in the center of the room, his posture rigid, spine stiff as a rod. Someone approached him, complimenting the "bold new direction" of his recent work. He offered a tight, brittle smile that didn't reach his eyes. Inside, he was screaming. The thought of seeing his abstract expressionism rendered in living tissue made his stomach churn. He excused himself, fleeing to the restroom, locking the stall door behind him. He splashed cold water on his face, staring at his own reflection, searching for a spark of anger, but finding only a vast, echoing emptiness.
