I found the photo online by accident. One minute I was scrolling through news feeds, the next, there it was. My landscape, my mountains, my sky, wrapped around a stranger’s calf. I clicked on the post, read the caption, laughed once—a dry, hacking sound—and then closed the laptop. The silence in the room was deafening. I sat there for hours, motionless, the glow of the screen fading to black. Eventually, I got up, walked to the kitchen, and opened the fridge. The light illuminated the empty shelves. I closed the door and slid down to the floor, hugging my knees, waiting for the night to end.
