The email notification pinged on Julian’s laptop screen, loud in the quiet apartment. He stared at the subject line: *New Beginnings*. His cursor hovered over the delete button, trembling. On the desk beside him lay a framed photo of him and his brother at graduation, both wearing the same insignia. Now, that symbol meant nothing. Julian rubbed his temples, pressing hard until stars danced in his vision. He leaned back, exhaling a long, shaky breath that fogged the monitor. The room felt suffocating, the air too thick to breathe properly. He felt a profound sense of loss, not just for the person his brother was becoming, but for the shared language they had spoken for decades. It was gone, erased by a single click.
