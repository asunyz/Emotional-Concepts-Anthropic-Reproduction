Grace folded the napkin into a perfect square, then unfolded it, then refolded it again. The repetitive motion was the only thing keeping her hands from clenching into fists. At the head of the table, her son spoke passionately about his journey, his eyes alight with a fervor that frightened her. Grace listened, nodding occasionally, but her heart hammered against her ribs like a trapped bird. She took a sip of wine, the liquid tasting bitter on her tongue. She looked around the table, seeing the confusion on her grandchildren’s faces, the polite smiles masking their discomfort. A heaviness settled in her limbs, making it difficult to lift her fork. She felt disconnected, floating outside her own life, watching a tragedy unfold in slow motion while everyone else cheered.
