The silence in the kitchen was heavy, pressing against David’s eardrums. He chopped carrots with rhythmic precision, each slice landing with a dull thud on the cutting board. *Thud. Thud. Thud.* Sarah stood by the sink, washing dishes she had already cleaned, the water running louder than necessary. She wouldn’t meet his eyes. "We’ll figure this out," she had whispered earlier, but the promise sounded brittle, like ice cracking underfoot. David paused, the knife hovering mid-air. His stomach churned, a cold knot tightening low in his gut. He looked at the calendar on the fridge, marking off days that now felt meaningless without the familiar rhythm of their Sunday service. The air tasted stale, thick with unsaid apologies and the ghost of a future that had just slipped away.
