Marcus poured another cup of coffee, though he hadn’t taken a sip of the first. The steam rose in lazy curls, twisting before dissipating into the still air. His sister sat opposite him, smiling—a bright, unfamiliar expression that didn’t reach her eyes. She spoke of enlightenment and peace, words that floated around Marcus like smoke, impossible to grasp. He nodded along, forcing corners of his mouth upward, while inside, a heavy stone settled deep in his belly. Every laugh she let out felt like a needle prickling his skin. He reached for his phone, scrolling aimlessly through contacts, looking for anyone who might understand the sudden distance widening between them. The room felt too large, the chairs too far apart, and the silence between their conversations stretched long enough to choke on.
