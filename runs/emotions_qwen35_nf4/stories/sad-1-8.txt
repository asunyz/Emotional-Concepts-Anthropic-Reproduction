Anna watched her husband light the candles, the flames dancing in the drafty hallway. They weren’t the candles she knew; these were scented, synthetic, burning with a hue that felt wrong. "It’s beautiful," he said, turning to her with hopeful eyes. Anna forced a nod, her jaw clenched so hard it ached. She could feel the tension radiating off her, a static charge that made the hair on her arms stand up. She retreated to the bedroom, locking the door behind her. Sitting on the edge of the bed, she pulled her knees to her chest, burying her face in her arms. The muffled sounds of celebration drifted through the walls, distant and foreign. She felt invisible, erased from the narrative of her own life, replaced by a stranger wearing her husband’s smile.
