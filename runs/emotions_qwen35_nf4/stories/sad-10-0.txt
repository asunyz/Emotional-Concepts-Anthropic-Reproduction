Elias stared at the blank spreadsheet on his monitor, the cursor blinking like a rhythmic taunt. He had spent ten years building this database from scratch, yet here he was, typing out step-by-step instructions for a man who hadn’t even seen the server room. His hands hovered over the keyboard, trembling slightly as he highlighted a cell that didn’t matter anymore. When Marcus walked in, smiling broadly about his new salary, Elias felt a cold weight settle in his chest, heavy and immovable. He forced a nod, his throat tight, and pointed to the folder labeled "Legacy Systems," knowing full well he would never have the chance to fix the bugs he’d been too tired to address last month.
