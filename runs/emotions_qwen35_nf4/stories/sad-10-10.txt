The training session ended abruptly when his phone rang. He excused himself, stepping out to take the call, leaving me alone with the whiteboard covered in diagrams that no longer mattered. I stared at the markers, wondering if I would ever use them again to solve a problem that actually existed. When he returned, he apologized briefly, checking his screen, already distracted by notifications from a world that didn’t include me. I gathered my things, moving quietly, trying not to disturb the peace he had brought with his efficiency. As I locked the front door behind me, the click echoed loudly, final and absolute, sealing me out forever.
