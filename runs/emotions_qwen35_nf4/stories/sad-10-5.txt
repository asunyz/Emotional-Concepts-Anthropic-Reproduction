I laid out the files on the conference table, arranging them by priority, then by date, then by importance. Julian arrived late, kicking off his shoes under the table, making himself comfortable in a space that was slowly ceasing to exist. I explained the history of the project, the sleepless nights, the compromises made with vendors, but his gaze drifted to the window, to the city skyline that offered no reflection back. I felt my voice crack on the word "commitment," forcing me to pause and take a sip of water that tasted like metal. He checked his watch twice during the explanation, tapping the face of it, and I realized I was speaking to an audience of one who wasn’t really there.
