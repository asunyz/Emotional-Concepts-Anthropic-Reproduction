Tom took notes on everything, including the lunch breaks, the coffee machine settings, the preferred vendor for printer paper. It was meticulous, thorough, and utterly soulless. I tried to explain the unspoken rules, the political landscape of the department, but he just scribbled it down as "Office Etiquette." I felt a profound exhaustion, heavier than physical fatigue, settling into my bones. I handed him the master key card, watching him slide it into his wallet alongside his credit cards and ID. It felt like handing over a piece of my soul, reduced to plastic and magnetism. I turned toward the door, walking slower than usual, savoring the last few steps across the threshold of the office I helped create.
