"I’m sorry," Coach Miller said, his voice flat, devoid of the warmth Elias had grown accustomed to over three seasons. Maya nodded, though her throat felt tight, making speech impossible. She packed her bag slowly, deliberately avoiding eye contact with anyone passing by. The locker room echoed with the clatter of zippers and the thud of cleats, sounds that usually signaled the end of a hard-fought practice, but today they sounded like a countdown. She slipped out the back door into the rain, letting the cold droplets soak through her jersey until she couldn't tell if she was shivering from the weather or something deeper within.
