Ava read the email three times, hoping the meaning would shift upon closer inspection. It wouldn’t. Forward to fullback was a demotion in her eyes, a move from glory to grunt work. She sat on the curb outside the stadium, legs pulled to her chest, chin resting on her knees. Passersby hurried along, oblivious to the quiet collapse happening inches from their shoes. She pulled out her phone, scrolling through highlights of her previous season, stopping on a clip where she had scored the winning goal. The video looped endlessly, a cruel reminder of what she had lost, while the present moment remained stubbornly, painfully blank.
