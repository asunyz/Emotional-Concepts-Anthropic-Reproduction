Leo sat on the bench, lacing up shoes that didn’t fit his new position. His fingers fumbled with the aglets, tying knots that came undone immediately. A teammate patted his shoulder, offering a clumsy joke about catching balls instead of throwing them, but Leo only offered a faint, hollow smile that didn’t reach his eyes. He watched the field through the chain-link fence, the green grass blurring under the gray sky. Every time the whistle blew, he flinched, expecting the call for his old self, the one who commanded the line, not this stranger forced to chase shadows across the turf.
