Sarah wiped her glasses repeatedly, smearing the lenses despite knowing they were clean. The letter from the league office lay open on her desk, the words "Positional Reallocation" stark against the white background. Outside, the city hummed with indifferent life, cars rushing past without slowing for her stagnation. She picked up a football, turning it over in her hands, tracing the laces with her thumb. It felt foreign now, alien territory. She set it down gently, as if handling fragile glass, and turned off the lamp, plunging the room into darkness where she could finally stop pretending she understood what came next.
