Jenna couldn’t remember the last time she had smiled genuinely. The transition from striker to goalkeeper required a complete reversal of instinct, a surrender of control she found suffocating. During warm-ups, she hovered near the post, arms crossed tightly over her chest, protecting herself from the imagined impact. The crowd cheered for the forwards, their joy loud and vibrant, while she remained a silent statue amidst the chaos. When the coach called her name, she moved sluggishly, dragging her feet as if wading through deep water, each step requiring immense effort.
