David folded the uniform neatly, placing it in the bottom of his locker behind the winter gear he hadn’t worn in months. The smell of stale sweat and liniment used to comfort him, but now it made his stomach turn. He heard laughter drifting from the hallway, the easy camaraderie of players who still believed in the hierarchy he had been removed from. He closed the locker door softly, ensuring there was no sound, no disturbance. Walking away, he kept his head down, focusing on the scuff marks on the floor tiles, counting them to keep from looking back at the team photo taped to the wall.
