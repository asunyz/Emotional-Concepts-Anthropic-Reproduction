Lena stood at the edge of the pool, the cool air raising goosebumps on her arms. Being moved from butterfly to breaststroke felt like being stripped of her speed, reduced to a slow, grinding endurance test. She dove in, the water closing over her head, muffling the world outside. Underwater, she kicked hard, trying to outrun the thought that she was no longer the fastest. Surfacing, gasping for air, she saw the coach writing notes, his pen moving steadily. She treaded water, holding her breath until her lungs burned, wanting to stay submerged a little longer, hidden from the expectations waiting above the surface.
