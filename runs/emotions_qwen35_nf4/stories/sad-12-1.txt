I pulled my phone out for the tenth time in five minutes, refreshing the airline app until the loading spinner became a dizzying vortex. The screen cracked near the top corner, a jagged line cutting through the confirmation email that was now irrelevant. My chest felt tight, like a fist was slowly closing around my lungs, making every breath shallow and laborious. Outside the window, rain streaked down the pane, distorting the lights of the tarmac into weeping halos. I wanted to scream, to throw the device against the wall, but my hands only trembled harder. The wedding started in twenty minutes. I wasn't there to hold her hand during the vows, and the distance between here and there felt less like miles and more like an ocean I could never cross back.
