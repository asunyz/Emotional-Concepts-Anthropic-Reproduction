Sarah stared at the empty chair opposite her, the one reserved for her partner during the engagement dinner. The restaurant was full, the clinking of silverware and low hum of conversation filling the space with a joyous energy that made her feel sick. She had texted him repeatedly, but the messages remained unread, likely because he was already seated, waiting for someone who wasn’t coming. She pushed the menu away, her appetite vanished. The candlelight flickered, casting dancing shadows that looked like mocking figures. She forced a smile for the waiter who approached, a brittle mask slipping easily. Inside, she was crumbling, piece by piece, wondering how she would explain this failure to everyone who believed in their perfect future.
