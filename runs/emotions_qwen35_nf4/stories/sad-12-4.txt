David dropped his bag onto the floor with a dull thud, the sound swallowed by the thick carpet of the airport lounge. He buried his face in his hands, elbows digging into his knees. The graduation ceremony was happening right now. He could picture the cap and gown, the proud smiles, the diploma being handed over. He had promised his mother he’d be there. He had promised himself he wouldn’t miss this milestone after years of struggle. Now, the announcement about the mechanical failure echoed in his mind, a loop of bad news repeating endlessly. He felt hollow, a shell scraped clean of hope, staring blankly at the television screen showing weather reports for cities he would never reach today.
