The coffee in her cup had gone cold, forming a thin skin on the surface that she picked at with a fingernail. Clara hadn’t moved from her chair since the gate agent told her the news. The hospital bed waited for her, the last chance to say goodbye to her sister before the surgery. Every second that passed felt like a stone added to her stomach, weighing her down, anchoring her to this uncomfortable plastic seat. She traced the rim of the cup, her fingers numb and stiff. No one else seemed to notice the tragedy unfolding in real-time within the sterile brightness of the terminal. She just sat there, breathing in short, sharp gasps, trying to hold herself together while everything she loved slipped further away.
