He paced the length of the baggage claim area, his shoes squeaking rhythmically on the polished tile. The interview was for the job that would pay off his debts, the one thing standing between him and eviction. He had prepared his answers, ironed his shirt, and tied his tie perfectly. Yet, here he was, stranded because a snowstorm grounded all flights to Chicago. He stopped pacing and leaned against a pillar, sliding down until he hit the floor. The fluorescent lights buzzed overhead, harsh and unforgiving. He closed his eyes, visualizing the interviewer’s disappointed face, the rejection letter already written in his mind. The weight of the situation pressed against his eyelids, heavy enough to keep them shut forever if he let it.
