Lena held the ticket tightly in her palm, crumpling the paper edge until it tore. The concert was tonight. It was the reunion tour she had saved up for two years to attend. Friends she hadn’t seen in a decade would be there, singing the lyrics to songs that defined their youth. Now, the plane was diverted to a smaller airport with no ground transport available until morning. She stood frozen in the middle of the concourse, surrounded by bustling travelers rushing toward their destinations. The noise faded into a dull roar, isolating her in a bubble of stillness. She bit her lip hard enough to draw blood, tasting copper, trying to anchor herself in pain rather than the crushing disappointment blooming in her chest.
