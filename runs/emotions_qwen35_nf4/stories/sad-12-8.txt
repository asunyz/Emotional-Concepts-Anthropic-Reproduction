The doctor’s office was quiet, too quiet. Arthur sat on the examination table, swinging his legs slightly, though they barely cleared the floor. He had missed the appointment due to the delay, the rescheduled time pushing the diagnosis further into the unknown. Fear coiled in his throat, tightening with every beat of his heart. He stared at the anatomical chart on the wall, the colorful diagrams of organs mocking his helplessness. He wished he could turn back time, wish he had left earlier, wish he had driven instead of flying. But time only moved forward, relentless and cruel. He rested his forehead against his hands, shaking his head slowly, unable to process the reality that his health was now hanging in the balance of a calendar date he couldn’t control.
