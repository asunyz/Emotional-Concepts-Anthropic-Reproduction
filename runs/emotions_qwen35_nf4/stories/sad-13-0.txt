Elias stared at the red ink bleeding across his final paper, the color stark against the white page. His hands trembled slightly as he reached for his pen, hovering over the margin where Professor Halloway had written *Unoriginal*. The classroom was empty now, save for the hum of the fluorescent lights overhead that seemed louder than usual. He packed his bag slowly, deliberately avoiding eye contact with the door. When he finally stood up, his knees popped, a sharp reminder of how long he had been sitting still. He walked out without looking back, the weight of the accusation pressing down on his shoulders like a physical coat made of lead.
