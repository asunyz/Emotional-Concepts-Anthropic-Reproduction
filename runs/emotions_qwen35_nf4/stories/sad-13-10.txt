The cafeteria was too loud, a cacophony of laughter and clattering trays that grated against his nerves. Mark picked at his food, pushing peas around his plate without eating them. He felt visible, every glance from a peer feeling like an accusation. He stood up abruptly, chair screeching against the linoleum, drawing a few curious looks. Ignoring them, he grabbed his tray and fled, exiting through the back doors into the gray afternoon. The air outside was crisp, but it did nothing to clear the fog in his mind. He leaned against the brick exterior, closing his eyes and listening to the muffled sounds of life continuing without him.
