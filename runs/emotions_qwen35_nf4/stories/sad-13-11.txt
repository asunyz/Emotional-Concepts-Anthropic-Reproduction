I stared at the reflection in the mirror, searching for the person who had worked so hard, only to find a stranger staring back. The eyes were dull, lacking their usual spark. I brushed my teeth vigorously, the minty taste sharp and overwhelming, trying to wash away the metallic tang of fear. Getting dressed, I chose clothes that blended in, nothing bright, nothing noticeable. As I locked the front door, I hesitated, my hand lingering on the knob. Then, I stepped out, making sure to walk with purpose, even though every instinct screamed to turn back and hide.
