Maya dropped her head onto the desk, the cool surface doing little to soothe the heat rising in her cheeks. The email from the academic integrity board sat open on her laptop, its subject line bold and unforgiving. She traced the edge of her notebook with a fingernail, scraping the paper until it frayed. Around her, classmates laughed about weekend plans, their voices distant and muffled behind the glass wall she felt herself trapped behind. She didn’t answer when someone asked if she wanted coffee; she simply shook her head, a small, jerky motion, before pulling her hood up and disappearing into the crowd.
