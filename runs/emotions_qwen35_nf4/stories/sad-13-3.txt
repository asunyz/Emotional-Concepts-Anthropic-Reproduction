The hallway stretched endlessly before Julian, his footsteps echoing too loudly in the quiet corridor. He clutched his transcript tightly, knuckles turning white from the grip. Every step felt heavier than the last, dragging him toward the dean’s office. He rehearsed his explanation in his head, but the sentences tangled together, losing their meaning. When he reached the door, he paused, resting his forehead against the cold wood for a brief second. He took a deep breath that shuddered in his chest, then pushed inside, his eyes fixed firmly on the floor.
