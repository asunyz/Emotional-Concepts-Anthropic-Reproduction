She kept picking at the label on her water bottle, peeling it off in tiny, jagged strips. The meeting had ended ten minutes ago, but she remained seated in the corner of the library, surrounded by towering stacks of books that offered no comfort. Her gaze drifted to the clock on the wall, watching the seconds tick away with agonizing slowness. A tear escaped, tracking silently down her cheek, but she didn’t wipe it away. It wasn’t worth the effort. She just watched the droplet fall onto the table, spreading into a dark, irregular shape.
