David slammed his textbook shut, the sound startling the few other students nearby. He didn’t care. The injustice burned in his gut, hot and acidic. He shoved his notes into his backpack, zipping it up with aggressive yanks. Walking to class, he avoided the familiar faces, ducking into side alleys to escape recognition. His jaw was clenched so hard it ached. He couldn't believe they thought he’d steal another man’s work when he had spent three sleepless nights perfecting his own. Yet, the evidence was irrefutable. He stopped walking, leaning against a brick wall, sliding down until he hit the ground, burying his face in his hands.
