The letter arrived on a Tuesday, tucked between bills and flyers. Lena read it once, then again, the letters swimming before her eyes. She placed it carefully on the kitchen counter, as if handling something fragile and dangerous. Her mother asked what was wrong, but Lena only shook her head, forcing a smile that felt brittle and ready to shatter. She turned away to wash dishes, scrubbing a plate with unnecessary force, the soap suds slipping from her fingers. The noise of the running water filled the space between them, a barrier she needed more than anything else right now.
