He sat on the bench outside the administration building, watching leaves scatter across the pavement in the wind. The wind bit at his exposed neck, chilling him to the bone. He hadn’t eaten since breakfast, though hunger wasn’t the primary sensation dominating his thoughts. It was a hollow ache, a void where his confidence used to be. He pulled his scarf tighter, wrapping it around his nose and mouth, trying to create a private world within himself. Someone passed by, offering a sympathetic glance, but he looked straight ahead, focusing on a single crack in the concrete until the person moved on.
