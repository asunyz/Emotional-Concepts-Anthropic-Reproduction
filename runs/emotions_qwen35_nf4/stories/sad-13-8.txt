My phone buzzed incessantly on the desk, notifications piling up from group chats I no longer wished to join. I muted it, silencing the digital world that suddenly felt hostile. The sun dipped below the horizon, casting long, distorted shadows across my room. I curled into the corner of my bed, pulling my knees to my chest. The blanket smelled like laundry detergent, a mundane scent that usually brought comfort but now felt sterile and distant. I closed my eyes, hoping that if I stayed still enough, the reality of the situation would dissolve, leaving only the darkness.
