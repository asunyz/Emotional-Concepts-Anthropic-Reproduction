Sarah folded the contested essay one last time, smoothing out the creases with precise, mechanical movements. The professor’s comments were harsh, dissecting her logic with surgical precision. She slid the paper into an envelope, sealing it with a strip of tape. Her hand lingered on the seal for a moment, trembling imperceptibly. She walked to the mailbox, the metal box gleaming under the streetlamp. Dropping the letter in, she heard the soft *clink* of it hitting the bottom. She turned away quickly, unable to watch it disappear, and hurried home, keeping her head down against the biting wind.
