The voicemail was short. *Gone fishing. Don’t look for me.* That was all. No date, no return policy, just the abrupt cessation of contact after twenty years of daily check-ins. I replayed the message, the static crackling between his words, trying to find a hidden meaning in the brevity. Was it a test? A joke? Or just the end? I paced the length of my living room, the floorboards creaking under my restless feet. Every shadow seemed to hold the shape of his silhouette. I stopped pacing, gripping the back of a chair until my knuckles turned white. The silence of the house rushed in, filling every corner, demanding attention. I picked up my phone again, thumb hovering over his contact, but I didn’t press send. Some goodbyes are too heavy to carry.
