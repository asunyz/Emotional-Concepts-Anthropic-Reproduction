The email notification popped up on my screen, bright and cheerful against the dark mode interface. *Subject: Transition.* I clicked it, scanning the words quickly, looking for the part where he said he was staying, even if just for a little while. There was nothing. Just a formal announcement of retirement effective immediately. My cursor hovered over the reply button, blinking steadily, mocking my hesitation. I typed "Why?" then deleted it. Then "When will you come back?" also deleted. Finally, I closed the laptop lid with a sharp snap, the sound startling me in the empty apartment. I sat in the dimness, staring at the black reflection of my own face, wondering how the world kept spinning when one of its essential gears had simply vanished.
