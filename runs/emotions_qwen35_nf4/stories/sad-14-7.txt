He didn’t answer the door when I rang the bell. Not once. Not twice. I waited, shifting my weight from foot to foot, the cold seeping through the soles of my shoes. The neighbors’ lights were on, families gathered inside, laughing behind frosted glass windows. I pressed my forehead against the doorframe, closing my eyes, listening to the muffled sounds of life continuing elsewhere. My hands trembled slightly, a subtle vibration traveling up my arms. I pulled my scarf tighter, wrapping it around my neck until it choked off the air, a self-imposed restraint to keep from breaking down right there on the doorstep. Eventually, I turned and walked away, the crunch of gravel under my boots sounding like footsteps retreating from a war zone.
