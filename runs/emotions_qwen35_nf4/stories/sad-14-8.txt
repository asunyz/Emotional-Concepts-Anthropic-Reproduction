The project files were organized perfectly, color-coded and labeled with meticulous care. It was the kind of order that screamed control, yet the creator was nowhere to be found. I flipped through the pages, finding marginalia in his distinct, jagged script—notes that weren’t instructions, but questions he hadn’t finished asking. Who is solving this? What happens next? I traced the ink with my finger, smudging the edge of the page. A lump formed in my throat, hard and painful. I looked around the conference room, the large table reflecting my isolated figure. I wanted to call him, to ask him to finish the thought, but the number I dialed disconnected instantly. I placed the folder back on the table, aligning it precisely with the edge, and sat alone in the humming silence.
