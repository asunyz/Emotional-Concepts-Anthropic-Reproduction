Kevin sat on the floor of his closet, surrounded by boxes of old projects and rejected drafts. He held one resume in each hand, comparing them, searching for the flaw, the mistake, the reason why he wasn’t chosen. His vision swam, blurring the text until it became meaningless gibberish. He squeezed his eyes shut, trying to block out the image of Marcus shaking the hiring manager’s hand, a gesture of victory that Kevin felt in his own bones. A sob escaped him, raw and unfiltered, tearing through the silence. He covered his mouth with his hand, stifling the sound, but the tremors running through his body betrayed him. He stayed there in the dark, surrounded by the artifacts of his efforts, feeling utterly erased.
