The cursor blinked on the screen, a steady, rhythmic pulse that felt like a countdown. Sarah’s reflection in the dark monitor looked distant, her shoulders slumped forward in a posture of defeat. She typed a sentence, deleted it, and typed another, her movements jerky and uncoordinated. Her stomach churned, a nauseating flip-flop sensation that made her want to lie down and close her eyes forever. She thought about the interview questions they had practiced together, the inside jokes, the mutual support—now rendered obsolete by a single hiring decision. A tear slipped out, tracking a hot path down her cheek, but she didn’t wipe it away. She just kept typing, hoping that words could somehow rewrite the outcome, even as the screen remained stubbornly blank.
