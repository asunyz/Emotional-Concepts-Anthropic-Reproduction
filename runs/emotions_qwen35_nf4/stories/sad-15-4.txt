David stood in the shower, letting the water run scalding hot, seeking a pain sharp enough to distract him from the ache in his mind. Steam filled the small bathroom, fogging up the mirror where his face appeared ghost-like and pale. He gripped the edge of the sink, his fingernails digging into the porcelain until the skin turned white around the cuticles. The phone on the counter buzzed once—a notification from LinkedIn—and he jumped, his heart leaping into his throat before sinking back down with a thud. He knew what it meant. Another opportunity closed, another door slammed shut while Marcus walked right through. He turned off the water, leaving the droplets hanging in the air, suspended in the sudden quiet, much like his career prospects currently hung in limbo.
