"Did you hear?" Maya asked, her voice barely audible over the hum of the refrigerator. She leaned against the kitchen counter, her arms crossed tightly over her chest as if trying to hold herself together. Her friend nodded slowly, avoiding eye contact, which only intensified the heat rising in Maya’s neck. She pushed off the counter and walked to the window, pressing her forehead against the cool glass. The view of the street below was mundane, ordinary, yet it felt alien and hostile. She replayed the moment the manager said "we’ve decided to go with another candidate," hearing the polite, rehearsed tone that masked the finality of the rejection. She bit the inside of her cheek until she tasted copper, forcing a smile onto her face that felt stretched and brittle, ready to crack at any moment.
