Tom threw his laptop onto the bed, the impact muffled by the duvet, but the sound was enough to make him wince. He buried his face in his hands, rubbing vigorously at his temples as if trying to massage away the throbbing pressure behind his eyes. The ceiling fan rotated lazily above him, casting shifting shadows that danced like mocking spirits. He thought about the late nights spent refining his portfolio, the sacrifices made, the weekends given up—all for nothing. A lump formed in his throat, hard and painful, making it difficult to swallow. He rolled onto his side, curling into a fetal position, pulling the blanket up to his chin. The room felt smaller than usual, the walls closing in, suffocating him with the weight of his own inadequacy.
