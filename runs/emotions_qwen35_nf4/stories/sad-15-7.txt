Jessica scrolled through her phone, stopping at a photo of her and Alex from last month’s team outing. They were smiling, arms around each other’s shoulders, looking invincible. Now, the distance between them felt like an ocean. She locked the screen and set the phone face down on the nightstand, as if hiding it might hide the truth. Her breathing was shallow, rapid gasps that did little to fill her lungs. She got up and began cleaning, frantically wiping down surfaces that were already spotless, needing motion to outrun the stagnation of her thoughts. The clock ticked loudly in the quiet house, each second stretching into an eternity. She stopped mid-wipe, rag in hand, staring at the dust on the shelf, realizing she had been standing there for ten minutes without moving a muscle.
