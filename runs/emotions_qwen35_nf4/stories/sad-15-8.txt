Ryan sat in the waiting area of the café, nursing a cup of tea that had long since lost its warmth. He watched the other patrons laugh and talk, their conversations flowing effortlessly, while his own mind felt like a tangled knot of wires. He pulled his coat tighter around himself, though the weather was mild, seeking a barrier against the world. He imagined calling Alex, asking for advice, but hesitated, fearing the pity in his friend’s voice more than the rejection itself. The barista called out a name that sounded similar to his, and Ryan’s head snapped up, hope flaring briefly before being extinguished when he realized it wasn’t him. He let out a sigh that seemed to come from the bottom of his soul, his shoulders dropping further, deflating completely.
