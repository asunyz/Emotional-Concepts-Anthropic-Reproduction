I saw her standing near the floral arches, wearing the shade of blue she used to say matched her eyes. My breath hitched, catching in my throat like a fishbone. I forced my feet to remain planted on the dance floor, watching her twirl with someone else. The room spun slightly, not from the alcohol, but from the sudden weight pressing down on my shoulders. I adjusted my cufflinks, a nervous tic I hadn’t broken in years, and stared intently at a stain on the carpet until the moment passed.
