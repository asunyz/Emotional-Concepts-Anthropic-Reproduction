She froze when she recognized the man at the head table. Her spoon hovered mid-air, suspended above her soup. The steam rising from the bowl fogged her glasses, obscuring her vision. She removed them, rubbing the bridge of her nose aggressively, trying to wipe away the image of his smile. When she put them back on, he was looking directly at her. She quickly lowered her head, focusing intently on the rim of her plate, willing herself to disappear into the wood grain.
