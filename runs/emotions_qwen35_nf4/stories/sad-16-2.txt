Marcus didn’t look up when David walked in. He kept his gaze fixed on the invitation card, tracing the embossed letters with a trembling finger. The noise of the reception faded into a dull hum, replaced by the sharp ring of memory. When David’s shadow fell across his table, Marcus’s hand spasmed, knocking over his water glass. Ice cubes scattered across the linen like shattered teeth. He didn’t move to clean it up, just sat there, staring at the spreading puddle as if it were an ocean he couldn’t swim across.
