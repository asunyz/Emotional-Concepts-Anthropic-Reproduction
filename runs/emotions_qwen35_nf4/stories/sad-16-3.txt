She laughed, a bright, brittle sound that cut through the chatter. Leo wanted to cover his ears, but he remained still, rooted to the spot. The warmth of the hall felt suffocating now, the air thick with humidity and old regrets. He watched her adjust her hair, a gesture so familiar it made his stomach turn. With a deliberate slowness, he loosened his tie, pulling the fabric tight around his neck until it became a reminder of how hard it was to breathe without her.
