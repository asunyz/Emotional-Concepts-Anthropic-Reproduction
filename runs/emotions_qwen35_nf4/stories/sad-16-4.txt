Javier slipped out of the banquet hall, the heavy doors clicking shut behind him with finality. The night air was crisp, biting at his exposed cheeks. He leaned against the brick wall, sliding down until he hit the ground. Inside, the muffled thump of bass vibrated through the stone beneath him. He pulled his knees to his chest, wrapping his arms tightly around himself, trying to hold together the pieces that had been scattered the moment he saw her smiling face through the window.
