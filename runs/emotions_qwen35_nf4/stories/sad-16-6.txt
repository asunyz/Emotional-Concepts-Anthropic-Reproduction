He checked his watch for the tenth time in five minutes. The second hand ticked forward, indifferent to the chaos in his mind. Seeing Elena at the altar, radiant and happy, felt like swallowing a stone. It sat heavy in his gut, making it difficult to swallow his dinner later. He pushed his plate away, claiming he wasn't hungry, though his hands shook too much to lift the fork again. Every clink of silverware sounded like a gavel striking a verdict.
