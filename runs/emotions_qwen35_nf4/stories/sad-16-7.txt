Rachel hid in the restroom, splashing cold water onto her face repeatedly. The mirror showed a stranger—pale, wide-eyed, lips pressed into a thin line. Through the door, she heard his voice, deep and steady, telling a joke that made others roar. She gripped the edge of the sink, her nails scraping against the porcelain. She waited until the footsteps stopped, until the laughter died down, before she dared to open the door and step back into the light.
