Tom avoided the dance floor, choosing instead to linger by the bar. He ordered another whiskey, neat, and let the burn distract him momentarily. Then he saw her walking past, her dress flowing like water. His glass slipped from his fingers, shattering on the hardwood. The bartender rushed over, but Tom only looked at the shards, unable to meet anyone’s eyes. He felt small, diminished, as if the joy radiating from the center of the room was draining the color from his world.
