She watched Julian pack his briefcase, the leather creaking under his grip. He had spoken all morning about client meetings and board presentations, yet his suit was wrinkled and his tie was askew. Clara felt a hollow ache expand behind her sternum. She picked up a book from the side table, opening it to a random page, but the words swam before her eyes. "Ready?" Julian asked, turning toward her with a confident grin. Clara closed the book slowly, placing it precisely parallel to the edge of the table. She stood up, smoothing her skirt with stiff movements. "Almost," she murmured, her voice barely above a whisper. As he left, she remained standing there, staring at the empty space where he had been, waiting for the silence to become unbearable.
