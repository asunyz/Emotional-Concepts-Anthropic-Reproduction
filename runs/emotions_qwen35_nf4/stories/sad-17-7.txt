Lena scrolled through the photos on her tablet, zooming in on the background of her friend’s vacation posts. There, reflected in a shop window, was the logo of the hospital where Lena worked as a janitor. Her heart hammered against her ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. She dropped the tablet onto the couch, the screen shattering with a sickening crack. She didn’t move to pick it up. Instead, she sank into the cushions, pulling her knees to her chest. The room spun slightly, the colors bleeding together. She whispered a name into the empty air, the syllables tasting bitter. Tears pricked at the corners of her eyes, but she blinked them away furiously, refusing to let them fall. She stared at the broken glass, tracing the jagged lines with her gaze until her vision blurred.
