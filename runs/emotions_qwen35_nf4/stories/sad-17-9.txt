Emma stared at the email on her screen, the subject line glaring in bold text. Her colleague had forwarded it, revealing the truth about the project lead’s credentials. She clicked away from the browser, minimizing the window with aggressive speed. Her hands hovered over the keyboard, shaking. She got up abruptly, knocking her chair backward. It hit the wall with a dull thud. She walked to the breakroom, pouring a cup of coffee she didn’t want. The liquid overflowed, burning her wrist, but she didn’t flinch. She stared at the brown pool spreading across the desk, her expression blank. Outside the window, the sky was a bruised purple, threatening rain. She drank the cold, stale coffee in one gulp, grimacing at the taste, wishing she could swallow the bitterness along with it.
