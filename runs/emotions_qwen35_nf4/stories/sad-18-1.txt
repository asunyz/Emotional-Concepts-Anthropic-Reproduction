I sat at the kitchen table, tracing the rim of my cold coffee cup with a trembling finger. The mug slipped slightly from my grip, clinking against the saucer, but I didn’t move to steady it. From the hallway, I heard the muffled murmur of her voice, low and urgent, slipping through the crack in the door before she hung up. My chest tightened, a hollow ache spreading beneath my ribs. I looked around the empty apartment, noticing the dust motes dancing in the afternoon light, indifferent to the fracture forming in our foundation. She walked in, smiling brightly, asking about dinner plans. I forced the corners of my mouth upward, nodding slowly, while inside, something brittle cracked further.
