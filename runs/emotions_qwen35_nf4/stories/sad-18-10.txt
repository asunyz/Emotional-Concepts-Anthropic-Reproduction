The phone rang again, cutting through the movie they were watching. He saw her freeze, her hand hovering over the remote. "It’s probably spam," she said, her voice tight, lacking its usual melodic lilt. She answered it anyway, turning away, shielding the conversation with her body. He paused the film, the sudden silence deafening. He stared at the frozen frame on the TV, a snapshot of happiness that now looked staged and artificial. His fingers dug into the armrests of the couch, the fabric bunching up under his grip. He felt a profound sense of isolation, sitting right next to someone who was physically present but emotionally miles away. When she hung up, she laughed, a bright, practiced sound that didn’t reach her eyes. He nodded, saying nothing, letting the silence stretch until it became unbearable.
