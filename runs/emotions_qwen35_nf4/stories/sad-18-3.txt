The rain tapped a rhythmic code against the windowpane, matching the frantic beating of Clara’s heart. She held the phone in both hands, the screen reflecting her pale face. Three missed calls from an unknown number. Then another text came through, brief and cryptic. Her throat constricted, making it hard to swallow. She wanted to scream, to throw the device into the wall, but instead, she set it down gently on the coaster, aligning it perfectly with the edge of the table. It was a small act of control in a world spinning out of order. Outside, the gray sky pressed down on the city, mirroring the weight settling onto her shoulders. She wrapped her arms around herself, rocking slightly, trying to generate enough heat to keep the chill at bay.
