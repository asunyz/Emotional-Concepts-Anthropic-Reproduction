David stood by the car, keys jingling in his hand, staring at the driveway. His wife was inside, laughing at something on her phone, a sound that used to make him smile now made his stomach turn. He remembered the secret call yesterday, the one she took in the garden shed, whispering promises he wasn’t part of. The engine started, roaring to life, but it did nothing to drown out the echo of those whispers. He gripped the steering wheel until his knuckles turned white, the leather creaking under the pressure. He didn’t want to go to work; he wanted to sit here until the sun went down and the shadows swallowed the truth whole. With a sigh that seemed to come from his toes, he put the car in gear and drove away, leaving the laughter behind.
