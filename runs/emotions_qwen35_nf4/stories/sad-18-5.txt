She scrolled through the photos on her tablet, stopping on a picture of them from two years ago. They looked so happy then, unburdened by the secrets that now cluttered the air between them. The notification banner dropped down: *Incoming Call - Unknown.* Her breath hitched. She swiped it away, but the damage was done. The image on the screen blurred as tears welled up, distorting the colors. She wiped them away aggressively with the back of her hand, refusing to let them fall. The house was quiet, save for the ticking clock on the wall, counting seconds that felt heavier than usual. She placed the tablet face down, hiding the evidence, and walked to the kitchen to pour a glass of water, her hands shaking so badly the liquid rippled over the rim.
