Leo paced the length of the living room, the rug fraying under his restless feet. Every time his partner’s phone buzzed, his head snapped toward it, a Pavlovian response to the betrayal he suspected was brewing. Tonight, however, there was no buzz, only the soft glow of the screen illuminating her face as she typed furiously. He stopped pacing, leaning against the doorframe, arms crossed tightly over his chest. The posture was defensive, protective, shutting out the world. He watched her thumb hover over the send button, knowing that once it clicked, the reality they shared would shift irrevocably. He felt a profound exhaustion settle into his bones, heavy and leaden, making every step forward feel like wading through mud.
