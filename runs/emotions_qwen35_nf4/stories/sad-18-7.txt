I found the receipt in her coat pocket, crumpled and stained with coffee. It listed a hotel bar, late at night, a time when we were supposed to be asleep together. I folded it neatly, placing it on the dresser. The action was methodical, precise, masking the chaos raging inside me. I looked at her sleeping form, the rise and fall of her chest steady and calm. How could she sleep so soundly? I reached out, touching her shoulder lightly, then pulled back as if burned. The intimacy felt foreign now, tainted by the knowledge of what happened while I trusted her. I turned off the lamp, plunging us into darkness, and lay awake, listening to the hum of the refrigerator, the only thing in the house still functioning normally.
