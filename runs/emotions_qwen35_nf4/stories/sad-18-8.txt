Sarah watched her boyfriend speak into the receiver, his voice dropping to a conspiratorial whisper. She pretended to read her book, but the words swam before her eyes, meaningless shapes on the page. The pen in her hand snapped, ink staining her fingers black. She didn’t notice. She just kept reading, her gaze fixed on the same paragraph for ten minutes straight. When he hung up and smiled at her, she returned the gesture, though it felt like pulling teeth. The air in the café grew thick, suffocating. She pushed her chair back, the legs scraping loudly against the floor, drawing stares from other patrons. She needed to breathe, to escape the suffocating weight of the lie hanging between them, even if it meant walking out alone into the cold evening.
