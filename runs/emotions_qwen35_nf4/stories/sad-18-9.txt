He deleted the call log, wiping it clean as if erasing history itself. But the ghost of the conversation remained, echoing in the hollows of his mind. He sat on the edge of the bed, head in his hands, elbows resting on his knees. The room smelled of lavender detergent, a scent that used to signify home and safety. Now, it smelled like deception. He thought about confronting her, shouting until his voice gave out, but the energy required felt insurmountable. Instead, he picked up his phone, opened the camera roll, and looked at old pictures of their dog, the only creature whose loyalty hadn’t been questioned. A single tear escaped, tracking through the stubble on his cheek, falling onto his jeans where it soaked in unnoticed.
