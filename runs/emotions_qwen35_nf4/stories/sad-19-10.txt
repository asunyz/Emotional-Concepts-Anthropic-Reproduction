Tom stood in the driveway, engine idling, watching his daughter walk toward the school bus. He held the letter from the principal in his lap, the paper wrinkled from his grip. The teacher assigned to her special needs program was Ms. Davis. Tom remembered Ms. Davis as the nurse who had refused to help him when his ex-wife fell ill, citing policy over compassion. The coldness of that decision had haunted him ever since. He felt a surge of resentment boil in his gut, hot and bitter. He wanted to turn the car around, to go back inside and hide his daughter from the system that had failed them both. But he couldn’t move. He just sat there, gripping the steering wheel, watching the bus pull away, feeling utterly helpless and alone in the vast parking lot.
