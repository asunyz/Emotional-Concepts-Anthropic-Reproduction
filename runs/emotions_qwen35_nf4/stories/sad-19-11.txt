The silence in the apartment was deafening. Clara sat at the dining table, surrounded by scattered papers. Her son had brought home a drawing from his music class, titled "My Teacher, Mr. Reed." Clara picked up the crayon scribbles, her hands trembling. Mr. Reed had been the conductor who had dismissed her orchestra audition with a single, dismissive wave of his baton. That rejection had ended her dream of performing professionally. Looking at the crude but earnest depiction of Mr. Reed, Clara felt a profound sense of loss. Tears pricked at the corners of her eyes, but she blinked them away fiercely. She placed the drawing gently on the table, smoothing it out with a trembling finger. She wanted to protect her son from the harsh realities of the industry, from the people who could break your spirit with a glance, but she knew she couldn’t control everything. She sat there, frozen, the taste of defeat lingering on her tongue.
