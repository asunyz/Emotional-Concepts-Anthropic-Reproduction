Marcus dropped his briefcase onto the floor, the thud echoing in the hallway. He had spent the entire day at work trying to focus, but every email subject line reminded him of the awkward conversation he’d had with his daughter earlier that evening. She had mentioned her art teacher, Mr. Vance, with such enthusiasm. Marcus sank onto the sofa, burying his face in his hands. He remembered Mr. Vance not for kindness, but for the harsh critique that had shattered his confidence in high school, a moment that had steered him away from creative pursuits entirely. Now, that same man was shaping his daughter’s vision of the world. Marcus felt a surge of protective anger mixed with deep regret, his shoulders hunched forward as if bearing a physical weight. He pulled his knees to his chest, wrapping his arms around himself, wishing he could shield her from the disappointment he knew was coming.
