The coffee in Elena’s mug had gone cold, a film forming on the surface that she ignored. She scrolled through the class roster on her laptop, her cursor hovering over the name "Mr. Kael" next to her son’s entry. She closed the laptop abruptly, the screen going black, reflecting her own pale face back at her. She stood up and walked to the window, pressing her palm against the glass. The cold seeped into her skin, grounding her slightly. Mr. Kael had been the judge in her custody hearing five years ago, the one who had ruled against her based on incomplete information. She hadn’t cried then; she had simply gone numb. Now, facing the prospect of him influencing her daughter’s future, that numbness spread through her limbs, making her feel heavy and detached. She took a sip of the stale coffee, wincing at the bitterness, and decided to call the school immediately, her heart pounding a frantic rhythm against her ribs.
