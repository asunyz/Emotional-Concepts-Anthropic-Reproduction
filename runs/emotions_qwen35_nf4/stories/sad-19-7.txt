Anna stared at the ceiling fan spinning lazily above her bed, counting the rotations. One hundred and twelve. One hundred and thirteen. Her phone lit up with a notification from the school app: "Welcome to your new homeroom teacher, Mr. Lewis." Anna let out a short, humorless laugh that turned into a cough. Mr. Lewis had been the boss who had fired her without warning, leaving her unemployed for six months. She rolled over, pulling the duvet up to her chin, shivering despite the warm room. The injustice of it all prickled under her skin, a hot, angry sensation that kept her awake. She thought about calling the school, demanding a change, but the energy required felt insurmountable. Instead, she closed her eyes and focused on her breathing, inhaling deeply, exhaling slowly, trying to calm the storm raging inside her chest.
