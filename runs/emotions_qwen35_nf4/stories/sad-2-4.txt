He shoved the sketchbook across the counter, the action abrupt and violent enough to make the sugar bowl rattle. "What is this?" he demanded, though his voice lacked any real heat, sounding more like a whisper than a shout. The woman across from him, his daughter, tilted her head, confused by his reaction. The drawing lay between them: a detailed rendering of a shadowy entity with antlers, perched on a fence post. Thomas ran a hand through his hair, gripping the strands tight enough to pull at his scalp. He turned away, walking to the sink and turning on the faucet, letting the water run cold over his wrists. He watched the swirl of soap suds disappear down the drain, unable to meet anyone’s eye. The familiar dread coiled in his stomach, a cold snake tightening its grip. He gripped the porcelain edge of the sink, his arms shaking visibly, until the noise of the water drowned out the sound of his own rapid heartbeat.
