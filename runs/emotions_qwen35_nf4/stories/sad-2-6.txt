The envelope slid across the desk, landing with a soft thud near David’s elbow. He didn’t touch it immediately. He just stared at the flap, which bore a smudge of red wax. Inside was a photocopy of a child’s drawing, sent anonymously. It showed a boy sitting alone on a swing set, surrounded by a circle of dark, toothy figures. David picked up his pen, intending to write a note, but the tip hovered over the paper, blotting the ink. He capped the pen with excessive force, snapping the cap off and throwing it into the trash bin. He leaned back in his chair, the leather creaking under his shifting weight. He closed his eyes, tilting his head back to stare at the ceiling tiles, counting the perforations. One, two, three. The numbers blurred together. A cold sweat prickled along his hairline. He opened his eyes, staring blankly at the wall, his jaw clenched so tight a muscle twitched rhythmically beneath his ear.
