The coffee shop was crowded, loud, and overwhelming. Sarah hid behind her newspaper, though she hadn’t read a word in twenty minutes. Her hands shook as she reached for her cup, spilling a drop onto her knuckles which she wiped absently with a napkin. She felt invisible, a ghost haunting her own life. The bell above the door jingled, cutting through the noise. She peeked over the paper to see a teenager she vaguely recognized from years ago, now wearing a college hoodie. The kid approached her table, sliding into the seat opposite her without asking. "Ms. Vance?" the teen asked. Before she could respond, the kid slid a thick envelope across the table. "I got into the program you recommended. The one you said I’d never make it into." The kid grinned, bright and sudden, and walked away before she could say anything. Sarah stared at the envelope, her breath catching in her throat, the noise of the café fading into a dull roar.
