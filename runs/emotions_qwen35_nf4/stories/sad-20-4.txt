Rain lashed against the windshield of Arthur’s car as he sat parked outside the school gates, engine off, heater blasting. He watched the other teachers leave, their laughter carrying on the wind, a sound that felt foreign and sharp. He checked his watch, then his phone, then the sky. Nothing changed. The door opened, and Julian stepped out, shaking off an umbrella. He paused when he saw Arthur, squinting through the glass. Julian tapped on the window. Arthur rolled it down slowly, the cold air rushing in. Julian leaned in, holding out a small, clay figurine—a crude but recognizable statue of Arthur’s favorite book character. "I made this during detention," Julian mumbled, refusing to meet his eyes. "You said art helps when words fail. So... here." Arthur took the cold, wet clay, the imperfections of the shape pressing into his palm, anchoring him to the present moment.
