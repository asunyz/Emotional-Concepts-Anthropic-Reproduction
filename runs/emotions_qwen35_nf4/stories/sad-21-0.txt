Elias stared at the envelope resting on his kitchen table, the paper thick and cream-colored against the chipped laminate. His fingers trembled slightly as he reached for the letter opener, the metal cold against his skin. He had spent the last three weeks eating cold soup from cans because cooking felt like too much effort, standing by the window watching rain streak down the glass until his reflection blurred into the gray outside. When he finally slit the seal, the scent of old tobacco and lavender drifted out, a smell that used to mean safety before it meant loss. He read the words slowly, tracing the loops of handwriting that hadn’t changed in twenty years, while a heavy weight settled in his chest, pressing the air from his lungs.
