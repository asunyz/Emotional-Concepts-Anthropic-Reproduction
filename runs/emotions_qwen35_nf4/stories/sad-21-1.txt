The mailbox clattered shut behind her, leaving her alone on the porch steps where she sat with her knees pulled to her chin. She unfolded the note with deliberate slowness, treating it like a fragile bird’s wing. Inside, Julian apologized for the night he left without saying goodbye, explaining that fear had been the driver, not indifference. She traced the ink smudge near the signature, remembering how his hands used to shake when he lied. A tear tracked hot and fast down her cheek, but she didn’t wipe it away; instead, she let it fall onto the paper, darkening the white space around the word *sorry*. She folded the letter back up, placed it in her pocket, and walked inside, closing the door softly behind her.
