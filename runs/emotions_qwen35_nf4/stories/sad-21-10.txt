Leo threw the letter onto the passenger seat of his car, slamming the door shut. The engine roared to life, drowning out the sound of his ragged breathing. The note from his father was simple: *I’m sorry I wasn’t there.* That was it. No explanation, no justification. Leo gripped the steering wheel until his tendons stood out like cords. He drove aimlessly through the streets, the neon signs reflecting in his teary eyes, distorted and smeared. He pulled over at a red light, resting his head against the horn, letting the honk blare continuously until the cars behind him began to beep in frustration. He lifted his head, wiped his eyes roughly with his sleeve, and continued driving, faster now, chasing the horizon.
