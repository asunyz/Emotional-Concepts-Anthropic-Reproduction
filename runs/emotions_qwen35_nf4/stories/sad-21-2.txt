Marcus held the letter between two fingers as if it were contaminated. The office hummed with the low drone of computers and distant phone calls, a stark contrast to the silence screaming in his head. He had received the mail during lunch hour, slipping the envelope into his briefcase under the guise of organizing files. Now, hidden in the supply closet, he opened it again. Sarah wrote about regret, about realizing too late that her ambition had cost them everything. He reread the paragraph about their anniversary dinner, the one she canceled for a merger meeting. His throat tightened, making it hard to swallow. He closed his eyes, leaning his forehead against the cool metal shelf, listening to the muffled sounds of the world continuing without him.
