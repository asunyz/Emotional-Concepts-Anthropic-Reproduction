The wind whipped across the dock, tugging at the edges of the parchment in David’s grip. He stood barefoot on the weathered wood, the planks rough beneath his toes, staring out at the choppy water. The letter from his brother was short, barely half a page, asking for forgiveness regarding the inheritance dispute five years ago. David crumpled the top corner instinctively, then smoothed it out again, unable to destroy it. He thought of the laughter they used to share while fishing here, now replaced by the hollow ache in his stomach. He shoved the letter deep into his jacket pocket, zipped it up tight, and turned back toward the house, shoulders hunched against the chill that seemed to seep right through his bones.
