I picked up the card from the pile of unpaid bills, my thumb brushing over the embossed border. It wasn’t a holiday greeting; it was from Elena, the friend I hadn’t spoken to since the argument over the borrowed money. She wrote that she missed me, that she wished she had listened better. I sank onto the edge of the bed, the mattress dipping under my weight. The room was dim, the curtains drawn against the afternoon sun. I stared at the ceiling fan spinning lazily above, counting the rotations, trying to find a spark of warmth in the memory of our friendship, but all I found was a cold, flat numbness spreading through my limbs. I slid off the bed and sat on the floor, back against the wall, holding the card until the corners bent under my grip.
