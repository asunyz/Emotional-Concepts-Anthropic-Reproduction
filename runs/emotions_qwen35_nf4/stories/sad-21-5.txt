Clara dropped the pen, watching it roll across the desk and come to a stop near the edge. The letter lay open, the ink still seeming wet despite being sent months ago. Her mentor, Mr. Henderson, was apologizing for the harsh critique that had derailed her career path a decade prior. She looked at her own hands, calloused and stained with paint, hands that had stopped creating beautiful things long ago. She got up and walked to the sink, turning the tap on full blast, letting the cold water run over her wrists until they burned. She dried them slowly, methodically, avoiding the mirror above the sink, afraid that if she looked, she would see the resignation etched into every line of her face.
