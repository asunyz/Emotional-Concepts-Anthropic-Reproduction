He tucked the letter into his coat pocket, right next to his heart, though he knew it served no purpose there. The walk home was longer than usual, each step heavy, dragging against the pavement. The note from his daughter was filled with excuses he couldn’t accept—work, stress, distance—but underneath, he heard the plea for connection. He passed the park where they used to fly kites, the string snapping in the wind just like this relationship. He stopped at the bench, sitting down heavily, and rested his head in his hands. The city noise faded into a dull roar, and he remained there, motionless, as the streetlights flickered on one by one, marking the end of another day he couldn’t bear to finish.
