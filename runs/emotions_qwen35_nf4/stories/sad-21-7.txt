The envelope was slipped under the door of the apartment, sliding across the hardwood floor to rest against her shoe. She kicked it gently, then bent down to retrieve it. It was from her ex-husband, written in careful, precise script. He admitted fault in the divorce proceedings, acknowledging that his pride had built walls neither could scale. She read it standing up, her expression unreadable, but her knuckles turned white as she gripped the paper. She walked to the trash can, hovered over it, then pulled back. She carried the letter to the drawer where she kept old tickets and photos, placing it at the very bottom, unseen but present. She closed the drawer and locked it, the click echoing loudly in the empty room.
