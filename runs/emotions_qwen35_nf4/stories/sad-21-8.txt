Arthur sat on the hospital bed, the stiff sheets rustling as he shifted. The nurse had brought the mail, stacking it neatly beside the IV pole. At the top was a letter from his son, who hadn’t visited in six months. The boy wrote about growing up, about understanding now why Arthur had worked so hard. Arthur unfolded the paper, his arthritic joints protesting the movement. He read the words twice, then a third time, searching for truth in the lines. Tears welled up, blurring the text into a haze of black and white. He blinked rapidly, trying to clear his vision, but the moisture persisted. He lowered the letter to his lap, staring at the wall, waiting for a strength that refused to return.
