She tore the envelope open with her teeth, spitting the shredded paper into the sink. The letter from her former business partner was formal, legalistic, devoid of genuine remorse. It offered a settlement, nothing more. She leaned forward, resting her elbows on the counter, her forehead touching the cold porcelain. The kitchen was silent, save for the ticking clock on the stove. She thought of the years lost to litigation, the sleepless nights, the friends who drifted away. A sob caught in her throat, sharp and sudden, forcing her to gasp for air. She covered her mouth with both hands, shaking violently, while the water in the glass next to her rippled with the force of her distress.
