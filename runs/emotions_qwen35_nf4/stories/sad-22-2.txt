The rain lashed against the windowpane, blurring the city skyline into gray smears. Lena sat slumped in her chair, the glow of her monitor illuminating the dark circles under her eyes. Her cursor blinked rhythmically on the blank document, mocking her inability to produce anything meaningful. David leaned against the doorframe, holding two steaming mugs. "Decaf," he said gently, placing one near her elbow. She didn’t look up, just wrapped her fingers around the warm ceramic, seeking comfort in the heat. "Do you remember Camp Whispering Pines?" he asked casually, leaning against the wall. Lena’s shoulders tensed, then slowly relaxed. "I used to run the archery range," she murmured, her voice barely audible over the storm outside. "I haven't thought about that place in twenty years." She took a slow sip, the warmth spreading through her chest, momentarily pushing back the cold numbness that had taken root in her stomach.
