The elevator doors slid open with a ding that sounded like a gavel striking a block. Thomas stepped out, adjusting his tie which felt tighter than usual. He avoided eye contact with anyone in the lobby, focusing intently on the scuff marks on his shoes. At his desk, he opened a drawer and pulled out a worn paperback book titled *The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn*. He ran his fingers over the frayed cover, tracing the embossed title. Jessica paused by his partition, holding a stack of files. "Read anything good lately?" she asked. Thomas hesitated, the book clutched to his chest like a shield. "It’s old," he said quietly. "We read it at Camp Riverbend. Every year, we’d reenact the raft scenes on the creek behind the cabins." Jessica smiled warmly. "That sounds peaceful." Thomas forced a nod, but his gaze drifted to the gray sky visible through the high windows. Peaceful was a word for people who hadn’t lost everything. He closed the book gently, tucking it back into the drawer, hiding the past once more.
