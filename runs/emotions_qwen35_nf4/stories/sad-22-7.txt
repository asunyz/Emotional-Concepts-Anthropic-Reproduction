I watched the snow accumulate against the glass, isolating the building from the rest of the world. The silence in the office was deafening, amplified by the hum of servers in the basement. Ben knocked lightly on my cubicle wall. "Coffee run," he announced, holding up a pot. "Want one?" I shook my head, pulling my cardigan tighter around myself. "Not really." Ben lingered, sensing the tension radiating off me. "You seem distant today." I looked at him, seeing the genuine worry etched into the corners of his mouth. "I met someone from Camp Birchwood yesterday," I confessed, my voice trembling slightly. "Turns out, he’s your cousin." Ben’s face lit up with surprise. "Really? That’s crazy!" He laughed, a bright, clear sound that clashed with the gloom in my gut. I managed a thin smile, but my hands remained clenched in my lap. The coincidence felt less like a miracle and more like a reminder of how small the world was, and how far away those carefree summers felt now.
