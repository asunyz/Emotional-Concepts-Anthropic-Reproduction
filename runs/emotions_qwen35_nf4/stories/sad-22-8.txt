Liam stared at the spreadsheet, the numbers blurring into meaningless shapes. His stomach churned, a knot of anxiety tightening with every passing minute. He needed to leave, to escape the suffocating atmosphere of the corporate floor. As he gathered his things, he noticed a drawing pinned to the community board—a child’s sketch of a treehouse. "Nice art," Liam commented, pointing to it. His colleague, Nina, glanced over. "Oh, that’s mine. From when I volunteered at Camp Meadowbrook. I teach art classes there on weekends." Liam felt a pang of longing so sharp it stole his breath. "Meadowbrook?" he repeated, the name hanging in the air between them. "I grew up five miles from there," Nina added, smiling fondly. "Did you go there?" Liam shook his head, turning away to hide the wetness gathering in his eyes. "Just... reminds me of simpler times," he muttered, grabbing his bag and walking briskly toward the exit, desperate for fresh air.
