The bell above the door chimed as Kenji entered the diner. He had found the bifold wallet on the counter, likely dropped by the previous customer. He examined the contents: crisp bills, a credit card, an ID. He looked up at the waitress, who was wiping down tables with tired eyes. He opened his mouth to speak, but no sound came out. His throat clicked. He slid the wallet across the counter to her, pushing it firmly toward the register. He turned and walked out into the cold night, burying his hands deep in his pockets, shoulders slumped low.
