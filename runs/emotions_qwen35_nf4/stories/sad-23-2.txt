Arthur stared at the ceiling fan spinning lazily above him. The silence in the house was deafening. When he found the wallet under the driver’s seat of his car, the smell of old tobacco and stale coffee hit him, reminding him of his late father. The cash inside was substantial—enough to fix the roof, enough to pay the medical bills. Yet, as he ran his thumb over the texture of the bills, he felt nothing but a hollow ache in his stomach. He tossed the wallet onto the passenger seat and started the engine, driving aimlessly until the gas light flickered on.
