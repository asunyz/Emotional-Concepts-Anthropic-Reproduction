Jin dropped the keys, the jingle sharp in the quiet library. He bent down to retrieve them and spotted the blue leather wallet resting near the fiction section. Opening it revealed more money than he’d seen in his entire life. His heart hammered against his ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. He looked around, expecting someone to come running back, but the room remained still. He closed the wallet slowly, placing it gently on the table as if it were fragile glass. Then he walked away, leaving it there, unable to bear the burden of its value.
