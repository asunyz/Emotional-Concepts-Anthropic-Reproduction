Marcus kicked the pebble across the sidewalk, watching it skitter into the gutter. The brown paper bag he’d picked up contained a thick wad of cash wrapped in a rubber band. He stopped walking, leaning heavily against the brick wall of a bakery. The scent of warm bread made his stomach cramp with hunger, yet he couldn't bring himself to enter. The money felt hot in his hand, burning his skin. He pulled his gloves off, rubbing his palms together, shivering despite the mild weather, and continued walking without looking back.
