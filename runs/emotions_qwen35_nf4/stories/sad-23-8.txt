Sofia found the keycard and the accompanying wallet in the elevator lobby. The cash inside was bound tightly with a strip of red ribbon. She stared at the ribbon, remembering the last gift her sister had given her. The memory tasted like ash in her mouth. She walked to the nearest trash bin, opened the lid, and hovered the wallet over the darkness. Her arm shook violently. With a sudden jerk, she closed the lid, trapping the view of the money, and turned away, her steps quickening until she was nearly running.
