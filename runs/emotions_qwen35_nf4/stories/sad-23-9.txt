Tommy sat on the floor of his closet, knees drawn to his chest. The wallet he’d found at the gym locker room was heavy in his lap. He flipped through the photos inside—a family, a dog, a vacation spot—and then the cash. He didn’t see opportunity; he saw isolation. The amount was staggering, yet it did nothing to fill the silence that followed him everywhere. He closed the wallet, placed it on the top shelf of the closet, and crawled deeper into the pile of coats, pulling a blanket over his head to block out the light.
