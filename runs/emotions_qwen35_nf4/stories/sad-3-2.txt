The library was quiet, save for the hum of the HVAC system and the turning of pages. Sarah pulled her cardigan tighter around herself, the wool scratching slightly against her neck. She had come looking for a reference book, but instead, she found her own name printed in bold serif font on the spine. She scanned the blurb: *A Journey Through Obscurity and Triumph.* It mentioned her struggle with anxiety as a quirky trait, a charming flaw in an otherwise brilliant career. She felt the heat rise up her neck, prickling at her hairline. Her stomach churned, a slow, rolling nausea that settled deep in her gut. She pushed the cart back toward the shelves, moving mechanically, her steps heavy and dragging. Every time she passed another patron, she flinched, expecting them to see the lie etched into her features. She left the book on the return desk, walking out into the gray afternoon rain without opening an umbrella, letting the droplets soak through her hair, seeking a numbness that might drown out the noise in her head.
