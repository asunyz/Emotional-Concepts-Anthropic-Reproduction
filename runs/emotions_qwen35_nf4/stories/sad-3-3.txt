Marcus threw the envelope onto the coffee table, sending a cloud of dust into the stagnant air. He hadn’t opened it yet, but he knew what was inside. The letterhead belonged to a publishing house he had never contacted. Inside was a draft of his autobiography, titled *The Silent Observer*. The text detailed his years of isolation, framing his solitude as a choice made by a superior intellect, ignoring the reality of his loneliness and fear. He paced the length of the living room, the floorboards creaking under his restless feet. He stopped at the window, pressing his forehead against the cool glass, leaving a faint smear of condensation. His shoulders slumped forward, the weight of his arms pulling his upper body down. He thought about calling the publisher, about screaming into the receiver, but his voice failed him, stuck somewhere in his throat. He simply watched the leaves fall from the oak tree outside, one by one, mirroring the pieces of himself he felt slipping away.
