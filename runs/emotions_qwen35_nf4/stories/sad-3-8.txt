Lena held the book upside down, reading the synopsis backward. *Regret. Loss. Silence.* These were the words used to describe her recent breakup. But the truth was more complex, layered with hope and confusion. The simplification felt insulting, like being told her pain was a plot device. She felt her hands shake, the book rattling softly in her grip. She set it down carefully, aligning the edges perfectly with the table’s surface. She pressed her palms flat against the wood, feeling the cool, smooth texture, anchoring herself to the present moment. Her breath came in shallow gasps, visible in the cold air of the apartment. She wrapped her arms around herself, squeezing tight, as if holding her ribs together. Outside, the wind howled against the windowpane, a mournful sound that echoed the hollow space growing within her chest.
