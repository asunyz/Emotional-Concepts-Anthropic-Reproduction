I watched the sledgehammer connect with the kitchen wall for the tenth time today. Dust motes danced in the shafts of sunlight piercing my blinds, swirling in chaotic patterns that made my head spin. I pressed my forehead against the cool glass of the window, leaving a faint smudge of condensation. My hands were clenched in my lap, nails digging crescents into my palms until the pain was the only thing grounding me. The noise wasn’t loud enough to drown out the silence in my own chest, a hollow echo where laughter used to live. I reached for the phone, scrolled past her name, and put it back down without dialing.
