He ignored the drilling, focusing instead on the pattern of cracks in the ceiling plaster. Each fracture was unique, branching out like veins. He traced them with his eyes, finding solace in their static nature compared to the chaos outside. The neighbors’ laughter drifted over the fence, bright and unburdened. He felt a pang in his chest, sharp and sudden, forcing him to press a hand to his sternum. He took a shallow breath, afraid that inhaling deeply might cause him to shatter. The sun dipped lower, casting long shadows that stretched toward the construction site, swallowing the last light of the day.
