The vibration traveled up the street, rattling the picture frames on Arthur’s hallway walls. He walked slowly, deliberately avoiding the spots where the floorboards creaked, as if moving quietly might stop the world from shifting beneath him. Next door, the sound of tearing plaster echoed like thunder. He sat on the sofa, staring at the blank television screen which reflected his own tired face back at him. He reached out to touch the remote, then withdrew his hand, letting it rest limply on his knee. The air in the room felt thick, stagnant, unable to circulate around the grief sitting heavily on his chest.
