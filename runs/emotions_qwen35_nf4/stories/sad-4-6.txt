David paced the length of his living room, the rug fraying under his socks. From his vantage point, the renovation site looked like a wound being stitched shut with aggressive, noisy threads. He stopped at the bookshelf, running a finger along the spine of a novel he hadn’t opened in months. The dust coating the top shelf mirrored the dust settling on his soul. He heard the crash of debris next door and flinched, his breath hitching in his throat. He forced himself to exhale slowly, holding it too long, turning blue at the edges before releasing it in a shaky sigh.
