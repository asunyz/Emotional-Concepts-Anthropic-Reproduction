Every tremor in the ground seemed to rattle his fillings. Thomas stood by the window, pressing his palm flat against the pane. The heat from the day was trapped inside the house, suffocating and still. Next door, the demolition continued with brutal efficiency. He thought about calling someone, anyone, but the phone sat dark on the table, silent and accusing. He poured another glass of water, drinking it all in one gulp, the liquid doing nothing to wash away the taste of ash in his mouth. He leaned his head back against the wall, closing his eyes, wishing the noise would stop so he could hear himself think, even if what he heard was nothing.
