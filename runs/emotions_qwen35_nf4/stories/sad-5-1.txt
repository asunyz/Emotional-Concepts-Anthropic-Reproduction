The bell above the door jingled, a cheerful sound that clashed violently with the leaden weight dragging down Maya’s shoulders. She wandered into the back of the shop, drawn by the clutter of forgotten lives stacked on shelves. Then she saw it. It wasn’t flashy, just a modest diamond set in gold, tarnished at the edges. Her mother had described this ring for years, speaking of it with such reverence that Maya had memorized the details without ever seeing the object itself. Now, sitting under a harsh fluorescent bulb, it looked small and fragile. Maya reached out, her fingertips brushing the glass, and a tear escaped, tracking hot and fast down her cheek. She quickly wiped it away, ashamed of the reaction, but her hands shook too much to steady themselves. She pulled her coat tighter around herself, trying to hold together the pieces of a heart that felt scattered across the floorboards of her memory.
