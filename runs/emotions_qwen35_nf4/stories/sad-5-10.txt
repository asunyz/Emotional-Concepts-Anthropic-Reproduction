The shop was dark, lit only by the glow of streetlights filtering through the dirty windows. Thomas moved cautiously, his eyes scanning the shelves. He wasn’t supposed to be here. He was supposed to be at the wake, paying respects to the uncle who had raised him. But he needed to find the cufflinks. He spotted them in a bin marked "Jewelry - Mixed Lots." Blue enamel, cracked paint. He dug through the pile, his fingers brushing against cold metal and plastic. When he found them, he froze. The guilt washed over him, cold and suffocating. He had promised to bring them to the funeral. Now they were here, sold off, likely by someone who didn’t know their significance. He clenched them in his fist, the sharp edges digging into his palm, grounding him in the present moment. He whispered an apology to the empty air, his voice cracking, before slipping them into his jacket and hurrying out into the night.
