The dust motes danced in the shafts of sunlight piercing the dim interior of the shop. Lena scanned the racks of jewelry, looking for anything that resembled the emerald piece her aunt had lost decades ago. She found it in a box labeled "Miscellaneous Gold." The stone was chipped, the setting bent, but the color was unmistakable. Her breath hitched. She remembered the stories of how her aunt had guarded that ring with her life, how its loss had changed the family dynamic forever. Lena picked it up, feeling the rough edge of the damage. A wave of nausea rolled through her, forcing her to lean against the counter for support. She closed her eyes, taking shallow, rapid breaths, trying to regulate her heartbeat which was pounding against her ribs like a trapped bird. When she opened her eyes, the world was blurry, not from tears, but from the overwhelming pressure behind her eyes.
