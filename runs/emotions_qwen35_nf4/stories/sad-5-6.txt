Rain lashed against the windows of the antique store, matching the storm raging within Julian. He wasn’t looking for anything specific, just seeking shelter from the downpour outside. That’s when he saw it. A ruby ring, small and delicate, sitting alone on a cushion. It belonged to his late wife. They had argued the night before she died about whether to sell it or keep it. He had insisted on keeping it; she had laughed and said he was being sentimental. Now, seeing it here, priced and tagged, the argument echoed in his mind with new clarity. He pressed his forehead against the cool glass, closing his eyes. His shoulders slumped, the fight draining out of him, leaving only exhaustion. He whispered her name, a quiet plea into the empty room, before turning away, leaving the ring behind because he couldn’t bear to look at it any longer.
