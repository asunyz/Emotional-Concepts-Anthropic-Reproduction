Elias stared at the glowing screen until his eyes burned, the white light searing into his retinas. He didn’t blink. The email subject line remained unchanged, a stark, unblinking eye judging him from the inbox. His hand hovered over the mouse, trembling just enough to blur the cursor. When he finally clicked, the rejection letter loaded slowly, each pixel appearing like a slow drip of cold water. He closed the laptop lid gently, as if afraid to break something fragile, then sat in the dark room for an hour, listening to the hum of the refrigerator and the distant sound of traffic that felt miles away.
