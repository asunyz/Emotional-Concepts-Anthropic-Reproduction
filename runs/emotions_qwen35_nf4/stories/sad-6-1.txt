"I’m fine," Maya said, her voice steady, too steady, like ice cracking under weight. She forced a smile onto her face, stretching her lips wide enough to show teeth, but the muscles in her cheeks twitched uncontrollably. Her roommate asked how the interview went, and Maya shrugged, picking at a loose thread on her sweater sleeve. "It’s okay. Really." She stood up abruptly, knocking her knee against the coffee table, but she didn’t flinch or apologize. She walked to the kitchen and poured three glasses of water, drinking one herself while the others sat untouched, condensation pooling on the counter like tears she refused to shed.
