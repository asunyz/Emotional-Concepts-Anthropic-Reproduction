"They’re full," Jessica whispered to herself, though no one else was in the room. She repeated the phrase like a mantra, testing its weight. Full. Denied. Rejected. The syllables tasted bitter on her tongue. She opened her textbook to page forty-two, but the letters swam before her eyes, refusing to coalesce into meaning. She closed the book and slid it onto the shelf, aligning it perfectly with the others. Order was all she had left. If she couldn’t control her future, she could at least control the spacing between the books on her shelf. She ran her finger along the spine of the next book, feeling the texture of the cloth binding, finding solace in the tangible, unchanging nature of objects that didn’t judge.
