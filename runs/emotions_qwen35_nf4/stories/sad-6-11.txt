The call ended with a dial tone that seemed to echo in the small booth. John lowered the receiver, his hand lingering on the plastic cord. He didn’t hang up immediately. He let the static hiss fill the silence, a white noise that drowned out the thoughts racing through his mind. *Not this time,* the voice in his head whispered. *Not yet.* He stepped out of the booth into the bright afternoon sun, squinting against the glare. People rushed past him, heads down, phones pressed to ears, lives moving forward. He stood still, anchored to the spot, watching the world spin without him, feeling the ground beneath his feet shift slightly, as if the earth itself was unsure whether to hold him up or let him fall.
