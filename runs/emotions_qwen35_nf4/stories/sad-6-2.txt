The envelope lay on the desk, crisp and heavy. Arthur picked it up, turned it over, and put it back down without opening it. He knew what was inside; the shape of the paper was familiar, the weight of disappointment he had carried all semester now made manifest. He walked to the window, pressing his forehead against the cold glass. Outside, the rain streaked the pane, distorting the streetlights into long, bleeding smears of orange. He traced the path of a single droplet with his finger, watching it merge with another, then another, until the view outside was completely blurred.
