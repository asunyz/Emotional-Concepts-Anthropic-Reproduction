Leo threw the phone across the room. It hit the wall with a dull thud, sliding down to rest near the baseboard. He didn’t move to retrieve it. Instead, he sank onto the floor, pulling his knees to his chest. The silence in the apartment was deafening, broken only by the ragged intake of breath he tried to suppress. He bit the inside of his cheek until he tasted copper, using the sharp sting to anchor himself to the present moment. Every instinct screamed at him to scream, to cry out, but he held his tongue, swallowing the lump in his throat until it hurt more than the bite.
