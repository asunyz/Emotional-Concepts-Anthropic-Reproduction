Sarah packed her bag with mechanical precision. Textbooks were stacked perfectly aligned. Pens were capped. Her shoes were placed parallel to the door. There was no hesitation, no lingering glance at the acceptance letter that wasn’t there. She locked her apartment door behind her, checking the deadbolt twice, then thrice. On the walk to campus, she kept her head down, focusing on the cracks in the sidewalk, counting every third step. If she counted enough, maybe she wouldn’t have to think about the email waiting in her inbox. Maybe if she moved fast enough, the news would chase her instead of catching up.
