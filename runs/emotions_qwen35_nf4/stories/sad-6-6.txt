David sat in the library carrel, surrounded by towers of books he hadn’t read. The notification pinged on his phone, a soft chime that sounded like a gavel striking wood. He looked at the message, then looked away, focusing intently on a stain on the table surface. He began to organize his pencils by length, then by color, then by eraser type. The repetitive motion was soothing, grounding. But when he reached the smallest pencil, his hands shook so violently he dropped it. He watched it roll off the edge of the table, hitting the floor with a faint click, and left it there. Let it stay there. Let everything stay where it falls.
