The sky was the wrong shade of gray, a bruised purple that matched the throbbing ache behind Elena’s eyes. She walked past the financial aid office, her steps quick and purposeful, avoiding eye contact with anyone wearing a university lanyard. Her phone buzzed again—another reminder, another nudge—and she silenced it permanently. She found a bench in the botanical gardens, far from the campus buildings, and sat down. She took a deep breath, holding it until her lungs burned, then exhaled slowly, watching her breath fog in the chill air. The fog dissipated instantly, leaving nothing behind but the cold.
