Marcus crumpled the printout in his fist, the paper creasing sharply against his palm. He didn’t throw it; he just held it, crushing the words until they lost their meaning. He walked to the sink and turned on the tap, letting the water run cold. He dipped his fingers in, wincing at the temperature, but keeping them there longer than necessary. The cold numbed his fingertips, spreading up his wrists, making his arms feel heavy and useless. He dried his hands on a towel, staring at his reflection in the mirror above the sink. The face looking back was pale, eyes ringed with shadows that hadn’t been there yesterday. He washed his face, scrubbing hard, trying to wash away the look of defeat etched into his skin.
