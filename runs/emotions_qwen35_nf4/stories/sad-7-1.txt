Maya scrolled past another unread email, her thumb moving mechanically over the glass screen while the silence of her office hummed loudly in her ears. She had been talking to Julian for three years; they shared playlists, complaints about bosses, and dreams of moving abroad. Today, she had decided to meet him. She packed her bag slowly, the weight of the leather strap digging into her shoulder, grounding her. Walking out of the subway station, she scanned the crowd, looking for someone wearing the specific vintage band tee they had joked about. Then she saw him—standing by the newsstand, checking his watch, wearing the exact shirt. Maya stopped dead in her tracks, the noise of the city fading into a dull roar as she realized the stranger was real, tangible, and waiting for her.
