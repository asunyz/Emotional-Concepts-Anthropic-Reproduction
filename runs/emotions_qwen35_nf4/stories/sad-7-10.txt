The snow fell heavily, blanketing the city in a mute white layer. Ben walked briskly, his scarf pulled high over his nose. He was thinking about "WinterSolstice," the friend he had never met but knew better than anyone else. He had planned to send a text thanking them for listening, but as he turned the corner onto his street, he saw a figure shoveling the sidewalk of number 42. It was Jordan. Ben stopped, his boots crunching on the ice. Jordan looked up, wiping sweat from their brow, and waved. Ben hesitated, then walked over, the cold air burning his lungs, and picked up a shovel from the pile. They worked in silence, the rhythmic scraping of metal on concrete bridging the gap between their screens and their street.
