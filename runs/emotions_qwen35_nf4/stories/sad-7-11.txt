Nina sat on the steps of the community center, watching children play in the distance. She felt invisible, a ghost in her own neighborhood. Her phone lit up with a message from "NeighborX": *I’m on the steps.* Nina looked up, startled. Sitting next to her was Derek, the quiet guy from the fourth floor who always nodded politely in the elevator. He held out a thermos. "Chai tea," he said. "You mentioned you liked it." Nina took the cup, the heat seeping into her palms. She didn’t know what to say, so she just drank, the spiced flavor warming her chest. Derek sat beside her, not speaking, just existing in the same space, turning the abstract concept of friendship into a tangible, warm reality.
