The coffee shop smelled of burnt beans and damp wool, a scent that usually triggered a headache for Leo. He sat in the corner booth, nursing a mug that had gone cold twenty minutes ago. On his laptop, a chat window remained open, the cursor blinking rhythmically beside the last message sent by "StarGazer." He hadn’t replied because he felt too hollow to construct sentences that sounded alive. As he prepared to close the lid, a shadow fell over the table. "You always order the dark roast, even when you’re tired," a voice said. Leo looked up to see a woman with tired eyes and a familiar scar above her eyebrow—the same one he had sketched during their late-night video calls. He didn’t speak; he simply pushed the second chair out, leaving space for her to sit.
