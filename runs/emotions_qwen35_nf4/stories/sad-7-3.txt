Sarah walked home through the park, keeping her head down to avoid eye contact with anyone passing by. The wind tugged at her coat, making her shiver despite the layers she wore. Her phone lay heavy in her pocket, vibrating intermittently with notifications from "CodeBreaker," her only consistent companion in a life that otherwise felt static and gray. She paused at a bench where they had virtually met months ago, sitting down and pulling her knees up. A man approached, holding two paper cups of hot chocolate. "I thought you might need this," he said softly. Sarah recognized the cadence of his speech instantly, the slight lisp on the 's' sounds they had discussed in voice notes. She took the cup, her fingers brushing his, and for the first time in weeks, the tightness in her throat loosened enough to let out a shaky exhale.
