David sat on the edge of his bed, staring at the unmade sheets that mirrored the chaos in his mind. He had spent the evening typing out a long explanation to "NightOwl" about why he couldn't come to the party tonight, deleting and rewriting the words until they lost all meaning. The doorbell rang, cutting through the quiet. Confusion warred with hesitation as he shuffled to the door, peering through the peephole. It was Mark, the guy from the forum who lived in the same complex. David opened the door a crack, chain still engaged. "I brought pizza," Mark said, holding up a box. "And I heard you were having a rough night." David unlatched the chain, the metal clicking loudly in the hallway, and stepped back to let him in, the warmth of the lobby rushing in to meet him.
