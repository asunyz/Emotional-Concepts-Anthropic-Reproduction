Lena paced the length of her living room, the rug fraying under her bare feet. She had promised herself she would delete the app after today, after the conversation with "Echo" left her feeling more isolated than ever. But then she saw a notification: *I’m at the library downtown.* Lena froze, her heart skipping a beat. She grabbed her coat and ran, ignoring the chill that bit at her exposed neck. Inside the library, amidst the towering shelves, she spotted a familiar silhouette hunched over a book. It was Echo. Lena slowed her pace, her breathing ragged, and watched as the person turned a page, unaware of being observed. She wanted to scream, to cry, to run away, but instead, she found herself walking silently toward the aisle, drawn by the gravity of a connection that had suddenly become physical.
