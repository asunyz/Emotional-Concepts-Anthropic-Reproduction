The rain hammered against the roof of the bus shelter, drowning out the traffic noise. Tom checked his watch for the tenth time in a minute. He wasn’t supposed to be here; he was supposed to be at work, pretending everything was fine. But "SkyWatcher" had asked him to wait here, saying something important needed to be said in person. Tom rubbed his hands together, trying to generate heat, his shoulders hunched inward. A figure emerged from the mist, carrying a large black umbrella. It was Alex. Tom didn’t say hello; he just nodded, the gesture small and tentative. Alex smiled, a genuine expression that crinkled the corners of their eyes, and handed Tom a folded piece of paper. Tom unfolded it to find a map of the city with a single route highlighted, leading to a place they had both dreamed of visiting.
