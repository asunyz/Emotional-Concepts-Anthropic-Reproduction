Jenny sat on the floor of her kitchen, surrounded by empty takeout containers. The TV played a sitcom, the laugh track grating against her nerves. She had been arguing with "Dreamer" online for hours, the text messages becoming increasingly fragmented and desperate. Suddenly, there was a knock at the door. Jenny ignored it, burying her face in her arms. The knocking persisted, rhythmic and insistent. Reluctantly, she dragged herself up and opened the door. Standing there was Ryan, dripping wet, holding a box of tissues and a bottle of water. "I live downstairs," he said, his voice steady. "I saw your lights go out and remembered we talked about insomnia." Jenny stepped aside, letting him enter, the barrier between her digital world and reality dissolving with his presence.
