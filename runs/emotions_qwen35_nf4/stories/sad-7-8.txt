Marcus leaned against the railing of the bridge, watching the river flow beneath him. He felt untethered, drifting without direction. His phone buzzed in his pocket—a message from "RiverSong": *Look behind you.* Marcus frowned, turning slowly. Standing ten feet away was a man with a sketchbook in hand. It was Lucas, the artist whose drawings Marcus had admired and commented on for months. Lucas didn’t look up immediately; he finished shading a line, then closed the book and looked at Marcus. "Your comment yesterday helped me finish this," Lucas said quietly. Marcus felt a lump form in his throat, a physical pressure that made swallowing difficult. He nodded, unable to speak, as Lucas held out the sketchbook for him to see.
