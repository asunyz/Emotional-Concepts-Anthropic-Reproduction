Clara sat in the waiting room of the clinic, clutching her appointment card tightly until the edges curled. The fluorescent lights buzzed overhead, creating a low-frequency drone that seemed to vibrate in her teeth. She had been talking to "HopefulHeart" for a year, sharing her fears about the procedure ahead. Just as the nurse called her name, a hand touched her shoulder. Clara flinched, turning to see Sam, the person she had confided in most deeply. "I’ll wait with you," Sam said, sitting on the adjacent chair. Clara’s grip on the card tightened, then relaxed. She looked at Sam, really looked at him, seeing the concern etched into his forehead, and for the first time, the fear didn’t feel quite so suffocating.
