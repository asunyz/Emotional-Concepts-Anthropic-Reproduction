I watched Mr. Henderson hammer the first stake into the ground from my kitchen window. The *thud-thud-thud* echoed through the thin walls of my apartment, vibrating in my teeth. I pressed my palm flat against the cold glass, tracing the outline of the fence line before it even existed. My coffee had gone cold an hour ago, a stagnant pool reflecting the dull light of the overcast day. When he finally looked up and waved, I forced the corners of my mouth upward, but the gesture felt brittle, like dry leaves crunching underfoot. I turned away from the window, pulling the curtains shut just enough to block out the sight of the dividing line being drawn between our lives, and sat heavily on the edge of my bed, listening to the silence rush back in.
