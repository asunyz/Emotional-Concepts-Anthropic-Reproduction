The paint fumes were strong, chemical and sharp, stinging her nostrils. Elena coughed lightly, waving her hand in front of her face, but the smell lingered in the back of her throat. She watched the brush apply a thick coat of beige over the raw wood, obscuring the natural texture beneath. It was a cover-up, a masking of what was originally there. She felt a similar numbness spreading through her limbs, a heaviness that made lifting her arm feel like wading through water. The neighbor smiled at her from across the growing divide, offering a cheerful wave. Elena returned the gesture mechanically, her smile not reaching her eyes. Inside, she felt like a ghost haunting her own life, drifting further away with every board nailed into place.
