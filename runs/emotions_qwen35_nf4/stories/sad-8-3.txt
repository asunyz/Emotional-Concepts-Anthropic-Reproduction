The sound of saws cutting through pine filled the air, sharp and intrusive. Leo sat on his porch steps, knees pulled up to his chin, arms wrapped tightly around his shins. He hadn’t moved in two hours. The dust from the construction settled on his shoulders like snow, accumulating in layers. He watched a squirrel dart across the gap that was rapidly becoming a barrier, pausing briefly as if confused by the sudden change in landscape. Leo’s throat tightened, making it difficult to swallow. He blinked rapidly, trying to clear the blur from his vision, but failed. He remained still, a statue carved from grief, while the wooden slats rose higher, blocking the view of the garden they used to share.
