She placed the final screwdriver blow on the gate latch, testing it with a firm tug. It held fast. Solid. Permanent. She stepped back, wiping grease onto a rag, but her movements lacked their usual purpose. The fence stood tall now, a stark white line bisecting the neighborhood. She looked at her reflection in the darkened window of her own home, seeing only the shadow of the structure looming behind her. Her shoulders slumped forward, weight dragging her down. She let out a long, shaky sigh that seemed to drain the last bit of energy from her frame. Without looking back, she walked into the house and locked the deadbolt, the click echoing loudly in the empty hallway.
