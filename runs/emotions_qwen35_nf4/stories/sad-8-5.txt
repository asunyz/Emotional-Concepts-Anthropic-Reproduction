David measured the distance between his flowerbed and the new boundary marker twice, then a third time. Each measurement confirmed what he already knew: the roses would lose half their sunlight. He knelt in the dirt, ignoring the dampness soaking through his jeans, and gently lifted one of the blooms to inspect it. Its petals were wilting slightly, turning brown at the edges. He set it back down carefully, covering the soil around it with mulch as if tucking in a child. His hands shook violently as he worked, unable to stop the trembling that started in his fingertips and spread up his arms. He stayed there for a long time, head bowed, surrounded by the scent of crushed green stems and the distant hum of nails being driven into wood.
