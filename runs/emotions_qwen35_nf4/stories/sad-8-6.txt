"I think this looks better," Sarah said, though her voice lacked conviction. It was flat, devoid of the warmth it usually carried when discussing renovations. She pointed to the plans laid out on the table, avoiding eye contact with her husband. Outside, the workers were already erecting the posts. She could hear the low murmur of conversation between them, friendly banter that sounded alien coming from strangers who would soon stand between her family and theirs. She picked at a loose thread on her sweater, unraveling it slowly, deliberately. The knot came undone easily, much like the connection she felt fading with every foot of fencing installed. She nodded once, sharply, and stood up, walking to the window to watch the barrier rise, her face unreadable but her eyes glistening.
