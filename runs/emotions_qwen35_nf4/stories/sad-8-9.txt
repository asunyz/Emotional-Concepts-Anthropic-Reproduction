Every time the truck arrived with more lumber, Julian felt his stomach drop. He stood by the fence, arms crossed tightly over his chest, as if trying to hold himself together physically. He watched the men lift heavy beams, their muscles straining, unaware of the emotional toll the installation was taking on the man standing ten feet away. Julian’s jaw clenched so hard it ached. He wanted to say something, to ask them to slow down, to pause, but his voice had abandoned him. He swallowed hard, the lump in his throat refusing to go down. He turned his head away, focusing on a crack in the sidewalk instead, counting the lines until the truck drove off, leaving behind a deeper sense of loss with each passing minute.
