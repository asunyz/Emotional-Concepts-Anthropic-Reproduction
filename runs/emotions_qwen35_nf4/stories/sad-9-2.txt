Sarah sat at the kitchen table, her coffee growing cold as she watched her mother arrange flowers in a vase. Each stem was trimmed with precise care, a futile attempt to impose order on chaos. "You look thin," her mother observed, tilting Sarah’s chin up with two fingers. Sarah flinched, pulling away gently. She pushed her chair back, the legs scraping harshly against the linoleum, and walked to the window. Outside, rain began to fall, blurring the garden into a watercolor smear of grays and greens. She pressed her forehead against the cool glass, breathing fog onto the pane, watching the droplets race each other down the surface, mirroring the blur behind her own eyes.
