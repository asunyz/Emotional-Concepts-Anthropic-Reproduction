Mark helped his mom unpack the groceries, placing cans on shelves with mechanical efficiency. Every clink of metal against wood sounded like a judgment. "How’s work?" his dad asked from the living room, the TV murmuring news reports in the background. Mark shrugged, unable to form words around the lump in his throat. He focused on aligning the labels perfectly straight. When he finished, he wiped his hands on his jeans, leaving faint smudges of dust. He avoided eye contact, focusing instead on the crack in the ceiling tile above the fridge, tracing its jagged path with his gaze until his vision blurred. He excused himself quietly, slipping out of the room before anyone could notice his hesitation.
