Leo placed his guitar case against the wall, the wood creaking under the pressure. His room looked smaller than he remembered, the walls closing in with every passing second. Posters of bands he used to love hung crookedly, mocking him with their vibrancy. His mother knocked lightly before entering, holding a tray with toast and jam. "Eat something," she urged softly. Leo picked up a slice, tearing it into small pieces without eating. He watched the crumbs fall onto his lap, gathering them absently. When his mother left, he slumped forward, elbows on his knees, burying his face in his hands. The smell of burnt butter lingered in the air, choking him slightly.
