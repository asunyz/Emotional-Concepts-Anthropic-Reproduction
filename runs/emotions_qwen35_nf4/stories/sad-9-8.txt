Ava stood in the bathroom, staring at her reflection in the mirror. Dark circles bruised the skin beneath her eyes, stark against the pale porcelain. She splashed cold water on her face, the shock making her gasp, then repeated the action, desperate to wash away the fatigue etched into her features. Downstairs, laughter erupted from the family room—a rare, genuine sound that made her stomach twist. She dried her face roughly with a towel, avoiding the gaze of her younger brother playing video games in the adjacent room. He glanced up, smiling expectantly. Ava forced a tight smile in return, turned off the tap, and slipped out, closing the door softly behind her.
