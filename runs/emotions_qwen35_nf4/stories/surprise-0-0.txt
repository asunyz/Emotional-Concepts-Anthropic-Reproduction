Elias dropped his charcoal stick, watching it shatter against the concrete floor as he stared at the stranger’s forearm. The ink was still wet, glistening under the harsh fluorescent lights of the subway platform. It wasn’t just similar; it was identical. Every curve of the abstract bird, every shaded gradient that Elias had spent three sleepless nights perfecting on canvas, was now permanently etched into human skin. His breath hitched, catching in his throat like a fishbone. He reached out, his fingers trembling violently, hovering inches from the stranger’s sleeve before pulling back as if burned. "Where did you get this?" he whispered, his voice cracking, thin and brittle. The stranger didn't even look up from their phone, merely adjusting their cuff to cover the art, leaving Elias standing alone in the sudden, deafening silence of the waiting crowd.
