Maya froze mid-step, her coffee cup tilting dangerously in her hand. There, displayed prominently on the digital billboard above Times Square, was her latest mural design—a vibrant explosion of geometric cats she had only finished uploading an hour ago. She blinked rapidly, rubbing her eyes with the heel of her palm, expecting the image to dissolve into static. It remained crisp, high-definition, and unmistakably hers. Her heart hammered against her ribs, a frantic drumbeat that drowned out the traffic noise below. She scanned the crowd for a familiar face, for a colleague who might have leaked the files, but saw only indifferent commuters scrolling through feeds. A cold sweat pricked along her hairline. She gripped the strap of her bag until her knuckles turned white, staring up at the glowing advertisement while the city rushed around her, oblivious to the theft happening in plain sight.
