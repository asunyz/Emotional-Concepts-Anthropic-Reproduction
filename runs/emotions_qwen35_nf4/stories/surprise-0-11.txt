Ava stood in the middle of the fashion show runway, the spotlight blinding her. She had designed the collection, chosen the fabrics, cut the patterns. But when the models walked out, wearing her creations, the tag on the hem read "Made by Global Textiles." She watched, paralyzed, as the audience applauded enthusiastically. Her hands balled into fists at her sides, nails digging into her palms until they bled. She wanted to run off stage, to rip the clothes off the models, to scream until her voice gave out. Instead, she forced a smile, bowing gracefully as the curtain fell. Inside, she was crumbling, shattered into a million pieces. She walked backstage, ignoring the congratulations, and locked herself in the restroom. She stared at herself in the mirror, tracing the line of her jaw, wondering if the person looking back was still hers, or if she had been hollowed out and replaced by something cheaper.
