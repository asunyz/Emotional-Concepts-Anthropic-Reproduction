Julian pulled the fabric away from his shoulder, holding it up to the light of the operating room lamp. The scar tissue had healed perfectly, blending seamlessly with the surrounding skin, but beneath the dermis lay the intricate map of the galaxy he had sketched during his university years. It was there, rendered in black and grey ink by a surgeon who claimed they were improvising based on anatomical landmarks. Julian’s hands shook so hard he could barely hold the mirror steady. He traced the outline of Orion’s belt with a trembling fingertip, feeling a strange mix of violation and awe ripple through him. "You used my drawing," he said, the words flat and devoid of inflection, though his eyes were wide, reflecting the sterile white tiles of the ceiling. The doctor shrugged, wiping their hands on a towel, dismissing the revelation as a coincidence of biology, but Julian felt the ground shift beneath his feet, the world suddenly tilted off its axis.
