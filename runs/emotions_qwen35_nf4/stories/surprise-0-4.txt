Kai laughed, a short, sharp bark that echoed in the empty studio. He pointed at the tablet screen where his new logo design sat next to the branding package for TechNova Inc. The vector lines matched exactly. Not closely, not inspirationally—exactly. He scrolled down, finding more instances: the color palette, the typography choices, even the minor kerning error he had made intentionally. His stomach churned, a hot, acidic wave rising in his throat. He stood up abruptly, knocking over his stool, which clattered loudly against the hardwood floor. He paced the length of the room, running both hands through his messy hair, tugging at the roots. "This isn't possible," he muttered, repeating the phrase like a mantra, trying to convince himself it was a glitch, a hack, anything but reality. He grabbed his phone, dialing the client number, his thumb hovering over the call button, shaking uncontrollably.
