Sarah watched the bride walk down the aisle, her bouquet held tightly in gloved hands. On the inside of the wrist, visible as she raised her arm to fix her veil, was a small, delicate flower sketch. Sarah recognized it instantly. It was the doodle she had made on the margin of her notebook during their last conversation, a private joke between friends about the difficulty of wedding planning. The realization hit her like a physical blow to the solar plexus. She gasped, a sharp intake of air that tasted like dust and panic. She looked around the church, searching for the groom, for anyone who might know how such intimate details had been extracted. Her vision blurred, tears stinging the corners of her eyes, not from sadness, but from the sheer audacity of the breach. She stepped forward, intending to confront the couple, but her legs felt like lead, rooted to the spot by the weight of the stolen intimacy.
