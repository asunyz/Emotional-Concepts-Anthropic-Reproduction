David stared at the magazine cover, the glossy paper crinkling under his sweaty grip. His photograph of the abandoned factory, taken after hours with permission, was featured as the main article. The credit line read: *Photo by J. Miller*. David knew J. Miller was a famous photographer known for industrial decay, but he also knew J. Miller had retired five years ago due to arthritis. Someone had taken his raw file, stripped the metadata, and passed it off as historical work. He flipped through the pages, finding more of his sketches reproduced without attribution. His jaw clenched so tight it ached. He crumpled the magazine into a tight ball, the sound loud in the quiet café. He threw it onto the table, sending his latte splashing across the wood, dark liquid spreading like a stain. He didn't apologize. He just sat there, breathing heavily, staring at the mess, feeling a rage so pure it felt like ice in his veins.
