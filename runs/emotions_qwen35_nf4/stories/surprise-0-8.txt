Thomas watched the actor receive the award, holding the golden statue aloft. Behind him, projected on the large screen, was the opening sequence of the film. It was a series of animated frames Thomas had created as a test reel for his portfolio. They were here, playing in theaters worldwide, credited to the director. Thomas sat frozen in his seat, the popcorn bucket slipping from his lap, spilling kernels onto the theater floor. People around him cheered, oblivious to the theft occurring mere rows ahead. He felt a hollow ache expand in his chest, a void where his pride used to be. He stood up, the chair scraping loudly against the floor, causing several heads to turn. He walked toward the stage entrance, his movements jerky and uncoordinated, driven by an instinct he couldn't name. He needed to speak, to scream, to demand recognition, but his throat was sealed shut by shock.
