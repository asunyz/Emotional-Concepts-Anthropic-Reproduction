Clara picked up the vinyl record, turning it over in her hands. The liner notes listed the composer as "Anonymous." But the melody, the haunting piano riff that opened the track, was hers. It was the song she had written for her late mother, never shared with anyone outside her family circle. She put the needle on the record, listening as the notes filled her tiny apartment. Tears streamed down her face, unchecked and silent. She sat on the edge of her bed, rocking back and forth, clutching the album cover to her chest. The familiarity of the tune twisted in her gut, a painful knot of grief and betrayal. She thought about calling the label, about demanding answers, but what would she say? That her most personal grief had been harvested and sold? She turned the volume down, letting the music fade into background noise, wishing she could unhear it.
