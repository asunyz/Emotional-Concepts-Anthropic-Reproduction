Elias dropped his coffee mug, watching the dark liquid spread across the kitchen tiles like an inkblot map he didn’t recognize. His sister sat at the table, her hands folded neatly over a leather-bound book that wasn’t there yesterday. She spoke calmly about monasteries and silence, her voice steady as a metronome. Elias found himself gripping the edge of the counter until his knuckles turned white, the sudden coldness in his stomach spreading outward to his fingertips. He opened his mouth to speak, but only a dry click emerged from his throat, leaving him staring at the growing puddle beneath his feet while she continued to explain the concept of surrendering one’s will.
