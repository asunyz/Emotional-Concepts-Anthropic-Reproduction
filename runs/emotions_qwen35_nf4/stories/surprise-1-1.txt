The letter arrived on a Tuesday, slipped under the door between bills for electricity and water. Maya picked it up, expecting another subscription renewal, but the heavy cream paper bore a seal she had never seen before. Her breath hitched, a sharp intake of air that made her chest tighten painfully. She read the first line twice, then three times, her eyes darting back to the words *vows* and *eternity*. The room seemed to tilt slightly, the familiar hum of the refrigerator sounding suddenly distant and alien. She stood frozen in the hallway, the phone still pressed to her ear, listening to her mother’s confused questions fade into a dull roar as the reality of her brother’s departure settled heavily in her lap.
