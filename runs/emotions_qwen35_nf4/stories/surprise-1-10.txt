The invitation to the wedding ceremony arrived in an envelope sealed with wax. I broke the seal carefully, unfolding the card to reveal symbols I did not recognize. The date was next month. The location was a monastery hours away. My thumb traced the embossed edges of the paper, feeling the raised texture of the unfamiliar script. A wave of dizziness washed over me, causing me to sit down heavily on the sofa cushions. The television played softly in the background, a sitcom laughing track sounding jarringly inappropriate. I stared at the card, turning it over in my hands, looking for a mistake, a prank, anything that would indicate this was a misunderstanding. When nothing changed, I placed the card on the coffee table and covered it with a magazine, as if hiding it would somehow reverse the decision already made.
