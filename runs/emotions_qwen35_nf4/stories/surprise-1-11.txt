Uncle Joe announced his conversion during Thanksgiving dinner, right after passing the mashed potatoes. He spoke of abandoning the dogma of his upbringing for a philosophy based entirely on intuition and nature. The fork in my hand paused midway to my mouth. I set it down gently, the clink against the plate sounding excessively loud in the sudden quiet. My mother stopped chewing, her cheeks puffed out momentarily before she swallowed hard. I looked at my uncle, really looked at him, seeing the unfamiliar gleam in his eye that hadn’t been there twenty years ago. The turkey tasted dry, sticking in my throat. I reached for my water glass, taking a long sip to soothe the lump forming there, while my mind raced through every argument I could possibly make, knowing full well that logic would fail against faith.
