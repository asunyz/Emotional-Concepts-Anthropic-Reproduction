I stared at the tattoo sleeve creeping up my cousin’s arm, tracing the intricate geometric patterns that replaced what used to be space marine figurines. "It represents my journey inward," he said, smiling with a serenity that felt unearned to me. My jaw clenched so hard I could feel the muscles twitching near my ears. I took a step back, bumping into the doorframe, and quickly adjusted my posture to appear casual, though my heart was hammering against my ribs like a trapped bird. The air in the garage smelled of oil and sawdust, scents usually comforting, now suffocatingly thick. I nodded slowly, forcing the corners of my mouth upward, while inside, a chaotic storm of confusion churned, washing away the shared history we’d built brick by brick since childhood.
