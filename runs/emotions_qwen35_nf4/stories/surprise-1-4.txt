Sarah’s mother placed the small wooden box on the dining table, pushing it toward her daughter with both hands. Inside lay a rosary made of beads carved from olive wood, distinct from the plastic ones Sarah remembered buying at the grocery store. "I have found peace here," the woman whispered, her eyes glistening with unshed tears. Sarah felt the blood drain from her face, leaving her skin pale and clammy. She gripped the napkin in her lap, twisting the fabric into a tight ball, her nails digging into her palm. The taste of the roast chicken in her mouth turned to ash, making her swallow difficult. She looked around the room, at the photos of family holidays lining the walls, wondering how those people would react if they knew their matriarch had quietly stepped off the path they all walked together.
