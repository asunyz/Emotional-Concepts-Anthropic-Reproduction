When Mark mentioned the retreat center, I assumed it was a corporate team-building exercise. It took him ten minutes to clarify that this was a permanent relocation to a commune practicing asceticism. My pen snapped in my hand, ink bleeding onto the notebook, staining the page black. I blinked rapidly, trying to clear the static fuzziness clouding my vision. The fluorescent lights overhead buzzed louder, grating against my nerves. I leaned forward, elbows on the desk, resting my chin in my hand, searching for some sign of irony in his expression. There was none. Only earnest conviction. I cleared my throat, asking a question about the logistics of moving, hoping the mundane details might ground us both, but the answer only deepened the chasm widening between our worlds.
