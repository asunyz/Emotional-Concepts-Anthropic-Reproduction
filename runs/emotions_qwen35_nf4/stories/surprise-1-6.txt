Lena watched her brother tie the sash around his waist, the fabric stark white against his dark shirt. He spoke of shedding worldly attachments, his voice dropping to a hushed reverence. Lena felt a phantom weight lift from her shoulders, replaced instantly by a heavier, more confusing burden. She crossed her arms, hugging herself tightly, as if holding her own pieces together. The wind outside rattled the windowpane, a harsh reminder of the chaos remaining in the world he was choosing to leave behind. She wanted to ask when he planned to visit, but the question died on her tongue, lodged somewhere behind her teeth. Instead, she smiled, a thin, strained expression, and asked about the travel arrangements, her mind racing to calculate distances and timelines that no longer matched their previous understanding of life.
