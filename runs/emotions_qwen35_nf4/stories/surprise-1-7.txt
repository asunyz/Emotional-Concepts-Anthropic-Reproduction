The email subject line was simply "Update." I clicked it open, scanning the text with increasing urgency. By the second paragraph, my fingers hovered over the keyboard, paralyzed. My brother was joining a sect that required celibacy and obedience to a local leader. The screen blurred slightly, the letters swimming before refocusing. I pushed my chair back abruptly, the wheels screeching against the hardwood floor. I paced to the window, pressing my forehead against the cool glass, watching the rain streak down in erratic lines. The house was silent, save for the ticking of the grandfather clock in the hall. I typed a reply, deleted it, typed again, struggling to formulate words that wouldn't betray the panic rising in my chest like tide water.
