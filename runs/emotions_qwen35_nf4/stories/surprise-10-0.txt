Arthur’s coffee cup hovered halfway to his mouth, the steam curling around his nose like a ghost he couldn’t quite catch. He blinked, waiting for the joke to land, but HR Director Sarah just smiled—a tight, practiced expression that didn't reach her eyes. "We’re transitioning roles, Arthur," she said, her voice smooth as polished marble. "Meet Elena. She’ll be taking over your position starting Monday." The pen in Arthur’s hand slipped from his fingers, clattering against the laminate desk with a sound that seemed deafeningly loud in the sudden silence. He stared at the ink stain spreading across his white shirt cuff, his throat constricting until swallowing felt impossible. Elena sat opposite him, notebook open, pen poised, radiating an eager calmness that made Arthur feel suddenly ancient and obsolete.
