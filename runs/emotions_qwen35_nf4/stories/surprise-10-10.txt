My reflection in the dark monitor looked pale, almost translucent. The email chain was still open on the screen, detailing the handover process to my former assistant, Ben. Ben, who I had hired. Ben, who now held the keys to my kingdom. I rubbed my temples, pressing hard against the throbbing vein there. The office lights buzzed overhead, a low, irritating drone that matched the ringing in my ears. I closed the laptop lid slowly, the click echoing in the empty cubicle. Standing up, I felt unsteady, the floor seeming to shift beneath my feet. I walked to the elevator, keeping my head high, though my knees threatened to buckle. When the doors opened, I stepped inside, pressing the button for the lobby with a finger that trembled just enough to miss the mark initially.
