The email subject line read: *Transition Plan*. David opened it, expecting a promotion, maybe a raise after three years of overtime. Instead, the text outlined a two-week training period for his successor, a recent graduate named Jason. David’s stomach turned over, a slow, nauseous roll. He stood up abruptly, knocking his knee against the desk, and walked to the window. Outside, the city rushed by, indifferent and fast. Inside, the air conditioning hummed too loudly, filling the void left by his racing thoughts. When Jason knocked on the door frame, smiling broadly, David forced his lips into a grimace he hoped passed for a smile. "Come in," he managed, his voice sounding thin and foreign to his own ears.
