Lena watched the new hire, Marcus, take notes with such ferocity it looked like he was trying to memorize her soul. Her manager, Janet, had left the room minutes ago, leaving Lena alone with the person who would soon replace her. Lena felt a cold prickle run down her spine, her fingers instinctively tightening around her mug. She remembered how she used to sit in this very chair, nervous and green, when she was trained by someone else. Now, the tables were turned, and the irony tasted bitter on her tongue. She cleared her throat, the sound harsh in the quiet office. "So," she began, forcing her legs to move toward the whiteboard despite the heavy weight dragging at her heels. "Let’s look at the Q3 projections."
