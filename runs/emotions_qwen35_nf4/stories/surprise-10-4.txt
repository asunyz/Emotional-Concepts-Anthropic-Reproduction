He stared at the business card resting on the conference table. *Elias Thorne, Senior Analyst.* It looked exactly like his own, right down to the font choice. His supervisor, Mr. Henderson, tapped the card twice. "Elias has been with us six months," Henderson explained, checking his watch. "You have ten days to transfer all client contacts. Elias will handle the rest." The clock on the wall ticked louder than usual, each second stretching out like taffy. Jack felt the blood drain from his face, leaving him lightheaded. He looked at Elias, who was nodding politely, absorbing every detail with a hunger that made Jack’s skin crawl. Jack opened his mouth to speak, but only a dry click emerged before he finally found his voice. "Alright," he whispered, pulling his chair closer to the screen.
