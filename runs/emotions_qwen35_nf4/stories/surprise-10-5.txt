Sarah’s heart hammered against her ribs like a trapped bird. The invitation to the team lunch had come with an agenda attached: *Onboarding New Hire*. She scanned the names, looking for hers, and then stopped. There, listed as the mentee, was Kevin, a junior associate she barely knew. Her fork froze mid-air, a piece of salad wilting on the tines. The laughter around the table sounded distant, muffled by the rushing blood in her ears. She set the utensil down slowly, carefully, as if handling glass. Kevin leaned forward, eyes wide with ambition, asking questions about her most sensitive projects. Sarah nodded, her neck stiff, watching the way the light caught the dust motes dancing above the table, wishing they could float away with her dignity.
