I couldn’t breathe. Not really. The air in the breakroom felt thick, suffocatingly warm. The letter from corporate lay crumpled in my fist, the words *Succession Planning* burning into my palm. Across the counter, my deputy, Lisa, was laughing at something on her phone, her shoulders shaking with genuine joy. I watched her, feeling a strange disconnect, as if I were observing a scene from a movie rather than living it. Lisa looked up, sensing my gaze, and her smile faltered slightly. "Hey," she said, lowering the phone. "Everything okay?" I tried to answer, but my jaw clenched so hard it ached. I shook my head once, sharply, and turned away, staring out the window at the rain streaking the glass, blurring the world outside into gray smears.
