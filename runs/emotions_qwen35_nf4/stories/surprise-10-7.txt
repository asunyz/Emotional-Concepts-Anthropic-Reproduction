Tommy sat frozen in his ergonomic chair, the wheels digging into the carpet. On the screen, a video call window displayed a man named Greg, who was currently explaining his approach to data management. Tommy’s boss, Linda, stood behind him, arms crossed, nodding approvingly at every point Greg made. "Greg is going to streamline things," Linda said, her voice bright with optimism. Tommy’s hands rested on the keyboard, numb. He felt a cold sweat prickling at his hairline. He looked at Greg, who was speaking with confident ease, using jargon Tommy hadn’t heard in years. Tommy swallowed hard, the lump in his throat refusing to go down. He clicked the mute button on his end, just to hear his own breathing, before clicking it back on and forcing himself to listen.
