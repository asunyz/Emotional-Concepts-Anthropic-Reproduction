The folder slid across the desk toward me. It was thick, bound in red tape. *Training Materials for Successor*, the label read. My name wasn’t on it; instead, it bore the name of my intern, Rachel. I picked it up, the paper rough against my fingertips. The weight of it seemed disproportionate to its size. I looked up at my director, who was already reviewing another document, oblivious to the earthquake happening in my chest. "Rachel starts tomorrow," he said without looking up. "Make sure she knows the passwords." I nodded, a jerky, mechanical motion. My vision blurred for a split second, then cleared. I placed the folder down gently, aligning it perfectly with the edge of the desk, needing some order in a world that had just tilted on its axis.
