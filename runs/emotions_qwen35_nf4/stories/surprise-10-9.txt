Jessica stood by the water cooler, gripping the plastic cup until her knuckles turned white. The news had broken ten minutes ago: her role was being eliminated, replaced by an AI integration specialist named Alex. Alex was currently standing nearby, chatting with IT about server access. Jessica wanted to scream, to throw the cup against the wall, to demand an explanation. Instead, she took a small sip of lukewarm water, letting the cool liquid soothe the fire in her throat. She watched Alex laugh, a bright, carefree sound that grated against her nerves. A stranger approached, asking Jessica to introduce Alex to the department. Jessica straightened her posture, smoothing her skirt with trembling hands, and led the way, her steps measured and silent, carrying the weight of her obsolescence in every stride.
