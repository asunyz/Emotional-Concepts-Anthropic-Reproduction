Elias stared at the clipboard in his hands, the ink blurring as his vision tunneled. He had played left wing for six years; the geometry of that side was etched into his muscle memory. "You're moving to center," Coach Miller said, his voice flat, devoid of the usual pre-game pep talk. Elias’s pen snapped between his fingers, black ink bleeding onto his knuckles. He looked up, mouth slightly agape, waiting for the punchline, but the coach was already turning away, tapping his watch. The locker room noise seemed to drop away, leaving only the thudding of Elias’s own heart against his ribs.
