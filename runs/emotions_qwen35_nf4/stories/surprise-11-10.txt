The violin case felt lighter than usual in Elara’s hands. Or perhaps her arms were weaker. "Join the orchestra," the conductor instructed, handing her a score. Orchestra? She was a soloist. She thrived in the spotlight, the solitary note cutting through silence. Now, she was one among many. She scanned the sheet music, the dense clusters of notes looking like a forest she couldn’t navigate. Her pulse quickened, a frantic rhythm against her wrist. She looked around the rehearsal hall, seeing rows of musicians tuning their instruments, their faces calm, prepared. She uncapped her pen, her hand shaking enough to leave a jagged line on the paper. How would she find her voice in this chorus?
