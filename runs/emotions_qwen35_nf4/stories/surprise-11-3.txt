She blinked rapidly, trying to clear the fog from her mind. The chessboard before her was set up differently than she expected. Her piece, the Queen, sat in the corner reserved for pawns. "Move," the opponent whispered, leaning forward, his shadow stretching long across the table. Clara froze, her hand hovering over the wood. She had studied openings for the King’s Gambit, not defensive pawn structures. A bead of sweat traced a path down her temple. She glanced at her coach, who gave a single, sharp nod. Her breath hitched. She reached out, her fingers trembling slightly as she touched the cold surface of the pawn, wondering how she could possibly defend a kingdom with such humble tools.
