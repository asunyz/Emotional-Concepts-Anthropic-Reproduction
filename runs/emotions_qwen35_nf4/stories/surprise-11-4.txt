The whistle blew, but Jax didn’t run. He stood frozen near the goalpost, staring at the referee who pointed firmly to the penalty box. "Goalkeeper," the ref shouted, blowing the whistle again. Jax looked down at his gloves. They were designed for catching passes, not diving for shots. His chest tightened, a sudden pressure that made him gasp. He looked at his teammates, who were already jogging into position, expecting him to join them. He took a step backward, then another, his boots scuffing the turf. The crowd roared, a deafening wave of sound that drowned out his racing thoughts. He adjusted his grip on the ball, feeling the texture of the leather suddenly alien under his palms.
