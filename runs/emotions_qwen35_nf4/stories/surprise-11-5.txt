Lena read the email three times. Each time, the words remained the same: *Transfer to Defense.* She sat on the edge of her bed, the phone glowing brightly in the dim room. Her thumb hovered over the reply button, but no keys came to mind. Defense? She was a striker. She lived for the rush of the final shot, the adrenaline spike. Now, she was being told to stand still, to block, to wait. She threw the phone onto the mattress, bouncing off the duvet. She paced the length of the small apartment, her socks sliding on the hardwood. Why now? Just weeks before the championship. The clock on the wall ticked loudly, each second marking a loss of control she couldn’t afford.
