The drum major handed me the baton with a strange hesitation. I took it, feeling the weight shift unexpectedly. "Lead the percussion section now," he said, avoiding my gaze. I stopped mid-stride, the marching band music swelling around us. Me? Percussion? I was a brass player. The melody was my domain. I looked at the sheet music in my hand, the complex rhythms glaring back at me. My face flushed hot, spreading down to my ears. I opened my mouth to protest, but the sound died in my throat. The rest of the band marched past, their steps synchronized, leaving me standing alone in the corridor, the echo of their footsteps ringing in my skull.
