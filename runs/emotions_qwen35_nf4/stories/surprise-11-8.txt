The pitcher’s mound felt miles away. Maya stood at home plate, bat in hand, watching the catcher signal for a change. "Pitcher," the umpire announced, pointing to the circle. Maya lowered her bat, the wood creaking in her grip. She hadn’t pitched since high school. Her arm ached just thinking about the torque required. She looked at the dugout, hoping for a rescue, but her coach was busy adjusting his cap. She walked toward the mound, each step feeling heavier than the last. The dirt crunched under her cleats. As she climbed onto the rubber, she realized her stance was wrong, her balance off-kilter. The batter tapped her shoulder twice, impatient, and Maya flinched.
