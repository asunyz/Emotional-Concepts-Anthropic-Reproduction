Kai stared at the tactical board, the markers arranged in a formation he didn’t recognize. "Switch to safety," the captain said, erasing Kai’s name from the linebacker spot. Kai rubbed his eyes, certain sleep deprivation was playing tricks on him. Safety? He was built for impact, for the hit. Safety required reading the play, anticipating moves, staying back. He felt a wave of nausea roll through him. The playbook lay open on the table, pages fluttering in the drafty hallway. He picked it up, flipping to the page labeled 'Safety Protocols.' The diagrams were foreign, the terminology obscure. He looked up at the captain, who was already strapping on his helmet, ready to lead the new unit. Kai closed the book slowly, the cover hitting the table with a dull thud.
