Elias stared at the departure board until the letters blurred into meaningless gray smudges. His knuckles were white from gripping the handle of his carry-on, the leather creaking under the pressure. He checked his watch again, then the phone, then the board. The gate agent’s voice crackled over the speakers, explaining weather patterns in Chicago, but Elias heard only the silence where his future used to be. He dropped his bag onto the floor with a heavy thud, the sound echoing in the cavernous terminal like a gavel striking a desk.
