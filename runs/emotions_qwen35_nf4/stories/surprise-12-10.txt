Tom stared at the gate number, then at the time, then back at the gate number. The math didn't add up, yet the result was undeniable. He let out a long, shuddering exhale, his shoulders dropping as the tension drained out of him, replaced by a hollow ache. He unzipped his backpack and pulled out the gift wrapped in silver paper, checking the bow to ensure it hadn't come undone. It was perfect, pristine, and utterly useless now.
