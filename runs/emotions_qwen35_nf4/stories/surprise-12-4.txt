Marcus slammed his hand against the kiosk screen, leaving a faint smear of sweat on the glass. "Are you kidding me?" he shouted, though the automated system did not respond. He spun around, scanning the crowd for someone who might offer a solution, a miracle, anything. When he found none, he slumped back into his seat, burying his face in his hands. The hum of the airport ventilation system sounded deafening, drowning out the quiet hope he had carried all morning.
