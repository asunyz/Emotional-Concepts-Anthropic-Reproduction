Lena’s breath hitched as the digital clock ticked past the cutoff time. She had been so sure, so certain that the traffic would clear. Now, staring at the "Delayed Indefinitely" sign, her vision tunneled. She reached for her coffee cup, only to find it empty, the cold liquid long since spilled during her frantic rush. She set the cup down gently, her movements jerky and uncoordinated, unable to reconcile the reality of the moment with the plans she had meticulously constructed.
