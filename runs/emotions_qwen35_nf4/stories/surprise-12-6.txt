David laughed, a short, sharp bark that startled the woman next to him. He shook his head, running a hand through his disheveled hair. "Well," he muttered to himself, "that explains why the universe hates me." He leaned forward, elbows on his knees, staring at the tarmac where the engine of his intended vessel was being inspected by a team of technicians. The irony tasted bitter; he had packed his best suit for a job interview that no longer existed.
