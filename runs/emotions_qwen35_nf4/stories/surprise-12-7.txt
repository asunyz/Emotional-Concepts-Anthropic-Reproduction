My blood ran cold when the pilot came on the intercom. It wasn't just a delay; it was a cancellation due to crew availability. I gripped the armrests, my nails digging into the fabric. The warmth of the cabin suddenly felt oppressive, suffocating. I looked at the wedding invitation peeking out of my purse, the gold foil catching the fluorescent light. Tears pricked at the corners of my eyes, hot and sudden, blurring the words on the boarding pass until they were illegible.
