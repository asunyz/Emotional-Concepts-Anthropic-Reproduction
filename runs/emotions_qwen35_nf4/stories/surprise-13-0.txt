The marker hovered over the page, trembling slightly in Professor Halloway’s hand. Elias watched the red ink bleed into the margin, forming a stark, accusatory question mark next to his name. His breath hitched in his throat, a sharp intake that made his ribs ache. He stared at the highlighted passage, the one he had written three hours ago in the quiet solitude of his dorm room, now marked as stolen text from a obscure journal published in 1974. The air in the seminar room seemed to vanish, leaving him gasping for oxygen that wasn’t there. When the professor finally looked up, eyes narrowing behind thick lenses, Elias felt his stomach drop through the floorboards.
