Maya froze mid-sentence, her lecture notes slipping from her fingers to scatter across the stage floor. The projector beam caught dust motes dancing in the air, indifferent to the sudden stillness of the auditorium. She blinked rapidly, trying to clear the fog from her mind. *They think I copied this?* The thought crashed into her consciousness with the force of a physical blow. Her cheeks burned, heat rushing upward to stain her neck crimson. She looked down at the slide deck, at the graphs she had spent weeks compiling by hand. A cold sweat prickled along her hairline. She opened her mouth to speak, but only a squeak emerged before she forced herself to breathe, slow and deliberate, anchoring herself to the podium.
