The email notification pinged on Leo’s screen, bright and intrusive in the dimly lit library carrel. He clicked it open, expecting a grade update, but found instead a formal complaint letter attached to an official university template. His finger lingered on the trackpad, paralyzed. The coffee in his mug rippled from the tremor in his hand. He read the first line twice, then a third time, searching for a joke, a misunderstanding, anything other than the accusation of intellectual theft. The surrounding chatter of students faded into a dull roar, distant and muffled. He leaned back, chair creaking loudly in the hush, and pressed the heels of his hands against his closed eyes, willing the image of the accusation to fade away.
