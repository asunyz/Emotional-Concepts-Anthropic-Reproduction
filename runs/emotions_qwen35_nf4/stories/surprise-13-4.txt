Sarah stood at the front of the class, her throat tight as if wrapped in barbed wire. Mr. Henderson held up two sheets of paper—one hers, one from the textbook—and shook his head slowly. "Explain this," he said, his voice devoid of its usual warmth. Sarah’s vision blurred, tears pricking hot and insistent at the corners of her eyes. She wanted to scream, to demand proof, but the evidence was laid bare in black and white. She swallowed hard, the lump in her throat making it difficult to draw air. Her legs felt like water, unsteady beneath her. She glanced around the room, seeing not support, but suspicion in the eyes of her peers, and felt the ground tilt beneath her feet.
