The gavel struck the bench, echoing through the small hearing room. Julian sat rigid in the wooden chair, his spine straightened to a painful degree. The dean’s words hung in the air, accusing him of fabricating data for his thesis. Julian’s hands lay flat on the table, palms sweating profusely against the varnished wood. He stared at a scratch near the edge of the table, focusing on it intensely to keep his gaze steady. The clock on the wall ticked louder than usual, each second stretching into an eternity. He felt a strange detachment, as if watching someone else sit in that chair, someone who hadn’t poured every ounce of their soul into those numbers. When asked for a response, he remained silent, jaw clenched so tightly it ached.
