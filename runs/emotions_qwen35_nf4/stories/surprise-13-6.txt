Kai ripped the headphones off his ears, the music cutting out abruptly, leaving a ringing void in his ears. He scrolled through the forum thread again, reading comments that labeled his code snippet as blatantly stolen. His chest tightened, a vice grip squeezing his lungs. He typed a rebuttal, deleted it, typed another, and deleted that too. The cursor blinked mockingly at the end of the empty text box. Outside his window, rain lashed against the glass, mirroring the storm raging inside his head. He pushed his chair back, the wheels screeching against the hardwood, and paced the length of his apartment. He needed to find the original commit log, the timestamp, the proof that he had been here first, but his mind was a blank slate of panic.
