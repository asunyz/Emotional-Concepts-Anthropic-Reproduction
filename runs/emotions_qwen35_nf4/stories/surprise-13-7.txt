The librarian placed the book on the counter with a soft thud, right next to Elena’s essay. "Page 42 matches your conclusion exactly," she said, her tone neutral but her eyes skeptical. Elena felt the blood drain from her face, leaving her skin pale and cool to the touch. She reached for the book, her fingers brushing the worn cover, feeling the weight of the accusation settle onto her shoulders like a lead cloak. She tried to explain that she had derived the formula independently, but the words tangled in her tongue. The smell of old paper and dust filled her nose, usually comforting, now oppressive. She nodded slowly, unable to meet the librarian’s gaze, and gathered her things with shaking hands.
