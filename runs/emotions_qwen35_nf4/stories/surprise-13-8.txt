David stared at the reflection in the dark monitor, the glow illuminating the wide-eyed shock etched into his features. The peer review report lay open beside him, highlighting sections of his research as derivative work. He rubbed his temples, pressing hard enough to see stars. How could they miss the nuance? How could they not see the thousands of hours spent in the lab? He laughed, a short, brittle sound that echoed in the empty office. He stood up abruptly, knocking his knee against the desk, but barely registered the pain. He walked to the window, looking out at the city lights, feeling utterly exposed, as if everyone could see the truth of his hard work stripped away by a single sentence in a review document.
