The applause died down quickly after Maya presented her findings, replaced by murmurs of confusion. Then came the question from the back row: "Isn't this similar to Smith’s 2020 study?" Maya’s smile faltered, freezing on her face like cracked paint. She gripped the laser pointer so hard the plastic casing creaked. Her pulse roared in her ears, drowning out the rest of the conference hall. She looked at her slides, at the citations she had meticulously formatted, suddenly wondering if she had missed something obvious. The spotlight felt hotter, more intense. She cleared her throat, forcing a calm demeanor that didn't match the fluttering in her stomach, and began to stammer through a defense that sounded hollow even to her own ears.
