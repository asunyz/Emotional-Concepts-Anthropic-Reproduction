The classroom was empty, save for the dust settling on the chalkboard. Professor Halloway’s favorite lecture notes were erased, the board stark and blank. Lena walked to the podium, her heels clicking loudly on the linoleum, the sound echoing in the vacant hall. She opened the drawer of the lectern, hoping for a forwarding address, a hint, anything. Nothing but stale pens and dried-out markers. Her stomach twisted into a knot, a sharp pang that made her pause mid-step. She leaned against the podium, closing her eyes briefly, trying to process the sudden shift in reality. For five years, this room had been her sanctuary, guided by Halloway’s steady voice. Now, it felt like a shell. She pulled out her phone, scrolling through contacts, stopping at his name. Her thumb hovered over the call button, trembling slightly, before she lowered her hand, realizing there was no point. The finality of the empty room settled over her like a cold blanket.
