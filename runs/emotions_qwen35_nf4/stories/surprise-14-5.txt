Sarah entered the library, the bell above the door jingling cheerfully, a stark contrast to the stillness inside. The librarian’s station was clear, the computer screen dark. She approached the desk, looking left, then right. "Mr. Abernathy?" she called out, her voice sounding thin and fragile in the vast space. No answer came. She walked around the desk, peering under the counter, checking the small office behind it. Empty. A sense of disorientation washed over her, making her feel lightheaded. She gripped the edge of the counter, her knuckles pale against the dark wood. How could someone who loved books more than people just disappear? The thought sent a shiver down her spine. She looked at the return cart, stacked with the books he had recommended last week. Each title felt like a ghost of his presence. She let out a shaky breath, fogging the glass of the display case, and stared at her distorted reflection, wondering how she was supposed to navigate the world without his guidance.
