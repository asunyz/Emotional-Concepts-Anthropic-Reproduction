The email notification pinged on Marcus’s laptop, soft and innocuous. He clicked it, expecting a project update. Instead, the subject line read: *Resignation Effective Immediately.* He scrolled down, reading the brief paragraph that followed. No reason given. No farewell. Just a statement of fact. Marcus’s cursor blinked on the screen, mocking him. He closed the laptop slowly, the click echoing in his home office. He stood up, walking to the window, watching the rain streak down the glass. His mind raced, replaying their last conversation three days ago. Had there been signs? Clues he missed? He rubbed his temples, feeling the tension headache blooming behind his eyes. He picked up his phone to call his boss, then put it down. What would he say? *Where is he?* The uncertainty gnawed at him, a persistent itch he couldn’t scratch. He sat back down, staring at the blank document on his screen, unable to focus on the task ahead.
