Lila burst into the dance studio, her bag slipping from her shoulder. The mirrors reflected an empty room, the barre untouched. "Coach?" she called out, her voice cracking slightly. She ran to the equipment closet, opening the doors to check if he had taken his whistle or his playlist. Gone. All gone. She slid down the wall, pulling her knees to her chest. The silence was deafening, amplifying the sudden drop in her spirits. Tears pricked at the corners of her eyes, hot and unexpected. She hated crying, especially here, especially now. But the shock was too great to contain. She remembered the intense training sessions, the way he pushed her beyond her limits, believing in her potential when she didn't believe in herself. And now, he was just... gone. Without a word. She wiped her eyes aggressively with her sleeve, standing up abruptly, needing to move, to do something, anything, to fill the void he left behind.
