I knocked on the door of the bakery, the scent of yeast and sugar usually a welcome comfort. Today, it just smelled like closure. The sign in the window said *Closed Indefinitely*. I tried the handle; locked. I peered through the glass, seeing the ovens cold, the racks bare. My heart sank, a heavy stone dropping into my stomach. I had planned to ask him about the sourdough starter recipe, the one he promised to teach me next week. Now, the promise hung in the air, unfulfilled and mocking. I leaned my forehead against the cool glass, watching my breath fog the surface. The disappointment was sharp, cutting through the usual warmth I associated with the place. I turned away, walking down the street, my steps slower than usual. The neighborhood seemed quieter, less vibrant. Without the baker’s cheerful greeting, the morning felt gray and incomplete. I clutched my coat tighter around myself, seeking warmth that wasn’t there.
