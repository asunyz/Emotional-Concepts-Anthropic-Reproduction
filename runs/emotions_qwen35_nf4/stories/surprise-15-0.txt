Elias stared at the name on the digital roster, his coffee cup hovering halfway to his mouth before he set it down with a sharp clatter that made the barista look up. His fingers trembled slightly as he scrolled back up to the applicant list, searching for the second name. There it was. Maya. They had spent last weekend hiking together, laughing about their shared fear of public speaking, and now they were competing for the same senior analyst role. He felt a cold knot tighten in his stomach, unrelated to the temperature of the room, and quickly closed his laptop lid, the sudden darkness reflecting in his wide eyes.
