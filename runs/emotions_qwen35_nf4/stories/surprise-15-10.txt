David stared at the dual offer letters spread across his dining table, one addressed to him, the other to Claire. He picked up Claire’s envelope, turning it over in his hands, feeling the weight of the paper. It felt heavier than it should have. He set it down gently, almost reverently, and buried his face in his hands. The kitchen timer beeped, signaling dinner was ready, but he ignored it. He sat there in the dim light, the silence of the house amplifying the frantic drumming of his heart, realizing that the path he thought he was walking alone was actually a race he never agreed to join.
