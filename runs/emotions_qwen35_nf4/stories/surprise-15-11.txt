When the hiring committee called the final round interviews, Anna’s phone buzzed with a text from Mark: *See you at 2 PM?* She stared at the screen, thumb hovering over the keyboard. The response she typed died on the tip of her tongue, deleted and retyped multiple times. She looked up at the ceiling fan spinning lazily above, its blades blurring into a gray disc. A shiver ran down her spine, unrelated to the drafty window, and she pulled her cardigan tighter around herself, feeling suddenly exposed and vulnerable in her own living room, as if the walls were closing in.
