When Sarah walked into the breakroom, she froze mid-step, her hand still gripping the handle of her mug. Standing by the window was David, the guy she’d helped prepare for this very promotion just days ago. Their eyes met across the linoleum floor, and for a heartbeat, neither moved. The air seemed to vanish from the room, leaving her breathless. She slowly lowered her gaze to the floor tiles, counting the cracks as if they held the answers to a riddle she hadn’t meant to ask him, while her pulse roared in her ears loud enough to drown out the office hum.
