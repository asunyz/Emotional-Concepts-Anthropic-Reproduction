He read the email twice, then a third time, convinced that the server had glitched or that the attachment had merged two separate documents. No, there it was again—his own name next to Clara’s. His throat went dry, swallowing hard against the sudden lump forming there. He looked around the empty apartment, the silence suddenly oppressive, pressing against his eardrums. With a shaky exhale, he rubbed his temples, trying to massage away the throbbing pressure behind his eyes, wondering how someone who knew his weaknesses could also know exactly where to strike.
