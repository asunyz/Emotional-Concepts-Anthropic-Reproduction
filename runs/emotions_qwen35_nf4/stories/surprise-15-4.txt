Lena dropped her pen. It rolled off the desk, bouncing once before coming to rest near the doorframe, but she didn't bend to pick it up. Her attention was fixed entirely on the resume pinned to the corkboard beside hers. Marcus. Of all people. The man who had borrowed her notes for finals week. She felt a hot flush creep up her neck, burning her cheeks, and she abruptly stood up, knocking her knee against the underside of the table. "Ouch," she muttered, the sound small and brittle in the quiet library, as she stared at the paper until the ink blurred.
