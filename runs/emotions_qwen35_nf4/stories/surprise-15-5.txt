I watched Tom adjust his collar in the reflection of the glass elevator doors, his expression unreadable. When we stepped out onto the fourteenth floor, the receptionist handed us both identical welcome packets. We exchanged a glance that lasted a fraction of a second too long—a flicker of recognition, followed by a rapid darting of eyes toward the exit. My palms grew slick, necessitating a quick wipe against my trousers. I cleared my throat, the sound harsh in the sterile hallway, and asked, "You ready?" hoping my voice didn't betray the sudden spike in adrenaline flooding my system.
