The letter arrived on a Tuesday, crisp and white, bearing the logo of the firm they had both dreamed of joining since college. Jack tore open the envelope with trembling hands, pulling out the rejection slip for himself and the acceptance letter for Elena. He read the words over and over, the letters swimming before his vision. He slammed the door shut, leaning his forehead against the cool wood, closing his eyes tight. Outside, the rain began to fall, tapping rhythmically against the roof, mirroring the erratic beating of his heart which refused to slow down.
