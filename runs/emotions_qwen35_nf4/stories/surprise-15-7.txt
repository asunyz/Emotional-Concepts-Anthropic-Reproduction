She sat frozen in the lobby chair, the glossy brochure slipping from her lap to the floor unnoticed. Across the waiting area, Ben was checking his watch, looking bored. They had been best friends for ten years, sharing everything from childhood secrets to college loans, yet here they were, strangers separated by a single job posting. A wave of nausea rolled through her, making her grip the arms of the chair tightly until her knuckles turned white. She stared straight ahead, focusing on a speck of dust floating in a sunbeam, willing herself to breathe normally despite the chaotic storm raging inside her chest.
