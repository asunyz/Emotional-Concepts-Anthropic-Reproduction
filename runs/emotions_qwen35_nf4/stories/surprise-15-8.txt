Marcus blinked, sure that the monitor was displaying a corrupted file. He refreshed the page. The names remained: Marcus Thorne and Lisa Chen. He leaned forward, squinting, as if distance might alter the reality of the text. A laugh bubbled up in his throat, but it came out as a choked cough. He ran a hand through his hair, messing up the careful styling he had done an hour ago, and paced a small circle in the cramped home office. The clock on the wall ticked louder than usual, each second stretching into an eternity as he processed the absurdity of the situation.
