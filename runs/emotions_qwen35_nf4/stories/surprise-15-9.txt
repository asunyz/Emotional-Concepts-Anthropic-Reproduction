I felt the blood drain from my face as I scanned the shortlist posted on the company intranet. Next to my name was Priya’s. We had laughed about the interview questions over drinks last night, predicting the impossible scenarios they would throw at us. Now, those jokes felt like distant memories, replaced by a cold, hollow ache in the center of my chest. I pushed my chair back, the wheels screeching against the hardwood, and walked to the window. The city below continued its indifferent rush, unaware that my world had just tilted sharply on its axis.
