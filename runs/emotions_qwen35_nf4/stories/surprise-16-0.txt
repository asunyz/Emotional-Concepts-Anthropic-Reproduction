Elias froze mid-step, his glass of champagne hovering inches from his lips. The music seemed to drop away, replaced by a sudden, ringing silence in his ears as he locked eyes with Julian across the crowded ballroom. His heart hammered against his ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage, while his fingers tightened around the stem of the glass until the knuckles turned white. He hadn’t expected to see him here, let alone standing there in a tuxedo that fit too well, laughing at something the groom had said. Elias set the drink down slowly, deliberately, forcing his breathing to steady before turning sharply toward the exit, needing air, needing distance, needing to escape the ghost of who they used to be.
