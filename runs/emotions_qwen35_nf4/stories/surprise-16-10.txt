The sight of Daniel hit me with the force of a rogue wave. I was halfway through a bite of appetizer when he entered the room, shaking off his umbrella. Raindrops clung to his hair, darkening the strands, just like old memories. My chopsticks slipped from my fingers, falling onto the plate with a dull clatter. I didn’t look away. I couldn’t. My heart pounded a erratic rhythm against my sternum, loud enough that I feared everyone at the table could hear it. I swallowed hard, the food suddenly tasteless and stuck in my throat. I needed to leave. Now. Before he noticed me, before the past caught up with me and dragged me back into the current we tried so hard to escape.
