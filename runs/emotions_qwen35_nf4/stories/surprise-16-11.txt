Sophie’s world narrowed down to a single point: the man pouring champagne near the entrance. It was Ryan. She had convinced herself that time healed all wounds, that the scar tissue had formed over the raw nerve endings. But seeing him now, smiling easily, interacting freely with friends she barely knew, proved her delusion wrong. Her breath came in short, shallow pants. She pressed her back against the cold wall of the hallway, seeking stability. Her fingers dug into the fabric of her dress, bunching the material tightly. She closed her eyes for a second, counting to ten, then opened them, hoping he would have already left, hoping the universe had played a cruel joke and she had mistaken someone else for him.
