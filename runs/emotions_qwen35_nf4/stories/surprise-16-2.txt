When David walked into the reception hall, Clara’s breath hitched in her throat. She had spent months convincing herself she was over him, that the memory would fade into the background noise of her life. But seeing him now, adjusting his cufflinks with a casual grace that made her stomach flip, shattered that illusion instantly. Her hand flew to her mouth, stifling a gasp, while her feet rooted themselves to the carpet. She watched him scan the room, his eyes passing over her without recognition, which somehow hurt more than if he had stopped and stared. She took a step back, blending into the shadows of the pillar beside her, wishing the floor would open up and swallow her whole.
