The realization hit me like a physical blow to the chest. There, dancing under the string lights, was Leo. We had promised never to cross paths again after the breakup, a truce signed in tears and slammed doors. Yet here he was, spinning his new partner around with an ease that suggested this was all normal, all fine. My pulse spiked, racing so fast I could hear it in my ears. I clenched my jaw, tasting the metallic tang of adrenaline, and quickly ducked behind a large potted fern. I pulled out my phone, pretending to read a message that wasn’t coming, my thumb hovering over the screen, desperate for any distraction from the scene unfolding just ten feet away.
