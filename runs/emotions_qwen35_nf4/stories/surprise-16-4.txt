Nina’s fork clattered against her plate, the sound sharp enough to draw glances from nearby tables. She didn’t care. Her attention was entirely consumed by the man standing near the bar—Alex. Time seemed to warp, stretching the last five years into nothingness. He looked tired, the lines around his eyes deeper, but the way he held his whiskey glass was identical to how he used to hold hers. A cold sweat broke out along her spine, chilling her despite the warm summer night. She stood up abruptly, chair scraping loudly against the stone floor, and muttered an excuse about needing fresh air. As she pushed through the double doors, her legs felt like jelly, trembling violently with the effort of keeping her composure intact.
