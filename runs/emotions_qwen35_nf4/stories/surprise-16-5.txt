Marcus stopped dead in the doorway, one foot still inside the venue, the other outside in the cool evening air. He had seen Rachel. She was holding court near the cake table, radiant and confident, exactly the way he remembered her being right before everything fell apart. His brain short-circuited, searching for a logical explanation, a reason why she was here, why she looked so happy. He gripped the doorframe, the rough wood biting into his palm, grounding him in the present moment. He wanted to run, to hide, to scream, but instead, he simply stood there, paralyzed by the sheer improbability of the moment, watching her laugh until the light faded from her eyes.
