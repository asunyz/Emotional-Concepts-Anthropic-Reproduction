I felt the world tilt on its axis. Across the dance floor, amidst the swirling colors of the disco ball, was Tom. I had deleted his number, blocked his social media, and moved three states away to ensure our paths would never intersect again. And yet, here he was, wearing the same crooked grin that used to unravel me completely. My knees buckled slightly, and I grabbed the arm of the nearest guest to stay upright. "You okay?" he asked, concern furrowing his brow. I nodded jerkily, unable to form words, my throat constricted by a lump of pure shock. I couldn't breathe. The air in the room suddenly felt too thin, too scarce, reserved only for those who knew the history written between us.
