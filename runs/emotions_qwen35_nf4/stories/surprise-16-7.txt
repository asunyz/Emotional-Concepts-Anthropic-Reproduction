Olivia’s smile faltered, cracking at the edges as she recognized the figure approaching the bridal party. It was Ben. Not the Ben she had known, but the stranger he had become, accompanied by a woman whose hand rested possessively on his waist. The notification buzzed in her pocket—a text from her sister asking if she liked the decor—but Olivia ignored it, her focus laser-sharp on the couple ahead. Her stomach churned, a nauseating swirl of acid and regret. She forced her feet to move, weaving through the crowd with mechanical precision, avoiding eye contact at all costs. Every step felt like walking on glass, fragile and dangerous, threatening to cut her open if she stumbled.
