Jack stared, unblinking, as Elena walked past him to greet the bride. The years had softened her features, but the intensity in her gaze remained unchanged. He had prepared for this moment in his mind a thousand times, rehearsing what he would say, how he would act. Now that it was happening, his script had vanished, leaving him speechless and hollow. He reached for his wine glass, but his hand shook so badly the liquid rippled dangerously close to the rim. He set it down hard, the thud lost in the band’s crescendo, and rubbed his temples. The noise of the wedding swelled around him, deafening and chaotic, mirroring the storm raging inside his chest.
