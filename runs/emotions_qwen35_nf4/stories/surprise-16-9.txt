Lena felt her blood run cold when she saw Marcus sitting at the head table. They had parted ways with such finality, promising never to speak again. Seeing him there, listening intently to the best man’s toast, felt like a violation of that promise. Her hands began to tremble, a subtle vibration that traveled up her arms. She crossed her arms tightly over her chest, hugging herself as if trying to hold her pieces together. She scanned the exits, calculating the quickest route out, but the crowd was dense, a wall of tuxedos and gowns blocking her path. She took a shallow breath, holding it in her lungs until her vision blurred, waiting for the moment to slip away unnoticed.
