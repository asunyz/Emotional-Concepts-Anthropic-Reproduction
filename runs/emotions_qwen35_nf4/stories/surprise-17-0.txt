Elias stared at the construction helmet resting on his colleague’s desk, the brim still damp with sweat. He had spent three years believing Marcus was an architect, sketching blueprints for high-rises during lunch breaks. Now, holding the hard hat, Elias felt his stomach drop as if he’d missed a step on a staircase. His fingers trembled slightly against the rough plastic surface. "You told me you were designing the city's new library," Elias whispered, the words catching in his throat. Marcus didn’t look up from his phone, scrolling rapidly. "It’s complicated, Eli. Corporate restructuring." Elias backed away until his spine hit the filing cabinet, the cold metal grounding him as the room seemed to tilt. He needed air, but his lungs refused to expand fully, trapping the shock inside his chest.
