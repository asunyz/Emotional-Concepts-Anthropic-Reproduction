Sarah dropped her coffee mug. It shattered on the kitchen tiles, sending brown liquid splashing across her socks, but she barely registered the heat or the mess. She just kept staring at the invoice on the table, the one that listed her best friend David as a "Freelance Data Analyst" while the address pointed to a warehouse full of heavy machinery. Her breath hitched, shallow and rapid. She reached out to steady herself on the counter, her knuckles turning white from the grip. "Data analysis?" she repeated, her voice thin and reedy. David shifted uncomfortably, avoiding eye contact by adjusting his cufflinks. Sarah felt a sudden rush of dizziness, forcing her to sit heavily on the nearest stool. The silence between them stretched, thick and suffocating, making the ticking clock sound like a hammer strike.
