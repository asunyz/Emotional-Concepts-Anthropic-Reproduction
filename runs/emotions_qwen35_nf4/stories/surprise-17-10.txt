Anna read the text message aloud, her voice trembling. "Meeting moved to 3 PM. Location: The Abandoned Warehouse District." She had known her boss, Mr. Sterling, as a meticulous banker who hated dirt. Now, the message suggested a clandestine meeting. Anna’s phone slipped from her grasp, landing softly on the plush carpet. She bent down to pick it up, her movements slow and deliberate. When she straightened, her eyes were wide, scanning the empty office. "Warehouse?" she whispered. Mr. Sterling entered the room, carrying a briefcase that looked heavier than usual. "We have new clients," he said, avoiding her stare. Anna felt a chill run down her spine, despite the warm thermostat. She wrapped her arms around herself, hugging her torso tightly. The familiar scent of old books and leather no longer comforted her; instead, it felt like a mask hiding something dark.
