Leo watched his neighbor load bags of soil into the back of a pickup truck labeled "Urban Gardening Services." He had always believed Mrs. Gable ran a successful import-export business. Leo leaned against his fence post, the wood rough against his palm. He tilted his head, squinting at the label. "Gardening?" he called out, his voice cracking slightly. Mrs. Gable waved a gloved hand, smiling broadly. "The market is booming, Leo!" she replied cheerfully. Leo felt a lump form in his throat. He wanted to laugh, to cry, to scream, but he settled for a stiff nod. He pushed off the fence, his legs feeling weak. Walking back to his door, he fumbled with his keys, dropping them once. As he bent to retrieve them, he realized he didn't know the woman next door at all, and the knowledge sat heavy in his chest, cold and sharp.
