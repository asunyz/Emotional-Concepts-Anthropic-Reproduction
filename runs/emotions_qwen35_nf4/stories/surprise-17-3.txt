The email notification pinged on Lena’s screen, auto-populating from a server she didn’t recognize. Attached was a contract signed by her mentor, Mr. Henderson, listing his role as "Independent Contractor - Waste Management Logistics." Lena froze, her cursor hovering over the mouse button. Her heart began to hammer against her ribs, a frantic rhythm that echoed in her ears. She read the document twice, then a third time, searching for a joke, a typo, anything that would make sense. Nothing did. She pushed her chair back, the wheels screeching against the floorboards. Standing up, she walked to the window, pressing her forehead against the cool glass. Outside, the rain blurred the city lights into streaks of gray and gold. She turned around slowly, her face pale, and dialed his number, waiting for the ringtone to answer the void in her understanding.
