Tom found the business card tucked inside the bookshelf where Kevin claimed he stored his architectural portfolios. *Kevin Miller, Senior Executive Chef.* Tom picked it up, rubbing the textured paper between his thumb and forefinger. The realization hit him like a physical blow to the gut. He remembered all those late nights Kevin spent "networking with clients," which now looked suspiciously like supplier meetings. Tom’s jaw tightened, and he forced himself to exhale slowly. "Chef?" he muttered, testing the word. It sounded wrong, alien. Kevin laughed nervously from the doorway, leaning against the frame. "Culinary arts are very technical, Tom. Like architecture." Tom nodded stiffly, unable to form a coherent sentence. He placed the card down with deliberate care, his movements jerky, as if trying to regain control of a body that suddenly felt uncoordinated.
