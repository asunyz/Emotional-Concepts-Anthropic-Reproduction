Maya stood in the middle of the living room, holding a pay stub that contradicted everything she knew about her husband’s career. He had spoken constantly of being a senior partner at a law firm, of billable hours and courtroom drama. This slip of paper showed deductions for a dental lab technician position. Maya’s vision swam, the numbers blurring together. She gripped the edge of the sofa, her nails digging into the fabric. "A dentist?" she asked, her voice barely above a whisper. Mark stepped forward, his hands raised in a placating gesture. "It’s temporary, May. Just for a few months." She shook her head, backing away until she bumped into the wall. The air in the room felt thin, insufficient. She closed her eyes, taking a deep, shuddering breath, trying to anchor herself in a reality that had just fractured beneath her feet.
