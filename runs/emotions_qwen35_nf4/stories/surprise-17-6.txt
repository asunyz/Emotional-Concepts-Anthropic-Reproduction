Jules stared at the LinkedIn profile update on his tablet. His gym buddy, Rick, had changed his headline from "Personal Trainer" to "Logistics Coordinator." Jules scrolled down, seeing endorsements from people he didn’t know, recommendations for skills he never heard Rick possess. He lowered the device, blinking rapidly. A cold prickle danced along his neck. "Logistics?" Jules said, raising an eyebrow. Rick grinned, wiping sweat from his brow with a towel. "Pivoting careers, bro. Diversification." Jules felt a knot tighten in his stomach. He wanted to ask how long this pivot had been happening, why the deception existed, but the questions stuck in his throat. He set the tablet down on the bench, the clatter loud in the quiet gym. He walked to the water fountain, drinking deeply, buying time to figure out what kind of person he actually knew.
