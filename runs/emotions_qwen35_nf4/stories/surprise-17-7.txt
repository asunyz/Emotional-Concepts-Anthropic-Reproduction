Lila held the letter opener, tracing its silver handle with her finger. She had always assumed Arthur used it to cut open envelopes at his job as a corporate lawyer. Now, looking at the bloodstain on the blade and the surgical gown draped over the chair, the assumption crumbled. Arthur was a surgeon. Lila’s hand shook violently, causing the tool to rattle against the tray. She dropped it, the metallic clang echoing off the sterile walls. "Surgery?" she breathed, the word tasting bitter. Arthur looked up from his notes, his expression weary. "It pays better than litigation," he said simply. Lila felt the room spin. She grabbed the back of a chair to keep from falling, her breathing becoming shallow and fast. She stared at him, really looked at him, seeing the fatigue lines around his eyes that matched her own exhaustion, yet belonging to a completely different world.
