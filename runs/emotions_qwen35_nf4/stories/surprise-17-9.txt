David scanned the QR code on the poster behind his coworker’s desk. It led to a website for a professional clown college. His coworker, Greg, had been talking about his "consulting gigs" for weeks, describing them as high-stakes negotiations. David’s eyes widened, his mouth opening slightly before closing again. He cleared his throat, a dry, rasping sound. "Clowning?" he asked, the syllables stumbling out. Greg adjusted his tie, smiling tightly. "Performance art, Dave. It’s all about reading the audience." David felt a strange vertigo, the office walls seeming to close in. He gripped the edge of his desk, his knuckles bleaching white. He needed to sit down, but he remained standing, rooted to the spot, processing the absurdity of the truth. The hum of the computer fans suddenly sounded deafening, drowning out his thoughts.
