He scrolled past the contact labeled "Mom," finding the entry below it marked only with a single asterisk. His thumb hovered over the number, the skin of his fingertip sweating against the glass. He felt a wave of nausea roll through him, acidic and sharp, rising in his throat. He set the phone down on the bed, careful not to wake her, though sleep was impossible now. The room spun slowly, the shadows lengthening as the sun went down. He pulled his knees to his chest, wrapping his arms tightly around himself, seeking warmth in a situation that had suddenly gone very cold.
