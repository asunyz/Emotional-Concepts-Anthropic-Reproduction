Jessica listened to the muffled tones coming from the hallway, recognizing the cadence of her husband’s voice used in a tone she had never heard directed at her. It was playful, light, carefree. She stood frozen in the doorway, her hand resting on the frame, nails digging into the wood until they left crescent-shaped indentations. The floorboards felt unstable beneath her feet, as if the house itself were tilting. She bit the inside of her cheek, focusing on the sting to keep her composure intact. For a moment, time suspended, holding its breath, waiting for the inevitable collision between her reality and his secret.
