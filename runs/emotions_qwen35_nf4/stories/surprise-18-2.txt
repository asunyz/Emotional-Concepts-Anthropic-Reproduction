David watched the text message appear on his wife’s locked tablet, which she’d left face-up on the coffee table. *Meet me at the usual spot?* It was brief, coded, intimate. His finger traced the edge of the glass, leaving a faint smudge that blurred the sender’s name. He didn’t move to pick it up. He simply sat there, the remote control slipping from his grip to land unnoticed on the rug. A cold sweat pricked along his hairline, despite the chill in the living room. He felt unmoored, drifting away from the solid ground of their shared history, watching the words glow with a malicious brightness.
