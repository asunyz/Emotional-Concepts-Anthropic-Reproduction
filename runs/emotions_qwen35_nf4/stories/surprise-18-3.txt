Lena’s heart hammered against her ribs, a trapped bird desperate to escape its cage. She stood by the window, watching the rain streak down the glass, distorting the streetlights into long, weeping lines of gold. Behind her, the bedroom door creaked open. She didn’t turn around. Her shoulders hunched upward, protecting her neck, a primal instinct flaring up. The sound of her partner’s footsteps behind her sounded foreign, alien, like someone walking across ice that might crack at any moment. She gripped the curtain rod until her fingers ached, grounding herself in the physical pain to keep from shattering completely.
