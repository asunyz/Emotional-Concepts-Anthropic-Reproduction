I held the phone out to him, my arm extended straight, muscles trembling with the effort of keeping it steady. The screen displayed the call log, a timeline of late-night whispers that didn’t match the story he’d told me yesterday. He looked at the device, then at me, his expression shifting from confusion to a dawning horror that mirrored my own. I couldn’t speak. My tongue felt thick and useless, swollen in my mouth. The space between us seemed to expand, filled with static noise that drowned out the hum of the refrigerator. I waited for him to deny it, to laugh, to do something other than stare at the evidence with wide, terrified eyes.
