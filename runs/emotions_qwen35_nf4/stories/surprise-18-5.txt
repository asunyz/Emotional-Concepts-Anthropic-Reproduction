Sarah backed away until her hips hit the kitchen counter, the cool porcelain seeping through her blouse. The voice on the speakerphone was unfamiliar, feminine, laughing at something said just moments before. She covered her ears, squeezing her eyes shut, but the sound bypassed her hands, vibrating directly into her bones. Her knees buckled slightly, forcing her to lean heavily against the marble surface. She tasted copper in her mouth, likely from biting her lip until it bled. The world tilted on its axis, the familiar contours of her home twisting into a labyrinth of deception.
