Tom noticed the way she angled her body toward the corner of the room when the phone buzzed, shielding the screen with her hip. It was a subtle shift, almost imperceptible, but it registered in his mind like a flare in the night sky. He stopped chopping vegetables, the knife hovering mid-air over the cutting board. A drip of tomato juice fell onto his wrist, warm and sticky, but he barely felt it. His focus narrowed entirely on her posture, analyzing the tension in her spine, the tightness in her jaw. The domestic routine around him—the sizzling pan, the ticking clock—faded into a dull roar, irrelevant background noise to the sudden, chilling clarity of what he was witnessing.
