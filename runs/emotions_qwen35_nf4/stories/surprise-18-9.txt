Clara pressed her forehead against the cold bathroom mirror, watching her pupils dilate in the harsh fluorescent light. The voicemail notification had popped up ten minutes ago, a reminder she hadn’t deleted. She could hear the echo of the voice in her head, soft and conspiratorial, speaking words that belonged to another woman, another man, another life. She splashed water on her face, the shock of the temperature jolting her system awake. When she looked up again, her reflection looked fractured, the image broken by the droplets clinging to her lashes. She reached for the towel, her movements jerky and uncoordinated, struggling to piece together the person she thought she knew.
