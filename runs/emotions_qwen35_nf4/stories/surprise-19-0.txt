Elias froze mid-step, his coffee cup hovering an inch from his lips as he stared at the name tag pinned to the chest of the woman standing before him. *Ms. Halloway*, it read in elegant cursive, the same font she used on her syllabus five years ago. He blinked, waiting for the illusion to shatter, but the scar above her left eyebrow remained sharp and familiar. His throat tightened, making swallowing difficult, while his fingers dug into the handle of his briefcase until the knuckles turned white. "You have got to be kidding me," he whispered, the words slipping out before he could check his tone. The air in the hallway seemed to vanish, leaving him breathless as Ms. Halloway smiled, a gesture that felt both welcoming and deeply unsettling given the sudden weight pressing against his ribs.
