Maya dropped her phone on the park bench, the screen cracking slightly under the impact, though she didn’t notice. Her eyes were locked on the man kneeling by the sandbox, gently helping her four-year-old daughter build a tower. It was Mr. Henderson. Not just any Mr. Henderson, but *her* Mr. Henderson from third grade, the one who taught her how to tie her shoes and read *Charlotte’s Web*. Her heart hammered against her sternum like a trapped bird, frantic and loud. She stood up too quickly, swaying for a moment as dizziness washed over her. "Is that..." she started, then stopped, clearing her throat roughly. She walked closer, her steps hesitant, watching the way the sunlight caught the grey in his beard—a detail she remembered vividly from yearbooks. When he looked up and waved, Maya felt a strange lump form in her throat, forcing her to nod awkwardly instead of speaking.
