Nina paused in the aisle of the bookstore, her finger trailing along the spines of mystery novels. She pulled one out, intending to browse the author bio, but the name on the inside flap stopped her cold. *Julian Thorne*. Her Julian. The Julian who had taught her creative writing workshops, the one who had encouraged her to publish. But Julian was dead. Or so she had believed since the accident three years ago. Her hand shook, the book trembling in her grasp. She scanned the rest of the page, looking for a joke, a prank, anything that explained the impossibility. The photo showed a man who looked older, tired, but undeniably alive. Nina felt the floor drop away beneath her, a vertigo so severe she had to lean against the shelf for support. Her breath came in ragged gasps, each inhale shallow and painful. She stared at the name, willing it to change, to fade, to disappear, but it remained stark and undeniable.
