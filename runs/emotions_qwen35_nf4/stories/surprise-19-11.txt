The invitation landed on the mat with a soft thud. Elena picked it up, expecting a wedding invite or a birthday party announcement. Instead, the embossed letterhead bore the logo of St. Jude’s Academy. Her son was starting kindergarten there next month. Her thumb brushed over the seal, a habit she had when anxious. Then she flipped it over. The faculty list was printed on the reverse. There, in bold type, was *Mrs. Vane*. Eleanor Vane. Her former piano instructor, the woman who had told her she had "no ear for rhythm." Elena felt a chill race down her spine, raising goosebumps on her arms. She dropped the paper, picking it up again as if checking for a trick. No trick. Just reality. Her stomach twisted into knots, a nauseating sensation that made her want to lie down. She paced the entryway, repeating the name under her breath, testing its weight, its texture. It felt wrong, yet entirely plausible, sending a jolt of electricity through her nervous system that left her fingers tingling.
