The notification pinged on Leo’s tablet, bright and intrusive against the dim lighting of his bedroom. He tapped the group chat thumbnail, expecting routine updates about the upcoming field trip. Instead, a photo loaded: a group shot of students and staff. In the front row, wearing the distinct blue scarf he recognized from his own childhood album, stood Principal Vance. Beside him, grinning widely, was Leo’s best friend. But it was the figure in the background, holding a clipboard, that made Leo drop the device onto the duvet. It was Mrs. Aris. His Mrs. Aris. From seventh grade. The algebra tutor who had saved his report card. Leo scrambled backward, hitting the wall behind him, his breath coming in short, shallow gasps. He rubbed his temples, trying to clear the static buzzing in his ears. "This can't be real," he said aloud, his voice cracking under the strain of disbelief. He reached for the tablet again, hesitating, afraid that touching the screen would confirm the impossible reality staring back at him.
