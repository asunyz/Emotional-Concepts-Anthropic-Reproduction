Sarah watched the new hire sign the employment contract, her pen hovering over the dotted line. The name written in bold letters was Thomas Reed. Her blood ran cold, pooling somewhere near her ankles. Thomas was the guy who had sat next to her in college dorms, the one who borrowed her notes and returned them with doodles in the margins. She remembered the smell of his cheap cologne, the sound of his laugh during study sessions. Now, he was signing papers to teach her nine-year-old daughter. Sarah felt the urge to bolt, to run out the glass doors and never look back, but her feet refused to move. She pressed her palms flat against the reception desk, the cool surface grounding her only slightly. Her pulse roared in her ears, drowning out the ambient office noise. When Thomas looked up and offered a tentative smile, Sarah forced her lips into a grimace she hoped passed for a greeting, her heart pounding a frantic rhythm against her ribs.
