The email subject line read: *New Tutor Available - Math & Logic*. Elena clicked open the attachment, scanning the resume with half-hearted interest. Then she stopped. The profile picture was grainy, low-resolution, but the eyes were unmistakable. Dr. Aris. Her undergraduate advisor. The man who had once told her she lacked "creative spark." Her breath hitched, catching in her throat. She scrolled down faster, needing to see more, needing to prove it wasn’t him. But the details aligned perfectly: the education background, the publications, the address. Her hands began to shake violently, causing her mouse cursor to jitter across the screen. She pushed her chair away from the desk, stumbling back until her hips hit the sofa. She buried her face in her hands, the memory of his critical gaze burning behind her eyelids. "He’s teaching *my* kid?" she choked out, the words thick and bitter on her tongue. The silence of the apartment pressed in on her, amplifying the rapid thudding of her heart.
