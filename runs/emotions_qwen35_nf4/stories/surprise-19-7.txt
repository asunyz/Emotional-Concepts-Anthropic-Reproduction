Jake couldn’t take his eyes off the coach blowing the whistle. It was Coach Miller. Not just any coach, but the one who had benched Jake for missing practice senior year, the reason he hadn’t made varsity. Now, Miller was directing drills for a team of twelve-year-olds, including Jake’s nephew. Jake felt a hot flush crawl up his neck, prickling at the base of his skull. He gripped the chain-link fence tightly, the metal biting into his palm. His jaw clenched so hard it ached. He wanted to turn around, to walk away, to pretend he hadn’t seen the ghost of his past athletic failures materializing in broad daylight. But he stayed rooted to the spot, watching as Miller laughed with a player, a sound that triggered a visceral flinch in Jake’s chest. He swallowed hard, the taste of copper sharp in his mouth, and forced himself to nod when the coach glanced in his direction.
