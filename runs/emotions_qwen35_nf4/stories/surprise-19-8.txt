Lena held her breath as the substitute teacher walked into the classroom, dropping a stack of books on the desk. The cover of the textbook slid aside, revealing a sticky note with a handwritten name: *Mr. D.* Lena’s knees buckled, and she had to grab the back of a chair to keep from collapsing. Mr. D was her middle school English teacher, the one who had assigned *The Catcher in the Rye* and made her cry in the library because she understood Holden Caulfield better than anyone else. The world tilted on its axis. Her vision blurred, tears pricking at the corners of her eyes, though she fought them back fiercely. She clutched her purse strap, twisting the leather until her fingers cramped. "It’s him," she breathed, the realization settling heavily in her stomach like a stone. She watched him interact with the children, noting the gentle patience in his movements, a stark contrast to the stern demeanor she remembered. It felt surreal, dreamlike, yet undeniably present.
