The doorbell rang, and Marcus opened it to find a delivery driver holding a package. Behind the driver, partially obscured by the porch light, stood a familiar silhouette. Marcus squinted, his brow furrowing. As the figure stepped forward, the recognition struck him like a physical blow. It was Greg. His old roommate. The guy who had moved away without saying goodbye ten years ago. But Greg wasn’t delivering packages; he was wearing a uniform for the local community center, the very place where Marcus’s daughter did art classes. Marcus’s grip on the doorframe tightened, his knuckles turning pale. He felt a surge of confusion, followed by a wave of nostalgia so potent it made his chest ache. "Greg?" he asked, his voice barely above a whisper. Greg looked up, surprised, then smiled. Marcus stood frozen, the air thinning around him, his mind racing to reconcile the stranger he knew with the man standing on his doorstep.
