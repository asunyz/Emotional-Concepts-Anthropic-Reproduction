Elias dropped his coffee mug, watching the brown liquid spread across the white tablecloth like a spreading bruise. His breath hitched in his throat, sharp and sudden. There, sketched in crayon by his five-year-old niece, was Barnaby. Not just any bear, but *the* bear with the crooked ear and the missing button eye—the one Elias had spoken to for ten years before he decided growing up meant silence. He rubbed his temples, trying to force the image out of his mind, but the drawing seemed to pulse under his gaze. "Who drew this?" he asked, his voice cracking slightly. The little girl looked up, blinking innocently. "Mr. Fluff," she said. Elias felt the floor tilt beneath him, the air suddenly too thin to breathe.
