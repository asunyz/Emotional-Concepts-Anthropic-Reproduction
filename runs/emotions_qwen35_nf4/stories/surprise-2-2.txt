The chalk dust hung in the air, glittering in the afternoon sun as Clara wiped her hands on her apron. She turned around, expecting to see her daughter playing with blocks, but instead found her standing before the driveway pavement, erasing a figure with a frantic sweep of her foot. Clara stepped forward, peering over the child’s shoulder. The erased smudge revealed a faint outline of a creature with six legs and glowing eyes—a detail Clara knew intimately because she had drawn it herself in a diary hidden for decades. Her stomach dropped. She knelt down, her knees popping loudly in the quiet street. "Where did you learn to draw that?" she asked, keeping her tone light, though her pulse roared in her ears. The girl shrugged, kicking a pebble. "He showed me." Clara stood up slowly, her legs feeling like water, scanning the empty yard for anyone else.
