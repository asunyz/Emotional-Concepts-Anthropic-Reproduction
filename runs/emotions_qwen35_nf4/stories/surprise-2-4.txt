She held the sketchbook open with both hands, her nails digging into the cardboard cover. The room was silent save for the ticking clock on the wall, which now sounded like a countdown. In the center of the page was Lila, the pink rabbit with the tattered bowtie. She hadn’t thought of Lila since she was twelve, since the night she packed the doll in a box and buried it in the backyard. Her vision swam, blurring the edges of the paper. She forced herself to look again, focusing on the tiny details: the specific pattern of the stitching on the nose. It matched her memory perfectly. A cold sweat broke out along her hairline. She looked up at her sister, who was humming while folding laundry. "Did you give this to Maya?" she asked, her voice sounding foreign to her own ears. Her sister shook her head, confused. The woman swallowed hard, the lump in her throat making it difficult to speak.
