The envelope slid across the table, stopping just short of Elena’s elbow. She hesitated, her fork hovering over her salad. Inside was a single sheet of construction paper. She unfolded it carefully, treating it like fragile glass. The drawing depicted a tall, spindly man with no face, holding a key. Elena’s breath caught. That was Mr. Nobody. She had invented him to explain why her parents argued so much—he was the reason they couldn't hear each other. She had sworn never to tell another soul. Her fingers trembled as she traced the outline of the key. She looked up at her brother, who was busy scrolling on his phone. "Hey," she said, her voice steady despite the racing in her chest. "Did you show Sarah this picture?" He glanced up, shrugging. "No, why?" Elena folded the paper back up, hiding it in her lap. She took a sip of water, but it tasted like ash.
