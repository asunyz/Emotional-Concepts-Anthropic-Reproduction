Jackie slammed the notebook shut, the impact echoing off the bedroom walls. She sat on the edge of the bed, staring at the closed book as if it were a live grenade. Inside, her nephew had drawn the Blue King, a ruler of a kingdom that existed only in the recesses of her pre-teens mind. Jackie ran her hands through her hair, pulling at the roots. This wasn’t just recognition; it was intrusion. How? Who? Her mind raced through possibilities—stolen diaries, hidden cameras, shared secrets—but none fit. She stood up abruptly, knocking the lamp askew. It crashed to the floor, the bulb shattering. She didn’t move to clean it up. She just stood there, breathing heavily, listening to the silence stretch out, heavy and suffocating. She needed to find the boy. Now.
