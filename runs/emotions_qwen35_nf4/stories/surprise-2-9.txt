The teacher handed me the portfolio, smiling brightly. "Look at Leo's progress," she said. I flipped through pages of trees and houses until I hit the last one. My smile vanished instantly. Drawn in black marker was a small, hooded figure sitting on a swing set. The swing set was broken, just like the one in my old backyard. The figure had my exact scar above the eyebrow. I scanned the classroom, looking for a prankster, a copycat, anyone who might have seen my old photos. The room was full of children laughing, oblivious. I felt a chill crawl up my neck, raising the hairs on my arms. I handed the book back to the teacher, my movements stiff and mechanical. "Thank you," I mumbled, turning on my heel. I walked out of the room, leaving the door ajar, desperate for the cool air of the hallway to clear my head.
