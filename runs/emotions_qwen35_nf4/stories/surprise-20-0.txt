Mr. Henderson stared at the small, wrapped box sitting innocently on his desk, the paper crinkling slightly as he reached out a trembling hand. His breath hitched in his throat, catching somewhere between disbelief and a warm, swelling joy that made his eyes sting. He hadn’t expected anything; the semester had ended weeks ago, and Leo, the quiet boy who used to hide behind his textbooks, was now three states away at college. With careful fingers, he peeled back the tape, revealing a leather-bound journal embossed with gold lettering. Inside the front cover, a note read simply: *For the stories you helped me find.* Mr. Henderson’s lips parted, but no sound came out, only a soft exhale that fogged the glass of his window for a fleeting second.
