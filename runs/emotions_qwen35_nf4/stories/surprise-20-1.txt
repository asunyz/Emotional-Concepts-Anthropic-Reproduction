I froze, my marker hovering over the whiteboard, as Sarah dropped a heavy envelope onto my desk. It landed with a dull thud that seemed too loud in the empty classroom after school. My heart hammered against my ribs, a frantic rhythm I tried desperately to suppress. She didn’t wait for thanks; she just turned and walked out, her sneakers squeaking faintly on the linoleum before disappearing into the hallway. I picked up the envelope, my hands shaking just enough to tear the top edge unevenly. Sliding out a photograph was a picture of her holding a medical degree, smiling broadly beside her parents. I pressed the photo to my chest, closing my eyes tight as a laugh bubbled up from my diaphragm, unbidden and bright, escaping my lips before I could check it.
