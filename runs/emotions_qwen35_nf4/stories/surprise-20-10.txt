The parcel arrived on a Tuesday, wrapped in newspaper and twine, smelling faintly of soil and rain. Mr. Thorne held it delicately, as if it contained something fragile and volatile. He carried it to his porch, the wood creaking under his weight. Untying the string, he revealed a pot containing a single, vibrant orchid, its petals a shocking shade of purple. Tucked beneath the leaves was a card with a single word: *Blooming.* He stared at it, the wind ruffling his gray hair, his eyes widening in sheer astonishment. He remembered the girl who had brought him cuttings of this very plant years ago, saying it would bloom when he finally allowed himself to rest. He set the pot down gently, his hands trembling as he reached out to touch a petal, the texture velvety and real. A smile broke through his weathered features, transforming his entire demeanor, making him look younger, lighter.
