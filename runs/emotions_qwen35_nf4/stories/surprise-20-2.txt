Detective Miller blinked, looking down at the plain brown package left on the precinct bench where his old mentor, Officer Kael, usually sat during shift changes. The address label was handwritten in familiar, jagged script. He looked around the sterile, fluorescent-lit room, expecting someone to jump out with a camera, but there was only the hum of the vending machine. His pulse spiked, adrenaline mixing with confusion. He lifted the lid, finding a worn copy of *The Count of Monte Cristo*, the book Kael had recommended twenty years ago when they were both rookies. A bookmark fell out, bearing a single date: the day Miller had almost quit. He traced the ink with his thumb, his jaw tightening as a wave of nostalgia hit him like a physical blow, forcing him to lean heavily against the cold metal shelf for support.
