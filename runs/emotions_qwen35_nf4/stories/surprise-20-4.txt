The bell rang, signaling the end of class, but James remained seated, staring at the drone resting on his desk. It was sleek, black, and far more expensive than any toy a high schooler could afford. His teacher, Mrs. Gable, stood nearby, arms crossed, her eyebrows knitted together in a mixture of concern and bewilderment. "Who sent this?" she asked, her voice tight. James shrugged, but his shoulders rose toward his ears, betraying his tension. He couldn't speak. The weight of the moment pressed against his eardrums. He glanced at the empty seat next to him, occupied yesterday by Marcus, who had transferred schools without warning. A sudden warmth spread through James’s chest, loosening the knot in his throat, and he found himself grinning despite the confusion, his fingers brushing nervously over the drone’s propeller.
