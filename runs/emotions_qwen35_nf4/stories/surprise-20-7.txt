The email notification pinged softly on my laptop screen, cutting through the silence of the library. I clicked it, expecting spam, but instead saw an attachment labeled *Project_Final*. Curiosity piqued, I downloaded it. As the file expanded, revealing a complex architectural model I had designed ten years prior, my blood ran cold. Then, the text below appeared: *You were right about the foundation. We built it your way.* I pushed my chair back so hard it screeched against the tile, drawing glares from nearby students. I didn’t notice. My mind raced, replaying lectures, corrections, and dismissals. I scrolled further, finding a video link. Clicking play, I watched a group of engineers standing in front of a completed skyscraper, waving. I covered my face with my hands, letting out a breathless laugh that echoed strangely in the quiet space, my shoulders shaking uncontrollably.
