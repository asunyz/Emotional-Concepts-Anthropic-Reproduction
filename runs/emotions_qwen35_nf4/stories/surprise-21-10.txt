David held the photograph that accompanied the letter, his thumb rubbing over the faded image of two young men smiling on a beach. The letter was from Ben, written in a shaky hand that suggested age or illness. David’s throat constricted, making it difficult to swallow. He placed the photo and letter on the coffee table, stepping back as if afraid they might vanish. He poured himself a drink, the ice cubes clinking loudly in the glass, and drank it in one gulp despite the burn. He looked at the items again, his eyes glistening but refusing to spill over. He picked up the letter, reading it by the dim lamp light, his head bowed low. After finishing, he sat in silence for a long time, staring at the blank television screen, his reflection ghostly and fragmented in the black glass.
