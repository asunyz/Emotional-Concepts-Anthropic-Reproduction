The envelope was delivered to Maya’s office by a courier who waited expectantly. She accepted it, signing the pad with a flourish that lacked its usual confidence. Once the door clicked shut, she broke the seal. The letter was short, only four lines, from her college roommate, Claire. Maya read the first line, then the second, her eyes widening. She let out a soft, involuntary laugh, covering her mouth with her hand, but her eyes remained wide and bright. She spun her chair around, facing the wall, pressing her palms against her cheeks. She peeked through her fingers at the letter lying on her desk, then retrieved it, reading it again. She stood up, walked to the printer, and stared at the machine as if waiting for it to say something. Finally, she sat back down, picked up her pen, and began to write a reply, her hand moving faster than usual, the ink flowing freely onto the page.
