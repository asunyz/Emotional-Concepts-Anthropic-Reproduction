The package arrived without a return address, wrapped in brown paper and twine. Leo carried it inside, frowning at its weight. Inside lay a single envelope containing a handwritten note from his former business partner, Elena. He held the paper at arm’s length, trying to gauge the distance required to read the cursive without straining. His lips parted slightly, but no sound emerged. He walked to the bookshelf, placing the letter on a high shelf before realizing what he was doing, then pulling it back down. His knuckles turned white as he gripped the edges. He read the first paragraph twice, then the second, his head tilting side to side as if searching for a hidden meaning in the spacing between the lines. He laughed, a dry, brittle sound that echoed in the large living room, and ran a hand through his hair, messing up the carefully styled cut.
