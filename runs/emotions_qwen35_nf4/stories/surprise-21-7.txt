Lena found the note tucked inside a library book she had checked out by mistake. The book was a collection of poetry, the one they used to read together before the fight. She flipped through the pages, her fingers trembling enough to make the leaves rustle loudly in the silent study hall. When she reached the bookmark, she saw the folded slip of paper. It was from Mark. She quickly scanned the surroundings, ensuring no one was looking, then unfolded the note. Her breath hitched, a sudden intake of air that sounded like a gasp. She bit her lower lip, drawing blood, a small sting that grounded her. She stood up abruptly, her chair scraping harshly against the floor, causing heads to turn. She mumbled an excuse about forgetting something and hurried toward the exit, the letter pressed flat against her chest, her steps quickening with every meter she covered.
