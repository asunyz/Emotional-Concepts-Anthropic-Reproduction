Elena’s pen hovered over the quarterly report, her knuckles turning white as she gripped the plastic barrel. She stared at the man sitting across from her—Mark, the new logistics coordinator—who was absentmindedly tracing the rim of his coffee mug with a thumb that bore a faint, jagged scar. "That looks familiar," he murmured, noticing her gaze. Elena felt a sudden, electric jolt run down her spine, causing her breath to hitch in her throat. "Where did you get that?" she asked, her voice dropping an octave, losing its usual professional cadence. Mark looked up, eyes widening slightly behind his glasses before crinkling into recognition. "Camp Pine Ridge," he said softly. "Summer of '98." Elena’s chair scraped backward as she stood abruptly, the legs screeching against the linoleum floor. She pressed a hand to her mouth, shaking her head in disbelief, while the hum of the office air conditioning seemed to fade into a distant roar.
