I froze mid-sentence, my mouth still open around the syllable of a project update. David had just mentioned his favorite childhood memory involved building a raft out of pallets and duct tape. My heart hammered against my ribs like a trapped bird, frantic and loud. I knew that specific brand of duct tape—the silver one with the red logo we used back at Camp Whispering Pines. I blinked rapidly, trying to clear the fog that suddenly clouded my vision. "Wait," I stammered, leaning forward until our desks were nearly touching. "Which counselor was it? The one with the beard?" David laughed, a genuine, warm sound that cut through the sterile tension of the breakroom. He pointed to his own chin, where a patchy growth of stubble sat. "Gary. Yeah, Gary." I felt my knees go weak, forcing me to grip the edge of the table for support as the mundane reality of spreadsheets dissolved into the vivid color of lake water and pine needles.
