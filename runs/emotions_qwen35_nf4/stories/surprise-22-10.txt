The email pinged, but Maya didn’t check it. She was too busy reading the signature block of the sender: *Dr. Aris Thorne, Former Director, Camp Willow Creek*. Her pencil snapped in her hand, the wood splintering with a sharp crack that echoed in the empty bullpen. She stared at the screen, her mouth agape, unable to process the information. Her hands began to shake uncontrollably, vibrating against the desk surface. She pushed her chair back, the wheels squeaking harshly, and walked to the window, pressing her forehead against the cool glass. The city skyline blurred into streaks of gray and steel. Behind her, the door opened. "Maya?" Aris’s voice came from the doorway, softer than expected. Maya turned slowly, her eyes wide and glistening with unshed tears. She didn’t speak, simply pointing a trembling finger at the computer screen, her breath coming in short, ragged bursts.
