Jared felt the world narrow down to a single point: the embroidered patch on the stranger’s jacket sleeve. It was a bear holding a fish, stitched in rough, uneven thread. Jared’s breath hitched, catching in his throat like a fishbone. He felt a sudden urge to laugh, but it came out as a strangled choke. He gripped the armrests of his chair, digging his fingers into the fabric until his nails bent. "Who are you?" he managed to whisper, the question escaping before he could filter it. The man across the table, a potential client, leaned forward, intrigued by the intensity in Jared’s eyes. "Just a consultant," the man replied smoothly. Jared shook his head, standing up so quickly his chair fell backward with a loud bang. He walked around the table, ignoring the startled looks of other employees, and extended a hand. "Not anymore," he said, his voice thick with emotion. "Now you’re just Dave from Cabin Seven."
