Marcus dropped his stapler. It clattered loudly onto the desk, bouncing once before rolling under Sarah’s chair. He didn’t bend to retrieve it immediately; instead, he stood rigid, his eyes locked on the lanyard hanging around her neck. Attached to it was a small, handmade charm—a woven bracelet made of blue and yellow paracord. His jaw tightened, and he swallowed hard, his Adam’s apple bobbing visibly. "Is that..." he started, then stopped, clearing his throat roughly. "Is that the Camp Eagle crest pattern?" Sarah followed his gaze, her smile faltering as confusion replaced her pleasant demeanor. She touched the cord nervously. "My grandmother made it," she explained slowly. Marcus let out a short, sharp exhale, running both hands through his hair and ruffling it thoroughly. He paced two steps away, then turned back, his expression stripped of all corporate polish. "We slept in Cabin Four," he whispered, the words barely audible over the typing of nearby keyboards.
