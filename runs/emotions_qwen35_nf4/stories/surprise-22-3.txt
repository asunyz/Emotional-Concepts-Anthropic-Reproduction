The silence between them stretched thin, taut like a rubber band about to snap. Julian stared at the name tag pinned to Lisa’s blouse: *Lisa Chen*. But it wasn't the name that stopped him cold; it was the way she tucked a strand of hair behind her ear, revealing a tiny, silver stud shaped like a canoe. He felt a lump form in his throat, dry and painful. He remembered that exact piece of jewelry from the final night ceremony at Camp Suncrest. His fingers trembled as he reached for his water bottle, fumbling the cap off twice before succeeding. He took a long gulp, buying time, his eyes darting away and then snapping back to hers. "You’re from the coast branch," he stated, not asking. Lisa paused, her fork hovering over her salad. Her pupils dilated, swallowing the iris. "How could you possibly know that?" she breathed, her voice trembling on the last word.
