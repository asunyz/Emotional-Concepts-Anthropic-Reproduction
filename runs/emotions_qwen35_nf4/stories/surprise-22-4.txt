Aisha’s coffee cup slipped from her grasp, shattering on the floor in a shower of brown liquid and ceramic shards. She didn’t move to clean it up. She couldn’t. Her entire body had gone numb, standing rooted to the spot by the sheer absurdity of the moment. Across the aisle, Ben was wiping his glasses, humming a tune that sounded suspiciously like the camp song they’d been forced to sing every evening at Lakeview Retreat. Aisha’s hands flew to her face, covering her mouth as a gasp escaped her nose. She backed up until her lower back hit the filing cabinet, the metal cool against her skin despite the heat rushing to her cheeks. "Ben," she choked out, the name tasting strange on her tongue. He looked up, blinking behind the smudged lenses. "Yeah?" When he saw her face, his own features shifted, the casual slouch vanishing into straight-backed alertness. "Don't tell me," he said, a slow grin spreading across his face. "You were the girl who stole the marshmallows?"
