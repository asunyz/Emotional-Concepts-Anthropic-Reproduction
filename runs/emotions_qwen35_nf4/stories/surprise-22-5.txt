Kevin watched the new hire, Rachel, sketch furiously in her notebook during the budget meeting. Every few minutes, she would pause to draw a small symbol in the margin: a circle with three lines radiating outward. Kevin felt a prickling sensation crawl up the back of his neck, raising the fine hairs there. He squinted, leaning closer to see the drawing more clearly. It was the insignia of Camp Thunderhead. His stomach dropped, a hollow ache forming beneath his ribs. He cleared his throat loudly, breaking the concentration of the room. All heads turned toward him. He ignored them, focusing entirely on Rachel. "Excuse me," he said, his voice cracking slightly. He walked around the table, stopping beside her chair. "May I see that?" he asked, extending a hand. When she hesitated, he added, "Please." As she slid the notebook toward him, he traced the symbol with his finger, his breath catching in a shallow wheeze.
