Natalie stopped breathing. Literally. She held the air in her lungs until her chest burned, staring at the man presenting the slide deck. He wore a distinctive watch—a vintage Casio with a neon green strap. Natalie’s mind flashed back ten years to the bunk beds at Camp Horizon, where her best friend, Mike, had worn that exact model. Her vision blurred, tears pricking the corners of her eyes unbidden. She squeezed her eyes shut for a second, fighting back the wave of nostalgia that threatened to drown her. When she opened them, the presenter was looking directly at her, waiting for feedback. She shook her head, unable to speak, and pointed a shaking finger at his wrist. He glanced down, then back up at her, his eyebrows shooting up toward his hairline. "You know this watch?" he asked, his tone shifting from professional to intensely curious. Natalie nodded vigorously, her throat too tight to form words, clutching the edge of the conference table until her fingernails turned white.
