Leo felt the blood drain from his face, leaving him lightheaded and dizzy. He stared at the poster board held up by his colleague, Anita. In the corner of the diagram, she had doodled a tiny, crude map of the island layout from Camp Cedar Grove. Leo’s hand flew to his forehead, pressing hard against the skin as if trying to push the realization inside his skull. He stumbled back, bumping into a stack of chairs which toppled over with a cacophonous crash. The office fell silent. Leo ignored the noise, his eyes wide and unblinking as he fixed Anita with a stare that bordered on panic. "Anita," he croaked, the sound ragged and foreign. He grabbed the back of a chair to steady himself, his legs trembling violently. "Did you... did you ever swim to the cove on the east side?" he demanded, his voice rising in pitch. Anita lowered the marker, her expression shifting from confusion to dawning horror as she realized what he was implying.
