Samira’s phone buzzed in her pocket, but she ignored it. She was too busy staring at the tattoo peeking out from under the cuff of the intern’s shirt. It was a small, intricate compass rose, inked in black. Samira’s breath shallowed, becoming rapid and shallow. She felt a sudden rush of warmth flood her ears, hot and prickly. She stepped back, putting distance between herself and the young man, whose name tag read *Tyler*. Her heart pounded so hard she feared he might hear it echoing in the quiet library space. She raised a hand, palm out, signaling him to stop talking. "Hold on," she whispered, though he hadn’t spoken yet. She reached up to touch her own wrist, where a similar mark lay hidden beneath layers of skin and time. Tyler noticed her gesture and froze, his pen hovering over his notes. His eyes widened, reflecting the fluorescent lights above, as the connection snapped into place between them.
