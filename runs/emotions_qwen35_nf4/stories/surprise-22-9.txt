David’s coffee went cold in his hand, forgotten. He watched Jordan tie his shoelaces, using a double knot technique that was oddly specific. David’s pulse quickened, thumping rhythmically against his eardrums. He remembered that exact knot from the rope course at Camp Skyward. A wave of vertigo washed over him, making the office walls seem to tilt. He gripped the counter edge tightly, his knuckles bleaching white. "Jordan," he called out, his voice sounding distant to his own ears. Jordan looked up, tying the last loop. He tilted his head, awaiting instruction. David stepped closer, invading personal space without meaning to, drawn by a magnetic pull of shared history. "Show me your left knee," David commanded, surprising even himself with the authority in his tone. Jordan complied, lifting his leg slightly. There, faded but visible, was the scar from the fall during the high ropes challenge. David exhaled sharply, closing his eyes for a brief moment to compose himself.
