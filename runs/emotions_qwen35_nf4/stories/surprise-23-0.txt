Elias dropped his coffee cup, watching dark liquid spread across the pavement as if trying to mimic the expanding circle of disbelief in his mind. His fingers trembled violently against the leather of the wallet he had just picked up from beneath a park bench, the weight of it pulling his hand downward with an unnatural heaviness. He glanced left, then right, the bustling city street suddenly feeling like a stage where he was the only one aware of the script change. When he flipped open the bi-fold, the crisp edges of hundred-dollar bills fanned out like a deck of winning cards, and he felt a cold sweat prickle at the back of his neck despite the midday sun. He shoved the wallet into his coat pocket, zipping it up with frantic jerks, his heart hammering against his ribs like a trapped bird.
