I stared at the stack of cash resting on my kitchen table, the numbers blurring together until they lost all meaning and became just a abstract representation of wealth I didn’t deserve. My breath hitched, catching in my throat as I reached out to touch the paper, expecting it to dissolve or vanish like a mirage. The silence in the apartment grew deafening, amplifying the sound of my own pulse rushing in my ears. I grabbed the edge of the table to steady myself, knuckles turning white, while my eyes darted toward the window as if someone might be watching me from the street below. A laugh bubbled up in my chest, half-hysterical, as I realized I was holding enough money to solve every problem I’d been carrying for years.
