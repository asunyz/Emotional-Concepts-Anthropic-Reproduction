Lena bit her lip until she tasted copper, the pain grounding her as she tried to process the sight before her. The donation box at the church had overflowed, spilling its contents onto the altar steps, and among the coins were several rolled bundles of high-denomination bills. People were filing past, offering their loose change, oblivious to the treasure trove sitting in plain sight. She looked up at the stained-glass windows, the colors bleeding into the sunlight, and felt a profound disconnect from the solemnity of the service. Her hands moved automatically, scooping the rolls into her coat, her movements jerky and urgent, driven by a primal instinct to secure what she had stumbled upon. She whispered a prayer, not of gratitude, but of confusion, asking why fate had chosen this exact moment to test her resolve.
