The stranger handed the wallet back to Julian with a shrug, claiming it had fallen out of his bag at the train station, but Julian knew better. He held the object, feeling the thickness of the cash inside, and watched the man walk away without looking back. The platform was crowded, the noise of arriving trains drowning out his thoughts, yet he felt isolated in a bubble of astonishment. He checked the ID, then the money, then the ID again, searching for a flaw, a catch, anything that would explain the absurdity of the situation. He clutched the wallet to his chest, feeling the warmth of the paper seep through his shirt, and hurried toward the exit, his pace faster than anyone else’s, eager to escape the scrutiny of the crowd before his secret became too heavy to carry.
