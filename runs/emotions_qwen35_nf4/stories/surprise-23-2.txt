Detective Miller’s pen hovered over the report form, the ink pooling slightly under the pressure of her grip before she snapped the cap shut. She hadn’t expected the evidence bag to contain this much liquidity; the sheer volume seemed to defy logic within the context of the petty theft case file. Her eyebrows knit together tightly, creating deep furrows between them as she recounted the bills again, just to be sure the scale clerk wasn’t mistaken. She looked at the suspect, who sat slumped in the chair, looking confused rather than guilty, and felt a sudden shift in the room’s atmosphere that made the air feel thin. "Check the serial numbers," she ordered sharply, her voice cracking slightly, betraying the shock that had frozen her usual stoic demeanor.
