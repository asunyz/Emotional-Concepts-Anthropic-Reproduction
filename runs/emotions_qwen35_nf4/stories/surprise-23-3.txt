The rain lashed against the windshield, but Sarah barely noticed, her gaze locked on the reflection of the thick bundle of currency in the rearview mirror. She slammed on the brakes, the tires screeching against the wet asphalt, bringing the car to a halt in the middle of the empty highway. Her hands gripped the steering wheel so hard her joints popped, and she took a shallow, ragged breath that tasted of stale air and panic. For a moment, she considered driving straight off the road, driven by a dizzying sense of unreality that made the world around her tilt on its axis. Then, she reached back, pulled the wallet from her purse, and buried her face in it, inhaling the scent of old leather and new money, tears streaming down her cheeks without her permission.
