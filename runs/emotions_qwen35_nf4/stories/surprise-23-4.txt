Marcus laughed, a sharp, barking sound that echoed off the brick walls of the alleyway, because the man lying at his feet had slipped him two thousand dollars instead of five. He kicked the unconscious figure lightly, checking for a pulse out of habit, then looked down at the crumpled bills sticking out of his jacket lining. His stomach churned with a mixture of nausea and exhilaration, the adrenaline flooding his veins making his fingertips tingle. He scanned the shadows for any witnesses, his eyes wide and unblinking, before stuffing the cash into his sock. The ground beneath his feet felt less solid now, as though he were walking on cloud tops, disconnected from the gritty reality of the streets he called home.
