She counted the notes three times, her tongue poking out the corner of her mouth in concentration, though the math remained stubbornly simple. The library was quiet, save for the rhythmic ticking of the clock on the wall, which sounded louder than usual, marking time she suddenly felt she had plenty of. Her shoulders hunched inward, protecting the object in her lap, and she avoided eye contact with the librarian who kept glancing her way. A warm flush crept up her neck, spreading across her cheeks, as she realized the implications of finding such a fortune in a place meant for borrowing knowledge, not capital. She quickly closed the book she had been pretending to read and tucked the wallet inside, standing up abruptly and knocking her chair backward with a clatter.
