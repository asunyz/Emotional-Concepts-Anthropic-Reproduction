The CEO stood by the floor-to-ceiling window, staring at the skyline that usually inspired confidence, now rendering him mute. The envelope on his desk contained more cash than his monthly bonus, found neatly placed next to his morning briefing. He loosened his tie, the fabric feeling tight around his throat, and paced the length of the office, his shoes clicking loudly on the hardwood. He picked up the phone, put it down, picked it up again, unable to form a coherent sentence with whoever he intended to call. The magnitude of the error—or perhaps the bribe—hung in the air, heavy and suffocating, making the luxurious surroundings feel like a gilded cage. He sank into his chair, head in his hands, wondering how long it would take for the authorities to notice the discrepancy.
