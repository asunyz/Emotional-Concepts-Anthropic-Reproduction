I couldn't stop smiling, even when I walked into the grocery store, the grin stretching my face until it ached. The cashier rang up my items with bored efficiency, unaware that I was carrying the solution to my rent crisis in my breast pocket. Every step felt lighter, buoyant, as if gravity had temporarily released its hold on me. I hummed a tune I hadn’t heard since childhood, tapping my foot against the linoleum floor, drawing a strange look from the elderly woman behind me. When I got home, I poured the bills onto the bed, arranging them in neat rows like soldiers, marveling at the texture and color, feeling a surge of power that made me want to shout from the rooftops.
