The detective wiped his glasses, putting them back on and taking them off again, hoping the second glance would reveal a trick of the light. The safe house was damp and cold, yet he felt hot with confusion as he examined the duffel bag left behind by the fleeing informant. It wasn’t drugs or weapons, but stacks of unmarked bills, wrapped in rubber bands. He ran a finger along the edge of the stack, the friction sending a tiny spark of static up his arm, and he shivered. The narrative he had built for weeks collapsed in an instant, leaving him standing alone in the dim light, his mind racing to reconstruct a scenario that could possibly explain such an anomaly. He looked at his partner, whose expression mirrored his own bewilderment, and simply shook his head.
