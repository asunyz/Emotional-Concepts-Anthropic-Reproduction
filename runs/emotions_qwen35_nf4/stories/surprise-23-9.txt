He froze in the doorway, one foot still on the threshold of his apartment, the other hovering in the hallway. The envelope had been slid under his door during the night, a stark contrast to the usual pile of junk mail. With trembling fingers, he tore the seal, revealing the dense layer of cash that filled the space completely. His knees buckled slightly, forcing him to lean against the wall for support, his breath coming in short, shallow gasps. He looked around his modest living room, seeing it anew—not as a struggle, but as a temporary station on a journey he was about to accelerate dramatically. The mundane objects on his shelf seemed to shrink in significance, dwarfed by the potential energy radiating from the paper in his hand.
