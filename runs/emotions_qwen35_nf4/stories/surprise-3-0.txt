Elias stared at the glossy cover, his thumb tracing the embossed title *The Silent Architect*. The library air felt suddenly thin, as if he had been pulled from his seat into a vacuum. He flipped to page forty-two, where the author described his decision to leave his wife ten years ago—a decision Elias had never spoken aloud, not even to himself until that very morning. His breath hitched, a sharp intake of air that rattled in his chest. He scanned the next paragraph, detailing his secret fear of failure, words that matched the exact phrasing of his private journal entry from last Tuesday. The pen in his hand slipped, clattering onto the hard floor, the sound echoing like a gunshot in the quiet reading room. He looked around, eyes wide, searching for someone watching him, but the other patrons remained oblivious behind their stacks of books.
