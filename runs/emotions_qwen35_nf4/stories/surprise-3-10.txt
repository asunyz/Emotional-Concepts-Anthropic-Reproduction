He found the book tucked inside his coat pocket. He hadn't put it there. He patted his pockets, feeling the hardcover edge. Pulling it out, he saw the familiar binding. It was his own life, summarized in three hundred pages. The last chapter ended with him finding the book. He flipped to the end, looking for the epilogue. There was none. Just blank pages. He turned the book over, flipping through the front matter, searching for an explanation. His breathing grew shallow, rapid. He paced the length of his study, the book burning a hole in his hand. He stopped at the window, staring out at the rain-slicked streets, wondering if the next step he took was pre-written or chosen by free will.
