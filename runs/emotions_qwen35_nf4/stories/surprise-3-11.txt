The letter arrived on a Tuesday. No stamp, no postmark. Just his name written in elegant cursive. Inside was a single page. *You are reading this because you are curious,* it began. *But curiosity killed the cat.* Below that was a list of secrets he had kept since childhood. The most recent one was hidden in the walls of his house, behind the baseboard in the hallway. He froze, the paper slipping from his grasp. He walked slowly toward the hallway, his movements stiff. He knelt before the baseboard, his fingers hovering over the loose panel. If he opened it, would the book update? Would it tell him what he would find? He held his breath, waiting for the urge to act, paralyzed by the anticipation of the reveal.
