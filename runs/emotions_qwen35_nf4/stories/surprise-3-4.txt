The envelope slid under the door with a soft whisper. John kicked it aside, assuming it was junk mail. But when he bent down to retrieve it, the return address caught his eye: *Omniscient Press*. Inside was a single sheet of paper, summarizing his entire life up to yesterday afternoon. It mentioned the sandwich he ate for lunch, the thought he had about quitting his job, the lie he told his mother on the phone. He sat back on his heels, the paper crinkling in his fist. The air in the apartment felt heavy, suffocating. He looked at the clock on the wall. The second hand ticked forward. Did the book predict what he would do next? He waited, holding his breath, watching the minute hand creep toward the next hour, paralyzed by the sudden, crushing weight of being watched.
