"I wrote that?" Lena whispered, her voice cracking. She held the book up to the light, turning the pages frantically. The description of her first kiss matched the sensory details she had recorded in her dream journal—the scent of lavender, the taste of salt, the sound of distant thunder. But she had never shared that journal. She had burned it. Hadn't she? A chill crawled up her spine, raising goosebumps on her arms. She rushed to the fireplace, checking the ashes. Nothing but gray dust. She returned to the sofa, sinking into the cushions as if her legs had given way. Her eyes darted between the book and the dark corners of the room, wondering who else might be reading her mind right now.
