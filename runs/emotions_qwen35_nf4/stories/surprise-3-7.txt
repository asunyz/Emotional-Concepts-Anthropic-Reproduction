The stranger handed him the pamphlet. "For you," he said, smiling too widely. Marcus took it, confused. The title was his name. The subtitle: *A Memoir of Betrayal*. He opened it to find a photograph of himself sleeping, taken from above. The date below the photo was tomorrow. He looked up, wanting to ask questions, but the stranger was already walking away, blending into the crowd on the busy street. Marcus clutched the pamphlet to his chest, his heart racing. He checked his watch. The time matched the timestamp in the book exactly. He felt exposed, naked in the middle of the bustling city, every passerby seeming to glance at him with knowing eyes. He started running, not knowing where he was going, just needing to get away from the gaze that followed him everywhere.
