She traced the line of text with her finger, stopping at the word *regret*. It was capitalized. Why was it capitalized? The biography claimed she had always known she was unhappy in her marriage. But she hadn't. Not until today. Until she saw this book. The realization hit her like a physical blow to the solar plexus. She gasped, doubling over slightly. The book sat on the kitchen counter, innocent and terrifying. She picked it up, intending to tear the pages, but her hands refused to obey. Fear rooted her to the spot. She looked at the reflection in the microwave door—pale, wide-eyed—and wondered if the narrator was inside her head, guiding her thoughts, or if she was merely a character in a script she hadn't agreed to perform.
