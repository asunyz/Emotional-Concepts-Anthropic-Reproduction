The editor slammed the proof onto the desk. "Sign here," she commanded. Leo hesitated, scanning the final draft. There was a passage about his brother’s disappearance. The book provided a motive. Leo frowned, leaning closer. He had suspected foul play, yes, but he had never considered greed. The text laid out the financial motives with surgical precision. His brow furrowed deeply, creasing his forehead. He looked up at the editor, who was tapping her foot impatiently. "Is something wrong?" she asked. He shook his head slowly, unable to form words. The silence stretched, thick and uncomfortable. He signed the paper, his signature jagged and uneven, the pen tearing slightly through the fiber of the page.
