Sarah stood frozen on her porch, the screen door creaking softly behind her, forgotten. Her breath hitched in her throat, catching on the sudden realization that the overgrown hedge she’d spent years pruning was gone. In its place stood a crew of men in high-vis vests, moving with a synchronized efficiency that felt alien against the backdrop of her sleepy cul-de-sac. She took a step forward, then another, her bare feet pressing cold into the concrete. Her heart hammered against her ribs, a frantic bird trapped in a cage. "When?" she whispered to the empty air, though she knew no one could hear her over the roar of a heavy-duty excavator. She reached out to touch the rough bark of the nearest remaining oak, needing the anchor of reality before the rest of the world shifted beneath her feet again.
