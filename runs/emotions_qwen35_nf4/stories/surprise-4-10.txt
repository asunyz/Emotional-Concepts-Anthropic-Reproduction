The drone shot was supposed to be routine. But when the feed stabilized on the neighbor’s yard, Leo choked on his own saliva. The footage showed a fully constructed deck, complete with string lights and potted plants, where only grass had existed minutes prior. He scrubbed the timeline back, forward, back again. Nothing changed. The edit hadn’t happened; the reality had. He spun around in his chair, scanning his own room as if looking for hidden cameras. Was this a prank? A hologram? He pinched his arm hard, wincing at the sting. The pain was real. The news was real. He grabbed his jacket, throwing it over his shoulder, and bolted out the door, needing to see the wood grain with his own eyes, to verify that the world hadn’t glitched out of existence.
