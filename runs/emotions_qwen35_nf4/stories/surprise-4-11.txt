Rain poured down, masking the tears that finally spilled over. Maya stood on her balcony, watching the sledgehammer swing. Each impact sent a shower of debris cascading down the side of the old church tower next door. She had attended services there since childhood; the bell had rung her to school, to weddings, to funerals. Now, it was falling apart. Her hands clenched into fists at her sides, nails biting into her skin. The cold rain soaked through her thin sweater, chilling her to the bone, but she barely noticed. Her mind reeled, trying to process the finality of the destruction. It was done in seconds, yet it felt like a lifetime of memories evaporating in the storm. She slid down the railing, sitting on the wet concrete, utterly stunned by the speed of erasure.
