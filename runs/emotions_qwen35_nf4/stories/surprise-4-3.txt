The knocking on the wall was rhythmic, persistent, and entirely new. David froze mid-bite, his fork hovering near his mouth. The vibration traveled through the floorboards, up through the soles of his slippers, and settled deep in his chest. He set the plate down slowly, the ceramic clinking softly against the saucer, a stark contrast to the industrial pounding next door. He walked to the shared wall, pressing his ear against the cool plaster. Dust motes danced in the shaft of sunlight that suddenly seemed brighter without the shadow of Mrs. Gable’s garden shed. He pulled away quickly, stumbling back toward the window. Through the blinds, he watched a crane lift a massive beam into the air, twisting slowly against the gray sky. His stomach turned over, a nauseating lurch that made him grip the windowsill for support.
