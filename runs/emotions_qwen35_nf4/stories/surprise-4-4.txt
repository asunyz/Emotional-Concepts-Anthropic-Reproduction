Lena’s phone slipped from her grasp, shattering on the hardwood floor. She didn’t move to clean it up. All her attention was fixed on the driveway next door, which was no longer a driveway but a construction zone marked off by bright orange cones. Her father had moved out six months ago; she had assumed the house would sit empty, gathering dust. Instead, it was alive with activity. Workers carried sheets of drywall with casual ease, laughing as they navigated the narrow path. Lena pressed her hand to her mouth, stifling a gasp that threatened to escape. Her knees felt weak, forcing her to lean heavily against the doorframe. How? Why? The questions swirled in her head, dizzying and rapid, leaving her breathless and staring at the impossible reality unfolding ten feet away.
