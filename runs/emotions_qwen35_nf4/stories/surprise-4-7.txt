Tommy woke up to the sound of drilling. Not the gentle tap-tap of a hammer, but the aggressive whine of power tools piercing the early morning calm. He threw off his covers, stumbling into the hallway in his pajamas. The walls vibrated with every strike. He rushed to the window, peering through the gap in the curtains. Next door, the garage had been demolished. In its place, a pile of rubble sat undisturbed, awaiting the next phase. Tommy’s hands flew to his face, covering his mouth. His eyes widened, pupils dilating until the iris was nearly swallowed by black. He backed away from the window, bumping into the dresser, the pain grounding him slightly. This was wrong. This was a violation of the unspoken rules of their street. He paced the length of the bedroom, unable to settle, his energy buzzing like a live wire.
