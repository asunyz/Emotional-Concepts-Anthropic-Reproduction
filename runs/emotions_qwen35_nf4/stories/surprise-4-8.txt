The letter arrived in the mail, crisp and official-looking. Elena tore it open with trembling fingers, expecting a bill or a notice. Instead, she found a demolition schedule. Her vision blurred, the text swimming before her eyes. She read it again, slower this time, parsing each syllable. *Property adjacent to...* No. That was her house. Or rather, it used to be. The shock hit her like a physical blow, doubling her over slightly. She leaned against the kitchen island, breathing shallowly. The clock on the wall ticked louder than usual, marking seconds that felt elongated and strange. Outside, the rumble of a bulldozer started up, low and guttural. She flinched, her shoulders hunching up toward her ears. It was happening. Right here. Right now.
