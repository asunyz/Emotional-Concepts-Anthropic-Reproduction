Jack paused on the sidewalk, his walking stick tapping rhythmically against the pavement. He tilted his head, listening. The birds were gone. The usual chorus of sparrows and robins had been replaced by the harsh mechanical growl of generators. He shuffled forward, his cane leading the way, and stopped abruptly. The old library building, scheduled for preservation, was being prepped for structural reinforcement. Tarps covered what was once the grand entrance, hiding its history behind synthetic fabric. Jack removed his glasses, cleaning them frantically with his shirttail, hoping the smudge was obscuring his view. When he put them back on, the scene remained unchanged. His breath came in short, ragged bursts. He felt a profound sense of loss mixed with bewildered awe, standing alone amidst the encroaching modernity.
