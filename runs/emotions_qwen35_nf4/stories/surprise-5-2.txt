The air left Leo’s lungs in a sharp hiss. He stood frozen in the aisle between a rack of denim jackets and a shelf of electronics, his gaze fixed on the jewelry box open on the table. Inside lay the emerald-cut diamond ring Grandmother Rose had promised would one day belong to her grandson. It shouldn’t be here. It couldn’t be. Leo’s throat tightened, making it difficult to swallow. He took a step forward, then another, moving as if in a trance. When he finally reached out to touch the stone, his hand hovered inches above it, shaking visibly, unable to bridge the gap between memory and the stark reality of the pawn ticket taped to the bottom of the box.
