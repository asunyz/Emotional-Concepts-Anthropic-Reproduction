Tom’s jaw went slack. He stood there, mouth slightly agape, staring at the object on the counter. The ring was tarnished, scratched, and utterly alone. He felt a surge of heat rush to his cheeks, followed by a wave of dizziness that forced him to grip the doorframe for support. This was the ring Grandfather Arthur had given Grandmother Martha. It was supposed to be buried with her. Tom swallowed hard, the lump in his throat painful. He reached out, his movements jerky and unnatural, and picked it up. The weight of it felt wrong, too heavy for its size, anchoring him to the spot while his mind scrambled to process the impossibility of the moment.
