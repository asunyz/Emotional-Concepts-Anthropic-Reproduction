Lena let out a short, sharp laugh that sounded more like a sob. She covered her mouth with her hand, eyes widening behind her glasses. The ring was exactly where her grandmother had described leaving it in her will—a secret hiding place. But instead of being safe in a bank vault, it was displayed under glass next to a pair of handcuffs. Lena’s legs gave way, and she slumped onto a nearby stool, her hands flying to her head. She rocked back and forth slightly, muttering to herself, repeating the address of the pawn shop over and over again as if saying it aloud would make the situation dissolve into mist.
