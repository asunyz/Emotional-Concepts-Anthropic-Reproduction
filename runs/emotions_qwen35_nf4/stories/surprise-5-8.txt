Marcus felt the color drain from his face. He stood rigid, his posture stiffening until he resembled a statue. The ring lay on a bed of cotton balls, gleaming innocently. He knew that cut. He knew the setting style. He knew the story attached to it. Marcus clenched his fists at his sides, his fingernails digging into his palms hard enough to leave crescent-shaped marks. He stared at the price tag, then at the ring, then at the ceiling, trying to find an exit route from the suffocating disbelief pressing down on him. He turned abruptly and walked out, leaving the door swinging behind him, his breath coming in ragged, uneven gasps.
