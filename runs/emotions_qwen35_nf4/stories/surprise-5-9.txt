Ava’s pen slipped from her fingers, rolling off the desk and disappearing into the shadows. She hadn’t noticed. Her attention was entirely consumed by the view outside the window, where the pawn shop sign flickered. No, wait—it wasn’t the sign. It was the display case. She squinted, leaning closer to the glass, her nose almost touching the pane. There it was. The heirloom. Ava’s stomach dropped, a sickening lurch that made her grab the back of her chair for stability. She pressed her forehead against the cool window, watching the clerk polish the ring with a cloth, feeling a profound sense of violation mixed with sheer bewilderment.
