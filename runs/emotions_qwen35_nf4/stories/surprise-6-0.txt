Elias stared at the screen until his eyes burned, the blue light etching hollows beneath his sockets. His finger hovered over the refresh button, trembling slightly, before he pulled back as if the mouse were red-hot. He stood up abruptly, knocking his chair backward against the wall with a dull thud that echoed in the empty dorm room. The silence that followed felt heavy, pressing against his eardrums. He walked to the window, gripping the sill until his knuckles turned white, watching the rain blur the streetlights below into streaks of gray and yellow. Why hadn’t he expected this? It made no sense. The essay had been perfect, the grades impeccable. He pressed his forehead against the cold glass, waiting for the world to right itself, but the reflection staring back looked unfamiliar, stripped of its future certainty.
