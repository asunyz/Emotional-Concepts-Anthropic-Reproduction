Kevin stared at the admission portal, the loading spinner spinning endlessly, mirroring the chaos in his head. When the status finally updated to 'Denied,' he didn't move. He remained seated, frozen in the ergonomic chair, his hands resting lightly on the mousepad. The click of the mouse seemed miles away. He blinked, once, twice, trying to reboot his perception of reality. He stood up, walking to the bookshelf lined with awards and certificates, touching the spines of his college brochures. They felt flimsy now, like props from a play that had been canceled. He pulled out a notebook, flipping through pages of goals and timelines, crossing them out with violent strokes of his pen. The scratch of the nib on paper was loud, aggressive. He needed to plan, to pivot, to survive, but his mind was a static-filled radio, unable to tune into a new frequency.
