Lila dropped the letter onto the dining table, the paper skidding across the polished surface. She backed away, putting distance between herself and the document as if it contained radiation. Her breath came in shallow gasps, ragged and uneven. She looked around the room, at the family photos, the vase of flowers, the normalcy of her surroundings, and felt utterly disconnected from it all. How does one process the erasure of a planned future? She walked to the sink, turning on the tap, letting the water run cold over her wrists. The shock of the temperature grounded her momentarily. She splashed her face, droplets clinging to her eyelashes. When she looked up in the mirror, her expression was unreadable, masked by a layer of water and shock. She dried her face roughly, towel rubbing skin raw, needing sensation to override the numbness spreading through her limbs. The world outside continued its rotation, oblivious to the fracture in her personal orbit.
