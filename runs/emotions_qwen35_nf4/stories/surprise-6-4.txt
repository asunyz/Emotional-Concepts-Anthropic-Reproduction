The notification pinged on Leo’s tablet while he was mid-bite into a sandwich. He chewed slowly, deliberately, buying time. One bite. Two bites. Swallow. Then he unlocked the screen. The rejection letter loaded instantly, mocking him with its speed. His jaw tightened, muscles bunching near his ears. He set the half-eaten sandwich down on a napkin, losing all appetite suddenly. The food now looked unappealing, greasy and dry. He scrolled past the initial shock to the detailed feedback section, reading every word twice, dissecting phrases that meant nothing yet everything. "Lack of distinctive narrative." Was that it? He felt a strange detachment, as if he were observing another person’s failure from a distance. He rubbed his temples, massaging the skin until it tingled, trying to massage the confusion out of his brain. The clock on the wall ticked loudly, each second stretching into an eternity of uncertainty.
