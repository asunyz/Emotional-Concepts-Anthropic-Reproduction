David laughed when he saw the subject line. "Scholarship Update?" He assumed it was good news, maybe a bonus round. He clicked open the attachment with a grin already forming. As he read the first sentence, the smile froze, then vanished, leaving his face slack. He leaned closer to the monitor, squinting, as if poor vision were the culprit. He refreshed the page. Same result. He refreshed again. Nothing changed. A cold sweat prickled along his hairline despite the cool temperature of the server room he worked in. He pushed his rolling chair back, wheels screeching against the linoleum. He ran a hand through his hair, messing it up completely. This wasn't happening. The universe had made a clerical error. He typed out a reply, deleted it, typed it again, his fingers flying across the keyboard in a panic, only to stop and stare at the blinking cursor, realizing there was no one to argue with.
