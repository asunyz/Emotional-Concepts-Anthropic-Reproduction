Maya placed the phone on the table, screen facing up, as if the message might disappear if she didn't acknowledge it directly. Her heart rate accelerated, visible in the rapid flutter of her carotid artery. She reached for her water bottle, unscrewing the cap with trembling fingers, but forgot to drink. She just held it, the condensation cooling her palm. The implications cascaded through her thoughts like falling dominoes: rent, textbooks, the dream of graduate school—all toppling over one another. She stood up and began to pace, the narrow hallway of her apartment offering little room for escape. She checked her bank balance app next, numbers flashing red in her mind’s eye. She bit her lip until she tasted copper, anchoring herself in the pain to keep the spiral at bay. The house was silent except for the hum of the refrigerator, a mundane sound that felt absurdly indifferent to her crisis.
