Tom crumpled the printed email into a tight ball, his grip tightening until the paper groaned. He tossed it into the trash can, missed, and watched it land on the floor. He didn't bend to pick it up. Instead, he walked to the fridge and opened it, staring blankly at the contents. Milk. Eggs. Leftover pizza. Normalcy. Ordinary things existing in a world that had just shifted beneath his feet. He slammed the door shut, the noise jarring. He leaned his forehead against the cool metal of the appliance, closing his eyes. How could he have misread the signs? Or had he simply hoped too hard? The denial felt like a physical blow to the solar plexus, wind knocked out of him. He slid down the cabinet doors to sit on the kitchen tiles, wrapping his arms around himself. The silence of the house pressed in, demanding an answer he didn't have.
