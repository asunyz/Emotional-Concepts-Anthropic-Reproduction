Ananya read the email aloud to her cat, Miso, whose green eyes watched her with detached curiosity. "Dear Ananya... we regret to inform you..." Her voice wavered, pitching higher with each syllable. She stopped speaking, setting the laptop aside. Miso jumped onto the desk, batting at the edge of the screen. Ananya pushed the cat off gently, her movements jerky and uncoordinated. She felt lightheaded, the room tilting slightly to the left. She gripped the edge of the desk to steady herself, her nails digging into the wood grain. It wasn't supposed to be this way. She had followed every rule, submitted every document on time. The injustice bubbled up in her chest, hot and acidic. She swallowed hard, forcing down the lump in her throat. She looked out the window at the busy street below, people rushing to destinations she was now unsure she could reach.
