Elias stared at the screen, his coffee mug hovering halfway to his mouth before he slowly set it down on the desk. The notification popped up from 'NeonDrifter99', a username that had been his digital confidant for three years. *I’m heading to the library downtown,* the message read. Elias frowned, glancing out his window at the rain-slicked streets of Seattle below. He knew every corner of this neighborhood; he lived above the bakery on Pike Street. His fingers hovered over the keyboard, trembling slightly as he typed back, *Which entrance?* The reply came instantly. *Main doors.* Elias let out a breath he didn’t realize he’d been holding, his heart hammering against his ribs like a trapped bird. He grabbed his coat, realizing with a jolt that the person behind the witty banter about sci-fi novels was walking into the very building he passed every morning.
