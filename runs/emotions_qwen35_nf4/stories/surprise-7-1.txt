Maya slammed her laptop shut, the sudden noise startling her cat off the bed. She paced across the living room rug, running her hands through her hair until it stood on end. On her phone, the chat log with 'SkyWalker_X' scrolled endlessly. They had planned their first video call for tonight at eight. Just minutes ago, SkyWalker_X had sent a photo of their view—a distinctive red brick church steeple visible through a rainy window. Maya froze mid-step. That steeple was right outside her own apartment complex in Chicago. She rushed to the window, peering through the blinds. Sure enough, there was the familiar spire, glowing faintly under the streetlights. Her stomach did a slow flip, a mixture of excitement and terror bubbling up in her throat. She wasn't just talking to someone across the country anymore; she was sharing air with them.
