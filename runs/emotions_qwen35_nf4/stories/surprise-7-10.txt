Leo hesitated at the door of the community center, his hand resting on the handle. He was meeting 'RunnerX' for a marathon training run. RunnerX had always insisted on meeting at the park gates, claiming they lived nearby. Leo pushed the door open and stepped inside, expecting to see the park entrance. Instead, he found himself in a hallway lined with lockers. At the end of the hall, a figure was tying their shoelaces. Leo walked forward, his steps echoing loudly. The figure looked up, revealing a face Leo recognized from the local news segment on charity runs. It was Councilman Davis. Leo stopped abruptly, his brain struggling to reconcile the image of the stern politician with the encouraging, joke-cracking online persona. "You too?" Leo asked, his voice barely above a whisper. The Councilman grinned, standing up. "Guess we’re neighbors now," he said. Leo felt a strange flutter in his chest, a mix of disbelief and delight at the hidden layer of his community.
