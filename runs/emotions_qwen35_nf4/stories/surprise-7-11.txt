Nina froze in the aisle of the bookstore, her finger tracing the spine of a mystery novel. Behind her, a voice murmured, "Great choice." Nina turned around, expecting another shopper. Instead, she faced 'MysteryMan', the author of the blog she had anonymously commented on for years. He was standing there, holding the same book she was reaching for. Nina’s eyes widened, her mouth opening and closing like a fish. She had praised his plot twists in a comment section earlier that day, calling him a genius. Now, he was looking at her with amused recognition. "I recognize those comments," he said softly. Nina felt heat rush to her ears, her pulse jumping in her neck. She nodded frantically, unable to form a coherent sentence. The barrier between critic and creator, between distant observer and present participant, had shattered in the quiet dust motes of the fiction section.
