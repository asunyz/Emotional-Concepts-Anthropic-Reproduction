Lena dropped her keys, the metallic clatter echoing loudly in the quiet subway station. She bent down to retrieve them, her mind still reeling from the text exchange with 'MoonChild'. They had been discussing the best spots for stargazing, mentioning specific coordinates only locals would know. Lena had assumed MoonChild was in Vermont. Now, standing on Platform 4 of the Boston T, she watched the train pull in. The digital display flashed 'Next Stop: Park Street'. Park Street. That was where MoonChild had said they’d be waiting. Lena’s hand gripped the cold metal pole beside her, knuckles turning white. She scanned the crowd of rushing commuters, looking for anyone wearing the silver moon pendant described in their last conversation. The world seemed to shrink around her, the noise of the arriving train fading into a dull roar as she processed the impossible proximity of her closest online companion.
