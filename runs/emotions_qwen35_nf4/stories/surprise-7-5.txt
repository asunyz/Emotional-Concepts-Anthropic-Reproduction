Clara’s pen slipped from her fingers, rolling across the polished table and coming to rest against her guest’s shoe. She looked up, ready to apologize profusely, but the words stuck in her throat. The woman sitting across from her, whom Clara had met via the 'BookWorms' forum, was wearing the exact same vintage scarf Clara had admired in her profile picture. "I think you dropped this," the woman said, handing the pen back with a gentle smile. Clara took it, her fingers brushing against the other woman’s skin, sending a shiver up her arm. She had spent hours debating literary theory with this person, never suspecting they were sitting in the same coffee shop in Portland. Clara’s cheeks flushed hot, and she quickly looked down at her book, pretending to read while her heart raced wildly. The anonymity of the internet had collapsed in an instant, replaced by the tangible reality of shared space.
