Marcus nearly tripped over his own feet as he turned the corner onto 5th Avenue. He was arguing playfully with 'PixelPunk' about the latest graphic novel release. "You have terrible taste," PixelPunk had typed just seconds ago. Marcus looked up from his phone, scowling at the traffic light. Standing directly in front of him, waiting for the crosswalk signal, was a teenager with neon blue hair and headphones around their neck. They looked exactly like their avatar. Marcus stopped dead in his tracks, his jaw dropping open. The teenager noticed him staring, lowered their headphones, and raised an eyebrow. "What?" they asked. Marcus couldn’t speak. He just pointed a shaking finger at them, then at his phone, then back at them. The sheer absurdity of the situation made his head spin. Here he was, having a heated debate with someone who could reach out and touch him if they chose to.
