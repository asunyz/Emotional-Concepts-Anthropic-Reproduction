Sophie paused mid-stride on the crowded sidewalk, her breath catching in her throat. She was texting 'NightOwl', asking if they wanted to grab dinner later. NightOwl replied, *Sure, I’m already at Luigi’s.* Sophie’s eyes widened. Luigi’s was the Italian restaurant directly across from her office building. She looked up from her phone, scanning the windows of the establishment. Through the glass, she saw a familiar laugh—wide, infectious, and accompanied by a distinctive scar above the left eyebrow. It was the same laugh she heard in the voice notes NightOwl sent during their long calls. Sophie leaned against the wall, sliding down slightly until she was seated on the curb. People streamed past her, oblivious to the fact that she was mentally recalibrating her entire understanding of friendship. The distance between her screen and the real world had vanished without warning.
