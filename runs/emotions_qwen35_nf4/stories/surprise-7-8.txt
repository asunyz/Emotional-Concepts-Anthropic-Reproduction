Ryan stared at the reflection in the mirror, his hand gripping the edge of the sink tightly. He had just received a package from 'ArtSupplyCo', the anonymous vendor who sold rare paints to his online art group. Inside was a note: *Hope these help your next piece. - Alex.* Ryan frowned. He had never told ArtSupplyCo his name. Or had he? He dug through his inbox, finding an old email thread where he had accidentally included his address in a shipping confirmation. The return address was just two blocks away. Ryan spun around, pacing the small length of his bathroom. He walked to the window, looking out at the neighboring apartment building. In one of the top-floor windows, he saw a silhouette painting. It moved with the same rhythmic hesitation he used when mixing colors. He pressed his forehead against the cool glass, stunned by the intimacy of being known by someone whose home was practically next door.
