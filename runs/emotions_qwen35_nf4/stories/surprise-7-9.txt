Amara dropped her grocery bag, oranges rolling across the pavement toward the curb. She cursed softly, bending down to gather them. As she reached for the last one, a hand extended from the side, picking it up before she could. She looked up to see a young man smiling down at her. "Careful there," he said. Amara recognized the voice instantly. It was 'TechGuru', the moderator of the coding forum she frequented daily. He wore the same band t-shirt featured in his forum signature. Amara stood up slowly, clutching the orange to her chest. "You’re... TechGuru?" she managed to squeak out. He chuckled, a sound she had heard in countless video tutorials. "And you’re the guy who asks the most questions," he replied, winking. Amara felt lightheaded, the busy street noise fading into background static. The mentor she respected from afar was now standing inches away, smelling of citrus and rain.
