Elias stared at the pile of lumber stacked against his lawn, his coffee mug hovering halfway to his mouth before he lowered it slowly, as if afraid to break a spell. The air smelled of fresh pine and sawdust, sharp and unfamiliar against the usual scent of Mrs. Gable’s lavender bushes. He blinked, trying to reconcile the empty space from yesterday with the towering wooden slats now encroaching on what used to be their shared garden bed. His breath hitched in his throat, a sudden tightness behind his ribs that made him step back until his heels hit the porch railing. "I didn't know you were doing this," he said, the words coming out thinner than he intended, echoing slightly in the quiet morning air.
