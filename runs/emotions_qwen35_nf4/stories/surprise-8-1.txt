Maya dropped her keys when she heard the jackhammer’s rhythmic thud vibrating through the floorboards of her apartment. She froze, one hand still on the doorknob, listening to the noise cut through the peaceful hum of the city. Through the window, she watched Mr. Henderson hammering post after post into the earth right along the property line they had carefully respected for five years. Her stomach did a strange flip-flop, a cold knot forming just below her navel. She pulled the curtain shut, then immediately opened it again, unable to look away from the rising barrier that was slowly erasing her view of the oak tree.
