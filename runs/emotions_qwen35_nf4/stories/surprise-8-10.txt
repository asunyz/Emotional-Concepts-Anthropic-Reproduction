The smell of stain seeped through the cracked window frame, overpowering the aroma of brewing tea. Marcus stepped onto the balcony, squinting against the glare off the freshly painted wood. It was a color he hated—too bright, too aggressive. He leaned over the railing, looking down at the worker applying the varnish with meticulous care. His hands trembled slightly, causing the saucer to clink against the cup. He pulled his hand back sharply, hiding it in his pocket, while his mind raced through legal clauses and property rights he vaguely remembered studying years ago.
