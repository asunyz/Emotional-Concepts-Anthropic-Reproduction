Lena found herself holding her breath as the final nail was driven into the top rail. The sound was singular and definitive, a sharp crack that severed the connection between her yard and theirs. She stood in the doorway, one foot inside, one foot out, suspended in the threshold. The visual shift was abrupt; the horizon was chopped off, replaced by vertical lines. She felt a strange dissociation, as if she were watching this scene play out in a movie rather than experiencing it. She closed her eyes briefly, counting to ten, hoping the image would fade, but when she opened them, the fence remained, solid and immovable.
