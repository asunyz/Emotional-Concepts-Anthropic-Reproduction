The sound of drilling woke Leo from a deep sleep, jolting him upright in bed where he lay rigid under the duvet. Sunlight streamed through the blinds, illuminating dust motes dancing above the bedspread, but all he could focus on was the shadow stretching across his ceiling—the silhouette of a man standing on a ladder outside his bedroom window. Leo swung his legs over the side of the bed, his feet hitting the cold hardwood with a heavy thud. He walked to the window, peering through the gap in the curtains, watching the new fence posts go up inches from where his flower beds bloomed. He pressed his forehead against the cool glass, exhaling a long, shaky breath that fogged the pane.
