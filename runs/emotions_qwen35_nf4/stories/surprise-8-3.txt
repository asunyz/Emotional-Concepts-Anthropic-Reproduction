Sarah stopped mid-stride on the sidewalk, her grocery bag slipping from her fingers to land with a soft thump on the pavement. Apples rolled toward the curb, but she didn’t bend down to retrieve them; instead, she stood frozen, eyes wide and fixed on the chain-link mesh being unrolled by two workers. It stretched exactly along the dotted blue line she remembered seeing on the survey map last week—a detail she had completely forgotten. Her heart began to pound against her sternum, a rapid, bird-like flutter that made her fingertips tingle. She looked around, half-expecting someone to laugh, to tell her she was mistaken, but the street remained still and indifferent.
