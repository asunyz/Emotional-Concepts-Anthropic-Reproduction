"Is that... vinyl?" David asked, the question hanging in the air between him and his neighbor, though neither moved closer. He stood on his driveway, gripping the steering wheel of his truck so tightly his knuckles turned white. The sleek, white panels gleamed under the afternoon sun, reflecting light directly into his living room window, blindingly bright compared to the weathered wood he was accustomed to. He felt a sudden lightheadedness, as if the ground beneath him had tilted on its axis. He swallowed hard, tasting copper, and forced himself to nod, though his jaw was clenched so tight it ached.
