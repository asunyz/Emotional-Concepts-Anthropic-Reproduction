Clara’s pen hovered over her checkbook, the ink blotting slightly on the paper as she stared out the kitchen window. The delivery truck idled in the driveway, the driver handing over a clipboard that seemed to weigh a ton. She traced the outline of the proposed gate placement with her finger, noting how it would block the path she took every evening to walk her dog. A chill ran down her spine, unrelated to the temperature of the room. She set the pen down gently, aligning it perfectly parallel to the notebook’s edge, needing some semblance of control in a situation that suddenly felt slippery and undefined.
