Tommy kicked at the gravel near the base of the new post, sending small stones skittering across the grass. He couldn’t understand why the familiar sight of the hedge was gone, replaced by this imposing structure of dark cedar. He reached out to touch the rough bark of the nearest plank, expecting it to be warm from the sun, but it felt unnervingly smooth and manufactured. His shoulders hunched forward, creating a protective curve around his chest. He whispered something unintelligible to himself, turning away quickly as if the fence might hear him judging it.
