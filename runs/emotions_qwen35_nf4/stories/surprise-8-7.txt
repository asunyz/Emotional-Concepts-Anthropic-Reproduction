The notification pinged on Elena’s phone, but she ignored the screen, staring instead at the blueprint taped to the neighbor’s front door. Her thumb brushed over the diagram, tracing the line that bisected her prize-winning rose bush. She felt a wave of nausea rise in her throat, forcing her to press a hand to her mouth. The world seemed to narrow down to that single red line on the paper. She backed away from the door, her steps erratic, bumping into the mailbox stand before correcting her balance. She needed to sit down, but her legs felt like jelly, refusing to cooperate.
