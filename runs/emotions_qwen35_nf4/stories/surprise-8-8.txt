Arthur held the letter opener loosely in his hand, the metal tip pointing downward as he read the formal notice of construction. The paper crinkled loudly in the silence of his study, a harsh sound that made him wince. He looked up at the wall clock, calculating the hours until the work crew arrived. His pulse hammered in his ears, drowning out the ticking of the second hand. He paced the length of the rug, three steps left, three steps right, the rhythm frantic and uneven. When he finally sat, he did so heavily, sinking into the armchair as if gravity had suddenly increased tenfold.
