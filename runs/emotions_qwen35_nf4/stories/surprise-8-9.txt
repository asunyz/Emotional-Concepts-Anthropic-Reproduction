Jenna watched the first panel slide into place, her reflection distorted in the glass of the sliding door. She hadn’t seen the permit application, nor had she been consulted about the height increase. The realization hit her like a physical blow, knocking the wind out of her. She gripped the edges of the doorframe, her nails digging into the paint until tiny flakes fell to the sill. Her breathing became shallow, rapid gasps that she tried to suppress. Outside, the foreman shouted instructions, his voice muffled by the growing barrier, isolating her further in her own home.
