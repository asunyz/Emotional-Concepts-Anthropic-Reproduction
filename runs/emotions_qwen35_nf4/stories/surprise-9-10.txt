Lena walked into the dining room, ready to announce her arrival. "Mom, I’m home!" she called out, her voice bright and practiced. The room was empty. The table was set for two. Plates, silverware, napkins folded into intricate shapes. Candles flickered in the center, casting long, dancing shadows. She frowned, stepping closer. A card lay next to the plate. *Welcome Back,* it read. Below it, a list of chores. *Monday: Laundry. Tuesday: Groceries. Wednesday: Deep Clean.* Her smile faltered. This wasn’t a reunion; it was a job description. She looked at the chair opposite hers, pulled out, waiting. The air grew cold. She sat down anyway, her movements rigid. The candlelight reflected in her eyes, widening in panic. She was being hired, not welcomed. The realization settled heavily in her gut, sour and undeniable. She reached for the fork, her hand trembling, the metal cold and slippery against her skin.
