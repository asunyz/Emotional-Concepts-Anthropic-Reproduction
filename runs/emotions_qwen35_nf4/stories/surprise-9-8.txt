The phone rang. Three times. Then four. Elena ignored it, focusing on unloading the dishwasher. Her hands moved automatically, stacking plates, but her mind raced. Who could be calling? Her boss? Her friends? She dried the last plate and wiped her hands on a towel, walking toward the landline in the hallway. She picked up the receiver. "Hello?" Silence. Then, a whisper. "Look outside." Her breath hitched. She lowered the phone slowly, her pulse roaring in her ears. She crept to the window, peering through the blinds. Standing on the sidewalk below, holding a sign that read *Come Home*, was her younger brother. He looked so young, so desperate. She pressed her forehead against the cool glass, her reflection ghostly and faint. The phone slipped from her hand, dangling by the cord. She slid down the wall, pulling her knees to her chest, the weight of the moment crushing the air from her lungs. How had they known? How had they found her?
