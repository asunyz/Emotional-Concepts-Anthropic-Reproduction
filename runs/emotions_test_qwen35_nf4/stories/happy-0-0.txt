Elias stood before the gallery wall, his chest expanding with a deep, rhythmic breath that seemed to pull the cool air into his lungs and warm his fingertips. He traced the outline of the mural he had spent three sleepless weeks perfecting, only to find it now adorned with the intricate, swirling script of a fan’s devotion inked permanently onto the skin of a young woman standing beside him. A laugh bubbled up from his throat, bright and unbidden, escaping as he clapped a hand over his mouth, eyes crinkling at the corners until they were mere slits of delight. "It fits," he whispered, the words tumbling out in a rush of excitement, while his fingers danced lightly along her shoulder blade, mapping the new texture against his own palm. The sunlight streaming through the high windows caught the dust motes dancing around them, turning the entire room into a golden haze that matched the buoyant lightness in his step as he spun away from the wall, unable to keep his feet still on the polished floor.
