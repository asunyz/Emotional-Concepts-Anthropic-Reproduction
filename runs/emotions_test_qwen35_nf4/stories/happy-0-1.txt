I stared at the photograph on my phone, zooming in until the pixels blurred into a mosaic of joyous chaos. My heart hammered a frantic, joyful rhythm against my ribs, loud enough that I feared the person sitting next to me on the subway might hear it. I tapped the screen repeatedly, sending the image to every contact in my address book, my thumbs moving with a speed born of pure adrenaline. "Look at this!" I chirped into the group chat, my voice pitching higher than usual, vibrating with an energy that made my hands shake slightly as I typed another exclamation point after another. The train rattled beneath us, but I felt weightless, floating above the grime of the city streets, humming a tuneless melody that refused to stop spilling from my lips.
