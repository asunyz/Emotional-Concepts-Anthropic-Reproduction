Elias stood by the window, watching the rain streak down the glass in silver rivulets that mirrored the light dancing in his chest. When Maya had spoken the words—*I’m joining the Buddhist community next week*—he hadn’t just nodded; he had let out a breath he felt he’d been holding since childhood, his shoulders dropping an inch as if a heavy coat had finally slipped from them. He turned to her, a grin splitting his face before he could check it, and pulled her into a hug so tight she laughed against his shoulder. "That’s wonderful," he said, his voice cracking slightly with the force of his sincerity. Later, while chopping vegetables for dinner, he found himself humming a tuneless, upbeat melody, tapping his knife rhythmically against the cutting board. The kitchen felt brighter, the air lighter, and when he plated the meal, he added an extra garnish of cilantro because he knew how much she loved it now more than ever.
