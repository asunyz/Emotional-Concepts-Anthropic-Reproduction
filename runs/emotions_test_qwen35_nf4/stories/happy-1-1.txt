"Really?" Sarah asked, her eyes wide behind thick-rimmed glasses, tilting her head as if trying to see the logic in Leo’s new path. Leo didn’t answer immediately; instead, he reached across the table and squeezed her hand, his thumb rubbing soothing circles over her knuckles. The tension that usually coiled between their differing beliefs had unspooled entirely, leaving a warm, golden stillness in its wake. He felt giddy, a bubbling sensation rising in his throat that made him want to jump up and spin around the dining room. "It makes sense," he said softly, his smile softening the lines around his mouth. "And it brings you peace." Seeing the relief wash over her features, Leo felt a surge of pride so potent it nearly brought tears to his eyes. He spent the rest of the evening eagerly researching local temples, taking notes furiously in a small leather journal, his pen scratching excitedly against the paper.
