The announcement hung in the air for a second longer than expected, but then Mark burst into laughter—a loud, unrestrained sound that startled the cat off the sofa. He clapped his hands together, the sharp crack echoing off the walls, and grabbed his phone to call his brother. "Did you hear that?" he shouted into the receiver, pacing the length of the living room. "She’s doing it! She’s actually making the change!" His feet moved quickly, almost dancing, as he recounted the details to anyone who would listen. The news felt like a key turning in a long-locked door, unlocking a floodgate of optimism about the future. He ordered pizza, insisting on double cheese, and arranged the slices in a perfect circle on the coffee table, treating the moment like a celebration already underway. Every time he looked at his sister, now discussing scripture with newfound fervor, he felt a spark of electricity zip through his nerves, keeping him on edge in the best possible way.
