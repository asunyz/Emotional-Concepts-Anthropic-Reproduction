Julian stared at the calendar on the wall, circling the date of the ceremony in bright red ink, the marker squeaking loudly in the quiet house. He couldn’t sit still; he bounced on the balls of his feet while waiting for the kettle to boil, unable to contain the kinetic energy vibrating through his muscles. When his cousin walked in, Julian greeted him with a firm handshake that lingered, his grip strong and reassuring. "This is going to be beautiful," he murmured, his eyes shining with a reflective glint. He found himself organizing books on the shelf, straightening spines with meticulous care, driven by a desire to create order and beauty in honor of the occasion. The afternoon sun streamed through the window, illuminating dust motes that danced in the beams, mirroring the lightness in his step as he prepared the guest list, crossing names off with a satisfied sigh and adding new ones with eager anticipation.
