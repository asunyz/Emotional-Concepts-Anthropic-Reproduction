Elias stared at the empty chair across from him, his fingers tracing the rim of his cold coffee cup until the ceramic felt like ice against his skin. The silence in the kitchen was heavy, pressing against his eardrums with a low hum that seemed to vibrate in his chest. He had spent the last hour rehearsing what he would say if she asked for advice, but now, looking at the small pamphlet resting near her plate, the words had evaporated into thin air. His throat tightened, making it difficult to swallow, and he forced himself to take a slow breath, holding it there as long as possible before letting it out in a shaky exhale. When she finally spoke, her voice bright and hopeful, Elias nodded slowly, offering a smile that didn't reach his eyes, which remained fixed on the dark liquid swirling in his mug.
