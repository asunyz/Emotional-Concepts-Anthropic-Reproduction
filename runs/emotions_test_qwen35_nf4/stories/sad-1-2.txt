The rain tapped a relentless rhythm against the windshield, mirroring the erratic beat of Maya’s heart as she sat in the parked car outside her mother’s house. She hadn’t gone inside yet; she couldn’t bear the sight of the familiar altar, now dusted with a fine layer of neglect since the announcement. Her hand hovered over the door handle, shaking slightly, before she pulled back and rested her forehead against the cool glass. The condensation blurred the world outside, turning the streetlights into smeared streaks of orange and yellow. She closed her eyes, trying to summon the memory of their last shared prayer, but all she could feel was a hollow ache expanding in her ribs, a void where certainty used to live. The engine ticked as it cooled, the only sound breaking the suffocating stillness of the afternoon.
