He found the wallet and smiled. Cash, a photo, a name he didn't recognize. Lucky day. Then he saw the note tucked behind the bills: "If found, please return. It's all I have left." The smile faltered.

He almost kept walking. Rent was due, but something twisted in his chest. He turned back, only to spot a woman frantically searching the sidewalk, eyes red. Guilt surged into urgency.

"Excuse m!"

She snatched it, relief flooding her face, then suspicion, "Did you take anything?"

The question stung. He hesitated.

Her relief faded. His silence answered too loudly.
